So, recently I was passing by a Flea Market and decided to look around
and see if I could find anything interesting. After looking for a little
bit, I found a CD of My Chemical Romance’s fourth album, Danger Days,
which seemed to be in really good condition. I asked the person selling
it why they would sell such a nice CD, and they explained it belonged to
their brother who passed away a couple weeks ago. I didn’t think
anything of it at the time, but I would come to regret that decision.
I made it back to the dorm I’m staying at, and later that day I decided
to give it a listen. While Danger Days is definitely not my favourite
MCR album, I still really like it and was enjoying myself as I was
listening to it. However, I was interrupted when my roommate began
blasting Weezer on his speaker at full volume (I believe it was their
song ‘Tired Of Sex’). I was pretty annoyed by this and it stopped me
from enjoying listening to the album, so I stopped even though I had
only made it to the fifth song , ‘Planetary (Go!)’. Little did I know,
if I had kept listening to that CD, things would have gone very
differently.
Later that day, my roommate asked about the CD I was listening to, and I
told her it was MCR and that she could give it a listen if she wanted,
but she just laughed at me and said MCR was stupid (big talk coming from
a Weezer fan). I didn’t realise it at the time, but me telling my
roommate she could listen to my CD was one of the biggest mistakes I
have made in my life.
The next day I went to my Lecture as normal, spent some time with some
associates, and eventually went back to my dorm. Unbeknownst to me, if I
had just went back a little sooner, things would have gone very
differently.
When I opened the door to my dorm, I found my roommate lying on the
ground, dead. She had headphones on her head and was listening to my MCR
CD. I swiftly and effectively reported her death to the authorities, but
the event was most troubling to me. I stayed away from that CD, and soon
inquired about the cause of death; she had died of an inexplicable
explosion of the brain that had remained contained inside her skull. No
one seemed to have any idea what could have caused it, except for me of
course. I instantly knew something was wrong with that CD.
I looked into it online, and using my connections I discovered rumours
of a conspiracy. Apparently, the government are using dark magic to
create cursed CD’s that cause your brain to explode when you listen to
them, and sends them out to people who they identify as threats; I
theorise the brother of the person I bought this from was considered a
threat by The Government. I would have likely met the same fate if not
for my roommate’s love of Weezer, though that doesn’t change the fact
that I still hate that band.
If I had to give you any advice, don’t listen to any suspicious or
untrustworthy CD’s. If you do, you could be next.
