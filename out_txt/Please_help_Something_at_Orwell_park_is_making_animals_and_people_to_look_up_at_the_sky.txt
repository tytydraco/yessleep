However, there’s been something really strange going on for the past few
years and so. But I’ve never really looked much into it until something
crazy that happened just yesterday, but I’ll get to that later.

My earliest memory of something strange happening at the park was about
two or three years ago, I don’t remember exactly, but I was definitely
in high school when it happened. I used the bus to get to and from
school. It was early in the morning, the grass was wet in morning dew,
the sun was rising but still hiding behind houses, casting long shadows
everywhere. I was walking my way over to the bus stop, the park was
slowing rolling into view behind the corner of a house. That’s when I
first noticed them.

They were off to the side of the park, right next to the sidewalk, I was
hard to tell because they were a bit far away. It look like a father and
his son. They were just standing there, facing towards the center of the
park and looking up. I followed their gaze upward, nothing. It was just
a clear, orange tinted sky. No clouds, nothing.

At first I thought it was some weird mediation exercise or something.
But they just continued to stand there, again, I couldn’t see what they
were doing easily because they were standing far away. And I didn’t feel
like bothering some guy and his kid about standing in the park eight in
the morning. So I just stood there until the bus came.

I waited for the bus for like 30 or so minutes, not once did they move
or look away, at least from what I could tell. Even as the sun rose and
sunlight went into their faces, they still stood there. Heads pointed up
at the sky. They were like frozen or something, it was so weird. They
were gone by the time I came back from school and the bus dropped me
off. I remember it not sitting well with me, even before the other stuff
that happened afterward. Still, it didn’t trigger alarm bells or
anything.

The next instance of something like this happening at the park was a
couple months later. Winter was on the horizon, and all the geese were
migrating. This time it was something a lot more vivid. I remember
autumn leaves blowing past my shoes as I was on my phone, texting my
friend about some partner project back at school. There were a flock of
geese flying straight over the park, in their usual V formation.

Man, it was so sudden, it was almost as if a switch was flipped. I
remember everything stopping all at once. The wind that was bombarding
my face suddenly died, the trees and leaves stopped rustling. Singing
birds became dead silent. Even the honking geese. Something became wrong
with the flying geese especially.

I looked up from my phone as this was happening, the geese suddenly just
fell. Not just that, they weren’t even moving. They’re made no sounds
nor movements as they tumbled down through the air. All except for their
heads. All of their heads were facing the same direction, all downwards,
they were even tilting and angling their heads to keep staring towards
this specific point like they were locking onto something. Despite them
twisting and turning while falling through the sky

I watched as they kept falling and falling. I was too stunned to even
speak or process what the hell was happening. As the geese fell, they’re
heads slowly began turning up and up. In the empty silence, I watched as
their falls concluded one by one. Crashing onto the grass. I could even
hear the sounds of some bones breaking, they were all likely dead or
dying at this point. But still. Their full attention, was whatever was
in the sky.

Then, as suddenly as it appeared, the flip switched back off. The birds
sang again, the wind blew in my face as it did before. Leaves drifted
off branches and rolled past my feet as if nothing occurred. Even as the
world seemed to return back to normal, their bodies continued to lay
there, bloodied feathers rustling in the wind, blowing along the sea of
grass.

I cant even describe the whirlwind of thoughts and emotions that I was
going through in that moment. That was when I realized that something
was horribly wrong with Orwell park. That feeling stuck to me through
the whole day, I couldn’t even sleep that night. Was it normal for geese
to even fucking do that? was I hallucinating? Do I tell someone about
this? who would I even call? what would I even say? I didn’t know what
to even make of it.

Apart from the park, life pretty much continued on like normal through

The next notable event that I witnessed was spring of that year. Around

I think it’s important to mention that there’s these electrical towers

Anyways, that’s what these birds were sitting on top of. All of them

times. But what was different this time was that all of them were
staring almost level to the powerline. So, whatever the hell they were
looking at, if they were even looking at anything at all, was floating

That’s when I noticed the trees, they’re planted all along the sides of

It looked like all the trees were leaning or even gravitating towards

Anyways, there was nothing else that was worth taking note of. The birds

This continued for the entire time I was in high school. The times I

Now, the last event I witnessed literally happened yesterday. And, is

I’m going to be honest, I almost completely forgot about all the shit

“Butters! come on! what has gotten up with you?”
