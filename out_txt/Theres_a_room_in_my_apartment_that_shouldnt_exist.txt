 Hello all, my name is Ezekiel, and before I begin i’d like to clarify a
few things. First off, yes this is a true experience. Secondly, no, I do
not take ANY hallucinogenics. I smoke rarely, but even then I doubt that
counts. Third, and most importantly, read the entire thing before you
say anything. Because at some parts you’re going to be very confused,
but you have to hear me out. Good? Okay, now here’s the situation…

I am currrently a 25 year old man, and I am looking forward to finishing
my Masters degree in education. I live in a two bedroom/two bathroom
apartment that’s around a thousand square feet. Pretty spacious, if you
don’t take into account my girlfriend, her two cats, my little puppy,
and whatever new hyperfixation of the week she has. After all of that,
then you can probably get what our apartment looks like. Chaotic, messy,
and so noisy I’m surprised we haven’t been evicted yet. 

I tell you guys this because something creepy as fuck happened
yesterday. Firstly, when you enter our apartment it immediately splits
in two directions. On the left there’s the living room, the right is the
kitchen, and if keep going straight down you make it to both bedrooms
and the guest bathroom. Easy to understand, right? It’s normal, it’s a
normal apartment. And yesterday is when it all came falling apart.

For some reason, and I couldn’t really tell you why, they placed the
central unit for the hvac in the main bedroom. It was hard to get used
to at first, my girlfriend and I were CONSTANTLY being woken up in the
middle of the night to the filter banging around. However, after a few
months, it became our routine…or I guess my routine now that I think
about it.

See, a few nights ago I was dreaming about something, can’t remember
what at this point, and that’s when I noticed it. The deafening silence.
The lack of noise must have woken me up I guess, cause I remember the
room having an eerie silence to it.

It was like nothing I’ve ever seen before, for the first time since we
moved in the apartment was completely silent. Though after the initial
stage of peace and tranquility left me, I realized something that could
be really wrong here. The filter wasn’t knocking around. I was worried
now, was there a dust built up that was blocking the filter? Was it time
for a change and our air was now super dusty?

I went to go check it out, but it was the damndest thing though, there
was no dust or build up AT ALL. So why wasn’t it knocking around like it
used to? That’s when the odor hit me, something slight and gnarly. I
guess I hadn’t smelt it before, because I’m used to cleaning up the
litter box and dog shit. But once it hit me, it hit me BAD. I plugged my
nose up, pulling out my phone’s flashlight to see what it was, and right
there on the side of the unit a mouse had died trying to get inside of
it. 

I can’t tell you what’s stranger, the fact the mouse may have been
trying to get into the machine, or that it may have accidentally been
pulled in somehow. Anyways, as I went over to get a better look and
remove the problem, that’s when I see it. Normally the unit is either in
a small little room by itself, or it’s fixed into the wall, right? But
this one wasn’t. It sat in a spacious closet-esque room, but this was
the first time I had gotten to see behind it. And before anyone asks,
the reason I didn’t normally check was because the knocking would become
way too damn loud without the door shut. Even when switching out the
damn filters!

Anyways. Long story short, I noticed that behind it were a long set of
wooden stairs that led down into a blacky abyss. Obviously I wanted to
go wake up my girlfriend, but since she’s been on this new medication
she couldn’t wake up to save her life…it scared me at first, but I’ve
taken many precautions to make sure that doesn’t happen.

So, all alone now, my curiosity overtook me and I began to travel down
the stairs. One at a time, to make sure that none of them had any rusty
nails poking out. Even though I had my shoes on, you can never be too
sure nowadays. As I reached the bottom step I looked back up to see my
distance, and lets just say it was long. Longer than any normal flight
of stairs should be.

Sure, you could MAYBE chalk this all up to an old shelter made back in
the 60’s, if the apartment I was living in was on the ground floor.

But it wasn’t, we were on the third floor of the building. There should
be no way, no perceivable way, that this room could exist.

As soon as I stepped forward though I could hear the splashing of water,
looking down to see my foot was now soaked in some mysterious black
liquid. It freaked me out too much, and I scurried up those stairs
faster than I ever had before, shutting the door to the unit as hard as
I could.

I guess I slammed it harder than i thought because just then the filter
kicked back on, knocking around and pushing cool air into the room once
more.

I did the right thing, I put my shoes into the bathtub, took off my
socks, and placed them in a bag next to them, and then I properly washed
my feet off.

I needed the evidence, something to prove this happened just in case.
However, as I sat back onto my side of the bed, reaching for the phone
on the nightstand to call the authorities about a potential killer
hiding in my apartment, everything went black.

I woke up to my girlfriend leaning over me, asking me why the hell I
left some mouldy smelling shoes and socks in the bathtub last night? 
She kept asking me things like did I sneak out? Did I go to the creek
with my friends again? Was I on something or do something to someone?
Finally I woke up enough to push her away lightly, asking her what time
it was.

Somehow, it was now eight thirty in the morning, sunshine peeking
through the curtains. How the hell had I passed out, my hand was just
reaching for the phone right? Maybe I had just fallen down and went to
sleep. Pretty normal experience, especially if you can’t recall how you
might’ve went to sleep. Well, to my surprise, when I opened the unit
door to show my girlfriend, it was gone, and when I told her I figured
this might happen which is why I kept the shoes, she just looked at me
disappointedly.

She knows I’d never do anything, no hard dugs, no psychedelics, nothing!
I know this is hard to believe, a staircase and a room filled with water
inside of our apartment? It’s something only someone twisted like
Stephen King could think of.

But it’s real, it happened to me, and my girlfriend is becoming
suspicious. Please, I need any help, because ever since then I can’t do
anything to get the smell out of my shoes. No bleach, alcohol, perfume,
cologne, anything, will cover up the smell. I don’t know where the room
went, I don’t know what this stuff is on my shoes, but I need answers.

Please, can anyone tell me what the hell that mysterious room was, and
where did it go?
