My daughter wished for a brighter sun.
She prayed and prayed until her knuckles bled, as if a slaughterhouse
had opened up.
She left a shadow in her room from all the kneeling she had done there.

To my surprise, the sun did shine brighter.
I came out of my room and was struck by the heavenly surprise.
She broke us apart with the heat and the light.
My baby queen.

I watched my daughter that night to see what could be the force behind
her.
She slept like a beaver, eyes closed, ears not hearing.
Nothing could convince me that this was anything out of the ordinary.
Yet I believed something else was going on.

I did not sleep the next night.
I dreamed of the human shell that hid inside itself.
It was me, but it had different faces.
The rotten sights of somebody who had nothing going on in their pathetic
being.

I prayed for our crops to grow fifty metres tall.
The wheat could reach the sky and feed all the homeless in our village.
The sun’s rays touched my eyes.
It was right there, crawling up toward the blue with its tingly arms.
The neighbours came to watch the fields before I could even change my
clothes.
It became the local news.

How could this happen from a simple prayer?
How could this house produce such unexplainable dreams out of nothing?
Will I get everything I want if I speak the words with all my
confidence?

The next day, I heard whispers from my son’s room.
I walked as quietly as I could toward the door, holding myself close to
the wall.
I became the shadow that could kill with just a touch if somebody could
reach him.
The kid spoke in his sleep.

“I want to reach the sky. Take me from where I lie, Lord.”

This cryptic message stole my eyes and ears and every sense.
While I was trying to touch heaven, something kept me going through the
dreams.
God was trying to taste my sights.

“You do not have to suffer.
You can be in the bliss I created just because.”

I did not listen, somehow.
I followed each step, and somehow I was on the wrong path.

The next day, scrambled eggs crept into the noses of every single living
being in this house.
Except my son.

We searched and searched, but we could not find the body.
We could only grasp the thought that fled while we slept in the making
of it.

I walked into the forest where I believed he had stayed.
This was his favourite place.
Something sinister kept hurting my home, and I wanted it dead.

There he was, near his waterfall.
It was just as miserable as he was, yet I kept following the steps.
The organism did not move at all.

“Hey, son, are you awake?”

I asked with all the confidence in my voice.
It did not reach my ears; it reached my soul.

“You will perish like a hidden bug would.”

I woke up in my bed in the world.
I had seen the room just as white as it was in my dreams.
I had no son, no daughter.

I believed in the future, yet the past kept me way too deep.
I woke up unwanted, with shadows wanting to take me back.
With pills to steal myself from myself and hurt me deeply.
With thoughts that disappeared just as I did in that beautiful view.
With nothing that resembled everything, as in that hurtful dream.

I had myself, but it was way too much.
