This is probably a pretty boring story compared to most of the stuff on
here, but I figured I’d share it anyway.

I’m a military cook, and this is my experience with the old mess hall
our base used until 2019.

In late 2019, a new dining facility was built about 500 feet across from
the old one because of the increase in personnel. The old one ended up
getting turned into a pantry.

The old mess hall itself is pretty spooky, even during the daytime. It’s
a really low building with all the windows sealed up and no electricity
inside, so it’s very, very dark.

The building is basically just one giant long rectangle. From the glass
entrance you can see Sector H, but it’s pretty deep inside the facility.
The kitchen door is diagonally across from the entrance around Sector H,
so you can see it when you first walk in, although everything around it
gets harder to see the farther inside you look.

There are shelves and stacks of boxes all over the place, and some old
tables and chairs that were left behind when they moved everything to
the new facility. There are also some faded signs and empty bulletin
boards still on the walls, which is kind of weird because you can still
tell it used to be a normal mess hall.

If you go in when it’s dark outside, you need a flashlight because even
moonlight doesn’t really reach inside.

That’s why we usually check after dinner if we need any ingredients for
breakfast the next morning, since we start making breakfast at around 4
a.m. You really can’t see anything in there at that time.

Though sometimes we forget, and have to go search around the building
with a flashlight at dawn.

Recently, I had to check if we had any sugar.

Pretty boring reason to go into the old mess hall, I know.

I unlocked the chained entrance and stepped inside. I looked at the
overview chart first to see where the sugar was supposed to be, and it
said Sector H.

So I walked over there.

I could see the kitchen door from the entrance, but it took a little
while to actually get to Sector H. My footsteps were echoing around the
building, which isn’t really unusual in there, but when you’re alone it
does make you notice every sound a lot more.

When I got there, there was no sugar.

I thought maybe we didn’t update the chart after restocking, so I
searched around a bit. Behind shelves, inside unlabeled boxes, under
desks, basically anywhere someone might have put it.

The pantry smelled like cardboard, dust, and paper, and I had to be
careful moving things around because some of the boxes were stacked
pretty high.

Still nothing.

So I went back to Sector H just to check one last time before leaving.

I stepped behind the pile of ingredients to see if maybe the sugar had
fallen behind the stacks, and while I was doing that I accidentally
opened the kitchen door.

The wooden door opened, and the smell that came out was pretty bad. It
smelled like fungus and metal, like the room had been closed up for
years.

I looked inside.

There was a bunch of barbed wire on the floor and a bunch of tables and
chairs that had been flipped over. There was some old kitchen equipment
too, although I couldn’t really see much of it because it was so dark.

I kinda just stared into the kitchen with no particular emotion other
than slight amusement because I’d never seen what the old kitchen looked
like.

Then I started feeling a little uneasy.

The barbed wire was tangled everywhere across the floor and looked
really rusty, and I remember thinking that it would be pretty easy to
trip over it if I actually went inside.

I looked behind me before closing the door, mostly because I didn’t want
to trip over the boxes.

Then I heard a footstep.

Just one.

It came from somewhere inside the building, pretty far away, from what
sounded like the deepest part of it.

I stopped for a second because I wasn’t expecting anyone else to be in
there.

I thought maybe I’d made the sound myself, so I took a step to hear what
my own footsteps sounded like.

It definitely wasn’t mine.

I don’t know how else to explain it. It just sounded different, and it
had come from way farther away.

I didn’t say anything.

I crouched down between the stacked-up boxes next to the kitchen door in
Sector H and just listened for a while.

I peeked over the boxes toward where I thought the footstep had come
from, but I couldn’t see anything.

The building was just sitting there like it normally did, with the
shelves and boxes and the long stretch of darkness behind them.

I waited for another sound.

Nothing.

Then I heard something scrape against the floor somewhere farther
inside.

It wasn’t very loud, and I couldn’t even tell what direction it came
from exactly.

I just stayed there for a few seconds, listening.

I remember looking toward the far end of the building and realizing I
couldn’t actually see all the way there from Sector H. There were too
many shelves and boxes in the way, and the flashlight only lit up
whatever was directly in front of me.

I don’t know what I was expecting to see anyway.

I finally decided I was done and got up, then rushed toward the
entrance.

I nearly tripped over one of the boxes on the way out.

Once I got to the glass door, I got outside, chained the entrance back
up, and ran back to our kitchen.

I didn’t tell anyone what happened.

I just went back to work and started cooking.

I never did find the sugar.
