I had a recurring nightmare that honestly feels like it could be a
horror series
Okay, this is probably going to sound insane, but I’ve had this dream
2–3 times recently, and after waking up I realized the whole thing has
the structure of a psychological horror/mystery series.
I don’t remember every detail because the dream feels extremely complex
and most of it disappears when I wake up, but here’s what I remember.
Working title: THE SAFE HOUR
Genre: Psychological horror / mystery / supernatural thriller
The story takes place inside my mother’s house.
A brother and his sister are trapped on the second floor of the house.
They cannot leave the house.
Something is inside.
Monsters.
They are somewhere beyond the apartment door, meaning the brother and
sister have to stay inside their apartment to survive.
But there is one strange rule:
There is a specific hour every day when they can leave the apartment.
During this hour, the monsters apparently cannot attack them.
But there’s a catch:
They still cannot leave the house.
They can only move around inside it.
So they use that hour to go downstairs, search for food, explore other
rooms, look for their mother, and try to understand what happened.
Then, when the hour ends, they have to get back upstairs and lock
themselves inside before the monsters become dangerous again.
Nobody knows why this happens.
Nobody knows who created the rule.
And nobody knows what happens if you’re still downstairs when the hour
ends.

THE STRANGER
During one of these trips downstairs, the brother encounters a man he
doesn’t recognize.
The man isn’t someone he knows well.
And somehow, their cat is with him.
The brother has no idea how the cat got downstairs.
He doesn’t know who the man is.
And the stranger doesn’t seem nearly as confused or afraid as he should
be.
It’s almost like he already understands the rules of the house.
But he refuses to explain everything.

THE MISSING MOTHER
There’s another strange detail:
Their mother never appears.
The house belongs to her.
They’re trapped inside her house.
But she isn’t there.
Did she escape?
Did the monsters take her?
Is she hiding somewhere in the house?
Or does she know what is happening?

THE HOUSE
As the brother and sister explore during the safe hour, they slowly
realize that the house is much stranger than they thought.
Rooms don’t always seem to be where they remember them.
Things move.
Sounds come from empty rooms.
Sometimes they find evidence that someone else has been living there.
And occasionally, they discover things that suggest their mother knew
this was going to happen.
The safe hour becomes their only opportunity to investigate.
But every day, they discover something worse.

THE REAL MYSTERY
Eventually, they realize the monsters aren’t simply random creatures
trapped inside the house.
They follow rules.
They react to certain things.
They seem to be protecting certain rooms.
And they seem particularly interested in the second floor.
The brother begins to suspect that the apartment isn’t protecting them
from the monsters.
It might be keeping the monsters away from something upstairs.
And eventually he starts wondering:
Why are the monsters waiting for them to leave?

Season 1 ending idea
During one safe hour, the brother stays downstairs too long.
The hour ends.
The house becomes completely silent.
He looks toward the staircase.
Something is standing at the top.
But instead of attacking him, it simply says:
“You weren’t supposed to come downstairs.”
Cut to black.

The actual dream was much more confusing and I don’t remember
everything, but the feeling was extremely real: being trapped somewhere
familiar, knowing there’s something dangerous outside the apartment,
knowing there’s a specific time when you can leave the apartment, and
having absolutely no idea what the rules are.
I genuinely woke up thinking, “Why does this feel like an entire TV
series?” 😂
Would you watch something like this?
