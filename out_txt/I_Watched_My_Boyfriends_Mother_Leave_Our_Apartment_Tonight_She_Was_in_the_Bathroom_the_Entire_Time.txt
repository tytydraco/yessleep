I’m writing this only a few hours after it happened, and I genuinely
don’t know what to make of it.

I’ve experienced the usual strange things before.

Catching something out of the corner of my eye, thinking I heard someone
call my name, or mistaking a shadow for a person.

But this was different.

This wasn’t a fleeting shadow that disappeared when I looked at it.

This was something that unfolded over several minutes. Something so
ordinary at the time that I didn’t even question it.

And now, after talking to everyone who was home, I’m left with a
question I really wish I didn’t have to ask.

Who, or what, did I watch leave our apartment tonight?

Let me explain.

Earlier this evening, I was in the kitchen preparing dinner. I had a
podcast playing on my phone while I stood in front of the stove.

My boyfriend was in our bedroom, and his mother was getting ready to
take a bath.

Our apartment has a particular layout. The kitchen sits directly across
from the front door. When I’m standing at the stove, the white apartment
door is visible in my peripheral vision to the right.

However, I can’t see down the hallway or into the living room from that
position. I can only see the area around the front door.

Remember that.

Just before I started cooking, my boyfriend’s mother and I crossed
paths. She was heading into the bathroom, and I was walking into the
kitchen.

I remember seeing the bathroom door slightly open behind her.

A short while later, I heard it close.

Everything was completely normal.

About five minutes later, I heard her talking on the phone. It sounded
like she was somewhere in the hallway or living room.

I distinctly remember hearing her tell someone that she’d be right down.

It was her normal voice. Familiar enough that I didn’t give it a second
thought.

Earlier that evening, she’d mentioned wanting to walk to the store, so I
assumed she was arranging to meet someone downstairs.

I continued cooking.

A few minutes later, movement caught my attention from the corner of my
right eye. Something dark was in front of our white apartment door.

It was shaped like a person.

Long black hair. Dark clothing. What appeared to be a shorter black
jacket.

My boyfriend’s mother has long black hair and almost always wears black.
She also owns a short black leather jacket.

So naturally, I assumed it was her.

But here’s what I’ve been thinking about ever since.

I never actually looked directly at the figure.

I saw it in my peripheral vision, where the black shape stood out
sharply against the white door.

I couldn’t see where it had come from. I couldn’t see it approaching
from the hallway.

It was simply there, in the exact place where someone would stand before
leaving the apartment.

Then I heard the front door open.

A voice said something like, “Be right up.”

And I heard the door close.

I didn’t turn around.
I didn’t say goodbye.
I didn’t even acknowledge it.

But there’s something about that voice that bothers me now. It wasn’t
quite her normal voice.

My boyfriend’s mother sometimes jokingly puts on this strange, deeper,
mumbled voice when talking to him.

That’s what I heard at the door.

It sounded like her, but not the way she’d been speaking on the phone
just minutes earlier.

At the time, I assumed she was joking around with my boyfriend. So I
ignored it.

I had no reason not to.

In fact, I was actually irritated.

I figured someone had arrived downstairs, and she was going to let them
into the building.
I remember thinking, Great. We’re going to have company.

I was hot, sweaty, wearing shorts, and trying to get dinner finished.
The last thing I wanted was to socialize with someone unexpectedly.

I even started worrying about whether there would be enough food.

Think about that for a second.

I was standing in my kitchen, planning how to accommodate a guest,
because I was so convinced I’d just watched someone leave.

There was nothing frightening about it.

Not yet.

Several minutes passed.

Nobody came upstairs.

Eventually, I started wondering what was taking her so long.

Maybe I’d misunderstood what she’d said.
Maybe it wasn’t “be right up.”
Maybe she’d said “be right back.”

I remembered her mentioning the store earlier, and I figured she’d
probably gotten picked up by someone.

That seemed reasonable.

So I stopped thinking about it.

I went back to cooking.

And then something happened that has made me question the entire
evening.

Maybe five minutes later, I noticed movement to my right again.

This time, it was coming from the hallway.

My boyfriend’s mother appeared from the direction of the bathroom.

Wearing nothing but a towel.

She casually said something about forgetting to grab something, hurried
into the living room, grabbed whatever she needed, and disappeared back
into the bathroom.

I barely reacted.

At first.

Then, about a minute later, I felt this sudden wave of confusion.

Wait a minute.

How did she get back inside?

I stopped cooking for a moment.

I looked toward the front door.

I’d been standing directly across from it the entire time.

I hadn’t heard anyone come back.
I hadn’t heard the door unlock.
I hadn’t heard it open.
I hadn’t heard it close.

And I certainly hadn’t seen anyone enter.

Yet there she was.

Back in the bathroom.

Taking the bath she’d supposedly left the apartment in the middle of
preparing.

I tried to make sense of it.

Maybe I’d mistaken my boyfriend for her?

But that didn’t make sense.

The figure had looked like her. The voice had sounded like her. And my
boyfriend wouldn’t normally leave without telling me where he was going.

Still, I needed to check.

I walked into our bedroom.

My boyfriend was lying on the bed.

Fast asleep.

He hadn’t gone anywhere.

I stood there for a moment, trying to put the pieces together.

If he was asleep…

And his mother was in the bathroom…

Who had I just seen at our front door?

I went back to the kitchen.

I didn’t know what else to do.

Dinner was almost ready, and I was trying to convince myself there had
to be some simple explanation.

Maybe I’d gotten distracted.
Maybe I’d misunderstood something.

But I couldn’t shake the memory.

The long black hair.

The dark figure against the white door.

The voice.

The unmistakable sound of someone leaving.

Eventually, I finished dinner and woke my boyfriend.

We sat down to eat.

And as we were eating, the entire sequence of events came rushing back
into my head.

I started telling him what had happened.

At first, I was just confused.

But the more I explained it out loud, the more uncomfortable I became.

Because I was beginning to realize something.

I hadn’t just thought I’d seen someone. I’d spent the next several
minutes behaving as though that person had actually left.

I’d wondered who she was meeting.
I’d worried about unexpected company.
I’d questioned why she was taking so long.
I’d even come up with an explanation for why she hadn’t returned.

It had all made perfect sense.

Until she walked out of the bathroom.

When she finally finished bathing, my boyfriend and I went to talk to
her.

I asked her whether she’d gone downstairs earlier.

She looked confused.

“No.”

I asked again, explaining that I’d heard her talking on the phone and
watched what I believed was her leaving through the front door.

She insisted she hadn’t gone anywhere.

I described hearing her say she’d be right down.

Then hearing the door open.

Then hearing that deeper, mumbled voice say she’d be right up.

Then the door closing.

She had no idea what I was talking about.

According to her, she’d been in the bathroom the entire time.

The only time she’d stepped out was when I’d seen her wearing a towel,
briefly retrieving something from the living room.

That was it.

She hadn’t gone downstairs.
She hadn’t opened the front door.
She hadn’t returned from anywhere.

And my boyfriend had been asleep.

As I finished explaining everything, all three of us started getting
goosebumps.

Because nobody could account for what I’d experienced.

I’ve been replaying it in my head ever since.

And the more I think about it, the more certain details bother me.

I heard her normal voice first, apparently speaking on the phone.

Then, minutes later, I saw what I believed was her standing at the front
door.

But the voice at the door was different.

Deeper.

Mumbled.

Almost like someone deliberately imitating that joking voice she
sometimes uses.

And the figure itself…

I keep remembering how black it looked against the white door.

At the time, my brain immediately identified it as her.

But thinking back, I can’t say I actually saw her face.

I saw the shape of a person.

Long black hair.

Dark clothing.

A silhouette that looked familiar enough that I never questioned it.

And then there was the door.

I heard it open.
I heard it close.

But I never actually watched it move.

I never turned my head.
I never looked directly at whoever was standing there.

I simply accepted that it was her.

And carried on making dinner.

I’ve heard stories about things called mimics.

Things that supposedly imitate the voices and appearances of people you
know.

I’ve also come across stories warning people not to acknowledge them,
not to answer familiar voices coming from strange places, and not to
follow something that looks like someone you recognize.

I don’t know whether I believe any of that.

Until tonight, I would’ve probably brushed those stories off as creepy
internet entertainment.

But now I can’t stop thinking about one particular detail.

Whatever I saw, I never gave it my attention.

I never answered.
I never turned around.
I never made eye contact.

Not because I sensed something was wrong.
Not because I was frightened.

But because I was so completely convinced it was someone I knew.

And that’s what disturbs me the most.

If I’d turned around, what would I have seen?

Would it have looked exactly like her?
