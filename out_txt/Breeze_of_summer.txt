I really have no words for what I’ve seen as of lately, because its
something I’ve been carrying from a long time ago, and its something I
never thought I would ever have to talk about again, but, I guess I just
need to write this down…

I’m in my mid 20’s, and this experience has brought back many emotions,
which I thought I already knew how to handle, but, turns out I was wrong
all along, and it feels like I really didn’t learn anything from it,
back to square one.

Last week, I was doing my usual tasks in my workplace.

I work for the local soda company, what I usually do, alongside Satō, a
good friend of mine who’s some years older than me, is taking our
products to the local restaurants, stores, refill vending machines,
check and organise inventories and see what’s in stock in each of them,
as well as replace old products and items.

In short, our company is in charge of distributing soda and cola.

Last month, Satō and I were in the truck heading towards our last stop
in the middle of the night, a very warm night to be more precise. An old
yet beautiful ramen place owned by a man older than the place itslef,
his name is Koichi. He waved at us when he saw the truck, then we went
to the backdoor leading to the kitchen, carrying all the merchandise in
four heavily loaded dollys. One in each hand for both of us.

Mr. Koichi opened the door to let us in, and I could smell the udon
boiling, the fried pork meat and a warm wave of broth coming from the
kitchen. Going all around town, all day long with Satō to load and
unload afterwards was really exhausting, specially that day. I thought
of grabbing some ramen before going back to the plant, however I knew
that wouldn’t be possible because our boss is pretty strict with
punctuality, and I understand, it was a friday night, no one wants to
stay there when you could be home early and rest.

Then, Satō unloaded his dollys inside the fridges while I unloaded mine
in the kitchen for display.

Afterwards, Mr. Koichi helped us out by putting our dollys back into the
trunk. Satō bought some soda from the restaurant. He got in the truck
and started the engine.

I looked one last time at the restaurant’s kitchen, knowing I wouldn’t
be able to have some delicious ramen that night, particularly
Mr. Koichi’s ramen, its so good after a long day of hard work.

Man! Are you coming or not? You know how pissed Mr. Rio is gonna get,
come here and get in already!

I knew Satō was right so I got inside the truck.

— I’m sorry Satō-san, I was just thinking about the ramen, you know?

— I don’t blame you man, but if Mr. Rio decides to scold us for being
late, I won’t even have an apetite for the rest of the weekend, you know
how his face becomes really weird with all those wrinkles when he gets
angry, not to mention the smell of his breath…

We both laughed, knowing Mr. Rio’s temper and his bad breath.

I kept seeing many trees and lights along the highway, Satō wanted to
listen to some music on the truck’s radio, however it was to no avail
because it couldn’t catch any station or anything like that, so we could
only listen to the sound of a few cars passing.

A few minutes passed and I saw some water drops hitting the front
window, it was a dew.

Despite the plant not being too close to the city, we still made it just
in time.

We parked the car and we got inside the employee’s room, and Mr. Rio was
already there, he talked to us in a neutral tone.

Hey guys, you made it in time, Satō, gimme your delivery vauchers, I’ll
sort them out, both of you, go change your clothes, good job.

I was pretty relieved by the fact that Mr. Rio would take care of the
paperwork that was left, it didn’t take him too long to get it all done
anyways.

After we changed back into our casual clothes, Mr. Rio turned off the
lights and began to close everything.

I was concerned because I thought the rain had become heavier while we
where inside, but once we got out to the parking lot, I saw it had
already stopped.

We weren’t the only ones leaving already, there were at least another 30
employees getting ready to go. Mr. Rio grabbed his car keys and spoke to
us for the final time.

Good job guys, I’ll see you on monday, take care and rest, good luck.

After saying those words he drove away.

Satō and I said goodbye, he went with other guys because one of them had
a truck and offered them to take them home safely.

I took my bike and rode along the highway, it was pretty quiet, I
thought I would head straight home and forget about everything else. On
my way home I saw a small shrine, very close to the road.

“It’s been a while since the last time I went to one of these…”

I stopped for a bit, just to check out the place. It was all really
quiet, and it looked like nobody had been there for quite some time.
Before going home I made a short prayer for good luck and left to get my
bike.

All I had in my mind was about getting home as soon as I could and have
something to eat, but something was going on with my bike.

It started doing some weird noises until it stopped and wouldn’t move
anymore. It had stopped in a place of the highway where there was almost
no artificial light, only moonlight. There were no trees around that
part so I could actually watch my step.

I took off my helmet, and took a deep breath, then I pushed my bike to
the side of the road, where it wouldn’t be an obstacle for anyone
driving.

I pulled out my phone to see if I had signal, in case I had to call
someone to tow my bike and give me a ride home.

I checked the bike and it seemed alright, there wasn’t really anything
causing a malfunction, and I just couldn’t understand why it even had
happened in the first place.

I felt a breeze, gentle and fresh, and I figured out I could stay there
for a few minutes to see the place I was standing on, after all, even
with all the trips I made with Satō back and forth from the plant to the
town, I never payed much attention to the surroundings. I saw some rice
fields, a few hills, and some trees. Although it wasn’t much or
something too impressive, I felt like a tourist in a place I had passed
by so many times but never bothered to explore it.

From all the things I saw, there was a small clearing not too far away
from a rice field. I decided to see that spot and began walking.

When I got there, I sat down on the grass. It was all really quiet,
except for the distant sound of some crickets.

Just for a brief moment I lied down on the grass, and it was pretty
comfortable.

I heard a faint noise that resembled a the one of a creek, it wasn’t too
far away so I followed the sound.

I walked next to some trees and stones, and suddenly the air around me
became cooler.

I figured out it would have something to do with the wind, humidity, it
was nightime anyways. After a few meters a found the creek, it wasn’t
too big or deep, but it gave me an eerie feeling.

It was pretty dark by this point, but I steel could make out the edges
of the creek.

It was between two pieces of land, there were trees everywhere, rocks, I
even saw some fireflies, but then, I saw a kid.

He didn’t seem to notice me, standing there, at least 10 meters away
from him, and he looked confused

It looked as if he was looking for something in the creek. His feet were
in the water but it didn’t seem like he was cold at all, just staring at
the water.

I wasn’t sure about asking or even saying anything at all because I had
a strange feeling about the whole thing. Why would a child be here? Is
he alone? and overall, What is he even doing here?

I walked around for a bit to see if there was anyone else, anyone that
would know anything about the child, but it didn’t take me too long to
figure out he was totally on his own.

I came back to see the kid, he was still doing his thing and again, he
didn’t notice me.

I shouted to get his attention.

Oi! Oi!

For a second, the kid stopped doing whatever he was doing, then he
turned his head, almost as if he had heard my voice, but didn’t know
where it came from, he was looking everywhere, until he stopped, I
looked away for a moment to check if there really wasn’t anyone else
around until I suddenly heard his voice.

— Are you perhaps lost, mister? - He inquired

His sudden question spooked me, I really wasn’t prepared for it,
specially after it seemed like he couldn’t figure out where I was.

— Uh, no? I was just wondering, where’s your parents at? - I replied.

— Oh, they’re at home, but don’t worry mister, I’m not alone, I came
here with my friend!

— Your friend, you say? Where is he? - I said as I looked around to see
if his friend was anywhere to be seen.

— Yes, we’re looking for stones for the fish pond at my home!

— You sure are, but I don’t see your friend anywhere around…

— I think he wen’t back home, I heard him say something about getting
help…

— And just how long ago did he leave?

— What? Hmmm, about three minutes ago or so, I think.

— Three minutes, huh?

The whole place became quiet, I had no idea if this kid’s friend would
return soon. And, what about his parents? They’ve gotta be worried sick
about their child being outside so late in this cold.

— So, how old are you? - I asked.

— Hm? I’m 9 years old, mister.

Suddenly I realized I didn’t even tell him who I was, which made me feel
a bit ashamed.

— My apologies, I haven’t introduced myself. My name is Oda, Takeshi
Oda, its a pleasure.

— Oh! My name is Josuiji, Josuiji Hayato, its my pleasure.

Just as he finished introducing himself, he looked at the water as he
began searching for more stones.

“That’s odd”, I thought, he didn’t seem distraught by the fact that he’s
all alone, pretty much in the middle of nowhere, in the dark, in the
middle of the cold stream.

The silence of the whole place was oddly unnerving, I could feel my skin
shiver and the cold of my clothes almost made me jump everytime they
touched my body because of the wind.

I knew this kid could contract a disease if he didn’t get out of the
water in time, or worse, he could get a hypothermia and die.

— Hey, Hayato, I know you’re busy with your rocks, I get it, but its
pretty late already, I bet your parents are worried about you!

— It’s not that late, mister! Besides, my friend shouldn’t take that
long…

I interrupted him mid sentence — Ha- Hayato, you said earlier that your
friend went away to get help, right?

He looked a bit mad that I had interrupted him, but nodded in response.

— Alright, what kind of help did you guys need? - I asked

Hayato looked a bit puzzled, then he stared at me for a brief moment.

— He said I was hurt, I think I fell, but I feel okay, I promise! - He
replied in a frustrated tone

— I see, so you fell, where did you hit yourself?

— Mister, I promise! I didn’t hurt myself! - He sounded angrier than
before

I thought of asking more questions until I saw the kid looking at me
angrily, he stared at me that way before turning around to search for
more stones.

A little ray of moonlight that passed through the trees lit his head,
but when I looked at it, I noticed part of his hair was wet, but the
liquid on his hair didn’t look like water, the moonlight showed it was
red.

I panicked for a bit, but I made a decision.

— Oi! Hayato! Can you come here, please? - I said while I moved my hand
in signal that I needed him to come close.

— Hm? Yeah? - He said as he walked to where I was standing.

Once he got there, I took out my phone and turned on the flashlight to
take a closer look at his head, but even before doing so, I spotted see
a bruise on his head.

— Oh boy, Hayato, I think your friend might have been right after all,
you might have a little bruise… - I said in a way that he wouldn’t get
too scared but would understand that he wasn’t really okay as he thought
he was.

Hayato’s hair was in the way, so I decided to move it in case his bruise
was worse than I had thought

— Alright, Hayato. — I said — I’m gonna have to move your hair just a
bit…

Right when my hand touched his head, Hayato fell to the ground.

I gasped before yelling: — Oi! Hayato! Come on, hang on! What’s wrong?

However, Hayato rolled all the way into the stream; I hurried up and ran
there to see if he hadn’t hit his head again and stop him from
potentially drowning.

I yelled again: — Hayato! Hayato! Hang on! - I got there as fast as I
could, then, I lifted him above the water, he looked unconcious, his
skin was cold and he wasn’t breathing.

I took another look at his head, but he didn’t have more bruises.

I looked away for a second, but when I looked at him again, his eyes
were already open, Hayato was staring at me. I was a bit surprised but
figured out he would be alright.

Right as I was about put my hand on the side of his head, his body
suddenly felt lighter. When I looked down, I couldn’t see Hayato’s body
anymore, then I looked back up too see I was now holding a small human
skull facing me.

I yelled and dropped the skull into the stream, where it just
dissapeared as soon as it touched the water.

I yelled again, thinking I was just imagining stuff. — Hayato! Hayato!

I looked around, thinking Hayato would be somewhere. I was breathing
heavily, and suddenly I felt as if the force of the stream was about to
drag my feet.

My heart was racing and the cold water made it real hard for me to move,
I felt as if something was wrapping my chest, and I woke up.

I was laying at the small clearing, not too far away from the rice
fields, I couldn’t understand it. I stood up and ran back to the stream,
hoping I would see Hayato somewhere. I saw the same trees and rocks that
I saw the first time I met Hayato.

I yelled: — Hayato! Oi! Hayato! Where are you!? - But there was no
response, only the most unnerving kind of silence anyone could imagine.

My heart was still racing, I was very tired from running as fast as I
could to see if Hayato was there, and I was just so confused, I thought
that maybe something had happened to him, maybe his friend had come back
and they went back home?

There were so many questions in my head, but none of them made sense, I
felt like I was going crazy, I couldn’t breathe properly and I couldn’t
shake the feeling that something was very wrong.

I walked back to my bike, I tried starting it and it was working again.
I put my helmet back on and I took a deep breath before riding off back
home.

I suddenly felt very hungry and decided to go for a change of plan,
instead of heading straight home I would stop by Mr. Koichi’s
restaurant.

Soon I had arrived, but by then I was feeling a little bit less spooked
or so I thought, I took of my helmet and parked my bike next to the
restaurant.

As soon as I stepped into the restaurant, Mr. Koichi saw me from the
counter.

— Welcome! Eh? Oi, Mr. Oda, you look really pale, did you see a ghost or
something? - He said as he laughed.

I was a bit shook, it took me a second to respond; — Wha- Oh! No, no, ha
ha. Nothing like that.

I quickly noticed the place was empty, so I would be eating in silence
that night.

I ate some ramen, after all, a large and warm ramen bowl was all I
needed to feel better, although I was still wondering what that had been
all about, but I figured out it was just a weird nightmare.

After finishing my bowl I walked up to the counter to pay for my meal,
but something caught my attention.

On the counter there were some photographs, in one of them there was a
kid.

My heart began racing again as I realized that kid looked a lot like
Hayato.

Mr. Koichi, noticed me looking at the picture.

His face suddenly changed, he looked nostalgic and a bit melancholic.

He let out a sigh before speaking; — That was my son, Hayato, he was my
first son, he died in a stream. We never knew what he was doing out
there, he had many bruises on his head and his neck was broken.
Sometimes I think about him.

I felt as if a cold wind surrounded my body, I stood in silence before
speaking again. — I- I’m sorry to hear that, I had no idea…

— Its alright. — He said with a barely noticeable smile on his face — It
was a very long time ago, I’ve moved on and always try to be a better
father to the rest of my children, even if they rarely come by to visit,
they’ve all moved to the big city a while ago…

After that, I walked out of the restaurant, the street was in complete
silence and only a few street lights were working then, I couldn’t even
hear any cars passing by, maybe because it was already pretty late, it
all felt very lonely, no different from that place where I had been
earlier.

Suddenly a soft, yet, cold and dry breeze made it clear for me.

It was time for me to go home.
