I want to share one of the scariest things that has ever happened to me.
I know how unbelievable this is going to sound, and if I had experienced
it alone, I might have convinced myself that I had imagined the whole
thing. But I wasn’t alone. My friend was there, and he saw it too.

I was around sixteen at the time. Every Wednesday, a few of my friends
and I would get together to play soccer. It was something we did
regularly, so those evenings never felt particularly special. We would
play for a while, talk, joke around, and eventually make our way home.
One of the friends who was with me that night will remain anonymous, so
I’ll call him Jacob.

That day had been especially good. We had played soccer, had a couple of
drinks, and enjoyed ourselves. Just to be clear, neither of us was
drunk. We were completely aware of what was going on, and there was
nothing unusual about the way we were feeling. When it got late, we got
into the car and started driving home.

We put some music on and carried on talking. The drive was normal, and I
remember feeling tired in that comfortable way you feel after spending
the afternoon playing sports. There was nothing that made me think the
night would turn into something I would still be trying to explain years
later.

When we turned onto our street, we noticed a horse-drawn cart coming
toward us. It wasn’t something that immediately caught our attention as
strange. I could see the person guiding it, the horses pulling it, and
the cart itself. It all looked perfectly ordinary. I remember looking at
it for a moment, then turning my attention back to the road.

We soon reached my house and turned into the driveway. I needed to get
out and open the gate so we could bring the car inside. Jacob was still
nearby while I went to open it.

That’s when I looked back toward the street.

There was a streetlight nearby, and it was the only light illuminating
that particular stretch of road. Its light fell directly over the cart.
At first, I didn’t understand what I was seeing. The cart seemed to be
getting darker, but not in the way something looks when it moves into a
shadow. It was as if its actual shape and appearance were changing right
in front of me.

The darkness spread across it. Its outline began to distort, and the
cart seemed to lose its original shape. Within moments, it no longer
looked like a cart at all. It became a completely black mass, something
that seemed to stretch upward and grow taller.

I froze.

I couldn’t make sense of it, and I knew I had to get Jacob to look
before whatever I was seeing disappeared. I turned toward him and said,
“Dude, look at that. Do you see it?”

He looked in the direction I was pointing.

And then I knew I hadn’t imagined it.

The cart was gone. The horses were gone. The person who had been guiding
them was gone too. In their place was something long, thin, and
completely black. It almost looked like a tall, narrow pole standing
beneath the streetlight, except it hadn’t been there before. We had both
just watched the cart turn into it.

For a brief moment, we could only stare.

Then the thing moved.

It didn’t walk away, and it didn’t move like an animal or a person. It
suddenly shot sideways, as if it had been thrown through the air, and
disappeared behind the houses. The movement was so strange and
unexpected that I barely had time to follow it with my eyes.

And then there was nothing.

No cart. No horses. No person. Just the street, the houses, and the same
streetlight shining as if nothing had happened.

I remember looking at Jacob, trying to understand whether he had seen
exactly what I had seen. I didn’t need to explain much. His reaction
told me everything. He was just as confused as I was. We had both
witnessed the same impossible transformation, and neither of us could
come up with a reasonable explanation.

Even now, I don’t know what happened that night. I don’t know whether
the cart was somehow an illusion, whether we saw something that we
couldn’t understand, or what became of the person and the horses. I have
gone over that moment in my head countless times, trying to find some
detail that might make it all make sense.

But I always come back to the same part: the cart was there, clearly
visible beneath the streetlight, and then it changed into something
completely black and flew away.

I wish I could say I found an explanation. I wish I could say it was
just a trick of the light.

But Jacob saw it too.

And to this day, neither of us knows what was standing beneath that
streetlight.
