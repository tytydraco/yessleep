I have kept this a secret for my entire life, but based on recent events
I have decided that other people need to know about this. I need help,
and I need it quick, but I have to explain first.

 

A lot of my childhood was spent with my parents covering my eyes. With
their hands, with blindfolds, or just telling me to keep my eyes shut as
tight as possible; anything that kept me from seeing for a majority of
my time around the house. For the first six years of my life, I
practically grew up blind. We also never had visitors at my house
outside of family. I was never allowed play dates or sleepovers, and my
parents never brought any friends around. As a baby, I didn’t know any
better and probably didn’t care at all (it’s not like I can remember
it). As a toddler, it was just a fun game of peekaboo, and why would I
even notice that no one came to the house other than my family? As a
child who started going to school, I realized it was strange. I started
asking questions until I was told what was really going on in my
household.

 

I remember the day my parents took me on a walk and explained our
predicament to the best of their ability. I was eleven. I had been
asking about the blindfolds I had to use around the house since
Kindergarten — when I found out it wasn’t a normal thing for other kids
my age — but recently I had been asking more and more. My parents were
lucky I never told any of my teachers about it, or else they might have
had a visit from police or CPS. Maybe I would have been better off that
way. My parents decided to buy me ice cream that day, maybe to take the
edge off of what they were about to tell me. It didn’t end up helping.
When you’re a kid, you tend to believe everything your parents tell you,
but this was just too questionable, even for an eleven year old. For my
entire life up to this point my parents had reinforced that the monsters
under my bed and in my closet weren’t real. They had recently told me
that Santa Claus and all the other magical beings I believed in weren’t
real. They told me about science and evolution and scoffed at the idea
of paranormal. They were stout atheists who shut down any idea of a God
when I mentioned learning that some of my friends went to church on
Sundays. But now they were telling me a being of some sort lived in our
house and if you looked at it or spoke of it in it’s presence it would
kill you.

 

It’s safe to say I didn’t take it very well and didn’t believe them in
the slightest at first. I thought it was a weird joke or game. But they
started explaining further and further. I had multiple dead relatives
that died young, many of which before I was born; and all of which were
on my father’s side of the family. I had a younger cousin who had died
when she was two. I was only six when it happened but I still remember
attending her funeral, and I still clearly remember the hushed whispers
from my father to his brother (my cousin’s father).

 

I know it sounds ridiculous, but it’s true. My father’s bloodline is
cursed. From what I know, it started with his great great grandpa. He
had nine kids. Only the youngest two survived to adulthood. No one is
quite sure how he brought home this being, but it has stuck with my
family for generations. It always has a primary host, but it is able to
kill anyone; even those outside the bloodline (and animals). After its
primary host dies, it will pass onto the next of kin (which usually
happens to be the eldest child of the last primary host). The being
resides in the house of the primary host and likes to hide in corners
and dark spaces. It’s far too smart and it is desperately trying to
trick everyone it comes into contact with.

 

Contrary to what my family originally thought, you can look at it, but
you cannot acknowledge it. You must look through it and have zero
reaction to its presence. You cannot speak of it in its presence, you
cannot even write about it or draw it (I am currently at my local
library and writing this on a computer). Even if you wanted to draw it
though, it’s impossible. It’s impossible to describe its appearance as
well. Not that it supernaturally prevents you, just that it is so
grotesque and strange that you could not possibly translate what you are
seeing into something as simple as written words or a drawing. Nothing I
could tell you would allow me to truly describe its appearance. No
drawing I could make would be an accurate depiction of that thing.
Nothing could fill you with complete dread and a desire to end yourself
that seeing the being sitting in the corner does.

 

My predicament is this: I am an only child. I am now twenty-one. I moved
out of my parents house and I have an apartment. I have friends, a
boyfriend, and I’m in university. Despite my childhood, despite my
family, despite it, I am living the perfect life. But yesterday, the
being appeared in my house. It almost got me. I almost reacted. I could
never have expected it to be there. After I got home and realized it was
there I left almost immediately, but of course I couldn’t act too
nervous or rash when leaving. As soon as I was a block away from my
apartment I called my mom and began sobbing, convinced my father had
died in a tragic accident. I was so panicked that she could barely
understand what I was saying. But when I finally managed to properly say
“It’s in my house! Is dad dead?” my mother could only go silent on the
other end of the phone.

 

The pure terror in her voice still echoes in my brain as I’m writing
this.

 

“Your father is sitting right next to me.”

 

So that’s why I come here to ask for help. My family has dealt with this
being, this horrific “pet” for decades. We thought we knew how to deal
with it. We thought we knew how to survive it. But now it’s all thrown
out. Why are there two of them?

 

I became so desensitized to my family’s problems. I was able to sleep in
my childhood bedroom for years with no fear of the being because I knew
how to simply look past it. I knew how to ignore it. But I can’t ignore
it any longer. Who knows what it’s actually capable of now? Will it
eventually be able to break free of whatever it is that keeps it from
attacking us when we act like it’s not there?

 

So once again I’m desperately asking for help and advice. Anything you
can suggest is something I’m willing to try. I need to get rid of what
has plagued my family for far too long.

 

The library is closing soon. I have to head home to pick up some stuff
so I can stay at my boyfriend’s for a bit. I’ll have to make up some
excuse as to why I can’t stay at my apartment for now. At this point,
all I can hope is that I’m able to stop myself from acknowledging the
“pet” that has infiltrated my apartment.
