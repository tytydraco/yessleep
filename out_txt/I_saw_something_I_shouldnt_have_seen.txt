I really love going to abandoned places. A few weeks ago, I decided to
go to an abandoned church, one that wasn’t too far away.

After a 30 to 40 minute journey, I arrived at the church, for some
reason, a shiver had run through me even while I was still on the way.
Perhaps the fact that it was a religious site caused me some extra
stress.

I noticed that the door was open and realized I wouldn’t have to go to
the trouble of getting inside.

A very foul odor was emanating from inside so bad, in fact, that it
could easily be detected even from in front of the door.

Because the building was old and abandoned, it hadn’t been maintained
for a long time, perhaps that’s why there was that smell.

I went inside without much thought and began to look around.

The walls were all peeling, the floors were covered in broken glass,
everything was coated in dust, and the pews were broken.

As I moved forward, that foul stench began to intensify. While wandering
around, I noticed a light, I wasn’t alone here. When I reached the spot
where the light was coming from, I couldn’t believe my eyes.

A Star of David had been drawn on the ground, at each point of the star
sat a candle, and at each of these points lay a human limb, a head,
arms, legs, and a torso.

I was shocked by what I saw and couldn’t move for a few seconds. Then, I
heard footsteps and immediately hid.

Six individuals arrived, wearing red outfits reminiscent of Ku Klux Klan
robes, and each took their place at one of the points of the triangle.
They spoke in a language I did not understand, most likely phrases
related to some sort of ritual.

I tried to be as quiet as I could, and after saying a few more
sentences, they blew out the candles and left.

I couldn’t move from where I was. After they left, I waited inside a
little longer, then I went outside as well. I moved some distance away
from the church, immediately called the police, and explained what had
happened.

There was no one around. After a short wait, the police arrived and went
inside. They searched the premises for at least 30 minutes, and when
they came back out, they asked me whether I was under the influence of
anything.

At first, I didn’t understand what was going on. Then, I was told that
there was nothing inside. I immediately went in, accompanied by the
police, and when I reached the spot where I had seen the incident, I
realized that there was, in fact, nothing there.

Due to the rise in drug use, they assumed I was also under the influence
of drugs, so we went to the hospital for a blood test. After providing a
blood sample, I had to stay in a holding cell while waiting for the
results. That time felt like hell.

The results came back negative, and the police officer who released me
told me that if I wasted the police’s time again, I would end up in
jail.

When I got home, I was still questioning the reality of what I had seen.
No one else had entered after me, so it was impossible for them to have
destroyed the ritual items. Yet, with my own eyes, I saw that there was
nothing there.

Three days had passed since these events, and I was trying to console
myself with the thought that perhaps I had just imagined it. until
around 4PM, I saw one of those men in red clothing outside my house,
standing under a tree and looking up at my window.

I was very frightened, I immediately grabbed my pistol and began
watching him from the window as well.

Fifteen minutes had passed, and the man was still watching me without
moving. There was a knock at the door, and I jumped in fright. I went to
the door and looked through the peephole, but I saw no one there.

I ran straight to the window, and the man was no longer there.

The next day, the man in the same clothes was back in the same spot, at
the same time, watching my window. I began watching him in return with
my pistol in hand, and this time, I wasn’t going to get up and leave,
even if there was a knock at the door.

My phone suddenly started ringing, and I turned my head for a moment, it
was an unknown number. When I turned back, the man was gone again.

The next day, the same events occurred again at the same time.

I don’t know what to do. Even if I go to the police, they’ll just
disappear again and this time, there’s a chance I could end up in
prison.

If you have any advice, please tell me.
