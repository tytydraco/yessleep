I know, I know. It sounds bad. But I’ve tried everything, everything, to
manage my period. None of it worked.

It’s been a problem since the beginning. Nothing can prepare you, as an
eleven year old, for waking up in a pool of blood. 

My legs, panties, shorts, and bedsheets would be covered in thick,
sticky blood, and I would run crying to the bathroom to clean up. You
can’t imagine how embarrassing this was.

Throughout middle school I was terrified to be caught without a tampon,
or that I would get up when the bell rang only to find a stain on my
plastic chair. 

So as soon as I was in college I went to the nurses office to get
whatever I could.

I started with the patch. Besides having a peeling, tacky sticker on my
arm which clung to all my clothes and made me look like I was trying to
quit smoking, it also gave me soul crushing depression. 

From there, I went on to the ring, which fell out when I had sex and
wildly increased my anxiety, to an IUD which was inserted with all the
tender care of a chainsaw. I hobbled to my car after the appointment,
crippled by the pain that should have been “just a pinch.” I was so
terrified of the removal that I endured the debilitating, torrential
periods for years before I finally had it removed.

Even with it out, there was no relief. 

Every month I endured migraines, extreme cramps, and low energy. And of
course, the blood. It was no better now than when I was in middle
school. Every month I looked like a pale, sickly scarecrow while my lady
bits spewed a level of liquid that would make a surgeon queasy. 

How could I still be dealing with this as an adult woman? It seemed like
everyone else had a solution that worked for them. Mine seemed like it
was getting worse with age, not better.

I stayed up late every night googling side effects of different
medications, pros and cons of surgery, and what worked for everyone
else. Every pill name I typed into the r/birthcontrol sub dredged up
posts with comments like “THIS PILL RUINED MY SEX DRIVE,” “my skin looks
like shit now,” or “I gained 25 lbs and my anxiety is worse.”

Well I didn’t want to take that one I guess.

But one comment led to another and the next thing I knew I was on the
holistic pages. Underneath the bullshit suggestions to try turmeric and
center my aura, I found the first comment referring to a solution.

“I’ve used Judy’s method, it helped me more than anything else. I’m
feeling great.” One user wrote.

I squinted at the screen. What the fuck was Judy’s method?

I read on.

“I wish I’d tried it sooner.”

“Pain is gone, migraines are gone, period is gone.”

“I feel better than ever.”

I opened a new tab and typed “Judy’s method +period +reddit” and hit
enter.

Google redirected me to some posts on r/birthcontrol, r/naturalremedies
, and r/craft_users. I clicked on the last one. 

The post brought up a recipe. I read eagerly, growing more and more
confused. 

What kind of a craft page was this? 

I checked the header, which described itself as “a gathering place for
natural witches, practitioners of holistic exploration, and those
seeking alternative solutions to our medical challenges.” 

Ok.

I went back to the page. I did have the ingredients.

Salt, from the kitchen.
Sage, from my garden.
Blood, from my own body.
Seven candles.
Three rusty nails. 
And hair from a familiar.

I wondered if my pumpkin spice candles and dog hair tumbleweeds would
work. I skimmed the instructions. They seemed simple enough, especially
for someone who had no experience with anything like this. 

What the hell, I thought. Nothing else has worked. Might as well give it
a shot.

 Over the next week, I gathered up the items on the list while I waited
for my period to start.

One mortons salt canister, a bundle of sage, seven candles, three rusty
nails pulled from my fence, and a clump of dog hair later, I was ready. 

Oh. And a small plastic shot bottle of menstrual blood.

It was a full moon and the Saturday before Halloween, which felt right.
I went down into my basement and pulled up a screenshot of the
instructions on my phone. 

With the salt, I drew a circle.

The sage I lit, blew out, and sat on a plate to smoke.

I spaced the candles evenly around the circle, hoping that cucumber
melon and pumpkin spice didn’t cancel each other out.

The nails I laid between the candles, point in.

The dog’s hair I sprinkled around.

And the blood vial I held in my hand as I sat cross-legged in the
middle.

I pulled up the screenshot on my phone and re-read the instructions. I
read the words out loud, chanting them exactly seven times-

Except in the middle of the chant, the box fan in the corner, which was
on a timer, kicked on and blew out 3 of the candles.

“Dammit,” I muttered. I got up and pulled the plug on the fan. I sat
back down, relit the candles, and finished the chant.

The candles flickered. I shivered, and exhaled slowly. Nothing seemed
any different. I laughed it off and cleaned up my mess before going to
bed.

But when I woke up this morning I felt like total shit. 

My skin was cold, clammy, and whiter than ever. The skin under my eyes
looked green, and a huge angry zit was forming on my chin. 

And there was blood everywhere.

 It’s like an explosion down there. I’m typing this from the toilet
right now. At this rate, my period will be over in a few hours instead
of a few days.

Or I’ll bleed out, and be dead.

I searched for Judy’s method again. When I found the page, my heart
sank.

My screenshot cut off a few steps. I hadn’t finished the spell.

“Fuck,” I whispered. I went back to the reddit page and commented on the
original Judy’s method post.

“Hey guys.. I feel so much worse after doing this. I might have missed
some steps and my fan kicked on in the middle of the ceremony so I had
to get up and re-light some candles. Thoughts?”

Within moments my phone pinged. 

“You broke the circle? You might have invited something in.”

Well that sounded bad. It pinged again:

“Was it a full moon? It’s supposed to be performed on a new moon for
emptiness.”

Oops. Missed that one..

“Sounds like your uterus might be possessed now.”

Great. 

I feel like shit, look like hell, and everything is coming out of
everywhere. Im beginning to wonder.. is my uterus possessed? Am I about
to die from blood loss? Has anyone else tried Judy’s method?? Send help.
