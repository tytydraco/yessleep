In my home town there’s this “haunted” tunnel.

There’s a website, or there was, about all these apparently haunted
locations and for us that was the closest one to get to.

The story or myth was that 2 trains collided inside the tunnel and
dozens of people died so they haunt the tunnel.

Actual research into the spot shows a less horrifying but more subtle
creepy factor. It was actually one conductor who got pinned between 2
trains who died inside the tunnel; and a mass disturbance of hundreds of
graves that were previously atop the tunnel area had to be dug up and
moved in order to build the tunnel.

Alas, I have never believed in spirits or ghosts and of course going to
this haunted tunnel in the middle of the woods would be fun, not scary.

So I gathered my toughest friend and we followed the crude instructions
through this forest taking pictures with our old digital camera (this
was 20 years ago) and we captured a ton of “Orbs” on the way. Creepy
yes, but to me it had to get easily explained - perhaps condensation in
the air or something.

Finally we come around a corner and there is this massive dark gaping
gateway in the side of the hill. admittedly it was freaky but I was just
enjoying myself so not scared just exhilarated.

As we approached, we heard voices and when we got up there we noticed
there was a small group of people standing outside the tunnel. Turns out
they were amateur “ghost hunters” and were doing some survey or
something.

My buddy was a hard pass on going in the tunnel. He was tough as nails
but his mother had always told him not to mess with paranormal stuff so
he strictly said he wasn’t going in.

So one of the more brave members of the ghost hunter people and I went
in. We had flash lights so really it was nothing. I have lots of
pictures of it. Just a creepy train tunnel with wooden planks and arches
etc.

We got maybe 100 yards in and the others yelled to us that there was
something right above our heads that they had captured on camera. I
didn’t believe it but we went back out and they showed us the picture
and yes there was this giant smokey orb thing right above us.

Still, I don’t believe in that stuff so I knew there had to be some
explanation.

After some small talk the other group left and it was now just me, my
buddy, and the tunnel - and something(s) else?

I said, man, I’m not going to get any value out of this unless I go in
there by myself, without any flashlights.

He said you’re crazy man but I’m staying out here

So I did.

I slowly walked all the way to the other end where I could see it was
caved in and there was no way out. I admit adrenaline was going but
truly I don’t believe in that shit so I wasn’t “scared” of ghosts.

Eventually I got back to my friend and he said man can we just get the
fuck out of here already.

I said sure, I just have to do one more thing.

I turned back to the tunnel.

And I said, “Okay! If there are any spirits or ghosts out here we are
leaving now. Now’s your last chance to show yourself!”

https://imgur.com/gallery/thing-blue-ghost-tunnel-FHJvvLO

Now this was on an old digital camera - you take the picture and you
look down and see it.

Considering what I took a picture of was the fucking tunnel and all you
could see was the grass on the sides and a tunnel opening - NO SMOKE or
anything else…. well we both said wtf and started running.

Admittedly I’m laughing but decently freaked out. He’s screaming at me
saying what the fuck and he’s going to kill me for scaring him, but we
really haven’t looked yet. All we saw was the smoke. Once we got like
2-3 away from the tunnel we stopped to really look closely.

https://imgur.com/gallery/thing-blue-ghost-tunnel-FHJvvLO

I know you can see it too.

The thing that creeps me out the most is the way it’s like a gust of
smoke flying in like a spiral, then flying out.

To this day I cannot explain what that was.

You can easily claim that’s a Photoshop image etc but it’s not. I took
that photo myself and remained decently freaked out for the remainder of
the week, even after going back a couple times.

Ultimately others say it’s that science thing where your mind tells you
it’s a face but it’s not really a face. Yeah I don’t buy that.

Admittedly, I still to this day do not believe in spirits but I have no
explanation as to what that was or if it followed us home that night.

Sadly, not too long after that my friend died from a brain tumor. He was
healthy as far as we knew before going to that tunnel.
