***July 28th, 1970:***

 For future reference this notebook is a distillation of what I learn

***July 29th, 1970:***

I had the day off work, spent it looking through M.L.’s research, a
desperate attempt to find any other leads on him and for my own

Paranoia can be a gift or a curse, I’m not going to be prey to the

*“What’s new?”* a man wearing a hat said

*“What if a car hits that man, I wonder what hour it’ll happen, I think

*“I know he’s still in the house, down the hall, still in the house…

Sitting still, trying to pull it together, trying to ignore all the

The paper at the top of the page was a complex sigil, carefully drawn
with tools. A cross at the center with symbols extending from the ends
of the cross, contained within a pentagram; more symbols filled the gaps
within the pentagram. Surrounding it and extending beyond, fractals of
hexagons and other geometry within the empty gaps of intersecting lines

I’ve seen this before, at a museum. It was made during the mid-to-late
1500s prior to the British Empire, a term coined by a contemporary in
that age, John Dee. During the reign of Elizabeth the first, a personal
teacher, occultist, mathematician, astrologer, alchemist named John Dee
created this exact symbol. It was used as just the top piece of
infrastructure for a greater occult object, one meant to invoke the
angels closest to God. They were given a language from the angels, using
that language they supposedly created more sophisticated scrying,
channeling occult technology, like a next level Ouija board. The
channeling sessions were hosted via Dee’s schizophrenic, psychic, and
alleged con-man assistant, Edward Kelley; his handler John Dee said the
angels called their language Enochian. Visually it looks like a
potential precursor to any language.

Below this, were a series of writings, which I also recognized as a part
of Aleister Crowley’s work, a ritual, a meditation; he referred to it as
crossing the abyss, which involves the initiates first going beyond
language, then the imagination, dreams, hallucinations. After that
point, they perform a series of rituals in an attempt to expand
themselves enough to launch their awareness across the brain-cosmic
barrier; what lies beyond the walls of the skull, walls only sieged and
ravished by prolonged silence of mind. Exception, glimpses seen of the
same kind in the mental-thrashing brought by mind-altering chemicals.

The instructions of the paper are as follows:

Arm yourself with a ritual dagger for the dweller of the abyss, Chorozon

perimeter by drinking wine from your chalice. Perform the lesser

Invoke our holy guardian angel and our lady, the Whore of Babylon.

10th Aethyr

*“I, Ixomaxip, and she, who is the light, demand entrance to ZAX!”*

*“Why do you want the light”*

The words felt demonic, in a mocking tone.

*“Do you fear your shadow?”*

*“There is nothing hidden that is not true already. There are no secrets

*“There’s a place of blue skies and sweet music and dancing! What if you

*“Look at the shadows, should you think they sleep? They never sleep!”*

The darkness began to swirl and spin with faces in the shadows around

I looked away from the wolves, to a half-filled bookshelf, a free slot

*“We will be eaten by our own shadows”*

The four wolves in the four corners of the room began to mutate,

A thought formed in the back of my head *“You want to know what feels

A weight around my throat wraps tighter, out of breath I close my eyes.
The voices stop, the radio turns off, a deafening humming takes the

I could feel someone breathing on the back of my neck, I couldn’t move.

I tried to open my eyes to escape the imagery but they felt glued shut.

A man with a handlamp walked toward me, with every blink he got closer,

*“They are perfect when they sleep”* my lips moved.

*“They are watching me again, I never know where.”* this phrase

A little boy in his bed, softly asleep. Slowly my body crept itself

***July 31st, 1970:***

Lifting my face from the puddle of drool, dirt, and leaves, I see a
forest with sober eyes. Close-by to my right side is an abstractly
shaped effigy, made of a bundle of sticks with other smaller structures
like ornaments hanging from the branches. As I walk closer, the center
of the sculpture is a skull, reconstructed from household items, with
what look like mini feather tipped spears coming out the top of the head
and blonde hair stuffed into one of the small surgical holes in the back
right of the skull. I stepped back from the cabinet sized altar.
Horrified, yet, the attention to detail, small intricate etchings in
brittle wood, bundles wrapped with hair, human and or animal of all
colors. Whispering to myself

*“this couldn’t have been made in a single night…”*

I started following the river, walking in a direction that looked like
civilization, making note of natural landmarks to find my way back to
the shrine. Thinking about the origins of the altar and the events of
last night frightened and intrigued me, in a morbid way. It didn’t take
long to eventually stumble onto the street I lived on. Walking down the
road, within eyeshot of my house, I see a white van and silver impala,
empty. Instinctively I went to unclip my gun holster, I wasn’t wearing
it.

Opening the door to my home, checking each corner as I passed from room
to room, I enacted a plan that had been unfolding in my gut since I
started to really dig into M.L. ’s research. I packed canned food,
clothes, protection into a backpack and then all his research into a
duffle bag. I left within the hour to see Mae at the radio station and
collect the rest of his research.

Did not make a report about the altar incident to co-workers, or the
