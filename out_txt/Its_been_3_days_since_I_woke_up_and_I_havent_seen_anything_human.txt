Hey y’all. I’m hoping people can see and are reading this.

So, my situation. I’m an engineer and I’ve been on holiday in this
remote part of Britain - yes, I’m British - for a few weeks now. A week
or so ago, though it’s harder to tell, I got knocked unconscious by a
light in the sky. Not like anything hit me. I woke up in the night, saw
it, and just dropped. Dead, almost. It was two days, from what I can
tell. after that, that I woke up to… this. Nothing. I remember it too
well. The light was gone. It was morning. My air BNB was covered in moss
and other foliage, though it still had electricity, water, and my phone
showed only two days passing. My clothes were dusty, my phone was on 100
percent and as far as I could tell, the normal sound of people talking
that normally woke me hadn’t done so. There weren’t any birds in the
sky. Or planes. And everything’s silent. I knew something was wrong.

Now, the crazy thing is that even though the exterior is covered, the
interior was completely fine. I rented a pretty decent house, with a
small bathroom, kitchen, bedroom and living room, so at the point of
noticing the moss covering my window and the rest of the house (I only
guessed that the entire house was covered, but figured it obvious, which
I was right about.) I could pretty easily get out of bed and check the
rest of the house. So I went and got a drink from my tap, ate breakfast,
then went to look around the house, to which the previous statement was
confirmed. I checked outside and I was right that my house - along with
the convenience store. Small neighbourhood of shops I lived near, and
cinema were also all covered. While I think that contradicts my earlier
statement of this place being remote, I think I should say there were
people here - enough for it to be decent - but it was in a remote enough
area that I consider it so. But now, no people are in site.

Realising that something, whatever it may be, is wrong, I decided to
call up my wife; she’s on holiday with her friends, so I decided to go
on a smaller holiday while we were apart. No reply. Nada. So I went
through my contact list one by one. Still, nothing. As if they don’t
exist.

So. Freaked me out a bit. I decided that I would figure out if anyone
was nearby, first and foremost. If there was some kind of global event I
didn’t know about, I need to try to figure it out. So I figured I would
check each house on my street. If they were empty, I would check for
anything useful - that being tools, a weapon, maybe food, as we’re
assuming that I’ll need it more that they will. After that, I would get
a head start on the shopfront and cinema before anything else, hopefully
before dark, as I didn’t feel like walking in darkness. On the contrary,
I would keep an eye out for anything breathing - incase there are humans
nearby.

So. First house didn’t go well. The entire time there was breathing - in
the walls, as if the house was alive - and a strange infestation of
weird rot in the basement. Regardless, I found a baseball bat. I
wouldn’t be finding firearms any time soon, so I would need to be
careful. I also found a lighter. The rest of the houses weren’t a
problem save for 7 on the left in which I saw something that looked
human but… different. It was made of a substance. Like oil but with no
ooze or dripping, just… plastered to it. So not oil. It seemed like it
wasn’t friendly either. I found a rake and some non-expired food. The
moss seemed to have entered the interior of this house. Though. And the
rot had created a cocoon in the basement of the house as-well.

I’m not going to ignore what happened, but I’m not going to confront It
either yet. It could be that I’m losing my mind in the silence, or just
trying to come up with an excuse to see someone. Regardless, I need to
continue. First. The cinema. Something - no, multiple things - we’re all
stuck into the floor, ceiling, walls. The creatures I saw in the seventh
house. They were all in a half-crouching ball position, with their arms
splayed as if trying to grasp something. I locked the door. I won’t be
going back in there anytime soon. The stores had nothing out of the
ordinary. I decided to set up camp for now in a furniture store as it
would be the warmest and safest as of right now. I boarded myself up
with mattresses and chairs, then warmed up some food on the lighter I
found. It didn’t taste nice, but it worked. One thing I noticed in the
house was that they didn’t have electricity, which begs the question of
why mine did. No water either: weird. I decided to go to sleep.

Shit. Fuck. Somethings outside my fort. I just got up, there’s breathing
outside. Scratching and ripping against the mattresses. I’ve got my rake
raised. Think it’s the thing I saw in the seventh house. Maybe the same
one. I’ll wait out until it leaves.

It’s evening. Finally safe, I think. When I checked outside, there was
rot plastering the whole store. Everywhere. I’m gonna post now. I need
to check on my house and the cinema. I’ll update soon.
