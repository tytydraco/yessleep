I still remember it like it was yesterday.

It was a cold dark night, as most winter nights are. The sun had already
set around 6 and now it was 7.30 i had just finished up with work in my
studio- i work as a photographer but had to stay later then usual that
day because of some missed days and minor technical errors- i walked out
of the studio, shivering as the cold air bit into my skin. I locked it
up then checked the locks twice over before going over to my car and
sitting in it. For the first few seconds I didn’t drive I simply turned
the car and heater on. I scrolled through my phone, i dont remember what
I was checking anymore, all I remember is noticing the time and finally
forcing myself to drive. I usually took a shortcut home, empty urban
roads, empty fields on either side and nothing but the darkness to
accompany me along the way.

I turned some music on as I always did, I passed by a tree that I pass
by everyday. It stood out amongst the other ones due to a red cloth that
had been tied to it for as long as I could remember. I passed by it
once, then again maybe it was just my imagination though afterall its
not like i thought about it much i passed so many i had simply been
mistakened but it came again, this time i slowed down the car but didn’t
stop and surely after a few long seconds i passed by it once more. A
chill ran through my spine, i sat up straighter then I ever had and then
I came round to it again. How was this possible? Its a straight road
there are no turns for me to be driving around in circles. I stopped the
car right in front of that tree and picked up my phone but its screen
was frozen. The song kept playing. It was repeating the part from 00:54
to 1:46 again and again. I hadn’t noticed before but I did now and every
second that went by the song seemed to haunt me more and more. I couldnt
take it anymore so i opened the phone’s back and took the battery out.
Finally the screen turned back, i put the battery back in and tried to
switch it on but i couldnt. All there was now was a black screen and the
silence surrounding me.

I wanted to cry. My head hit the car seat and the phone slipped out of
my hands. For a few seconds i kept my eyes closed and i opened them
again i saw a woman walking towards the direction of my car. She was
still far but i could make out her silhouette from light of the moon-
she was too far for my car headlights to hit her. I felt.. Relieved i
didnt think for a even a second what a woman would be doing alone on
this road that was this dark. I almost got out of the car but my body
wouldnt let me. Something was stopping me from stepping out, all i could
do was sit there and watch as she got closer and closer.

As she got closer something seemed off about her. She didnt walk right..
She walked like a child who hadnt learned how to walk yet… her head
moment was also very weird, it was bent in an odd way and no matter how
hard i tried i could not make out any facial features on her. I couldnt
make out the general shape of her face either! It was as if her face
specifically was surrounded in darkness. The closer she got the more
unsafe i felt. Even though i was inside a locked car i still felt
vulnerable, my whole bodying was shouting at me to just get out! Just
drive off! And so thats what i did, i put my hands back on the steering
wheel and floored it out of there. As i drove past her i didnt once look
at her and i didnt check the mirrors either once i had passed her.

This time the road went on and i finally reached home. When i picked up
my phone again it was turned on and read…11pm. I had left the studio
hours ago and in what felt like an hour 3 and a half hour had passed.
That night i didnt bother eating dinner and simply collapsed onto the
bed.

I have driven through that road multiple times since and nothing like
this has happened since.
