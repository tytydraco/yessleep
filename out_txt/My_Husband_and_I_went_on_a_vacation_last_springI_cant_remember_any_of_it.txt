10/3/2026 11:34 AM

My therapist said it would be a good idea to start journaling to record
my feelings, and help me map out what might be an anxiety trigger. The
past few years of my marriage have been really hard. Not with each other
so much as just circumstances. Normal stuff, like everyone else
experiences. Money struggles, trying to find a house we can afford.
Looking for better jobs. It’s just been a struggle to get ahead. But
luckily for us, this year my husband Jordan has been doing great in his
career. He’s actually been promoted. I’m so proud of him. It’s made life
so much easier. 

Even though I should be worrying less now, it’s like the opposite has
happened. For the last few months my anxiety has been sky high. It
builds up all day long until I can’t stand it anymore, and then I go for
a run so I can sleep. Sometimes that’s not enough and I still wake up in
the middle of the night with my heart pounding, my mind all over the
place. Hopefully this journaling will help.

 

10/7/2026- 7:30 PM

I’m feeling a little off right now so I thought I would take some time
to write out my feelings again. Earlier today I was working on the
credit card statements and creating a big spreadsheet of all our yearly
expenditures, going back several months. In the process I came across a
charge from March for an Airbnb for $227.50. I thought this was weird
because we haven’t gone anywhere this year and I hate to admit it, but I
immediately thought maybe my husband was cheating. My stomach dropped
and I started breathing like I might pass out. But what was weird was
that when I confronted him, he looked at me like I was crazy and said,
“That was for the weekend cabin rental. Don’t you remember?” That’s just
the thing, I don’t remember anything. I have no memory of going
anywhere. He pulled out his phone and showed me a picture of us in front
of a trailhead. I was shocked. I didn’t want to sound crazy so I just
said, “Oh yeah, that. I forgot,” And left the room. But I DON’T remember
going there and staying in a cabin and hiking. At all. 

Later that night I pulled up the Airbnb account and browsed our past
stays. There it was- this rundown cabin up in the hills. I screenshotted
the address and pulled it up on google maps. It’s about 2 hours away,
right at the edge of a national park. 

I just feel so crazy, like I’m losing my mind. I really don’t remember
this. And I don’t know why.

 

10/17/2026- 6:47 PM

I know I shouldn’t have, but I couldn’t stop myself. I kept thinking
about the vacation. I kept looking at that screenshot on my phone. I
pulled up the listing on Airbnb and looked at it several times. It
didn’t have good reviews, and really didn’t look that nice. So why had
Jordan picked this place for a romantic getaway weekend last spring?
None of it made any sense to me. So, when I saw that the cabin had a
free weekend coming up the same weekend Jordan would be out of town for
work, I booked a night there, on my card where he wouldn’t see. 

I waited for him to leave before I tossed a few things in a bag and
jumped in the car. The drive was uneventful, even boring. The roads got
smaller and smaller with fewer and fewer houses around, just miles and
miles of forest. This time of year, the forest is blanketed in fog. You
can barely see twenty feet ahead of you, it really gets that bad. By the
end of it I was creeping along at 20 miles an hour, headlights barely
doing anything. But I made it.

I’m here now.

This place is… very isolated. On my drive in I saw the trail head
parking lot, just a mile or so down the road from the cabin. So at least
there are hikers in the area, but otherwise, this cabin is all off by
itself. Just that alone is kinda creepy. 

Then I went inside. 

It smells musty, the floors are dingy, and there are spiders in the
shower. It’s gross. I don’t even want to look at the sheets. I went all
over the place, just checking it out. You never can be too sure. 

That’s how I found the camera.

My first thought was “Eww,” because what kind of creep puts a camera
inside an Airbnb, and worse, what kind of stuff had it recorded? But
then I wondered if it had captured our stay here a few months ago. 

Maybe it recorded me.

 

10/17/26- 10:34 PM

I don’t even know where to start. First, I’ll just say I did NOT stay in
the cabin, not after I saw what was on the camera. I just got right back
in my car and drove off. I’m not sure I even locked the door. I bounced
out of there that fast. I went to the nearest Holiday Inn Express and I
took a hot shower just to wash the ick off and calm down some. 

I took the SD card out of the camera and plugged it into my laptop
(luckily the card was the same size as the one I used for my old camera,
so I had a USB reader). I’m not an idiot. I have heard about weird
Airbnb owners who record their guests, and I was prepared to see the
worst.

When I pulled the files up on the SD card, I could see that the camera
was motion activated. There were multiple clip files from a single day,
and then nothing for long stretches of time. I did my best to scroll up
to the dates when we were in the cabin and not look at anything, or
anyone else, that had been staying there. That was just too weird. 

Then I found the clips from our stay. All 27 of them.

I started at the beginning. Normal stuff there, 2 second clips of my
husband entering the front door and walking to the bedroom with a
suitcase, followed by a second clip of me following him. (I should
mention the camera view covers the front door, living room area, and
kitchen. I found it on top of a kitchen cabinet).

Several clips of us just walking around the cabin, looking at things.

A clip of us leaving. About three hours later, another clip of us coming
back in. I assume this is when we went hiking.

There’s a clip of us cooking a simple dinner of spaghetti; a clip of us
sitting at the table; and then a clip of me washing dishes.

The next clip I’m still standing at the kitchen sink, looking out the
window. My husband is right behind me. He’s holding something in his
hand- looks like a rock. 

And then he hits me on the head. The clip shows me falling to the floor.

The next one shows him dragging me into the living room and arranging my
limbs.

In the next one, he’s drawn a chalk circle and lit some candles. He’s
reading something- but the audio is so low I can’t tell what he’s
saying. It doesn’t sound like English.

And then- there’s a clip of him LEAVING. He just LEAVES me, laying there
on the floor with the candles burning and everything. I’m lucky the
house didn’t burn down.

The next video is 27 minutes later. I am standing up.

The next clip is 4 seconds long. I am standing, wavering. And talking to
someone. No one is there, no one has come in. The audio is muffled.

The next few clips are basically the same. I am still standing and
whispering. 

In the next one there is a PERSON standing in the living room near me.
They are very dark on the video but there is someone THERE. 

The next clip is just a partial, it shows me again for half a second and
then it cuts to black. There is still muffled audio that I can’t make
out. That clip is 42 seconds long.

Three more clips of blackness, varying lengths, with unintelligible
audio. 

And then- nothing. Not until the next morning.

I’m still in the circle. It looks like I’ve slept there. The front door
opens, Jordan comes in. He goes over to me and helps me stand up. He
walks me out the front door. I look dazed.

I don’t appear on the camera again. He comes in one more time and grabs
the unopened suitcase.

 

10/18/26- 9:27 AM

I took some Benadryl last night and I was able to calm down enough to go

I looked back through our texts this morning while I was drinking coffee

I need help knowing what to do next. My first step is to report the
