Hey Reddit, I don’t usually do this but I’m an exhausted boy-mom looking
for advice and I don’t know where else to turn. 16 years ago, I had an
adorable baby boy. The delivery was super unexpected and it happened on
the side of the road next to a cemetery. Luckily a kind old woman who
just happened to be around just so happened to be an experienced
midwife. The baby was perfect in every way. He was even born with a full
set of teeth. When I turned to thank the old woman, she had disappeared,
but it didn’t matter, all was well and my husband and I took little
Bobby home ready to start our new family. Everything was great.

Weeks passed and it turned out that Bobby was a very hungry baby. He was
a big bitter and after a few too many bloody wounds clean through my
nipples, we tried to wean him to the bottle. That didn’t work either, he
wouldn’t suck, he just wanted to bite and would scream the house down if
he didn’t have anything to eat, physically chomp down on and eat. We
tried everything but as it turns out, only meat would do, the rawer the
better. All the same, it wasn’t a big deal, must have been one of those
new baby cravings, and we started buying him raw steak to eat every day.
He would eat and eat and eat, he didn’t even sleep, he would be either
screaming the house down until the foundations rattled, or eating chunks
of raw bloody meat twice his size. My husband started acting funny, I
don’t know why though, babies were full of unexpected surprises,
everyone knows that.

Well, anyway, a few years passed and the marriage got a little
difficult. Hubbie was always going on and on about how we needed to see
a doctor, that there was something wrong with Bobby, that he hadn’t
grown even a little since the day he was born despite eating five times
his weight every day in meat. I don’t know what the hell had gotten into
him, Bobby just needed a little more protein and he would sprout like
Jack’s beanstalk, this was all very normal, the love of my life just
didn’t understand the sacrifices that parents had to make for their
children. He even stopped holding Bobby just because he had gotten a few
love bites in the process. That’s just teething, I told him, my hands
were wrapped in bandages and I had lost a pinky and some of my ring
finger but you didn’t see me complaining.

I tried to explain it to him but he looked at me like I was crazy, he
looked at the adorable blind naked bundle of cuteness crawling all over
chunks of meat in the bathtub like it was an abomination. It broke my
heart that the two most important people in my life couldn’t get along.
It all came to a head when he found out I had spent all our savings and
gone into 30,000 dollars of debt to buy meat for the baby. He yelled at
me for the first time since we were high school sweethearts and it was
so heartbreaking. How could he not see that we had to feed our baby,
that this was our pride and joy and that any sacrifice was worth it for
him.

He started praying and carrying a crucifix around then he said that he
was going to have Bobby commited, that something was wrong with him and
that people had to know. We had a big old fight over it, how could he
even think of letting anyone take our precious baby away. At some point
I got a little too agitated and my mommy stress got the better of me,
and my sweet handsome hubbie wound up with a kitchen knife in his chest.
It was all very sad. I cried and cried and Bobby cried and screamed too.
I’m sure Bobby was heartbroken too, it wasn’t just the meat cravings
this time, I could tell, a mother can always tell.

Well, I couldn’t let them take me away from my baby, and people were
asking questions, so I decided to do the next best thing, I would have
the two loves of my life as one. I carried my husband’s body to Bobby’s
bathtub and he very solemnly ate him. I helped, and it was a very
spiritual experience too, sort of like eating the flesh of Jesus. We
were broke though and were going to be evicted from our house so Bobby
and I left for an old farmhouse deep in the sticks that belonged to my
old aunt Elvira. We were going to start over together, a mom and her son
against the world.

A few more years passed and we had gotten into a nice groove. At night I
would go out and steal pigs and goats and sheep from surrounding
ranches. Just one at a time of course, and I always went for the ones in
big herds so that they wouldn’t be noticed. The farmers blamed wolves
and mountain lions and started putting up electric fences and keeping
scary dogs around. It didn’t matter though because I could make up for
it by hunting deer and wild boar. I got to be a very good hunter too,
even though I only had 7 fingers and 6 toes left. I was like a lioness
dragging back carcasses for her sweet little cub to grow big and strong.
He was still as tiny as the day he was born but for sure, any day now, I
would wake up and he would be a big strong lion ready to take care of
his momma. On Bobby’s 10th birthday, I shot a big old grizzly bear and
we ate and remembered all the fun times we had had. I showed Bobby my
scrapbook filled with fun family photos of him climbing bloody carcasses
like Simba on Pride Rock while I clapped and cheered in the background.
The bloodier photos were always the most nostalgic ones. Bobby looked
like he was only focused on gnawing on those giant bear bones but I
could tell he was nostalgic too. A mother can always tell.

More years passed and all the farmers and ranchers started to move away.
They talked of vampires and werewolves haunting the area. The animals
also vanished and I kept having to hike farther and farther to hunt.
When I got desperate one day, after Bobby had been screaming for 3 days
straight I did what I had to do and grabbed a sweet farmer’s boy from
the neighbourhood. I admit, it was an unfortunate thing, the poor
screaming little boy, bound and gagged in Bobby’s bathtub, but who
wouldn’t do anything for their precious baby. What else could I do? Well
when children started vanishing, people really got spooked, half the
town moved away and the police started crawling all over the place. One
night after a long hunting trip, I came back at midnight with nothing
more than a skinny fawn to find three police officers in our home, they
had my scrapbook and were flipping through the pages, their faces filled
with terror and disgust. One of them ran back downstairs and collapsed,
rocking on the floor in a foetal position

“I’m not going back up there! Oh my God, I’m not going back up there!”

he kept mumbling, shaking and crying.

They hadn’t noticed me, and I was so mad at they way they were judging
my precious baby and our sweet family photos, I couldn’t help myself. I
raised my rifle before any of them could react, and two of them dropped.
One escaped through the window with a graze, and our irreplaceable
scrapbook but atleast Bobby and I had a full course dinner that night,
with a side of fawn.

We had to run away before the sun came up, nobody would ever understand.
That police officer would tell them all sorts of lies and all our
memories in that scrapbook would be taken out of context. We ran and ran
and found ourselves in an old cottage deep in the woods. We’ve had tough
times ever since, for Bobby’s 15th birthday, we had nothing but a family
of beavers. Hunting is hard as a single aging mother, and I’ve ran out
of bullets. I got caught in a bear trap the other day so I walk with a
limp now. I only have 3 fingers left, just enough to pull a bowstring. I
raid campgrounds sometimes, campers and hikers can be a nice treat,
Bobby loves them. I can never enjoy them though since I always find
newspapers that say all sorts of mean things about Bobby and I. So full
of lies and malice, they just don’t understand us. The world will never
understand the bond between a mother and her baby.

On my last raid I salvaged a smartphone, so I decided to turn to the
internet. Maybe there’s moms out there on Reddit who would understand
what I’m going through. I know that Bobby is due for his growth spurt
any day now, but how much longer do you fellow boy-moms think I need to
wait? Also, do you know of any other sources of protein for a growing 16
year old baby? I dont think he’s malnourished but you can never be too
careful.
