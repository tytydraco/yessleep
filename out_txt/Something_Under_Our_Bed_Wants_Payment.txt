A few weeks ago, I first noticed that something was wrong with Patrick.
He was pale, and dark circles had started appearing under his eyes. When
I asked him what was going on, he said he was just overworked from his
job. For a while, I thought that was really all it was. On top of that,
his dog had died. He loved that dog. It was unexpected because the dog
was still young, and even the vet couldn’t explain what had happened. I
told myself that it was all too much for him, but one evening when I
went into his room, I saw him lying on the floor beside his bed, staring
underneath it.

He was crying and whispering that he was sorry. That he would make it
right and pay.

Then he went quiet and listened, as if someone were talking to him.

“Okay. I’ll pay three times as much. I promise it won’t happen again.”

I couldn’t understand what I was seeing or hearing.

“Patrick, are you okay?”

He flinched, looked at me, and shouted at me to get out of his room,
that I couldn’t understand.

That night, I couldn’t sleep. I read through various discussions online,
trying to find out what his behavior could mean. I learned that it could
be several different mental disorders, but that without a professional,
there was no way to know exactly which one it was. There was one thing
all the articles agreed on, though. This should be dealt with right
away, otherwise it could end badly.

In the morning, I was determined to talk to him. I knocked on Patrick’s
door, but there was no answer. I pressed the handle and opened the door.
He was sitting beside the bed, and there was blood everywhere. There
were pliers next to him, and all of his fingernails had been completely
torn off.

I screamed in horror and immediately ran over to him. He looked at me
and smiled.

“It’s okay, Mom. I paid. It’s fed. I’ll have some peace for a while
now.”

I sat down beside him and told him I had to call for help. He shook his
head in protest. I picked up my phone and was about to call an
ambulance, but he hugged me and started crying. I had never seen my
child in such a state, and I can’t describe the feeling of hopelessness
that came over me.

In the end, we agreed to look for help online. We were supposed to see a
professional the next day. Patrick was given some medication, and he
completely changed. I was so relieved, and I thought that we had finally
gotten through it.

A few days ago, however, I heard a horrible scream coming from his room.
When I ran in, I saw him lying unconscious on the floor beside that damn
bed. There was a meat cleaver covered in blood next to him, and his arm
was gone.

It took me a moment to fully understand what I was seeing. After that,
everything happened quickly. I called an ambulance, and Patrick was
taken to the hospital and then to a psychiatric hospital.

When I was finally allowed to visit him, he hugged me and cried. He said
he was sorry and that this was the last time we would see each other. I
didn’t understand why he was saying things like that. I thought that
since he had already been there for several days, they would be able to
help him, but the doctors said that sometimes it simply took longer.

I’ll never forget what he told me the last time I saw him.

“Mom, I’m sorry, but they won’t let me make the payments here. I talked
to him. He’s coming one last time today, and then… then he’s coming for
the debt from you.”

I tried to stay calm and just reassured him that everything would be
okay.

The next morning, they called me to tell me that Patrick had died. They
found him on the floor with no signs of life.

My life completely fell apart. I thought nothing could be worse, but I
was wrong.

When they performed the autopsy, they told me they couldn’t determine
the cause of death. His body had simply shut down.

A few days later, I started hearing it. First, scratching from
underneath the bed, along with an awful feeling of dread every time I
heard it. Then came the whispering. It wasn’t a human voice. It was
raspy, as if it didn’t belong to our world. And it kept repeating one
single word.

“Payment.”

I went to doctors and was given medication, but nothing helped.

“Fuck your payment!” I screamed desperately one day.

Shortly afterward, my father called me and told me that my mother had
died. Apparently, she had a heart attack. I talked to him for a very
long time, and we both cried. In the end, it was just the two of us.

I sank to the floor and continued silently crying into the silence.

Then I heard that raspy voice from underneath the bed.

“If you don’t pay, I’ll take him too.”

Several weeks have passed since then. I tried. I really tried. My teeth
are gone, parts of my fingers are missing, and so are several pieces of
skin.

But I can’t do this anymore. My father died a few days ago. I know it’s
coming one last time today because I can’t make any more payments. I
don’t have the strength.

If it chooses one of you next, I honestly don’t know what I would tell
you to do. Maybe you’ll handle it better than I did.
