I’m lonely. Not in the sense of “having no friends”—I have people I
could call “friends,” “peers,” or “people who share some of my
interests”, but it feels just like having no one at all.

I always check in on them, always ask if they’re okay, and always try to
find things to talk about, but they don’t do the same. They don’t want
to listen to what I have to say, nor do they want to heed my advice. All
I get from them is indifference. Perhaps I’m the only one who considers
them friends.

I am confused. I’ve tried to be honest and lay all my feelings out for
them, yet they don’t reciprocate. Aren’t “friends” supposed to point out
the flaws of those they care about so they can become better? Why do
they distance themselves from me instead of doing that?

Maybe I should try “creating” friends of my own.

Some say, when your heart feels dim, you should try writing whatever you
want. So, I decided to type out a story in the middle of the night. It
isn’t fiction, it’s all based on reality, though I tweaked a few details
to suit my own wishes.

I created characters based on the friends who had drifted away from me.
Since I didn’t know what their faces or hairstyles looked like—we had
never met outside of online chats—I gave them new appearances. I also
created a character based on myself. I wrote out what the “Me” character
would say to the “Friend” character, and I made them follow my advice.

I’m obsessed. It’s the ultimate form of escapism. I can make anyone do
what I want. I can orchestrate the best scenarios for other people. One
of my friends has abusive parents and has to look after her infant
sibling every day; she kept saying she wanted to die until I got sick of
hearing it, so the “Me” character suggested the “Friend” character burn
her parents alive and stomp her baby sibling to death. That way, she’d
be free. Another friend had financial troubles, so “Me” suggested the
second “Friend” steal from and scam the wealthy. Problem solved.

I still need to get some sleep. I’ll continue tomorrow.

I wake up a bit late since it’s Sunday. As soon as I open social media,
a top post stares right at me, making my stomach churn:

Student of University X Cold-Bloodedly Kills Infant Sibling After
Burning Parents Alive

What I wrote became real.

I felt scared, then that fear turned into a feeling of incredible
relief. Perhaps my second friend was currently shaking down the
businessman who had never cared about him; I just hoped he wouldn’t get
caught.

I went back to typing. Maybe now my friend who was caught is facing
trial or a troublesome interrogation, so I created a scenario where
“Friend” is at the police station, facing the officers together with her
lawyer. Suddenly, the scenario shifted: the “Friend” character was still
in the interrogation room with the police, but a new character, a
“Psychologist”, was there with them.

Maybe I should just leave it be for now, I thought.

My breath nearly stopped when the Microsoft Word document I was using
began displaying sentences I hadn’t typed. Upon closer inspection, the
text turned out to be a conversation—half of which I couldn’t
comprehend. It was all in English, of course, but there were lines that
made absolutely no sense, no matter how hard I tried to wrap my head
around them.

Psychologist: “We’ve investigated your parents’ situation. We know what
they did, and… it must have been incredibly hard for you.”

Friend: “This… this wasn’t supposed to happen… I never wanted this…
Milly… I just wanted it all to end… I don’t know… i-it was like
something had taken over me…”

Psychologist: “We understand.”

I didn’t understand. My friend should have been happy, right? There was
nothing weighing her down anymore. No crying to jolt her awake at night.
No pointless arguments deafening her ears.

Friend: “I-I heard someone whisper… kill everyone, kill your father,
mother, and sister… and—and as soon as I opened my eyes… my feet were
covered in blood… and Milly… and then the smell of Mom and Dad burning…”

Please understand. This is all for your freedom. Don’t cry.

My fingers moved again on the keyboard, forming beautiful words for “Me”
to say, “You’re free now. You can start over. Ask another family to
adopt you, maybe someone will adopt an adult. If they’re mean to you,
you can kill them too.”

Friend: “NO! STOP SAYING THAT BULLSHIT! I DON’T NEED YOUR HELP!”

Fuck you.

You’re no longer my friend.

I wrote, “Then you should just die.”

And the conversation ended. About ten seconds later, a line came out
that was clearly not from my former friend.

Police officer: “Yes, this is Detective Roberts. I need help. Miss *****
Lovelett stabbed herself in the throat with a pen and is now in critical
condition. Send a medic immediately!”

Guilt choked me. My hands began to shake. I didn’t mean to kill her. She
was still my friend, I still loved her. I just want her to be free from
the problems that are preventing her from being happy. I wrote, “Don’t
die. Don’t leave me,” and hoped it wasn’t too late.

I’m writing here to ask you: what should I do? I don’t want to lose my
friend. I don’t want to lose what I should have. I’m working so hard for
her. I just want her to understand.

Please. Listen to me.
