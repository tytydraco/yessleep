It’s late, around midnight I believe. I can only hope my phone picks up
my words right. There’s stinging soap in my eyes. I’m in the shower
alone, or so I thought. Right now I’m leaned forward, palms flat on the
wall and every muscle taut as steel coil. Only the dark–I only see the
dark–only the blackness. Eyes closed–closed–closed.

It’s in the shower with me.
I don’t know what it is. Every now and again, I feel dry fingertips
caress the back of my neck; a craggy corpse texture. Moving up, twirling
my hair.

It doesn’t breathe, or at the very least it doesn’t need to. Cool air
hits my back, made colder by the water running down. It’s pretending to
breathe. I don’t know what it is.

Mistakes, what mistake did I make. Where did I go wrong? I locked every
door, closed every blind, made every prayer. There isn’t one mistake I
made. I can’t make mistakes, I didn’t make a mistake. Whatever’s in the
shower with me– it isn’t my fault.

The smell…
A pestilent rose, sickly sweet and predatory. Primal. Primordial. The
smell of sex, of murder. A pheromonal spiral and Christ now I want to
turn around.

Olfactory cocaine, a single scent sniffed and I’m addicted–I have to
turn around. No. NO. Don’t turn around. I can’t do it.

There’s a dense and muffled crack with every movement it makes like jaws
breaking ribs in the jowls of a carnivore. How abominable can it be? So
horrible that every minor contortion snaps bone. A million demented
visualizations in my mind; is it a person? It can’t be.

The cracking stops. I don’t know what changed but the room seems darker
now; darker despite the fact that I can’t see anything. Was that it?
Killed me and I didn’t even know it?

No, I think the lights went out. It blew them so that I couldn’t see
even if I opened my eyes. Why? Why? Why? Why? Anything. I’ll do anything
to get out. I’ll sell the souls of my wife–kids–mother–father and every
pure person on the planet and damn them to damnation and I’d do it
without a second thought or the smallest twinge of guilt–help me, help
me.

It whispers to me. Solemn sermons made with the sole purpose of getting
me to turn around but I won’t. Speaking to me in a voice that sounds
like nothing at all. Words unfit for my mind let alone my ears. Oh,
that’s– a jarring vibration as I listen, this lingering shock to my
nerves. Try again–NO. Not for me not meant for me can’t be, no no no no
no.

Speaking with a cruel condescension as if I’m not good enough. I’m not
good enough? Oh keep whispering, every inflection in it’s words another
knot inside me and it’s rising with an opera of beauty and disgust and
my brain throbs with every failed attempt to understand–like forcing tar
through a kinked garden hose–that anorgasmic pulsing of my frontal
lobe—endless—fucking endless.

Endless.

Kill me. Don’t save me. Kill me please. I’ll kill everyone to get out.
I’ll kill them all. I’ll do it please leave.

What did I do? What could I have done to deserve this?

Is this it? Can I turn around?

No, I won’t.

No please, please…

I’m in hell.

Help me.
