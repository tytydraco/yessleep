ridiculous smile she always gave me when she knew she was right.

Me: “…Listen, Erica. On a more serious note, I want to say that I’m
sorry about what happened. I hope you can forgive me. I honestly don’t

silence of a dull conversation.

Her: “Of course you’ll be fine, Steven. Because I’m here. I’m still
alive.”

My smile disappeared.

Me: “Wait. What do you mean you’re still alive?”

someone breathing. Then came a soft, wet sound, like a mouth opening
underwater.

Me: “Erica?”
