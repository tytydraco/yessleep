(TW dead animals and death of a child)

she came back to the house from the woods. covered in dirt and blood.

she was brought to the hospital. the doctors said she was okay, just a
fever.

she shouldn’t be.

she cried in my mom’s arms. apologizing for worrying her.

she shouldn’t have.

when she came back to school. she was shy and always stayed to herself,
like she always did.

but she shouldn’t have, she shouldn’t be okay.

She acts weird. Her walk is shaky, she forgets words often, I mean words
that someone really shouldn’t forget. 

Like the other day, she called a dog “the animal that goes woof
woof”.  She even has trouble with pronouns. 

Anyway, the other night. I got up to use the bathroom. And I saw her
standing near my parents bed. While they were sleeping, just staring at
them. 

I nearly pissed myself. I went to the bathroom and she was still there.
I rushed back to my bedroom. Too scared to sleep. 

The next night was even worse because. This time, she was just at the
foot of my bed. Pupils almost glowing in the darkness. 

“What the hell are you doing here?!” I asked. “I’m watching you.
Whatever you’re doing.” she replied in a cheerful manner. 

“Sleeping?!” 

“Sleeping? What is sleeping?” she asked. 

I was baffled. How the fuck do you not know what sleeping is!? “You
know. When you close your eyes and recharge!” I almost yelled. 

“Ooooooh! Yeah yeah I remember! Everyone sleeps! Even me!” she corrected
herself. 

“I know you’re not her!” I finally blurt out. Her eyes dart to me. Even
in the dark, she or rather, it looked, scared and shocked before acting
dumb. 

“Wha-what do you mean by that sis-”

“You don’t have to act. Nothing you do will convince me otherwise.” I
interrupted.

“B-but I am your sister see!” she continued.

I got out of bed. Clearly this thing wasn’t gonna leave until I proved
that I knew its secret. As I put my socks on, the thing pretending to be
my sister asked “what are you doing?” 

“I’m going out. Come.” I answered. It went to it’s room to get it’s own
socks on.

I packed a knife, a flashlight and my dad’s gun. Just in case. 

 We put our coats and shoes on and treaded into the forest as I grabbed
my dad’s shovel. 

It’s hand holding mine, reminding me of the times me and my real sister
used to play in the woods. I tried not to think of that. 

We arrived to the cliff. Went around it to find it. I jammed the shovel
into the ground.

“Stand back.” I told her as I dug. 

I hit a decaying mischief of rats. I threw it at my “sister”. She
screeched and threw it to the ground. 

I dug further, finally, I hit it. I sweeped the dirt away. I told the
thing to come see what was inside.

I turned on the flashlight. The bright light revealed my sister.
The real one. Decaying. Maggots crawling over her. Head still bloodied
from hitting the the ground. 

The thing stared at her in horror. “I-is she-”

“Yes. She’s dead.” I answered somberly. 

I swear the thing almost looked sick. I felt bad for it, whatever it
was. 

“It was an accident. I didn’t mean to kill her!” I Confessed, tears
welling up in my eyes. I started crying. 

The thing stared at me before coming towards me. “Why didn’t you tell
m-mom and dad?” she asked. 

“Then it would ruin everything, if I told them she died. Everything
would change. I don’t want anything to change. I don’t want anything to
change.” 

The thing looked down at her feet. 

I asked her “please don’t leave. I don’t want to be alone…”

since then, she hasn’t left. I help it try to hide better. it almost
feels like the real thing.

last night, we watched The Thing. Its actually pretty nice.

were friends! they’re gonna call the police. They’re gonna find out,
they’re gonna take me away. Its all that thing’s fault!!!
