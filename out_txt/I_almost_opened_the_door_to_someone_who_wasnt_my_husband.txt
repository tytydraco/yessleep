My husband had been away on a business trip for three days. He was
supposed to fly back that evening, and I’d been looking forward to
seeing him all day. I was home alone, watching TV in the living room,
when someone suddenly rang the doorbell. Then I heard my husband’s
voice. “Babe, it’s me!” I remember smiling because I thought he’d
finally made it home. The only thing that seemed a little strange was
that he was ringing the bell. He had his own keys, and he normally just
let himself in. But I didn’t really think much of it. Maybe his hands
were full of luggage or he’d forgotten his keys somewhere.

I got up from the couch and started walking toward the door. I was
already halfway there when my phone buzzed. It was a text from my
husband. “Hey babe, bad news. My flight got canceled. They booked me on
another one tomorrow morning. I’m so sorry. I’ll call you in a bit.” I
stopped walking. For a few seconds, I just stood there staring at the
message, trying to understand what I’d just read. My husband was still
in another city. So who the hell had just called me babe from outside my
apartment?

I looked toward the front door. Everything was completely silent now. I
wanted to believe I’d imagined the voice, or maybe it had been someone
in the hallway talking to another person. But I knew what I’d heard. It
wasn’t just a voice that sounded similar to my husband’s. It was his
voice. The same tone, the same way he says “babe,” even the little laugh
he sometimes makes at the end of a sentence. I slowly walked toward the
door, trying not to make any noise. My heart was beating so hard I could
hear it.

I looked through the peephole. Nobody was there. I checked again,
thinking maybe someone was standing to the side, but I couldn’t see
anyone in the hallway. I immediately locked the deadbolt and called my
husband. He answered from the airport. I could hear people talking and
announcements in the background. I asked him if he was absolutely sure
he hadn’t somehow made it home. He thought I was joking. When I
explained what had happened, he went quiet. Then he told me not to open
the door for anyone and to call the police if someone came back.

Nobody rang the bell again that night. I barely slept. Every little
sound from the hallway made me jump, and I kept checking that the door
was locked. My husband came home the next morning, and I made him show
me his flight details before I opened the door. He laughed at first, but
stopped when he saw how shaken I was.

I still don’t know who was standing outside my apartment that evening.
The thing that scares me most isn’t even that someone sounded exactly
like my husband. It’s how close I came to opening that door. If his
flight hadn’t been canceled, or if he’d sent that message just thirty
seconds later, I would’ve opened it without a second thought. I wouldn’t
even have looked through the peephole. Because I swear to God, that was
my husband’s voice.
