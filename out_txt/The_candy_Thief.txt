I woke up to the ringing from my cellphone. I picked it up groggy and
barely able to hear the other side.

“This better be good,” I complained. 

“Detective we need you at 13th Boulevard there’s been a robbery,” the
voice said from the phone.

I said groggily,” I’m on my way.” I put on my clothes, strapped my gun,
and went out to my car grabbing my hat at the door. I didn’t realize
this day would change my life.

When I arrived there was a couple hugging each other and a little boy
right next to them. I approached them. Brewing up some questions.

“Tell me about what happened,” I stated. 

“We heard objects breaking upstairs and our son screaming,” the dad
replied. “We ran upstairs and when we opened the door our son was curled
up in the corner of his bed crying and as we looked around we caught a
glimpse of someone at the window which was locked before they ran away.
We checked the window and we all went outside to check the roof but no
one was there.” 

“What did the perpetrator steal from you guys?” I asked.

The couple looked at their boy.

 The little boy answered,” It took my candy.” 

“What else did it take?” I interrogated being startled by the fact he
said it. 

“That’s it,” he stuttered. “Please catch that monster.” 

Chills ran down my spine. The window was never opened but his candy was
taken by a monster?

“Where were you guys at the moment,” I asked the parents.

“Watching TV in the living room,” the mother replied. 

“Mind if I look around?” I asked the parents. 

“Please and make sure no one is here,” they replied. 

First I made my way upstairs and checked the rooms up there. Once I got
to the boys room it looked messy. Clothes and toys thrown everywhere. 

I checked the window and saw it was locked. I checked for any candy bags
and didn’t seem to spot any. 

“Did you find any candy?” I asked the police that were investigating
before me.

“No just wrappers, and an empty bag of sugar in the kitchen.” They
replied. 

“Take me to the bag.” I said as he promptly waved his hand to follow
him. 

“It’s weird how clean this house is but a shredded bag of sugar is
emptied here. Not a speck of sugar to be seen.” 

“So whoever is doing this is looking for a sugar high,” I joked. 

The officer scoffed at the terrible joke and then we proceeded to scope
out the rest of the house. 

I thought to myself about the oddity of someone robbing a house just for
sugar and nothing of value. 

As I walked back outside I met with the parents.

“Did you guys use up a whole bag of sugar just barely?” I questioned.

“No,” they replied,” we almost never use our sugar.” 

“It is quite weird that nothing of value was taken, you guys didn’t just
steal your kids candy-“ I got cut off. 

“Sir there’s been a reported murder just down the street.” An officer
exclaimed. 

“In this small town two crimes in a row?” I questioned myself . I
apologized to the parents and quickly finished with telling them to make
sure to lock their doors and all windows and to call me if anything else
weird happened. I got into my car and sped over to the next house
thinking I could solve both crimes by catching one person. 

When I got there the scene was already heavier. The parents were outside
crying as officers had them detained for questioning. 

I got out of the car and asked for a briefing from another officer
that’s told me silently, “Their little girl was found in the bed
completely mutilated. The scene is hard to look at.”

My heart was pounding in my ears when the officer said that. I slowly
turned to head towards the house and I took my hat off to introduce
myself to the couple. 

I then asked for their story. 

They cried and the mother confusingly answered,” We just heard loud
crashes coming from our girls room.” She stopped to sniffle. “We got up
to go see if something fell down or if our little girl fell out of bed,
because she does occasionally.” 

She stopped and cried on her husband’s shoulder. 

“We found our girl dead,” the husband said, barely able to speak. “Find
whoever did this to our girl.”

Hesitant to go look at the crime I walked into the house slowly
following the officer that was questioning the couple. I wish I didn’t
see what was lying behind the door. 
The moment I walked in my body froze and my eyes lost focus. The officer
shook me back to my senses as a tear rolled down my eye. I walked closer
to the body. She was jaggedly cut in half from head down. Her organs
were all visible except for her stomach. I got closer and as I looked
down I was able to catch a glimpse of the floor. Candy wrappers strewn
about on the ground. An empty candy bag. My head spinning as I now knew
this was from the same criminal. 

“Look for any sugar bags,” I frantically told the officer. As he
scurried off I looked closer to see if the girl even still had her
stomach. 

“How sick can someone be to kill a girl for the candy in her stomach.” I
interrogated myself. “Or something.” I looked around the room to see if
there was a break in, but only a locked window. And a trail of candy
wrappers leading to the forest. My jaw dropped as I saw the bread crumbs
laying right before me. I looked into the forest from the window and
noticed a figure slowly moving deeper into the darkness. The officer
came back and mentioned there’s a few sugar bags shredded on the ground
in the pantry.

“Get your flashlight ready,” I said with my head racing and blood
pulsing as my hands throbbed. “I’m on the killer’s trail.” I pointed to
the candy wrappers leading to the forest. We called for some forensic
testing on both houses. Then the officer passed by and the parents told
them to stay put and ran out towards the forest. 

We followed the trail until it disappeared. No more candy wrappers, and
no footprints left behind. The only thing I knew was that a person was
out there with us. We flashed the lights around looking for any more
movement. We took some of the candy wrappers as evidence and a few
pictures and then headed back. 

When we got back we talked to the other officer about the trail and we
decided to do a search that day so we could see better. We told the
parents it would be best to find somewhere to sleep for the night and
left. I was looking forward to the forensics results and rushed to bed
after paperwork. 

I almost didn’t sleep that night. I could only think about how many more
children could end up dead. Most importantly was the killer even human? 

The search the next day turned out to be a dead end so my only hope was
to see if the killer’s DNA turned out. The candy wrappers weren’t in a
line anymore and there was still no trace of anyone in the woods. 

The DNA results came up inconclusive. Only the families DNA was
discovered at the house and the case went cold. I put out a statement
about the Halloween killer, and for anyone with information to send it
to the department. 

I received an email about 6 months later titled The Candy Thief. I
didn’t have a lead for so long so I clicked on it. It was a list of a
bunch of news articles and stories from social media talking about a
person who robbed houses only stealing candy. Each case got more and
more strange. The first ones that happened 10 years ago started with
candy just disappearing. 5 years ago it started to get into sugar bags.
The killer moved slowly. Terrorizing a town for 3 years before moving
on. 
The weirdest part was that it always was on the day of Halloween, and
the first time a death happened was just 6 months ago. 

“So we have a Halloween Candy Thief in our hands.” I said connecting
dots. “Am I really going to wait 6 more months to capture this guy?
Gives me time to set him up then.” I started to dig deeper to see what
patterns there were. The victim was always a child. Oddly enough the
streets always had a number 3 in it. He always seemed to go after sugar
and candy. 

“I feel like I’m missing something,” I muttered. 

I unfortunately had to wait for Halloween where I personally patrolled
every street with the number 3. What happened changed me forever. I was
driving looking for any suspicious behavior while the children trick or
treated. I couldn’t find any clues of someone stalking the children. So
I decided to patrol all the other streets. I was still unable to
perceive any threats so I parked my car until the trick or treaters
slowly dwindled down. 

I picked my patrolling back up and started going down every street with
a three again. Until I saw a large shadow figure in the backyard of a
house. I flipped on my lights as I saw the figure climb up the building
and disappear into a window. I immediately parked by the house and ran
inside after I heard the parents and a deafening scream of a screeching
child. I opened the door to the house, luckily. I followed the parents
running upstairs to their child’s room and I yelled police coming
through. The screeching in the child’s room slowly died down and I heard
a loud thud on the ground. We opened the door and what I saw made me
turn and puke out any bit of Halloween candy I had. As I did, a black
figure ran by and my puke was gone. I followed the figure into the
kitchen as a ripped bag of sugar was left on the floor. I saw the figure
run out the back door and I chased it jumping over the little white
picket fence. It started down the road so I got in my car hoping to
catch up. It all happens in such a blur I barely even saw the creature.
But as I raced down the road chasing it I could see it better. It was
almost humanoid except bigger and taller. It had a bouncing gut. It had
uneven shoulder as if it had scoliosis. It was grey in color and had
flesh falling off of its body. It had minimal hair like a dog with
mange. I called for backup, then I flashed my lights and turned on my
siren and chased the monster. I chased hoping I could catch this
horrific beast. Without noticing I was chasing the thing all the way out
of town. 

“It’s leading me to a dead end,” I muttered to myself.

This creature is smart. It wants me dead. I followed with more caution
ready to stop when I needed to.  Then it stopped and immediately made a
detour for the woods. I stopped with a screeching halt. I radioed the
progress and then got out of my car. I ran into the dark forest beaming
my flashlight at the speedy movement in front of me. Before I knew it I
ran into a rock solid object. As I looked up there were razor sharp
teeth and drool dripping onto my face. The dark red eyes gazing into my
very being. The giant arms that could crush me hanging to its side. I
immediately pulled out my gun and shot at the creature. This caused its
whole body to ripple. It gave an ear breaking screech that sounded like
a monkey mixed with an Aztec death whistle. It also sounded almost
human. It then turned and scampered away into the woods. With the
adrenaline I didn’t realize my left arm was bent backwards and my ankle
felt swollen to my shoe. I got up and slowly limped to the car resting
on every tree I could. I saw more red and blue lights in the woods and
started to yell. After a few minutes two police wandered out from the
trees and helped carry me to the road. 

“It got away,” I managed to tell the others. 

“Let’s just get you back to the road,” the one on my left said.

“What do you mean by.. it,” the cop on my right questioned.

“You’ll see on my camera,” I said as my stumbling got worse. 

We never released the footage. The FBI took it over. No access to any of
its files. We were told to never mention this story and just say the
killer got away. I tried to look more into this creature, but only lead
to dead ends. The closest I could find would be the Wendigo. Not the pop
culture creature with the deer head and antlers. The more humanlike
figure from Native American cultures. It especially had the gluttony of
one. Not of human flesh but of candy. Gluttony so strong it killed
others. Many will write this up to me being crazy. Especially since
that’s all the FBI would make me look like. I’ve quit my job and a new
chief from out of town has taken over. People whisper of the monster,
but it never spreads to close towns. All I ask is to be careful on
Halloween and not eat too much candy. In fact, just keep it downstairs.
I pray, God almighty. You have until tomorrow night.
