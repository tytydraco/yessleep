Hello again! If you don’t remember me, I wrote a story about the time I
was caught in what I think was a time loop. That hasn’t happened again
but afterwards about a week later I was questioned about a
disappearance. Apparently the car that I pulled over to investigate,
yeah…no one knows where they went and they found my finger prints on the
car. So, they questioned me about it, but I told them I know nothing
other than what had happened. So….thats weird but this isn’t about that.
Something just as creepy or creepier has happened. And….im genuinely
afraid of driving to work now.

So as you all know or maybe you’re new, I drive about an hour and some
change to get to and from work. Most of the time it’s a pleasant trip
with no surprises. Minus the time loop and now what has happened just
recently. Honestly, I’d take the time loop over what I’ve been
experiencing now any day of the week.

We’re coming up on the time of year where the temperature fluctuates
wildly from day to night and early morning. Naturally, this results in
fog. No big deal, it’s fog, you see it all the time. Couple rules while
driving in fog, don’t speed unless you want to meet Death really
quickly. And don’t use your god damn highbeams! Can’t stress that
enough, stop with the highbeams while driving in fog. Anyway, I’m
getting off track let me tell you what has been happening.

A few weeks back, I got up like I normally do, pet my dog, feed her some
of my breakfast and tell her to be good while I leave for work. As soon
as I pull out of the garage I look and see fog everywhere, great, it’s
gonna be one of those mornings. I start driving and the fog isn’t too
thick but it feels like it’s just heavy and not moving.

Driving through the city was uneventful. Until I got to the two lane
road out in the country before reaching the highway. There are little to
no street lights out here and the fog seems to be thicker out here than
it had been. So it’s just me driving along with my headlights cutting
what little they can in front of me. Haven’t seen another car for a
while, and that’s nothing new, it is 3am after all. What you do see a
lot though are animals, more specifically deer crossing the roads. With
the fog as thick as it is, I have to remain hyper vigilant. That’s when
I saw them first.

I glanced to my right because I saw something out of the corner of my
eye. I looked and immediately hovered my foot over the brake pedal just
in case a deer decided NOW would be the perfect time to cross. But that
didn’t happen thankfully, but I did see something run deeper into the
fog and out of my sight. I sighed in relief and kept on my way. Less
than a minute later I have to slam on my brakes because something,
assuming a deer suddenly leaps from my left into the road. I come to a
screeching halt and my headlights shine on the creature. Sure enough, it
was in fact a deer. It seems to look at me, as if I inconvenienced it!
After a moment of judging me, he runs off to the right and disappears
into the fog.

I couldn’t help but chuckle to myself about that incident. After the
deer leaves, I continue to drive onwards. Fog just as thick as before,
when I see headlights, sorry, BRIGHTS coming up from behind me and fast.
Not only is this person using their brights be they are speeding. Before
I could react, they cut across the double lines, pass me and speeds off.
I care not, that person wants to drive like that in these conditions let
them.

Not long after they passed me do I see the faint glow of tail lights
ahead of me on the side of the road. I wonder if they hit something and
pulled over. As I approach the rear lights I slow down, hoping not see
someone or something mangled. I do see their car door is open but no one
inside. As soon as I face forward again, I have to again slam on my
brakes. There is a person in the middle of the road waving his arms. I
stop a few feet from running them over, my headlights illuminating their
whole body. I throw my car into park and put my flashers on just in
case, by the time I do that the man is at my driver side window. I roll
it down and ask him if he’s ok, did he hit a deer or something?

The man looks shaken, as anyone who just hit someone or something with
their car. He looks at me and says “I….i think I hit a person. There was
a figure and….fuck man, it darted out in front of me as I got near it. I
pulled over to see what I hit only…..I haven’t seen anything. Can you
help me, I’m so shaken up and I can’t think straight.”

My gut was telling me to leave but my empathetic side was telling me to
help this person. They clearly are in dire straights, so, I say yes I’ll
help and pull over and turn my car off. As I climb out of my car he
thanks me again for the help, he is really rattled by this. I ask him
about where he hit the deer and he points behind us a bit. I then tell
him, just to sit in his car and collect himself for a moment, he nods
and does just that. I approach the front of his car and look it over
real quick, I don’t see any blood or anything but he definitely hit
SOMETHING. There is damage but not much, maybe just some garbage that
fell out or something.

I start walking back to about where he pointed and try my best to see
anything. I can hear the guy sitting in his car breathing heavily trying
to calm himself down. Adrenaline is a hell of a thing. I keep scanning
the road and while walking on the shoulder looking for anything. After a
few moments I do find something. A foot print, or hoof print….something.
I kneel down to look, definitely something ran here, big enough to leave
a deep print in the soft wet morning dirt. It looks like it was coming
from the right side of the road, so from the passenger side of the car
if you were driving. I start to walk out to cross the road to find out.
I get about halfway and I find something, it’s not blood or a body but
some type of inky black fluid. It’s not oil, I don’t know what it is. My
gut says to not touch it, but I ignore that and do it anyway. It’s warm,
and sticky like blood, just not red. I quickly wipe it off on the road
and stand up and move down the road from that spot. If he did hit
something, it might have flown forward.

After walking about 20 feet, I spot something. It doesn’t look like an
animal, it’s black and almost seems blurry. Like it’s out of focus or
something, it’s hard to describe. Almost like something you’d see on a
CCTV footage from the 90’s. My hair starts to stand on the back of my
neck but I press forward closer to this heap in the road. I get about
five feet from it when I see it start to move, I stop and kneel down
trying to conceal myself. It slowly stands up and is impossibly tall for
what it looked like a moment ago. I sat there and watched as this out of
focus completely black thing seemed to stretch its limbs above what I
assume to be its head. As if waking from a nap, that’s when I notice its
yellow eyes looking in my direction. “Does it see me?” I thought, too
afraid to move. The creature takes a step toward me, I fall back on my
ass and continue to stare at it. It stops, makes no noise and then its
inky form begins to shift to match the fog around us and disappears.

I sat there dumbfounded at what I just witnessed. Too scared to move or
even come up with a reasonable explanation. That’s when I hear the guy
calling for me, it’s muffled at first as I couldn’t focus on anything
other than what I just saw. After a moment my hearing comes back and the
guy found me in the road.

“There you are, are you ok? Did you find anything?” He asked walking up
to me.

“I…..I did see something.” I tell him, still sitting in the middle of
the fog drenched road. He walks over to me and offers to help me up. I
take his hand and he asks if it was a person he hit. I tell him no, not
exactly. Then explain to him what I found and what I saw. He looked at
me as if I was crazy.

“Do YOU need help?” He asks while trying to keep his composure. I tell
him I’m fine but, what I told him was in fact the truth. I show him the
inky black spot on the road and take him to the foot prints in the dirt.
He looks back to me afterward and his eyes widen, that’s when I realize
he isn’t looking at me but behind me. I turn to see what it was, on the
other side of the road there are two yellow eyes towering above us in
the fog. They are looking right at us, it seems to study us for a moment
then turns and disappears. We look at each other and confirm that we
both saw the same thing. We agree and run back to our respective cars.

He hops in his and starts it up and speeds off down the road. A moment
later I get in my car and start it up and look forward through my
windshield only to see an enormous hand print on it that wasn’t there
before. I lock my car doors and look around before driving off.

A few miles down the road my eyes are still wide as saucers and my body
is still in fight or flight mode. Currently, flight is winning and I’m
trying to get away as fast as possible.

Little bit later the fog begins to let up and I start to calm down. I’m
still shaken up by what I just saw, but at least I’m not the only one to
have seen it. I come to a stop light and rest a moment. I put my head on
my steering wheel and wait to see the green glow on my car before
looking up again. When I do I keep my foot on the brake pedal. I look
forward and see, multiple pairs of yellow eyes just ahead of me. I feel
myself start to panic but then, my headlights reveal three deer crossing
the road. A huge sigh of relief washes over me as I start to pull away.
I laugh to myself thinking those deer nearly gave me a heart attack. I
look in the rear view mirror and my jaw almost falls off at what I see.
Another out of focus black entity seems to be…..waving at me as I drive
away. I didn’t know what else to do, so I wave and keep driving.

I don’t know what these things are, I don’t think they mean us any harm
but they are terrifying all the same. Since then, I have been hard
pressed to drive through heavy fog or any fog for that matter. I
shouldn’t be, they can’t be real can they? It must’ve been our minds
playing tricks on us……right?
