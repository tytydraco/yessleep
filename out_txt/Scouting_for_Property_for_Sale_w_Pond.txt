So, my roommate and I are looking to upgrade our current living
situation.

My buddy, a gorgeous trout, is in need of much more space than my
current apartment can provide. She’s the greatest life partner one could
ask for. She’s my everything. We’re really hopeful that somewhere out
there someone’s holding the keys to our next adventure!

It’s crazy to think it’s only been a year since we first met.

I was stuck at my soulless job, counting the days until my next round of
vacation escape. I honestly can’t even remember what I used to do there!
I wasted so much time in stupid pencil skirts when I could have been
looking for Trout.

We have the kind of connection that people kill for. Like, I had no idea
what I was missing until I first laid eyes on her.

I was sitting in my car, unaware of reality. It had been raining all day
and I was debating waiting another ten minutes for it to lighten up. I
had just come home from another mindless shift and was looking forward
to nothing more than a half-heartedly reheated microwave meal.

Eventually, I convinced myself that my mythical opening for departure
had arrived and bolted towards the door. I entered just as soaked as I
would have ten minutes prior, but it was the effort that counted. I
exchanged glances and giggles with the guard at the reception desk. I
must have looked like a real fool, LOL!

I shuffled into the elevator and mentally cursed myself out once I
realized the papers in my bag were soaked. My brief moment of happiness
was immediately vaporized with the promise of what tomorrow would bring.

I shoved my way into my apartment, the same one I’ve had since
graduating, and dropped various sopping garments onto the floor on my
way over to the sink. I was mindlessly filling my mug with water when I
finally looked down and made eye contact with her.

It was like my brain finished developing in the span of several seconds.
My startled scream died in my throat as I realized who I was looking at.
It’s Trout! I love, have always loved, Trout. Trout is everything I am.
She has defined the very meaning of my existence.

She was smaller back then, swimming several loops around the sink in
murky water before growing bored. I strained my eyes to the point of
tears just to make sure I never wasted another second without her. I
ordered every book I could find online about caring for fish. Usually I
would’ve gone to the library to spare the expense, but I was worried for
her safety. Who would keep her company if I left?

The next morning I woke up to a phone call from my job that I’d been let
go, but the person on the other end of the phone didn’t seem to really
understand why. I couldn’t have been more relieved, more time with
Trout!

But don’t worry, we can afford any offers for the right place. Trout is
the best provider around! I haven’t had any reason to leave the
apartment since meeting her. Well, besides fetching her fresh water from
the local lake.

It’s a pretty tiresome task though. At first it was pretty easy, just a
few half gallons every once in a while. Even with the elevator, it’s a
whole ordeal. Lately, it’s been at least two back breaking trips a week.
She can’t even fit in anything small enough for me to carry out to the
car anymore. Without her, I feel this deep gnawing itch inside my head.
It makes it harder to focus on bringing back as much water as I ought
to.

She’s always displeased when I’m gone for too long too, like I even have
any reason to leave. She also doesn’t like when I distract myself with
other people’s existences, so we probably won’t be able to formally
discuss a contract in person or over the phone. Physical letters should
be fine though!

For the past few months, I’ve slept on the ground near her so she can
watch me sleep. It’s the only way I can settle down long enough. It also
saves me the trip to the sink when I’m thirsty at night. She says the
water’s good for me! I feel more rejuvenated every night. Most mornings
I wake up plastered to the tank with her face as close as she can get
it.

So, we’re looking for any place that comes with any sort of enclosed
body of natural fresh water. I think it’ll really help improve our
sleeping arrangements and connection. It’ll also make it easier for her
to avoid the sunlight with the deeper depths. Obviously we’d want water
samples sent to us for examination first, but no rush!

She’s said multiple times that she’s worried what all the false water I
had before her has been doing to me. She’s a real worry-wart I’ll tell
you that much, LOL!

In between researching and cleaning the place up, we just live to pass
the time with skin to skin contact and affirmations. She says she’s
never letting go of me and I haven’t felt anything but pure JOY since
then. It’s like my brain is working overtime to encompass her love to
the point of headaches! Hahahaha! :D

Anyway, please consider our generous offer and think about how much
you’ll be contributing to the next chapter of our life!!! <3 xoxo
