My brother told me this story a few years ago, and he still gets
uncomfortable whenever someone brings it up. He was driving home late at
night with two of his friends. They’d been visiting another town and
decided to take a quieter road because it was shorter. It was probably
around 2 AM, and they hadn’t seen another car for at least twenty
minutes. There were no houses nearby, no streetlights, just trees and an
empty road stretching ahead of them.

At some point, they noticed a young woman standing on the side of the
road. She looked completely normal, maybe in her early twenties, wearing
jeans and a light jacket. My brother thought she might be having car
trouble or needed a ride, so they pulled over. The girl immediately
walked toward their car, opened the back door and leaned inside. She
looked at all three of them without saying anything. Then, completely
out of nowhere, she slammed the door and ran away. They were confused,
but one of his friends joked that she’d probably gotten scared after
realizing there were three guys in the car. They laughed about it and
continued driving.

About ten minutes later, they saw another girl standing beside the road.
As they got closer, my brother realized she looked exactly like the girl
they’d just met. Same clothes, same hair, everything. At first they
thought it was some weird coincidence. Maybe they’d taken a wrong turn
and somehow ended up on the same road again. But that made no sense
because they’d been driving straight the entire time. They passed her
without stopping, although all three of them turned around to look.

A few minutes later, she appeared again. This time nobody said anything
for a while. My brother asked the driver if he was sure they weren’t
going in circles, and his friend got annoyed because there literally
hadn’t been anywhere to turn. They were driving around 80 km/h, so there
was absolutely no way a person walking could have gotten ahead of them.
They started looking for another car, thinking someone might be playing
a prank on them, but the road was completely empty. The girl wasn’t even
trying to wave them down anymore. She was just standing there, watching
their car approach.

They saw her again a few minutes later, and then once more after that.
Five times in total, always the same girl, always standing alone beside
the road. My brother said the worst part was how normal she looked. She
wasn’t covered in blood, wasn’t smiling strangely or doing anything
particularly frightening. She simply kept appearing in places she
couldn’t possibly have reached. By the last time, nobody was laughing or
trying to come up with explanations. They didn’t even slow down. My
brother said they drove the rest of the way in complete silence and only
started talking again when they reached a town with other cars and
people around.

They dont stopped for her again because they were scared shitless.
