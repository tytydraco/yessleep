I never was able to have a place to crash. Last landlord decided to
evict me unless I pay for damn holes in the wall that were already
THERE. Before that my parents charged me rent. Fuck parents that do
that!

Well it seems most of my family can’t stand them for related reasons.
Well my sister was one of them. I was on the path to a shelter and got
so desperate I called her up. She by some miracle not only answered but
even had a place for me.

“Yeah it’s a studio I use every blue moon. I would be happy if you could
use what it was made for and have yourself a home.”

She told me. I thanked her and she offered to show me around since she
wanted to pick up some art supplies to get out of the way for me. I
thanked her again and said I couldn’t wait.

It was a small little cozy brick walled place on the curb. She showed me
around but made sure to point out the door’s unique lock system so I
could get used to it. It has a slide lock for added security given (at
least in the past) it used to be a pretty shady area. So you must slide
it and that means it can be done only on the inside. Thus if someone
picked the lock of the knob they still could not get in.

It kind of worried me but she assured that it’s not necessary anymore
given the area has cleaned up. But being she is an artist, she defended
it claiming it added character to an otherwise minimalist place. I
agreed and she picked up her supplies but did tell me she may be back
for more in the morning. She went on her way as I shut and locked the
knob and the latch.

That night I was asleep and I had that usual insomnia when you are just
settling into a new bed in a new location. So I was still up past
midnight. During another session of me tossing and turning to find a new
spot in the bed for me to hopefully fall asleep in. I heard something.
It was like the most vague sound of presence. It was like a creak of
some kind. But then I started to hear footsteps.

I was at first unnerved, before remembering my sister did say she would
come by to pick up her supplies left behind. However she said that would
be in the morning not while I am here trying to sleep. What was odd most
of all is I heard no sound of the front door opening either. The
footsteps stopped and there was a metal clack sound. My heart was
pounding and I heard mumbling of some kind. But maybe it is just her
rambling, artists do that I guess.

I tried to go back to sleep. After all she might have just snuck in
really well and wanted to be out of my hair faster. How nice of her! She
was always good at Hide and Seek when we were kids before the divorce of
my parents and mom took custody. Eventually I finally fell asleep.

That morning I received a text from her that she was coming to pick up
her supplies. I replied saying that’s cool and she came over. She
knocked on the door and I opened the door unlocking the knob. We talked
a bit but it was obvious she was in a rush for something. She told me
she just wanted to get back into painting as soon as possible. I was
happy for her and she left shortly after.

After I had shut the door and locked the knob I went to lock the latch.
Then that is when I realized it was already unlocked when I went to let
her in. My blood went cold and I texted her if she came by last night
when I was trying to sleep. She sent a question mark replying,

“I was asleep last night.”
