Update: I Went Into the Storage Attic Above My Apartment, and I Wish I
Hadn’t

Hi everyone, it’s me again. I didn’t expect to come back here so soon,
but what happened yesterday made me feel like I couldn’t keep this to
myself.

After posting my first story, I spent some time reading your comments.
Some of you suggested that the noises might be coming from the pipes,
while others told me to speak to my landlord. Honestly, I was hoping it
was something ordinary.

Yesterday afternoon, I called my landlord and asked if I could go into
the old storage attic above my apartment. He hesitated for a moment
before telling me that the door had been locked for years and that there
was no reason for me to go up there.

That answer made me even more suspicious.

I asked him to come and check the noises himself, but he said he was
busy and would talk to me later.

That evening, I sat in my living room, listening for any movement coming
from the ceiling. At around 11:30 p.m., I heard slow footsteps in the
hallway upstairs.

Then I heard a door opening.

I hurried up the stairs, but when I reached the upper floor, nobody was
there. My neighbor’s apartment door was closed, and the storage attic
was at the end of the hallway.

What shocked me was that the attic door was slightly open.

I was certain my landlord had told me it had been locked for years.

I don’t know what made me do it, but I walked over and slowly pushed the
door open.

The room smelled of dust and dampness. I turned on my phone flashlight
and saw some old chairs, cardboard boxes, and furniture covered with
white sheets.

Everything looked normal until I noticed something strange.

There was a small table in the middle of the room, with a glass of water
sitting on it. The glass was still almost half full.

It was clean, with no dust on it at all.

Before I could figure out what that meant, I heard a voice behind me.

“What are you doing here?”

I turned around quickly.

My neighbor was standing in the doorway.

He didn’t look angry, but his face was pale, and his eyes were fixed on
the glass sitting on the table.

I told him I’d heard the noises and just wanted to find out where they
were coming from.

He remained silent for a few moments before saying:

“You should go back to your apartment, Safaa.”

I felt a chill when I heard him say my name. I couldn’t remember ever
telling him what it was.

Maybe he’d seen it on one of my letters, I told myself.

But before I left, I noticed an old photograph hanging on the wall
behind him.

It showed the same apartment building, although the photograph looked
newer than the other things in the attic.

I moved closer and saw a group of people standing in front of the
entrance.

My neighbor was one of them.

The same face, the same hair, even the same expression.

The problem was that the date written beneath the photograph was from
thirty years ago.

I stared at the picture, then looked back at my neighbor.

I didn’t say anything.

Neither did he.

He simply stood in front of me before closing the attic door.

I returned to my apartment without speaking to him again.

Since yesterday, I’ve been trying to find an explanation for that
photograph. Maybe the date was wrong, or maybe the man in the picture
was simply someone who looked like my neighbor.

But this morning, I found a folded piece of paper underneath my
apartment door.

There was no name or signature on it.

Just one sentence, written by hand:

“If you want to know who lives above you, don’t ask the landlord.”

And now I have only one question: Why is my neighbor so afraid of me
finding out what’s inside that attic?
