Please believe me here. Ive never experienced anything as scary as this.
Nothing can compare to the rabbit hole of terrors this house had in
store.

I’ve never shared this story online before, but it’s been exactly ten
years since it happened, and him reminding me of it makes me want to
relive the dread.
Back in 2016, my friend—I’ll call him Leo—and his family moved into a
massive, gorgeous Victorian house out in a remote, rural town in North
Dakota. From the outside, the place looked like a total dream home.
Clean paint, huge wrapped porch, beautiful yard. But the moment you
stepped inside, the energy completely shifted. The layout was just…
wrong. It was a chaotic maze of dual hallways, weird angles, and
strange, useless crawlspaces. Later, we looked into the property records
and found out the place had cycled through at least fifteen different
owners in just a few decades. Nobody ever stayed for more than a year or
two.
A few weeks after they moved in, Leo and I were exploring the house,
looking through old built-in cabinets to see if any of those past owners
had left anything behind. In the laundry room, there was a stack of deep
wooden drawers. When we pulled the bottom one all the way out, we found
something that didn’t make physical sense.
Flushed into the bottom of the drawer cavity was a rusted, heavy metal
pipe, about six inches wide. We shined our phone flashlights down it,
but the beam just dissolved. It looked like it dropped straight down
into a total, bottomless void. Intrigued, we went outside, grabbed a
medium-sized rock from the yard, brought it back in, and dropped it
right into the center of the pipe.
We stood there and waited. Five seconds. Ten seconds. Nothing. No thud,
no splash, no echo. The darkness just swallowed it completely.
After that day, the house got worse. Leo’s parents started seeing dark
silhouettes out of the corners of their eyes. Doors would unlatch on
their own. While mapping out the hallways, we actually found a tiny,
hidden room behind a false wall that contained nothing but an old iron
cage and a single, thick bone resting on the floor. The air in the house
always felt freezing and heavy, like the rooms were constantly holding
their breath.
But the absolute breaking point happened on a Friday night. Leo and I
were back in the laundry room, completely silent, just staring at that
open drawer. Suddenly, a sound drifted up from the depths of the pipe.
It wasn’t a draft, and it wasn’t a pipe creaking. It was a laugh.
It wasn’t a playful, high-pitched horror movie laugh either. It was a
deep, guttural, wrathful boom—incredibly faint, but loud enough to
vibrate the metal rim of the pipe. It sounded completely ancient, and
completely inhuman.
We packed our bags and his entire family left the house that exact
night. They put it on the market immediately, practically giving it
away. Within a month, a commercial developer bought it to flatten the
land and build a roadside motel.
But right before they handed over the keys, Leo went back to grab the
last of their boxes. Hidden in a false panel in that same laundry room
cabinet, he found a crumpled piece of paper left behind by one of the
previous tenants. He took a picture of it before destroying the physical
paper.
The note read:
“Please, for the love of God, do not investigate the pipe in the
cabinet. Our entire family has been tormented by the horrors of this
house, it is without a doubt haunted. However the pipe drawer is the
most haunting rabbit hole we have encountered. i will spare you most of
the details, but it leads down into a cellar, with the most horrifying
things we have ever seen, and there is a hatch in that cellar that leads
to a seemingly endless concrete stair spiral. everything down their is
downright demonic, and there were things actively watching us in that
room. the pipe is the root of it all.”
Later on, Leo’s dad did some digging and found out that one of the
previous owners had actually discovered that cellar years before us.
Apparently, they got so terrified of whatever was down there that they
brought in cement trucks and poured tons of solid concrete directly into
the void to seal it off from society forever.
That means when Leo and I heard that faint, wrathful laugh… it was
echoing through solid concrete.
