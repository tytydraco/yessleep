Hello.

I want to start my story with this. I’m not sure how much of it is real.

So, first off, last year I was diagnosed with early onset Parkinson’s. I
only mention this because some of the symptoms include vivid dreaming,
which I’ve always had, and possible hallucinations. Hmm.

I work in Brisbane, Australia and go home to the farm in the South
Burnett each weekend. Its about 250 km away, so its alright for a weekly
commute. I sleep in my car on my work nights. Its in a technology
estate; the same as an industrial estate except its not industrial and
there are no night shifts.

I park under the awning at the end of our driveway on the right, before
our two, forty foot storage containers and while its visible from the
road, I get no visitors, apart from the odd drug deal or domestic
dispute, and they’re few and thankfully very far between.

We start at six am so I usually leave about quarter to three on the
Monday mornings, to get to work in plenty of time. There isn’t much on
the road, just a few supermarket trucks and fuel tankers coming the
other way. So far I haven’t hit a kangaroo, as its a bit too early for
them and I’m down in the city by sunrise. Or I’ve just been lucky.

I’ve usually got my phone in the cradle with blue tooth headphones on,
since my stereo is pretty defunct, and I’m listening to metal bangers
for white noise. I thought I’d change it up two weeks ago; I was playing
an indie horror audio podcast that a mate, (lets call him X) had put me
onto instead. Changed his life, he said. Rewired his brain, he said.
Scariest thing he’s ever done, he said. I laughed. Its been done to
death, cursed tapes and malevolent spirits; we’ve all seen the films.
But then he dropped off the radar. Completely.

We’re all in our late fifties and well settled, so this was completely
out of character and his wife was freaked. What’s a bloke to do? I was
raised religious but I’m a staunch atheist now. I don’t believe in god,
ghosts or ghouls and I relish my skepticism. Of course I downloaded it.

I start the podcast as I’m leaving my driveway and there’s some
background music. The narrator sounds like he’s sitting in a tin shed.
He’s droning on about ancient unkempt spirits and the like and my
attention is waning. I’m considering pulling over to throw some music on
and then he says and I quote, “would you like to hear from some of our
past participants, Jason”.

That’s me, obviously, and I’m at a loss. That’s a pretty nifty trick.
I’m obviously not buying it, so I wait and the music stops. There’s just
some slight feedback and a muffled bump like someone tapping the mic.
Then, “Hello mate. I think I might have made a bit of a mistake here.”
Its unmistakably X, “can you do me a favor and tell (his wife) I’m
sorry. Tell (their son) I love him and not too get up to too much shit.
Sorry to drop this on you mate.” He’s gone. So, hallucination? Has to
have been.

I rip the headphones off and throw them on the passenger seat, reach
over for the bottle of turkey, pull the cork and have a slug. Yeah, not
the smartest thing, but I’m a little freaked. That has to have been in
my head, because the alternative doesn’t bear thinking about.

It had been raining on and off since I left and now the moon was poking
through the streaky clouds. The atmosphere was pretty good for freaking
me out. As I drove it felt like the trees on either side of the road
started to close in and reach down. I swear I could hear them brushing
against the doors.

There were faces in them, blending and morphing together like the
monsters from my childhood bible stories, reaching down to get at me
through the windscreen, and drag me off to a dark wasteland devoid of
any love or warmth or sunlight. Over my life I have been vigilant in
holding myself in check and have a pretty good hold on reality, but at
that moment? It was slipping.

By now I was about an hour into the trip, which means driving down the
Blackbutt range, between the towns of Blackbutt (who would think) and
Moore. The road used to be a bit rough, but the council has done a ton
of work and now there are no slips, and the road is good. I came down
the range too fast, with nobody around and in the pitch dark. The moon
was gone again. It was like a black soup with no boundaries. I slowed
down a bit because I had a bad feeling I was going to make a panic
induced mistake. A wooden cross and some plastic flowers weren’t on my
bucket list.

My breathing exercises seemed to be working and I was feeling more in
control, until I looked in the mirror at the back seat and there’s a
medium sized dark figure in a cloak. I laughed in my head thinking,
“what, did you come with the podcast?

Then I noticed the small black dog, just like X’s, that died last year.
Without any reason I said, “who’s a good girl B” and she attacked the
figure in the cloak. A shriek came from my useless stereo that filled
the car and then I swear, on my life, the figure came at me and soaked
through my skin. I felt a searing cold, a putrid bitterness in my mouth
that almost made me retch, and then it was gone. And the ghost dog? She
was sitting there still watching me. So, again, hallucination?

Then, as if the morning couldn’t get any weirder there’s an amber glow
up ahead and that’s when I saw the small fire burning brightly on the
curb of the fourth inside turn. The barriers were all intact with no
sign of skid marks. It wasn’t a bush fire. but it was really going. I
slowed down some more but didn’t stop. It’s ten kilometers either way to
the nearest town and its not somewhere you would walk. So, all things
considered, I’m more than a bit freaked, again.

And now? Just to tip me right over the edge, because that’s what I need,
who do I see, or imagine, squatting on his haunches next to the fire?
Just my close friend, P, that I used to play footy with back in New
Zealand, still looking like he did thirty years ago. I shook my head and
looked again and there was nobody. So, I imagined it as well, right?
Well I had to have, because he died in a forestry accident twenty five
years ago.

My sense of adventure had evaporated and my ability to laugh at myself
was gone. I got to the bottom of the range and floored it. It felt good
to go fast, like in the old horror films when the protagonist tries to
outrun the devil. Ghost riders in the sky, came into my head and it
stayed there, until I pulled over by the toilets at Moore, under a
streetlight and put my screamo playlist back on. With the white noise I
started to relax.

Okay, that was Monday morning. I’m always tired on Mondays, because its
a long day, so I easily slept straight through the night. Now, I’m in a
sedan with the passenger seat laid down and a couple of pillows and
doonas; I’m pretty comfortable. I have my door ajar since my car’s
control module glitches. Basically, the car locks by itself sometimes,
nothing freaky and I know why, but its a nuisance when you’re inside it.

So, Tuesday, I shower and do the normal day. I finish work, do a workout
across the road at the gym and have a couple of beers. Go to sleep and
then wake up about two. The place is completely silent, which is odd
because there’s usually a bit of background noise from the motorway
about a kilometer away, or some wind, but its really dark, dead calm and
silent. I feel weird, but its all in my head, right?

Then I hear music; an Irish pop band, The Corrs. I think about P,
because he loved them, and even though its been years, I miss him. I
hadn’t thought about him for a while because what good would that do me?
I can’t bring him back, so its better to focus my energy on the things I
can influence, right?

I drift back now with the music to when I knew him. A lot of people died
in that town we lived in. Forestry accidents, farming accidents,
speedway accidents, drunk driving accidents involving both the innocent
and the guilty. A lot of people died and it’s him I miss the most. The
local Maori said it was tapu before the Golden Downs forestry built it,
impure. We all said it was mumbo jumbo. Imagined. Anyway…

I closed my eyes, did my breathing exercises and the music stopped. I
went back to sleep. I was instantly back the morning before, driving
down the range, but this time I did a u turn and stopped by the fire.
He’s there, standing at the passenger door. He’s grinning, like,
“surprise, bloke!” and goes to open it, without saying anything. But I’m
feeling sick and cold and dark again, like he’s a threat, so instead of
saying, “bloody hell mate, been a while”, I freaked, once more. Peeling
out, I booted it, fishtailing down the range.

Then, as they say in the movies, I woke up, caked in sweat. I jumped out
of the car and walked around the car park until I got my breathing in
check. The exercises wouldn’t work. I mean we both knew he was dead.
Irrelevant. We were tight and it should have filled me with warmth to
see the scoundrel again, but it didn’t. It filled me with dread and that
started to worry me. Why on earth would I fear him? So for the last
time? Hallucination? Shit.

Wednesday? I slept straight through, with the aid of a few more beers,
but had the very worst dreams. Like I said at the start, they’re always
vivid but these were completely unhinged. I won’t delve into the details
but my body revolted so badly that there was a tidy pile of puke outside
my car that I had to hose away before everyone else got to work.

They say you can never visualize true horror, that its conceptual.
Bullshit. I felt like I’d been through a demonic washing machine inside
out and I don’t believe in any of that shit that I dreamed so where did
it come from? Something had changed inside me and it wasn’t good.

Thursday? He’s back in my dreams again. This time, in the dream I pull
up at work very early on the Monday. It’s only quarter to five so I hop
in the passenger seat, kick my feet up on the dash and browse Reddit. I
find a sub talking about this podcast so I browse it. Then I hear X in
my head again,”hello mate. You know you can’t run from this. There is no
escape now. Just embrace it, come to the dark.” its so clear that I
swear he’s in the car with me.

I look over my shoulder and there he is. But it isn’t him. Not the real
him. There are sores all over his face and his teeth are falling out as
he speaks. He looks at me and for a second I see my mate inside those
eyes, screaming in torment.

In a flash the back door flies open and P is there, glancing at me
before he drives a knife into X’s chest. My car speakers shriek again as
X disappears leaving the amorphous patch of darkness shimmering before
it rushed me, overpowering me. For the briefest moment I heard his
voice, “Goodbye mate and I am sorry, yeah?” I’m off again in forever
land.

Instead of my usual vivid dreams I’m in the darkest depraved corners of
some hellish insanity. This isn’t me. Apparently, more accurately, this
wasn’t me. Then, P is outside the car and while I feel revulsion from
whatever has infested me, the part of me that’s still me smiles. He
looks sad but he nods. He starts to open the door and I look up at him,
waiting, for the same release he gave X. Then a workmate turns into the
drive way and his headlights wake me up, shaking and caked in sweat
again. Frustrated and disgusted.

So that’s my story so far. I was scared but not so much now. If this is
actually happening I’d rather be disposed of, and if its purely
hallucination, then I guess I’m going to be spending my heavily sedated
future in a rubber room. I just wanted to share and warn anyone who
could make my mistake, unless this is all in my mind.

Thanks for reading.
