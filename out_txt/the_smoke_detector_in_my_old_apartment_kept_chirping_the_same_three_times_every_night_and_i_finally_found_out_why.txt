so this was two apartments ago, I lived alone in this studio for about
eight months before i moved. nothing special about the place except one
thing that bugged me the whole time i lived there

the smoke detector in the hallway (shared with my neighbor’s unit, it
was one of those old buildings where two doors share a little vestibule)
would chirp. not the normal low-battery chirp, i know what that sounds
like, this was different. three chirps, exact same spacing every time,
then silence for hours, then three chirps again. always around 2-3am. i
changed the battery twice. didn’t stop. i had the building super come
look at it, he said the unit was fine, tested it himself, no issues,
“sometimes they just do that.” cool, great, very helpful

i started sleeping with earplugs just to deal with it and mostly forgot
about it, until one night i woke up without my earplugs in (i must’ve
taken them out in my sleep, still don’t know why) and i heard it happen
live for the first time in months

three chirps. and then, right after, so quiet i almost thought i
imagined it

a voice said “almost”

i sat straight up. told myself i was half asleep and my brain was doing
pattern-recognition on white noise, that’s a real thing that happens, i
looked it up the next day to reassure myself, it’s called auditory
pareidolia, totally normal your brain does it to ambiguous sound

except it happened again like four nights later. three chirps. then
“almost.” same flat tone both times, not urgent, not scary sounding
even, just matter of fact, like someone reading off a countdown

i finally asked my neighbor about it, the guy who shared the vestibule,
if he ever heard anything weird from the detector. he got really
uncomfortable and said he sleeps with noise cancelling headphones every
night and has for years and wouldn’t say why when i asked if that was
because of the chirping

i moved out four months later for unrelated reasons, better apartment
came up, nothing dramatic. i didn’t think about the smoke detector again
until last month when i ran into my old super at the hardware store and
asked, mostly as a joke, if the chirping thing ever got fixed for
whoever lives there now

he got quiet and said the unit’s been vacant since i left. nobody’s
rented it. when i asked why, since it’s a decent apartment in a decent
building, he just said corporate “took it off the market” after the last
two tenants both broke lease early within a month of moving in, both
citing the same reason on their forms

both of them wrote “the countdown finished”

i didn’t ask what that meant. i already knew i wasn’t going to like the
answer, and some nights i still catch myself listening for three chirps
in the quiet of my new apartment, even though there’s no detector
anywhere near my bedroom to make them
