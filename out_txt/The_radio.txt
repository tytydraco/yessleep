I(22F) always loved having old/vintage things, i would go in abandoned
houses and find whatever I could. That changed last month, when I was
exploring a house that was built In 1935, I found this old rackety radio
that still somehow works, it was mostly static so I kept it as
decoration on the shelf in my living room with most of my vintage stuff,

until a few weeks ago I heard a word, “hello!” In that fake television
voice from the mid century, and during that time period more and more
broadcasts started to happen, most of them were just talking about the
weather or stuff going on, but in the past few days it’s gotten
creepier,

It would say stuff like “please” in a tone that sounds like whining in
pain and then static sounds. I never really minded it, honestly I liked
being on edge, until recently, i have a great pyrenees named goose. and
three days ago I heard the radio make a barking sound and then a whining
noise, that’s Goose’s exact tone.

Panicked, I ran into my room where Goose was sleeping on his bed, I
must’ve startled him because he got up and licked my knee, I gently
grabbed his collar and took him into the living room and turned off the
radio and sat on the floor and cuddled him for a whole hour

until my fiancé(24M) Uran, came home, worried, he asked what happened
and I told him nothing happened and said I got scared, he just chuckled
and embraced me and Goose. yesterday I turned the radio on and I heard
my fiancé laughing at my joke and saying “SHUT UP BABY!” In a playful
tone,

but when I was about to unplug it he, or it, said “Don’t.” In a flat
tone, I got filled with dread and unplugged it, but then my Alexa
started playing some old-timey Jazz song and a man singing “baby you can
listen to my radio ~ make my dreams come truee~ I’d fall in love with
you!”

then the radio came back on, still unplugged, singing the same song but
different lyrics, “baby I know you hear me~ just tune my channel on And
sing that pretty song.” I was shaking in fear and it kept getting louder
and louder until I screamed “STOP IT!” And fell to my knees it had
finally stopped,

Uran came back in from walking Goose and found me crying on my knees
with my hands over my ears, and he leaned down, kissed my forehead,
lifted my chin, and said “baby what’s wrong?” Tears welling in my eyes I
threw my arms around his shoulders and sobbed.

Uran didn’t question anything and just wrapped his arms around my waist
and whispered something comforting, I didn’t hear it though, I was to
busy Bawling my eyes out.

And today was the last straw. I was feeding Goose and the radio said
“come here Goose!” In my voice. Goose started walking over and I YANKED
his collar back and put his leash on and tied him to his food so he
wouldn’t go and he’d just eat his food

I called Uran and told him about the radio and why I kept breaking down,
and he just said “okay, I’ll just order a hotel room, I believe you.” I
sighed in relief and hung up. I’m now writing this in the hotel room
right now. Uran is sleeping next to me, Goose is on top of him, I can
hear the jazz song again, I promise I’ll update soon, goodbye for now.

Edit: it’s been two months since all that happened.
