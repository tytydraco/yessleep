I do not know how many darknesses came and went. I do not know how many
kills. The hole was never full. The hole was never empty. And then, one
day, I remembered. I have sat in church many times. I have heard the
preacher say how dangerous Hell is. The preacher said there would be a
lake of fire. He has talked about eternal darkness. The weight of our
sin would crush us. I wish I’d listened. I chose the life I had. I chose
work over home. I chose the bottle over my Naomi. I wanted to fill the
hole. The hole was so big. My soul calls out.

Then there was a movement. Bigger than the last one. My body moved
before I could think to stop. I jumped on it; I bit at it until it
stopped moving. The taste of blood was repulsive. I wanted to stop but
the hole was so big. The creature cried out. My body enjoyed the taste
greatly. I fought against it but nothing changed. I could feel every
crunch. I could feel my jaw breaking through its bones.

One of the others like me appeared. I could smell it before I could see
it. Could I experience the warmth again? The other started getting
louder. Started getting bigger. I was afraid. I don’t want to die. I
think it wants what I am eating. THIS IS MINE!! The other lunged at me.
I felt a heat over my face. My body was already attacking back. I tried
to run. I tried to just escape but my body stayed and fought. We clashed
and bounced off of each other. I need to fill the hole and this other
will not stop me from filling it. Suddenly, my teeth were around its
neck. PLEASE NO! I don’t want to kill. My body ripped its throat out. I
didn’t want to die. I wanted to fill the hole. This blood tasted like
acid in my mouth. I finally returned to my meal. My horrid meal. What
would she think of me? After eating, I returned to the darkness.

Where there was heat yesterday, today, there was cold. I could not see
through one of my eyes. My soul ached for the fallen. My body moved on.
The hole was smaller but not gone. I could smell more of what filled the
hole before. My nose led me but there was nothing to find. There were
many times that I entered the darkness while the hole grew bigger. How I
missed the source. I would see a movement, but it would be the wind.

It was getting harder to find warmth. I would find areas that I could
find enough warmth to fall but never enough. The cold was everywhere. It
hurt my body. During my trips to the darkness, I would see her hands.
She would turn toward me but before I would see her face, I would be
pulled from the darkness. I would wake up in the cold. Will I ever know
the shade of her eyes again?

I smelled something so strong that my body grumbled. I look and see the
shadow that I can almost make out. These eyes didn’t have the love I
expected. They were filled with hate. I moved in to get my fill. As I
approached, the eyes sharpened. They were wrong. I never knew these. The
creature grew to an impossible size. Please, I don’t want to die.

The creature moved faster than I could be ready for. Snapping his teeth
at me. A scream came from deep down inside of me. All of the hairs on my
body stood straight up. My fingernails became hot wires. I jumped to the
side, nearly being bitten. I could smell the decay on its breath. The
creature and I stood opposing each other. Could I run? Could I make it
away? I saw my moment. I could see an opening behind the creature. My
body moved, my soul yelled with excitement. I didn’t consider my blind
side. I felt teeth dig into my skin. The creature grabbed me and threw
me in the air.

I was almost home. It wasn’t that many drinks. I have driven this way
many times, much drunker than this. When that damn opossum… or was it
something else, ran across the street, I couldn’t just kill it. When I
hit the tree, I felt the glass shatter on my head. I felt the wind
rushing by me. Am I a bad person? Will I ever see my Natalie? I will
probably go to Hell. When I landed, I used all the energy I had to run.
The creature wasn’t interested in me anymore. Oh, how I miss the source.
Is this what happened to the source? Is this what happened to the
others? I hid then and I hide now.

The darkness came a little harder this time. My body ached. My soul was
terrified. Every breath was harder than the last. Why did the creature
hate me so much? The little ones with a bunch of legs move slower than
before. They didn’t fill the hole, but it was enough. There was this
smell around me that followed everywhere I go. Is death better than
this? Would I rather be in hell?
