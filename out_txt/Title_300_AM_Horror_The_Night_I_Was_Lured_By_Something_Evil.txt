Trigger Warning: Psychological Horror, Paranormal, Sexual Distraction

​It was August 2nd, around 1:30 AM. I was stuck in a nightmare so vivid,
I can still feel the cold sweat on my neck.

​In the dream, I had to carry something urgent from my old house to my
uncle’s house. It was 3:00 AM in that dark, dead night. The air felt
heavy, like someone was breathing right down my neck. I was terrified,
running as fast as my legs could carry me, holding the package tight in
my hands.

​Then, things got deeply unnerving.

​As I took a turn on the deserted street, I saw my father sleeping right
on the side of the road. On top of him was a massive blue drum, filled
to the brim with dirty water. Sitting right next to him was my uncle,
staring blankly into the dark.

​Before I could process that cursed scene, I saw it.

​A tiny toddler, barely 2 or 3 years old—someone who shouldn’t even be
able to walk properly—was sprinting towards me from the pitch-black end
of the road. In the middle of the night. Completely alone. Its face was
twisted in an unnatural way. Fear surged through my veins. As it reached
me, I panicked, pushed its head violently away, and sprinted blindly
toward my aunt’s house.

​I rushed inside, threw the package at my aunt, and turned back to leave.
That’s when I noticed a dark, shadowy shape lurking near the stairs. My
heart dropped.

​I pulled out my phone to use the flashlight, but what I saw made my
blood run cold:

My battery dropped from 30% to 5% in seconds… then 3%… 2%… 1%.

​Right as the screen went pitch black, I frantically plugged it into a
wall outlet.

​And that’s when SHE appeared.

down right in front of me. She had smooth, flawless skin, curves that

an irresistible, seductive voice:

“Just lay down with me… Sleep next to me right here.”

​For a second, my mind completely went blank. The raw desire and her
exposed body almost trapped me. My heart was pounding for a totally
different reason.

​But then, a terrifying realization hit me like a truck: This isn’t real.
She isn’t human. She’s a trap.

​The moment I realized it was a illusion, pure adrenaline took over. I
broke free from her spell and bolted out of the door toward my home.

​As I ran, my uncle suddenly glitched in front of me out of thin air,
trying to block my way. I shoved him aside and ran like a madman. I
reached my house, suffocating from fear, feeling a cold entity right
behind my back. I violently banged on the heavy wooden door and rattled
the windows, screaming to be let in.

​The door swung open. My parents looked at me with pale, confused faces
and said:

“Why are you banging so hard? The door was unlocked the whole time…”

​And at that exact second—I woke up in my bed, gasping for air, covered
in sweat.

​I can’t stop thinking about it:

​Who or what was that naked entity? Was she a Succubus trying to steal my
soul while I was vulnerable?

​What would have happened if I actually gave in to temptation and laid
down with her?

​Did I make the right choice by running away, or did I escape something
even worse?
