I am quite sure that you are informed on the usual properties of a crash
test dummy. Given this I assume you are similarly informed that they are
fabricated, the place of fabrication you are likely uninformed
regarding, however you know somewhere some factory is filled almost
entirely of those featureless dummies.

The story I am now willing to give you insight upon, is one which is
deeply personal and involved me, a homeless beggar who wanted money for
drugs a dead body and my lawyer.

It was quite late the night when I fractured my foot, I had been on a
downward spiral after first leaving school, I seemingly lost my way,
schooling provided such a simple routine providing me an easy outlet for
socialising, that the unemployed life was a jarring switch so I picked
up alcohol. That night I was particularly intoxicated, so much so that I
was asked to leave the bar I had been cooped up in. So I left I took my
car and began driving down the dark road. I can only remember vague
details of the actual night without reporting back to the newspaper, I
remember heavy bucketing rain, and I remember the bright flash that sent
me through my windshield.

I woke up quite dazed the next night in an empty hospital room with a
newspaper to my side, the front headline was: HEAD ON COLLISION AFTER
DRUNK DRIVER USES WRONG LANE.
It wasn’t long after picking up the paper a man came into the room. He
seemed to bounce across the room his thick cloud of hair bobbed with
every pep filled step, suddenly as if breaking character he sat and
glared at me morosely. “You’ve sent a mother and daughter into a coma,
I’m your court appointed lawyer, I’m here to make you look good and
repenting for the judge”, I was quite taken aback by his forwardness and
asked how old they each were. “Four and forty. your lucky they had a
broken indicator I can stretch that out. wait first I want to get a good
look at you stand on your crutches quickly.” I followed his orders and
looked at him inquisitively, “not great, you only have one plaster cast
on your left leg, I have some bandages and stuff at my office”.

So he drove me with excessive speed towards his office. It was only now
apparent why he seemed so eager to win at the cost of all morality and
decency, he was young, around my age.
“Hey, look at that there” he pointed his hands illuminated by the red
light, beyond his finger was an old homeless man dragging someone by
their leg through the gates of an abandoned factory.
My public defender ever eager sped to the nearest available parking
space, he turned to the door before quickly blurting out, that he was
going to check out the two and that if he doesn’t come back to call the
police using his burner phone in his glove box.
Then he bolted after the two into through the gate and under the
shutters of the building. On top a semi broken sign read: ED’S CRASH
TEST ACCESSORIES.

The most unfortunate thing about him leaving me was that I had no watch,
there were no phones for time, all I could do was listen out for any
shouts and attempt to count in my head, the quietness of the night was
only interrupted by the constant buzz of the light post.

I became extremely anxious for myself and then my lawyer so I decided to
just call the police then and there. I opened the glove box and was met
by stacks of paper and a cold piece of metal thudding onto my knees, the
metal was a loaded revolver there was no phone in sight.
From the factory I heard a sudden burst of a scream that stopped as soon
as it started.
I decided that staying here weakened my chances of doing well in my
trial so I slowly heaved myself out of his car with the gun. I hobbled
slowly across the empty road acclimating more to using the crutches.

The gate was red and brown with rust the loose chain hanging between the
two metal poles made my entrance difficult but I pulled myself up
through the other side and went shuffling slowly onwards towards the
graffitied shutter. It was just the right height for me to easily slouch
through. Inside was pitch black save for a flickering fire exit light, I
called out for my lawyer having forgot his name already by this point I
instead said “where are you!?” And “is everything ok!?”, no answer.

Suddenly under a door slit came a moving light, someone’s flashlight, a
bash sent the door flying open as my lawyer and the homeless man toppled
over each other dashing towards me, behind them the person from before
crawled out with four crash test dummies clinging onto him, my lawyers
light showed me that they were currently in the process of spinning a
cocoon around the body. The cocoon had a rectangular metal frame which
contained the body which now lay supine, the dummies weaved long strings
of wire around this frame whilst kicking themselves forwards to chase
the others.

My lawyer yelled at me to close the shutter, so I pulled it down
quickly, my lawyer just making it out, the homeless man was left
pounding it from inside before a metallic crunch shook the shutters and
stopped the pounding.

I intentionally lost my case after that anything was better than having
to stay outside in this nightmare.
