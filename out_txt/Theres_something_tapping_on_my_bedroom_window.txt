If you live in the outskirts of any Mississippi’s towns and hearing
tapping on your windows, do not get up and investigate the sound of the
tapping. You will never believe what your eyes had seen and it will be
too late to leave now. Someone I had knew had lived to tell a tale and
managed to remember details before they had relocated into a different
state. They have given me the 10 rules on what not to do and survive the
night.

1)When night falls begins, make sure that your cats and dogs are indoors
with you. No matter how hard they try to run back outdoors, keep them
away from open doors and windows that leads outdoors once you bring them
inside. If you have multiple pets, make sure all of them are indoors and
count everyone once the doors and windows are locked.

2)Keep all doors and windows that leads outside locked and curtains
closed right afterward. Whatever out there won’t come in when the home
is on lockdown. It’s best to keep the windows covered and locked. Even
the windows on the doors need to be covered.

3)Have your phone fully charged and close by you at all times. You will
need the phone to call for help when something is tapping at your
window. Make sure that you called the police once the tapping turns into
banging.

4)If you hear the tapping on the window, do not move close to the window
to see who or what was tapping on the window. If you noticed your pets
started to react towards the window, grab them and relocate to a
different place of the home that doesn’t have a window but also have an
escape route. Also, make sure that you have a weapon with you when
something does break that window.

5)If the tapping stops and you hear a familiar voice calling your name,
do not respond back to what’s out there. No matter how familiar that
voice is towards you, don’t respond at all. It will know that you’re at
home and will try to lure you out of your safe place. Do not shout I
have police on the phone or Leave me alone towards it. Whatever it is
out there will break that window and will grab you from your hiding
place. Call the police and inform them that there’s something outside of
your home trying to break in.

6)If they still tapping while calling your name, stay in your hiding
place and call the police. Stay on the phone with the police and contact
anyone you know by text to let them meet the police so you can escape
from the home. Keep the police from hanging up so that way they can
allow you to leave once they check the area.

7)If the tapping turns into banging and calling into screaming, locked
that door of the room fast and blocked the door with anything big and
heavy to keep the thing breaking that door down. Keep all pets quiet and
do not make any sounds at all. Make sure that your phone is still on the
phone with the police and weapon close to you.

8)If you heard the window breaks and footsteps echo in the home,
whatever is outside is now in your home. Stay calm and quiet while it
breaks everything in the home looking for you. Do not make a sound or
try not to unblock the door to peak what’s out there. If you that, it’s
already too late for you to escape from that hiding spot.

9)If you hear police breaking down the door and gun fires, stay in that
room until they speak with you over the phone to come out. Do not start
unblocking the door until the officer say you can come out. The gun
sounds have to match up to the sound on the phone because the officers
will stay in contact with you. If you hear the gun sounds in the house
but not over the phone, the thing is tricking you to leave the home so
it can drag you into the darkness.

10)Once the officer say you can come out, take down the items on the
door and unlock it. The officer will help you into your car or their
car. They will also help you to transport your pets with you into the
city. Anyone you had reached out should be with the police so they could
help out get out of there safely.

If you follow all of these rules, you make it to tell your story. Most
did not make it pass rule 4 and they were never seen again after that
night. Some didn’t make it to rule 8 and they were torn into parts along
with their pets. If you do not follow all of the rules, it’s already
spotted you and you don’t even know it.
