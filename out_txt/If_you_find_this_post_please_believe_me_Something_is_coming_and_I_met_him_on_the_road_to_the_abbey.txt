I always was a man that strived for greatness. I wanted all that the
world could give. I never was rich, attractive, or incredibly smart, but
perhaps that was the reason why my desires were so immense. I wanted
everything, and nobody could stop me. Still, I always said to myself
that it was normal, we all just want what is best for us, like, who
would stop me? As I grew older, I realized that getting things done for
me wasn’t, in the end, so hard. You just had to know your way. You don’t
have to be the strongest to win the fight, you just have to know where
or when to strike. You don’t have to be the tallest or most handsome to
have many women, you only have to know the right things to say to seem
like the best man. You must deceive and manipulate, and if you are good
enough at it, they will believe you. I strived for it and I was good at
it. Perhaps it was because of my childhood, where I never got enough
attention, and my father always beat me and humiliated me.

Maybe all these things made me the man I was. So, I decided to start
taking what was mine. I wanted all the women just for the attention,
just to feel enough. When I was a young guy, it was hard for me, and it
was my dream to be admired by many. As I grew older, I realized how to
do it and I perfected my craft. At some point, I had a lot of women
around, all hooked on my sweet lies, the right things said at the right
time, because they really believed that no one could be this good at
manipulating them. They did not even realize it was manipulation, that’s
how good I was. I was like a god. I had so much.

But still, I wasn’t happy. I always had the idea inside my mind that
once I was desired by enough people, I would find my happiness because I
would feel enough. I was kind of living the life younger me would want.
Still, I wasn’t happy, but why? This painful question was eating at me
day and night. It seemed as if I could never have enough, not enough
good food, sex, company, fun, things, never enough. I wanted more. I
wanted more because I deserved it, why wouldn’t I, right? But this
darkness inside of me was still growing to the point where I was
unhappy. I began to grow antisocial and I began to stay more at home. I
had nothing to do, literally nothing, and I could lie in my bed for the
whole day. It was getting so bad that I couldn’t even sleep at night,
not because I couldn’t fall asleep, but because I did not even go. I
could not force myself to go. I knew it was bad, but I was too weak to
act against it. I went with it on and on. I was falling asleep in the
middle of the day when my body was so tired it wouldn’t even let me be
awake anymore. I didn’t even go out with friends or anyone because I
simply couldn’t. I was too tired, or I was asleep during the hours when
normal people lived their lives. I scheduled many meetings or dates, but
never arrived because the night before I just couldn’t force myself to
fall asleep. So, I lived this twisted life until one day, after being
too tired to even move, I fell asleep.

I had a strange dream about a road that was behind my house in the
countryside, a road leading straight into an old abbey, and it felt as
if something was telling me to go there. When I woke up, I remembered it
to the detail. I looked at the clock, and it was 2 a.m. Still, I felt
the immense desire, which was almost controlling my body, to go to the
road and the abbey behind my house, and so I went.

The night seemed almost feverish to me. Everything had this mystic tone.
It was a peaceful night, stars shining, and I remember it being cold. I
did not necessarily know what the goal of my journey was, but I figured
that I would just go to the old abbey, which I heard was used as a
religious place and as a mental asylum. I was a bit afraid and confused
about why I was really doing it. Out of nowhere, I realized I saw a
figure of a man that must have been tall and with incredibly attractive
features. I was a bit stunned because there was something really wrong
about him. First of all, he wasn’t moving at all, it was as if he didn’t
even need to breathe. Second of all, he was really tall, almost in an
inhuman way. And third of all, it was as if his whole existence was
emitting this weird and also really dim light. I felt a slight unease
and almost a fear. Though I figured that it was just some man looking at
the sky in the middle of the night.

I had no way but to go past him because he was right in the middle of
the road. The closer I got, the bigger my unease and feeling of an
immense horror of something that shouldn’t be there grew. I approached
him and asked, “Who are you, and are you insane, being here in the
middle of the night?” The person looked down on me with such contempt
and disgust in his eyes that I completely lost my composure. He was so
attractive, beautiful like an angel. After a silence that might have
seemed eternal, he slowly spoke, “I am you, I am me, and I am many
others. You would never understand the significance of my actions.”
Words said with such authority and pronunciation as if he was trying to
carve them into a stone wall. I felt as if every syllable was meant to
insult me, and every letter sucked the light out of the world around us.

Even confused and afraid, I asked again because I was curious, “Then
what is your name?” my voice shaking from the sudden feeling of
coldness, as if someone was pouring ice water down my spine. The
stranger, looking almost bothered by my entire existence, began to
speak, “You call me many names, some bring light, some bring none.
Samael for now might seem the one. Your questions bother me, and leaving
during these wicked hours for a human seems brave. No longer of your
soul shall I be deprived,” his voice resonating deeply in the night.
Images quickly flashed in my mind, images of eldritch horrors rarely
spoken aloud, rituals so wicked and twisted only a few know about, and
promises and secrets so wicked that no human should ever know about
their existence. At the same time, the man was standing there so
beautiful and tall, long straight hair, perfect face, and muscular body,
and even up close he was emitting a strange, almost paranormal glow
right at the edge of hallucination. I was really confused and said to
myself that he was probably crazy, that someone had probably escaped
from the abbey.

Then I decided to walk away without uttering a single word. All my body
was tingling and twitching as if it wanted to be far, far away from this
place. I tried to move, but to my horror, I realized that I couldn’t. I
thought that I must be hallucinating. I struggled and fought against my
own body, but nothing would help me. With fear that was slowly creeping
into every part of my cold body, I looked at the entity calling itself
Samael. As I raised my sight, he let out this sound almost resembling
laughter, laughter so twisted, a sound so disgusting, as if a beast made
it. No human could sound like this was the only thing I could think
about. Samael raised his hand, slowly pointed at me, and spoke, “Because
I see myself in you, and you shall never be me, one wish before the
utter end yours shall be.”

I was moved by the gravity of his words and the realization of what he
had just said. I tried to run, tried to scream, but I simply couldn’t,
as if something was pushing on my entire existence. Suddenly, a creeping
feeling arrived at my mind, and that is when I started to slowly drift
into what would later become uncontrolled insanity. During his entire
speech, he never moved his lips or mouth, not even once. Still, there
was no mistaking he pronounced those words, I knew he did. I heard them
straight in my head. “Ask now, or your wishes forever unpronounced will
be,” he said, almost like nothing had happened. His voice was so sweet
and so perfect, though sweet to such an extent almost resembling
beautiful, tasty, fresh fruit beginning to rot deeply to its core.

Out of nowhere, I responded to him with this sentence, so surprised by
it as if it wasn’t mine, “Tell me your story, tell me your story now,
and thus complete to me your solemn vow.” Samael, as if struck by
lightning, twisted his lips into a grim expression, scarring his perfect
face like a mockery to everything that is beautiful. Then he twitched
back right into an unbothered and seemingly calm expression and thus
spoke, “For a word once given, even I must heed the call. Now, mortal, a
story of the greatest fall of them all shall your unworthy presence
befall.” I was unable to respond, let alone move or run away. Slowly, I
realized that everything around me was turning dark, every sound around
me slowly fading away. After a while, I remained standing against Samael
in complete darkness and silence. Only his body was emitting a strange
glow, allowing me to see his body and expression.

That being then began to slowly speak with a voice resonating in
complete silence all around me, feeling like two stones pressing against
each other, “For a mortal, my story incomprehensible may seem, but you
finally will realize why us two so alike might have been. I once was the
greatest, everyone called my name, the perfect creation, beautiful and
kind, and my mind was sane. I had many brothers, still I was the one,
most beautiful and strongest. In my existence, a flaw was none. I was
with Him, as we all once were. No mortals that time with us were there.
He showed me the plan, but never the whole, and thus He began the
creation of His own. I only saw parts, though my worry was none. Hence I
had no equal, I had all the fun. Told I was who I am by His grace. He
said, ‘You have free will,’ and that was the only phrase. Knowing I am
the brightest and the light, a sudden doubt blocked my sight. So
beautiful I was and wise, a creation like this should never get the
second prize. The more impatient I grew looking at Him, and the light in
me began to dim. How can He be the best? He is only forcing me lies. I
am the best, not one brother of mine shall to me suffice. So once, when
He told me what to do, the blood in my veins began to brew. He must pay
for making me suffer. Such words of blasphemy I had to utter. The light
and kindness failed to shine, and thus I was the first who stepped out
of the line.”

“To take His place was the only thing I need. This hunger in me I began
to feed. I spoke to brothers, and some I managed to deceive, that He
above shall no longer breathe. One day I approached with evil in my
heart at the gate, and so everything around began to shake. Some of my
brothers by my side went into the grounds to make Him pay the price. I
came for Him, but someone stepped into my way. Michael, he must have
been insane. My sibling looked into eyes of mine and found none of the
mercy or former empathy, not even a dime. I struck at him, a weapon in
my hand. He could never hope to match me, but for some reason he had
faith. All around me a war broke loose. Now or never, I knew this act
could not be excused. I fought my brother with fury and wrath, but
Michael remained composed, to my utmost surprise. A duel that a new time
had shaped, the just versus depraved, but my brother couldn’t be swayed.
I knew He was watching, so I channeled my wrath, launched at Michael,
but his flaming sword went straight through my heart. I fell to my
knees, tears in my eyes, everything around me aflame, my loyal brothers
paying the ultimate price. I looked at Michael, seeing in him the mercy
of the just. I couldn’t move anymore, the only feeling in me anymore was
the lust.”

“Suddenly, all the sky began to shake, and an immense storm roared and
quaked. I looked and saw a great lightning bolt heading for me, and then
I knew that not anymore would I be free. And thus, it struck me, and so
I and companions of mine fell. Greatest fall, as you shall tell. Once
the greatest, now that’s all gone. I had everything I could want, now I
have none. To be bound to this place and cannot leave, to roam the
earth, lie, tempt, and deceive. I suffer every day, and everyone in here
for it shall pay. So, the battle continues, only not on such a scale.
The fight between lust and the just, the battle is still the same. Now
you see it. He created you too, aren’t we all just simply His little
tool? I can smell the desire in your soul, I see the soil where my ideas
might swiftly grow. For we are here, I can give you everything. Tell me
now, hence I am fulfilling my vow. No, simply a fool I see. I shall use
your body and mind, to my will you never shall be free.”

“Thus, you know my story and everything I have told. Now it is time for
your soul to be sold. Tell me, human, what do you desire, and pay the
price. I will fulfill your wish, even though in my great plan you are
simply a mouse.” So, he ended his breathtaking speech and looked at me
with disgust. Again, I thought that such a beautiful face wasn’t made
for such a disgraceful expression as he bore, but here it was. I wanted
to cry and yell. I wanted to take all my sins back. I saw myself in him
and I began to shake. I knew exactly what he meant when he talked about
similarities. An immense horror was creeping through my body. Ideas of
rituals so old and depraved kept playing in my head because I knew who
was standing in front of me.

There was no mistaking it anymore. But now it was only me and him. I
realized that he had told me he was Samael only to trick me. The time of
this name was long past gone, so I stood against him and was supposed to
tell my wish and then pay the price. Suddenly, I remembered and I spoke,
“You can fulfill anything? Well, I am not so sure. It still seems that
you are only the second in command, am I not right? Only a mouse, you
say? I agree that we are similar. You for sure are also so small in His
eyes.” Suddenly, I saw it inside his eyes, a flame burning bright, an
anger so big that allows you to fight your own brother to death, a
contempt without a limit, and also far in the back a sadness, a sorrow
for days long past gone, a desire so immense that you are not able to
control. He seemed to lose control for a moment, and thus the darkness
and illusion around me fell apart. For a moment, I wasn’t in his
control.

I scrambled to my legs and ran, ran without realizing it straight to the
small abbey. I could see its gates no longer so far away. I could hear
him behind me, completely without any self control, yelling slurs and
running behind me. I heard his steps quickly catching up to me. I
sprinted for my life and soul, it was only a matter of time before he
caught up to me. Suddenly, music started to sound from the abbey. I
recognized it right away and it made me terrified to the core because I
knew its meaning. It was none other than the Dies Irae by Giuseppe
Verdi. Still, I fought for my life, the gates were just before me.
Suddenly, I was grabbed from the back and fell to the ground. Looking
up, realizing that so called Samael had caught up to me. There was no
help now, and the music was soaring almost as if the sky played it.
Samael picked me up and threw me against the gate, fuming with anger. He
was approaching me, and I realized that these were my last moments, and
I said, “Oh God, please help me, I am so sorry, I never knew.” At that
instance, the entity stopped almost with fear, music now playing with
almost a devilish volume.

Out of nowhere, everything stopped, even the wind. It was absolute
silence. I looked at Samael and what I saw made me lose the rest of the
sanity that I still held on to. This being with immense power, this
entity was standing almost in fear. Then it began, the sound that was
almost cutting into my soul, a sound so immense and full I couldn’t
believe it. Such a thing can only be described to us humans as the sound
of a trumpet. It played one time, everything was shaking. It resonated
for the second time, out of nowhere animals began to run away. It soared
the third time, and it started to rain like I had never witnessed in my
life. Fourth time, fifth, and now the sixth time, I heard huge cracking
sounds in the sky and religious chanting which I thought was from the
abbey. I looked in front of me and he was standing there now almost
ready for something. There was no mistaking he was levitating a few
inches from the ground, and suddenly the seventh sound came, bigger than
all of the ones before. The sky was glowing, and suddenly, in the middle
of the night, a small crack full of light began to appear in it. I
remember only having this sentence in my head, “The time has come.” The
sky, now glowing with light, finally cracked in half, and I saw Samael
rising up to it. Out of the sky suddenly descended a figure, and I saw
thousands behind it. The figure launched straight at Samael with a sword
aflame in its hands, and I lost consciousness. In my mind, I saw
pictures of utter destruction, the end of all. I saw the final rebellion
and its finale in what I could only describe as an endless lake of fire.
I saw the judgment in front of a place I could only call a white throne,
and I realized the significance of repentance before it is too late.

Suddenly, I opened my eyes, confused. Where am I? Aren’t I dead? I
looked around, confused, and suddenly it hit me. I was sitting on the
road to the abbey, it was the middle of the night, just the wind howling
in the trees. I must have passed out. What happened? Is it possible that
it was all in my mind? But I was sure it happened, I just knew that I
wasn’t picturing it. I decided to stand up, and then I felt a pain going
through my back, such a pain that reminded me of something. I touched
the place where it was going through, I looked at my hand and saw blood.
That was the moment when I lost all my doubts and I realized, it was the
place where Samael grabbed me when he was chasing me. I could recognize
the markings that his fingers, almost like claws, made in my skin. At
that moment, I knew that something was terribly wrong.

I am writing this text in the hope of easing my mind because I feel like
I am slowly drifting into insanity. I talked with many people about the
horrors I had been through. I talked with experts and doctors. Nobody
believes me, they think I am insane. At first, they laughed at me, but
as I grew desperate and tried to explain to people over and over again,
the doctors pronounced me ill and locked me up in a mental asylum, the
one in the abbey where I must spend the rest of my days. That is where I
am writing this text in hopes of it maybe getting to the right person,
the person that will believe and take action, because I know something
is terribly wrong and we need to act. I am not insane, right, or am I?
No, I am not, I know it. You need to do it, you need to do the ritual,
yes, the one, and we will know. You believe me, you must, before it is
too late.
