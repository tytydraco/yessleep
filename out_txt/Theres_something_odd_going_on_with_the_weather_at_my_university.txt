Okay you guys, I need other people to weigh in, because I feel like this
isn’t normal. I need a second opinion, or several, on what could

I’m a student at a university on the east coast of Canada. I came here
because my mom’s family is from here and I’m pretty familiar with the
province, but I’ve only ever been here in summer. Some strange things
are happening now that it’s fall.
I expected it to be pretty misty and cool here- we’re right on the
Atlantic, and it can get pretty overcast and gloomy even in the summer.
I don’t mind it personally, I love the rain.
But there are aspects to the weather that I’m starting to find kind of
strange. (And please tell me if this is just me losing it and cracking
from the stress of schoolwork- I don’t want to make something out of
nothing).

But does anyone else live somewhere where they get a storm almost on a
schedule? I’ve been noticing it more and more lately and it’s weirding
me out. It’s almost like clockwork. Every two weeks, give or take a day
or two, we get a ‘storm day’.
I know that probably sounds ridiculous. I would have thought nothing of
it except that it’s been like clockwork for months now. But the timing
is only part of the weirdness.

We get an emergency alert for it every single time telling us to shelter
indoors immediately. It always gives us a high wind warning- which, I
guess it can get kind of windy- but it also keeps on stressing to take
shelter indoors and specifically to close, lock, and cover all doors and
windows.
My uni’s residence held a mandatory meeting the first time it happened
and showed us all how to do it. Just make sure your windows and shut and
fastened completely, then close your curtains- ideally pin them to the
frame with some pushpins, so you can’t see outside at all until you
remove them. The RAs come by every now and again during storm days to
make sure we have our windows locked and covered, and we’re not supposed
to leave the building.

I don’t get this. The winds are high, but not unusually so. Why would we
need to close out curtains all the way and pin them? I sort of dismissed
it as hysterical and put it out of my mind till the regularity of the
rain started nagging at me.
Well, lately I’ve been studying at the very top floor of the law
building, which has an area with a class wall and ceiling, like a
sunroom. I just… study through the storm. I quite like the sound of the
rain, it helps me focus. I thought nothing of it until I mentioned it to
a friend of mine and she told me I needed to be inside during the
storms.
I said I was inside.
She said no, inside inside. Like in a place with no windows. She was
pretty insistent about it.
Am I missing something here? I don’t want to dismiss some sort of
obvious threat if there is one, but to me it just seems like a pleasant
study environment where you can hear the rain thrumming on the ceiling
above you. I can’t really see any of my proper immediate surroundings,
being so high up and in the middle of the room, but when I look out at
the horizon it just looks like another overcast rainy day outside. I can
see fog clouding some of the houses in the distance, but that’s about
it.
I noticed that the city always puts the streetlights on all day too. It
tends to get pretty dark on those days, and you can see the sky
beginning to darken already around three-thirty in the afternoon. It’s
orange streetlights, too, not the usual white ones. Strange.

But the thing is, the whole city seems to prepare for these like it’s a
hurricane. Everyone goes out and gets storm chips and alcohol, and
everyone kind of huddles up inside and parties and drinks till it’s
over. It’s like a citywide event. It seems like nobody really goes out
and does anything. I’ve seen some people on my city’s subreddit
reference being stuck at work and miserable during a storm, so it
doesn’t seem like everyone is at home, but it’s like things pause in
place until it’s over. If it’s on a weekday classes always get cancelled
for ‘inclement weather’, even though these storms really aren’t that
bad. Why is it that we’re apparently required to cover all our windows?
Apparently you can get in major trouble with the school for it too if
you don’t, I heard about one girl who nearly got kicked out of
residence.
Is this some sort of citywide hysteria phenomenon? Am I missing
something? These seems like completely normal rainstorms, but the whole
city is shutting down for them.

Here’s the thing, though. The last time I was alone in the law building
studying, something really strange happened. I was getting ready to go,

I’d decided to grab a couple books before I left, and so I was walking

To put it bluntly, I don’t like this. I haven’t studied late in that
