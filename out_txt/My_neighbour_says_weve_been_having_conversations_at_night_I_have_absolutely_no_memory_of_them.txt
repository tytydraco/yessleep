I live alone in a block of flats and there’s an older woman who lives
directly opposite me. She’s probably in her 70s, really nice lady and
we’ve known each other for about three years.

Last Saturday morning I bumped into her downstairs and she asked if I’d
managed to get some sleep. I said yeah, why? She said I’d seemed quite
worried when we’d spoken the night before. Apparently I’d knocked on her
door at around 2:30am because I thought someone was hanging around
outside my flat.

I told her she must have confused me with someone else because I’d been
asleep, but she was absolutely certain it was me. She even said we’d
been having these conversations several times over the past two weeks
and described what I’d been wearing.

Anyway, there’s CCTV covering the communal corridor, so on Monday I
contacted the management company and asked if they could check the
footage. I was actually starting to wonder if I could be sleepwalking or
something, although I’ve never done that before.

They called me back the following day and confirmed my front door hadn’t
opened once that night. Which was exactly what I expected. But they also
mentioned something strange.

At around 2:30am my neighbour had come out of her flat and stood in the
corridor for about five minutes, apparently having a conversation with
someone. She was nodding, gesturing and even laughing at certain points,
except there was nobody else there. I asked if she could have been on
the phone but they said it didn’t look like it.

I assumed she must be getting confused and actually felt quite sorry for
her. So yesterday I went round to see her. I didn’t mention the CCTV
because I didn’t want to upset her, but I asked if she remembered what
we’d supposedly been talking about.

She mentioned a few ordinary things, then casually said I’d told her
about finding an old photograph of my dad standing next to his red car.

Now this is the part I really can’t explain. On Friday evening I’d been
sorting through some boxes in my bedroom and found that exact photograph
tucked inside an old birthday card. I’d been looking for it for years
and hadn’t told anyone I’d found it.

I asked if she remembered anything else about the photo and she said,
“Only that you’d found it inside a birthday card.”

I honestly didn’t know what to say. She couldn’t possibly have known
that. I’d only found it a few hours before and hadn’t mentioned it to
anyone.

I still haven’t told her about the CCTV and I’m not sure if I should.
I’ve been going over the whole thing in my head trying to find a
reasonable explanation, but nothing really makes sense.

According to the CCTV, I never left my flat that night and she was
standing in the corridor talking to nobody. Yet somehow she knew exactly
what I’d been doing inside my flat just a few hours earlier.
