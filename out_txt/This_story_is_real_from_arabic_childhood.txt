The Toys at the Bottom of the Window

My childhood nightmare… or something else?

My name is Mohammed, and this happened when I was around 3–4 years old.

When I was little, my parents used to buy me toys. I loved them, and
they were practically my friends.

When I turned four, my parents bought me an iPad. Naturally, I became
obsessed with it. I gradually stopped playing with my toys, and
eventually, I started throwing them out of my bedroom window, one by
one, until I had gotten rid of almost all of them.

Now, here’s something you need to understand about our old house.

My bedroom was shared with my siblings. There was a window, but it
didn’t face the outside world. Instead, it overlooked a narrow, enclosed
space inside the building.

The space was roughly 1.25 × 1.25 meters, surrounded by walls with
windows belonging to different parts of the building. There was red soil
at the bottom, about 3.5 meters below our window.

All the windows had metal bars because falling into that space could be
dangerous.

Anyway, after throwing away all my toys, I eventually forgot about them.

Until one day, my iPad stopped being the center of my attention.

My phone had fallen into my bowl of soup and stopped working.

I suddenly missed my old toys.

I remembered throwing them out of the window, so I looked down into that
narrow space, expecting to see them buried under dirt by now.

But they were still there.

They looked almost exactly as I remembered them.

However, there were also some toys I didn’t recognize.

I wondered if other children in the building had thrown their toys down
there, or if I had simply forgotten owning some of them.

I couldn’t retrieve them because the window had metal bars, and the
ground was too far below.

I eventually went to bed.

But then something strange happened.

I heard someone calling my name.

” Mohammed…”

The voice was coming from below the window.

It was clear. It sounded like an actual person standing down there,
calling for me.

I didn’t recognize the voice.

At first, I tried to ignore it. I even convinced myself that someone was
probably calling another Mohammed in the building.

But there was a problem.

My family was the only family that had ever lived in that building.

I couldn’t sleep.

Eventually, I decided I was going to get my toys back, even though it
was nighttime.

The enclosed space was completely dark. I could barely see anything down
there.

I approached the window and started trying to break or bend the metal
bars with my hands.

Then I noticed something.

There was a black figure standing at the bottom.

I couldn’t make out what it was.

Suddenly, it turned on a flashlight.

It was a woman wearing a black niqab.

For a moment, I thought it was my mother.

But then her niqab began to tear.

And what I saw underneath wasn’t my mother.

Her face looked like Death Worm, a monster from a game I used to love.
It had the appearance of a giant worm, with a mouth full of teeth.

I was terrified.

Somehow, I fell through the window bars.

I closed my eyes, expecting to hit the ground.

But when I opened them…

I wasn’t in that enclosed space anymore.

I was standing in the middle of the street outside our house.

It was nighttime.

The street was completely empty.

No people. No cars. Nothing.

There was only one streetlight illuminating a small part of the road.

I screamed.

I tried to run back home, but my legs wouldn’t move.

Then I heard something.

A distant noise, almost like an airplane engine or an extremely powerful
gust of wind.

It was getting closer.

And louder.

And louder.

Until it became unbearable.

Then…

I woke up.

I was absolutely terrified.

The noise had stopped completely.

Even now, I can’t remember clearly falling asleep that night. I only
remember trying to sleep, failing, and eventually deciding to retrieve
my toys.

I also can’t explain why I remember the experience so vividly.

Was it all just a nightmare?

Did I hear someone calling my name while I was half asleep?

Was the woman simply something my childhood imagination created?

Or did my brain somehow combine real memories with a dream?

I don’t know.

But there’s one detail I still find strange.

I never forgot that night.

And sometimes, when I remember those toys lying in the red dirt…

I wonder what those unfamiliar toys were doing down there.

I still don’t know whether I dreamed the whole thing, or whether some
parts of it actually happened.
