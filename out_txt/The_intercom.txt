I’m still trying to make sense of what happened last night, and the more
I think about it, the worse it feels. It was a perfectly ordinary
Sunday. Nothing unusual had happened during the day, and my wife and I
went to bed at roughly the usual time. We had finished dinner, cleaned
up the kitchen, said goodnight to our cat, and were walking down the
hallway toward the bedroom when the intercom rang. We stopped and looked
at each other. It wasn’t terribly late, but it was late enough that
neither of us was expecting anyone. I went back to the kitchen and
picked up the handset.

“Hello? Who is it?”

There was no answer, only what sounded like wind coming through the
speaker. No breathing, no footsteps, no voice. I waited a few seconds
and hung up, assuming someone had pressed the wrong button or a kid in
the building was messing around. But before I even reached the bedroom,
it rang again. This time I was annoyed, and I snapped, “What do you
want?” The same silence came back. I hung up and told my wife it was
probably some idiot playing games, and we got into bed, though neither
of us was relaxed anymore.

A few minutes later it rang a third time. I got up and, angry enough by
then, threatened to call the police if whoever was outside didn’t stop.
Again, nothing. I returned to bed feeling slightly better, mostly
because making the threat had given me the illusion of being in control.
About ten minutes later, the intercom rang again. This time I didn’t
bother asking who it was. I said that if they rang once more I would
come downstairs, and that whoever was doing this wouldn’t enjoy the
conversation. I hung up, and almost immediately it started ringing
again.

While I stood there staring at the handset, my wife went to the window
and pulled the curtain aside. There was absolutely nobody in the street.
No cars, no pedestrians, and the park opposite our building was empty
too, apart from the trees swaying slightly in the wind. That was the
moment it stopped feeling like a stupid prank.

We went back to bed, but neither of us really slept. Exhausted as we
were, we must have drifted off eventually, because I woke to the sound
of the intercom ringing yet again. I stayed where I was, listening, and
I could tell my wife was awake beside me. After several rings it
stopped, and a few seconds later it started again. This time, instead of
answering, I went to the window and looked down at the entrance of the
building, and there I saw someone: a figure standing just outside,
dressed entirely in black. The street was too dark to make out any
detail, but I watched for a few seconds until, for no apparent reason,
the figure turned and began to run toward the park.

I told my wife what I had seen, pulled on some clothes, and went
downstairs. I ran into the park and spent almost an hour searching the
paths and looking between the trees, but I found no one. Eventually I
gave up and went home. I was about to get into bed when I made the
mistake of looking out the window one last time.

The figure was back. It was standing in the park, and this time it
wasn’t running. It simply stood there, completely still. I couldn’t see
its face, but I had a horrible certainty that it was looking directly at
our apartment. I watched it for several seconds, trying to convince
myself I was imagining things, and I considered going down again, but my
wife grabbed my arm and told me not to. She was genuinely frightened by
then, and so was I.

The intercom rang twice more before morning, and I didn’t answer either
time. We stayed awake until dawn, and when the sun finally came up, the
park was empty and the figure was gone. I thought that was the end of
it.

Then, while I was making coffee, I noticed something I hadn’t seen
during the night: wet footprints on the floor directly in front of our
apartment door. They came up from the staircase and stopped right
outside our door. There was no trail leading back the other way.

And then the intercom rang one last time, except this time nobody was
calling from downstairs. The handset was already off the hook, and
through the speaker, very quietly, we heard my wife’s voice say:

“Who is it?”

My wife was standing next to me in the kitchen when we heard it.
