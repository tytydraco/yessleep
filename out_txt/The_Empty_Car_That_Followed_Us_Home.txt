This happened over 3 years ago, but I still get chills when I think
about it.

It was very late at night. My family and I — my mom, dad, sister,
cousin, and I — were driving back to our hometown after shopping in the
city.

To get to our house, we have to take a detour from the main road. It’s a
steep, deserted mountain road, completely dark at that hour.

About halfway through, we noticed a car behind us. Its headlights were
on, keeping a steady distance. We didn’t think much of it at first.

Then we turned onto the mountain road. The car turned too.

We drove for another 5 minutes in silence. That’s when we reached the
big stone pond. Right opposite the pond, there’s a narrow, creepy
stretch — a road covered with trees and loose stones that leads directly
to the back of our house.

The car was still following us.

My dad slowed down and moved to the side of the road, giving it space to
overtake us. But it didn’t. It stopped. Just a few meters behind us, in
the middle of the dark road.

That’s when it hit me. None of our neighbours own a car. No one from the
city has any reason to come down that isolated road at midnight.

My sister, my cousin, and I slowly turned around to look.

The car’s headlights were blazing. The windshield was completely clear.
We could see straight into it.

And there was no one inside.

No driver. No passenger. The seats were completely empty.

We stared for almost two minutes, frozen. No one got out. No shadow
moved inside. It was just… an empty car, parked behind us in the middle
of nowhere, watching us.

My hands started shaking. I leaned forward and whispered to my dad what
we saw.

He didn’t ask a single question. He just slammed on the accelerator and
drove as fast as that mountain road would allow.

We finally reached home.

Our house is in a forest-like area. In the morning it’s peaceful and
beautiful, but at night… it’s pitch black and creepy. The forest
swallows everything.

We didn’t park in the front. We stopped at the back parking lot, jumped
out, and ran through the backyard like our lives depended on it. We
locked the gate, slammed the main door, and locked it twice.

My mom pulled all of us kids upstairs to the bedroom. We didn’t turn on
a single light. We were breathing so loud in the dark.

I couldn’t help it. I slowly crept to the window and looked down at our
backyard and the back road that leads to our house.

Nothing. No one. No car. Empty.

I thought it was over.

Then my dad called my mom in a whisper. The kind of whisper that makes
your stomach drop.

My mom and I tip-toed downstairs like thieves, our footsteps silent. My
dad was standing frozen in the sit-out, just staring outside.

We looked where he was looking.

On the small common road in front of our house — the road shared by 4-5
houses including our neighbours — there it was.

The same white car.

Parked right in front of our house.

We were in shock. How did it know? How did it know the front entrance of
our house when we came from the back road? We had never taken that
common road.

We stood there in the dark, watching it silently. Then, right in front
of our eyes, its headlights went off. Click. Darkness. Now we could only
see the faint glare of its white body in the moonlight.

My dad grabbed my hand and said in a low voice, “Don’t come outside. Go
to your room and sleep.”

How could we possibly sleep?

All of us kids huddled in one room, whispering about what we just saw.
Later, my parents came and slept in our room too. All five of us in one
room, doors locked.

After a long time, my dad looked one more time from the sit-out.

The car was gone.

Just like that. No sound. No engine. No lights driving away. It had just
vanished.

Till this day, we don’t know where that car came from. Or how it knew
where we lived.
