I was a regular kid in school, I would do my homework on time and try to
listen to the teacher as much as possible, this was what most of the
students did but there was one student who was different from the rest,
his name was Joel, he would do whatever he wanted whether it be
listening to Taylor Swift in the back of the class and ignoring the
teacher to pulling the fire alarm cause he thought it was funny.

I would always get mad at this dude cause I just wanted to exist in life
and let everything happen that was supposed to happen but it seemed like
Joel, was always fighting against everything, one day I built up the
courage and asked him, “why aren’t you like everyone else”,

He said,”if I was like everyone else I would end up going crazy, it’s my
differences that help me stand out and help my life have a semblance of
meaning.”

I responded with,”don’t be so silly, all you want to do is make yourself
the center of attention, one day I hope everyone forgets about you and
moves on, cause one day you’ll realize that just like rest of us, you
are a water droplet in the vast ocean of water.”

Joel looked at me and he had a certain glare to his eyes as if his heart
had dropped, and he suddenly for once in his life felt reality coming
crashing down all over him, he ran out of the classroom and in the rush
made me drop my computer which I had saved up for all summer to get.

A few weeks passed, and every day, Joel seemed more and more numb, he
would no longer have any center of attention behaviors anymore, he would
just sit in class and be obedient, but there was something off about
him, he was so obedient he never spoke to anyone, and kept to himself, I
tried talking to him and few times and he straight up just looked past
me as if there was nothing in the world but his mind and himself.

This would continue for months until one day in late April, he just
didn’t show up to class, I asked our math teacher, a woman by the name
of Mrs.Heck, “where the heck is Joel, he’s not here today?”

Mrs.Heck answered,”he didn’t feel good or at least that’s what his
parents said, you can go check in on him if you like, you guys live in
the same neighborhood right?”

I nodded in approval and went home since it was otherwise a regular
Monday I ate some cookies when got home and then had bright idea to
actually listen to my teacher and visit Joel, however when I went to his
house, something was different, there were no cars in front of the
house, just some tire marks on the driveway, and as I peered to the
house, I saw broken window and got a little scared. I pushed through the
fear though and opened the door which budged open without much effort, I
thought that was odd, but then I felt a sudden calmness being in my
friends house again and sat down to wait for people to come back.

3 hours passed by and it was getting late but right as I was about to
leave, I felt like someone was with me in the house, as I looked around
I couldn’t see anybody but the feeling wouldn’t go away, then as I
called out,”stop playing with me, if anyone is there, reveal yourself!”,

Someone responded,“No, I will not, since you took away my purpose in
life now I shall take away yours,”suddenly the room went dark and I fell
unconscious.

I woke up in a room with a single sign that said, YOU ARE FORGOTTEN, I
screamed for Joel or whoever took me there to let me out but the louder
I hit the walls the more they echoed and it drove me crazy so I stopped
trying and then I cried, the room was about 20 feet tall and only exit
was from the top, so I was pretty much going to be stuck there forever,
or at least so I thought cause I heard someone pass by a day or two
after being down there and asked, “can you help me out a crazy guy
trapped me down here?”,

They responded with,”I am not crazy, and what you see is not what is,
try to pinch yourself,”, as soon as I tried that, I woke up in my bed,
however what confused me was that it seemed so real and to my horror I
saw the same sign on my wall, YOU ARE FORGOTTEN, now when I ventured
outside my room I see my parents are missing and the door is broken and
window broken and tire marks on driveway, everything I had seen in
Joel’s house, I am lost…
