We all have one a person we have never met and hopefully never will a
person who appears only inside you, at your command. Close your eyes for
a moment, open the doors of your imagination, and think of a person, the
most terrifying person you can imagine. You have surely seen this same
person before in the exact same situation as now; it is always the same
person. I have always been a curious person, so it was natural that this
very insight piqued my curiosity my cursed curiosity. I wanted to
understand why it was always this same person, and why they looked both
human and inhuman at the same time. This, at least in the beginning, was
the goal of my research. Nowadays, my only goal is to flee, to get away
from it. My only goal now is to survive.
It all started six months ago. Curiosity has always been my driving
force, the power that led me to become a researcher in Manhattan. My lab
was my castle, my home, and my downfall. The project was born from a
dream after a sixteen-hour shift. There he stood. That figure we all
carry, but which I now saw with terrifying clarity. He is disgustingly
tall. A gangly silhouette with slick, black hair parted in a perfect,
sickly side part. His face is a waxen mask. That smile… it isn’t joy;
it’s a bared skull that promises suffering. His eyes are black holes
that suck the life out of everything living.
In the dream, he chased me. I tried to run, but my feet felt like lead.
He just walked. Slow, articulated steps with his spider-like legs. But
he was getting closer. Always closer. When he finally reached me, I
didn’t wake up. The coldness of his grip burned through my skin. I was
paralyzed. He lifted me, light as a withered leaf, and fixed my gaze.
“One day, you will be attached to this place instead of me,” he hissed.
His nails dug deep into my flesh. The pain was electric. He flung me
across the concrete floor, and in the fall, I opened my eyes in my dark
lab. The scar from his nails remained even when I woke up.
A theory hit me, dreams are not fantasy. They are a dimension. The fifth
dimension. Five months of feverish work later, the machine was ready. A
helmet, a tangle of cables, a ticket to the unknown. I pressed the red
button. Sleep came instantly, forced by specific frequencies. I stepped
in. At first, it was a game. I conjured up a school. Long, echoing
corridors. Row after row of metal lockers. Everything felt solid, real.
I enjoyed wandering there as a student again. To be with my old freinds
again.
Then came the darkness. Flickering fluorescent lights. A sudden ice-cold
wind that made the hairs on my neck stand up. My old classmates stopped.
They turned around in a synchronized movement. Their eyes were erased,
just empty hollows staring through me. They began to dissolve like ash
in the wind. A voice, raspy and dry as old parchment, cut through the
silence: “So, you think you’re the one in control here?”
My heart hammered against my ribs. “Who are you?” I gasped. Sweeping
footsteps were heard behind me. “I am you,” the answer came from the
shadows. “I am every monster you’ve shaped in your thoughts. Every
spider-leg, every shadowy face. I am your collected fear.” He stepped
forward. He was enormous, his head scraped the corridor ceiling. He
moved with an unnatural, jerky speed. “I am tired of my prison. I have
your memories. I know how you tremble. And now, you have come here with
your soul on a silver platter, ready for me to take over.”
I fled. My lungs burned. The corridors seemed to stretch into infinity.
He was everywhere. The walls breathed his name. “Run then,” he taunted.
“I am in control here.” Suddenly, the air turned thick like syrup. Every
step required an inhuman effort. I stumbled. His ice-cold breath hit the
back of my neck. I turned around and was met by that hideous, bared
smile. “Now, we switch places.”
He tore me up. The world around us imploded into an absolute, bottomless
darkness. He held me in a chokehold. “Welcome to your new home. Your
body belongs to me now.” He let go, and I fell. There was nothing to
wake up to. No body to return to. A month has passed since this
happened. This computer is just a projection of my will. I built a
portal to get out before I used the helmet, but, I’m sorry, I realized
too late the portal is open to everything, and he is the one who decides
what comes through. It is his world. We have all seen him in different
disguises, he is the one who controls the dream world. All the darkness
in here can now wander out into your world. im sorry, dream carefully.
He is already on his way.
