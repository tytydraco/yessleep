In my early adult years, I moved into a home on a suburban block.

I felt a little uneasy to move into a new place. Being by myself the
first time was daunting. But I was able to ease in enough.

I watched TV throughout the day. After a week in, while in the living
room. Over the sound of the TV, I heard the sound of glass “pop”.

I jumped up and turned. I walked over and found shattered glass. The
kitchen light bulb popped.

I knew the house was checked. My dad looked all throughout. Yet, found
nothing. Nothing to suggest a sudden pulse break a glass. I thought
maybe something hit it. But that would be impossible. Unless…

Something hit it.

I scoffed at the idea but I then gave it some thought. Fear took hold of
me and I needed to investigate. I tip-toed through the kitchen. I looked
around the whole home, nothing.

I was relieved and just wanted to forget about it. After all the glass
breaking made me more annoyed than anything. Yet, every so often I would
still remember when a bulb shattered for no reason..

I was asleep at night when something woke me. I felt a chill. I woke up,
usually cold is better for sleep. But it was so sudden it was like
someone slapped me. I sat up and checked my phone. (It’s a bad habit of
mine.)

It was dead.

But, I had charged it before. I knew I did!

I ignored this upon feeling a rush of heat. It passed through the room,
I felt it move. Then it was coming in one direction. I turned and saw
something.

There, in the corner of my room. A figure, it was a shimmering shape.
Like looking over a fire. Then, I felt the current move up. My eyes
followed the figure as it rose up. . .

It vanished into the ceiling along with the heat. I knew that was
basically nothing, it was just a weird experience. At least, that is
what I told myself. But I could not deny how cold I now was.

The room was freezing, like it had all of its heat zapped.

It made me remember the light bulb. Though I did not want to. Something
like that is impossible to move that way. Yet it did and could have done
it twice. Once in the kitchen, another in my bedroom.

But, is this an energy anomaly of some kind? After all I was scared of
what that could entail. But I tried to go back to sleep. Shortly then, I
fell asleep.

I woke up to the sound of something buzzing. It was my keys on the
nightstand. I was so tired I ignored it. But I still remember this
somehow.

The next morning I was in the washing room. As I got the clothes out of
the dryer, and turned on the washing machine. I heard rumbling coming
from the garage.

I brushed it off and did the laundry.

When I came back to the den, the washing machine was off.

But I had just started it. The rumbling sound was gone. But I sensed
something. It felt like when you dip into water. I felt something was
around me as I stood there, in the den.

Then, I heard something again. The light in the kitchen, it was coming
from that same light. I panicked that it would break again like last
time. It started to flash, but then it was at a steady rhythm.

It beat like a heart…

By some instinct I got prepared. Not sure what for, I just waited.

It then started to beat in a specific way, over and over. It was like I
could count it. Then I remembered I heard this before.

Back in school I taught myself Morse code to curse around my dad.

It was such a random memory, but it was right. It did sound like a word
I learned in Morse code.

I managed to decipher it, but more from memory. I could not have
expected it to form anything coherent. But to my amazement, and horror.

It did.

It was just one, word.

LEAVE

I felt paranoid after that. Around the house watching TV, I felt like I
was being watched. When walking, I would feel a sense that there was
something around the corner.

One day it was storming outside. I was there in the living room. Trying
to just relax in the day. I was not sleeping that well by that point.
But with the rain, somehow I managed to feel a little relaxed.

However, I started to feel tired.

Given I had not been sleeping well. I managed to doze off.

When I woke up, first thing I noticed was somehow I managed sleep. But
then I was wide awake when there was one missing detail.

The TV was off.

It was on when I fell asleep. My heart sank. But for some bizarre
reason, I felt like the feeling left me. That feeling of me being
watched. It was like I could finally go to sleep.

That realization, made me tired. Because I could finally sneak in some
good rest. After all, it was just late in the morning. I got off the
couch and saw something.

At the corner of my eye, a figure. It looked like a shadow. But also
that “mirage” again I saw before. It was at the end of the hall.

It vanished traveling up through the ceiling.

My heart was pounding, I almost did not want to sleep anymore. But I
pushed it down. What could it really do? It’s not a person or animal.
It’s just a “mirage”. Worst thing it could do is raise my electric bill.

I was sleeping like a baby then and there…

I woke up, feeling heat. It moved across my body, like I was being
touched. It felt that close. As I stirred, it quickly ran out the room.
A heavy draft filled the room in an instant.

I sat upright, then I heard something. In the attic, the sound of an old
clock. Playing a particular melody I remember.

It was the Westminster Chime.

My hair was standing up…

It was just in the middle of the day. Probably past 5 AM. That was early
enough to still have sunlight. Considering it was spring at the time.

I felt confident, unlike if it had been in the middle of the night. So I
got out of bed.

I walked through the hallway and into the kitchen. I somehow knew the
path it went. It stopped just down the hall ahead.

It was the same one where I saw that figure. That hall in specific has
the door to the attic.

I was shaking, noticing even my breath was showing. I walked down the
hall, and reached for the door. I pulled down the ladder, and prepared
to head up.

I slowly went up each step. I was now in the attic.

I noticed there were a few things left behind. But otherwise it was a
surprisingly spacious dark attic. One of the things left behind was an
old grandfather clock. I figured that is where that sound came from. But
what could have made it come on?

The attic was not all dark. Dreary daylight outside was cracking from a
small window. It went past me and behind to the rest of the space.

In the light’s direction, I felt a source of heat. It moved…

There it was, in the darkness. I squinted my eyes towards it. There I
saw a figure, but not just any;

It was in the shape of a child.

My heart started pounding, but I was still curious. What could something
like this be? If it really was some anomaly I would not know what. All I
could assume was it’s merely something odd, and I was the first to
discover it.

But, something dawned on me. The idea cracked through weeks of attempts
at ignoring. Whatever the thought was I brushed off. As I felt something
move, it was the heat. Strangely, I felt as if the attic was now empty.
I looked and saw nothing in the room. I concluded the attic truly now
empty.

I prepared to go down the ladder. About to descend, I noticed the room
was somehow darker.

As I fixated in one particular spot in the void…

I heard the sound of a child giggling.

I was out of there not long.

I never went in that house again.
