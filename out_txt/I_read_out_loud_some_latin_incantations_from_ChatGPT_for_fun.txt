I’ve just been house bound the last few months recovering from an
accident. I’m bored and there’s only enough movies and series to watch.

So I started talking to ChatGPT (free of course because fuck AI blah
blah) on random stuff. And I asked it to make up some movie scripts
where a character would say some incantation in latin to summon a demon,
a spirit, an entity or whatever. Make some scary sounding latin phrases
movies would put in these possession movies. And I read all of them out
loud for fun. I showed my husband, we laughed and both tried to read
them out loud, rolling the R’s and what not.

Since then there’s been weird stuff I can’t write off as coincidences
after a while.

We have a touch switch for the kitchen top lights. Basically a button
placed flat in the corner where 2 cabinets met, and we just touch it to
turn the lights on or off. The first time it turned off on its own while
both me and my husband are across the room from it we both laughed it
off. Until it keeps turning off on its own multiple times. We asked the
electrician to have a look. Nothing was wrong. What keeps touching it to
switch off the kitchen top lights?

Stains started appearing on different spots on the ceiling of our flat.
In the bedroom above our bed, in my husband’s work room above his desk.
We asked maintenance to come in and they said there was no leakage from
anywhere for water to seep in to cause stains. But we kept looking at
them grow larger. Today I saw a new spot in the living room. They all
started small, like a hand print, then grew larger in random shapes in
brownish reddish colours.

Yesterday we were watching Netflix and the doorbell rang. Before either
of us moved the show paused on its own. Neither of us touched the
remote. It wasn’t the “Are you still watching?” pause. It just paused.
We went to answer the door, there wasn’t anyone there. We checked the
doorbell cam. There wasn’t anyone there when the doorbell rang.

I started hearing muffled conversations and things being banged about
coming from the next door neighbours. They used to fight a lot so it
wasn’t anything I was not used to. My bathroom is next to their living
room so I heard them fight all the time whenever I would take a bath.
But they had moved out, we both helped when they moved out months ago.
We know that flat is vacant. I called my husband in the bathroom one of
the times I heard the noises. We listened for a bit. I couldn’t hear
anything other than voices going back and forth and things being thrown
about. We looked at each other. I shrugged. Then he asked me “Doesn’t it
sound like our voices a bit?”. I really couldn’t tell.

We talked about the occurrences before bed sometimes. We joked about it
even. I think the stain above our bed looks like a caterpillar while the
one above his desk looks like a malformed penis. He called the dream me
his dream wife because apparently we never bicker and “she” never talks
back. We called out if the “ghost” could help turn off the hallway light
while we’re already tucked in and too lazy to get up for it. But of
course what lever it is it doesn’t work on our schedule or beck and
call.

We started having bad sleep, but work has been intense and we stayed up
late joking about the “touch light ghost” too often. One morning we were
making coffee and my husband casually mentioned the dream he had. He
said he dreamt that we were snuggling in bed and having a full
conversation about having a kid, about me being pregnant, our future
plans etc. And then he woke up and I was still sleeping and he realised
he was dreaming. “One of those lucid dreams that was so real you know?”
He said casually pouring the coffee, and I just felt chill about all
this for the first time. Because he just described exactly the dream I
had, only that when I woke up he was the one still sleeping.

We laugh about it, even told friends we might have “something” living
with us, to keep me company while I’m house bound. For months we watched
and laughed together. Lights turning on and off. Frames falling off even
though the nail is on and intact. Ruffling noises we’re not sure coming
from our living room or the vacant flat next door whenever we’re about
to sleep. Similar voices like his or mine echoing late in the night when
we woke up from those half real half sleep dreams.

Nothing too malicious or evil. Just inconveniences where we have to turn
lights back on, the TV shifts channels or shows without us touching it,
noises at night. The only thing I dislike really is the identical lucid
vivid dreams we both kept having but waling up to see the other person
still asleep.

Then I started feeling ill, worse off than I already was. And I was
supposed to be in recovery from a minor incident. I went for a checkup
this morning. They told me I am 2 months pregnant. We have not had sex
because I was injured for the last 3 months.

I went back into the chat history to see what the hell was the
incantations I read out loud for jest. Which one I read and which one I
asked my husband to read with me. I went back to the conversation. The
chat was still there. Every message I’d sent was intact. But every reply
it gave me had been replaced with the same sentence : This content is no
longer available.

So I’m not sure what we did or said or called out to if anything at all.
But I’m growing something inside my body and it is not a child conceived
by the two of us that I am certain.
