I can hardly believe this really happened.

My hometown has a tale that we always tell when we go to the wood about
a 15 minute walk to get there from my house.

It goes by many names ‘the Stitcher’, ‘the Needle-Worker’ and most
famously ‘Stitched Jaw’.

Its said that back in the 1800-1900’s there was a renowned surgeon who
lived here at our small old hospital.

He was supposedly so amazing that people joked that anyone stitched by
his black thread and hands could never die.

Countless live saved. People who came from all around the world would
come to his surgery.

But one day he stitched himself.

He felt younger, more powerful.

Like he could do anything.

The more he stitched the longer he lived.

By the time he reached his sixties everyone had forgotten about him. His
gift had driven him to madness, drunken with power.

He had gone so insane he sliced his own fingers off, joint by joint and
then he stitched them back together again.

But he didn’t stop there… He kept going, stitching off his hands, arms,
legs. Entire body besides his head. And stitching himself back together.

And when his body kept aging and becoming more decrepit, he began to
replace every last body part.

Becoming a skeleton of what he used to be.

His skin became as white as snow during a cold winter season.

And the reason he’s called Stitched Jaw?

Because it’s all that’s left of his original body.

According to legend he disguises himself in the snow with his pale skin.
The only thing giving him away is the black thread keeping him together.

It’s said that every winter in the very wood nearby my town he searches
for his next victim to replace his decaying body.

I’ve always been a skeptic of this story.

Until last night.

Me and my friends, Cam, Jason, Kyle, Olivia, Clara and me, all decided
to go camping in the wood.

When we all set up it was around midday so we all grabbed our sandwiches
and it was all normal, a little chilly cause it’s dead of winter but its
always cold around here.

And then it all got worse at night.

The full moon beamed down on us as we lit the fire and sat around it.

Cam, always being the dickhead told the story to us, his torch pressed
against his chin as he speaks.

Shivers run down all our backs.

Then Clara speaks up, her voice shaking “You know it’s just a story
right…? It’s just the 3rd years scaring you.”

Then I stupidly back her up “Yeah it’s just a campfire story. Things
like that don’t happen.”

Oh but they do.

Cam grins at us then says “If you’re so sure then go out in the forest…
Alone. We need more firewood anyway.”

Clara slowly nods, fear in her eyes. “Alright let’s do it.” 

I laugh trying to hide my fear. “Yeah, let’s do it.” 

But as we descended into the dark, snowy wood, in a pile of glistening
white snow was a single black thread stitched into something as pale as
fresh white snow. 

Clara takes small steps, “Why did you have to do that. We’re gonna die
out here. What if it finds us.” 

I wave my flashlight around. “It’s not real. He just wanted to scare
us.” 

“If so, mission accomplished.” Clara shivers “Can we go now?” 

“We need more firewood.” I point my flashlight at a pile of conveniently
placed firewood all in one pile over top a mound of snow. 

“At least we found some fast.” 

“All thanks to me” I laugh. 

“Well hurry up and pick some up. It’s cold and scary out here.” 

“Oh, so you think stitched Jaw’s gonna come get you. Soo scary.” 

Jess reaches out to grab some firewood. 

But underneath the firewood is a single black thread. 

Clara’s eyes widen. “I-Is th-that. Jess don’t!” she yells, stammering
and fumbling over her words. 

my hand continues to get closer. “Don’t do what?”  

CRUNCH. 

Teeth meet bone. 

I scream when I look at my hand, a large bite mark with blood covering
my hand like a skin tight glove.

There he was.

There it was.

Pale skin so pale you could hardly see it against the snow, small
strands of hair barely making it not bald.

It had wonky yellow teeth… Or well what’s left of the teeth but they
looked too sharp like a freshly sharpened knife type of sharp.

The Jaw was at a 90 degree angle to the rest of its head.

Black thread loose around its jaw as if it undone the stitches just for
the bite.

“What is this weird Frankenstein looking thing!” I yell. 

It begins to move closer. 

Clara starts to run “I think it’s S-Stitched Jaw.”

I grab her hand as I begin to sprint after her.

but so does Stitched Jaw. 

We weave through trees and corners, jumping over rocks and stumps but it
just won’t stop chasing after us. 

“Why is it still following us.” I scream.

“I don’t know.”

We make a sharp left turn and we keep running. Clara jumps over a river
and I follow.

When it instead continues chasing us the black threads keeping its feet
attached to the rest of it’s body comes loose on a rock and it collapses
the floor.

We keep running until we reach the camp and we yell at everyone to hurry
and back up but they don’t listen.

Then Stitched Jaw finally catches up with us but everyone just stands
frozen in place with disbelief and fear.

Jason, the charismatic yet caring one of the group snaps out of the fear
and picks a stick from the ground and setting it alight from the
campfire, he tries to scare it off but it seems unfazed.

I attempt to unfreeze them all and then they do eventually snap out of
it and get to action while Jason wards it off.

We grab everything and I go over to help him while Cam just stands there
like a dumbass.

By the time we reach the path its already 4am and everyone is terrified
and exhausted.

Let’s just say I won’t be going in that wood ever again.
