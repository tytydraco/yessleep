A close friend of mine recommended I share my experiences here. Together
we might be able to make some sense of what’s going on.

I have been keeping a daily journal since I was around twelve years old.
At first it was all on paper composition notebooks, but on my fifteenth
birthday my parents gave me a macbook pro that I’ve been writing on ever
since.

A couple months back, I was backing up my journal on the cloud when I
got an error for it being full. I didn’t expect it to happen this
quickly, I had just upgraded to the 2TB tier after all.

A quick check and I found thousands of files maxing out the storage. All
of mine were still in there, but there was a bunch of repeat entries to
my journal. Most of them were pretty mundane, just simple repeats of
exactly what I had written. However, a few of these entries were pretty
significantly different than what I remember. I distinctly remember my
23rd birthday being a small party with a couple friends and multiple
slightly drunk games of Catan. In this entry though I went to the
library and had a reading marathon until a late night drinking game
where I had one too many and had to write the entry on the following
morning.

Even if that one was a bit strange, my interest wasn’t properly peaked
until I found this entry. For the sake of clarity and to give all of you
the same intrigue as I feel, the following is copied from the file
directly.

“June 27, 2022

This morning I had what was left of my deer jerky, as well as some dried
fruits and berries. I skipped lunch and for dinner boiled two potatoes.

The journey north has been quiet so far. Edna and Rory were talkative as
always and filled the boring safe hours with their warmth. After setting
camp for the night those two wondered off to find some suitable firewood
and set up the perimeter. In those few hours without them I realized
exactly how grating this trip would be without company, rather I believe
by the end of it I might have gone insane.

During the travel period today, we saw three husks. There were no
confrontations as we were limited on ammo and medical supplies. I was
also happy to avoid the husks as my leg was still bothering me from lask
week.

The first husk we are calling ‘David’ and was fairly human looking with
a bark texture on his left forearm and a slight elongation in all limbs.
He was still wearing a suit and tie, which had only sustained minor
tears. For the most part he kept his distance and didn’t do much.

The second husk was by far the least normal. It looked like and ashy
grey puddle on the ground. The only humanoid feature it retained was a
face that shifted around the ‘body’ of the husk. After about ten minutes
of seeped into the ground and was no longer visible. We moved quickly
after that encounter.

The third husk was a typical type two specimen. We named this one
‘Johnny’ since we spotted it knocking over some porta pottys. The only
thing of note was a healing injury it had retained on its front left
paw.

If we continue with the pace we have been traveling at, we should get to
New Harvest within a week or so.”

I ruled out the possibility of this being a simple prank. First of all,
the metadata of all these files confirmed they were written on the right
dates for the entries. I also know that spoofing metadata is
considerably harder than just editing the files. Second, after looking
over all the files, I determined that they are all written in the exact
same style. The way I start my entries, they way I finish them, and all
the verbage choices. They all lined up with my style.

I have only read through a small fraction of the new entries, and I’m
currently deciding on the best way to handle this going forward. I will
likely post more of the interesting ones in the coming days, and I hope
this is as exciting for y’all as it is for me.
