I know this sounds crazy, and even I’m starting to think I might be
wrong, but my house is changing every day. Furniture I never had is
appearing in my house while things I’ve had since childhood are
disappearing. But nobody seems to believe me. Not even my parents seem
to realize what’s going on.

I’m not crazy.

It started about a month ago. I’d come back from work and find my stuff
moved from where I’d left it. Nothing major at first. My laptop would be
on my bed instead of my desk. My keys would be on the kitchen counter
when I was sure I’d left them beside my computer.

I thought my mom was moving things around, but she always denied it. I
figured maybe I was just forgetting where I’d put stuff. It happens.

Then I started noticing other things.

A chair appeared in the living room that I’d never seen before. I asked
my parents where it came from, and they looked at me like I was stupid.
They said we’d had it for years.

I genuinely have no memory of that chair.

Then, about a week ago, I woke up in the middle of the night and went to
the bathroom to pee, but the entire room had disappeared.

Instead, there were stairs leading down to the basement.

I just stood there for a few seconds because my brain couldn’t process
what I was looking at.

I clearly remember our bathroom being right across from my room, down
the hallway. I’ve been living here for 10 years. I know the layout of my
own house.

There shouldn’t have been stairs there.

I even turned the hallway light on and checked the other rooms because I
thought maybe I’d somehow gotten confused after waking up. But
everything else looked normal.

The bathroom was somehow on the opposite side of the house.

I told my parents the next morning, and they acted like I’d completely
lost my mind. My dad actually laughed and told me I should get more
sleep.

The problem is that they don’t remember the bathroom ever being where I
remember it.

It didn’t stop there.

There was a wardrobe in my room that just disappeared today.

I’ve had that wardrobe for years. I had clothes in it, along with some
small trinkets from my travels that I actually cared about. They’re gone
too.

I searched every room in the house. Nothing.

I asked my parents if they’d moved the wardrobe somewhere, and they said
there had never been a wardrobe there.

I showed them where it used to stand.

They just looked confused.

I don’t know what is happening. I’m not lying, and I’m not crazy. I know
what I’ve seen.

Nobody is believing me.

I’m posting here in the hope that someone might be able to help me
figure out what’s going on.

Update 1: Since so many people are asking about it in the comments, I’ll
just make this clear.

I’m not schizophrenic, nor do I have any history of hallucinations. I
don’t do drugs either. I wasn’t under the influence of alcohol during
any of these incidents.

I’ve also been sleeping normally. At least as far as I know.

And before anyone suggests it, I don’t think I need therapy for this.
This isn’t the result of any trauma either.

Update 2: To the people saying things like I’m dimension-jumping or that
this is a glitch in the Matrix, I think you might actually be right.

There is no other explanation.

I checked my phone today for pictures of the missing wardrobe and the
things that were inside it.

There are no pictures.

I know I took pictures of some of those things. I’m absolutely certain I
did.

And the worst part is that I’m somehow starting to forget the missing
items.

I can remember that something was there, but I can’t remember exactly
what it looked like anymore.

According to one of the comments, I might be synchronizing with this
universe. That’s the reason for my memory loss.

I don’t know if that’s actually possible, but at this point I don’t know
what else to believe.

What should I do?

Update 3: Things have gotten worse.

I’ve had a headache for the last two days. It’s been getting
progressively worse, and today I had a seizure.

I went to the doctor, and he recommended that I get an MRI.

I’m honestly terrified right now.

I’ll update once I get the report.

Update 4: The reports came today.

I have a brain tumor.
