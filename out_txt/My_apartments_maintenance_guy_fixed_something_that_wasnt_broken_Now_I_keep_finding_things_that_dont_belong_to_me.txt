I live in a regular, slightly run down apartment building the ones you
know you’d imagine someone who doesn’t have that much money to afford a
decent apartment complex. It’s old but it’s good enough for now anyways
that’s not the point.

Yesterday when I came back from my job I found a note stuck to my door,
it read “Notice of Entry” slip I took it I didn’t read further well I
opened my door got in changes clothes to something comfortable like
pajamas. I was curious because I hadn’t finish reading the note so I
went back to read the whole note because I was curious, the note read
“Resolved reported wall cavity leak / resealed drywall.”

Funny enough or maybe the weird part? Is that I hadn’t reported anything
I never reported a leak and I myself didn’t even know there was a
problem with that wall. I checked behind my bedroom closet where they
had worked supposedly, the drywall is completely freshly patched but the
paint doesn’t completely match, anyways I didn’t think much about it
after I checked I ate dinner watched tv a little in my couch that I had
found in a garage sell after that I decided it was time for bed around
12:30 AM

Because I still had work the next day. I went to bed and before like
always I drank a glass of water like always it’s a bad habit because it
has me waking up in the middle of the night to use the bathroom but to
me it’s necessary, long story short I went to sleep and like usually I
woke up around mid night around 3:25 Am to use the bathroom.

I stood up slowly and I walked to the bathroom I did my business and
went back to bed as I was trying to regain my precious sleep I suddenly
hear tapping sounds now I thought my brain was playing a trick on me. So
I tried to ignore it but then I hear it again like fingernails lightly
clicking against the back of the plaster where the wall was fixed. I
obviously got freaked out I hid under my covers and I went to sleep
somehow which I don’t remember quite how well next day I go to work like
any normal day, I return back home pretty early I had finish that day
early I was planning on cooking myself some pasta so I open the
silverware drawer and to my surprise.

I find a old rusty pair of brass scissors on top of my forks I get
freaked out obviously because I didn’t remember buying or placing it
there I carefully grab it just in case it had something and I throw it
out. Long story short I had to wash my silverware all over just in case
anyways my dad goes like usual dinner, Tv, bed I go to sleep I drink a
nice cup of water I sleep around mid night I wake up to use the bathroom
I do my business I go back to bed suddenly I have a gut feeling telling
me to check. My phone so I do I open it up and there I see it! Three
photos of me taken at 3:14 Am around 5 minutes before I woke up to use
the restroom now I’m confused and scared that night I went to my parents
house to sleep for the night I was just to scared to sleep that night in
that apartment.

The next day after work I go down to the managers office to ask if they
had seen anything in the cameras that were in the halls and also to ask
who authorized the Maintenance check the land lord looks at me. And gets
unusually quiet and he tells me the person who had authorized the
maintenance check was a guy named Carl..

He told me with a quiet voice that he had died three years ago, after
getting trapped in the buildings crawl space. I get freaked out
obviously but I decided to go back to my apartment now I’m writing this
at 3:14 Am I am under my covers coming back was my biggest mistake I can
hear the drywall patch behind my closet starting to bulge out I’m scared
I’m afraid I don’t know what to do? I’m afraid that if I run out
whatever is in there will chase me. I tried calling the damn landlord he
won’t pick up the phone I’m hearing footsteps above my ceiling but i
live on the top floor there is nothing or should be nothing up there
should I cut open the drywall or try to make a run for it?
