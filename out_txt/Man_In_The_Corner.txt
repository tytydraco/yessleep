I’ve always felt safe in our home, a large oak house painted red with
white facades.

The antique wallpaper had slight cracks, reminding me of all the
childhood mischief that used to take place in its rooms.

Needless to say, I have never felt unsafe or afraid playing and living
within these walls.

But that was about to change.

My parents had invited their mutual friends Jessica and Andrew. This was
not unusual since they enjoyed spending time with each other, and both
my brother Thomas and I found them very entertaining.

We finished eating the main course, after which we all thought it best
to take a break for a few minutes. Thomas and I did not complain since
we knew this meant dessert was to come after.

I, who had turned fifteen just a week earlier, was the main source of
conversation between the adults. Andrew had just asked me what I’d
gotten as a gift for my birthday when my face lit up.

I’d had the best gift a fifteen-year-old could ask for, the new Nintendo
Switch. Both Jessica and Andrew pestered me to go get it, which I didn’t
mind. I went into my room and grabbed the Nintendo, when I heard
something.

“Hey you! over here.”

Out of the corner of my eye I saw a man crouched down beside my bed. I
quickly turned towards the figure, who without a sound, puts his finger
over his lips.

“Shush, be quiet”

My muscles tensed up. I couldn’t move, I couldn’t scream. I stood there
as if frozen.

The man stays crouched as he moved towards me, He was just about to get
to me, when the door opened.

“What’s taking you so long?” Andrew asked.

I looked at Andrew, then back at the man, gone.

“He was just… there was someone….” I said, still in shock.

Andrew looks at me confused.

“What happened?, you look like you’ve seen a ghost” he said.

“I could’ve sworn I saw a man over there” I said pointing towards the
now pile of clothes on the floor.

Andrew walked me back to the table, where I told everyone what had just
occurred.

Jessica and Andrew chuckle a little, I peer over to my parents who both
sit there frozen, locking eyes with each other.

“Mom… Dad…. what are you doing?” Thomas asks.

Small pearls had started forming on my mothers forehead, her skin
turning white as snow.

She began breathing unsteadily, as she reached for a cup of water she
spills it on accident.

She swear under her breath, takes in a deep breath and gathers herself.

“Oh, nothing honey.” she said her voice shaking a little.

“Well, I can’t be the only one ready for dessert now can I?” my dad
said, wanting nothing more than for the subject to change.

We all nod in agreement and decide to continue on our evening as dessert
was served.

After a few more hours of chatting and reminiscing on old memories, it
was time for Jessica and Andrew to head home.

We waved them off and as the door came to a close, I was reminded about
the incident earlier.

“Why did you look so worried back there?” I asked gently.

“It brought back memories from a past incident that’s all.” dad
answered.

“Well what incident? Can’t you guys tell me?” I muttered back

“I suppose you’re old enough by now, but don’t tell your brother,
promise?” said mom.

“I promise” I said excitedly.

Me, Mom and Dad sat down in the living room as I handed my brother the
Nintendo and told him to go up and play. He jumped with excitement and
he followed my instructions and went upstairs.

My mom started telling me the story.

Ever since you were a toddler you’d always had the room you still reside
in. Your father and I would always take you in there to change diapers,
and put you to sleep. You’ve never had a problem with staying in that
room, until it came.

It started gently, once you turned three you would come wake us up.
Always saying the same thing….

“There’s someone in my room”

Every time we would follow you back into your room you’d point into the
same corner beside your bed.

We’d always chucked it up to childhood imagination or nightmares, until
your brother, around the time turned three, also began talking about
“the crouching man” or “the man in the corner”.

We’ve had countless of friends over with kids in the same age who all in
some way or another communicate that there’s someone there.

That was when your father and I also started to become frightened.

There were also occurrences of adults feeling as if they’re being
stalked or followed while walking up the stairs. Remotes and electrical
systems draining of battery even though they’ve newly been replaced.

One friend even stated to have seen and heard a man by the edge of the
bed at night.

My mother ended the story by telling me an old news report, told to her
by previous owners of the house.

The report stated that a father who’d just arrived home from work, found
an unknown man looming inside his three-year-old son’s bedroom. Being
shocked and angry the father charged the man, hitting him repeatedly
until he was unresponsive. Medical staff at the scene had tried to
revive the man but he was later pronounced dead at the scene.

Since the telling of this story I’ve always felt watched and frightened
to sleep in my room, and I’ve since moved out of that house into an
apartment.

I’ll just hope he doesn’t follow me here and that all of this has been a
trick played by our very own collective imagination.
