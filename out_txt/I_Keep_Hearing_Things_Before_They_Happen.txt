It sounds impossible. The ravings of a mad man. But it’s the only way to
explain what’s been happening.

 

 

It all started last week. I was getting ready for work when in the
shower I started humming this tune. I’m not usually one to sing in the
shower or anything like that but I just couldn’t seem to get this tune
out of my head. I couldn’t place it, for as far as I could tell I had
made it up. I was still humming it by the time I got in the car and
turned on the radio. I had moved to change the station, but I stopped
myself.  The song was playing. Even after hearing it I couldn’t place
where I had heard it before. It was then that the song ended and the
presenter cut in.

 

“Good morning everybody, that was Rory Porter with Got To Keep On off
his new album Stardust. We’ll have more off his new album released today
later on, so don’t go anywhere!”

 

 

Two days later, I was sitting in my flat watching television when there
was a knock at the door. I got up to see who it was but by the time I
got to the door no one was there and the hall was empty. I closed the
door and sat back down assuming I had been the victim of some childish
ding dong ditch prank - then the door went again. As before I got up and
opened the door and found no one. I went out into the hall hoping to
find the miscreants responsible but had no luck. I had just returned to
my door when the elevator across the hall opened and outstepped a young
woman with black hair. It was my girlfriend. In one hand she held a
bottle of red wine and the other a plastic bag marked with the words
“China Palace”. I let her in and she explained how she had been meaning
to surprise me and that I had spoiled her cute gesture. I let her in and
tried my best to not give away how disconcerted I was. Had I really
heard her knock before she had even arrived? Well, no. She hadn’t
actually gotten the chance to knock before I met her in the hall.

 

“Where’d you get this?”

 

She was referring to the new Mahogony coffee table she had begun setting
the food out on.

 

“My mum gave it to me, she didn’t need it anymore.”

 

“It’s nice”

 

She knocked on the wood four times testing its thickness.

 

 

If this had been all I might’ve managed to chalk it up to coincidence,
my mind imagining wonder in the mundanity. Even if I convinced myself
this was some kind of precognition, it wouldn’t be necessarily cause for
alarm. But this isn’t all I’ve been hearing, this is just the sounds I
can explain. The sounds don’t always immediately precede the action that
cause them. The sound of crunching snow even though its spring, someone
snoring in the middle of the day or my mother’s voice when she at her
house two hours away,

 

 

It’s worse at night. I often wake to what sounds like someone walking
around the house, their feet creaking up the wooden steps or shuffling
against the carpet just outside my door. The first time this happened I
had even called the police, sure there was a burglar or some axe
wielding crazy in my house. Even now I have to get up and scan the whole
house before I’m able to get back to sleep, it just sounds so real. But
maybe even this I could’ve lived with, pushed to the back of my mind.
That would be until tonight. Just ten minutes ago, I had once again been
awoken by a sound in the night, but this was different. A scream from
downstairs. A man’s scream. My scream.

 

 

While I write this, I still haven’t been downstairs, but I know I’ll
have to. Maybe there won’t be anything there tonight, though that might
be worse. It would just mean there’ll be something another day and all I
can do is wait for it. I’m going downstairs now, I think I can hear
breathing.
