Part 1 This is an update for those who are waiting.
 
My boyfriend started asking questions and I knew I wouldn’t be able to
truly tell him what was going on. He’s a lot like my parents in the fact
he’s never believed in the paranormal, and I worry if I try to explain
my family’s “pets” to him, he will claim I’m pranking him or that I’m
just plain crazy. I thought about a lot of excuses, but I knew I
couldn’t fully lie to him (surprisingly I’m a very bad liar), so I just
told him that I wanted to stay with him for a bit, which isn’t all that
unrealistic. Though, I couldn’t stay forever and I did need to go pick
up some extra clothes.
 
I tried to convince myself that everything would be okay. I just needed
to go in, stuff clothes in a suitcase, and then get out. But of course,
that would be far too easy.
 
This morning when I walked into my apartment and turned on the light, I
nearly screamed. I stifled it and managed to look past the creature with
a straight face. It was in the middle of the room. It has never been in
the middle of a room. Like I stated in my past post, it always hides in
shadows and corners, only moving when no one was around. But there it
was under clear lighting, staring me down. And then it did something
else new. It moved. Its steps were slow and deliberate. It was
completely silent and was heading in my direction. I knew I couldn’t
just stand there, so I walked past it towards my room. As I moved by the
creature I felt its hot breath on my face, and then I heard the most
disgusting gargled noise, almost like some type of laugh? That sound was
the most horrid noise I have ever heard in my life.
 
I closed my bedroom door behind me and immediately covered my mouth as I
started to cry. I wouldn’t allow myself to be too loud, I wouldn’t dare
let it think I saw it. But I heard it. A clicking sound that had haunted
my childhood. A sound you would use to grab an animal’s attention. A
sound that the creature always used to try and trick you. The being was
right outside my door.
 
I started packing as fast as I could. I shoved random clothes into a
suitcase, whatever I could grab first. I had no time to care about
having nice outfits. Just as I started zipping the suitcase, the most
terrifying noise I could have heard in that moment made me completely
freeze. The door opened.
 
I held my breath as I finished closing the zipper. I could feel the
presence behind me. I could feel the hot breath again. And I could hear
it continuing to click at me. I kept packing items. I put my textbooks
in a backpack. I threw pens in the bag as well. I started rummaging in a
drawer despite knowing I didn’t need anything from it. I was stalling. I
was too terrified to turn around. The room felt stuffy, I felt dizzy,
but I kept a neutral face. I kept my back to the thing that had stopped
clicking, but had began whistling. A low, long whistle that sounded
plain wrong. Something imitating a whistle sound, but not actually
whistling.
 
Then I felt it right behind me, I felt something on my shoulder and
heard my name whispered in my ear. It sounded so wrong. So incorrect. It
barely sounded like my name, but I know for a fact that it was. I held
back a shudder. I pushed down a scream. I resisted the urge to
completely break down.
 
I shut the drawer and pulled away from the creature, still not
acknowledging it. I grabbed my backpack and suitcase and walked briskly
through my apartment until I was outside of that now wretched place. I
kept walking. Down the hall, to the stairs, down to the lobby of the
apartment building. I kept walking. Outside, down the sidewalk, and all
the way to an empty park. Tears were streaming down my face. My entire
body was shaking. I finally felt that I was far away from the creature
and allowed myself to completely break down. My heart was still
pounding, it’s still pounding as I write this. I had horrible thoughts
running through my head. I didn’t know if I could handle this anymore,
handle it anymore. I felt dirty. I wanted to burn the shirt I was
wearing. I wanted to scrub the shoulder it touched until the skin was
raw and bleeding. I wanted to dig my fingers in my ears and tear out the
way it spoke my name, the way it acted like it knew me. Maybe it does
know me.
 
I knew I needed to find serious help.

I found some type of shaman online based on the advice of some comments
and DM’s I received from my last post. I spoke with him on the phone for
a long time, describing my situation in as much detail as I could. I
begged him to meet with me today, I begged him to help me. I offered
money I didn’t have. I was desperate and was willing to do anything. I
think he took pity on me because he offered his services for free.
 
A few hours ago, we met up at a coffee shop nearby my apartment
building. I don’t know what I expected, I guess one of the stereotypes
you see of spiritual people in movies, but he seemed like a normal man.
His name was Jacob. After I was confident he wasn’t a psychopath who was
going to murder me, we walked to my apartment.
 
As we took the two flights of stairs to my apartment floor, my heart was
racing. I gave him all the information I could, and he seemed confident
he could solve my problem, but I was still terrified it wouldn’t work. I
was terrified that this would make everything worse. As we approached my
front door, I took in a deep breath. I steadied myself, and opened the
door.
 
At first, the creature wasn’t visible. My eyes scanned the room in the
most casual way I could, and then I spotted it. Behind my television. It
was taller than before. It had a more threatening aura of before. I
stepped inside and immediately went to a kitchen cabinet and grabbed a
glass. I looked over at Jacob, he was following my lead and completely
ignoring the creature. I heard heavy footsteps, but didn’t dare glance
over. The clicking noise returns.
 
I filled my cup with water and took a long sip before I looked at Jacob,
“Well?” The word was more like a huff of air. My heart was still racing,
but I wouldn’t let it show on my face.
 
“The first step in the ritual is-” A blur moved across the room and
Jacob was on the ground screaming. The creature was on top of him,
growling and screeching as it ripped out his throat. I looked up at the
ceiling and drank my water again. I couldn’t acknowledge it. An innocent
man was being murdered in front of me and I acted like it wasn’t
happening. The wet slurping sounds from the creature made my stomach
roil. Acid rose in my throat, but I just swallowed it back down. I
turned and opened a different kitchen cabinet and grabbrd an anti-nausea
medication. I popped it in my mouth and swallowed it with my water
before heading to the exit of my apartment. I ignored the blood that got
on my foot as I walk past Jacob’s now lifeless body that was being
devoured.
 
There was a crunching sound as the being broke some type of bone in
Jacob’s body. I jumped. The noises the creature was making stopped and I
heard the floor creak as it stood up straight.
 
I ran as fast as my legs would take me. Out my apartment, down the hall,
down the stairs, out of the building and down the sidewalk. My heart
raced. My chest burned. I felt like I was about to vomit. The metallic
scent of the blood still stuck to me. The joy that the creature took in
destroying Jacob would play every time I allowed myself to close my eyes
because an innocent man’s eyes are closed forever and it’s my fault.
 
I don’t have many choices now. There’s a dead man in my apartment. Well
maybe his body isn’t left, but his blood is. When his family goes
looking for him my phone number will be in his phone. The police will
find my name and my address and will walk in on a bloodbath. Though,
every police officer might just die the moment they walk in. That will
also be my fault.
 
I grabbed all my stuff from my boyfriend’s place. He was confused, but I
couldn’t explain. I wouldn’t know how to start. I won’t stay with my
parents, can’t. I can’t handle seeing any of those creatures ever again.
I’m on the move. I plan on staying in random motels, but never for too
long. I worry that even a motel could be considered my new “home” and
therefore the creature will appear for me again. I don’t have much
money, but I’ll use my savings for now. I’ll keep going until I can’t
anymore.
 
I fear that the creature will have to catch up to me eventually, but
maybe that’s okay. I just hope that once I die, the curse dies with me.
