Preface:

I am a grown, fit man. Serviced in the USMC, and a LEO

Every Nov. a group of buddies go bow hunting in Ohio, on Wayne National
Forest. Not everyone from the group hunts the same area. A buddy and I
branch off and try to find the less travel portion of Public.

Year 4 (guessing):

We found a hunting bottom. We navigate a path togehwr through a thicket.
Once we get through the thick stuff I go left and he goes right. Sun
sets and know we find our way to the meeting spot, were our paths meet
so we can walk out together. We meet, and start walking towards the
trail. We cross the creek with my buddy leading the way, and are dipping
and bobbing through the thicket. Out of no where a scream comes from
behind us, maybe 40 yards away. It was an abnormal high pitch sound. He
looks back at me confused. All of a sudden it is on the left of us, and
closing in. I pull a arrow tipped with a broadhead from my quiver, as my
buddy retrieved his pistol. Before he could get the gun out this
disturbance is now off to our right, like it was circling us. Once the
gun was out the woods went quiet again. We make it to our side by side,
and beat feet out of there. Back at camp we try to rationalize what it
was. An owl, bobcat, coyote? How did it move so fast, how did it flank
us, and what the hell was the noise it made.

Year 6:

Last day of our hunting trip, but we are in the same bottom. This time
we move further into the bottom, and up on ridges. Once again he goes
left, I go right. We are probably 150 yards away from eachother on
separate hills ridges. We are texting and decided we are getting down at
1030. 1029 hits and I start removing my gear to decend down the tree.
Exactly in the direction from where my buddy should be I hear a yell
“JIM”. I froze. Trying to see if I being messed with, and trying to see
anyone in the area. Nothing, no one. Again I hear the same thing,
crystal clear “JIM”. I thought maybe my buddy had a fall, or was hanging
upside down because he knows my name is not Jim.Luckily at the top of my
stand I had cell service. I call my buddy. He answers. Confused he seems
normal. I asked if he was yelling Jim. No he answers. He says im still
in the stand. He didn’t hear the voice. How couldn’t he? It was coming
from his direction. The hair on my neck and shivers down my back still
happen thinking of it.

This year:

We hunted a different hill all together this yr. He goes right I go
left. He was hunting an old logging road. Hunts over get down meet on
the riding trail. This time he heard the name Jim. Just as clear as a
humans voice.

What is going on?
