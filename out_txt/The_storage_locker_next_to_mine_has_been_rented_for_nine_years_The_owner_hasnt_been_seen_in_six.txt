I live in a condo by Square One in Mississauga. Nothing fancy, just one
of those glass towers they threw up in 2016. My storage locker is on P2,
unit C-15, down the hall from the garbage room where it always smells
faintly like old mop water.

The locker next to mine is C-14. It has a padlock the color of dried
blood and a layer of dust on the mesh door so thick you could write your
name in it. I know because one night I did. Just my initials. Stupid, I
was waiting for my laundry.

I asked Dana at the front desk about it back in the spring. She pulled
it up on her computer and said the unit’s been rented since 2017,
payments come out automatically every month, never missed. I asked if
the owner ever comes by. She said the email on file bounces and the
phone number is disconnected. They can’t cut the lock without a court
order, and nobody’s going to court over a storage locker.

That should have been the end of it.

I do my laundry late. The machines are on P1 and there’s never anyone
down there after midnight, which I liked. About two months ago I was
folding shirts around 1 in the morning when I heard it. Three short
buzzes, from the direction of the lockers. Like a phone vibrating on a
hard surface.

I told myself it was the pipes. Old buildings make noises.

But I kept hearing it. Always three buzzes. Always between 2 and 3 AM. I
started staying down there on purpose, sitting on the bench by the
vending machine, just listening. On the third night I timed it: 2:47 AM,
three buzzes, three seconds apart.

I recorded it on my phone. You can barely hear it on the recording, but
it’s there if you turn the volume all the way up.

Last month I taped a note to the mesh of C-14. It said: “Your phone has
been buzzing every night. Please call the front desk.” I felt insane
doing it. I checked the next night and the note was gone. Not on the
floor. Gone.

The buzzing stopped for a week after that. I told myself I’d imagined
the whole thing, that the note probably fell behind the lockers.

Then it started again. But now it was four buzzes.

I went down there last Tuesday at 2:40 and put my ear right up to the
mesh door of C-14. At 2:47 the buzzing started. Three buzzes. And then,
so quiet I almost missed it, a fourth sound. Not a buzz. A voice. Faint,
like through a wall. It said “hello?” The way you answer a phone when
you’re not sure who’s calling.

I ran for the elevator and I didn’t wait for it. I took the stairs from
P2 to the 11th floor and my legs were shaking so bad I had to sit in the
hallway.

The next morning I told Dana everything. She went quiet in a way that
scared me more than the voice had. She said the name on the C-14 account
was a man who died in 2019. Car accident on the 401. The payments kept
coming because they’re automatic, and nobody at the management company
ever connected the dots because why would they.

She said she’d escalate it. That was four days ago.

Last night I went down to move my stuff out of C-15. I couldn’t stand
being next to that locker anymore. The padlock on C-14 was gone. The
mesh door was hanging open about six inches.

The locker was empty. Concrete floor, bare walls, nothing in it at all.
Except in the back corner, plugged directly into the concrete wall, was
a phone charger. There is no outlet in that corner. There are no outlets
in the storage lockers at all.

The little brick was still warm.
