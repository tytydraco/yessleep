My wife and I have been married for nearly four years now, and I would
be lying if I said things hadn’t cooled down since our honeymoon. The
fiery passion that once purveyed our early years dating through college
stuck around just long enough for us to seal the deal before seemingly
blowing out of the window the moment we landed back from St. Lucia.

It started with small things: little affections through the day, favors
done to take the edge off after work, and making love like we once did
all disappeared seemingly out of nowhere. When I attempted to bring this
up to her, I did so in a non-accusatory way. Of course, who was to say
she didn’t have something going on personally that I had no right to
judge her for?

“Honey, listen, I wanted to talk to you about how you’ve been feeling
lately. I guess I’m worried for you, maybe even worried for us.” We both
sat on our new couch, feet apart, her curled up in a little ball at the
very end. I waited for a few moments without a response before I spoke
again. “If you need space, I’ll give you as much as you need; it’s
whatever you want-”

“No, it’s okay, we can talk.” She did not look at me or even move when
she spoke; it was eerie. “There’s something I want to try, something I
need your help with.” Stunned by how direct she was being after all this
time, I obliged immediately.

“Yeah, of course. Anything. Are we talking communication? Boundary?
Therapy?”

“Sexual.”

“Oh, okay.” My wife and I had done nothing of the sort in many months.
“Well, I’d be happy to try anything that you’re comfortable with.” She
did not move.

“I am going to go into our room. I am going to start something. I want
you to finish it. Can you do that for me, baby?” Beyond all of my worry,
I could feel myself beginning to become excited, but still I was
suspicious.

“Sure, but would you tell me what it is we’re going to be doing?” She
did not respond, and many moments passed in silence. “Okay, I’ll let
surprises be surprises.”

Then slowly, she rose from her balled-up position and shuffled to our
bedroom, shutting the door behind her. Waiting for a moment in silence,
I went through in my mind anything of this nature that she and I had
ever done, trying to find a clue as to what was going on.  

After a time of waiting, I began hearing a strange scratching that
startled me upon first hearing it. Unnaturally loud it radiated even
from under the heavy shut door. Standing and facing it, I attempted to
imagine what on earth she could be doing in there before as quickly as
it began, it stopped. After a slight moment of silence, there was a
sickening crack followed by a deep moan of pleasure that raised the
hairs on the back of my neck.

Deeply conflicted, I listened as splintering cracks echoed out from the
room, followed by cries of pleasure from my wife to the degree I had
never heard before. Very soon the calls transitioned from simple
expressions of pleasure to my name and more than that, a beckoning for
me.

Stepping forward with a terrible mix of horror and excitement, I stood
before the door, unable to move. For moments I remained frozen, unable
to move, until without my doing, the door handle slipped free on its
own.

Falling open slowly, I blinked several times as the door revealed the
inside of the room. My wife sat on the bed, completely undressed and
beautiful just as I remembered, with the entirety of her scalp removed
and several fragments of skull missing. She dug her fingers into the
wound and produced another sickening snap of bone before moaning deeper
than ever.

Paralyzed, I watched as her face contorted in pure pleasure before
turning to mine.

“Please, please keep going, keep going,” she moaned, face twitching.
Stepping forward, she approached closer and closer until appearing just
inches from my face. Breathing her last breaths, she exhaled in
satisfaction before falling to the floor, brain spilling all over the
carpet in a mix of red and brown sludge.

In a state of shock, I attempted to rationalize what I had just seen but
fell short. Kneeling beside my wife for hours, I could not come to
understand what happened. Seeing no other choice I got into my car and
drove as far as I could in one go, watching through news outlets the
unfolding of the search for me in correlation to the “murder” of my
wife. I have no idea what I’m going to do or if it even matters in the
end. Never in a million years will my story be believed, so what choice
do I have but to run? I am having trouble finding the resolve to
continue, so I write to find any advice as to what I should do next.
