My name is Alex (24M). Two months ago, my girlfriend Chloe (23F) and I
rented a small, isolated mid-century house just outside of Seattle. We
had been searching for a place for months, drowning in high rent prices,
so when we found this property surrounded by thick pine trees for half
the market price, we thought we hit the jackpot. The landlord was an
eccentric elderly man who refused to meet us in person, conducting
everything through mail, but we didn’t care. Everything was absolutely
perfect until this week. Chloe has always been a quiet, peaceful
sleeper. She’s the kind of person who wraps herself like a tight burrito
in the blankets and doesn’t move a single inch until her alarm goes off.
She never talks, never walks, and barely even snores. But on Monday
night, something completely unnatural and malevolent took over her body,
and I am still shaking while typing this out.

It started at exactly 3:14 AM. I know the exact time because the bright
red numbers on our digital bedside clock felt like they were burning
into my eyes. I woke up freezing. The central heating was supposed to be
on, but the bedroom felt deathly cold, to the point where I could see
thick plumes of my own breath rolling into the dim air. The atmosphere
felt heavy, almost pressurized, and the room smelled overwhelmingly of
rusted metal, old copper coins, and damp, rotting earth. I turned around
to check if Chloe had pulled the blankets over herself, and my breath
caught instantly in my throat. The blanket was thrown on the floor.

Chloe was standing on her knees right in the middle of the mattress. Her
spine was bent backward at a sickening, sharp, completely impossible
angle for a human being. Her eyes were stretched wide open, staring
directly at the ceiling fan above us. She wasn’t blinking at all. Her
chest was heaving up and down violently with shallow, wet, animalistic
gasps that sounded like a dying predator. Before I could even form a
sentence or call her name, her head snapped toward me with a horrible,
wet clicking sound in her neck bones. Her face twisted into a
terrifying, distorted smile that literally stretched from ear to ear,
making her jaw look completely unhinged and hollow.

She lunged at me with impossible, brutal speed, pinning my wrists down
to the bed. For a girl who weighs barely 120 pounds, her grip felt like
heavy solid iron blocks crushing my bones. She leaned her face mere
inches from mine. Her pupils were so massively dilated that her eyes
appeared entirely pitch-black, swallowing the whites completely. Cold,
thick, black saliva dripped from her trembling lips directly onto my
cheek, burning my skin with an icy chill. When she spoke, it wasn’t her
voice. It was a chaotic, vibrating layer of three distinct voices
speaking in perfect unison—a crying young child, a raspy, whistling old
man, and something heavy, deep, and dead. The entity hissed: “This house
is already full. Get out before we dig your grave next to the others.”

Panic completely took over my brain. I screamed at the top of my lungs
and kicked her off me with all the strength I had left. She flew
backward off the bed, crashed violently against the heavy oak
nightstand, and shattered our large ceramic lamp into a thousand sharp
pieces. For a terrifying ten seconds, she just lay there on the floor in
the dark among the broken glass, growling and snapping her teeth like a
rabid dog. I was trapped against the headboard, sobbing, waiting for her
to attack again.

Then, at exactly 4:00 AM on the dot, the grandfather clock downstairs
chimed. Instantly, all the tension left her body. Her eyes closed, her
breathing stabilized, and she fell into a deep, normal sleep right there
on the floor. In the morning, she woke up confused, complaining that her
shoulders were covered in deep purple bruises and asking why there was
blood and shattered glass everywhere. She remembers absolutely nothing.
I had to lie and tell her that she had a severe sleepwalking episode and
knocked the lamp over. I am writing this on Tuesday afternoon, my hands
are trembling uncontrollably, and I am absolutely terrified of what she
will do when the clock hits 3:00 AM tonight.
