It was on a cold random Tuesday and i just came back from school, one
boring Tuesday. I grabbed a coat and then told my mom I was going over
to Damon’s place since we had a YouTube channel and he was the one with
the PC– I didn’t have one, I was raised by a single mom so I didn’t
really have a lot of things.

When I arrived at his place I found Jack, Peter, Alias, and Matt. We’ve
been friends since the first day of high school and we also had one of
the greatest YouTube channels on paranormal activity. All of it was
staged and rigged. Our recent videos had a huge number of viewers and
comments. We didn’t know what video to make next. As we were replying to
comments we saw a comment which said “I bet you 200 bucks you won’t
survive the night at Goneman’s woods” by a guy called Hungry Waffles.

It had that name for a reason. Only a few people who went in ever came
back out, especially in the night. Officials say that it’s due to bear
attacks– my part of town had a big population of black bears. Some
people report seeing a seven foot figure which looked a bit human but
has long limbs and it walks on all fours with glowing eyes that look
like human and beast. Some say it has long powerful hands and it smelled
like rotting flesh. Most of them follow our YouTube channel so it had to
be a hoax right?

Damon jumped off his chair and looked at me. I knew what he meant, all
of us did. He always does this when he has ideas on what videos we
should make. So we prepared a fake replica of the figure that people
kept describing. We grabbed torches and a couple of snacks. Gonemman’s
woods weren’t far from town so we walked there. The sun was slowly
setting.

We were outside the woods. The sun was still setting. It was getting
foggy so we planned to go in and then out in a few minutes. We entered
and the sun set. We didn’t walk long and then we turned back. The trail
we used getting in here was gone. Fog was everywhere and we were
surrounded by massive trees. I was starting to get scared, the guys saw
that and started mocking me. We kept walking, searching for the way out.
We eventually got tired, I was worried that my mom would go out looking
for me. Damon told us he was going to take a piss. We sat there messing
around with the replica. We waited for him so we didn’t get separated.

For a moment it was quiet. Then we heard Damon singing, he hated
singing. Peter, Matt and Alias went to look and they told me and Jack to
remain. Damon stopped singing and then we heard screaming and struggling
and then a crunch. My heart fell in my gut because that’s the exact same
sound I hear when Bruno chews on a soft bone and then I hear Damon’s
voice and then the three. They were laughing. I sighed in relief
believing it was a prank. We saw an old man in the distance, something
felt wrong and my blood began to freeze. “Kids follow me there’s a bear
that has been spotted in this part!” He yelled. Jack didn’t hasitate. I
hasitated for a second and then I followed. I thought Damon and the
other’s were behind us when we ran.

As I got close I saw it. The man’s eyes skin was too pale. He looked
dead, and his eyes were entirely black. It was too late for Jack. When
he realized a huge hand was on his neck, he was turning grey and he
looked like a balloon about to pop and the camera dropped. His back
snapped. I turned back and began to run when I realized I was the only
one there. Where was Damon and the other’s? Why did the old man kill
Jack? I ran hard as I asked myself those questions. While I was running
I heard loud footsteps running behind me. Then I tripped over and then I
got up. The footsteps had stopped. I hid in the bushes to catch a
breath. It was very quiet, too quiet. I cautiously got out. I quietly
and swiftly tried finding my way back to the trail.

My mind was everywhere and my heart was violently beating from the
encounter. I heard a scream, it wasn’t human or animal but something
that sounded sinister. I looked behind me. There it was, a seven foot
figure with wide red eyes, long limbs, features that looked wrong and
unnatural and it was on All Fours looking at me. Then I realized all
this time I didn’t outrun it, it was simply preying on me. It’s cold
smile told me one thing. That it was having fun. I screamed and called
for help, it was in vein no one heard me. I tried stepping back. It
violently pulled my legs quick and then I hit my head on a rock. I felt
something warm and wet running down my neck.

I felt it dragging me through the woods. I was facing up and I could see
the sun slowly rising. Time went by quickly. I could feel my skin
peeling. My eyes began failing me and I was too tired to scream or move
my body. Then in a short while all I saw was darkness. My body felt cold
and numb, I couldn’t feel the pain anymore. I felt like I was drifting
into a deep sleep. That was the last sunrise I ever saw.

Morning came but it was as if it was just another bear attack.
