First time posting but have had the memories and experiences of this
house on my mind for years.
For as long as I can remember being the oldest of five siblings, we’ve
always lived in close quarters in small apartments. A family of seven
sharing two-bedroom apartments, my single mother just doing what she
could to keep us happy fed and housed. I’m grateful for all that she has
done for us, even if it meant sharing a room with two sisters for almost
ten years. From kindergarten all the way into high school I’ve shared
with my sisters while my little brothers slept in my moms room.
Eventually end in the living room when my mother got married. Before
moving into a three level home in Saint Paul we were living in an
underground apartment that had only one window and one exit and two
bedrooms. Mind you we know this wasn’t up to code this is just what my
mother was willing to take at the time.

Before moving my mom got a divorce and ended up re marrying, my baby
brother’s father. A decent Guatemalan man, he didn’t talk much but was
strong and had a high standards. A leader’s mentality. He made good
money working construction and other odd jobs. He loved and wanted to
take care of my mother, even if it meant moving her seven bastard
children into a huge home so his son could have a better place to grow
up. I believe he just wanted to get away from the memories of our old
apartment and what was left of her ex-husband, my little sister’s
father.

My mother and him found the Thomas house. Not the best neighborhood,
more like the hood. Which is probably why they got a great price on the
home with a room for every one of us, which is something neither of my
siblings had ever experienced before. There was a two-car garage, a
fenced-in backyard and an all-season porch at the front of the house.
There was a decently sized living room, kitchen and dining room. The
basement as well had two finished rooms. Beautiful dark wood details and
built-in’s, a cute pantry to match. It had an attic space with a huge
room hallway and big room, from the main level of the house was a door
with stairs that led to the attic. Up there stairs and down the hallways
adjacent to each other were two crawl space doors one door was
permanently locked and the other was your average foam-filled, dusty
crawl space of the roof. Farther along the hallways was a walk in closet
with sliding doors and past that was a door that led to What was my
bedroom. Being the oldest I got dibs on the largest room of the house
secluded from everyone else at the highest level.

17 years old, At the time having lived in a basement room with no
windows and sharing it for almost nine years I was absolutely grateful.
A dream come true, especially when I saw the two windows that led out to
the roof from the front and back of the house. I definitely planned on
hanging out there when I sparked up. In the daylight all I could think
of was that my dream had come true for me and all my siblings. They
would all settle into their new rooms which was very different from
sharing everything but I knew they were grateful as well. Thinking back
on it now I’m grateful we moved at the ages we were considering we were
all getting older and needed the space to grow into the individuals we
really wanted to be and no longer the kids operating off of survival and
constantly fighting each other.

Once nighttime came the first night of living in our new home was when
the first experience started for me and my family. Setting up my twin
bed for the first night I had spent all day moving my stuff around and
decorating what I could. Having shared such a tiny room with my sister
before I realized I didn’t have enough stuff to really fill this new
huge room. So everything looked pretty bare. Just a twin bed in the
corner of the room closest to the window of the front house, a single
shelf in the middle of the room for when I got a tv. My art already
littering the walls and a single led strip closest to my bed which
belonged to my old room. It was dark now and the yellow light of the
bedroom was all that I had. My youngest sister (star) was with me.

Coming from a Hispanic family there is a superstition about spirits and
evil eye energy. So all the siblings had red string bracelets on their
wrists from Mexico to protect them from evil energy. Mine had already
broken off a while ago and my youngest sister who was only four at the
time still had her bracelet. I can’t remember exactly what we were doing
but I know we were on my bed just talking about the new house when she
was handing me something. I noticed as she lifted her hand to me I
watched as her once tightly double-knitted string bracelet unraveled
right before my eyes. I literally watched the string unravel itself in
slow motion, we both did. Seeing it fall to the floor my heart sank with
it. Being a spiritual person myself, seeing this made me instantly
uncomfortable but I tried not to show it. I quickly picked it up and
tied it back around her wrist as tight as I could so the knot would not
slip again. And if you know anything about these evil eye protection red
strings and bracelets it’s known that when these pieces of jewelry
broke, it meant that it had just protected you from evil energy. I had
her run back downstairs to go be with other siblings and we just acted
as if nothing really happened. Considering how young she was in sure she
payed it no mind but as she left me alone in my new attic room I felt
disturbed to say the least.

This having just happened had me remember a dream I had before moving to
this new home. The dream consisted of my family in a big house with a
fenced backyard surrounded by tall trees. In this house I found myself
walking through it with no light and there was a specific closet that
had the darkest energy of all. Walking past it in my dream I could hear
the energy of an old women which reminded me of the insidious movie.

This is just one of the first small encounters in this home that I think
about before the storm really started. Going along I have so many times
weird shi has happened in this place so I’m just gonna break up the
experiences into their own posts. But there will be more to come as I
write them up and remember all the little details. Living here was what
really sparked my interest and fear of the supernatural and confirmed
everything. Living in this place was very traumatizing to my psyche and
having healed and grown into an adult in her own home I finally feel
comfortable enough to share.
