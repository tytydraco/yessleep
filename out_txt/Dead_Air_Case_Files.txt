Hello everyone. This is going to be a long intro, and something to help
ease into this. This is a new account, and it’s not associated with my
regular account, privacy reasons.

I work for a Sheriffs Office here in Colorado. We’re pretty rural, and
having been here for about 10 years I wear many hats. Everything from
patrol, investigations, traffic enforcement, and most recently, IA,
Internal Affairs. 

This was not something I really wanted to do, and it’s not really a fun
position to be in. No one wants to be the “shit bag” IA cop, but I’m
fortunate that I work in a good area and have good deputies. I’m not
gonna fall into the rabbit hole that exists to slam or praise police,
but in general our department avoids a lot of attention, due in part to
our deputies not being a bunch of assholes. And this helps a lot in
terms of looking into cases.  

In my time here I have worked both day and night shifts. Everyone has a
personal preference, I was a night shift guy when I could be because the
good calls happen there. Day shift was a lot of administrative stuff,
low priority calls, and running traffic. Night shift was interdiction,
burglaries, interesting shit, but as time goes on, you eventually get
tricked into promoting, and eventually you’re sitting at a desk most of
the time and reviewing what everyone else sends in. 

Looking back to when I first started, I had been told all of the creepy
mysteries, and legends, and all the spooky shit you see in rural
policing. It happens, and every so often, you find yourself seeing
something, or hearing something, or responding to a call out on some
abandoned county road because some local meth head calls in aliens.
Sometimes you arrive, and there are strange lights that suddenly
disappear, and said meth head is actually a grandpa who is sober as a
judge, and you really don’t know how to tell the guy it was probably
nothing. 

So, if people reading this have interest, I’ll keep it going into
multiple entries, because there are a lot of strange and unexplained
calls. I will be redacting information, locations, names, anything that
may reveal where we are, deputy identities, and reporting parties, as I
am not going to go through and locate all of these people to see if they
are okay with me telling their stories. Some of these may have body cam
footage, or in car video. IF, and it’s a big IF, I’m able to redact some
of the video, I may try to upload supporting video, but it can be a real
pain in the ass to redact info, cover faces, and other things of that
nature. I also don’t want this to turn into a problem for the
department, so if it turns into that, I will stop.

This is one of those cases that our current sheriff still talks about.
He was called out for the search, even though he was still a jail
