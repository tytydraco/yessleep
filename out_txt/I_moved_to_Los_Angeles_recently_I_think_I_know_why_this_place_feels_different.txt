Hey everyone. My name is Cassandra, but for brevity’s sake you can call
me Cass. I’ve been living in LA for half a year, but last week was when
things got… weird. A little bit about me before I get into the meat and
potatoes. I’m a 30-year-old university dropout from Arizona with a
proclivity for political history which I planned to take hold of and
turn into my Bachelor’s in Political Science, but uni proved to be not
what I was expecting. After I turned off the lights on that waste of my
time, I decided it was time for me to try something different somewhere
else.

Once I got the money, I packed my bags and moved out from my old place
in Mesa to a decent enough apartment in Encino. This was my first time
being able to actually live somewhere in another state rather than just
staying in a hotel, so I had the usual jitters.

As my plane crossed state lines into LAX, I felt a rush of slight nausea
the second I entered the boundaries of the city. I attributed it to the
anticipation, but I’ve been on an airplane before, so a part of my soul
was apprehensive about what this feeling meant. Brushing it off, I got
myself situated in the city fairly easily.

I won’t bore you all with every detail of how the past six months have
been, but I landed myself a nice job as a librarian ( to satisfy my
inner history nerd ) and I get to enjoy the nightlife of the city.

Now, here is where things get weird.

I have always been interested in the paranormal since I was a kid, with
my first taste of it being when I was 11 and I saw a man in late 19th
century clothing standing on top of a building and walk away while
fading into nothing. Granted, these sightings have been few and far
between, but from what I’ve heard talking to people who have also seen
things, it shapes their entire perception of the world. You can tell
what’s fake and what’s not by experience on the matter.

This is not to say I’m a whackjob about all other things paranormal. I
don’t believe in demons or fae causing people trouble. With how crazy
this world is, you’d think somebody would have caught definitive proof
on camera by now. But for all my time on this earth, I haven’t once seen
anything huge.

Until last week.

I was talking on the phone with my new boyfriend Chris about work. We’ve
known each other for about eight months now. One of the reasons I wanted
to move to LA was to be closer to him, and so far things have been
working out well for us. I walked through the dining area, and out of
the corner of my eye I noticed a utensil drawer was opened all the way
despite it being closed for the entire time I was home. As I tried to
brush it off and continue my conversation, I looked past the window and
spotted a faint multicolored shimmering where I could see out into the
rest of the neighborhood. Before I could get a better look, it seemed to
drift out of sight, at which point the drawer slammed shut.

Since that incident, I’ve noticed it more and more over the week.
Sometimes chills come over me, I see something out of the corner of my
eye when I’m out and about, things get moved when I’m not looking. I
need answers, because the paranormal believer side of me has been slowly
overtaking my rational side.

I can’t help but feel like there’s something else to Los Angeles that
makes it feel the way it does. And that thing has something to do with
it. Any help would be appreciated.

Cass
