Hi. I’m not very familiar with this app, so I’m not really sure if this
is how you’re supposed to do this. Anyway, my name is Luca, and I’ve
been seeing some strange things lately.

I’ve always had strange dreams, usually confusing ones. I’ve dreamed
about almost everything I’ve seen in my life: friends from school,
neighbors from my street, members of my own family. All of them have
appeared in my dreams at some point. I’ve learned to live with it since
I was a kid. Somehow, it went unnoticed until my late teens. I don’t see
dead people or anything like that, which made it take me a long time to
understand what was happening to me. At first, they were more like
memories.

They started bothering me when I was around seventeen. Back then, they
weren’t particularly unusual. I used to see my mother as a young woman,
dancing at her debutante ball. It didn’t seem strange to me. There was a
huge framed picture of that day in my father’s office, and my mother
herself had told me that story countless times.

I’d see her waltzing with my father, moving back and forth across the
dance floor in her shiny navy-blue dress, her blonde hair pinned up. My
father wore a black suit that looked a little too loose on his thin
frame. Everything else was blurry. The other young people at the party
looked like smoke, surrounded by darkness, while the two of them
remained perfectly clear. I liked that dream for a long time.

But shortly after my seventeenth birthday, my uncle’s house caught fire.
He and my cousin barely managed to get out. After that, they had to stay
at our house for a while. During that period, I slept in the living
room. My parents thought it would be better to let them have my bedroom
so they could feel more comfortable after the shock of the accident and
all that.

On the second night they stayed with us, I dreamed about something else.
I could still see my mother, but she was a little younger than she had
been in the dancing dream. She was crying. Back then, it took a while
for these dreams to become coherent. It took me a few days before I
could make out anything else. Gradually, I started seeing my uncle. He
was crying too. The two of them were embracing in what looked like a
hospital waiting room. I remember spending many nights there. Whenever I
found myself in that dream, I’d watch them, trying to understand what
was going on. Eventually, after night after night, I started seeing more
things: the two of them in the waiting room, holding each other and
crying, while my grandfather argued with a doctor. It took several more
nights for that argument to become clear. He was angry, talking about my
grandmother. She was sick. Something terrible had happened.

The conversations in these dreams are hazy. It was difficult to remember
what was being said. Even when people spoke, their words sounded like
scattered noises coming from everywhere, echoing throughout the dream. I
struggled to make sense of the conversation: my grandmother was going to
have surgery, she was pregnant, and the baby had died.

That dream kept me awake for a long time. I had never heard that story
before. Until then, it had seemed like just another lucid dream, the
kind of nightmare you have more than once. Those repeated nights I
mentioned weren’t intentional. They were scattered, spread out over
time, and seemed distant just hours after waking up. But that particular
experience stayed with me for days, until I finally worked up the
courage to ask my mother about it.

She was startled and nervously asked where I’d heard that story. She
told me it was true, but made me promise never to bring it up with
Grandma or Grandpa. After that, the strange dreams continued. I kept
seeing things I had no way of knowing about. It took me a long time to
understand that those “dreams” weren’t what they seemed to be. I was
seeing things. Real things that had happened at some point in the past.

The first time I deliberately tried to see something was during my last
year of high school. Back then, I had a girlfriend named Sheila. She was
a nice girl. We were together for three years. I never told her about
any of this, not even when we slept together and I spent the entire
night dreaming about the day she fell off a slide as a child and broke
her leg.

During our second year together, someone broke into her school locker.
And while I was helping her fix the lock, I experienced one of the
things I would eventually come to call a Message. It came like a flash,
suddenly and without warning. I got dizzy and had to be helped by
Sheila. It took me a while to understand what had happened. Later that
day, I decided to try again. I touched the locker door.

I tried to calm myself down the same way I did when I was falling
asleep. It took a while. I thought I was finally losing my mind,
imagining that my hallucinations were becoming something more than that.
Then, one last time, I closed my eyes. When I opened them, I was
somewhere else in the hallway. At the very beginning of it.

Being inside a Message is difficult to explain. I know I’m still in the
real world. I could feel my hand against the cold metal locker door, but
the sensation of walking around inside that place felt so real. It was
like being in two places at once.

I walked down the hallway. The walls and lockers were made of mist,
almost solid, yet somehow not quite. Sheila’s locker stood out among the
others, glowing faintly. I stared at it for a while until I heard
footsteps. A boy appeared from the direction I’d come from. I can’t
remember his name now. I think it was Junior or something like that. I
watched him emerge from the mist, becoming visible while the rest of the
place remained hazy. He used a hairpin to pick the lock and, with quick
hands, pulled Sheila’s bag out.

That afternoon, I stopped him on his way out and told him I wouldn’t rat
him out if he gave the bag back. I managed to get my girlfriend’s things
back, and after that, reaching the Messages became easier and easier.

Now I understand that my “gift” (I like calling it that) was basically
the ability to see the traces things leave behind in the universe.
Whenever someone does something, or anything happens at all, a trace is
left behind. Over time, I realized that more recent Messages were easier
to see. The further back they went, the hazier they became. And above
all, I couldn’t see my own traces.

I think I’ve been seeking out more and more Messages lately. My usual
pastime was going to a bar near my house. It was an old place, and there
were Messages everywhere: two soldiers secretly kissing in the bathroom
hallway, a waitress with dyed hair slipping and breaking her knee
because of a shattered soda bottle, the owner of the bar dancing with
his wife when the place first opened. The past had become a refuge for
me.

I’m telling you all this so you can understand the context of what I’m
about to describe. Well, two days ago, I found something in the
Messages.

It really started yesterday. I’m staying at my mother’s house for a
while. Since I left my last job, I’ve been able to spend some time with
her. She’s been feeling pretty lonely since my father passed away.

It was nighttime. I left my room looking for something to eat. I knew my
mother hid chocolates somewhere. I searched the drawers, the cupboards,
the refrigerator, even the false bottom of the wine cabinet. Nothing. So
I decided to take a peek into the “before.” I touched the floor and, in
the blink of an eye, I was somewhere else in time.

I saw the kitchen bathed in the light of the setting sun. My mother was
sitting at the table, enjoying a box of chocolates. I waited, and after
stuffing her mouth with several pieces, she closed the box. I watched
her walk over to the refrigerator and hide the chocolates underneath the
vegetables, all the way at the back. She closed the refrigerator and
headed into the living room.

I was just starting to wake up from the Message when, out of the corner
of my eye, I glanced at the window and saw something in the backyard.

When I came to, I looked at the window again, but there was nothing
there. For a moment, I’d even forgotten about the chocolate. I went back
into the Message: my mother eating the chocolates, hiding them in the
refrigerator, and then leaving. That’s when I became certain of what I
was seeing.

When I entered a Message, I couldn’t feel the cold or be affected by
anything. But this… it was as if I could actually smell it. Everything
else was soft and made of smoke, but HE was so real that I began to
doubt whether I was still inside the Message.

He was standing on the other side of the window, out in the backyard,
close enough for me to see him from the waist up. A man, covered in mud
and leaves. He was staring at me silently, with a calm, gentle smile on
his lips. He was completely still, without moving a single muscle. And
there I was, staring at him, while he stared right back at me.

Then, it moved.

Its hands pressed flat against the glass and pushed the window upward.
The window opened, and an entire arm reached inside. When it brought its
head through, I stumbled backward.

I snapped out of the Message, fell backward, then quickly got back to my
feet and looked at the window again. I panicked. I looked around the
room, which suddenly seemed enormous. Had that thing gotten inside? If
it had, where had it gone? I needed to know. I couldn’t leave something
like that inside the house with my mother there.

I touched the floor again. And there I was, watching my mother eat the
chocolates, put them away, walk into the kitchen, and then that thing
appearing at the window.

I held my breath.

First, it watched. Then it opened the window. First the arm, then the
head. By the time I could see its entire chest inside the window, I was
practically suffocating from holding my breath. Water and mud dripped
from it everywhere. It looked naked.

Just as I could see half of its body, a noise rang out. A howl? A
scream? I couldn’t tell. Something sounded in the distance, and in one
sudden, rapid movement, completely unlike its agonizingly slow movements
before, it jumped back out and turned to one side. The noise sounded
again. It turned toward the opposite side and ran.

I’ve never seen anyone move as robotically as that son of a bitch.

I snapped out of the Message. I was standing in the kitchen, staring at
the now-closed window. That Message had been pretty clear, which meant
it had probably happened recently. I looked outside, toward the woods
surrounding the small town where my mother lived. Nothing out of the
ordinary.

I couldn’t stop replaying what I’d seen in my head. The way its body
twisted as it crawled through the window, so slowly it almost seemed to
be moving in slow motion. Its fingers gripping the wall so tightly that
they’d left marks.

I stayed awake all night. My mother was working the night shift at the
hospital, where she’s a nurse. When she got home this morning, she
mentioned that she’d noticed things had been moved around, but told me
not to bother looking for her chocolates because they were all gone.

“I saw you, kid,” she said with a laugh. “Don’t bother looking. I’m
starting a diet. No more chocolate.”

Honestly, I’d completely forgotten about those sweets. I wanted to ask
her something else.

“Mom, do you remember seeing any mud on that window?” I pointed toward
the spot where I’d seen HIM.

“Oh, yeah. I’ve scrubbed those stains on the wall, but they just won’t
come off. There was a stray dog getting into everything around here over
the past few days. I think I must’ve left the window open, and it tried
to get inside. The whole window was covered in mud when I noticed it.”

“Has that happened before?”

“Those stray animals are always showing up. Can you believe one of them
got into Elizabeth’s kitchen? Poor woman found her whole house covered
in mud. Good thing she wasn’t home, or she would’ve had a heart attack.”

Elizabeth was an elderly neighbor. She was a nice woman. When I was a
kid, I’d often help her tune her TV channels. I thought about that thing
getting into her house and looking for her. My mother asked why I was so
curious about it, but I changed the subject.

I haven’t tried again since yesterday. I know this might be difficult to
understand, but I’m genuinely confused about all of this. I’ve thought
about trying to see it again, maybe getting a better look at that guy.
But I have to admit, I’m scared.

I don’t know if I should do it. I honestly have no idea what I’m
supposed to do. I’ve been keeping all the windows and doors locked ever
since, without letting my mother notice.

I need some advice on what to do.
