The path to exorcism is paved in blood. But what many don’t know is it’s

With the advent of a worldwide church and the resulting influx of

This is known, what they do for miracles. But what is less known is what

I am Dr. S, a member of the committee devoted to confirming satanic

In the bowels of the holy city there is a concrete bunker that is
unavailable to the general public. Those who know of it often spread the
rumor that it is some sort of doomsday structure for the Pope and a
select few Cardinals. We appreciate the tale, as it obscures the truth.

In reality, this is where we house our subjects.

The elevator ride to the cell blocks takes seven minutes. In that time,
enclosed in the ten foot square wood paneled space, I began to sweat. I
confess, even considering my line of work, I am more skeptical about the
supernatural than believing. Schizophrenia, psychosis, and even
sociopathy are more common sources for violence and evil than we give
them credit for. But even though my mind told me this man was simply
some devious, charismatic cult personality, there was a darker presence
that seemed to loom above the three of us as we went to meet him.

To calm my nerves, I went over our objective in my head. Get the
evidence, then get out. Then some other poor sod can get on with the
exorcism.

The elevator stopped, and we were greeted with the cell blocks
themselves. Their walls were three feet thick, consisting of smooth,
poured concrete. Inside the rock were embedded religious signs from six
different faith traditions. We never questioned as to why such mystical
artifacts outside of the catholic canon were included, we just knew that
they worked. No subject had ever escaped before.

The hall leading to each compartment was surprisingly luxurious.
Carpeted floors, a mahogany waiting desk. Abundance was not a stranger
here, even in the cursed depths of the Vatican. The soft fibers
cushioned our feet, and silence made our footsteps, our breathing, sound
loud and troublesome. We announced our arrival by the very fact that we
were alive.

An armed guard stood watch, and examined each of our name badges. Once
he was certain of our identity, we were subjected to a search. Trust
runs thin down here. He confiscated Dr. F’s fountain pen (he forgot that
gilded surfaces are forbidden down here, the inherent deception in their
design makes the user susceptible to satanic influence) and asked Mr. M
to turn out his pockets twice. Once we were deemed non hazardous, he led
us down the cell block.

Our destination was the last door to the left. The guard slid a pure
silver key in the lock and turned. He stepped aside. Mr. M took a moment
and sifted through his file. “Ready?”

Dr. F glanced towards me, waiting for my answer before giving his own.
But my stomach was in knots, my chest a boiling mess of panicked
heartbeats. I wanted this to be done. So even though it was a lie, I
nodded. Ready.

After a moment Dr. F followed suit, and Mr. M grasped the doorhandle.

The door opened, and we three slipped inside. It slammed shut behind us.

The interrogation room was sparse. There were padded walls, a padded

We had been given photos of the subject as part of our preliminary

his claim that he was, in fact, possessed. But the man behind the table

doctors.”

This was not the face of insanity. I would easily pass this man on the

Mr. M was the first to get moving again. “Good evening.” With a

occupied, went to stand in the corner.

Carefully, we opened our files and pulled out our questions. Dr. F took
a portable recorder, switched it on then set it on the table. There came
a clank as the possessed man shifted in his chair, iron shackles wrapped

In essence, what we were doing here was like a play, each researcher
