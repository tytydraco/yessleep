It was a long day. I just wanted to get through it. I just wanted the
pressure to easy. Stop by the bar was just part of my routine. I told
myself to only have two or three drinks, but you know how it goes. Two
or three drinks becomes four or five. I needed to get home. I did not
want to leave my car at the bar. I have driven home a hundred times
before and always been safe. As I drove, the lines on the road seem to
move but I am almost home. The damn radio station is playing that same
song that is so frustrating. I just looked down for a second. When I
looked up, there was a cat in the middle of the road. I swerved; I lost
control.

Here I lay, looking up at the night sky. I can’t feel my body. I can’t
move my body. I can taste blood. I can’t die. There is so much I wanted
to do, so much I wanted to apologize for. I wanted to see her again. Am
I a bad person? Is there a heaven? Shit, is there a hell? I must live! I
must get up. I must get up. Why can’t I move. I can’t die now!

I can see an approaching light. If only I could get help, I will be ok.
The light is getting brighter. The light is so bright. The light is so
big. What if I get hit again. Why can’t I move? The light grows so
bright and then the light is out. Complete darkness. No sound, no
feeling. Will I be ok? A warmness complete overwhelms me. It doesn’t
hurt anymore. I go to speak but nothing comes out. I can feel this
pressure over all of me. I can feel the pressure building. I am suddenly
falling. All the warmth is sucked out of me. It is almost like I have
never experienced warmth before. The cold is bitter. I scream. I can
finally scream. I scream for anyone to hear. Please help me. Is there
anyone there!?

I am weak and hungry. I scream into the darkness. I am trying to look
but I must have something over my eyes. I try to remove what is over my
eyes. I feel a thickness on my face. It just smears. Something grabs me.
I thought I was yelling before. I thought I was cold before. My mouth is
being filled with something. All I know is it is warm and it fills me.
Finally, the panic is gone. How sweet is this taste. I could be here
forever. I love this feeling. I love the warmth.

While this feeling gets almost too much, it is ripped from me. I want it
back! I can’t find the source. I need more. The feeling was so intense.
How could I ever live without this. I am still warm, but I want to be
filled. I search and search and I can’t find the source. In my search, I
find myself struggling to move. I have a darkness come over my thoughts.
The pressure is back. I must live. I didn’t get a chance to see her. Who
is she? Before I can have my next thought. I can’t think anymore.

I am awake. I can move but my movements are so slow. I can see now!
Where is that source? I scream but this time, I am suddenly warm again.
I find the source and it is beautiful again. It warms my insides. Oh,
how much I love it. There are times where I can get my fill and days
where I am ripped away from the source. Sometimes, I have to fight to
find it but every time I find it, it is total bliss. This was on repeat,
I would wake, find the source, eat, and sleep. Slowly, I gain the
ability to move. Slowly, I gain the ability to see.

I tried to stand but my back wouldn’t allow me. I tried to see what was
around but it felt like everything was daunting and wet. The noises
around me were sharp and sudden. I wanted to scream but I became afraid.
What was I afraid of? When I would go to the source, there were others.
Others like me. I was cautious of them, but they seem to bring me no
harm.

I was awake. I was fed. I was safe. This was the way things were. When I
slept, I would have these dreams. Dreams of a place I use to know. There
would be these hands. They would touch my face. They were warm. Warm
like the source. The warmth, the taste, the comfort. After many days of
this bliss, there was this sound. I ran. There was a crash and then it
was chaos. The others were screaming. They were running. I could hear
gnashing of teeth. I could not move. I don’t want to die. Then there was
a silence. A silence that was dangerous. I waited. I fell into the
familiar darkness. When I woke to find the source, there was nothing.
This world has grown cold again.

I screamed out like before, but nothing came. I looked around but there
was no source to find. I waited. The source did not come. I screamed
again. Nothing answered. I did not know what to do. I did not know where
to go. But my body knew. My body moved. And I followed. I went from
shadow to shadow.

The hunger grew. I needed to eat. Where before, hunger was a call, the
source would answer. Now hunger is a hole. I found a place that was
quiet. I curled up. There was no warmth to find. I created my own. The
world was cold but I was safe.

When I woke, I saw movement. It wasn’t the wind. My hunger spoke to me.
I immediately got ready. I waited. I waited for the perfect moment. It
came into view. It had a bunch of legs. It looked disgusting but my body
craved it. My body jumped. My mouth bit in. The crunch, the legs. I
tried to spit it out. I tried to gag. My body ate the whole thing. The
hole was a little smaller. I did not want it. The hunger was quieter. I
hated that it helped. I hated that I fell into the darkness a little
easier.
