So, this recent Halloween in the college town I’m currently in reminded
me of something that happened last Halloween to me and mt friends from
my hometown.

I’m from a smaller town in a place with a hell of a lot of woods. I
won’t be saying the name of my town but just know that it is near a lot
of woods to the point where hunting is a common thing people do and
tourists go there for camping. I’m in college now, far far away from
there and I couldn’t be more grateful. Outside of the wondeful forest
landscape there is something in those woods my town refuses to do
anythign abotu. In fact, they act like it doesn’t exist. Anyone from my
town or the surrounding area has heard stories of things called “Wood
Devils”. They are tall, gaunt, with a moose like face, eyes that glow in
the night, sharp teeth and claws while all being covered in shaggy grey
hair. The stories paint them as shy things that hide behind trees or act
as trees when humans approach and as such my town refuses to acknowledge
they exist. But I swear on my life the stories are bullshit becuase one
of those damned things almost killed me and my friends last Halloween.

So last Halloween to celebrate our last year together before going our
own ways for college me and my friends, who for this post will be called
AJ, Kai, June, and Luke, and me decided to go on a Halloween camping
trip sicne we would all be working most of the summer. Normally, I would
be down for a weekend in the woods but during that time, I had heard
high pitched, piercing howl or shirek like sound comign from the woods
near my house. It was so bad that even our dogs were afaird, the same
dogs who have gone toe to toe with a coyote and won, and my dad’s trail
cameras had been catching something moving around there at night. The
photos showed the glow of eye shine and tall shadows. But my dad brushed
it off as a bear. That’s the thing in my town, whenever something
happens that is clearly related to the Wood Devils the town jsut brushes
it under the rgu and acts like nothign si wrong. So, I was more than
ready to not go on the camping trip but AJ managed to convince me
otherwise. I helped get us ready, even as I had a sinking feeling in my
stomach. In fact, I was so nervous abotu being in the woods I brought
one of the dogs, Oscar, with us. My friends didn’t mind because they
loved Oscar.

Halloween was a school day, so we could only be able to leave around
4:30 PM. We piled into the minivan Luke was borrowing form his parents
and set off for the campground. As I got in I really wondered what would
happen if I just didn’t get in the minivan but I knew my parents would
be made if I didn’t stay with Oscar so I got in, even though my gut
feeling was telling me that something was gonna happen. But we got to
the campsite just fine and set up was perfectly normal. I stared to
think that maybe, just maybe I was being a bit paranoid. But I kept
feeling as if we were being watched. Oscar also seemed to sense
something was up because his ears were basically glued to his skull from
the moment we got to the campsite.

I remember the night it happened. The sun had gone down, it was lightly
raining and sort of quiet in the forest around us. Not dead slient, but
the kind of slient that comes with the rain on a cool night. June was
trying to make a campfire. As we sat around and watched her try. It was
kind of funny at the time when we all heard a loud, shriek like sound
echoing from the trees around us. We all kind of stopped and stared at
each other, mentally questioning if we had all just heard whatever that
was. Oscar looked terrified and I was also ready to pack up and leave.
But AJ just brushed it off as an elk. I remember the arguement we had
that night.

Me: “Dude, that is not an elk! We don’t even have wild elk around here!”

AJ: “Fine, a deer, jsut quit being a scaredy cat and relax”

Me: “There is soemthing out here and I don’t want to stick around for
when shit goes wrong!”

AJ: “Well, Luke has the ekys and the rest of us want to stay. Jsut
relax, we’re in the woods and there are all sorts of weird animals out
here”

I remember being so mad at AJ for that. Just because we’re out in the
woods doesn’t mean we could just ignore the weird screaming. But the
others were on their side, so I jsut sat there, in the worsening rain,
and watched as June gave up on making a fire. We didn’t do any
activities that required a cmapfire that nigth and just slept in the
tent. Oscar crawled inside with us before we zipped the tent shut. We
didn’t hear anything else but the rain for an hour or two. I was
starting to think that I was overreacting again when we heard something
walking around our camp. The footsteps moved fast around our campsite
and beside the rain the only sound we were hearing waere Oscar’s
whimpers of fear. Dread filled my stomach but I didn’t say anything. I
don’t any of us did. The footsteps eventually seemed to wander away from
oru campsite and we tried going abck to normal. But then another
shrieking sound was heard, follwoed by more howls. AJ tried to say it
was coyotes but I remember that he ahd this look on his face that seemed
like even he didn’t believe what he was saying. I remember the feeling
of panic I had as the footsteps got closer to teh tent and I rememebr
hearing whatever was on the other side breathing, slow and steady. None
of use moved or even breathed.

I also remember the thing on the other side tearing into the tent as we
all screamed. I think AJ tried stabbing the thing’s hand? Paw? I
couldn’t really tell or see it well in the dark and the rain. The thing
let out a shriek and we all scrambled to get out of the tent and into
the minivan but the thing was fast. Oscar let out a pained yelp when it
hurt his back leg. I didn’t want to leave him behind so I ran abck for
him, getting attacked in the process. By the time I was back in the
minivan I was covered in blood that could eithe rbe dog or human. But I
didn’t care abck then, none of us did. We just wanted to get out of
there. Luke started the minivan and as the headlights came on we saw it.
I know it sounds crazy but I swear on my life that I saw a pack of Wood
Devils. Luke turned around fast and tore out of there, well he tried
anyway. The things, the Wood Devils, they surrounded us and each time
the minivan moved they got closer, howling and shrieking so loud I felt
like my ear drums were going to burst. The things tore at the minivan’s
metal body and hit at the windows, tryign to get us. I remember the
sound of glass shattering as a wood devil grabbed me and dragged me out
through the broken window. I rememebr the feeling of glass dragging into
my skin and the sting as I fell to the dirt. I remember smelling the
stink of rotten flesh and blood as the thing I think was a wood devil
bit into my shoulder.

I screamed so hard and so loud that I felt like my throat was going to
start bleeding. I couldn’t move no matter how much I wanted to. But then
it let go for reasons I still don’t know. I forced myself to stand whiel
my friend helped me back inside the minivan. I vuagly remember the pain
in my shoudler and my friends shouting in panic while Oscar whimpered.
People were shouting for me not to close my eyes and I felt the car

This is where my memory gets a little fuzzy because I think I passed out
from either the pain or blood loss. My vision went a black and when I
could see clear again my eyes were assulted with bright light and I
could hear a beeping sound. I was in a hospital. The whole hospital stay
is a blur to be honest. Most of what I know is from my family and
friends telling me afterwards. Apparently when my friends brought me in

a lot of blood and needed a fuck ton of stitiches and other stuff. But I
managed to pull through and well, my friends were also a tad beat up.
But the time after I was out of hospital was the worse, not just because
of the stuff I needed to do to prevent all my stitches and shit from
getting infected but because me and my friends had to explain what the
fuck happened becuase what was left of campsite was found. I say what
was elft ebcause the cops told us that all our stuff was torn to shreds

what happened but all they did was keep asking us what attacked our
camp, acting like we didn’t answer every time we told them it was the
Wood Devils. We were so fucking mad at that but they didn’t care.

That’s the thing. In my hometown the Wood Devils do a lot of shit that
gets people hurt or worse and the town does nothing abotu it! They were
told Wood Devils and put the cause of the attack on an angry bear! We
know what we saw and heard and it was no god damn bear! Worse part is,

Though, according to Kai, (who is still living in town) they might not.
I honestly don’t know and I’m worried. As much as I want things to be

Anyway, I needed to tell othe rpeople about what happened because I feel
