My sister (25F) and I (29F) are close. We’re close enough to keep each
other updated on our lives, but not close enough to share relationship
problems or mental health struggles.

She started college before COVID. Before she left, she fell in love with
a boy and got into a relationship with him. I talked to him once or
twice over phone, and both times he was respectful and welcoming. He
even made plans with me to visit my sister, who by then was living 1,000
miles away in a big city.

Then COVID happened, and for the first year my sister stayed on campus.
I think the isolation was taking a toll on her, because she complained
about him a few times. Mostly she said he was dumb. he wasn’t smart
enough to understand the politics behind so many people dying in our
country compared to others. I agreed he was gullible, but I told her he
was still a sweet boy.

A couple of months later, she called me one morning, crying, to tell me
he had died. I didn’t ask much. I just booked her a ticket home so she
could be with our parents.

A few weeks later, I went home to check on her, and she told me what had
happened. They’d had a fight that morning, one of their regular fights
(she was cranky for some reason). I don’t know if she mentioned breaking
up with him impulsively. After the fight, he went out on his scooty. A
few minutes later, someone called his family to tell them he was dead.
His bike was parked on one side of the highway, and his skull had been
crushed by a truck. Not his body, just his skull. It happened in the
morning, and he wasn’t drunk.

We don’t know whether it was suicide or an accident. I think sister
blames herself. I think, deep down, she believes he killed himself
because of her. That’s why she has never agreed to talk about him, even
years later. At least that’s my theory.

The first time I saw her talking to her dead boyfriend was an accident.
I wanted to send myself some pictures we’d taken together from her phone
when I saw a text on the notification bar from her dead boyfriend. It
had already been a year since he died. I know I shouldn’t have, but I
opened the chat and kept scrolling. They had been chatting regularly. I
checked her WhatsApp calls, and yes, she had been talking to him for
hours at night. When I confronted her, she was furious that I had
breached her privacy and refused to answer any questions. She didn’t
talk to me for the next two years.

Cut to my wedding: the day before my wedding, I saw her texting and
giggling again. That night, we were sharing a hotel room near the venue,
and just as I’d feared, she got a call around 11 and went out to the
balcony, in the cold of December, and talked to him for an hour.

It’s been three years since my wedding, and I haven’t seen her since.
We’re supposed to meet next week. I’m scared she’s still talking to him.
How do I help?
