We were on our way back, still pretty far out. The winding roads and
intermittent speeds messed with my perception of distance. My best
probable guess is maybe thirty miles out from Dirthop. Still, not sure.
I don’t know much about frostbite either but I might get it, it’s
already hard enough using my fingers and this is taking too long to
type. I think I’m safe here, if this gets out, then at least I’ve made
it that far.

I’m low as hell on supplies, after we saw it the first time Aaron kept
the rifle up front, and that got lost with him so the best weapons I’ve
got right now are my Bowie knife, torch, and hammer. And I’ve been
trying to stay positive but, these won’t cut it. There’s nothing in here
either, you’d think an abandoned ranger station might have something.
Even the copper in the wires from the walls radios have been stripped
from this place. Ain’t jack shit. I found a candle, some crackers, and a
spoon. Don’t know if the spoon could be of some use but I stashed it
anyway. I’m using the candle as I write this and I’m eating the crackers
too, figured I’d save the food in my backpack for tomorrow.

I’m not going to sleep. I can’t anyway, everytime I close my eyes longer
than a second I see that thing ripping Aaron out from the windshield.
Fuck man I’ve never heard someone scream like that. I think I might
still be in shock, I didn’t cry or scream or anything, and I still
haven’t. I’m definitely heartbroken, Aaron was one of the boys. One of
my best friends. But im handling this better than Ricky did, he wouldn’t
shut up and he made it come back. I tried to get him to stop but he just
wouldn’t stop freaking out. Grown man was crying his eyes out. Fucking
making all kinds of noise and trying to get out of his seat, I’m just
glad I didn’t react like he did. I get that it happened next to him but
I was in the back, I saw it all too, but I knew to keep quiet.

When Aaron shot it after we crashed it barreled straight towards the
front of the truck, it smashed its arm through and ripped him out of his
seat so fast the belt broke. I remember watching the rifle bend In
crooked angle around around his arm when that thing grabbed him,
trapping one of his hands, and rendering the gun useless. And the noises
it made ripping into his body right there, right outside of the truck,
the skin ripping, it sounded like a wet stack of paper being torn apart.
I heard him gasp and moan until he didn’t make anymore noise at all. I
just stayed where I was, the crash had thrown me around a bit but I was
in the back with the pillows and most our bags, and besides banging my
shoulder on the window pretty good I’m alright.

Maybe Ricky was still in general shock after the crash, I don’t really
think he was coherent until he saw it dragging a mangled corpse from the
truck, cause that’s when he started losing it. I told him to stay calm
and just wait but he payed me no mind. Whatever that thing was, it got
sent into a rage once it realized it left Ricky behind. It screamed a
furious cackling howl, and the way it breathed, so fucking loud I could
hear it. It sounded like wooden gears banging together, almost like
dragging a 2x4 behind you up a staircase, a popping clanging sound. It’s
unnatural, it sounds like it shouldn’t exist but it does. After its
scream it unhinged it massive jaw and shoved what was left of Aaron into
its mouth, broke him down between his jaws and swallowed. Then it turned
to the truck. Again it started stampeding straight for us. Ricky only
screamed louder as it got closer. I stayed put and didn’t move. It
slammed into the truck before it pulled off the already bent open
passenger door. I closed my eyes and hoped I’d gone unnoticed. It was
loud but it over quick, and it started dragging away its second victim.

I don’t know what it is and I honestly don’t care. I just need to get as
far away as fast as possible and out of these mountains but with that
thing out here still, I’m not sure how I’ll go about doing that. I’m
pretty sure it’s following me, I heard it’s howl about 20 minutes before
I got here. Now there’s something outside, I hoped it was just a deer
when I saw the shadows pass by the window, but I hear that damn wood
smacking sound. I locked the door earlier and I don’t know if it
understands weak points so I think I’m ok. I’ll update when I can, I
have to let this phone recharge and with it being broken at this point
it doesn’t like charging with it powered on, I have to power the
motherfucker off all the way, each time. I got enough portable chargers
so I can do this for a while but it’s not fun going without a
flashlight. I don’t want to use all the fuel in my torch either so I’m
gonna sit here and try and warm my fingers up till this candle burns out
and hope that thing doesn’t get in here. The most ironic part of all of
this is that it’s my birthday today, the boys apologized about
forgetting a cake or ice cream but never been a fan of that shit anyway
and how would they store that crap in a cooler on a camping trip. At
least I got a candle. Wish me luck.
