When I was little, I lived in one of those suburban neighborhoods where
every house looked almost exactly the same. Same little tree out front.
Same driveway. Same two story shape. Even most of the houses were
painted in the same few muted colors. During the day it was boring more
than anything, but at night the whole neighborhood felt different. I
used to like the quiet of it.

My bed was pushed up against the wall right beside my window. Across the
street was another house, almost directly in front of mine. I used to
look out the window a lot before I fell asleep. I wasn’t scared of
anything back then. I’d just lay there and stare outside, making up
stories in my head or watching cars pass until I eventually got tired
enough to sleep.

At first I really did think it was just a dog. It was standing inside
the house across the street, visible through one of their upstairs
windows. It looked kind of like a golden retriever. Long pale fur, big
body. Oddly friendly face despite the context. Except it was standing on
two legs, and not with its paws against the glass. That’s the part I
always have to explain. It wasn’t leaning against the window like dogs
sometimes do when they’re excited or trying to see outside. It was
standing in the middle of the room behind the window.

Completely upright. Like a person. I remember staring at it for a few
moments because my brain couldn’t really understand what I was looking
at, then I realized it was looking back at me. Not toward my house. At
me. I got this horrible cold feeling that went through my whole body,
and I remember pulling my blanket up higher, but I didn’t look away.

The dog didn’t move either. It just stood there. Eventually I must have
fallen asleep because the next morning it was gone. I told myself it had
probably just been some weird angle. Maybe the dog had been standing on
furniture or maybe I was half asleep.

That explanation worked pretty well until the next night, and It was
there again. Standing upright, watching me.

After that, it became almost normal.
Not normal exactly, but something I expected. Some nights I would look
out and it would already be there. Other nights I’d look and the window
would be empty, and then later I’d check again and suddenly it would be
standing there.

I never saw it arrive. I never saw it leave.

The weirdest part was that I never saw the people across the street with
a dog. I saw them leave for work. I saw them carry groceries inside.
Sometimes I saw people standing around in their driveway, but I never
saw anybody walking a dog. Still, I kept telling myself it was probably
theirs.

There were little things about it that bothered me more the longer I
watched. It was too tall. Its legs looked wrong underneath the fur. And
even from across the street, I could see the whites of its eyes. Dogs
don’t usually show that much white in their eyes, unless they’re scared
or nervous I suppose. But it didn’t seem scared. It also looked like it
was smiling, and I know how stupid that sounds. Dogs can definitely look
like they’re smiling. Its mouth would be slightly open when it watched
me, and I could see teeth. Not sharp dog teeth…human-looking teeth.
Small, flat, white teeth lined up behind its lips.

Sometimes I would convince myself I imagined that part.

Eventually my family moved. I remember being relieved, even though I
never told anyone why. The last night we slept in that house, I looked
across the street one more time and the window was empty. I actually
remember feeling disappointed for a second, like maybe I had built the
whole thing up in my head.

I never saw it again after we moved, at least never while I was awake.
Twenty years later I still have horrid dreams about it. Sometimes in the
dreams I’m back in my childhood bedroom and I already know it’s going to
be there before I even look outside. Sometimes the house across the
street isn’t the same house.

Sometimes it’s outside a hotel, Sometimes it’s across from an apartment
I’ve never seen before. But the window is always there. And so is the
“dog”. Always standing on two legs, always smiling, always staring. But
in my dreams it had this odd leer that I can only explain by telling you
it looks like it’s mocking me. Before it was just staring, now it has
this luring quality as if it wants me to come closer but I never do.

I’ve been afraid of dogs most of my life, and I’ve never really been
able to explain it to anyone without sounding ridiculous. Sometimes I
think moving away didn’t make it lose me, maybe it just made finding me
take longer. and lately, I’ve started looking out my window again at
night. I don’t know why.

A few nights ago, there was nothing unusual outside. Just houses,
streetlights. But then I noticed something in the upstairs window of the
house across from mine.

At first, I thought it was a coat hanging on a door. Then it moved. It
stood up. And even from across the street, I could see its teeth.
