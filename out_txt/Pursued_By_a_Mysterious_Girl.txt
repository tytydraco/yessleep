“In June 2026, I started a course to get a job after being laid off from
my first employment due to a problem the company was facing. I start the
course at 7 PM and finish at 10 PM. I usually drive to the course, but
on June 19th, a rainy Friday, things got strange. I didn’t use the car
because my mother needed it to go shopping. So far, so good; I would
catch a ride with a friend of mine who was also taking the course.

When I arrived, I saw a girl sitting on a bench in a square right next
to where the course takes place. I always sit on that square bench too,
but because of the rainy weather, I stayed in the cafeteria. The girl
came inside as well. She looked different from the others. She walked up
to me and said:

‘Hello.’

I replied the same to her, and then she said:

‘Are you doing well?’

I said, ‘Yeah,’ and she just stood there without saying anything. I felt
a bit uncomfortable and asked:

‘Are you okay?’

She didn’t answer the question, and suddenly her nose started to bleed.
I couldn’t understand what was happening, and right then I felt a chill.
A friend of mine had just arrived, so I told the girl:

‘Excuse me.’

I walked over to my friend, and soon other students arrived too. Nobody
noticed the girl except me. The teacher arrived, and I asked her:

‘Teacher, is that student new?’

The teacher replied:

‘That student doesn’t take classes with us.’

I replied:

‘Could she be from another class?’

The teacher said:

‘We are the only ones here at night. The welding crew had already
finished their class.’

After working on the materials for airplanes, I forgot about the girl
and stayed focused on the course. 9:45 PM arrived, and as usual, we
cleaned the workshop and the benches. There are four benches; I am part
of bench four. We cleaned the bench and then swept the floor. When it
was time to leave, my friend was going to give me a ride. Suddenly, the
most bizarre thing happened. It started raining even harder. In the car,
my friend and I chatted. I didn’t even remember the girl anymore; I was
only thinking about watching ‘Superman’ since the movie was available on
Amazon Prime.

When I got home, I had to lock up my dogs. My mother and stepfather were
supposed to arrive at midnight. I brought my cat inside since she sleeps
in my bed with me as usual. Suddenly, someone knocked on the gate. I
thought it was the pizza I had ordered that night. To my surprise, the
person knocking on the gate was that girl. I looked through the cameras,
since I have the habit of checking the camera to see who is at the gate,
as some kids usually knock on the gate and run away. I was terrified.
How did she get my address when I had never told anyone where I live? I
didn’t open the gate and kept staring at the camera for three minutes
until I felt the urge to use the bathroom. After leaving the bathroom,
10 seconds later, I went to check the camera and the girl was gone. 30
seconds after that, the pizza arrived. I paid 30 to the delivery guy,
put on Amazon Prime, and it was 11:45 PM. I received a message from my
mother saying that she and my stepfather were going to sleep over at my
aunt’s house. So far, so good. I let my dogs out, locked the gate and
the doors of my house, and decided to go to sleep and finish the movie
the next day. My cat went to sleep with me.

At 2:00 AM, I received a phone call. I answered, and I only heard a
woman’s laughter, and then the call abruptly ended. I went back to sleep
peacefully. At 2:30 AM, the phone rang again. I answered, and again, the
same laughter. I got scared and responded:

‘Who is this?’

The call ended. I decided to block the number and went back to sleep. At
3:00 AM, the phone rang again. I said:

‘This can’t be happening.’

I turned off the phone and went back to sleep. At 3:30 AM, my laptop
sent a message that read:

‘Hello.’

I replied:

‘Who are you?’

A few seconds later, a new message was sent saying:

‘I am going to kill you.’

I froze with fear. I called my girlfriend, but she didn’t answer, and I
was terrified by what was happening. I believed that mysterious girl was
involved in this. The next day, my mother and stepfather arrived home at
11:00 AM, and I told them everything that had happened. After that, I
decided to go to my girlfriend’s house. When I got there, she was
crying, and I asked:

‘What happened?’

She replied:

‘My mother died of a heart attack.’

I was shocked because my mother-in-law was healthy and in good shape. I
froze with fear. At the wake, I held my girlfriend in my arms the entire
day. After the burial, she decided to sleep at my house, and she slept
hugging me and my cat. Later, I woke up to go to the bathroom. I usually
take my phone with me, so I opened Instagram and saw something
terrifying: the photo of that girl from the night before on a news page
that read:

‘19-year-old girl found dead after being missing for 3 years; the main
suspect is her father.’

The girl’s name was Emily. I was horrified and said to myself:

‘How is this possible?’

I was in deep shock. After leaving the bathroom, I went back to bed.

Currently, I am still taking my course, and what happened in the past
has never happened again. I have never been able to get this out of my
head. I even changed my phone number and deleted my account on my
laptop. Today, after one month, everything is calmer, but that still
messes with my mind to this day.”
