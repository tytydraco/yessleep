I wasn’t alone, or maybe I was. They told me they weren’t coming—should
I trust them? Of course not, but I still don’t know. Did someone stand
by my bed? Did they whisper a word or words I probably didn’t catch at
the time, which is why I can’t remember them? Oh well, it doesn’t
matter; as long as I’m typing right now, I’m fine. I just have no idea
what’s going to happen to me the next time I close my eyes.

They cut off my lower limbs, I’m sure of it. I can’t feel them, I can’t
move my body. The light is blindingly bright… Wait, wait, I can’t hear
anything anymore. I’m not typing, I’m thinking—maybe someone else is
typing my thoughts. Listen, whoever you are typing this: tell them I’m
the victim of your selfishness and your desperation to spark the
curiosity of a few users who will never know anything about you other
than that you’re just some user trying to get attention. I’m suffering
here! Please, pay attention to him—maybe he’ll stop hacking away at me.

Someone was brought into my room just a moment ago; I think he whispered
something in my ear like, ‘No one cared.’ Well, how do you all know that
I’m just a story? I have a name and I have a nose—my name is Simon, and
my nose tells me I have a cold! Anyway, I don’t care anymore… Let the
author write an ending for me that befits a man with a cold and
amputated limbs.

I thought about changing my name to Seamus, I don’t know why, maybe
because I’m actually limbless now. Wait, I think something fell into my
eye! Damn it, how am I going to get it out?! This cursed writer is
trying to drive me crazy. My left eye has turned red and tears are
coming out of it, I’m just saying this so he won’t take advantage of it
and write that I cried heavily… I didn’t cry! He dropped something in my
eye, damn it, maybe pepper! Damn it, he put it on my right eye too!

I’ve lost all my senses except hearing, and here come the footsteps
back! Damn it, the author made the man coming into my room have a cold
just to infect me, I hope he doesn’t have a sexually transmitted
disease! Did you hear that, you cursed writer? I’m giving you a brand
new idea right now. Well, I’ve beaten you to it now, you won’t use it
because I got to it first, right? Wait a second… Damn it, he did it!

There is good news and bad news; the bad is that the author released me
with almost no body at all, and the good is that I discovered I’m gay.
This self-discovery experience was useful; I have a new name and a new
life, and I no longer care about my appearance or wear a sun—no need for
wild imaginations, I meant sunscreen because I lost my penis too, what a
shame! I suppose I failed to understand others, which is why I’m writing
a pathetic story about someone just to torture him, only to find myself
being tortured. Maybe my writer is being tortured too! In the story I’m
writing, my character doesn’t seem very entertaining; he cries a lot.
Perhaps my writer knows about me, but he knows nothing about the
character I’m writing.

Well, it seems Simon’s character—I mean the philosophical one—wrote a
character who has the ability to communicate with the first writer. Wait
a second, he named him ‘Bababa’! He says you should give me back my
limbs and I’ll give Simon’s back so we can live in peace. Can you? Damn
it! Do I have to wait for the chain to reach the first writer for a
change to happen?! Why doesn’t one of the damned writers step down and
shorten the road for us? Wait a second, why don’t I step down? But how
can I write salvation for my character while I’m limbless?! Wait, how
did I manage to write Simon’s character in the first place? Hold on a
second, I think I’ve been tricked! You’ve been manipulating me from the
very beginning, you made me believe things just to achieve your goal,
you deranged writer! You just want to grab the readers’ attention by
being a damned bore! Fine, I’ve had enough, I won’t utter a single word…
Wait a second, how do I utter anything without a tongue?! Damn it, I
don’t even have a body to begin with! It seems I died during that sexual
encounter I thought I tricked you into not writing… I hope it wasn’t the
same guy with the cold!
