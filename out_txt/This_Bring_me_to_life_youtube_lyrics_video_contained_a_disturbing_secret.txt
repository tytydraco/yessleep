“Does anyone remember a creepy lyric video for the song bring me to life
by Evanescence?” 

That’s what I posted a few days ago on reddit, hoping I could find other
people that remember that video, but first, let me give you some
context. 

I’m Michael, I’m 23 years old, and back in 2015, I was a big evanescence
fan, especially of their song, “Bring me to life”, so I did anything a
normal fan would do, search for a lyrics video, that’s where this entire
mystery began. 

One of the first videos that popped up was by a user named
“BrokenLyrics”, and at first, it seemed normal, but after a few seconds
something weird started happening. 

In sections with only instrumental, for barely 0.1 seconds, a picture of
a woman with red hair appeared, the woman was completely unrelated to
Evanescence or the song, and pictures of her kept flashing throughout
the video, each one being weirder than the previous.  

At first, it was just a selfie, then the pictures turned into ones that
were taken by a friend, and then the final photo, right at the end was
taken from afar, almost as if the person taking it didn’t want to get
caught. 

I had forgotten about this, until I suddenly remembered, and I tried
searching for the video again, but to no avail. The channel
“BrokenLyrics” still existed, but the “bring me to life” video wasn’t
there anymore. That’s what made me ask people on reddit if they
remembered, or even better, if they had the video. 

 

“Deathwish_590: I remember, it was weird but I never really thought much
of it” 

“Chibi_Jules: It’s likely the video is gone because of copyright
unfortunately” 

“DeadByDawn: this is so intriguing, i hope this gets found” 

 

Unfortunately, despite the fact that the thread had become somewhat
popular, no one had the video. 

A month later, something incredible happened, I checked the channel
again, and the video came back. 

I clicked on the “Bring me to life” video, the pictures were still
there, and there was a comment by a channel that had that same woman as
their profile picture, which read the following: 

 

“I told you to stop uploading these”. 

 

The comment seemingly belonged to the redhead herself, and in the
channel, there was a link to her Instagram account, I was so curious to
know more about this mystery that I tried messaging her, but I got no
response and it seems she has been inactive since September 2017. 

The following day, “BrokenLyrics” uploaded a new video after years of
inactivity, this time of the song “My last breath”. This video, however,
didn’t contain any pictures of the woman, but it did include a message
at the end for 0.1 seconds that said “I remembered again” and then for
another 0.1 seconds, a photo of a gate. 

I checked the channel again to see if there were more new videos, and I
realized that there were a few videos uploaded years ago that weren’t
there originally, I assumed they were unlisted or private videos that
the user made public again, so I clicked on some of them to see if there
was anything weird in them, and as I suspected, this case just kept
getting stranger. 

In the lyric video for the song “Lithium”, not only did pictures of that
redhead flash throughout the video, but there were also photos of two
male teenagers flashing for 0.1 seconds as the song was playing, but the
picture that really scared me, was the last one. 

It was taken from afar, the redhead and the other two teens could be
seen, with backpacks, in front of a school gate… that gate… it looked so
familiar… 

 It was the same gate seen in the “My Last Breath” lyric video,
seriously all I could think now was “what the hell is this channel?!”. 

 There were two comments made by the redhead back in 2017 that said:
“You need help” and “Leave us alone, especially them” 

I had to report this to reddit, just in case, and just two hours after I
updated the thread with this new info, I got an email from a mysterious
person. 

The email read the following: “Hello Michael, I read your thread, please
stop, I warn you, I’m sure you don’t know what could happen if you keep
going, but I do, this is coming from someone concerned, trust me, it’s
not worth it”. 

While I did get kind of scared when I read this, I honestly didn’t think
much of this, i mean, I’m just some random user on the internet, even if
this “BrokenLyrics” guy is some sort of psycho, there’s nothing he can
do to find my address of anything, and my curiosity was just growing, so
I wasn’t going to stop, I couldn’t stop. 

 

“Deathwish_590” said the following on the reddit thread: “Hey, I found
something, the second teen shown on the lithium lyric video has been
missing since June of 2017” and there was a link to a missing persons
site, and there he was, a teen named Robert, last seen on June 18^(th),
2017, he was last seen outside a grocery store, he was probably going
home, but he never made it. 

What left me worried was the date, June 18^(th), 2017, the same date in
which “BrokenLyrics” uploaded his “Lithium” video. 

At this point I had every right to suspect that whoever this
“BrokenLyrics” guy is, he was probably responsible for Robert’s
disappearance, and who knows where the other guy and the redhead are
right now, I thought “maybe they’re alive, but it wouldn’t surprise me
if this user did something to them too” 

Then I had an idea, I searched Robert’s full name on Facebook to see if
his profile showed up, I thought that maybe if I found him, I could also
find the other teen and the redhead’s profiles, if I found them, I could
possibly verify if they’re ok nowadays. 

After many incorrect Roberts, I found the right one, and as I expected,
there has been no activity since 2017, but I did go to his friend list
and found the other guy in the videos, and I did not get positive
information. 

His name was Harold, and the last post was on June 27^(th), 2017, which
read the following: “You were the best son a mother could ever have, I
will miss you forever, my Harold”. 

The sick bastard had killed them both, that’s all I could think, of
course I didn’t know for sure, but it seemed so obvious, the redhead was
right, this guy needed help, he never got it, and I fear she was the
last victim. 

However, my fears were temporarily calmed when i finally got a reply on
Instagram from the redhead girl, I will transcribe our conversation: 

Redhead girl: “Hey, sorry for not replying, i don’t usually check my
DM’s, who are you?” 

Michael: Hello, I’m investigating a mystery regarding a YouTube channel
that’s using your images, I wanted to find out if you knew anything
about this user, or if you could give me any information that could
resolve the mystery of who this guy is: 

Redhead girl: I’m sorry, this account doesn’t belong to her anymore. 

Michael: What? Why? 

Redhead Girl: She died back in 2018 

Michael: I don’t understand, who are you? 

Redhead Girl: None of your business 

Michael: But how did she die? 

Redhead girl: Listen to me you little shit, I don’t know what the hell
you are looking for with this, do you not have a life? You are reopening
a wound that took many years to heal, I know what you’re thinking,
you’re thinking that she was killed or something, but no, she wasn’t,
she just died, I don’t have to tell you the cause of death, I don’t know
you, i regret many things, what I regret the most was not taking enough
care of her, my sweet little angel, i miss her every day” 

 

After this, the account blocked me. So now I knew that her two male
friends were dead, and she was dead too, which made me wonder, if all
three people shown in the videos are dead… then who sent me that email? 

I had to know the identity of the person who sent the email, so i simply
sent an email to them with the following text: “Who are you?” 

The person simply replied with a video archive, titled grief.mp4. 

I downloaded the video, I wish I had never done it. 

The video started with a masked man adjusting the camera, looking
directly at it. It seems like he’s about to speak, when he suddenly
bursts into tears, he cries uncontrollably, he tries to speak many
times, but he can’t, he’s crying so hard that words just won’t come out
of his mouth. 

After 3 minutes of uncontrollable crying, he finally manages to speak. 

The man on the video is apparently “BrokenLyrics” himself, he says the
following: 

 

“Hello, BrokenLyrics here… I don’t know if i will even upload this… i
just… I fucked up, I thought- i thought she was… she was changing her
mind, I thought she had finally seen I was the one” 

He bursts into tears again, before continuing to speak. 

“But I have bad news guys… I don’t think… I don’t think she liked it
here, she missed her friends, her family… why, WHY! WHY! WHY!” 

The man picks up his camera and walks down some stairs that lead to a
basement. The camera is filming his face as he walks, and then when he
finally gets to his basement, he films what’s in front of him, that’s
when i wished i had never even started this investigation. 

 “MY SWEET LITTLE ANGEL!” 

What could be seen in front of him was the redhead girl, foaming at the
mouth, with pills all over the floor. 

As soon as i saw this, I closed the video and deleted it, even though
there were another 5 minutes left, I knew I didn’t want to watch
anymore, it was too disturbing, BrokenLyrics really had ruined the lives
of these 3 people, he was a maniac obsessed with this girl, as to why he
killed her two friends, I still don’t know, but to be honest, I don’t
think it’s worth it to keep investigating.

One of the most disturbing parts of this to me was what he called her
right at the end, “my sweet little angel”, then i thought about it, the
person behind the redhead’s Instagram account also called her that… i
wonder if that was just a coincidence or… to be honest i don’t think i
wanna even think about this very much. 

It’s been 2 weeks since I saw that video, and ever since then I’ve
noticed that my webcam has been acting weird, sometimes it turns on for
barely a second, and then turns off, the fact that all of this happened
as soon as I saw the video left me really worried, so I decided to
install an antivirus to see if anything was wrong. 

While the antivirus didn’t notice anything wrong with my PC, my webcam
thankfully still stopped acting weird as soon as I installed it, so I
was relieved that I could finally leave all of this behind. 

That was until “BrokenLyrics” uploaded again. 

The maniac had uploaded his “Bring me to life” lyrics video again, which
really confused me at first, but then I saw that his original video had
been deleted, alongside many other videos, including the “Lithium” one. 

I clicked on the new video… nothing weird happened. 

It’s just the lyrics of the song, that’s it, a black background, white
lyrics, no random pictures. 

Or so I thought. 

Of course, there is an unrelated picture, at the last microsecond. I
fully expected him to include something related to the redhead, or one
of her friends, but what I saw left me so frightened, I don’t think I
had ever been so scared in my entire life. 

It was a picture of me from my webcam.
