It started about a week ago. It was a typical morning, I was leaving to
go to work. I work as a history teacher at my local high school so I am
always out the door before my wife. That’s when I found the first one.

A white piece of paper folded and taped to my driver’s side door. It
read YOU LEFT THE GARAGE DOOR OPEN. I didn’t think much of it. I thought
it was my wife playing a silly game or something along those lines.

The following day I found another one on the bottom of my laptop. This
one read YOU NEED TO FIX THE BASEMENT DOOR.

I walked down the stairs and into the basement. I examined the door and
realized it didn’t lock fully. I noticed with a little pressure on it,
the door would open. The deadbolt was not fully seating into the door
frame.

At the dinner table later that night, I told my wife I fixed the
basement door.

“What was wrong with it?” She asked.

“Just the lock wasn’t fully locking the door.” I replied. “Don’t worry
it’s fixed now”

“Oh good, I would never even have known.” “I’m never down there”.

I was puzzled at this moment. “ But you left me that note… on my
laptop.”

“I have no clue what you’re talking about.” My wife said.

I changed the subject knowing she was playing a prank on me. Since our
relationship started she has always been a jokester. Or at least I
always thought it was her.

The following morning, I received my third note. It was placed under my
car keys which were on the dining room table.

SHE IS CHEATING ON YOU. I felt my heart beat become gradually stronger.
I went to see my wife who was still asleep in our bed.

“This isn’t a fuck joke!” I howled at her.

“What isn’t?” She asked rubbing her eyes awake.

“The notes!” “ Oh ya and now your cheating on me?”

“I have no idea what you are talking about.”

“Whatever.” I shake my head as I leave the house and go to work.

I enter my classroom and turn on the lights.

“This can’t fucking be.” I whispered to myself.

A note stared directly at me taped to the whiteboard. CHECK HER PHONE.

That night i waited for her to fall asleep. She wore an eye mask
luckily, the brightness from her phone wouldn’t wake her up.

I picked her phone up from the nightstand and unlocked it. I went to her
messages, everything seemed normal. Text from her mom, her dad and me
appeared on the screen. I scrolled a little further down and noticed a
contact “BG”. I read the thread.

“He is so fucking dumb he wouldn’t even know.”

“When are we going to do this?”

“Soon I’m getting so fucking sick of him.”

“Good I just bought the knife I wanted to use, it was kinda like
shopping for a new car.”

“HAHAHA. He is crazy people will think its suicide anyway.”

“I can’t wait to spend my life with you after he’s gone.”

Rage filled my brain. She’s been cheating on me? She wants me dead?
After everything we have been through. I hovered over her motionless
body.

“What if he is outside of my house right now?” I thought to myself.

I can feel the paranoia taking control of my body like a demon
possessing a soul. Hyperventilating, I exit the bedroom and sat in the
kitchen to catch my breath.

With my head in my palms, I try to gather myself. Another note lies on
the table.

KILL US BEFORE WE KILL YOU.

I sprint to the window. Nothing is out there. I sprint to the front
door. I check out the peephole.

I see a silhouette of a man slowly walking toward the door. I ran to the
kitchen and grabbed a steak knife and locked myself in the bathroom.

The doorknob rattled.

“What the fuck, get out of the bathroom!” My wife hollered.

“No get away, I know everything!”

I hear the doorknob click. They picked the lock.

I blindly lunged forward with the knife while screaming.

I feel the knife sink into flesh. It felt like squeezing a stress ball I
thought as the knife slid deeper into the skin.

I opened my eyes.

Standing in front of me was my wife with a face of horror. Protruding
from her neck was the steak knife.

“OH MY GOD!”

I pulled the knife from her neck. A fine crimson mist sprayed on the
side of the wall.

I quickly hurdled over her body and locked myself in the bedroom waiting
for my wife’s lover to try to break the door down.

But nothing. There was nothing.

I can’t remember how long I waited but I couldn’t hear any sound
throughout the house. I picked up my wife’s phone to call “BG”.

Maybe I will hear his phone ring I thought.

I go to her messages. Nothing. There was no BG. Not in her messages, not
in her contacts.

I sprint to my side of the bed. I pull out a box from underneath the
bed. I saved all the mysterious notes I found and put them in it.

Empty.

“This can’t be.”

“I put them in here.”
“She must have stolen them.”

The color of my face drains as the weight of realization hits me like a
ten-ton truck.

“I got to leave I got to go” my voice trembled.

I grabbed my car keys, sprinted to the car and took off.

Here I am now a fugitive on the run. A crazy person who killed his wife.
The news will not stop reporting about it. My face plastered on the
national news.

I have no clue where I am right now. Somewhere in Nevada I believe. I
saw a roach motel on the side of the road I can stop there for a few.

Room 915. The date my wife and I got married on ironically. I opened the
door. I see two twin beds with a desk with a tv on it. I walk over to
throw my room key onto the bed..

A note.

YOU CAN STOP RUNNING NOW

I lowered the note from my eyes.

A razor blade sat on the bed.
