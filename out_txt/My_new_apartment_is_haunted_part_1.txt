I don’t have any writing skills and also my English still needs
improvement.

I am 24(f) who has been working in this new city for almost half a year.
When I first got this job my boyfriend insisted on moving with him into
his apartment so that we can finally start a life together. At first I
wasn’t so sure about this but I thought it would be nice to live with
him . Things were really nice in the start . Late night works , traffic,
heavy headache everything was laying me down but knowing he was with me
made me feel much better. It was on his birthday, I planned to surprise
him , for that I left my office early and went to our apartment, I also
bought a bday cake with me. But when I got there the scene in front of
me shattered me into pieces…..

I couldn’t stand there watching him cheat on me …. That very night I
realised that I don’t even have any friends over there. Because I didn’t
know I would face a situation like this, I hadn’t thought about making a
lot of friends before.

To be honest , whenever I—an introvert—brought up the idea of making new
friends, my boyfriend would tell me that I didn’t need anyone else in
his absence, because he would always be there. Back then, his words felt
like a comforting safety net. But today, I finally look around and
realize the deep isolation those words have trapped me in.

In the end, I decided to go to the apartment of the only friend I still
had. Even though it was a long way from my workplace, I had absolutely
no other choice, so I went there anyway …..However, things turned out to
be much worse than I expected. You can imagine the state of three people
living in an apartment that wasn’t even big enough for two. On top of
that, it took me at least two hours to get through all the traffic to
the office and back to the apartment. Shoving myself into the exhausted,
crowded metro after all that was even worse. When everything was done
and I thought about just lying down to sleep, I couldn’t get any sleep.
And if I wanted to cry, I couldn’t even do that because it would disturb
the sleep of my friend lying right next to me.

I decided to find a room or an apartment and move out as quickly as
possible. However, apartment hunting turned out to be much worse than I
had expected. There was absolutely nothing available near my workplace
that fit within my budget. Finally, after two weeks of searching, an
agent helped me find a place. It was in a housing complex, an apartment
that had been sitting vacant for a while. The rent was perfect for my
budget. It had a bedroom, an office room, a kitchen, and a bathroom.
Even the deposit amount was low. Since my only thought was to escape my
current living situation, I didn’t look into it too deeply—I just paid
the cash, signed the agreement, and moved in.

But that would turn out to be the absolute worst decision of my life. I
had packed up and moved into that place, completely blind to the horror
that was waiting for me.
