Something happened to me when I was 18 that I still can’t fully explain.
The closest thing I can think of is that it was some sort of demonic or
evil oppression. Have you ever experienced something like this? I’d love
to hear your input on what you think it could have been or if you’ve
ever experienced something similar.

I’ve been wanting to share this somewhere for a while. I’m also planning
to start a YouTube channel centered around real people’s unsettling,
unexplained, creepy, or just plain strange experiences, so I figured I’d
start by sharing one of my own, which I plan to post to my channel.

This happened when I was around 18 or 19. It’s been a long time, so some
of the smaller details between events have gotten fuzzy, but there are
certain moments from that time that I remember incredibly clearly.

I moved out of my parents’ house pretty much as soon as I turned 18. I’d
had a rough childhood and really just wanted out, so I ended up living
with a friend of a friend in a town I hadn’t lived in before.

It wasn’t far from where I grew up, but at 18 it felt like starting
over.

Not long after moving there, I met a guy who lived only a couple blocks
away. We started dating, and pretty quickly I was spending most of my
time at his house.

It was a big, older house and his entire family lived there.

I remember feeling uncomfortable there almost immediately.

There was just a heaviness to the house that I couldn’t really explain.
Even their dog would sometimes stare into empty areas of the house and
start barking.

Eventually I mentioned how strange the house felt, and that’s when his
family told me that they believed it was haunted.

Not just haunted, either. They believed there was something genuinely
bad there.

According to them, most of the activity happened upstairs and around the
attic. I honestly don’t remember all of the stories they told me
anymore, but I definitely remember what started happening while I was
around that house.

The first thing was sleep paralysis and I started getting it constantly,
like almost every night.

And what really scared me was that it didn’t only happen at his house. I
started experiencing it at my own place too.

At the time, I was convinced sleep paralysis was supernatural. I
understand now that there’s a medical explanation for it, but at 18,
combined with everything else that was happening, I became terrified to
go to sleep. And let’s be honest, I still believe there’s a supernatural
explanation for sleep paralysis.

But then little things started happening at my house.

I’d leave my shoes near the front door and later find them somewhere
completely different.

Things would randomly fall and I’d keep losing things.

One night I came home and very clearly heard my roommate call my name,
when I answered them they looked at me completely confused because they
hadn’t said anything. At the time I had heard of mimics or how spirits
could trick you into thinking they were something familiar.

That was probably the first time I seriously wondered whether something
from his house had somehow followed me back to mine.

I told my boyfriend I didn’t feel safe anymore, and he gave me a
necklace he used to wear. It had a pendant of the Virgin Mary and had
supposedly been blessed. He told me to wear it for protection.

I remember the clasp specifically because it was one of those tiny
spring clasps where you have to pull the little lever back to open it.
That becomes important later.

There was another incident around this time that I’ve never been able to
make sense of.

We were in bed together one night, I was awake watching tv and my
boyfriend at the time kept waking up thinking I was touching him.

The first time, I told him I wasn’t and he went back to sleep.

Then it happened a few more times.

He woke me up asking why I kept touching or brushing against him.

Again, I hadn’t touched him.

Eventually he woke up completely freaking out and started frantically
brushing at his body and legs like something was crawling on him.

We turned on the light.

There were fresh red scratches running up and down his legs.

I hadn’t scratched him. As far as I knew, he hadn’t scratched himself.

We just stared at them.

I don’t remember sleeping very much after that.

Then came one of the images from that period that I still remember
vividly.

I woke up in his room one night. The TV was on, so there was enough
light for me to see him lying next to me.

I turned toward him.

And he was staring at me.

Except his face didn’t look right.

His mouth was stretched into this huge, unnatural grin.

The best way I can describe it is that his face looked distorted, almost
like looking at someone through warped glass. It was still his face, but
something about it looked completely wrong.

I blinked and rubbed my eyes.

And suddenly he was normal.

His eyes were closed…..

He was asleep…..

seeing his face like that scared the absolute hell out of me. And
honestly made me kind of scared of him. Was whatever this presence was
attached to him? His house? His family? I had no clue.

Eventually we started spending more time at my place because I didn’t
want to be in his house.

That didn’t seem to help.

One night we were sitting in my basement when we started hearing tapping
sounds coming from the walls.

We tried to ignore it.

Then the whole room started feeling strange. Heavy is the only word I
know how to use for it.

And then we heard what sounded like chairs scraping across the kitchen
floor directly above us.

Nobody was upstairs. My roommate at the time traveled for work and was
gone.

We both froze and started praying. I never considered myself religious,
but that’s all I knew what to do at the time.

We stayed downstairs until it stopped.

Another night, back in his basement bedroom, I was lying in bed facing
the open doorway when I heard something that sounded like a voice coming
from underneath the bed.

It was deep and guttural and seemed to be speaking in something I
couldn’t understand.

At the time, I described it as sounding like someone speaking in
tongues.

I sat up immediately.

And right around the same time, both of us saw a shadow move past the
open bedroom doorway and disappear farther into the basement. We got up
to go inspect thinking it could have been his siblings, but no one was
down there.

That was one of the few experiences where I wasn’t the only person who
saw something.

Around this point, our relationship was also becoming increasingly
negative. There was a lot of fighting and tension, as with most young
relationships, and whether it was related or not, everything seemed to
get worse as the relationship did.

Then I started getting physically sick.

I couldn’t keep food down.

Eventually I could barely keep water down.

I started losing weight quickly and became incredibly weak.

I went to the doctor and had testing done, but nothing obvious was
found. As far as the testing showed, I was healthy.

Meanwhile, my boyfriend at the time started going out at night with his
friends more often, which meant I was sometimes left at his house alone.

I hated it.

One night I was asleep in his bedroom by myself when I suddenly woke up
because I felt something lightly touching my back.

I immediately had this overwhelming feeling of dread.

Then I realized something was moving underneath my shirt.

It was the necklace.

The blessed Virgin Mary necklace he’d given me was completely unclasped
and sliding down my back inside my shirt. It wasn’t broken, it was
unclasped like something physically touched it.

I sat up.

And I saw what looked like a shadow move quickly across the room toward
the window.

I was completely alone. I clutched the necklace as if it could protect
me.

I know there could be explanations for individual pieces of this story.

But experiencing all of it together, during that specific period of my
life, was terrifying.

Eventually I couldn’t take it anymore.

I called my family and asked if I could come home.

Within days, I packed my things and left.

And this is the part I’ve always found strange.

I got better almost immediately.

I could eat again.

My strength came back.

The constant sleep paralysis stopped.

And that oppressive, heavy feeling I’d been living with seemed to
disappear.

I’ve never experienced anything on that level again.

I can’t tell you exactly what happened.

Maybe stress and an unhealthy relationship were affecting me far more
physically and psychologically than I understood at 18.

Maybe some of the things we experienced had completely ordinary
explanations.

Or maybe there was something about that house that I still don’t
understand.

I just know that when I left, it stopped. I’m no longer with that guy,
we haven’t talked since I left and I haven’t experienced anything like
it ever again.
