live on a little patch of family land just outside Kountze, right where
the houses thin out and the Big Thicket starts. Single-wide, couple
acres, woods starting maybe thirty yards from the back steps. Nothing
special. I’ve been out here most of my life. Work construction in the
refineries like most around here, come home, mess with the truck, sit on
the porch with a beer or similar. Same as always.

About a month ago the nights changed.

It was after that stretch of heavy rain we got. Everything was still
soaked and the air felt thick. I was outside late, just smoking, and I
realized I couldn’t hear the frogs. Or the cicadas. Nothing. In summer
around here that’s not normal. Those things never shut up. You get used
to the constant noise so much that when it’s gone it feels like your
ears are ringing.

I figured it was just the weather. Told myself that for a few days.

Then I started catching movement at the tree line. Not animals.
Something taller, thinner. Standing still between the pines. I’d look,
and it would be there. I’d look away for a second and it wouldn’t be, or
it would be a few feet over. Never close enough to really see. Just a
shape that thought maybe it was just a tree and I was over thinking it,

I checked the property the next morning. Found some tracks in the soft
dirt near the fence line. Long and narrow. Not deer, not dog, not hog. I
don’t know tracks that well, but they didn’t look right. A couple days
later there were two dead armadillos laid out neat, side by side, like
someone placed them there. Not scattered or torn up the way coyotes
leave things.

My dog quit going past the edge of the yard. Used to run the whole
property every evening. Now he just stands at the tree line, staring,
and then turns around and comes back under the trailer. Won’t even take
a piss out there after dark anymore.

I tried to write it off. Trespasser. Homeless guy. Somebody’s kid
messing around. I walked the line with a flashlight a couple times.
Called out once. Nothing answered. Neighbor down the road said he hadn’t
seen anybody. Sheriff’s deputy I know from high school told me to call
if anything actually happened, but he didn’t sound interested.

The quiet kept coming and going. Some nights the insects would start
back up like nothing was wrong. Other nights it was dead silent from
sundown on. Those were the nights I’d see the shape again.

One night last week the motion light on the back of the trailer kicked
on by itself. I looked out the kitchen window and it was standing right
at the edge of the light. Closer than before. Maybe fifteen or twenty
feet from the steps. I couldn’t make out a face. Just the outline. Too
tall, arms hanging wrong. I grabbed the shotgun and stepped out on the
porch. The second the beam hit it, it moved. Not like a person turns and
runs. It just wasn’t there anymore. No sound of feet in the leaves or
branches breaking. Just gone.

I stood there a long time. Wondering if im losing my mind.

I haven’t stayed the night out there since. Been crashing on a buddy’s
couch in vidor or at a hotel…. Told him the A/C went out. Went back once
in daylight to grab clothes and everything just seemed odd. Even then
the woods felt off. Too still.

I keep telling myself it’s stress, or lack of sleep, or my eyes playing
tricks in the dark. But the dog knows something’s wrong. And I can’t
shake the feeling that whatever it is doesn’t need to come any closer.
It’s already where it wants to be.

I’m writing this from the Whataburger parking lot off 69. Thinking of
trying one more night home but not reqlly wanting to I don’t know what
to do with the place. Can’t sell it easy, and I can’t keep paying for a
motel every night. But I also can’t go back out there after dark and
pretend everything’s fine.

If you’re from around Hardin County and the woods have gone quiet on you
lately… maybe just stay inside.
