Lets go back a couple of years ago.. 2024. I was 31 years old and a
bartender at a small dive bar. I had been working there for a couple of
years and I had made some friends, different regulars as well as my
coworkers. I met a girl that was my soul best friend there. We did
EVERYTHING together, including getting into trouble.

Well one night in early 2024, we did a fun thing after work and got
absolutely wasted! We convinced some guy we had met that it would be fun
to ride in the bed of his truck around the neighborhood of the bar. He
obliged. We had so much fun until it wasn’t fun anymore…

He took a sharp turn and went a little too quickly and we both fell out
of the truck. Broken boned and completely wasted, my friend and I lay on
the ground. We were both rushed to the hospital where I suffered a
broken leg as well as a fractured skull. My friend ended up breaking her
ribs. She had tried to hold onto the truck and I had not.

She got sent home with instructions on how to care for her ribs, but
there was not much the hospital could do for her. For me however, I got
to spend a week in the hospital with a leg that needed surgery as well
as a major concussion and a fractured skull. Yay! it was horrible!

Not only was I out of work for 4 months, but I also was now susceptible
to seizures and some memory loss. Not crazy memory loss, thank goodness,
but just little things.. like when I put on makeup again for the first
time I couldn’t remember if I put on foundation first or mascara. It was
just little things but it did suck while I was healing.

I also was mixing up my words, but the doctor said that it would heal
over time and that I would be back to normal while my concussion healed
and my fracture healed. It was all still very horrifying because I am an
avid reader and it was tricky for me to read for about 4 weeks.

This is when I started to see things.. it started while I was healing in
bed and I would see something move in my peripherals.. I would look and
nothing was there. I would see my then boyfriend who was taking care of
me since I couldn’t walk for the first 6 weeks and I would be talking to
him from my room into the kitchen because I could have swore I saw him.

Since I was disabled I couldn’t just walk into the kitchen so I would
keep calling for him and he wouldn’t be there. He did have a job and so
would be there. When he would get home I would ask him if he had heard
me calling for him and he would tell me no babe, I was at work. It would
just confuse me so badly.

At my first follow up appointment, I told my doctor what I was seeing
and he said that it was because of some of the damage I had caused when
I fractured my skull, but that it should go away. I told him it was a
little scary for me to be seeing these things. He asked me which side I
would normally see them from and it was always my right side and he said
it was because I damaged my left side of my brain and that was normal.

It made me calmer to know that it was normal, but that doesn’t mean that
it wasn’t scary. I was also on some pain killers and that was seeming to
take over for a little bit too. Every time I took my medication for
pain, I would see much more “normal” figures moving around me.

I don’t know if any of you have been put on opioids for pain after
surgery or anything like that but they are highly addicting. And safe to
say that I got addicted and it opened the door for all of the terrible
things I did to my body before I went sober. I started doing all sorts
of drugs because there was always pain in my body after the accident.

I couldn’t run normally, and working out was harder ever after I was
healed. I just felt like I messed up my whole life. Drugs and alcohol
was a way to get away from it all and just go into a numb state. The
only issue was that now these things I was seeing, were coming from all
directions and now they were talking, but I was so strung out all the
time that I just ignored them or talked back.

That was when I met this lady named Lisa. I was prompted to go to NA or
AA or something like that because I was coming to work piss drunk or out
of my mind on drugs. If I didn’t get together I would lose everything
that I worked for. So I decided to go. I was drunk when I went and
listened to all these people and just felt like a stranger. I hated it.

After the meeting I was trying to gather all my things to get out of
there as fast as possible when I was approached by Lisa. She’s an older
woman who was one of the main people there. She was a sponsor and she
made it a point to welcome me to the group and that she would love to
see me come back. I had anxiety because there was no way you would think
I was sober.

But she didn’t judge me, she didn’t make me feel bad. she didn’t point
it out that she knew I was drunk. she just smiled at me and told me that
it would be nice to see me again whenever I could come back for another
meeting. She gave me her number and told me to contact her whenever I
needed someone to talk to.

That was really reassuring but also I felt like I phony. I felt like I
would never go back. But then I had these things in my vision, I don’t
think they are ghosts, but more like nightmares that walk straight
through my head and into reality. They looked almost human but something
was always slightly off about them. I had gotten so used to them,
because the last time I remember being totally sober was the night
before I broke my leg. During the hospital I was on pain killers and
morphine, all that stuff and then straight after to all the other
medications I was on.

Then my addiction and thats all she wrote. Drinking was almost everyday,
but drugs were everyday for me. I didn’t know how to function without
them, but I also knew I was killing myself slowly through treating my
body so badly.

So, I had to get sober and I had to have people help me. Over time Lisa
was so good to me and helped me to get sober. She was there when I was
withdrawing, there when I wanted to harm myself, there when I was
thinking about just drinking instead of drugs. She helped me through it
all and before I knew it I was actually becoming sober.

I never thought it possible for me. And these real life nightmares I was
seeing started to become fewer and fewer. I was starting to really see a
brightness in my life. Thank God. And it really felt good to not feel
hungover all the time, and I was also saving a lot of money.

Then one day I went to collect my 6 month chip and I was so happy to
receive it. I texted Lisa and I was so proud to be walking to grab my
chip in front of her. But when I got there, she wasn’t there. I called
and her phone went straight to voicemail. I felt lost all of a sudden.

I still walked up to get my chip and that was really nice, but I really
wanted to talk to Lisa. Then I went the next day and so on and so on. I
couldn’t find her. I couldn’t reach her and in my head I felt like she
was avoiding me or I did something to upset her.

Finally, I reached out to another member of the NA, Matt, and asked
about Lisa and Matt sounded confused, “Lisa? But.. why..” and then the
phone got disconnected. I tried to call back and call back and no one
was answering me. I didn’t know what I was doing wrong.

The next day I saw Matt at another meeting and I waited until the
meeting was over and I pulled him over..

“Matt, I don’t know what happened on the phone yesterday but I was
asking about Lisa..?”

“Yes, I am wondering what you wanna know about her.”

“Well, I am wondering where she is.. I have been calling and texting her
and it goes to voicemail and her messages don’t seem to go through.”

“Lisa hasn’t been in this group in a long time…”

At this point, not only was I feeling confused.. I was also feeling
stupid like I had made a huge mistake. Anxiety started humming through
my body because I had been seeing things for the last couple of years
and I now don’t know if Lisa is real or just something from my mind. I
didn’t wanna ask what he meant and I was starting to get embarrassed so
I just said…

“Oh.. right, I am sorry.. I haven’t had much sleep lately, my schedule
got changed at work and.. my bad.”

“Well that’s okay…but,”

I turned and walked out of the room, to my car and straight home. I
didn’t wanna hear what Matt had to say but I can see his confusion was
making me more confused.

When I got home I tried to call Lisa again and I couldn’t get a hold of
her. From what Matt said, she hadn’t been in that group for a while. But
she was! She helped me change my life around. I couldn’t have done it
without her.

Confused and embarrassed, I cried myself to sleep. I woke up from a
crazy dream I was having. Lisa was there in the dream and she was
telling me that her work was done and that I didn’t need her anymore. In
the dream I cried for her and I asked her, begged her not to leave me.
She held my hand and smiled softly at me telling me I was going to be
okay and that it would all turn out alright.

The next night I went back to NA, I got there a little early because I
had gotten off work a little early and just went straight there. I was
in the lobby outside the main meeting room and I was looking at all the
announcements on the wall. I was looking at the pictures from when the
group first started.

Then I saw a photo that made no sense to me. I was looking at a camping
photo the group had taken from when the group first started. On the
frame it said, Lake Tahoe, 1996. In the photo, smack in the middle was
Lisa. She looked like how she did now. I started getting hot flashes and
I had tears in my eyes. It was then that I realized I was fucking crazy.
I always assumed that the things I saw were always out of my own mind.

“I told you its been a while since Lisa has been in this group..”

I jumped because Matt was standing beside me seeing me looking at the
photo.

“I… I”

“It’s okay,” Matt said, “She was a really special lady to this group..
Before you left that night I was going to tell you that Lisa had passed
away just about a week before you showed up for your first meeting.”

“Oh…”

“Yes, but I want to tell you that it is okay. You are not the only one
that sees Lisa around this place. She comes and goes and sometimes she
talks to us, sometimes she claps for us. She was one of the founders of
this group and I think its her way of continuing the good work that she
dedicated her life to.”

“But I have texts from her..”

I pulled out my phone and pulled up the messages and saw that it was all
one sided. It was only me talking to a number that wasn’t even in
service. None of the text messages said delivered.. And I was replying
to nothing. It was 6 months worth of me talking to no one. That chilled
me to the bone; I had seen the messages. I had spoken to her on the
phone!

“It’s okay. I know it seems like a lot right now…”

I couldn’t listen to anymore, I ran out of there and spent the next
couple of days in a bubble in my mind. I was in a complete haze. I
really felt like I was going crazy. I am surprised that I haven’t turned
back to drugs or alcohol.

I ended up going to a different meeting in the next city over and I
walked in and sat awkwardly messing with a string on my shirt when I
looked up and saw Lisa. She was sitting across the room from me and gave
me a little wave and a smile.
