This happened a few years ago when I was studying in college, and some
strange situation happened to me.

I was home alone, working on a paper that was due the next morning. It
was pretty late, probably around midnight, and I’d been sitting at my
desk for hours. I had my headphones on for most of the evening, so I
wasn’t really paying attention to anything going on around me. Our house
had a long hallway connecting all the bedrooms. My room was at one end,
and my brother’s was farther down. There were no windows in the hallway,
so at night it was completely dark unless someone turned on the lights.

At some point, I needed something from my brother’s room. I don’t even
remember what it was anymore. I just remember getting up from my desk,
opening my bedroom door, and looking down the hallway. And there he was.
My brother was standing in the doorway of his room. I couldn’t see his
face clearly because the only light was coming from my bedroom behind
me. But I could make out his silhouette. His height, his shoulders, the
way he was standing. It looked exactly like him. He wasn’t moving. He
was just standing there, facing in my direction.

I remember thinking, “Why the hell is he just standing there in the
dark?” I turned around, went back into my room, and closed the door.
Then I sat down at my desk, and suddenly I remembered something that
made my stomach drop. I was at home alone. I can’t really describe the
feeling that hit me when I realized that. My whole body went cold, and I
got goosebumps all over my arms. I sat there staring at my bedroom door,
trying to convince myself I’d imagined it. Maybe it was a shadow. Maybe
my eyes were playing tricks on me because I’d been staring at a computer
screen for hours. I didn’t open my door again. I didn’t even get up to
turn on another light. I just sat at my desk with my laptop open,
pretending to work while listening to every little noise coming from the
hallway.

Every creak of the floorboards made my stomach drop. At one point, I
thought I heard something moving outside my room. It was so quiet that I
couldn’t tell if it was footsteps or just the house settling. I held my
breath and waited, but nothing happened. I wanted to call my brother,
but I was terrified that if I made any noise, I’d be screwed. So
instead, I texted him asking when he’d be home. He replied that he was
already on his way and would be there soon.

I stayed in my room until my brother finally came home about an hour
later. I told him everything that had happened, and he was scared
shitless. We ended up sleeping in the same room that night because
neither of us wanted to be alone. And for the next month, I refused to
turn off the hallway light at night.
