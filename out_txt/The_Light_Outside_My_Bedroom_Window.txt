I was out with some of my office friends. It was nighttime, and we were
all sitting together, eating and talking. Nothing unusual was happening.
Eventually, I started feeling tired and decided it was time for me to go
home and sleep.

I told myself that if my friends came over later, I could simply open
the door for them.

I went home and went straight to my bedroom.

There’s a window on the left side of my bed. Outside that window is a
bulb that gives off a faint, slightly blurry glow through the glass. It
isn’t particularly bright, but at night, it’s just enough to illuminate
part of the room.

I fell asleep.

At some point, I suddenly woke up.

I thought I had heard a sound outside my room.

Still half-asleep, I assumed it was one of my friends. Maybe they had
arrived and were waiting for me to open the door.

So I stayed quiet and waited for the knock.

Nothing.

I waited a little longer.

Still nothing.

Then I noticed the light outside my window.

It switched off.

Then back on.

I stared at it.

A few seconds later, it switched off again.

Then back on.

And then, one final time.

Off.

On.

It happened three times, very slowly.

I don’t know why, but at that moment I became completely still.

I didn’t say anything.

Neither did whoever was outside.

There was just silence.

I kept staring at the window, trying to convince myself that there was a
perfectly normal explanation. Maybe someone was walking past the bulb.
Maybe there was an electrical problem. Maybe one of my friends was
messing with me.

But I couldn’t shake the feeling that someone was standing outside.

So, for some reason that makes absolutely no sense to me now, I decided
to check.

I thought that if someone was actually there, opening the window would
let me see who it was.

I slowly got out of bed.

I walked toward the window.

My heart was already pounding.

I reached out and opened it.

And that’s when I saw her.

She was standing directly in front of me.

Her face was almost completely covered by long, messy black hair. Her
skin looked pale in the faint light. She was impossibly close, staring
straight at me.

For a second, neither of us moved.

Then she suddenly lunged toward me.

Her mouth opened impossibly wide and she screamed:

“BAHHHHHH!”

It wasn’t a normal scream.

It was this deep, distorted, womanly voice that sounded almost demonic.

I screamed too.

I fell backward onto my butt, scrambling away from the window while
desperately trying to breathe.

My chest was heaving.

I was panting like crazy.

And then…

I woke up.

I was lying in my actual bed.

My heart was pounding so hard I could feel it in my chest.

I was breathing heavily, staring at the exact same window on the left
side of my bed.

The room was dark.

The bulb outside was glowing faintly through the glass.

I just lay there for a while, trying to calm myself down.

Then the bulb went off.

I froze.

A few seconds later, it came back on.

I haven’t looked at the window again.

It’s been 20 minutes and I still can’t comprehend what just happened
even though it’s just a dream. I made this into a comic panel.
