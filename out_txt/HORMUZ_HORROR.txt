I believe everyone now know what strait of Hormuz is. Ever since this
escalation started in FEB 2026, this tiny stretch of water has been the
deadliest waterway in the world. Everyday multiple merchant ships try to
cross it to export crude oil from Persian Gulf to many different parts
of world. 20 percent of world oil supply passed through Hormuz. Many
ships get hit by coalition forces of IRAN and USA. These ships after
getting hit are abandoned by their crews.

This is where me and my crew comes in.

I have a small company which specializes in salvage operations of these
ships and their cargo. It is a dangerous job but business is booming.
This Hormuz escalation is minimum of 50 years duration. The nearest it
can come resolving if democrats win in 2028. But as per me the world
needs now to adjust with it and live with its impact on world economy.

Anyways I received a salvage request from one of my references . As soon
as I heard about it something wasn’t sitting quite right.

Normally in most basic salvage operations (if the vessel is still afloat
or partially afloat) we have to tow it to the designated safe harbor for
repairs. If the vessel is sunk then we have to dive down and retrieve
the VDR, VOYAGE DATA RECORDER, similar to what a BLACKBOX IN AVIATION.
The extent of salvage operations can vary depending upon nature of
circumstances.

In this salvage operation I had to take a group of priests on a big
container vessel. This ship is a giant 350 mtr long, neopanamax ship
with a capacity of carrying 13000 containers. I had to take these
priests to a particular container, inside no 3-cargo hold. These big
container ships carry containers both on deck and inside the cargo hold.

In all my years in sea this is the first time where, I have to take a
group of priests to a dead ship in a dangerous area. Usually there is a
mountain load of paperwork and permissions before I took them to that
ship but this time it seems that doesn’t matter. The money offered was
10 times the usual rate. The urgency was so much that we didn’t have to
carry out our initial risk assessment also.

So long story short, my job was basically to take them to that deadship
in night time. Secure the entry, lead them to container hold no 3 , once
the particular container is located, go back outside and wait for them
to come out. Once they are out, put them back in my tugboat and bring
them back. Sounds easy right ….

As if this wasn’t weird enough, only I am required to board the
containership with them priests, all my crew must remain on the tugboat
beside the containership.

There were 3 priests in total , 2 man and 1 women. I am not Christian by
faith so I didn’t know much about their designations.  We started out in
late afternoon and we reach the deadship in 5 hours just when the sun
sets down. We located the small entrance door in the hull . This
entrance door are standard features where the mooring pilots enter and
exit these container ships , for berthing and unberthing purposes.

To our luck the outer door was open which was pretty unusual for me but
this vessel was targeted by a drone strike so I thought maybe some crew
left it open in order to escape. I led the 3 priests to the top deck
from the stairway. It was like climbing 4 floors of stairs. The 2 male
priests were looking out of breath. The lady however seems ok.

Once out on open deck it was dark with cool breeze flowing. The general
structure of the deck and accommodation were looking like old derelict
structures. I wanted to inspect accommodation block but I declined. The
entrance for the no 3 cargo hold was from the main deck. Once we reached
the manhold entrance for no 3 hold . I stopped and told the priests to
follow me down I the hold.

 They never tell me the exact location of container in the hold. FYI
each container has been assigned with row, bay and tier no prior loading
for identification purposes. I could have easily taken them to their
container.

One we reach the hold entrance they told me to stand outside and only
they will go inside the hold. I tried to convince them it is extremely
dangerous to go down alone in a dark cargo hold but they didn’t listen.
I watched the 2 male priest go inside one by one . The lady suddenly
turn around and told me to not enter the hold in any condition, till
they come out. They went inside and locked the hold cover from inside.

It was night time and curiosity got the best of me. I went around for a
round of ship deck. I entered inside accommodation block and went
straight upto bridge. I was dark and cold in there. Without electricity
it was like a ghost building inside. I was curious for the container
inside the hold no 3. I went to bridge for purpose of getting my hands
on cargo manifest. The cargo manifest I luckily found in bridge. The
cargo holds no 3 was carrying only one reefer container.

This I found extremely odd as it was highly unusual for having only 1
cargo for entire cargo hold which can accommodate hundreds of containers
easily. This was also extremely complicated for maintain ship’s
stability.  What strikes me odd was that it was a reefer container.
Reefer container is basically like your basic refrigerator in home. They
need continuous electric supply to maintain a standard temp.

Now with this newfound information I walk back myself to the entrance of
hold no 3. When I arrive there, I found that the hold entrance was open.
I was surprised. I radioed my crew down in the tugboat but they didn’t
reply. I walk over to railing to check and found that the navigational
lights were off in my tugboat.  I shouted from top of the deck to my
tugboat but nobody replied.

Now the panic started to set in.  Something was definitely wrong. I went
back to hold entrance and shouted inside but nothing replied. I decided
to go and check inside the hold first then go back to check on my crew.
I took a deep breath and entered inside the hold.

As I reach down to the bottom of cargo hold, I saw that the lone
container was sitting in the centre of hold. The gate of container was
open. My crew and 3 priests were bowing down in front of the container. 

Then from inside the container something came out. It was a very tall
human male. Completely naked. He had long black hair, chiselled body,
dark eyes. I was in completely awe struck by his aura.

He picked up a crew member of mine, tore him apart and started eating
him.

He said something to me which I didn’t understand.

Then came a heavy voice which I understand

“Do the world still follows that guy name Jesus, we invented him after
we know we were betrayed. I am awake and it’s our time to rule again. “

 

I was too shocked to reply.

I watch him smiled and continued eating my crew member.

Then came the voice again It said something but I only remember running
out towards the hold 3 exit.

 

When the rescue services found me adrift in sea, I was in coma for last
2 weeks.

When I was awake, I found that a high-ranking Vatican official was
waiting for me to wake up.

He told me my tugboat was sunk with a drone attack and my whole crew
lost. He told me I was uttering a single word over and over again.

I asked what was the word.

“TARTARIA “
