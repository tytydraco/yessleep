Okay so, before I say anything else I want to make it crystal clear that
I am NOT seeking medical advice. I have an appointment with my PCP
scheduled next week and like hopefully a dozen follow up appointments
after that. I honestly shouldn’t even bother posting here, but I’m
desperate to hear at least one other person say “oh me too!” instead of
just staring at me like I’m crazy. Which I’m not!

I’m not typically a forgetful person, but it feels like this has all
started to build up over time. I used to be able to remember everything
perfectly, I know every single one of my friend’s and families
birthdays. Lately, some things have started to slip through the cracks
but I’m only 24! I can’t be having such a sharp decline now, I have no
idea why I can’t remember writing the diary entries.

The first few were easy to dismiss at first. Sometimes I really do tend
to scribble down my thoughts in a random part of my notebook during
class, but I always cut it out and paste it into my actual journal when
I’m home. I’ve never just forgotten an entry before, especially not this
many.

I’d like to write them all out on the post to prove I’m not just making
this up, but there’s too many personal ones for that. If I start posting
all of them online, I’m probably just going to get even more paranoid
anyway. I think I’m just really worried someone on campus has stumbled
across one of them and is secretly judging me. I keep imagining this
feeling of being watched.

I remember I found one of the first ones while I was flipping through my
notepad to rip out an extra sheet of paper. It was near the back and on
the opposite side of the page.

They’re all in my handwriting and have never been older by a week or so
by the time I find them.

One of the first ones I found (to serve as an example) goes as follows:

-10/23/26-

“hi!!!!!! lol it’s been so long since I last journaled in here. So much
stupid crap has been going on and like I can’t be bothered to write all
the context down sinc my pen looks like it’s gonna die any second now.
As per usual, mother has been rather bothersome about my grades and
brian’s still a douche. He keeps going on about how he wasn’t totally
making out with hannah but like, a girl has eyes y’know?

Gordan has been a dear lately. Like, who am I to complain lol? #husband
material if you ask me sweetheart. He’s maintained a connection, a deep
bond between us. I’m soooooo sick of bethy telling us to “get a
rooooom!” she is like so genuinely jealous could she be more obvious?

godddddd i am so sick of having to put up with everyone. I have to go
catch the bus now tho, should probaably remember to sneak this one into
the journal too.

Hugs and much kisses, AG”

It ends there. It’s completely identical to my handwriting, I know how I
write. If someone had to compare this to my other entries (that I know I
wrote!) they probably wouldn’t be able to tell much of a difference
between them. Well, except for the weird phrasing and like,
writing-style? I don’t know how to explain that it wasn’t me when it
looks like me but it isn’t.

I have been having arguments with my mom lately over degree choices and
my soon to be ex-boyfriend, but it just isn’t even getting basic facts
right. Like, for starters “Brian” is meant to be spelled Bryan and he
was cheating on me with some chick named Heather, not Hannah. I never
actually saw the two of them together, just the usual stuff like texts
and selfies she kept sending him.

No clue who Gordan is, but maybe I was referencing an old character from
a cartoon I used to watch? I haven’t written about him in my diary since
I was like twelve though. I also drive to class now, I haven’t taken a
bus for like three semesters.

Frankly, I just thought I wrote it after knocking back some extra
espresso or something, I’ve been reading about the impact of sleep
deprivation lately (among other things.)

I only started thinking there was a real issue when they started popping
up everywhere, and I really mean everywhere.

I had to reprint an entire set of a Professor’s requested copies of a
reading for one of the entry level literature courses when I noticed I’d
written on the back of all THIRTY-TWO of them!!! They weren’t just
accidentally photo-copied either, each entry was completely unique. If I
hadn’t caught that error I probably would have been fired from the
department. They’re impossible to avoid.

Notebooks, loose paper, magazines. If it had paper, it would have an
entry from me. One of the more tense ones was in a fashion magazine at
my dentist’s office. I’ve never been much of a rulebreaker, so it was
very difficult to try to casually rip out an entry going into detail
about my uncle’s death without alerting the front desk.

It got his cause of death wrong and kept referring to my uncle as the
perpetrator, not the victim he really was. I seemed to be so angry at
him, but I love Uncle Bobby! He’d been like a second father to me for so
long. I had written with so much rage that I had ripped into the next
few pages, slashing some random model’s face with ink.

I had to resist the urge to flip through the rest of them. Maybe they
only appear when I’m looking for them, I don’t know. Nothing makes any
sense any more. They always feel familiar when I see them, like
remembering a name you were trying to recall in a conversation a week
ago.

I lost my car keys a few days ago. I’m scared. How can I remember
something that has never existed while forgetting my everyday at the
same time?

I found the latest one inside my cereal box. I was up late because I
couldn’t sleep. Every time I close my eyes I worry I’m going to find
another stupid entry somewhere else.

I’d been debating duct-taping my hands together or to the bed post to
see if that would finally stop the sudden barrage of writing, but
eventually just gave up. I know I’m not writing them and wasting money
on tape (or god forbid chains) isn’t the answer here.

So, I went to pour my miseries into a soggy bowl of rice krispies and
shined my phone’s flashlight in to make sure I wouldn’t get surprised by
some random bugs. I do honestly tend to forget to clip it back up
sometimes before shutting the box.

Once I saw the date on the cardboard behind the plastic of the bag, I
ripped the box open and read it. It was by far the strangest one I’ve
seen so far. It was just directions from campus to my house.

Every main street I take and every side street I swerve onto if traffic
looks bad. It’s still in my handwriting. When I brushed my thumb against
it, the ink smudged.

I’ve been awake. I know I’ve been awake. My bed is still made and
there’s literally no way I would have been able to contort my arm to fit
inside of the box. Sure the box looks pretty beat up, but there’s
literally no way I could have written this without deconstructing the
box and gluing it back together. It’s not physically possible.

All my pens are still in the box I locked them in. There’s no way I
wrote it, but it’s clearly mine.

I wish I could talk to a professional about this right now, I can’t even
remember where I put my car. It’s not where I parked it on the street.
I’m worried I wont remember my face tomorrow.

I just need to make it to the appointments, then the doctors will tell
me I’m crazy and need meds or something. Then this will all go away! I
think I need to just stay home and take a few absences until then.

I just need to remember that it wasn’t me. Please don’t forget that it
wasn’t me.
