Issac and I are startled once again by the sound of the EAS tones. This
time, however, they sound different.

 

You know how emergency alerts will usually sound with those three short
blasts, then follow up with a longer, higher pitched buzzing? This time,
the alert only sounded with the buzzing.

 

Me and Issac both look at the TV. No voice sounded out this time, which
unnerved me. Instead, the screen showed text, with no noise accompanying
it.

 

ATTENTION

 

This EAS alert will remain silent for your safety. Please pay attention
for information on the unfolding situation. Attention tones will sound
off every 4 hours, followed by a repeat of this text.

 

I look over at Issac, who is looking intently at the screen. His hands
are shaking. Mine are too. I look back at the screen.

 

Witnesses report large, unidentified creatures wandering areas with high
populations. These creatures appear hundreds of feet tall, based on
different reports.

 

There is little information on the appearance of these creatures.
Reports of appearance differ by each report. The only similarities
include the legs.

 

Reports show the creatures are tripodal, though not humanoid. They
appear to have legs compared to a reptile, with hard keratin-like plated
covering the legs.

 

Unfortunately, this is all the information able to be provided.

 

These creatures are to be treated as hostile. As of right now, stay
inside your shelters. Do not make any excessive noise. Do not go
outside. It is not safe. If you hear loud, rhythmic sounds outside, do
not investigate. Let them pass. If they do not pass, and instead stop
right outside your shelter, drop to the ground. Lay flat on your
stomach, cover your mouth. Breath slowly through your nose. Do not make
any noise. Wait for the sounds to retreat.

 

This message will repeat silently every four hours until further notice.
Instructions will stay on the screen.

 

And with that, the screen displays the same instructions.

 

After that message, Issac stands up and starts walking around the house.
I assume he’s trying to find things to muffle sound with. I follow,
wondering if we’re dealing with Death Angels, Tripods, Godzilla, or some
kind of chimera of the three.

 

Issac looks at me, blindly trying to barricade his back door with a
chair from his dining room. “Go around the house and double check if
everything is barricaded. Doors, windows, anything.”

 

I nod and start off. I first check the front door, which is barricaded
by the sofa. I just saw Issac barricade the back door. I look around at
the windows in the living room, kitchen and dining room. I barricade
them with pillows, blankets, jackets, anything I can get my hands on.

 

“There’s a garage connected to my house too, with a side door! Get that
too!” Issac yells.

 

“Okay!” I look around for the garage, and I find a white metal door. I
open it, and I see his garage. It’s a mess. Tools and wood scraps
everywhere, a generator in the middle of the room, jerry cans strewn
about. There’s a huge spiderweb in the corner, and I’m pretty sure I see
eggs on it. I shudder and look around for a door.

 

I notice the large sectional door, but I can’t think of any way to
barricade that. I notice the wood scraps and see a long piece of wood
sticking up. I grab that and shove it into the door handle. I try to
turn it, and it isn’t budging. Good.

 

I notice the side door Issac was yelling about. I look around for
anything to jam against the door, and my eyes land on the generator.

 

I heave the generator up on its wheels. Fuck, this is heavy. I haul it
over to the door and lower it down in front of the door.

 

I move back into the main house. Issac has brought up from his basement
a bunch of cases of water.

 

“This should last us… Uh… … It’ll last us.” Issac says, unsure of how
long.

 

I would normally smile at a comment like that, but the current situation
forbids me from doing so.

 

I look back at the TV. The instructions are still displayed on the
screen.

 

Don’t go outside.

Barricade all entrances to your shelter.

Stay silent.

If you hear loud stomping outside, drop to the ground, control your
breathing, and do not move.

Don’t get up until you hear the stomping retreat.

If lights pass outside, stay away from the windows.

Don’t answer for knocking.

Don’t answer for yelling.

If something is screaming for help outside, ignore it.

 

I shiver at the last three. Whatever is outside is starting to mimic
humans.

 

I close my eyes, the bright light from the TV and surrounding lights
slightly illuminating my eyelids. I open them. They widen as I hear
something outside.

 

Thmp. Thmp. Thmp. The noises sound distant, but they’re getting closer.
Thmp. Thmp. Thmp.

 

Me and Issac simultaneously drop to the ground. I cover my mouth with
both hands and breathe through my nose as the alert told. I shut my
eyes. The noises get louder outside. THMP. THMP.  THMP. They’re right
outside. Then they suddenly stop.

 

I don’t move. Whatever’s outside isn’t moving. What the hell is it
doing? A minute passes. Two. Three. Still nothing. Fucking leave. Four.
Five. FUCKING LEAVE.

 

I start to hear something else outside. A weird sound. Weird, but all
the more terrifying.

 

It sounds like short, heavy exhales, but if whatever was making those
sounds was also simultaneously grunting. The voice wasn’t normal. It was
deep and high pitched at the same time, like multiple voices grunting at
the same time. It sounded like 5 voices, all grunting at the same time
together.

 

Finally I hear the stomping noises start to retreat. I wait until the
noises are far, then until I can’t hear them anymore. I let out a huge
breath that I didn’t know I was holding.

 

I open my eyes and stand up. That’s weird. The lights are out. The TV is
off too. Issac sounds off behind me.

 

“What the fuck was that?” He says, voice shaking.

 

“I don’t fucking know.” I say.

 

Issac looks around. I can barely see him. It’s pitch black in here. I
can’t see more than 2 feet in front of me. Issac disappears into the
darkness, then I hear a soft click.

 

“… The fucking power is out.” He says.

 

I sigh. Fucking phenomenal.

 

Issac appears back into my sight. “I need you to go out into the garage
and fuel the generator out there. There’re cans of gasoline in there
too.”

 

“Why can’t you do it?” I say like the coward I am.

 

“Because I asked you, you’re my brother, and I love you dearly. Now
fucking go do it.”

 

Fuck you, Issac.

 

“Do you at least have a flashlight or something?”

 

Something hits my chest, then falls to the floor. I bend down and see a
small flashlight. I turn it on and ‘accidentally’ shine it into Issac’s
eyes. “Oops.”

 

I make my way over to the garage door and fumble it open. I shine the
flashlight around the room. The generator is sitting against the door
where I left it. I follow a wire under the sectional door. That must be
plugged into the electrical panel. I don’t know why his is outside.

 

I fiddle around with the jerry cans, trying to find one that actually
had gas in it. I finally find one and pour it into the generator’s tank.
I pull the cord. The generator sparks to life, puttering moderately
loudly.

 

The lights don’t turn on.

 

I peek my head back into the main house. “Issac, the generator is on,
but the power isn’t turning back on.”

 

Issac yells back. “It might not be plugged in. You might have to go
outside and plug it in.”

 

“Are you fucking serious?” I yell back.

 

“Yes, I’m fucking serious. It’s right outside. You won’t be out there
for more than 2 minutes.”

 

Fuck. You. Issac.

 

I need to pull the generator away from the door. I lift it with a huff
and start to lug it away from the door, when the wheels break off. A
loud sound rings out from the break. God dammit.

 

I drag the generator to the center of the room. I wince as it makes a
loud screeching noise as the metal grinds against the pavement. I
finally get it to the center of the room and set it down with a loud
thunk.

 

I slowly push open the side door, expecting to see the Death Angel
Tripod Godzilla hybrid. Deathpodzilla. I can’t see anything. It’s
pitch-black outside. Night.

 

I inch my way out of the house, my back sticking to the wall
subconsciously. I scuttle to the right, praying that I’m going the right
way. The grass is too tall to see the wire, so I can’t follow that.

 

I end up doing a full loop around the house until I reach it. My hand
hits the box and I jump instinctively, stifling a small yelp. As soon as
I realize what it is, I move in front of the electrical panel and fling
it open while groping at the ground to find the plug. I grasp it and
pull it up, jamming it into whatever inlet I can find. I sigh in relief.

 

I suck it back in.

 

Thmp. Thmp. Thmp.

 

I find myself on the floor within seconds, covering my mouth. Fuck,
fuck, fuck.

 

The sound gets louder. Louder. This isn’t happening.

 

THMP. THMP. THMP.

 

The sound pauses Not this again. Please don’t do this again.

 

One minute passes. Two. Three. THMP. THMP. THMP. It’s moving closer. The
ground shakes with every step it takes. Am I being too loud? I hold my
breath. Can it smell me?

 

Another pause. I slowly let the breath exit my lungs.

 

A massive reptilian foot lands on the ground, fucking inches from my
face. It takes every ounce of willpower in my body not to scream, not to
move. I lay as still as a stone as I see, just barely in view, as two
more reptilian legs land in front of me. The feet are all facing away
from each other. They are positioned in a triangle shape. Those claws
could impale three people long-way.

 

I slowly look up. I can’t see past the thing’s knees. The legs are thin
but muscular, like the legs of a Spinosaurus. My eyes are forced to
squint as the thing starts to rotate itself. The foot in front of my
face lifts up. Another foot lands next to me. Then it lifts up.

 

Eventually the creature starts to walk away. It gets further, and
further, until I can’t see it, then until I can’t hear it.

 

I exhale in a half grunt, half sigh. I pant heavily, the realization
that I almost died closing in on me.

 

I stand and retreat back into the house within a second. I slam the door
behind me and sprint into the main house. I reach the living room and
just collapse. Nothing’s wrong with me, I’m not hurt, but I just
collapse.

 

I look up to see Issac staring strangely at me. “Did something happen?”

 

I speak so fast you would think I’m an auctioneer.
“Iwasoutsideandwaspluggingthethinginandsomethingalmostcrushedmeand—”

 

“Calm down.” Issac says, shockingly calm. Didn’t he hear what happened
outside?

 

I stand up, regaining my composure. “Sorry. Something almost crushed me
out there. Some… Big fuckin’… I don’t even know. I only saw it’s legs…
It looked like a three-legged Godzilla.”

 

Issac tilts his head. “I didn’t hear anything.”

 

My face contorts into a dumbfounded look. “… How?”

 

Issac shakes his head. “I think you were just dreaming.”

 

I go to respond but I shut up when I start to smell something. It smells
like a mixture of feces, rotten meat, and soil. I look to the basement
door, where the smell is emanating from.

 

Issac follows my gaze. “Oh yeah. While you were out there, a raccoon
came in here. I panicked and killed it. I left it downstairs.”

 

I scrunch up my face. “Eugh…”

 

Issac turns his back to me, and my heart drops like a 50-pound dumbbell.

 

My brother Issac got a tattoo when he turned 18. It was of a bird with a
sword in its mouth. He got it on the right forearm.

 

I noticed it was now on the left.
