It’s always a space I’d never need to use. If I did, it’d move. I’ve
dropped things down—no bottom. It’s been in a ceiling before, like a
bottomless sky. I named it Chasm. I don’t know why—maybe like I have
some control. It doesn’t help.

Nobody believes you anyway. I stopped telling people because no one sees
it. No matter how much I try, how much I point and beg and plead. They
look right through it. Or it moves. I don’t know which is worse. If I go
in, I think I’ll die. Like being pulled toward light, the way people
talk about at death, except this isn’t that. This is some kind of hell.
I don’t even believe in hell, but what else do you call it? What else
even fits?

Maybe I have a new disease. Cancer, dementia, brain spasm, deformity.
Maybe this is how people just vanish. I paid for a brain scan. They said
it looked “whatever normal is.” Typical. No professional has a
diagnosis. I’ve tried everything I know to do. I’m not stupid—or at
least, I don’t think I am. Same for crazy. But if you were crazy, how
would you know? The most honest answer: I do not know.

I love horror stories and now I’m in one. I’m not lying. I know how
crazy it sounds. But what would you do? You’d justify it too. You’d have
to to get through the night.

I never used to sleep with socks on, but I do now. I’ve heard it helps
you sleep better, but really it’s if I need to run away. Because it’s
just open. I don’t know what will come out of it or where it will be
next. There’s no sound. Once, I think I was dreaming, but I heard a low,
wet turning, like something shifting. Then nothing. I think about that a
lot.

Anywhere I go it’s there, just a room, a hallway, a thin wall away. I
noticed it about a year ago, but I hate wondering how long before I saw.
What if it was there when I was a kid? For a while, it came and went.
Now I think it was always somewhere near me. I went camping—it was in
the bushes. A black oval against the dirt. I slept on a boat in the
middle of a lake and it was there, at the bottom of the boat, staring up
through the wood.

I can’t find anybody with anything that’s ever been similar. Bottomless
pit stories, yes, but I’m skeptical of them all. I don’t think anyone
will ever be able to help me, and eventually I might go down that hole.
You’re wondering: what happens when you throw something in?
It disappears. I don’t know where it goes. But it won’t let me throw
things in anymore. I tried a coin last week—it hovered, trembling, and
rolled back toward my feet. I tried again, harder. Same thing. Like
Chasm spat it out. Saving space..?

Other people can’t see it either. At first I’d try to show them—but
nothing. Now it hides when I point. Adapts. I don’t know what it is. I
don’t know if it’s learning.

I’m writing this down because I feel like I’m running out of time. I
don’t feel danger every second—just this constant, gnawing anxiety that
never leaves. I can’t ever relax. I’ve written before and those notes
disappear. Or they get shorter. Or the words change. I don’t know if
this will even survive. But I’m trying.

I don’t want to die. But if this is how I go—I had a good run. I’m not
scared of death, just the unknowing—the pain. I’m not suicidal, but I
ask myself: do I jump before it takes me, or do I climb in? I don’t
know. I’m just beside myself, but I guess we all are with death. We all
face death, just ourselves. I’m not trying to paint some insightful
metaphor or allegory, I’m just here, experiencing this, alone. Not ready
to die, but feeling like I have to prepare for it.

Last night it was close—at the top of my closet. I shut the door and
wouldn’t look. Sometimes it stays in one place a while. I heard
something though.

But this morning, my closet door was open.
My socks were gone.
