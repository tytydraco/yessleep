I’m 17. I recently started college (which is called “college” in the
American system, at my country “collage” is little bit diffrent termin),
and I signed up for an app for students from all over the country. You
can only sign up for it through Telegram, but your profile won’t be
displayed, it’s just to verify the authenticity of your account.

I hadn’t posted anything, but on Friday I decided I wanted to try to
climb to the top of the rankings. After just a couple of posts, I’d
already gone from 800th place to 47th. I didn’t expect it myself, but I
was pleasantly surprised. In the end, I posted a photo in one of my
profile pictures that showed off my style, but without showing my face.
My style is actually pretty unusual for my country. Hoodie with fluffy
hood, chains with stars, and stuff like that.

I received a private message on this app from a girl who wrote that I
had beautiful hands and that she wanted to hold them in hers. I laughed,
thanked her, and forgot about it. I didn’t ask her out because her
profile listed a university in another city. That evening, I went to the
mall, one of the biggest in town, but to my surprise, it was half-empty.
I don’t go there often, but a friend of mine lives nearby, so he said it
was unusual for a place like that.

After shopping, I was standing on the street waiting for the bus. It was
already very dark, and there weren’t many people around. While waiting,
I went into a nearby café, and when I paid with Apple Pay, I saw a
message from that app. In it, that girl had left me another private
message that completely knocked me off my feet. “So you live near (that
one shopping centre), did I guess right?” I checked her profile, the
city hadn’t changed, and I hadn’t posted any photos from there. I read
it but didn’t reply, and just as I was getting close to home, a new
message came in: “Please don’t ignore me I want to be friends.” I
blocked her and reported to admins of this app.

Tonight I went for a walk with my dog, and I usually walk around my
neighborhood. I haven’t shown my neighborhood anywhere, nor have I even
mentioned it. When I left the house and took out my phone to play some
music, I saw a message from a different account, but with a similar
writing style. In it, she asked why I had blocked her and whether the
dog in my profile picture was mine. This scared me even more, because
the dog is only on my profile picture in Telegram, which a regular user
of the app shouldn’t have access to. Glancing over my shoulder the whole
time, I went for a walk with my dog. As I was walking down a street
where the streetlights usually aren’t on (but where I’ve walked since
childhood, which made me feel safe), I saw a man running down the hill.
And he was running straight toward me.

Of course I was scared; I quickly reached into my bag for a knife while
the dog was barking, and by the time I pulled the knife out with
trembling hands, the guy was already gone. Glancing around nervously, I
dragged the dog home and locked myself in my room without turning on the
light. Those two accounts were deleted from the app, and I don’t know
what to do. I’ve temporarily hidden all my posts from there, deleted my
profile picture, and changed my username. I don’t know what to do…
