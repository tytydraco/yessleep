This happened around two years ago and I still don’t like driving alone
at night because of it.

I’ve driven on empty roads plenty of times before, but this was the only
time something happened that I genuinely couldn’t explain.

I was going towards Kalimpong (India) and was somewhere past Sevoke on
NH10. This was around 3 or maybe 3:30 in the morning.

I’d left later than I originally planned and was just trying to get
there without making too many stops.
I’d already crossed the Kali Mandir and Coronation Bridge area.

If you know that road you know it gets pretty empty after Sevoke,
especially that late. My friend from Siliguri had also told me not to
take it at night because of landslides and accidents around Kalijhora
but I didn’t really think much of it.

I’d driven through similar roads before and figured I’d be fine as long
as I was careful.

There weren’t many cars. I remember one truck near Sevoke but after that
I was basically alone for a while. I was using my high beams whenever I
could because some stretches were completely dark.

At some point I saw a guy standing on the right side of the road.

He had a big dark hoodie on and was facing away from the road, like
literally looking into the trees. He wasn’t moving or doing anything
that I could see. Just standing there with his back towards the road.

I thought maybe he was pissing or something and drove past. It seemed
like a strange place to stop but I didn’t really have any reason to
think about it further.

Around five minutes later I saw him again.

Or at least someone who looked exactly like him.

Same hoodie, same height, standing the same way with his back towards
me. I remember trying to get a better look as I approached because
something about the whole thing felt familiar. I didn’t see his face
either time.

This is when I started getting freaked out because nobody had passed me.
I was doing around 60 too. There was no way that guy could’ve somehow
got ahead of me.

There weren’t any other vehicles around that I could remember, and I
hadn’t stopped anywhere. I even checked my mirrors because I was
starting to feel uncomfortable.

I just kept driving. I didn’t want to stop and I definitely didn’t want
to turn around to check if it was the same person.

A little later, somewhere before Kalijhora I think, there was a gravel
area on the side where trucks sometimes stop. I’d seen these little
stopping areas along the road before, so the parked car itself didn’t
seem unusual at first.

There was an old white Alto parked there with everything off.
And there was a guy sitting on the bonnet.

Same fucking hoodie.

I only saw him for maybe two seconds but his head was tilted really far
back and his mouth looked open. Like he was looking straight up. I
couldn’t see his face properly because of the angle, but the way he was
sitting looked really unnatural.

His body seemed completely still. I remember gripping the steering wheel
harder when I realised what I was looking at.

Right after I passed the Alto the hazards blinked once.
I don’t mean they were already blinking. They came on once and went off.
I caught it in my rearview mirror and I’m certain both lights flashed
together. I kept looking back for a few seconds but they never came on
again.

At that point I just wanted to get somewhere with people. I wasn’t even
thinking about reaching Kalimpong anymore. I just wanted to find
somewhere I could stop without being completely alone.

I eventually found a petrol pump further down and stopped there. I don’t
remember exactly which one. I sat in the car until it started getting
light because I didn’t really want to get out. I kept checking my
mirrors whenever I heard another vehicle and tried distracting myself
with my phone.

I remember feeling a little stupid because nothing had actually happened
to me, but I still couldn’t shake the feeling that something was wrong.

When I finally did, I noticed a handprint on the rear passenger window
because the glass had fogged up.
It looked like someone had put their hand there and dragged their
fingers down a little.

I could make out the palm and the individual fingers pretty clearly. It
wasn’t a perfect print but it definitely looked like a hand.

I checked it from outside first. I assumed someone must have touched the
window at some point and the condensation had made the mark visible.

The print was on the inside.

Could’ve been an old handprint that only showed up because of the
condensation.

That’s probably what it was. I tried remembering the last time someone
had sat in the back seat, but I honestly couldn’t recall anyone touching
that window.

But I was alone for that entire trip and I still have no explanation for
seeing that guy three times.
