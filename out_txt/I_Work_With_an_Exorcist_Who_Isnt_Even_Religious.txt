(Part1)..

I worked with a very well-known exorcist. His name is Dick Night.

The funny thing about this dude is that he isn’t even religious. He has
some pretty weird and strange ways of banishing ghosts, demons, and
other supernatural creatures.

He’s a hard ex-military dude who always wears sunglasses, even while
asleep. He has short blonde hair that’s always slicked back, a hard
expression, and a deep voice that somehow makes everything he says sound
serious.

We live in a big apartment with a wide living room that isn’t separated
from where we sleep. The kitchen doesn’t have its own enclosed space
either. It’s basically one big room with a bed, a couch, a kitchen, and
whatever paranormal equipment Dick has decided to leave lying around.

We get emails or calls from clients who need help with something
supernatural that isn’t right.

One night, I was resting on my bed, taking a nap before the next
mission. I was suddenly jump-scared by Dick’s loud voice.

“Lady! You don’t worry. I, Dick Night, will handle this phenomenon right
away.”

He hung up the call.

He turned to me while gritting his teeth, his lips pulled into a hard
expression.

“JOHN! We got a needy HOT 44-year-old woman with a daughter calling for
our help.”

I narrowed my eyes and looked at him in confusion.

“The hell is wrong with you?”

He dashed toward me and lifted me easily by the collar.

“NO TIME TO EXPLAIN.”

He was spitting everywhere on my face as he dragged me out of the
apartment and down to his black BMW.

It seemed he didn’t consider me even a human with rights at that point.

He swung the front passenger door open and threw me inside as if I was a
bag. Mind you, Dick carried me as if I was a literal bag down the ten
floors of the apartment complex we lived in. Just wanting you to picture
how terrifying this dude is.

Anyway, he got in, started the engine, and drove to the destination that
was on the GPS.

Twenty minutes into the drive, we were passing through a massive forest.
The route we took went between a forest called Woodcrave.

I had heard of Woodcrave before. Not because it was some famous tourist
destination or anything like that. People just talked about the place in
a way that made you want to change the subject.

The trees were packed tightly together on both sides of the road, their
branches twisting over the asphalt and blocking whatever little
moonlight managed to get through. The forest was so dark that the
headlights barely seemed to reach beyond the first few rows of trees.

The wind was strong enough to make the trees sway violently from side to
side.

But there was something strange about it.

Every time the wind stopped, the trees stopped too.

All of them.

The forest would become completely still for a few seconds, as if
someone had pressed a pause button on the entire landscape. Then the
wind would return, and the branches would start thrashing again.

I stared through the passenger window.

For a moment, I thought I saw something standing between the trees.

I blinked.

Nothing.

Probably just a tree.

I looked back toward the road.

“Hmmm, JOHN! How long will it take us to reach the castle?” Dick asked
me while gripping the wheel hard enough to bend it.

“Castle? Wait, you didn’t tell me we are heading to a castle…” I rubbed
my left eye. I was still sleepy a bit.

“Does it matter?” he said.

“W-what do you mean, ‘Does it matter?’ Of course, Dick. I’m your
partner, dude.”

He snapped his head toward me as he kept driving, not regarding the fact
he wasn’t even looking at the pitch-black road ahead.

“Fine, I will explain to you,” he said as he removed his hands
completely off the wheel.

“Uhh, Dick?”

“We are possibly dealing with a paranormal entity of some kind. The hot
mother of one daughter. This woman’s name is Olivia.”

I kept looking at the road to make sure we don’t hit a tree and die
before even reaching our destination.

“Dick, I changed my mind…”

“OLIVIA heard about us, John, THE NIGHT JACKERS. Our brand name is very
well known.”

He grabbed the wheel and snapped back to the road.

“Yeah, that’s it pretty much,” he said calmly.

I sighed in relief.

“Five minutes and we will be there,” I said.

“Good,” Dick replied as he jammed his foot into the accelerator pedal,
driving faster.

We reached the castle in no time.

The castle was enormous. It stood at the end of the road like something
that had been there long before the surrounding forest, its tall stone
walls disappearing into the darkness above us. Ahead of us were the
castle gates.

Dick parked and was about to lower the window to talk through the
intercom, but the gates suddenly opened on their own.

“Hmmm. Efficient,” he said calmly.

We drove through the gates as they closed behind us.

I looked back through the rear window.

The gates shut with a heavy metallic groan.

Beyond them, Woodcrave seemed even darker than it had from the road.

West of the castle was a big garden that seemed to be locked up. A tall
iron fence surrounded it, and the gates were secured with several thick
chains. I couldn’t see much beyond the fence. The trees and overgrown
bushes inside swallowed almost all of the light.

I didn’t dwell much on it.

Dick parked the car right in front of the castle.

Then he kicked the driver’s door off.

Literally off.

The entire door went flying and crashed into the locked garden gates.

He jumped out and took a deep inhale of the air around the castle. He
always does that to assess the place.

How, you may ask?

I have no clue.

“You know you could have just… opened the door?” I said as I got out of
the car, closing the passenger door behind me.

He snapped his head toward me very fast.

“THAT if I were a pussy. Come on, John. We got work to do. Go grab the
bags with the equipment.”

I rolled my eyes as I walked behind the trunk to get the equipment bag.

The castle was strangely quiet.

There was no sound coming from inside. No voices. No footsteps. Not even
the usual creaking you would expect from an old building this large.

Just the wind moving around us.

I followed Dick up the stairs toward the front door.

After getting the bag, I walked up to the front door where Dick stood.

“Well? Why didn’t you knock?”

He put his index finger between my lips.

“I smell something suspicious.”

His nose wrinkled as he sniffed hard.

“Step aside.”

He pushed me back.

“Dick, you didn’t even—”

With a primal roar, he ran into the two doors of the castle, barging
through them as they swung open and smacked loudly against the exterior
walls.

He stumbled and fell face-first onto the floor.

Then, in a split second, he stood up, keeping his form steady and ready.
He clearly didn’t want to leave himself in a vulnerable position.

I sighed as I walked into the castle and closed the doors behind me.

The moment I stepped inside, the atmosphere changed.

The castle was classy, but something about it felt deeply uncomfortable.
The floor was made of dark-brown ceramic, polished enough to reflect the
weak light coming from the windows. Elegant wallpaper covered the walls,
and old paintings hung between tall wooden pillars.

Everything looked expensive.

Everything looked maintained.

Yet the place felt abandoned.

The air was colder inside than it should have been.

I glanced toward the enormous staircase leading deeper into the castle.
The darkness beyond it seemed unusually thick, almost as though the
light from the entrance refused to travel any farther.

“Honestly, Dick, those people called for our help. Why the fuck did you
barge in like a madman? You could have given someone a heart attack.”

His eyebrows tensed as he took a crouched combat pose, raising one
finger as he motioned for me to crouch too.

One thing I don’t question Dick about is his instincts.

I crouched down too.

“What?” I whispered.

“I smell it.”

He paused.

“It is reeking of piss.”

I narrowed my eyes in disgust.

Dick immediately stood up and pulled out his guns.

“WHO IS THERE!” he shouted as he took a 180-degree turn.

A woman stood in the darkness.

She looked to be in her forties. Probably Olivia.

Her brown hair was tied into a messy bun, and she wore a nightgown. She
held out a candle through the dark open space.

The small flame illuminated her face, but everything behind her remained
completely black.

“Oh my, my. My apologies if I scared you,” she said in a soothing voice.

“Oh, please, don’t apologise to us, Ms. We, after all, are the intruders
who barged in.”

She laughed softly.

“No worries. My friend warned me about Mr. Night’s extreme actions.”

I exhaled in relief.

Thank God she understood.

Dick pulled away his guns and walked toward Olivia.

“My apology, madam. My top priority was to make sure you and your
daughter were safe. No evil intentions were meant.”

He grabbed her hand.

Olivia gasped as he kissed it.

She smiled awkwardly.

“Thanks, Mr. Night. No need to be too formal. I am rather thankful that
you two came to finally deal with this thing that has been terrorising
me and my daughter.”

“Well, would you please tell us the nature of this thing?” I asked her
as I brought out my notebook.

She nodded.

“It is not a ghost nor a demon. It is a weird entity that seems to have
taken my castle as its new play toy to play with.”

She looked toward the dark hallway behind her.

“It keeps putting out doors that lead to other spaces I have never seen
in my life.”

“Hmm, sounds like we are dealing with a tricky fella.” Dick crossed his
arms.

That’s when he snapped his head toward the big staircase leading to the
second floor.

“Mom! Who is here?”

It was Olivia’s daughter.

“Don’t worry, sweetheart. Those are the Night Jackers I talked to you
about.”

I pinched the bridge of my nose whenever I heard our brand name.

“God…” I muttered, embarrassed.

Meanwhile, Dick puffed out his chest proudly.

Her name was Rosa. According to the description in our files, she had
black hair and blue eyes.

“Name’s Dick, and that there is my partner, John. We are here to deal—”

“I know who you guys are. Can you guys get to the point and start
dealing with that thing?”

I looked at Dick.

He looked at me.

He smirked.

“Will do.”

For the next hour and a half, we spent preparing to catch the entity. We
placed cameras around the castle and protective salt circles throughout
the living room and other areas.

The castle was huge, so it took us a while to cover everything.

“Alright, one last step,” Dick said.

He reached into the equipment bag and pulled out a jar containing
something yellow.

“What is that?” I asked, genuinely curious.

That was my biggest mistake.

“Twenty-year-old witch piss jar.”

I gagged without even smelling it.

“It will help us against the entity when it makes its presence known to
us. We will splash it.”

I rubbed the back of my neck, trying to calm myself down. now i was more
worried for the entity.

Dick snapped his head toward the two women.

“Ladies, I would advise you to stay HERE. Me and my partner will venture
up the second floor.”

Olivia nodded with hopeful eyes, while her daughter seemed skeptical of
us. Honestly, I didn’t blame her.

We walked up the staircase to the second floor. At the end of the stairs
was a long hallway with lots of rooms.

The hallway was unnaturally quiet. No creaking floorboards, no wind
coming through the windows, nothing. Even our footsteps seemed to
disappear into the carpet.

“Hmm, that sight reminds me of when I fought a cultist leader whose name
was Fang Gooner.”

“What does a long hallway full of different rooms have to do with Fang
Gooner?”

Dick scoffed at my question.

“I’m too lazy to explain. COME ON.”

He walked into the hallway, leaving me behind.

I shook my head, frustrated by him, and followed.

We kept walking past a lot of rooms.

Then we passed the same painting twice.

I stopped.

“Dick.”

He kept walking.

“Dick.”

“What?”

“I think we’ve already passed this.”

He glanced at the painting.

“Huh.”

He stared at it for a moment before shrugging.

“Probably has a twin.”

I stared at him.

“That’s not how paintings work.”

He continued walking.

I followed, but I started paying closer attention to the hallway. The
rooms on either side seemed to become less frequent the farther we went.
Some doors were slightly open, revealing nothing but darkness. Others
had no handles at all.

The hallway seemed to stretch farther every time we looked ahead.

Eventually, the lights stopped.

Ahead of us was pitch-black darkness.

“So what is the plan?”

Dick hummed.

“I’m working on it.”

Of course he was.

We kept moving until we suddenly heard something metallic scrape against
the darkness ahead.

Then silence.

I barely had time to turn my head.

Two needles shot through the darkness and pierced both of my shoulders.

I screamed in agony as I felt them sink deep into my bones.

Dick shoved me against one of the room doors to take cover.

“Dick! Take cover!” I winced through the pain.

He smirked.

“I got this, kid.”

Thousands of needles suddenly came flying toward Dick.

To my surprise, he started dodging them by doing a reverse backflip.

He kept flipping backward as the needles shot past him, somehow avoiding
every single one.

I was in shock.

I had to admit, that was pretty damn impressive.

Then I noticed something.

The staircase behind us was gone.

The hallway behind Dick seemed longer than it had been a moment ago. He
kept backing away until his heels reached the railing.

“D-Dick! Stop it!”

Dick didn’t stop his backflip dodge.

He went straight over the railing.

I watched him disappear from sight.

A second later, I heard him collapse somewhere below.

“AHH… FUCK! All good, JOHN!” he shouted.

I sighed in relief.

The needles stopped.

I slowly stood up, still in pain.

I looked toward the staircase.

There was no staircase.

Just a wall.

“Guess I will have to do this alone.”

I turned toward the darkness ahead.

The hallway stretched into it like it had been waiting for me.

I started walking.
