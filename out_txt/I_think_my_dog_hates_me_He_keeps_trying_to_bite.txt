I have loved dogs since I was a toddler. My mom says my first word was
“puppy”.

All my life, I knew I would be the absolute best dog owner. I imagined
myself taking him for walks, playing fetch, snuggling on the couch, all
of it. But that’s not what’s happened.

Instead of cuddles and bonding, my dog hates me. He growls and groans
when I walk in the room, and I’ve had to keep him muzzled because he
screams, howls, and bites whenever I get close. I don’t know what to do.
He shakes like he’s nervous and won’t look at me when I call his name.
He’s new, and I understand it takes time for a pet to acclimate to their
new environment, but I’m not seeing any progress. He has accidents and I
clean them up without getting upset or raising my voice, and I offer him
treats but he won’t take them. He just stays in my closet, curled up in
a ball. I feel bad for the poor guy, but it’s not the “best buddy”
situation I have dreamed of for years. I don’t dare take him for walks
because he’s a big breed and I’m worried he’ll escape the leash and run
off. I’m at a loss for what to do.

The other problem with all of this is my parents. They don’t know I have
a dog down in the basement with me. My dad was a voiceover artist for
several years, and when my parents first bought this house they
converted the basement into a sound-proof studio for him to get work
done. When I was 3, they changed it back into a bedroom and my dad took
on more of the sound engineering/editing side of the business.

I’m an only child after years of infertility treatments, and I’ll be
honest my parents let me get away with murder. I have the entire
basement to myself with my own tv, mini fridge, and king size bed. I
have all the newest VR equipment, an Xbox, PlayStation, whatever I want.
I have a car I can’t even legally drive yet, next year’s model.
Christmas is always huge at our house. The only thing my mom and dad
ever said no to was a dog.

My dad is deathly allergic to dogs. Well, he’s allergic to everything,
honestly. Cats, dogs, horses, peanuts, shellfish, chocolate, dairy,
gluten, pollen, bananas… The list goes on. For years, we were careful.
Washed our hands 47 times a day and never brought contaminents into the
house. In the meantime I researched hairless breeds of dogs, cats, hell
even guinea pigs! But no matter how “hypoallergenic” the breeder claims
an animal is, my dad can’t be around them.

I’m more than grateful to my parents for the life they’ve given me. I’ve
never had to ask for money to go to the mall, for a ride somewhere, or
tickets to a concert. I’m spoiled and super privileged, but I try not to
act like it. The one thing they ask of me is to be respectful of my
dad’s health issues.

But 6 months ago, I decided that I could pretty easily keep a secret
animal in the basement.

Since my dad moved to editing he never comes down here. He has an office
upstairs where he works, and in the basement there’s a walkout to the
backyard. I planned it all, keep the dog in my room/ walk-in closet,
only take him out through the back. As long as I got a breed that wasn’t
too vocal, there should be no issue.

Well, I tried it. It lasted for 2 days before my dad started sneezing
and wheezing and my mom got suspicious. I rehomed Buddy pretty quickly
without them noticing, and heartbroken I knew I would have to give up
the dream until I moved out in a few years.

However, a couple months later, I discovered another breed, a mutt, if
you will, that I know for sure my dad’s no allergic to. I tested it out
before I got it. I planted contaminated things around the house, small
things he wouldn’t think about. Dad didn’t so much as sniffle. That’s
when I knew this would work.

So I went to the dumpster behind the janky Burger King a few miles from
my school. I drove, illegally, and waited for the rascal to show up. I’d
been watching for a stray, something without paperwork that my parents
could track, and I found the perfect one. He had soft brown hair and
chocolate eyes, and the cutest bark that almost sounded like laughter. I
knew he would be perfect, and I was more than happy to rescue him.

Catching him was super difficult. He put up one hell of a fight,
screaming and writhing as I pinned him down. Luckily nobody noticed or
cared, and I finally got him into the trunk.

Getting him into the house was the hardest part. I had to leave the poor
thing in my trunk for an hour while I waited for my parents to go to
bed, but I promised him I’d make it up to him once we got to the
basement. He just whimpered, the poor guy.

I had to basically drag him from the car to my back door. He was making
such a ruckus I worried the neighbors would hear, but nobody’s lights
came on. I hauled him inside and locked him in my closet.

To say he freaked out was an understatement. He was howling and
screaming, forcing me to muzzle him and tie his legs together so he
couldn’t run. I know, I know, it’s not best practice, but I didn’t know
what to do! Google and Chat GPT were useless. The regular advice of
watch and wait or give him a treat or space were not helping. He
thrashed and fought me like the devil himself.

A few days later I went in for my nightly check-in.

“Okay, Rex. Are you going to be a good boy?” I reached out and ruffled
his hair gently.

He nodded slowly.

“Good. I’m going to take the sock out of your mouth so you can have some
water, okay boy? But be quiet, Mom and Dad are asleep upstairs.”

I slowly unclasped the muzzle and removed it from his face. There were
tear streaks all the way to his chin. I grabbed a tissue.

“Darn it, Rex. Sorry about your weepy eyes. I’ll call a vet tomorrow and
see what we can do.”

He whimpered.

I carefully reached out and pulled the sock from his mouth. He gasped,
bending immediately to take long gulps of water from the bowl at his
feet. After several minutes he sat up.

“Please,” he whispered. “Let.. me… Go.”

I tsk tsk’d at him. “Rexy, dogs don’t talk. I’ll have to muzzle you if
you try that again.”

“I’m not a dog, you psychopath!!!”

He lunged at me, his bound hands coming up toward my neck. I blocked
him, shoving his arms to the side, but he threw his weight on me and
pinned me down, biting me on the shoulder.

Luckily, I’m bigger than he is, so I fought until I rolled us over so I
was on top. I shoved the sock in his bloody mouth and held his face down
in the carpet until he stopped fighting. I grabbed the muzzle and
strapped it on him again.

“BAD dog. Bad.” I looked at my shoulder and swore. I’d have to figure
out how to explain the blood to my mom in the morning, but for now I was
exhausted.

I got up to leave the room and looked back. Rex was sitting up in the
corner, his brown eyes glaring murderously at me.

“I’ll be the best owner, if you’ll let me,” I said softly.

He replied something muffled I couldn’t understand.

I turned off the light and closed the door.

So now I’ve come to reddit for help. I’ve read about how dogs establish
dominance and have been trying to show him, but he keeps fighting me.
It’s been a month, and I’m not seeing any sign that we’re bonding. He’s
losing weight because he hardly eats. I bought 4 different kinds of
Purina and he leaves it mostly untouched. I can’t take him outside
because he’ll bolt. I get anxiety leaving him all day when I’m at
school. I really don’t believe in hitting your dog, so any suggestions
of abuse will be deleted.

All I ever wanted was a pet, and when I finally got one my dad’s not
allergic to, it hates me.

I just want him to be a good boy.
