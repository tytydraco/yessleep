This post has been a long time coming, I tried a while back with a new
account to put this on here and got karma filtered for the longest time.
I’ve been having the oddest dreams, and I believe they’re linked to my
childhood sleep paralysis. I just worry that now something has changed.

I (21yr old F) as a young child (about 8-15) was notorious for waking my
parents up with nightmares. It got to the point where my mom says I was
waking her up nearly every night with some new dream and begging to
sleep in her bed.

I’m aware this isn’t uncommon for kids to have a lot of nightmares, but
I wasn’t just getting normal nightmares. I slept walked and had regular
episodes of sleep paralysis so often that my mom put me through multiple
sleep studies as she was concerned with my mental health. There was even
a time when I was about 11 where I didn’t think good dreams were
actually real, just that everyone always got nightmares like I did.

My dreams and terrors had common threads too common characters and
themes. My mom told me about many times where I’d sleep walk and come
into her room saying creepy things and I’d complain of similar creatures
each night (this is part of why some of these stories may seem janky? I
guess, most of these I don’t remember and simply have my mom’s
retelling, usually of the times where I scared her with the things I
said.)

The first example I’ve got of these “episodes” was when I would sleep
walk up to my parents room. Most commonly it was there but my mom said
that it was like I’d seek her out and find her no matter what room she
was in. As she tells it I would peek in her room with the door just open
enough to see her. And I’d “stare” at her until she woke up. I say
“stare” as during these sleepwalking episodes my eyes would always be
closed. Once she’d woken up to me peeking in she’d invite me in and ask
me what was wrong, I’d walk in slowly and calmly and tell her very
evenly that I was dead.

The reason why I was dead would change for instance sometimes I’d just
calmly over and over say that my heart wasn’t beating. She could never
convince me otherwise either, she would listen to my heart and tell me
she could hear it, say every impossibility and I’d still continue to
repeat that it just wasn’t beating and once she finally gave up I’d just
slip under the covers with her and go to sleep.

Over time though these stories would evolve, like when I joined the auto
body program in my middle school she said I would sleep walk upstairs
and regale her with how I’d been working under a car and my head had
gotten crushed when the car rolled backwards, that time my mom had told
me I talked in this weird squeaky voice and when she asked why I had
told her it was because the car destroyed my windpipe. Or when I did
ballet I’d walk in and say I’d bent too far backwards and broken my
spine, she said I’d keep walking like I was overbalanced and stumbling
while I was talking, tilted at an odd angle. There were others but she
told me after a while she’d just invite me in and let me go on while she
plugged her ears because the stories of how I’d “died” would always keep
getting more detailed and graphic and she didn’t like hearing them.

When she told me about all these about five years later I was pretty
shocked. I remembered coming to her with nightmares alot but I had never
had any dreams correlating to this kind of thing before. I figured if I
was having nightmares like that I would have remembered them just
because so many of the creatures from my night terrors are still so
vivid to me to this day. But nothing, just these sleepwalking events
that I had no idea about that gave my mom terrible anxiety for years.

Like I said earlier, most of what I remember of this time in my life was
my night terrors, those started just near the end of my middle school
years, and my mom tells me that it was around this time that I stopped
sleep walking too.

Most of my nightmares were pretty standard kid stuff but these sleep
paralysis episodes were weird. As I recall there were three reoccurring
characters. The tall man, the little one, and the monster? I guess is
what we’ll call them. They were all these kind of shadow figure things
without many defining characteristics so they’re hard to define on here.
The first one to show up was the “tall man”. He was as the name
describes a tall shadow silhouette who would stand in the corner of my
room. This guy was so tall that his head had to be bent at a 90° angle
just to fit in there. His neck was pressed up against the ceiling at the
bend and I had a loft bed at the time so when he stared at me his head
would be about a foot or two over the railing of my bed. This guy was
the least “malevolent” I guess of my night terrors. He just wanted to
watch me lay there while I couldn’t move. The worst part was when he’d
decide to change where he was watching me from in the room and he’d
start to walk, his neck and head would make this horrible scraping noise
as he dragged them along the ceiling like the bones of his vertebrae
were exposed or something. But otherwise he never did anything to me.

The “little one” was next showing up in between episodes of the tall man
and other regular nightmares. I never knew if there was more than one of
them, but they or it were these little tiny crouched creatures that
would run around near the railings of my bed. I could follow them with
my eyes and they were quick as anything. Just running around in the
corners of my vision until they would stop, stock still and start
creeping up the bed towards my face. When this happened they’d always go
super slow like agonizingly slow. But I didn’t like the take my eyes off
them, just instinctually needing to know where they were even if there
wasn’t anything I could do to stop them. I still distinctly remember the
feeling of tiny feet walking over me muffled by my blanket. Once this
thing was about a few inches away from my face it would lean down and
put its hands on the covers. Slowly pushing them forward until it was
just about to touch my neck and then it would stop for a minute and look
into my eyes before standing up fully and scampering off towards the
railings again. This routine would usually happen 2 or 3 times before I
managed to snap out of the episode and wake up.

This last one… I’m unsure if it actually counts as “sleep paralysis”
because whenever the monster came around I felt like could move. I’m not
sure how I just knew internally that if I wanted to I could move,
instead of that regular feeling of helplessness. It was just when it
came to this thing, I really really didn’t want to move. I’m sure it
probably looks more animalistic than my other night terrors, I never
really saw the thing and I only had a night terror about it likee two
times, but the noises it made when it was…eating. I just can’t fathom
that those noises could come from anything with a Human-Shaped mouth.
Essentially it would start with me waking up facing the wall instead of
the rest of the room. And there would be like footsteps or scuffling
noises, just I could always audibly tell something was in there with me.
And then my door would creak open. It was big, it had to be. It always
made these giant thumping step noises when it walked in and whatever was
sniffling around my room would usually go quiet and start crying I
guess? There would be these weird quiet hiccuping sob noises from
wherever the thing was but it was hard to tell because the monster would
be breathing really heavily like it was excited and drown them out.

There wasn’t usually a chase, I don’t know if the thing in my room came
over to it on its own accord or if the monster could just reach it from
wherever it was but eventually the heavy breathing would stop. Replaced
by these tearing sounds, just crunching visceral noises that sound like
whatever was in my room was being ripped apart completely. The monster
seemed to enjoy this process. It would take a long long time to get
through it’s meal. I could always hear it chewing and dripping drool
through it’s teeth. And finally once it had finished the monster would
lean against the wall with a thump and just take a minute. Like it was
recuperating after a really filling meal, one that tires you out.
Eventually it would leave me there, closing the door behind it. And I
would “wake up”. Those ones were the hardest to separate from reality
because I always felt like I’d just sit up after the monster left, there
was no blackout no nothing just once it was gone, I’d move and was
awake.

At some point (it must have been the second of third time I heard it)
the monster left and I stopped getting night terrors. Never saw the tall
man or little one. Most of my nights became dreamless and I was really
grateful for it. That was until about a week ago. When I woke up in the
middle of the night and realized that I couldn’t move. I was laying on
my back and could look around so I just assumed that maybe there was a
gap and my night terrors were back. But nothing was ever there, no
creatures in the corner of my vision, no tall man watching me from the
side, It was just me laying in my bed completely paralyzed in a constant
state of waiting. These episodes are longer too. I hated my night
terrors. But these empty helpless episodes are worse. I’d rather see
something horrible than the uncertainty of knowing whether one day I’ll
never leave my own bed because I can’t move out of it.
