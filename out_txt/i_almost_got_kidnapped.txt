I (22 F) recently lost my car, which I’ve had since I was 16, so being
without one is new to me. I used to smoke in my car, but tonight, after
taking a tolerance break from weed (I’ve been a heavy smoker since 18),
I decided to walk two minutes down my street to a gazebo in my
neighborhood around 9:30 p.m. I didn’t want my grandma knowing I was
smoking (long story), so I thought nothing of it to walk right down the
street. I got to the gazebo just fine and smoked smoothly.
Unfortunately, my tolerance break worked TOO well, and I was high as
fuck.

I’ve always been naive about personal safety. I rarely go out unless I’m
with someone, going to work, or running errands, and I never carry
anything for protection. I’ve been lucky enough to never experience
anything too weird until now.

Around 10 p.m., I was walking home when a white van approached from the
opposite direction. It briefly flashed its lights, slowed down, and
stopped right next to me. The windows were heavily tinted, so I couldn’t
see inside. I froze. I was already high and anxious, and I genuinely
thought someone was about to jump out with a gun. I wanted to cry
because, in that moment, I thought I was going to die. At the same time,
I kept wondering if I was just too high and imagining things.

The van eventually drove off, and I immediately started running and kept
looking back. I’m 5’8”, around 135 pounds, and slim with a little
muscle, long legs and can run pretty fast but I was wearing oversized
sweatpants and a huge hoodie that probably made it harder to tell my
size.

Then I saw the van turn around.

My street has a sharp curve, so as i kept running, I couldn’t see
straight down the road anymore. I noticed headlights approaching, but
when the car came around the curve, it was a different vehicle. At the
time, I felt relieved because I thought having another car around meant
I had a witness, and my house was only three houses away. I kept looking
back, didn’t see the van following me, and made it safely inside.

Now that I’m home and thinking about it, I’m wondering if that second
car was involved, because i didn’t see it coming before i cut the
corner. It slowed down as well, although not nearly as much as the van,
and continued moving at a steady pace. I honestly don’t know if I’m
being paranoid because I was so high and scared that I might not have
noticed everything around me. I also wasn’t paying attention to the cars
parked in the cul-de-sac i passed because I was so focused on the van.

I never imagined something like this happening in my own middle-class
neighborhood, where a lot of older people live. I also don’t know why I
didn’t bring the pepper spray I literally own. I don’t know how much it
would’ve helped, but I clearly wasn’t thinking anything through.

I can’t sleep now because I’m worried the second car saw which house I
went into. I feel watched, my anxiety won’t go down, and I have work in
three hours.

I’ve learned my lesson and will never do this again, i’m a dumbass and
this situation forced me to stop being oblivious now i don’t want to go
outside my own house anymore or anywhere alone and i will be buying a
gun and will always keep pepper spray on me from now on.

Maybe I was overthinking that second car because I was high, but that
white van definitely didn’t have good intentions and i don’t know how
i’m still here to tell this story now.
