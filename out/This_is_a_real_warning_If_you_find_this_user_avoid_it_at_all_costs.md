This will probably make me look insane, but I need to warn you of something very serious. This seemed like the best way to get the word out.



Every night before sleeping, I scroll on my for you page. I turn all of my lights off and open my windows to signal to my body that it’s time to quiet down. A video popped up on my feed of a man wearing a very weird looking outfit. His face was covered by a black cloth with eyeholes cut out of it and he had black oil or coal on his eyelids. The videos at first seemed to just be a random guy who has really terrible humor, who likes moving in unsettling ways and being weird all around. The posts were all labeled as “relatable” masked man, but they are not relatable at all. They just looked messed up. 



I scoffed at my screen and scrolled on. My night went on as it normally does. I watched videos until I couldn’t keep my eyes open anymore, and then fell asleep with my phone in my hand. I woke up, showered and went about my day as I always did. I drove to work, punched in and began stocking the shelves. My break time slowly rolled around and I was able to sit down and have some lunch while watching social media again. For a little bit, I scrolled through videos and it was completely normal, but I got another video from that same weird guy on my FYP. He was infront of a webcam this time on a video, playing a game. He had a memorable voice. It was higher but smooth in a way that was normal but unnatural at the same time. It was more annoying than creepy, so I still just brushed it off. I scrolled past the video without clicking the like button, but to be honest I didn’t click the block button either like I should have. I swiped through a few normal videos, but the app brought me another video from the same person. I grunted and scrolled again. There was another one. I squeezed my jaw because of how annoyed I got. Most people who I find annoying didn’t give me the same weird feeling that he gave me. It was more than annoying, it was unsettling. There was something in his eyes that normal people don’t have. I don’t know how to explain it. 



Twenty minutes passed and my break was over. I shut off my phone and put it in my pocket. For the rest of the day, I couldn’t stop thinking about him, which is really out of the ordinary because things don’t usually bother me like that. I got home and after I had my dinner, I got into my bed, I pulled up my covers and I started having my nightly scroll. I didn’t realize until about an hour later, but I didn’t see him on my for you page. This should have been a relief, but it made my stomach turn instead. I don’t know what in the world made me think to do this, maybe it was curiosity, but I went into the search and looked him up. It wasn’t difficult to find. I went through his profile and only saw videos with the same bad humor, but the more I looked the more normal it felt. This did put me at ease a little bit and I was able to easily get to sleep. 



This is when it really started. I had a dream. I don’t even know if I should call it a dream or if I should call it a nightmare. The guy from the videos just stood there at the side of my bed, staring. Its eyes were wide like it was trying to take in all of my details. It was examining me quietly as a shadowy form. The nightmare seemed to go on forever while its inky black shadow hovered and leaned over me. My eyes felt like they were having holes drilled right through them from how hard the eyecontact was. My body felt hot with adrenaline, but I wasn’t able to move away. It didn’t blink. It just very slowly breathed in and out. 



The next night, I felt drawn to it again. This time I opened the comments and read some of them. The more I looked, the more obvious it became that it wasn’t just me. Many of them were about the videos, but there was also a lot of comments about how more people have had dreams with him in them. Some of them were even concerned in the same way I was, and there was even a video joking about dreams like it was taunting me. I guess it could have been what he wears or the power of suggestion, but isn’t it weird that so many people keep having dreams about the same thing? I powered down my phone and laid there silently in the dark. I started having the feeling that someone was watching me so I closed my bedroom window. I didn’t want to watch anything else. I didn’t want to sleep. I was stunned and confused. 



My eyelids started to get heavy and I closed them for a minute but I realized that I needed to use the bathroom, so I got up and made my way through the hallway. I did my business but when I turned around to leave the bathroom, a shiver went up my spine. The air felt colder than normal and goosebumps popped all over my skin. I thought that maybe a draft had blown through my window, but I remembered that I closed it just a minute ago. My subconscious brought me to start turning around to look behind me, but something in my mind screamed at me **DO NOT TURN AROUND**. I trusted my gut and quickly brought my head forward again. I took one step forward with my hands clenched closed. A weak breath brushed against the back of my ear. Something was behind me. My body flooded with fear and I ran as fast as I could to the other side of the house where I kept a metal bat. A loud slam was made when I pulled the coat closet’s door open with extreme force, and I quickly reached around the corner to find the bat while I kept my eyes locked on the opposite end of the dark hall. My hand grasped onto the cold metal and I pulled. It did not move. I thought that something must have been leaning on it, so I quickly looked into the closet and pulled with my other hand as well with still no success. Nothing was leaning on it and no object was weighing it down. I glanced behind me quickly and then back in the closet. 



The darkness of the closet wasn’t possible to see past. A creepy, silent moment passed. Two inhuman eyes appeared from the shadows. My blood froze the moment they met my own, and I stopped breathing. Its pupils were huge, like it was a shark hunting its prey. The metal bat loudly fell on the floor as its hands let go of it and I screamed louder than I ever screamed before. I was suddenly sitting up in bed, panting heavy in a cold sweat. My eyes quickly looked around my room. It was a nightmare. I didn’t even know that I fell asleep. I very quickly grabbed my phone off of my bedside cabinet and shot a text over to my best friend. He was the kind of person that you could turn to for anything. I never met anybody else in my whole life who was that friendly to people. Someone like that is very rare to find these days, but I’m very grateful that I met him. 



I texted him “Do you know this guy?” and sent a link to the social media page. Thankfully he responded fast and I wasn’t left waiting. I stared at the three dots and they bounced on the bottom of my screen while he typed. 



“Yea he’s on my FYP constantly. He’s so funny LOL.” That was the reply I got from him. After he sent it, he quickly moved on and started talking about something else like it was nothing out of the ordinary. I had to mention it again. 



I typed it up but was hesitating. I knew it was a weird question. “Have you had any dreams about him?”



“How did you know that? Yea I did.” Fear clouded my mind when I read the words on my screen. 



“This is the craziest thing I’ve ever seen. I’m having them too like really bad, and I saw so many comments where people were having them too. What happened in yours?” I sent. My body was shaking. I didn’t get a response for two hours after that message and I was getting worried that I pushed it too far. It was weird for him to not text back fast. My phone eventually buzzed.



He sent another message. “I had a dream where he was doing something in my room, but it was blurry and I don’t remember anything else. Are you ok?” My eyes filled up with tears because I realized that he probably thought I was crazy. 



I replied, “Please. Don’t you believe me?” My friend typed again for a few seconds and a new message popped up.



“I do believe you but I think that you might be getting yourself freaked out over something that isn’t happening. I want to say this more kindly, but I don’t know how.” Another message came on the screen. “Your feelings are real, but I have my doubts about this guy. He’s just a cosplayer or something and he can’t actually do anything like that.”



My blood went to my feet and I sat quietly looking at my phone. I didn’t respond. I just clicked the button on the side of my phone and the screen went black. It was still dark out and the house was getting cooler inside since a breeze was coming through my window. I thought I closed it, but maybe I didn’t. I felt like I was going insane. I didn’t know if I was still dreaming but I knew that it was a real possibility. 



I fell back into sleep and didn’t have any dreams at all. It was a shock that I woke up feeling rested and for the next few days, there were no disturbances. I didn’t see him on my for you page anymore after the last bad dream I had. It was suspicious but hey, I wasn’t complaining. By the fourth quiet day, I was able to forget that it happened and I almost even returned to my normal, undisturbed life. 



The peace that I felt quickly disappeared without any reason. My mind wandered back to those eyes I saw and anxiety started to burst inside of me. I thought that I was past this. My eyes started to look around my room. It wasn’t dark out but the sun was starting to set, and the sky was becoming dark gray.



After a few minutes I noticed that there was a tapping sound coming from my closet door. My blood ran cold and I stood up with my eyes focused on the door. My mind went back to the coat closet in the hallway from the nightmare. The eyes felt like they were peering at me from inside of my own brain. I shivered from the idea. I was slowly walking closer to the closet. My fingers grabbed hold of the handle and I pulled the door open while looking in. I was finally able to breathe when I saw that the closet was empty other than my clothes. My shoulders relaxed, and I turned around to walk to the kitchen and go get a snack since I was hungry. I knew it was just my mind playing pranks on me.



I entered the kitchen and when I pulled the refrigerator door open, the light inside turned on and slightly lit up the room. The white light caught something outside of the window. I saw it out the corner of my eye and looked up quickly. There was nothing there except for shrubbery. I thought that I was probably just tired, so I ignored it. I put two pieces of bread on a napkin and started making a ham, cheese and veggie sandwich. 



**“Hello.”** 



My entire body froze. The voice was familiar. I knew exactly where I recognized it from. It was that thing again. My stomach flipped like if I was on a roller coaster and my eyes shot to the other side of the kitchen. He was standing there staring at me, just like he did in the first dream. Examining. Expressionless. His neck twitched quickly in response to my eyecontact and his eyelids blinked very quickly before focusing on mine again. Its movements were terrifying and wrong, and a weird gurgle sound came from under its mask. It sounded like it would have been painful. 



I was angry and screamed “WHAT DO YOU WANT FROM ME?” One of its eyes squinted slightly because I was loud. “WHY ARE YOU DOING THIS? WHAT DID I DO TO DESERVE THIS?” It didn’t budge but I could hear the sound of it opening its wet mouth behind the cloth. 



It didn’t say much, except for the simple phrase “You know.” I didn’t know. It slowly pointed through the window to my neighbors yard. I was terrified to look away from its eyes, but I did it anyway and followed the line of its finger. My sight landed on the pile of burnt wood and ash in the yard. 



I turned back to face him. “That? I was going to light a firework! It was just a prank! I didn’t mean to light the shed!” Its eyes widened more while it stared at me. It still didn’t blink and its breathing got louder. 



“What about them?” It angrily moved its hand and pointed harder. I knew he was talking about the neighbor’s pets. They had three cats staying in that shed when i accidentally burned it down. 



I screamed “ARE YOU SERIOUS?” I waved my hands around. I was furious through and through, but I was also scared for my life. “I TOLD THEM IM SORRY!” The thing bolted at me and I quickly started to run away from it. “STOP!” I screamed at it again while I ran as fast as I could to the door. I tripped and banged my head on the floor. My skull ached. I flipped myself over on to my back and put my arms up to protect myself. Its hands grabbed my wrists tight while my head pounded. I blinked my eyes hard a few times but my vision was still blurry from the hit.  It cut circulation from my arms with its squeeze as it leaned very close to me. Its face was almost touching mine. An animal like scream boomed from the things lungs and rattled in my eardrums. The screech was piercing and was the loudest sound I ever heard in my life. My eyes squeezed shut while I cried but I wasn’t able to plug my ears. My whole body tensed up and suddenly, the sound was gone. The pressure on my wrists was gone too. I opened my eyes and sat with extreme speed. My chest raised and fell while each terrified breath was sucked in. 



It disappeared just as fast as it came. Because of my head injury, I was unable to stand so I crawled to my phone and dialed 911. I was brought to the hospital and it turns out that the hit on my head gave me a mild concussion, but I am ok. My ears are ringing and I don’t know if it’s from the concussion or the scream. The hair on my arms is standing up while I’m writing this and I know that it is still here. I can feel him around me and in my brain. I keep seeing shadows out the corner of my eye. My brother is staying over with me for the night but I know I will not sleep. That thing is not human. It might look and act like a normal person on social media but if you listen to anything please listen to this now. Be careful because this can happen to you too. Don’t be fooled.
