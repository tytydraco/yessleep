The first sign that something was wrong with my girlfriend was when she screamed my name in the woods, then looked me in the eyes and told me she hadn't.

"Come on, Sam, get your shoes on," my girlfriend, Grace, said.

"I will, but how about we first?" 

I winked at her and grabbed her hips.

"It's already late. I still want to see some of the trail."

"Okay, let's get you on that trail!"

We locked the motel door and got into our car. The sun set between a few storm clouds. 

Elk still strolled on the grasslands at the park entrance. Behind them in the mountains, something dark orange glowed.

"The brown mountain lights." 

"I thought it was a campfire."

"Maybe, but the old legends speak of wandering demons. Ones that are stuck to the mountains," Grace whispered.

"Sure, honey."

The trailhead parking lot was empty. We shivered as we got out of the car.

"Short hike?"

"Yeah."

The muddy trail wound through the thicket. The bugs buzzed around our flashlights. The smell of the approaching storm was in the air.

"Honey, look!" 

The orange glowing light was so close to us. It seemed close enough to touch. Then it disappeared as quickly as it came.

"That was definitely a ghost. People say they can bind you to the thicket."

"Or a creep starting a campfire," I said, tickling her.

She squeaked.

"Jeez, Grace, now he'll know for sure we're here."

"Stop, Sam! Now I'm gonna be scared," she said, laughing. 

She had the prettiest laugh I had ever heard.

"I feel like a storm is coming. Maybe we turn around soon?"

"Okay, I'll just go pee quickly."

"Beware of the creeps."

She laughed and walked into the thicket.

I checked my phone. No signal. I looked around the pretty thicket, taking in its leaves, vines, and…

"SAAAM!" Grace screamed.

Shock jolted through my body.

"Grace?!" 

I turned and ran toward her. She wasn't screaming anymore. I flashed my light everywhere, trying to find her.

"Grace?!"

I pushed a few vines out of my face. She sat on the ground next to a tree, her flashlight off. She turned toward me as I shone the light on her.

"Jesus, are you okay?"

"Yes, I am."

"What happened? Why did you scream?"

"I didn't scream."

Her face was blank.

"But I heard you call my name."

"I didn't."

"Are you hurt?"

"I'm okay."

Two small dots were on her hand.

"Did something attack you?"

"No. I'm fine."

She stood up.

"Okay."

On the way back, we barely talked. Grace just kept walking behind me, her face still. As we drove out of the parking lot, it started raining.

"It seems like a bad one."

"It will be a bad one."

"Can you see into the future, lady?"

"No. I just know."

"Seems like future-reading to me."

She didn't answer. 

A few minutes later, the rain began beating down. I focused on the road. Grace didn't speak the whole rest of the ride. 

When we arrived at the motel, I pulled the sweater over my head and ran in quickly. Grace walked slowly from the car.

"Don't mind the rain?"

"No."

I tried the light switch, but nothing happened.

"The power's probably out."

"Yes."

Grace took off her shoes, climbed onto the bed, and sat still, looking at the wall.

"You sure you're okay?"

"I'm just not sleepy."

"You wanna talk?"

"No, I'm okay."

"Alright. I'll head off to bed. No point in staying up without wifi, signal, or a TV."

"Sure."

I took my clothes off and got under the covers. I could hear the water dripping from her. I wanted to talk to her more, but didn't want to start another fight before bed. I just closed my eyes and hoped to fall asleep.

A loud sound woke me up. Lightning flashed outside our window. The storm was raging. I rubbed my eyes and looked to my right. Grace sat there, still awake, naked, staring at me.

"What's up?"

"The clothes were wet."

I looked closer at her. The two dots on her hand were now large and dark; a few vein-like lines spread across her arm.

"Do you want to sleep with me?"

"What?"

"You wanted to sleep with me. Now we can."

"I don't know, Grace."

"It's the last time before we have to go."

"Go where?"

Another bolt of lightning hit nearby. Grace perked up, ears like a cat's.

"Soon, Sam."

She came closer. I got off the bed.

"What's wrong, Grace?"

She climbed over the bed. I quickly put my clothes on and walked to the door.

"I don't like this, Grace."

She got off the bed and walked toward me, her eyes glued to mine.

"Last chance to sleep with me, Sam."

I opened the door and ran out. The rain drenched me instantly. It beat heavily against the parking lot. Grace came out of the room and walked slowly toward me. I could feel my heartbeat in my ears. My hand fished for keys in my pocket. 

Another flash of lightning. Grace's eyes flew open. She turned her head. A small orange light shone in the thicket.

"Too late, Sam. We have to go."

She bolted toward me. I held the keys, but she grabbed my hand so hard that I dropped them.

"Grace, stop!"

"We have to go now, Sam!"

She pulled me across the parking lot to the glowing thicket. Her jaw was clenched. She put all her force into every pull. I tried to push her hand away, pull her fingers, but nothing worked. The rain beat down hard. We were on the road now. One of her legs reached the thicket.

"Grace, please stop!"

She didn't answer. Half of her body was in the thicket. I almost crossed into the thicket too, but then another lightning bolt came. She stopped, turned toward the light, let go of me, and ran into the thicket. I stood in the road panting. Only the honking of a passing car brought me back to my senses. The rain had completely stopped. From somewhere in the thicket, I heard Grace's beautiful [laugh.](https://www.reddit.com/r/KanesHorrors/)
