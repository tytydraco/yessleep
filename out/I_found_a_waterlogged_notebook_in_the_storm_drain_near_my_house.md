I got something spooky to share. Yesterday I was coming a bike ride when I spied a small notebook under one of the storm drains leading into a pond. I fished it out and gave it a read, and unfortunately the last entry was the only part that was still readable:



*I’m writing this before I go. Rylee and Paul are dead. I am positive now that whatever it was inside that closed off mine in Montana is actively altering Russel and Amanda.* 

*From what I could observe from my hiding spot in the closet, Russel had grown a small but notable tail, and what seem to be wings that had not fully formed judging from their awkward appearance. The way that Amanda crushed and ate Rylee’s skull suggested her jaw had been strengthened and elongated in order to consume larger prey. Her teeth were not only sharper, but reinforced with a second row behind the first. The weak spot I’d previously discovered on Russel’s flank was now  covered with more scales, rendering attacks with knives or my previously useful garden hoe ineffective as a defense. In fact, given the hardness of the scales, my current hypothesis is that Russel and Amanda are now resistant to firearms, at least at low calibers. Using my rifle to put them down would be dangerous, their augmented senses can track movement and sounds. And even if I manage to kill one, the other is never too far away.* 

*Given their very close nature in hunting as well as standing idle, there is still some sense of connection between Russel and Amanda. Whether it is remnants of the love they had before they became what they are now, or some primitive sense of mating, is unclear to me. I must assume that these creatures are not unintelligent however. Paul made the mistake of thinking that they could not operate doors. And when she had her fill of Rylee, Amanda called my name. It was no mindless growl, but a fully formed word. I could not tell if she was calling for help or trying to trick me into showing myself. It may have been both.* 

*I’ve collected myself, and I don’t see anyone believing me if I told them what happened here. The bodies are gone, dragged away to somewhere hidden. They'll finish their feeding, and then they'll be on the move. They'll be going back to the mine, that much I'm sure of. The only thing I can do is follow them there, and put them out of their misery. Doing so will almost assuredly cost me my live, but I can't let any of this continue. I'll make some protection for myself as well as basic ordinance from the materials around me, and then I'll be off. I’m scared to death, I may not be thinking straight, and I am terribly out of my element, but I’m going. God help me.*

  
It ends there. I couldn't tell you what this person was talking about. There are no abandoned mines in the area, and upon doing a quick internet search, I didn't find anything matching the events described here. I put the notebook in my mystery box with the other things I find in the area that don't have an explanation to their appearance. Maybe I'll show it off to people who visit me or make a ghost story out of it.
