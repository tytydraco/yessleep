I’m from Russia, and I want to share something strange that happened at my grandmother’s dacha. To this day, I still can’t explain what happened that night.  
It happened at around 11:50 PM. My brother, my dad, my grandfather, my grandmother, and I were all inside the house.  
We have two small dogs. They’re usually very loud and bark at almost anything that enters the property. Even if a cat wanders into the yard, they’ll start barking. That’s why what happened that night was so unusual.  
I was sitting in the kitchen, drinking tea with my grandparents, while my dad was in the living room. The kitchen and living room are practically connected, so we could easily hear anything happening near the entrance.  
A couple of minutes before everything started, both dogs ran into the living room and hid under the couch.  
They stayed completely silent.  
That was unusual. Normally, they would bark immediately if they heard or noticed an animal outside. But this time, they just hid under the couch, as if they were afraid of something.  
At around 11:50 PM, we heard something scratching at the entrance door.  
It wasn’t a quiet scratching sound. Whatever was outside was scratching hard enough to make the door shake slightly.  
And there was something else that made the situation even stranger.  
The entire perimeter of our house is equipped with motion sensors that are supposed to turn on the lights whenever something moves nearby. But not a single light came on.  
We live in a region of Russia where large predators, such as wolves, aren’t normally found around residential properties. Of course, some ordinary animal could have been outside, but the situation didn’t seem to make much sense.  
My dad grabbed a firearm and went toward the entrance to check what was happening.  
That’s when I noticed my grandmother.  
Her facial expression suddenly changed. She looked genuinely frightened and immediately told my dad not to open the door.  
He didn’t listen.  
My grandmother stood up so abruptly that she actually tried to stop him physically, but he opened the door anyway.  
There was nobody outside.  
My dad looked straight ahead, but we didn’t search the entire property. So I can’t say that there was absolutely nothing out there. All I know is that nobody was standing directly outside the door when he opened it.  
Afterward, my grandmother said that my dad had let an evil spirit into the house. She then spent a long time praying.  
I don’t know why she reacted that way. Maybe she had her own beliefs or reasons for being so afraid. But the look on her face and the way she desperately tried to stop my dad are things I still remember clearly.  
The next morning, I decided to inspect the door.  
There were three scratches on the outside of it, approximately 60 centimeters above the ground.  
They looked like claw marks to me. I found it difficult to believe that an ordinary cat could have made them, although I can’t say for certain what caused them.  
We also found no tracks or other obvious signs on the ground near the entrance.  
No paw prints. Nothing that could clearly explain what had approached the door during the night.  
I still don’t have an explanation for what happened.  
I’m back home now, and while writing this story, I’ve noticed occasional cracking noises and other small sounds around me. I understand that these noises can have completely ordinary explanations, but after that night, I can’t help paying attention to them.  
I’ve even visited both a psychologist and a psychiatrist and undergone medical examinations. As far as I know, the results showed no health problems.  
I’m not claiming that something supernatural happened at my grandmother’s dacha. I’m simply trying to understand how all these things could have happened together: the dogs hiding in silence, the scratching, the shaking door, the motion sensors failing to activate, my grandmother’s reaction, and the three scratches we discovered the next morning.  
If anyone has a reasonable explanation, I’d genuinely like to hear it.
