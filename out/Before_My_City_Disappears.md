In previous years, my city had a thriving underground artistic scene. The folk music of the Mountains, here in our crooked towers had been transformed into something hectic, rhythmically complex, and filled with an energy that once it sinks its teeth in, is inescapable. The landscape itself, together with the old alphabet, and the visual motifs of ruins and cave walls, came to define our paintings, murals, graffiti, and almost psychedelic tapestries. The old woven patterns are still in our clothes. The elevation in our blood. All that's changing now, of course.

The first sign I noticed of the change to come, was when they got Wolf. We don't use the names of animals for ourselves, by the way, but I find a certain vague ambiguity necessary here. The trouble with Wolf, is he never did anything so bad. He never killed anyone. No kidnappings. Nothing that would normally get that kind of attention. He sold drugs, in a hill neighborhood of the sort the police would never... police, and that was it. So it was strange, when they picked him right up off the street, and off they drove with Wolf in the backseat.

Full disclosure, the entire extent of my involvement with criminal organizations, is I sing and play the guitar. Around here, you understand, a level of underworld cooperation is in many things expected, and necessary. And it was from this vantage, playing gigs in ghettos (we have a different word for it), that I saw many soon get taken just like Wolf.

Some made perfect sense. Mob bosses. Sex criminals. People who fought in the local petty "wars". But many more like Wolf. And some, like my close friend, Lamb, and my ex, Frog, who I never even knew had done anything wrong.

There were murmurings by then, on certain cracked cobblestone corners, of a crackdown. Crackdown is the word they used. Of course the presidential candidate had talked at length of a crackdown. But isn't that what politicians do? Talk big until they get elected, and then take their paycheck from who-knows-who and nothing happens? Surely should something actually happen, an assassination is in order, no?

The streets were mostly empty, the city mostly already gone, the day they got me.

What did I do? I asked the cop who pulled up next to me on the sidewalk outside the underground train station.

You were implicated, he said.

That's all the reason I got. You were implicated. And he drove me down a winding, terrifying mountain road, to the lower elevations where they had a complex of sorts, and I never should have accepted the bottled water I was offered, because it knocked me right out.

In my dream I was with Wolf, and Lamb, and Frog. The three of us were playing a show together, to a cheering, dancing crowd who all had tears in their eyes and slits in their necks.

When I woke up, I was back in my own flat.

Disoriented, (who wouldn't be?) I shot up and ran outside, and fallen leaves littered the streets.

I could not find anyone, and I ran and ran more, first on the sidewalks and then where the cars should be, to find anyone I could.

At last, I found a stranger.

The stranger recognized me, must have seen one of my shows at some point.

She said, Goat? They took you. You were implicated.

What does that mean? I asked. Why am I here again?

The stranger shrugged. She said, It must have been a mistake.

Then she nodded to herself, and swallowed, and continued on her way, while I stayed lost.

Before my city disappears, I want to understand, what happened to all the things we knew? Why did we, as a city, as a nation, as a world, make any of the choices that led us here? Did we even make them?

I walked, then, down into a train station. All of the signs were in English.
