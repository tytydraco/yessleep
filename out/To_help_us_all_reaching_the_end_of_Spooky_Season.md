So because we are unfortunately reaching the end of Spooky Season, I wish to relate a tale of terror that happened to me.

In the long ago times, I used to drive tractor trailer for a world wide salty food snack company. I delivered to the guys who delivered to the stores. Now it was a union gig, so we bid on runs, so it meant I normally was in NYC a few days a week, and Maine for the weekend. But one snowy weekend, I instead had to go to Vermont.

Because vacations happen, and so does near blizzards, I caught a run through the mountains of Vermont. Because we truckers know the weather, no one wanted it, so, yup, I caught it. Went to the shop to get the paperwork, and lucky for me there were a few guys that had been on the run before, and they gave me the rundown. They gave me great directions and told me how to do each stop, you know, because each building we deliver to is different.

So the first stop is easy to find, they says. Huge building, the size of a small aircraft hanger, right off the road. You can't miss it because it has a lit sign from when part of the building used to be a crematorium. It's not one any more, they says, but the sign is still lit up, and you can find it nice and easy.

And what really makes this stop awesome is because this building is massive, you back the truck right into the building! And with how hard the snow was already coming down in CT, I knew it had to be awful in VT, so I was liking that part! They filled me in on the rest of the stops and off I went!

Sure as sure I found the stop easy! Got into the building. Opened the rollup door, and backed the truck in, just like they said. Opened the back of the truck, put up the ramp, opened all of the individual areas for each guy I was delivering to, grabbed my hand truck, and off I went!

It really wasn't bad. Scoop up a giant stack of chips, turn around, down the ramp, into each area, drop the stack and repeat. It's really brainless work. Repeat until the truck is empty. Until about 20 minutes later.

Walking back to the truck I heard a thunk thunk. Blew it off, but I remember I heard it. About five minutes later, another thunk. Made me slow a step, but Mr. Rational Me thought 'Well, Me, wet snow is coming down like heck, so it's probably just snow falling off the roof.' And so I went back to work.

And so soon after another thunk. And a thunk. And a THUNK. Now that little child part of my brain is remembering that this place used to have a crematorium in it. And it's 3AM on a snowy Saturday morning, and no one should be here but me. And now I have to find what this noise is.

So I set aside the hand truck and go searching. Where I think the sound is coming from is about fifty feet away from the truck, off to the side and I walk that way. I see a large opening, easily ten by ten, and it has a board going across it waist high. And behind that board is...

Stygian darkness. Blacker than black. This is bottom of a coal mine black. And mind you, this is before cell phone flashlights, and the lights from the area I am working are not entering this blackness at all. So I slowly walk up to the board, because I'm a tough old veteran, and natch, I ain't scared.

So there I am. Building that used to be a crematorium. 3AM. Vermont. Blizzard. And I put my hands on that board. And I lean in, eyes straining to see anything in the inky black.

And the horse that was being boarded there, leaned into me and 'SNUFFED' right in my left ear.

I screamed like a child.
