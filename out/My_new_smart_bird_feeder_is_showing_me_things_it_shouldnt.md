I’m a pretty low-tech guy honestly. I like my flip phone and I usually just read physical books. But my girlfriend Jessica is obsessed with smart home gadgets. For my birthday last month she got me one of those fancy smart bird feeders. You know the kind. It connects to an app on your phone and sends you notifications when a bird lands on it. It even identifies the species. She thought it would be a nice way to get me to appreciate nature more. I live in a pretty quiet suburban area so we get a good variety of birds. Robins cardinals sparrows finches. The usual suspects.

  
At first it was kind of fun. I got a notification while I was at work and saw a beautiful blue jay perched on the feeder. The camera quality was surprisingly good. I showed it to Jessica and she was thrilled. We even started keeping a running tally of all the different birds we’d seen. It became a little game for us. I’d check the app throughout the day and send her screenshots.

  
Then the weird stuff started. It was about a week ago. I was at home watching TV. My phone buzzed. Bird feeder notification. I opened the app expecting to see a chickadee or something. Instead the image on my phone was just static. Like an old TV screen not tuned to a channel. I figured it was a glitch. I closed the app and reopened it but it was still static. I even tried restarting my phone. Nothing.

  
I went outside to check the feeder itself. It looked fine. The light was on and everything seemed to be working. I tapped it a few times. No change on the app. I shrugged it off. Tech glitches happen. I went back inside.

  
The next day the static was gone. The feeder was back to showing me birds. I saw a vibrant red cardinal. Everything seemed normal. Until that night. I got another notification. Opened the app. And this time it wasn’t static. It was a blurry image of my living room. From an angle high up near the ceiling. Like the camera had detached and floated into my house.

  
My heart pounded. I dropped my phone. That couldn’t be right. The bird feeder is outside. It’s fixed to a pole. It can’t just fly into my house. I picked up my phone again. The image flickered. It was definitely my living room. I could see my couch my coffee table. The TV was off. No one was there. Just the empty room. Then the image changed. It was a rapid sequence of blurry frames. Like a time lapse. But too fast to make out details.

  
I ran outside. The bird feeder was still on its pole. Right where it should be. The light was still on. I yanked it off the pole. Checked it over. There were no signs of tampering. No extra cameras. It was just the bird feeder. I took it inside. Unplugged it. Left it on the kitchen counter.

  
I didn’t get any more notifications for a few days. I thought maybe I’d broken it. Or maybe it was just a bizarre malfunction that fixed itself when I disconnected it. I told Jessica about it but she just laughed. Said I was probably tired and imagining things. She’s a science person so she doesn’t believe in anything supernatural.

  
Then yesterday the feeder buzzed to life on the counter. I hadn’t plugged it in. I hadn’t touched it. The little light came on. My phone got a notification. I picked it up with shaking hands. The app was open. The image on the screen was a close up of my own face. I was asleep. My mouth was slightly open. The angle was from above me. Near the ceiling again. Just like the living room shot.

  
I stared at it. Frozen. My own sleeping face. It was undeniably me. Then the image zoomed out slowly. And I saw it. Hovering above my bed. A dark shape. Indistinct. But definitely there. It was like a shadow. But with depth. A deeper black than anything in the room.

  
I screamed. Dropped the phone again. Jessica ran in from the living room. Asking what was wrong. I pointed at the phone. The image was back to showing a common sparrow on a branch outside. As if nothing had happened.

  
She looked at me like I was losing my mind. I couldn't explain it. She picked up the feeder. It was off again. No light. Nothing. She looked at the app history. All normal. Just birds. She told me I needed to get more sleep. That maybe I was dreaming.

  
But I wasn’t dreaming. I know what I saw. I haven’t slept since. Every time I close my eyes I see that dark shape hovering above my bed. I keep thinking about the static. Was it just a placeholder? A cover up? And those blurry fast images. What were they trying to show me?

  
I still have the feeder. It’s unplugged now. Stuffed in a drawer. But every now and then I hear a faint buzzing sound from the drawer. And I swear I can feel something watching me. Even when I’m alone.
