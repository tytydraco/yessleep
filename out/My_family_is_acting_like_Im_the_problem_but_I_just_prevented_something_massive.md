I’m posting this here because every time I try to talk to my mom or the doctors, they look at me like I’ve lost my mind. My brother Finn won't even come back to the apartment. He’s staying at a hotel with our dad. They are trying to make me out to be some kind of violent lunatic, but I know what I saw, and I know what was about to happen.

I work late nights doing data entry at a warehouse, so my sleep schedule is pretty messed up. A few weeks ago, I started noticing that things on my morning drive felt weirdly deliberate. Like, I’d see three red cars in a row at the same turn every night. Then the talk radio show I always listen to, hosted by Milo, started changing. He wasn't saying my name or anything, but he kept talking about "the younger brother" and "the upcoming fire." He played three songs in one hour that all had the word 'smoke' or 'burn' in them.

When I got home, the news rerun was on. The anchor shifted her papers and stared straight at the camera while reading a clip about an arson case. It felt completely coordinated. Like the media couldn't say it out loud because Finn was in the room, but they were trying to tip me off.

I didn't panic. I just tried to be smart. I hid the matches in the apartment. I pulled the plastic knobs off the stove. Finn noticed, obviously, and he started acting really defensive. He kept hiding his pockets from me and looking at me with this fake confusion whenever I asked what he was doing.

Yesterday morning, I walked into the kitchen and he was standing by the counter with a metal knife, staring into the toaster. Smoke was literally coming out of it.

The radio on the counter wasn't even plugged in, but I could hear Milo’s voice through the static in the walls telling me that this was it, that he was starting it.

I did what anyone would do. I tackled him to get the knife away. We fell hard against the cabinets, and I held him down by his neck so he couldn't break free and grab a lighter or something. He was crying and screaming that he was just trying to get a piece of stuck toast out, but I knew the act. I knew what I’d been warned about for weeks.

Our landlord heard the noise and came in, and he managed to pull me off him. Finn was bleeding from the back of his head where we hit the cabinets, so the landlord took him to the clinic.

Nobody called the police because my mom is terrified of me getting a record, but now everyone is treating me like a monster. Dad took Finn to a hotel, and my mom keeps calling me crying, asking if I'm taking my old prescriptions and telling me the radio was just playing a jazz automation playlist. They think if they lie to me enough, I'll just forget what the broadcasts said. They don't understand that if I hadn't jumped him at that exact second, the whole building would have gone up.
