Hello everyone, I’m a caretaker at a local zoo. My name is Chris… Yeah, let’s just call it that. I have a concerning warning to anyone who would want to listen.

I hope this warning goes out in time… I don’t think I’ll have much time anyways…

It’s about the place I work at. I told you I was a caretaker at a local zoo, but over the last few weeks… the last days, this zoo, it changed.

The animals. They look like people.

I know it sounds stupid, but please. For my sanity and yours, if you ever visit a zoo and you see an animal acting strangely, just run. Don’t stick around.

Don’t do what I did.

I’ll rewind the clock a few weeks, and I’ll start from the beginning.

It all started innocently. One day, we noticed the lions were refusing to eat. That’s usually a bad sign in a zoo, but not unheard of.

It could be stress, it could be a rejection of the diet, we weren’t sure at the time. We changed their diets on and off for that week to try to get them to eat anything, but they simply refused.

Beef, horse, pig, rabbits. They wouldn’t touch anything. Then one day, we noticed the lions started intently looking at us. More specifically, my colleague.

He was eating a burrito, wrapped in tin foil. It was his lunch.

My colleague, losing to his intrusive thoughts, threw the rest of his lunch to the lions.

It was immediately picked up and eaten by one of them, and we were left there dumbfounded. Needless to say, the lions’ diet has changed substantially in the last few weeks. They eat a diverse mix of foods now.

Mexican food, seared steaks, pasta. Even vegetables. This is not good for them, but they want to eat food that’s mainly cooked, and if you give them too much meat, which they’re supposed to have, they reject it. They want what we have.

They want what we eat.

They’re picky, if you can call it that. We closely monitor their health because this is NOT what you’re supposed to give them.

But somehow, they’re fine. We were going to get vets in last week to check on them, but… well…

That was before everything started falling apart.

Things got… weirder after that.

Even though I’m a zookeeper that has a lot of roles on site, I always had a special affinity towards apes.

We have a series of them, macaques, gorillas, chimps, gibbons, etc., etc.

Apes are very intelligent animals and even though they are usually harmless, the staff are not allowed to interact much with them. It could be dangerous for the animals and it could be dangerous for us.

Some weeks ago, there was an incident at the zoo, that we were all pressured into keeping secret.

Two chimps escaped and they harmed one of the staff. They harmed me.

I regularly feed the chimp enclosure and against my best wishes, some new fancy shmancy digital lock keeps the doors closed.

One day, when I was feeding them, I noticed them looking at me.

Chimps are close relatives to humans, but these chimps… they, they’re different. They feel like they’re people. They were looking at me, with expressions that could be described as human.

That was the day of the accident.

I really have no other way to describe it, but the chimps managed to figure out the lock combination by… watching me.

0C 09 0E 04 01, that was the chimp’s code to opening the doors. I don’t know how, but they escaped. But weirdly enough they seemed more interested in attacking me than properly escaping.

As soon as they got out, they spotted me and sprinted towards me.

I… I can’t remember much of my attack... All I know was that I was cut in the arm.

They ambushed me without me even noticing they were there. I managed to get out, obviously, but I still get jitters thinking about it.

I can’t really explain it, I’m not sure if it was the adrenaline or something, but I could have sworn, the chimps, they… were weak.

I don’t know how else to explain it. But a chimp should be able to easily overpower me and kill me. But these were weak. It felt like two weak dudes trying to hit me.

I suppose I should be grateful they’re smart and weak and not the other way around. That might have saved my life. I can see myself dying easily if five or more chimps escaped and attacked me.

There were no lawsuits or compensation for me. It’s not hazard pay; the zoo blamed me for the chimps escaping.

Pieces of shit. It is what it is, I guess.

They still left me a pretty deep, nasty gnash on my arm.

I had to go to the nurse’s office to get it taken care of. Standard procedure. Rabies is unlikely, but apes play around with feces and God knows what else, so it might get infected.

I remember talking to Nurse Maria. She always took a kindness to me.

“Nasty cut.” she told me.

“I’ve had worse.”

I lied. I don’t want to sound weak in front of her.

“You don’t have to act tough in front of me.” she replied.

I was caught immediately.

Makes sense. If a nurse calls something nasty, it definitely is nasty.

“How you holding up?” she continued.

“Me? It’s fine, a bit of pain drugs and I’ll make it.” I respond.

“I meant… your wife.”

I think that one stung more than the cut.

It’s no mystery that nurse Maria has had… a little crush on me. At least that’s what the zoo’s gossipers would want you to know. But I had a wife, so I wasn’t going to act out on anything, I’m not a scumbag.

I had a wife. Not anymore.

She died. Drunk driver accident. But it was my fault, I’m a piece of shit, I was drunk. We had an argument. I didn’t see the truck coming. Her name was Linda, and she died for my mistake.

I don’t drink anymore, not since then. I can barely atone for what I’ve done, but I try.

“Do you drink?”, nurse Maria asks.

“No!”, I replied.

“I meant… for the pain medication. Don’t drink anything when you take these. Okay, Chris?”

I catch myself and I simply nod. I take the pills and I leave the nurse’s office. It feels bad knowing someone has a crush on you, and it becoming abject pity.

Nurse Maria is a beautiful woman, but it’s way too early for me to start thinking about that again. It’s barely been a year. I can’t, I just can’t.

It would feel like I’d be betraying Linda. Her memory.

Plus, at this point she looks at me more like I’m a broken shell of a man. More like a wounded animal than anything else.

I take the pills in my hand and swallow them with a bit of my pride.

Time to get back to the animals.

The next thing that happened involved the public. It’s one of the reasons the zoo had to be temporarily closed.

We have, like most zoos, an aquatic exhibit.

Fish, amphibians, some snakes, we got it.

That’s where the… incident happened. We had to evacuate the public, children included.

It was just a regular day, if you can call it that.

We, the staff, know the animals were all acting weird, but the public didn’t know. We never expected anything like this to happen.

At roughly 5:00 P.M., there was a scream in the water exhibits. Then two, then three. Then a whole host of people started fleeing in unison from the fish part of the exhibit.

They went out to the streets and called us.

We asked what was going on but they just kept telling us there was “something wrong with the fish.”

As soon as we stepped in, we couldn’t believe our eyes.

There’s this movie called “Finding Nemo”? We have an exhibit where we put those species of fish together… One of them is called a Moorish Idol. You probably don’t know what that is. I don’t know what it’s called in the movie. But I… I would never be able to tell what species it is, given what I saw that day.

That… fish. The top of its fin was a finger. Like, a human finger.

Or I guess I should say three fingers. Two fully formed fingers and a half-growing one.

I’ll never forget that day. I still don’t fully understand what I was seeing.

The fingers dragged across the glass. I saw the fingertips twitch and spasm against the surface. It was getting closer to the glass, like it wanted to reach out to us.

Like a greeting.

We closed the zoo down early that day. Had to clear out the fish exhibit and take every fish that showed this… mutation, or disease or whatever it was, out.

We had to euthanize fifteen fish.

It got worse after that.

People started quitting. The zoo already didn’t pay well and we were understaffed. The zoo had to be open for fewer hours. Doctors and vets were sent over to figure out what the heck was going on.

Rhinos had grown territorial in a way I’ve never seen before. They would organize themselves in groups of three or four. They’d section a bit of their living area like it’s a home, and they stay there.

Like little families.

Every animal that’s large was getting smaller. Every small animal was getting larger. Their facial features were slowly changing. Eyes got rounder, skin would get smoother, patches of fur would fall out.

It’s… it’s not right, seeing large animals have such small noses and mouths. It’s unholy.

I didn’t matter. We had close temporarily as the animals got stranger and the visitors were fewer in number.

All that was left, was us, the caretakers. We had to look over the animals.

Yesterday, something happened. Something that made me want to quit.

It’s gone too far, this… this thing. Whatever is happening, it’s too much.

Last night I was tasked with filling up the giraffe’s pen with food.

We might not get many visitors by now, but the animals still have to eat and drink.

I don’t think I’ll ever forget that day.

As I was cleaning, I felt something coming closer behind me. Like the wind shifting.

The building I was in was small. Too small for the giraffes to get in.

But not with their necks. They can go inside with their necks.

I saw it in front of me. An animal. A creature. Something.

It wasn’t a giraffe anymore.

The snout was completely shrunken. The mouth was tiny and the teeth were small enough to match it.

It was smiling. Smiling at me.

God.

Even if I hadn’t sensed it, I would have heard it. It was breathing heavily, similar to someone after running a marathon. It seemed excited to see me.

I…I swear I’m not making this up. It… It talked.

It was a hoarse voice, but I heard it. I swear… I swear to God.

*“Haave… have yooou… been drinking?”*

I recognize that face. I… I ran.

It’s too much. This zoo, these animals, these things. There’s something more going on. Yesterday was going to be my last night. But now… Now I think it’s over.

I’m stuck.

This wasn’t just last night; it was a few hours ago. I’m stuck here. I was about to pack up my stuff and leave when I saw something.

I’ve been stuck inside the zoo since 1:00 A.M.

I’m writing this because I’m not sure if I’ll make it. And there’s nothing else I can do.

Please don’t ever come near this God-forsaken place.

It happened five hours ago, when I was finishing my rounds. That’s when I saw it.

Hands sliding out of the cages. Unceremoniously messing with their locks.

Fingers and limbs twisting knobs and clicking buttons.

Bears shouldn’t have fingers like that. Zebras shouldn’t have hands.

What the hell is happening?

I ran. They all escaped at the same time and I had nowhere else to go. I went up to the office and barricaded the door.

I know they’re coming. I shouldn’t have stayed; I should have left. I’m such a fucking idiot.

They’re coming for me. I can tell they’re out there, they all look the same now.

They all have brown hair like they’re wearing wigs. Their legs… Their arms…

The ones who shouldn’t be able to walk on two legs are walking towards me. The ones who can walk on two legs are running.

These are not animals anymore.

Please. If you ever read this, don’t ever come to this fucking zoo!

I called 911 and they’re not going to reach me in time. These things have some sense of smell or hearing that lets them know where I am. And they’re fast.

I don’t think I’m going to make it.

Jesus Christ, is this really it?

Oh God, why!? Why?! Why do they look like her?!

I’m stuck here. They’re coming to me.

I’m sorry, Linda. I’m sorry.

Oh God. Oh God.

Why do they have her face!?
