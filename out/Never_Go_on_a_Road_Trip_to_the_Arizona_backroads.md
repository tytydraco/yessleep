My five-man friend group and I decided that we wanted to disprove all the stories of skinwalkers.

Kasher, Eli, Josh, Dylan, and I are finally doing this. We are all packing our things and are going to head out soon.

Coming from a small town in Nebraska, we have always thought that these stories of "skinwalkers" were all full of shit. We couldn't have been more wrong.

Finally, after almost a day of swapping seats all the way through three different states, we finally made it.

We admired the vast desert landscape, the towering red sandstone cliffs, and the iconic Window Rock formation in the distance, taking it all in.

Before sunset was about to begin, we went to a small gas station in the middle of nowhere. We walked in to grab some snacks and drinks before heading deeper into the backcountry outside Window Rock.

When we walked up to the counter, the cashier asked,

"Why are you boys so far from home?"

All I could do was respond with,

"We're just trying to put a theory to rest."

He paused and stared at the lottery tickets on display for a little longer than he should, then said,

"I know what you are talking about, and I would advise you to go back to wherever you came from, because curiosity may unearth more than you can bury."

I gave his words little thought, paid for the snacks, filled the SUV, and we got back on the road.

By the time we made it to a lonely stretch of desert backcountry outside Window Rock, it was dark.

Dylan was complaining about staying in the car while Josh was trying to rile us up by saying "skinwalker" over and over again, because apparently, saying it attracts them.

I told him to shut up, and we continued.

All we could see was the land illuminated by the headlights of my SUV. Even though it wasn't much, I was not about to find out that skinwalkers were real by getting face-to-face with one.

Then, Kasher said that he had to go to the bathroom.

We pulled over along the side of a lonely dirt road, and he stepped out toward a patch of juniper trees and sandstone outcrops to answer nature's call while we waited.

One minute passed.

Two.

We all got nervous, then Eli suggested we go see what the hell was taking him so long.

We all stepped out, and we started calling out for him.

"Kasher!"

"Kasher!"

"What the fuck is taking you so damn long!"

All of a sudden, the desert went dead silent.

The only noise we could hear was the sound of our breathing and the slight hum of the idling SUV.

Then, Kasher stepped out from the brush, his clothes having a few minor scratches and tears on them, slightly stained with dirt and grass.

"Jesus Christ!" Dylan shouted, startled by Kasher's sudden appearance.

"Where the hell were you, and what the hell were you doing?" Josh said, scanning Kasher from head to toe, worry painted across his face.

I just stood there, silently wondering how and why his clothes were torn up.

It looked like he had been in a fight.

Then, Kasher opened his mouth.

He spoke, but his words were just slightly delayed, barely noticeable, and his voice was... off.

It sounded like he had forgotten how he normally talked.

He blinked slowly.

Too slowly.

Then he said,

"W-where were you guys? I-I was looking all over f-for you all."

We all stared, dumbfounded.

Kasher never stutters.

He only does when he is lying.

We brushed it off and went back to the car.

When we got in front of the headlights, I looked at Kasher's eyes.

They looked wrong.

I couldn't put my finger on it, but something deep in my gut told me that wasn't my friend.

Dylan noticed too.

We gave each other a creeped-out look and silently nudged Eli and Josh to look too.

They both realized something wasn't right.

Not the Kasher kind of strange.

The uncanny kind.

Maybe Josh's constant jokes had finally gotten into our heads.

How wrong I was.

After a few minutes of driving, Kasher told us that he, quote,

"wanted to show us something"

that he'd heard about somewhere.

We hesitantly agreed and pulled over again.

Kasher jogged ahead until he disappeared into the darkness.

Then we heard him call,

"OVER HERE!"

We all stopped.

That didn't sound excited.

It sounded terrified.

I remembered hearing that skinwalkers mimic the voices of people they've encountered and turned to the other three.

It dawned on us all at the same time.

Maybe they were real.

Maybe whatever walked back to the SUV wasn't Kasher.

Then, from somewhere ahead of us, we heard a scream unlike anything I had ever heard before.

It sounded human...

until it didn't.

That's when shit hit the fan.

I screamed,

"GET TO THE SUV! NOW!"

And we took off in a dead sprint toward the vehicle as fast as our legs would carry us.

I slammed the gear into drive and peeled out of there.

Eli screamed,

"WHAT THE FUCK WAS THAT?!"

And Dylan, Josh, and I all screamed,

"I DON'T KNOW!"

Dylan yelled,

"HOLY SHIT!"

Josh screamed,

"WE'RE FUCKED!"

Eli quietly started praying beside me while Dylan whispered,

"This is it... isn't it?"

We didn't even make it thirty feet before something massive slammed into the side of the SUV.

We rocked onto two wheels, Dylan and Josh's side lifting into the air.

We all screamed.

Only then did we realize that fear had completely taken over.

We had forgotten about Kasher.

I whipped around in a sharp U-turn.

For one second...

Just one...

The headlights caught it.

It had Kasher's lips.

Kasher's nose.

Kasher's eyes.

But its head was stretched unnaturally long.

Its skin was pulled so tightly over its face that it looked ready to tear.

Patchy strands of hair clung to its scalp.

Its eyes were black.

Completely black.

Its jaw stretched into something that resembled a smile.

It stood nearly eight feet tall.

Its arms and legs bent in ways they never should have.

Then it was gone.

I realized going back for Kasher was a lost cause.

So I floored it.

About thirty minutes later, we finally made it back to the gas station.

All of us sat in stunned silence before walking inside.

The same employee from before looked at us.

"There's one of you missing."

Josh stared at him, grief written across his face.

"We don't want to hear this right now."

We paid and started the painful drive home.

Nobody said much.

Eventually Dylan spoke.

"Kasher..."

"He's gone, isn't he?"

"Our friend isn't coming back... all because of some fucked up monster we thought wasn't real."

I looked back at him through tears.

"I don't know anymore. Maybe this is some fucked up nightmare. Maybe we'll wake up."

We didn't.

Obviously.

"I know you probably think this is fake, but it isn't," I said.

"Officer, every single one of us has told you the same story. Everything I've said is exactly what happened."

All of my friends and I were interrogated separately after Kasher disappeared.

Nobody believes us.

They never will.

They want answers.

We don't have any they'll accept.

If you think this story is bullshit...

Good.

I hope you never find out that it isn't.

We went looking for proof.

Kasher never came home.

If you ever find yourself out near Window Rock after dark...

And one of your friends disappears into the desert...

Make sure the one who comes back is really your friend.
