They worked around the space where Daniel had stood. No one looked toward the broken railing or the gate the officers had just closed.

Later, an attendant brought the green blanket to the receiving window for Seventeen. The frayed corner Daniel had shown me was visible through its evidence bag. I asked Vale to keep it with the little toys and to record Daniel's name. Seventeen would recognize the cloth immediately; he had slept with it for years. Mother had sent it because she worried he wouldn't rest without it. Earlier that morning she had selected the man who made it for the clean room, then offered Sam instead.

The radio operator recovered a partial inventory from the transfer terminal. Vale read it and passed it to Reese. Under TABLE, the newest entry bore the number from Sam's new wristband. Beside it someone had written APPROVED. I kept my hands on the edge of the table while Reese explained that rescue specialists were trying to reach the lower preparation rooms from outside.

I took out my phone to call Marisol and found Nora's photograph open instead. She stood beside her exhibition picture, smiling awkwardly with her ribbon in one hand. The picture showed an overturned wooden boat beside a lake we'd visited years ago. I texted her asking if she'd show me the rest of the photographs when I got home. She replied with a question mark, then, "You mean it?" I wrote, "Yes. All of them." Afterward I sat looking at Marisol's number. I knew what I'd promised her about passing on Sam's words, and I had no idea how to tell her he'd been taken again.

The last thing I saw from the gallery that morning was a worker repairing the panel beside the clean door. He wore an identification band like Seventeen's and carried an ordinary toolbox. As he stepped inside, a voice asked where the blue-marked arrivals should wait. Someone answered that the Housekeeper wanted them kept together until the afternoon. I could see folded cloths on a white counter behind him before the door shut. Sam had been standing there not long ago, holding Daniel up while the others escaped. I watched the door until Reese called me away, and even then I could hear Sam telling me what to say to his wife.
