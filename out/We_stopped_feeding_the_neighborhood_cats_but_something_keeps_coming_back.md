Hello, for context this post is about something gravely disturbing that just happened to me and I have honestly run out of any other options but to post it here to ask for advice.

Before I begin to explain, I’d like to apologize for any grammatical errors, I am very nervous while writing this.

I (19F) live with my parents right now as I am doing online community college for my pre-requisites. I’d like to believe that I’m quite responsible and my parents believe so too. They tend to go on trips frequently and this was no different. They had a trip to go on so they asked, “Penelope, could you stay home and take care of the dogs?” and of course I said yes. 

We have two dogs, Harry and Lenny. Harry is a big Golden Retriever and Lenny is a little Shih Tzu. My parents gave me my list of tasks for while they were gonna away for those two weeks, simple stuff like checking the mail, feeding the dogs, giving Harry his medicine for his epilepsy. There was one more task that I was fine with, at first… but looking back at it I really wish that I never did it to begin with.

Feeding the neighborhood cats.

We are a pretty nice family I’d like to believe. We always make sure the pack of stray cats never go hungry and we buy cat food to fill up their little bowl outside. It’s usually a grey mama cat and her four babies that come to our door every night expecting their food. Sometimes there are some other cats but it’s that family that’s the most frequent. 

I had absolutely zero issues with this either, I love cats! I love that they trust us enough to keep coming back, as that really warmed my heart! The issue arose when on around the third night of their absence the whole house got overwhelmed with the smell of a skunk. It was disgusting.

I’d go hang out with friends only for them to ask me, “Why do you smell like a skunk, P?” And that was just humiliating. I assume that the skunk sprayed a cat and the scent wafted into our air conditioning. As you may know, skunk smell takes a while to go away so the house stunk for a day or two. We get skunks and opossums eating out of the cat food too and obviously it’s becoming an issue.

I’d go and get the mail and the air would be fresh, only for me to re-enter my house and the nasty smell was noticeable. After a day or so I’d message my parents and ask them what to do about this. They reluctantly agreed that we might have to stop putting the food out for the cats if it’s gonna attract skunks and other critters. So the next night I tried not to refill the bowl. I felt awful when I saw the cats out there waiting for their food that wasn’t going to come. 

I couldn’t help myself, I filled the bowl and I put it further out. Not on the porch but on the corner of our path to the porch. Of course, the skunk showed back up and that smell returned. The next night I didn’t put the food out there. I avoided looking outside and letting the sad cats guilt me, I kept telling myself that they are little hunters, they can find food. A crawling, aching voice in my mind kept telling me that they might’ve gotten too dependent on our food though. 

Despite the voice, I didn’t fill the bowl this time. Maybe there would be some sort of skunk repellent that my parents could get when they come back, maybe we could keep feeding JUST the cats. So, for the remaining 11 days of my parents’ trip, the cats slowly stopped showing up and so did the smell.

But on the 5th night, something else showed up.

I had just got done giving Harry his medicine. I went and put him in his cage for bed and Lenny came back to the living room with me and sat by me. I made some microwaved ramen noodles and sat on the couch, putting a show on the TV. 

I was sitting there, petting Lenny, “Who’s a good boy, Len? You are!” I would tell him lovingly. That’s when I heard it though. Clawing at the front door. I assumed it was a cat or another animal trying to lure me out to refill the food bowl. I told Len to stay there and I went to check whatever that noise was and… nothing. There was nothing there. I just guessed that it scurried away before I got to the door. No big deal. 

The next night, it was the same deal. Clawing at the door, I go to check, nothings out there. This time Lenny barked a little at the first scrape, maybe that’s what scared it away this time? The next morning I asked my mom if there was anything at the door last night on the camera, she said that there weren’t any detections and hadn’t been for a few nights now. This also wasn’t that crazy, sometimes the camera wouldn’t pick up small animals. It didn’t always see the cats either. The camera also would reset every night at 1:00 AM on the dot. Screen would just turn black for 30 minutes. 

I always joked about that saying that would be the perfect time for a burglar to make their move on our house. My dad would laugh and my mom would always tell me to shut up and that she didn’t wanna think about that happening. Anyways, those scratch sounds kept happening around 1:00 AM so whatever the animal is, it’s showing up in that little window where you wouldn’t even see it.

The next day when I was out to get the mail I came back towards our door and I saw it. Not three… not four… but five scratch lines on the door. As if something with five… large claws… was scratching at the door. Thought that was very bewildering. If it’s a cat, opossum, or skunk I don’t think that the scratches would be that… large. That many lines honestly scared me because my first intruding thought was that the marks looked like they were from human nails. Sharp human nails. I told myself that was crazy and I went back inside.

I was sitting on the couch and honestly? I was rattled. I was starting to let doubt creep into my mind and that’s when I decided I’d wait near the door tonight. I’d let the scratching go longer… and then I’d peak to see what’s going on. 

To my utter horror, only for a moment I saw a gangly creature, no doubt that it was humanoid but I don’t know if I could call it human. It had long, dirty hair. It was very pale and had long legs but it was hunched over unnaturally looking at the door as it clawed. Then it saw me and for the moment it made eye contact I saw that it had sunken, cavernous, white eyes. The mouth was a thin line, almost like there were barely any lips. It looked dirty and malnourished. Its expression was devoid of emotion and then it ran down our porch and across our lawn. 

It was out of my field of vision. I am writing this now because I don’t know what to do. It’s fast, should I try to get a picture of it to send my parents? I don’t think they’d believe me without proof and if I call the police then they will think I’m crazy too. I live in a pretty nice suburb where this type of thing would be completely unheard of. Please, give me some advice.
