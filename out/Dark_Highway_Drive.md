So, for five days out of the week I drive an hour to work. It’s not too terrible but there are long stretches where there is nothing around. By that I mean, no street lights, stores, turn offs and lots of times no other cars. I fill the hour drive with music, podcasts and horror stories. Probably not the best thing to do with someone who over thinks all the time. Especially when those horror stories about driving alone on freeways or highways.

So anyway, my story follows…like many of those I listen to. As I’m driving, my mind starts to wonder about what I would do if something in these stories happened to me. As one does, we like to think of ourselves as hero’s, calm and cool. Able to make the right choice when the pressure is on and scoff at those in the stories for the dumb mistakes they make. Well I’ll tell you what, you’ll never know what you’ll do unless the situation actually happens. And happen to me it did, judge me all you want.

But until what happened to me, happens to you don’t say anything.

So, like I said, I’m driving down the dark highway like any other time. From time to time I see the shine of animals eyes crossing the road. Foxes, raccoons, deers, and rabbits, trying their best to pull a Frogger in real life. What happens more often than not is someone comes up behind you with their brights on. You know those types of people, assholes. This particular version is coming at me with their brights on.

You’d think it would be common courtesy to turn off your brights when coming towards someone, but this eludes so many. So to be a dick bag, I turn my brights on to show him my appreciation for all things lighted. We pass each other, both being assholes and I think nothing of it. I watch in my rear view mirror as they continue to drive away but then, I see them stop and turn around. They start coming back down the road but are now behind me. Greaaaaaat, I say to myself here he comes brights on coming up right on my bumper. They drive there for a moment before they start flashing their lights again and again and again. Starting to feel like I’m getting hit with a strobe light I flip my rearview up so the light stops getting in my eyes. They continue to do this for a few miles and I get tired of it and slow down when the road begins￼ to become two for a moment. To my surprise he speeds around and takes off. He drives so fast that he is gone in but a few moments. Way to start my fucking morning. 

I’m going along and forget about the absurdity of what occurred. I won’t let one asshole ruin my whole day. As I’m coming around one of the many big bends on my journey to work. My thoughts return to the nonsense I was thinking about before being attacked by a strobe light. As I round the corner suddenly I’m blasted in the face when someone turns their brights on. The problem is, they were sitting off the road and it felt like they were waiting for me. The sudden appearance of the sun, made me throw my free hand up to shield myself. While trying my best to stay in my lane, what a fucking asshole I say to myself. That’s next level petty shit right there, I’d almost applaud them if I wasnt trying to stay in my lane and not crash. I glance in my rearview to see if they are going to preform a 180 and get behind me again. They don’t, to my surprise so I mutter to myself as I continue on. 

Little time later, coming around another bend when, unbelievably, I’m hit again with a light to the eyes, I have to shield myself again and get past it. Did this guy call a friend to bring them into this pettiness against me? Again I look in the rearview but they stay right where they were as I continue down the road. Another bend is coming up and I’m getting a little worried about getting flash banged again. So I reach over and put my sunglasses on. Immediately think of the sunglassses on at night song, I’m old I know. Round the corner sure enough, I’m greeted with another sun!

At least I was prepared this time, I give them the finger as I drive by and take my sunglasses off. Again they don’t pursue and that’s fine with me. It dawns on me a moment later, three bends…..three cars suddenly throwing their brights on as I come around. 

This…..something’s wrong here and I feel like I’ve been driving way too long on this stretch of road. I look at my clock 3:15am, I am a little behind but not too bad. I keep going, another big bend is coming. I bite my bottom lip and think surly, not again. It can’t be, round the corner, flash banged again. GAH! What is happening?! How many friends does this guy have, how are this organized?! I keep driving and again am not pursued. I glance at my clock, still 3:15am….how can that be, it’s been at least ten minutes since the last flash. My mind races to all the horror stories I’ve listened to on this drive before. I make the conscious decision to pull over if it happens again, another big bend is coming up. 

I brace myself for the flash, sunglasses back on. Round the corner, BAM brights but I’mready for it. I drive past where they are parked and pull over just beyond them. I’m going to get to the bottom of this, take the sunglasses off and note the time, 3:15am. 

Throwing my car in park and undoing my seatbelt I get out with all the swagger of road rage madman. The air is cool and crisp, as I start making my way over to parked car with the sun lights still shining. As I get closer I hear a dinging sound, like what would play if a door was left open. I come around towards the driver side and see the door is open ajar. All my bravado seems to disappear the closer I get to the driver door. I keep looking around but can’t see anyone in the car. Finally at the driver door, I pull it open and look inside. Nothing, there’s no one in there. There is a coffee mug in the cup holder and a half eaten muffin, probably blueberry, on the passenger side seat. I stand back up & look all around but see nothing but inky blackness.

The air becomes colder as it dawns on me, there are no sounds anywhere but the dinging of the alarm. I close the door and look around again, nothing. I start to feel a bit on edge and get the feeling I should get the hell out of there. I sprint back to my car and hop in and drive away as fast as I can. Coming up to another bend I pray that there is no solar flare when I round the corner. The time is 3:15 still, I round the corner and….nothing but a straightaway. I sigh in relief & note the time.

3:16am. The rest of the drive continues as normal. I arrive at work and begin the drudgery of the day.

Nothing like that has happened since, I have no explanation for how it happened or how I managed to get out of it. My brain thinks I got caught in some type of time loop, I dont know. All I know is, I never want to do that again
