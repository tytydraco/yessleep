Rain and wind pounded on the old house as Danika stood at the screen door trying desparately to see or hear anyone comming. The raging storm and inky darkness reduced visibility to only a few feet. With eyes straining and puffy from crying she went over in her mind the events which had culminated in her present state of surreal worry. 

Craig had been following her around since grade school. What for her was a crush turned into friendship with familiarity and maturity. She could never tell him that the real reason for their closeness was pity, a guilt of not being able to reciprocate his affection. It was obvious that he was jealous of her highschool boyfriend, the tall handsome Jason. Her parents did not approve of the quiet and scrony Craig, and she came to agree with them. Jason, being likewise from a wealthy family, affable, fun and quite hot, was the one whom she felt real affection for. 

It was hoped that her and Jason's plan to invite Craig to a camp party with some of their friends would make clear to him the reality of the situation. Maybe he would even find interest in one of her single friends and the jealousy towards Jason would end. 

She had invited three friends to Jason's family's lake house; Tina, Scarlet, and Lacey. Jason had brought his best friends Devon and Thomas, Craig was meant to complete the balanced group. Things had initially gone well, with the guys out teaching Craig manly camp stuff and while she painted nails with the girls and tried to sell Craig's good qualities to them.

At lunch she could see that sime of the tension was gone from Craig. He fist bumped Jason as they made jokes abou the roasting hotdogs. Yet, as they walked off to the woods for an afternoon of hunting, an uneasy feeling crept over her. Maybe it was the weather.

There was a chance of rain later but the storm that swept in a short time later took them all off guard. 

Out in the forest Craig was enjoying the feeling of power that firing a gun bestowed. The guys had said he was a natural good shot and were excited about finding him targets when the sky datmrkened and driving rain crashed down. There were curses and shouts. The protests got even worse when Jason siad he could not fins the path in the dark. It was decided slpitting up would be the best way, Craig was paired with Jason. He suspected the plan and knew that Jason was aware that he loved Danika too. He stared intensely at  Jason's large soaked form crashing about in the brush. 'Not so tough and charming now huh?' he thought. How could Danika have fallen for this preppy hack and his shallow gifts? Only he trully knew her, appreciated her, and deserved her... 

Suddelny Jason slipped below the ground and a sharp shout was heard to penetrate the storm. Craig approached carefully and found Jason at the bottom of a tiny waterfall, the rappids of a small stream swelled by the deluge. His right leg was clearly broken and he was begining to stans on the left and reach out for help. Craig stood still in the darkness, watching the struggle below, as lightning flashes began to monentarily illuminate the scene. Thunder rolled over the hillside as Jason looked up and noticed him.

"Hey buddy, ah, my leg is messed up. Could you give me a hand?" was faintly heard from the shivering Jason. 

Craig still stood motionless, holding the rifle tight. Then the rifle was raised. Confusion, fear, and then rage overtook Jason as he understood at the last moment what Craig had been thinking about...

At the cabin, the small wooden building only had one central living room with a fireplace and two bedrooms, was quiet. Danika sat in a chair and watched anxiously as Tina twirled Scarlet's appropriately  coloured hair on the couch. Lacey was pacing behind the couch.

"They have been gone too long, I really think we should call someone." Danika said.

"Not yet," Lacey replied, "Jason knows this place really well so they are probably just at the neighbour's to get dry clothes or something." 

Danika was thinking about this and all the other possibilities when the side door suddebly blew open and the howl of wind rose sharply. Craig burst in and slammed the door shut. He was panting hard and dripping all over the wood floor, the gun was missing and so were the others. After he was given dry clothes and warmed Craig told the girls about how the storm had shrouded the forest in thick darkness and they had split up. He said that he lost sight of Jason at sone point but stumbled onto the trail shortly after and ran back to get help. 

Of course, he had left out some details. Such as a violent lightning bolt which struck quite close behind him as he turned to leave the scene of the murder, frightening him so that he dropped the weapon and began fumbling for the trail that he remembered was near the creek. Now all Craig feared was the police, but the rain would wash the body into the lake where it would become rotten and get eaten. There would be no evidence. Finally, Danika would be all his just as before...

The phone line was down and the teens in the cabin had been waiting around anxiously for fifteen more minutes. Danika considered Craig as he stood next to the armchair and tried half-heartedly to assuage her anxiety. Clearly something was distracting him, if anything he seemed happy about something. But what could he possibly-? THUD

Everyone's heads turned to the side door that Craig had entered from. No one moved, Tina and Scarlet were on the couch again and Lacey was in the restroom. It sounded similar to the falling branches broken off the trees, it was difficult to determine sounds in the maelstrom outside. It sounded right outside the door this time... BANG. The door was struck and swung open with a long CREEEEEEEEAAK. In the opening, visible only by faint firelight and old lamp bulbs was a human sillouette. But something was wrong. A lightning flash lit the grotesque figure and terror flooded the room as thunder shook the building. It was Jason, or rather, Jason's mangled corpse. Hair and wet clothes hung in clumps covered in dirt and blood. A leg was bent sideways near the knee and was supporting the weight uneasily, a glint of bone was visible. A dark stain dripped from a hole in the chest and, on the head, a burnt patch was discernable above bloodshot eyes. It smelled... fried? Like the distinct odour of electrical fire. 

Tina screamed long and hard before getting up and tripping on the foot table, her head hit the corner of an oak end table and she lay motionless on the floor. Blood pooled as the darkened figure took a left leg step into the room, then began dragging the ruined right leg into position. The others were too terrified to scream and did not yet move. Maybe Jason needed help? 

"N- n- no, it can't b- be." stammered Craig. "I saw you die, I was it!" He stepped back.

"What!?" Shouted Danika. She was not sure who to be more afraid of now. 

As Jason shuffled closer more became visible. His jaw was clenched and the eyes were dead and yet burning with hate. They were directed at the stupified Craig, still stammering and bumping backwards into the couch. 

RRRRRRRUUUAAAAAGH! A gutteral bellow erupted from Jason's open mouth as arms flew up and streatched out for Craig. Scarlet got up and ran out the front door into the raging storm. 

"No no no!" Shoutted Craig as the hideous attacker bore down on him. He tried to defend himself but Jason's head was flung forewards  with jaw open wide and clamped onto his neck with a sickening sound. At this Danika screamed and tipped the chair over backwards. 

She was huddled in a ball in the corner behind the chair when the terrible sounds stopped. Danika peeked through her fingers and saw that both Craig and Jason were motionless now. Two bodies piled together on the couch. Lacey oppened the restroom door,

"What the hell is-" she froze at the sight of deep red pooled around the couch and Tina's head. Lacey passed out and hit the ground with a THUMP next to Tina.

Thomas was found near death on the highway, suffering from exposure and trauma. He told police that he and Devon had encountered a 'zombie' in the forest and, while running away, Devon had tripped and been impaled on a falled tree limb.
