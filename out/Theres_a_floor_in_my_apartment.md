I need to start by saying that I know how this sounds.

I know what it sounds like when someone posts on the internet claiming they discovered a *secret floor* in their apartment building, or that their landlord has been hiding something, or that there’s a person living inside the walls.

I would have laughed at this six months ago. I don't anymore , because I’ve reached the point where I don’t know what else to do.

And because last night, someone knocked on my door and used my own voice.

I live alone in a twelve-story apartment building in a city I’m not going to name.

The building is old, but not that old. Maybe 40years. It was renovated a few years before I moved in, and from the outside it looks completely ordinary: concrete, narrow balconies, glass entrance doors, a parking garage underneath.

There are two elevators.

Both go from the basement to the 12th floor.

(I know this because I’ve lived here for almost three years.)

There is no 13th floor,14th floor or any hidden floor.

At least, there wasn't *supposed* to be.

It started with the elevator.

About six months ago, I came home late from work, it was around 11:40 p.m.

I got into the elevator on the ground floor and pressed 8 and the doors closed.

The elevator started going up.

Then it stopped at 10; I hadn't pressed 10.
The doors opened.

Nobody was standing there.

I waited for a few seconds, expecting someone to get in.
Nobody did.

So I pressed 8 again.
The doors closed.
The elevator moved.

Then the display changed.

Instead of showing 10, it showed 10.5..
I actually laughed and thought it was some kind of electrical glitch.

Then the elevator stopped.

The doors opened,there was a hallway outside.

It looked exactly like the hallway on my floor.

Same beige carpet.
Same yellowish wall lights.
Same little brass apartment numbers.

Except there was something wrong with it.

The hallway was much longer.

I could see doors stretching into the distance, maybe twenty or thirty of them.

Our building doesn't have that many apartments per floor.
I stood there staring.

Then I noticed the carpet.
It was wet.
Not soaked.

Just damp enough to shine beneath the lights.
I remember thinking that was strange.

Then I heard someone walking.

Bare feet.
Slow steps.
Coming toward the elevator from somewhere down the hallway.
I hit the door-close button.

Nothing happened.

The footsteps got closer.
I hit it again.
Still nothing.

Then I heard someone whisper:

“Wait.”

I don't know why, but that word scared me more than anything else.

I pressed the button for the ground floor.
The doors finally closed and the elevator dropped.

When I got home, I checked the security camera footage through the building's resident app.

The elevator camera showed me entering alone.

It showed the elevator stopping at 10.

It showed the doors opening.

Then the footage jumped forward almost nine minutes.

I was still standing inside the elevator with doors closed.

There was no footage of the hallway.

I assumed it was a camera glitch and forgot about it until *3 weeks later*...

I came home and found an envelope outside my apartment.
There was no stamp or address.
Just my apartment number written on it.

I opened it.

Inside was a photograph.

It was a picture of my apartment door,taken from the hallway.

The weird part was that the photograph had been taken from the opposite direction.

The hallway in the picture was much longer than mine.
And underneath the photo, someone had written:

YOU GOT OFF TOO EARLY.

I threw it away.

The next morning, it was back outside my door.
Same photograph.
Same handwriting.

But there was something new written on the back.

NEXT TIME, STAY ON.

I called the property manager.
She looked at me like I was wasting her time.

I showed her the photograph.
She went very quiet.
Then she asked:
“Where did you get this?”

I told her.

She took the photograph from me.
She didn't look surprised.
She looked scared.
Then she gave it back.

“Don't use the elevator after midnight,” she said.

I laughed because I thought she was joking.
She wasn't.
I asked why.

She just said:
“Take the stairs.”
Then she walked away.

That was the first time I seriously considered moving.

But moving is expensive, and I convinced myself that whatever was happening had nothing to do with me but.... I was wrong, so wrong..

The elevator started stopping at 10.5 every night.

I didn't use it.
I took the stairs.

For almost two weeks, nothing happened.
Then, one night, I heard knocking.
Three knocks.

From inside my apartment.
I was sitting on my couch watching television when it happened.

Knock.

Knock.

Knock.

I muted the television and checked the front door.

Nobody was there.

I checked the bathroom.
Nothing.
Bedroom.
Nothing.
Kitchen.
Nothing.

Then I heard it again.

Three knocks.

This time from the bedroom closet.

I stood there for almost a minute.
I didn't open it.
Eventually, I went to bed.

The next morning, there were muddy footprints on my bedroom floor.

They started at the closet and ended beside my bed.

There were no footprints going back.

I called the police.

They found nothing.

No broken locks,forced entry or  signs that anyone had been inside.

The officer told me I should probably install a camera.

So I did.

I put one facing my bedroom door and the other facing the front door.

I also bought a cheap motion sensor and put it in the hallway.

For three nights, nothing happened.

On the 4th night, the bedroom camera detected movement at 3:12 a.m.

I woke up and checked my phone.
The camera showed my bedroom.
Nothing was there.

Then the motion detector went off.

I looked toward the hallway.
Nothing.
The bedroom camera detected movement again.

This time, I opened the feed.
There was a person standing in my bedroom.

I nearly screamed.

They were standing *directly behind* me.

I turned around.

Nobody.
I looked back at the phone.
The person was still there.

They were tall,extremely thin and wearing dark clothes.

Their face wasn't visible because they were standing with their back toward the camera.

I slowly moved toward the bedroom door.

The figure on the camera didn't move.
I stepped into the hallway.
Still nothing.
I looked at the camera again.

The figure had turned around.

I could see their face now.
It was me.
Not someone who looked like me.

Me-Same face.

Same hair.
Same scar on my chin.
Same shirt I was wearing.
But the person on the screen was smiling.

I wasn't.
Then the figure raised one finger to its lips.

And the camera feed went black.

I didn't sleep that night.

At 7 a.m., I checked the camera recording.

There was no footage of anyone entering my apartment.
The recording simply showed my empty bedroom.

Except for one frame.
One single frame.

At 3:17 a.m.

The figure was standing beside my bed.

Looking directly into the camera.

And on the wall behind it, written in what looked like wet dirt, were four numbers:

10.5

I contacted the property manager again.

This time, she invited me into her office.
She locked the door.

Then she told me something I wish I had never heard.

Before the building was renovated, it had thirteen floors.
I asked why the current building only had twelve.
She said the 13th floor had been sealed.

I asked why.
She wouldn't tell me.
I asked what happened there.

She said:
“People disappeared.”
I thought she meant tenants.

She shook her head. “Employees.”

Maintenance workers,Security guards and Contractors.

People who went upstairs and *never came back down*.

Eventually, the city ordered the floor closed.

During the renovation, they removed the 13th-floor elevator button and changed the building plans.

Officially, the floor didn't exist anymore, but sometimes, the elevator still went there.

She called it “the half floor.”

I asked why.
She said:
“Because that's what the old maintenance workers called it.”
I asked what was on it.

She looked at me.
Then she said “Rooms.”
I waited.

She continued- “Rooms that shouldn't be there.”

I laughed.
She didn't.
She told me the most disturbing story I'd heard yet.

Years ago, a maintenance worker named Daniel had gotten trapped in the elevator after midnight.

The elevator stopped at the sealed floor.
He got out.
The security cameras showed him walking down the hallway.
Then he disappeared.

Three days later, he called the building manager.

From his own apartment.

The manager rushed there.

Daniel was sitting in his living room.

Except Daniel had been missing for three days.

His apartment had been empty.

No signs of forced entry.

No explanation.

When they asked him where he'd been, he wouldn't answer.

He only kept repeating:

“There's another building underneath this one.”

Then he killed himself.

I didn't ask for details.

I didn't want them.

I asked the property manager why she was telling me this.

She said - “Because the photographs started appearing again.”

I knew immediately what she meant.

She opened a drawer-Inside were dozens of photographs.

All of apartment doors.
Some were old.
Some were recent.
Different tenants,Different years.

But every photograph showed the same impossible hallway.

She said they had been collecting them for decades.
Then she showed me one.

It was my apartment.

My door.
Taken from the hallway.
But this photograph was different.

My apartment door was open.

And inside was a dark room with Someone was standing in the doorway.

I couldn't see the face.
But I knew who it was. Me.

I asked her what I was supposed to do.

She said- “Move.”
So I did.
Or I tried to.

I packed everything that night and stayed at a hotel.
The next morning, I went back for the rest of my things.

My apartment door was open.
I knew I'd locked it.
Inside, everything was exactly where I'd left it.

Except for one thing.

The bedroom closet was open.

I hadn't packed anything from there.
I walked towards it.
There was a smell coming from inside.

Wet concrete.
Like a basement after rain.
I looked inside.
The closet was empty.

But there was a door at the back.

A door that had never been there before.

I should have left.

Instead, I opened it.

There was a staircase which I used to go down and stood there for a long time.

Then I heard something from below.

My voice.

“Hello?”

I slammed the door shut, ran for my life and didn't go back.

I moved into a different apartment across town.
For almost two months, nothing happened.

I started believing I'd escaped but then, yesterday, I received an email.

No sender.
No subject.
There was one attachment.

A photograph- of my new apartment door.

Taken from inside my new apartment.

I could see the hallway outside.

And in the bottom corner of the photograph was a timestamp.

3:17 a.m.

I hadn't even been home at that time.
Then I noticed something written on the back.

Three words.
YOU MOVED UP.

I didn't know what that means and seriously didn't want to know.

But tonight I checked the elevator in my new building.

It has twelve floors.

I live on the eighth.
The elevator buttons go:
And underneath them, there is a tiny blank space.

I never noticed it before.

Tonight, when I pressed 8, the elevator stopped.

The display changed.
It didn't say 8.

It said-  10.5

The doors opened.
There was the hallway.
The long hallway.
The wet carpet.
The endless doors.

And at the very end was a door with my apartment number on it.
I didn't step out.
I couldn't.

Someone was standing at the far end.
I could barely see them.

Then they started walking toward me.
Bare feet.
Slow steps.

And when they got close enough for me to see their face, I realized something.

It wasn't me.
It was the property manager from my old building.

She looked terrified.
She put her hands against the elevator doors.
And whispered"Don't let it see you.”

Then something behind her grabbed her.

She disappeared backward into the darkness.

The elevator doors closed.
I didn't move or breathe.
I watched the display count down.

10.5

When I got to my floor, I ran to my apartment.
The door was locked.
Everything was normal.

Except there was a photograph lying on the floor.

I haven't touched it.
I'm looking at it right now.

It's a picture of the elevator.
The doors are open.
I'm standing inside.

And behind me, reflected in the elevator's metal wall, there is someone else.

A man.
Very thin.
Smiling.
Standing directly behind my shoulder.

I can't see his face clearly.
But I recognize his shirt.

It's the same shirt I was wearing the night I first saw the hallway.
And that's impossible.

Because that shirt is hanging in my closet.

I'm not going to sleep tonight.

I'm sitting on the floor with every light on And about five minutes ago, I heard three knocks.

Not on my front door.
Not from the hallway.

They came from inside the wall behind my bed.

Three slow knocks.
Then a pause.
Then three more.

And now there's a voice whispering through the wall.

It's my voice.

It's saying-“Let me out.”

I don't think the thing in the elevator was trying to get inside.
I think it was trying to get back out.

And I think I may have *brought it with me*....
