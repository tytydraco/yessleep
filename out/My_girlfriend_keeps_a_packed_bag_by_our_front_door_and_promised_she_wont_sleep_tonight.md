I don't really know how to start this so I'm just going to say it how it happened.

My girlfriend and I have been living together for like 8 months. Small place, second floor, nothing special. At first everything was normal. Like, boring normal.

Then little things started to feel... off. Not scary-movie off. Just weird.

It started with her bag. She always used to just drop it anywhere when she got home. Then one day it was by the front door. Zipped up. Like ready to go. I asked her if she was going somewhere.

She was like "No, why would I?"

But she left it there. For days. Right by the door.

The other thing is I have never been a good sleeper. Ever since I was a kid I have this dumb fear that I'll just... stop breathing in my sleep. I know it's stupid. I never told anyone that. Not even her. I just lay there at night listening to myself breathe and at some point I can't tell if I'm actually breathing or if I'm forcing myself to breathe. Does that make sense?

One night I woke up at like 4:12am. And she was sitting up. Not laying down. Sitting on the edge of the bed, feet on the floor, just looking at me.

I freaked out a little and I was like "What are you doing?"

She said "Nothing. You made a noise. Like you forgot how."

Forgot how to what? She didn't answer. She just lay back down.

Now here's the part that's messing with my head.

Every time I wake up suddenly in the middle of the night, her eyes are already open. Every single time. Just for a second. Then she blinks and pretends she just woke up too and goes "You okay?"

Last week we were just eating dinner. I wasn't saying anything, I was just in my head. You know when you look at someone you love and your brain goes she's gonna get tired of this, she's gonna leave? That loop?

She put her fork down and just looked at me and said "If I had to leave, would you want me to warn you first?"

We weren't even talking about that. We were talking about what to watch.

I said "Do you want to leave?"

She said "No. But if YOU had to leave, would you want me to be there?"

What does that even mean?

I started making my coffee different recently. No sugar, a lot of milk. Just to stay awake a bit longer. I never told her I switched.

Yesterday morning she brought me a coffee to the couch. No sugar, a lot of milk. Exactly how I've been making it when I'm alone.

She handed it to me and said "Here. For staying awake."

I didn't ask how she knew.

Tonight she went to bed before me. She never does that. She always waits for me.

She left the bedroom door open and the hallway light on. From the bed she said "Come when you want. I won't sleep tonight. I promise I won't."

Her bag is still by the front door.

I don't know if I should go lie down next to someone who just promised me she'll watch me all night to make sure I keep breathing.

Or if I should just stay on the couch and keep staring at that bag and try to figure out if it's packed for her to leave...

or for me.
