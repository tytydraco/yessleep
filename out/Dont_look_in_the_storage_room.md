I found these pages in the storage room of an apartment I had bought, not for myself.

There were thirty-eight sheets of paper. Some came from notebooks with squares, some with lines. Some had torn edges, some did not. Some were wrinkled, others were smooth. They were all different.

They were wrapped in an old newspaper. It was Literary Gazette. On the front was Pushkin’s profile, almost like a picture on a book cover.

The newspaper caught my attention first because I like reading old newspapers. I opened it and found the stack of papers inside.

I started reading.

Then I could not stop.

It was a diary.

A very strange diary.

The diary of a very strange person.

Or maybe… not even a person.

I know this sounds like a cheap story. How many books begin with someone finding another person’s papers? Writers have found lost manuscripts in bottles, in bathtubs, in old pockets…

Mine was on a shelf in a storage room, under old boots and dusty jars.

But what can I do if it really happened?

Well… almost everything happened.

I changed a few small things.

I fixed many mistakes.

I removed even more.

But the story itself is true.

The pages are here, next to my keyboard.

I can touch them.

Sometimes I want to push them away or put something heavy on top of them. Better yet, lock them in a drawer and forget they ever existed.

I will do that.

But only after I finish telling this story.

I am afraid.

Very afraid.

Because I believe these pages tell the truth.
