After they came to our door, my sibling stopped being a “problem.”

That’s what my parents wanted, at least. But all I can remember is them being a normal kid. Yeah, they caused trouble sometimes–but they were young. That’s it. That’s all it was. Nothing that wouldn’t have passed on its own.

And that’s exactly what those two people promised when they showed up. They didn’t look strange at first. Just calm. Polite. Like they’d done this a hundred times before. Five minutes, they said.

That’s all it would take.

My parents believed them. I don’t know why, but they did. They let them take my sibling outside without much hesitation, like it was just another appointment–something routine. Something safe.

I watched from the window. I don’t think I even meant to at first. I just… stayed there. Something about the way they walked them to the van didn’t feel right.

It was the way they moved.

At first, nothing happened. After they reached the van and the door shut, everything went still. For a second, I almost felt stupid for worrying.

Then something hit the inside of the window. Hard enough to spread across the glass in a way I couldn’t mistake for anything else.

The blood should've been the worst part. But it wasn't. The worst part was watching a hand press against the window from the inside. I recognized the sleeve immediately. Then another hand grabbed it and pulled it backward.

I stumbled away from the window, my mind refusing to make sense of what I'd just seen. My little sibling was in that van. They had to be. My heart hammered so hard it hurt, and every breath caught in my throat. I wanted to scream for my parents, to tell them to do something, but I couldn't move. I just stood there, frozen, staring at the floor while my body tried to convince itself it hadn't happened.

Then the doorbell rang.

Before I could even move my body, my parents were already at the door, and to my shock, my sibling was right there, no marks, no blood, not even blood on the visitors, had I imagined everything? And if so why would I imagine something so vivid like watching an arm slam on glass and blood from someone you love splatter.

But I noticed something immediately that my parents couldn’t notice. The expression on that thing is not something from my sibling, the way it looked at me when my parents allowed it inside it made a chill go down my spine.

The thing smiled at my parents after they let it inside. It thanked them too, in the same voice my sibling always used, and my parents seemed relieved to hear it. My mom hugged it again, asking if they were okay and that thing nodded before looking back at me. It didn’t say anything but I knew that thing was already skeptical of me. Because it just stared at me longer than it should have.

I couldn’t stop looking at its clothes. The same clothes my sibling had been wearing when they went outside, the same stupid stain that my mom has been trying to get out for weeks. Everything was exactly the same and somehow that made it worse.

Whatever happened in that van didn’t leave a single mark but I vividly remember the blood splatter against the glass. How my siblings hand hit against the window.

My dad thanked the two people at the door and asked them how they managed to do it so quickly. One of them just smiled and said, “We told you. Five minutes is all it takes.” 

They didn’t explain anything else. They didn’t need to. My parents were already convinced that whatever they'd done had worked.

I wanted to tell them.

I wanted to point at that thing and scream that it wasn’t them, that I had seen the blood splatter against that vans glass, that I’d seen a hand against the glass. But when I opened my mouth, nothing came out. It was just standing there watching me, and there was something in its eyes that made me feel like I needed to be careful.

Then it did something that made my stomach drop.

It walked over to me while smiling.

“What’s wrong?” it asked

It was my sibling's voice but I could tell something was wrong with that thing. It wasn’t them.

I stared at it, trying to find something to say–anything, anything that would prove I wasn’t losing my mind. Then it started to tilt its head slightly, waiting for me to answer.

“Nothing” I said.

That’s all that came out of my mouth.

It smiled again.

“Okay.”

And then walked past me like nothing had ever happened.

Later that night, as we had dinner, everything was perfectly normal. 

Like too perfect.

My sibling didn’t do a single slip up at all this.

I couldn’t keep my eyes off the thing, I know what I saw was real because this isn’t how they act, it's not their manners. The way they usually eat their food, nothing that really reminded me of my sibling.

But my parents didn’t seem to notice.

Or worse. They didn’t care to notice. All that matters is that it worked and their child is now well behaved.

It noticed how I was watching it during dinner but didn’t say anything. We just made eye contact for a few seconds and I knew that thing knew I was onto it.

But still not one slip up.

It made me worried about what it would do without my parents around.

But the next few days are a blur to me. I don’t know why but I have a feeling something was happening to me. That thing definitely has something to do with it but I don’t know what it was that it did.

But I remember waking up one night with that thing standing over my bed watching me sleep. I know that it noticed I woke up because it grinned after.

THIS IS NOT MY SIBLING.

I just closed my eyes. I wanted it to go away. Leave me and my parents alone. 

The next morning I was on high alert, this thing had to be planning something but I didn't know what that could be, I thought I was ready for anything to happen.

But I wasn’t. I couldn’t predict what that thing could be capable of. My parents didn’t even see it coming but I did. I watched as that thing murdered my parents with one of our kitchen knives. I ran as fast as I could to the kitchen. I had to get one to protect myself from this thing.

I reached a knife but barely made it because it noticed me running towards the blade. It ran towards me and slashed at my left arm, it felt pretty deep at the time. But thankfully I was able to make it to a knife just in time and I slashed at the things throat.

It stopped for a few seconds and that gave me a few seconds to get it off of me and I lunged for more stabs. I didn’t stop until the thing wasn’t moving anymore, I didn’t know what to do or what would happen next.

Now as I’m writing this I do know what’s happening after. I’m going on trial for murdering my family.

No one believes me about what happened and I don’t expect you to either.
