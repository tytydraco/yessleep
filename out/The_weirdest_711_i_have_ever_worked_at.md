So, I have been working gas stations and convenience stores for a while now. One of the things I like about it is if your day plans fall through you can almost always pick up a shift and make a little extra cash. 

One friday night, I had a big date set up with this girl Abigail that I had been seeing for a while. But the morning of she texts me that she is not feeling well and wants to do next friday instead. A bummer to be sure, but not super unexpected. Abby has an auto-immune condition and the medication she takes makes it difficult for her to go out without getting sick. 

No problem though, I can just cancel the date, pick up a 7-11 SOS and make some easy money on a graveyard shift. The thing with graveyard is that its not harder, its just weirder. Arguably more dangerous, but out here we don't get a lot of violent types. Mostly just druggies and shoplifters at worst.  and so few customers compared to the shift day. 

I found a listing for a request on the north side of town. I had never worked at that 7-11 before, but i had been inside a few times as a customer. And honestly, if you work one graveyard you work them All. I thought. 

When I went in, the manager took me into the office, "so Anon, you are the one picking up the graveyard tonight right?"

"That's me!"

"How much do you know about graveyard?"

"Its not my main shift. But i have done it plenty of times" 

A look of relief washed over her. This was fairly common, a lot of people pick up these SOS requests coming off of day shifts and are never prepared for how tiring being up all night really is. 

"So there are a few things at this store you need to make sure to do..."she started listing off a few extra chores, checking the temperature of the coolers, cleaning one of their special displays, every store has a few different tasks depending on the manager. But then it started to get... Well, honestly strange. 

"If a really dirty guy comes in asking for a pack of Spuds, tell him we are out, but that lucky strike just came out with a menthol for the same price" 

I have no earthly idea what potatoes have to do with menthol cigarettes, but i just assumed some tweaker was making strange requests. 

"There is a woman who comes by and tries to get the employees to go outside with her, do not go outside. And be sure she is fully gone before going out for a cigarette or cleaning the parking lot" 

This scared me "what happens if I follow her out of the store?"

The manager opened her mouth, then hesitated, after a second of consideration she reluctantly admitted "I honestly don't know, but a lot of our nightshift people stop showing up. And none of my employees who are still here have ever followed her out. For all I know, she kills them" she suddenly course corrects with "but she could just be recruiting them for a different job off camera or something. I don't really know, but I'm a little superstitious" 

"Okay, so clean, clean, menthol lucky strikes and don't follow strange women outside" 

"There is a customer that comes in from time to time... He will ask if his son is working tonight, tell him that Oliver has been moved to the day shift" 

This instruction was not as strange on the surface.  Its usually best practice to not disclose anyone's schedule. Stalkers are still a thing even in a smaller town. But if i have permission from this stores manager I assume its been cleared with the employee. 

"And one final thing, our system has a weird hickup with the transition from one day to another. Make sure that at 11:59 you clock out, and then at 12:01 clock back in. I will go back and fix your punches manually" 

The shift started at 8:00 P.M. The store was pretty busy at that time on a friday night. I was rushing through customers picking up beer last minute when I first noticed something peculiar. Across the street was a tall individual wearing a puffy black coat. The hood was drawn and they were wearing a face mask, so I could make out any details. At first I thought they were waiting for the bus, but the stop was several feet from where he stood, and no matter how many busses passed, they just stood their, looking directly at the store as he puffed on a cigarette. 

By 10:00 it had settled a little. I helped the last customer in the store before going out to have a smoke. The person across the street was still there. In the exact spot i had seen them in all night. Still locking cold blue eyes on the store. 

I saw a customer walk round the corner onto the store so I put my cigarette out and went to go help him. He was a very dirty individual, sweat and grime had turned into a mud caked into the crevices of his skin. His tanktop and overalls looked like they had been patched and repatched several times. He kind of reminded me of an old timey prospector. 

"Packa spuds" he coughed out. 

Now, we are firmly past covid at this point, but I'm not super excited that this guy is coughing like a foot away from my face. Suddenly I notice his steely cold blue eyes. 

"Would you like a mask sir?"

He shot me a puzzled look, " 'xcuse me?"

I pulled out a paper facemask, "so you don't get anyone sick" 

He ignores the mask all together and says "just the spuds, thanks" 

That's when I remember, "oh shoot, we are actually out of spuds," I turn around and grab a pack of Menthol luckies off the shelf "these are new though, and the same price" 

The mans eyes widen "cheap luckies? And they come in menthol now? I'll take it" 

He sets two dimes on the counter confidently and takes his smokes and leaves before I can even scan the item or tell him that its not enough money. When i went to put the dimes away I noticed a lot of things wrong with them. For one, the face on the front looked like the statue of liberty or somethihg, and the back had this weird looking axe on it. So not only did the guy steal the smokes, he tried to pass off two obviously fake dimes as payment... fucking tweakers

There weren't a lot of customers over the next hour. One, who i imagine was a regular, came in around 11:00 and immediately broke into laughter "aaaaaaawwwww shit, they got some poor new fool working the graveyard" the jacket and face mask looked familiar, but before I could ask if he was the one staring at the store all night, I noticed his dark brown eyes.  And still across the street was my specter. Still smoking, still lurking. 

"I'm just a floater for tonight. Figured i could pick up some hours" 

"Oh cool. Well, at least I know why I won't see YOU again" he grabbed a 12 pack of Modelos paid for it, then asked "hey man, you got a lighter I can borrow? I'll give you a cigarette" 

We went out and had a smoke. I asked him about the comment he made about not seeing me again.

"This place is weird dude, I don't even know" he took a big drag and continued "but every time i come in here, there is either someone new or someone looking at old photos on their phone" 

"Old photos?" 

He took another drag "yeah, like of the store, or old people. I guess this property has been a convenience store in one way or another for a long ass time. At least since the mines were open" he flicked his cigarette, and went on "one of the last dudes here,  told me about the old Texaco building burning down. An employee was apparently napping in the back during his graveyard shift and burned to death" 

A chill ran down my spine. 

"He said it was like Hunter something or something Hunter" 

"Oliver?"

His face lit up, like two innocent thoughts lining up "yeah! that's what he said, Oliver Hunter!"

"What about the girl?"

"No clue on that one" he said. The fact that he understood what i meant from such a vague statement was a bit off putting. He grabbed another cigarette and beckoned for the lighter before asking, "you see her yet?"

"No" 

"You might not see her" he lit up his smoke, puts the lighter in the pack and stows in in his pocket, "the guy who wants old cigarettes always shows up apparently, but her not so much" 

"What does she look like? My manager said not to follow her outside but was really vague" 

"Whatever she wants, I think, or there is more than one of her. But like, just about Any time a woman asks to go smoke with her or help her carry a bag to her car, graveyard folks try to let her down easy" 

"What about that guy there?"

He looked out across the street, shrugged and went "no clue, but i like his style" he smiled and tugged at his matching coat. "Probably just on Fent, that shit fucks you up. You know I had this cousin-"

I wanted to stay and listen. Maybe ask more questions. But a big group of customers was coming up. These were a bar crowd. They start trickling in around 11:00 as they leave the bars, but want more beer to party with at home. I put the rest of my cigarette out and run back in to get behind the counter before they can finish shopping. 

It was mostly Buzzballs and beat boxes. Several of those little fireball bottles. But than at the end of the line was a man dressed like some kind of noir privet eye. He had a think wool jacket and a fedora. He set a 6 pack of budweiser on the counter and paid in cash. After i bagged the bottles, he lookeup from his wallet and asked "by the way, do you know if my son is working tonight?" His eyes were blue, the same blue as the figure across the street, and the man who took the lucky strikes earlier. 

"Oliver right? I think he is being moved to one of the day shifts" 

The man grab his bag and with a polite thank you, left the store. I went back out to keep talking to the remarkably well informed customer only to see that he had moved on. With an additional cigarette butt in the ashtray. I patted around and realized that the dude had my lighter. Before I went back in, i noticed a small glow across the street. The looming presence was where it had been. Smoking a cigarette. Watching 

"HEY! HEY!" I shouted, "COULD I USE YOUR LIGHTER!?" It didn't react. 

"I'LL BUM YOU A CIGARETTE IF YOU LET ME USE IT!"

This elicited the first reaction i had ever seen from the person. They looked at me, their eyes widened. But they did not move. After a moment I gave up and went back into the store to buy myself a lighter. 

"Was that you yelling out there?" 

The voice was unmistakable, that was Abby. I turned around and sure enough it was her. I looked her in the eye. Blue, but not the steely cold blue i was developing a pattern for. Jut her usual, vibrant blue eyes. 

"Yeah, i let some guy use my lighter and he forgot to give it back. I was trying to see if the guy across the street wou-" the situation finally processed in my mind "wait, I thought you didn't feel good tonight babe, why are you out here?"

She looked away from me, first to the chips, then the soda, "I was feeling a little better and I wanted a drink so I figured I would come in and get something" 

"That's fair, but why all the way out here? There's a plaid less than a block from your apartment" 

She looked down and her face sunk somewhat. "I just... Felt bad about having to cancel on you again. Its like the third weekend in a row where I was too sick to go out" she looked back up to me, i could see she was trying hard to not cry. Abby is really strong, but I know that her health issues make her feel unreliable and flaky, even though we all remind her that we understand. 

I gave her a big hug, "you don't need to do that for me, hun. You know I love to see you and hang out, but your health is more important" 

"Thank you" she choked, "let me get a Pepsi really quick and maybe we can have a smoke real fast and then I will go home?" 

"Of course," I sold her the Pepsi (i let her use the employee discount when she comes to visit) and followed her to the door before glancing at the clock. 11:59

"Hold up, i just need to clock out and in real quick" 

"Its fine Anon, just come out for a smoke" 

"But it will mess up my hours, this will be super quick" 

"No" 

When she said that i was taken aback, she normally isn't this demanding, especially over waiting 2 minutes. 

"Babe, this will literally take just a minute. I promise I will be right out" 

"You never want to be around Me" she turned round and continued, "You like that I'm always sick don't you? My neediness makes you feel important. But you don't have the decency to BE THERE FOR ME WHEN I WANT YOU TO BE. YOU ALWAYS MAKE ME SPEND WHAT LITTLE HEALTHY TIME I HAVE WAITING FOR YOU TO NOT BE REPULSED BY MY SNOT AND MY SORES AND COUGHING"

"Oh good, your still here" was all he had to say to me.
I went back into the office and clocked out. Grabbed my keys and went out to my car. As I passed I counted the cigarette butts, the same number as last time. So maybe he was real?  I didn't know. And i didn't want to know. I wanted to sleep.
