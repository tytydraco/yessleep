So I'm (21 M) a college student in the midwest (college redacted for privacy purposes). I'm going into my 3rd year (English major btw) and I got a random roommate (24M)- I didn't really have many standards I just wanted him to be same major as me so we would have something we could relate to. 

It started off fine; he was super into Murakami, and we bonded on that. He said that he liked it becuase it reminded him of his family. I thought that was kinda weird, after I found out Kafka on the Shore was his favorite novel (he has a stepsister btw). 

One day he was cooking pasta. He said it was Alfredo, his favorite. I knew it was his favorite because he was cooking it. While he was eating, he kept complaining that his head hurt and that he kept getting nosebleeds. Right at that instant, I noticed that there was some leftover blood in his nose, and it fell into his pasta. The red blood dropped onto the ocean of white, and fear called to me like a great white (from the movie Jaws by Steven Spielberg) from the deepest oceans of my heart- I was terrified.

Just then, his eyes rolled back in their sockets. The whiteness of his eyes shone in the white alfredo sauce, before additional blood spilled on it. 

He started levitating in that moment.

I didn't know what to do. My roommate was levitating before my very eyes. His feet weren't touching the ground at all (which was saying something, because he was 6'3, like me. So he was really tall). It was almost as if he was levitating. I couldn't believe my eyes (which were not white, because I wasn't levitating. In fact, they are blue). 

Just then, it struck 3Am (we were having a late dinner and it was a Saturday. We don't skip class.). I heard the grandfother clock chime, but just then, I realized that we don't have one of those. In fact, we only use our iPhone clocks to tell the time (iPhone 5c). At this point, I was really scared and super confused/skeptical.

Just then, I heard a terrible sound... CRACK! POP! SNAP!

His limbs were contorting at weird angles. Angles that were unnatural. Obtuse angles. Even if you did yoga all your life (like me), you wouldn't be able to do this. I'm serious.

His eyes vanished, leaving only empty sockets. In those sockets, I saw nothing because there were no eyes. His eyes were gone. I wondered if he could see me at all. He had no eyes. Like black-eyed peas (without the eyes) in the middle of the night and it was dark too. Too dark to see. So he would've never been able to see me even if I called out to him through that darkness (which I did), even if he still had eyes, which he didn't.

"\[name redacted for privacy reasons\]!", I yelled.

"What's wrong! Are you okay? Can you see me?!?" I said calmly.

He didn't respond initially. He then let out a sound that I still have nightmares about till this day. I get no sleep anymore because of this. 

"Schwelopaowai", he said. I didn't know that meant, even though I was an English major. This made me think he wasn't speaking English at all. I thought this was weird because he only ever spoke English to me.

And then I saw it: the creature. It looked so scary, I felt a shiver run down my spine. Straight goosebumps. He stuck out his hand. Just then, right before my eyes, my friend was turned inside out. It is a sight that I still think about to this day. I get no sleep anymore. This reddit is my only consolation. I need to know that I'm not crazy. Has anyone else seen something like this?

As I write this, I'm hiding in my closet right now. I'm so scared and I can't sleep.
