In a segment on episode ten of Cosmos, Carl Sagan explored the idea of Flatland. A two dimensional plane of existence where all its inhabitants were completely flat. They knew of left and right, forward and backwards, but they did not know of up and down. It was beyond their comprehension. If a third dimensional creature were to happen upon a two dimensional creature, and decided to communicate with them, they would only be perceived as an ethereal voice. Almost as if it were coming from inside themself. 

If the three dimensional creature were to cross through the other creature's plane of existence, only a slice of their body would be able to be seen. They would almost seem to morph into different shapes as they passed through. 

If they decided to grab onto the two dimensional creatures and pull them into the third dimension, that creature would not be able to comprehend what they were seeing. Their mind would struggle to make sense of anything. After being set back down in their own dimension, it would seem like they just appeared out of nowhere to their fellow inhabitants. When they are asked where they came from, they would not know where to point. They would not be able to comprehend the notion that they were in a place called ‘up’.

I didn't do too well in my science classes, but I loved to read and watch pop science. I always found it fascinating, and this particular segment of Cosmos always stood out to me. 

It's been a week since my Latin teacher seemed to lose their mind and go missing. You can read about the events that led up to that [here](https://www.reddit.com/r/nosleep/s/Ear7UDsDE9). Since then I've had an itch that I am unable to scratch. Not a physical itch on my skin, but an itch deep down in the crevices of my psyche. 

See, my Latin Teacher had a book on loan from a museum. He was in the middle of translating it when everything went down. He let me read one of the pages, which I firmly regret ever doing. My mind has never been the same ever since. It buried a fear deep into my soul that I cannot dislodge. However, I can't help but to want to read more. 

I feel like I'm always being watched anymore. I can never find the person watching me, but I just can't shake the feeling. An ever persistent voyeur who hides in the shadows of my mind. 

I don't know if I'm going to burn the book, or continue reading it, but I do know that I need to get into that office.

My head perked up. 

“You give a monkey enough time on a type writer.” He continued
