Lately, I've been doing various odd jobs around town. I really need 3,000 euros, and I'm trying to save up that amount any way I can. Recently, I saw a job posting for a illegal Korean bar in Koreatown in our city.

I worked yesterday, and today they told me that because of the off-the-books accounting, I'll have to quit. They were supposed to pay me at the end of the day, and that's the only reason I stayed until 11 p.m.

When we were closing up, I walked through the staff room to the locker room in another part of the building, in the basement. While I was changing into my regular clothes, I realized I’d left my charger on the counter by the entrance. I ran back there, and to avoid turning on the light in the electrical panel, I used my phone as a flashlight.

And when I picked up my charger and the money I’d left there (I was so tired I could have left without them at all), I walked through a very dark room toward the staff exit. I was told that this exit had never been locked, but I still don't understand why.

While I was walking, I ordered a taxi so I wouldn't have to wait for the bus. And since I needed Wi-Fi, I stayed there for another three minutes. Then, when I stepped outside, only the streetlights were on. Not even the windows were lit up.

And as I sat there with my headphones on, I felt someone’s gaze on me. I looked up, scanned the area, and when I saw something, my heart sank. I thought my soul would fly right out of my head from fear.

A man in torn clothes, holding some kind of metal object, was standing there looking at me. The streets were deserted, since the city’s curfew begins at midnight.

He stood there, looking at me, and didn't leave until the taxi arrived. When I heard the notification, I glanced out of the corner of my eye to see where the taxi was, trying not to lose sight of that man for too long.

The taxi was coming from the avenue, and I was coming from the alley, so I had to walk with my back to it, keeping my eyes on it the whole time. When I turned the corner and started running, I heard footsteps. Instinctively, I glanced back, and there was that guy standing right around the corner, staring at me and the taxi driver. I still had my back turned as I approached the car, got in without even checking to see if it was my taxi, and told the driver to go as fast as possible.

I explained to the taxi driver that curfew was coming up, although he probably could tell from my expression that something was wrong. As we drove away, I saw him go through the staff entrance.

I don't know who it is or why, but tomorrow I'll be keeping an eye on all the news, and if anything comes up, I'll post an update... But what scares me is that if I had been late with the taxi, or with the charging and the money, he might have attacked me right then and there...
