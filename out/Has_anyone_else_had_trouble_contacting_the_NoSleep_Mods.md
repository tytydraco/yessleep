So about 20 days ago I messaged the mods through modmail to try and repost a story. Normally, my interactions with the mods have been very prompt. Even if I don’t agree with rulings, they are extremely punctual. However, here we are nearing a month later and I’ve heard nothing from them. I understand that they’re unpaid volunteers and everything, but it just seemed so… I don’t know… out of character?

This isn’t just a weird thing with that post either. There was a while I just thought the message had glitched. However, later I had tried to contact them again about another story and still got nothing. Even when I accidentally broke a rule and the Automod removed my post, no actual moderator messaged me. I found this very strange. Any time I’ve had a post removed, the mods always message me with a detailed explanation on what I did wrong.

I decided to do a little exploring on the site about a week ago, looked around at a lot of the posts that were going up. I found at least 3 that were completely AI generated, the comments even calling them out, and yet nothing was being done. I’m sure you all know this, but AI generated content is not allowed on this site. At this point, I had almost completely forgotten about my story I was trying to repost and just wanted to know what the hell was going on.

Now, I know the rules are very clear that I cannot message the moderators outside of modmail, so that was always going to be a last resort. I also didn’t want to get banned, so I couldn’t just run around the site breaking rules left and right in hopes the mods would come for me. After some deliberation, I decided to try something a little unorthodox. A few months ago, I posted a story on another site where, at the end, the two main characters end up in a movie theater where they have to watch movie forever. I’d then write comments under other stories as those two characters talking about the post like it was a movie they just watched. It was kinda cool, I enjoyed it. However, I eventually got the idea to do the same thing here. Since I was technically still “in character” I wasn’t breaking any rules. However, doing this would certainly get a mod’s attention to ask wtf I was doing.

So I posted it, read a handful of stories and commented larping as the two characters. Can you guess what happened?

Nothing, absolutely nothing happened. No mod reached out asking about the no context jargon I was leaving on other stories. It was just… just dead silence on their end. Only thing that did reach out was the Automod who said one of my comments was flagged.

At this point, I was over it all. They could ban me if they wanted, I was going to reach out. I clicked on a mod’s username, started typing a message and halfway through, stopped. I had a message.

It was the Automod.

“Are you sure you want to do that?”

I stared at the message for a while before the notification floated back outside of my screen. Didn’t really know what to think, besides the obvious: what the fuck?

I closed the text box and opened my notifications.

There was nothing there.

Now, I know I’m not crazy. I saw that message with my own two eyes. It popped up the second I started trying to message the mods. That was weird, but at least now we were getting somewhere. Maybe if I kept going-

Automod: “Just walk away. There is nothing for you here.”

I tried to tap the notification, but it ignored all input and vanished off the edge of the screen. That was the last straw. I reopened the draft and started messaging the mod. Just wrote, “what the fuck is going on?”

The entire time the Automod was messaging me:

“Stop.”

“Stop.”

“Stop.”

“Stop.”

“Stop.”

I sent the message.

They responded instantly.

It was a video. The moment I received it, the Automod’s messages ceased. I took a deep breath and clicked it.

The sound of screaming crackled through my speakers so loud it made me jump in my seat. It was very dark wherever they were, but the flash on the camera showed me tiny pinpricks of the hell they were suffer through. The screen displayed the wet interior of something fleshy and smooth. Not gore, no this looked like those videos you see when doctors shove a camera into someone’s body. Living pink with visible veins poking through a layer of mucus. Slowly, a tiny hand rose into view, the skin on it nearly fully dissolved. It lightly pushed against the fleshy wall’s membrane. I heard several voices, all screaming and crying to let them out. Let them out. Let them-

At once the wall flexed, crashed towards the camera and my screen went black. All I could see was my own reflection in the dark screen. Gasping and shaking in my chair.

I got a message from Automod:

“Rule 7 Reminder

Be sure to contact nosleep mods ONLY through Modmail. To learn more, see Modmail, Removal and Reposts.

BE PATIENT. If NoSleep Mods don't reply to your modmail right away/fast enough for your liking, that doesn't mean you can do whatever you want. NoSleep is a large, active subreddit and you're not the only person with whom Mods are working; they \*will\* respond to you as soon as possible.

And until they do, you can always rely on the Automod to keep the community in check.”
