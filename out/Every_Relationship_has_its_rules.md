Mommy says it’s nothing. But I know there’s something in my closet. I can hear it rustling at night, like it’s trying to get comfortable, sometimes I can hear it breathing. The worst nights are the ones where I can feel it watching me, one bloody red eye peeking out of the slight crack in the closet. Its gaze undressing me, trying to figure out what part of me it should eat first. If only I had known its true intentions.

I’ve asked mom and dad to go into the closet and make it go away but every time they come in it hides, then they leave and it comes back out to watch me again. It was unsettling at first, it still leaves me a little scared, but I’ve learned to deal with it. Sometimes I even consider it a friend or a type of guardian angel, like it’s protecting me from something even worse than itself. 

For years it lived in that closet, as far as I know it never left. We had an unspoken relationship, it stayed in the closet, and I don’t open the doors at night.

Unfortunately, change is unavoidable in life. Despite our protests time always moves forward mercilessly, and arrangements that were previously respected can become outdated or forgotten.  

I was only 13 when I had my first sleepover at my house. I had a group of friends, and we always took turns for whose house we would have a sleepover at. It was my turn to host; I would always find an excuse to switch it to someone else’s house but this time I couldn’t. 

I had four friends, Gary, James, Lenny, and Jarrod. We were inseparable, and we would always be getting into some sort of mischief. Rules weren’t something that we liked to follow most of the time. But if they were staying over at my house, I had to tell them to follow one specific rule. The one thing I have been doing since I was a child.

“There’s only one rule I need you guys to follow. When it goes past 10, we can’t open the closet doors.” I said while twiddling my fingers and pinching myself. 

“Why?” Gary asked, trying to hold back a laugh. “Afraid were gonna find your diary?”

The other guys started laughing. I chuckled nervously and thought to myself, maybe things will be okay, maybe it won’t be there tonight if other people are here. 

“No” I stated sheepishly, “My parents just have some weird rules. Look just promise me you’ll keep the doors closed.” 

“Fine, I promise.” Gary said.

I looked at the other three.

“We promise.” James and Lenny said in unison.

“Jarrod?” I looked at him skeptically. 

“I won’t make any promises, but if it’s so important to you, then I’ll try my best.” Jarrod said with a devilish grin across his face. 

“I’m serious Jarrod! Just promise me.”

“Fine, I won’t open the closet door after 10.” Something laid disguised behind his words. 

The day went as planned, we spent the whole day outside playing basketball, swimming, running around. The morning sun followed us across the sky as it went from one side of the world to the next. The sky was peach colored, and the bright orange ball was hiding behind the mountainous horizon when my mother called us inside for dinner. 

We were having sausage and rice for dinner; we all grabbed our plates and sat at the dinner table talking about our days and what me and my friends had planned for the night. 

Then Jarrod asked, “So what’s with the no opening the closet doors after 10 rule?”

I shot daggers at him. My parents looked at each other confused then looked at me and they began to laugh a bit. The boys shot glances back and forth between each other and me. 

“Still afraid of the monster in your closet?” My father asked me with a big grin on his face. He and my mother began to laugh, and my friends shortly followed. 

“Ever since Alex was young, he would ask us to look in his closet to see if there were any monsters in his closet.”  his mom stated, “What was the thing called again?”

My face was red and I was staring intensely at my food; I wouldn’t dare look up at all of them laughing at me. “The Watcher.” 

The rest of the dinner turned into everyone making fun of me and laughing at old scars that hadn’t fully healed yet. I was embarrassed but thankfully Gary changed the subject to something about basketball and that was what we talked about for the rest of dinner. 

After dinner we went down to my basement. It was a comfortable basement, the floors were covered in a lemony colored carpet, the walls were painted eggshell white. The white panels of the ceiling matched the walls. The basement was a rectangle with the stairs on the side of it. One end had a TV and a couch facing it, the other end had a single closet. The wooden closet doors were unpainted, but the wood had a dark walnut stain applied to it. 

We laid out our sleeping bags in the middle of the basement and then all piled onto the couch to watch a movie on the TV. By the time the movie finished it was almost 10. The boys started getting ready for bed and I remember getting into my sleeping bag. I looked around and saw the other boys all had grins on their face.

“What’s going…” I started before Jarrod yelled “NOW!”

The other boys leapt on me and held me down. No matter how much I struggled I couldn’t move.

“It’s time to face your fears.” Said Jarrod, smiling so much his face might be stuck like that. 

The boys picked me up and began to drag me to the closet. 

“Please stop!” I shrieked. Tears were rolling down my face. No matter how much I struggled they kept pulling me. 

Jarrod stood by the closet door, looked at the clock and said, “Stop It’s 10 now.”

The boys stopped dragging and began to laugh. They still kept their iron grip on me, refusing to let me go. It felt like an eternity as they held me there. I had no idea what they were waiting for.

Still watching the clock, Jarrod said “Now it’s 10:05. I didn’t promise I wouldn’t open it past 10:05.”  His smile even wider now. He pulled open the closet door, and the boys began to drag me in.

In a last-ditch attempt to break free, I managed to kick my right leg free, Lenny went to grab it, and I kicked him in his chest, sending him stumbling backwards into the closet. The other boys began to laugh, and I was able to break free from them and ran back to other side of the room. 

The boys were rolling on the floor with tears streaming down their faces as they laughed. Finally, Jarrod said, “Okay, come on Lenny, get out of the closet.”

There was no response. The laughing slowly died and the boys slowly sat up as they watched the closet. A thick darkness resided in the closet, not even the light from the TV could penetrate it. 

“Very funny, Lenny but you can come out now.” There was a tinge of fear in Jarrod’s voice. It was starting to become evident that something was off. We all stared into the void hoping that Lenny would be playing a prank on them and come out. But there was nothing but an uncomfortable silence coming from the closet. 

We were paralyzed, unable to shift our gaze from it. A single moment felt like years, and an uncomfortable truth began to set in on us as we began to realize something unfathomably wrong was occurring. 

“Someone needs to go in there and get him.” Gary said.

“Do you volunteer?” Jarrod replied sarcastically.

“We need to get Alex’s parents!” James said frantically.

I was curled up in a ball in the corner, listening to them argue. I had goosebumps and was shaking viciously. 

The three of them went back and forth on what to do for a few minutes before being interrupted by an accursed sound. An insidious laughter could be heard from the closet; it was deep, slow, and reverberated around the room. The boys cautiously turned to the closet only to see an eyeball roll out from the closet leaving a trail of blood. 

A blood-red eyeball emerged from darkness and stared at us. There was an indescribable intensity to its penetrating gaze. It slowly turned towards each of us, one-by-one, taking in our whole beings. That damned laugh started again as the other boys began to back up towards the other side of the room. 

Long fingers stretched out of the darkness gripping the closet doors. The fingernails were as sharp as daggers, they scratched the closet doors as the long, thin, fingers began to grip them. A large lanky figure began to step out of the closet, the red eye becoming taller and taller with each passing second. The TV light bounced off its malformed face, it looked like a pile of mashed potatoes. It had a large mouth that seemed to stretch from one ear to the other. It was covered in blood. It was missing an eye; with its one good eye it scanned the room. When it was fully out of the closet it almost touched the ceiling of the room. The lanky figure looked like a skeleton with exaggerated features. 

The figure stumbled slowly towards Jarrod and reached its long stringy arms out towards him. A twisted smile stretched across the monster’s face, its mouth opened. It was filled with multiple rows of sharp teeth that seemed to have no end. The teeth weren’t even the worst part. It was the long pointy tongue that stretched out from its mouth. The tongue was so long it barely fit in his mouth. It hung down the side with drops of saliva falling down towards the floor.  

The lanky figure crouched down on its skinny legs and embraced Jarrod. It licked his face with its long tongue. Within an instant the creature wrapped its entire mouth around Jarrod’s head and ate it like a grape. 

Gary, James and I piled into the farthest corner of the room as we watched it chew on Jarrod. It stuck its dagger like fingernails into its mouth and picked out a piece of his skull and examined it before flinging it to the side. The monster moved backwards towards the closet and threw the remaining part of his body into it. 

We were shivering in the corner of the room with the TV light illuminating a small path. James ran and tried to get upstairs by going past the monster while it was distracted. Despite its slow movements, the thing was lightning fast. It wrapped its hand around James’s leg as he began to go up the stairs. It pulled him down with such force he broke his nose on the steps and blood began to run down his face. It pulled him into the center of the room and held him down on the ground smiling at him with a demonic grin. Its eye shifting up and down not sure where to start its mutilation.

James screamed and began to cry. The monster stuck its fingers into his mouth and ripped out his tongue; it played with the appendage for a little before casting it aside. There were only the slightest sounds of James gurgling as blood began to squirt out of his mouth like a fountain. It then used its fingers to pluck out James’s eyes like kebob and ate them one by one. 

Poor James was still alive as the creature began to exact its undeserved cruelty on him. It cut open his chest with its long fingernails and began to slowly eat his intestines like spaghetti. I turned away and could only hear James’s kicking and gurgling for minutes before the sounds faded. 

It was just me and Gary as we hugged each other. Shaking as we listened to the creature chomp on our dead friend. The noise stopped and we saw the monstrosity crawling on all four of its limbs towards us. Heavy plops were heard as the creature slowly rounded the couch, its blood red eye looking directly at us as we tried to phase into the wall to avoid it. The sadistic smile still on its face as it crawled over to us. Its tongue hanging out its mouth like a dog panting on a hot day. 

The creature lunged at us; me and Gary jumped in different directions. The monster pinned me in the corner and Gary tried to make it up the stairs like James. He was a stair away from the door before it grabbed him and held him over its head. Legs in one hand and arms in another. The monster pulled Gary in opposite directions until he was split in half. 

I knew that I was next. When it was done with Gary, it turned towards me and smiled. It crawled towards me like an ape and stopped right in front of me.

“B..b..but I foll..followed the rules… I kept the door closed.” I managed to spit out.

The Watcher tilted its head and laughed its horrendous laugh, the sound piercing my ear drums from this distance, it patted me on the head before grabbing my ankle and dragging me into the closet with it. 

My parents never knew what happened to me, neither did the other parents with their children. Everyone assumed my parents did it but with a lack of any evidence they couldn’t be sent to jail. Eventually they sold the house and moved somewhere far away.

But I stayed here. New families came and left. Some with children, some without. Some saw me, some didn’t. It was like I said earlier, there is an unspoken relationship sometimes. Despite how much time passes certain rules must be followed. So now I watch to make sure no one breaks those rules otherwise consequences will follow. 

Maybe it’s selfish but I’m beginning to like it when they break the rules, I still have a lot to learn from the watcher but next time he’s going to take me with him, I can feel it.
