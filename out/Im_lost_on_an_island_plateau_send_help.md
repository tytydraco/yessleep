He didn’t land right.

 

It was a normal guy. Just some man who tried jumping me, was probably hoping to break his fall by landing on me, but ended up hitting the ground when I evaded him. His right leg was completely ruined.

Just… Why…?

 

I remember his screams… Those ugly screams.

He shouted at me, “You b\*tch! YOU B\*TCH!” and made other… threats. So vulgar, no class.

I also noticed a… bulge in his pants… So, I took a wide berth around him and kept walking.

To those wondering “Why didn’t you finish him off by keying him in the neck?”

This man had an average, but fit build… Meaning, if he grabbed me, even with his broken leg, he could’ve easily overpowered me… It wasn’t worth the risk. So, I left him. He can’t come after me anyways… Not with a leg like that.

 

After I’d traveled about half a kilometer, although I could still hear his groans and shouts echoing, he’d quieted down a bit. Until he yelped really loudly once more… and silence returned to the forest.

I took my shoes off again and walked with my double layered socks.

Guess he drew the monster in with his screaming… So, I decided to go back to being as quiet as possible. Maybe it was satisfied with that kill and wouldn’t come after me…

Things went well for a while, went a whole 3 kilometers with no interruptions. I was walking, reciting Ayat al Kursi in my head, doing pretty well given the circumstances…

Then, I looked back… and kept walking.

It took me a few seconds but, it finally registered and I looked back again… The lamps along the path, behind me, were going out… Only half a kilometer behind me was lit.

The path I was on went up a little incline, so I was able to see the path back down behind me.

When I was looking back, another light quietly went dark.

I looked up and noticed the radio tower’s red light was also out. I felt my eyelids widen…

I put my shoes back on and started jogging.

I had no idea what was turning the lights off or why, but I knew getting caught in the darkness would probably be bad.

And I still had 2 kilometers to go…

Now, I may have noodle arms, but I’ve got cardio. I could definitely jog 2 kilometers… But, the issue was, how fast were those lights going out behind me? Could it outpace me?

I decided I’d keep a healthy, non-exhausting jog.

If the lights started going out got too close? THAT’S when I’d run or sprint for the exit.

As I jogged, I occasionally looked back, to make sure the extinguishing lights weren’t catching up… I was keeping ahead of the darkening lights but, after another kilometer of jogging, I noticed something... It made me stop and turn around briefly.

In the darkness, I saw… 7? 8? It was so dark, hard to tell how many… I saw many thin, lanky, 50-60 meter tall humanoid shapes. They walked slowly and more frighteningly, completely silently.

I’m not even sure “walking” is the right word… They were certainly moving, and their movement resulted in forward motion but… It was like staring into the dark for too long, and when you start to see things, and the things you see don’t make sense. They were kinda like that…

 

I turned and started to run.

Final stretch… I gunned it for the top of the incline.

After reaching the top of the forest path and starting to descend, I slowed back down to a jog. Gravity would be helping my speed here and I could conserve energy.

I looked over my shoulder briefly after cresting the small hill. They weren’t going as fast as I thought they’d be, given how tall they were. It’s as though they were only walking within the dark places… Like they couldn’t step into light.

 

Once I was quickly descending the hill, I saw what I presumed was the exit just ahead… Half a kilometer away… The concrete foot path widened into a road, and at the end of the road was a dark, stone, road tunnel… Just like the one that took me to this dark plateau to begin with.

 

Final stretch… This was surely the way out, I thought. I gunned it for the entrance… and then slowed down as I reached the mouth of the tunnel.

I turned around again, looked up the incline, and after the light on the top of the hill flickered off… No more lights turned off after it.

I waited…

Watched…

My exit was right there but… I was overcome with this curiosity…

5 minutes and the remaining lights stayed on. There were also no signs of the giants. I looked around, scanning the area, nothing.

And so, we come to where I am now… Here, in the mouth of the tunnel, sat down.

So, why am I sitting here? The lights ceasing to turn off is giving me pause…

Why did they stop coming after me?

This is… probably the way out.

Maybe they’re trying to trick me. Thinking that if they make it easy for me to escape, I’ll get paranoid and think it’s a reverse psychology trick… That I’ll think, “This was too easy, the lights have stopped going off, this must be a trick. I’m NOT going to enter this tunnel! It’s probably a trap!”

… No… No, I learned my lesson back at the hotel… This must be the way out and I’m TAKING IT… I’m not going to overthink this again.

Besides, I outran the closing lights. They know they can’t catch me, so now they’re trying to trick me into turning back and running into them.

…

A light at the top of the hill just turned back on.

No… If I go back to have a look, they’ll probably just shut the lights off once I’m under it.

…

I’m going to hit “upload” now and walk into the tunnel…

Make dua for me.

 

I’m still nervous…
