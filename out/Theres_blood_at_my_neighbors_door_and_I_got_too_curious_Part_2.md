I think I might be okay now, however, I’m not totally sure. I’m in the hospital now. They’re taking good care of me. I’ve had some of the police come and try to ask me about the situation. I could only vomit.



Yesterday was a rollercoaster, terrifying and traumatic. When I woke up after getting bitten, his instructions and commands were ingrained into my mind. But here’s what happened. I woke up around noon, the sun was beige and dull like a stale soda. It was a harsh light that brought no warmth. Again I was in my living room in the same spot where my neighbor had assaulted me. Bruises, scratches and small stains of blood scattered and dappled the skin all over my body. I rubbed my hands across my face. Everything felt clammy and feverish. My fingers ran through my hair and I winced when they found a deep scratch covered with dried and coagulated blood that matted the hair on my scalp. The underside of my forearm came into vision and I looked at the spot where he sank his jaws into my flesh. There was nothing. No bite mark, no scratch, no blood, bruises or anything. My face scrunched as I began to feel the need to sob in my chest. I felt disgusted. I needed to shower, no I needed to kill that motherfucker, no I needed to call the police. So many thoughts raced through my head and none did anything to overcome my situation or soothe my electric nerves. I breathed in once deeply, and I broke down. As my moans and sobs reduced me to a ball, scrunched and sorrowful, I began to lose my senses. My crying turned to visceral screams that ripped my vocal chords.



“Good afternoon Micah.”



My screaming continued but my emotions turned from self destructive anguish to terror that was base and primal. I turned to my neighbors voice and there he was, sitting on my couch slumped deep into the seating, his long legs outstretched into a wide spread. His head, bald on the top with long wispy frayed gray-white hair splayed from his scalp like cobwebs on decrepit furniture. His piercing eyes glared through me like a predator. Blood coated his mouth and nose like a lion who had just sunken his whole face into the abdomen of a fresh kill. It dribbled down his chin onto a white t-shirt tucked into faded jeans with boots tucked into his pant legs. He let out a deep sigh as his eyes widened at me.  



“You scream but you know that does nothing.”



He said this with absolute certainty. His tone sent waves of fear through my body and a stiff nervousness to the bottom of my gut but I stopped screaming. I sat up straight and he did as I did. In a strange and terrifying way he was either mocking me or matching my movements so he would always have the upper hand. 



“You should know I detest you. You fucking make me sick.” He licked his lips with his red tongue.



I was shaking and I felt my skin was already pale but now I was feeling faint. 



“I’m going to devour you. It’s what I was born to do.” He stood from his seat and lumbered over to me. “You’re scared, that makes your flesh all the more tender.” He began to breathe heavily in and out through his gory mouth. With every rise and fall of his chest there was a palpable violent energy flowing and growing within him. He stared blankly for moments that seemed like minutes and I felt like I was going to die.



“And yet I can’t. Not me at least. It’s time you met Charles.” His face relaxed and his shoulders were released from his intensity. “Follow me.” He moved at a swift pace with heavy thumps of his boots on the floor. I couldn’t move. My heart thumping ceaselessly in my ears and out of my chest. My shaky muscles could barely hold my own weight. He placed his hand on the doorknob of the front door and without looking at me he said like an impatient parent, “Micah… are you going to get up?”  I simply couldn’t. 



“And what makes you think I won’t just kill you here and now for making me wait?”



I scuttled to my feet after tripping over myself. 



“Good.”



He walked outside and I followed him. With confident strides, he made his way to his door. I shuffled behind him without a singular thought in my head.



As I stepped inside, a foul smell bombarded my nose and made my eyes water. His apartment was the same as mine. A small and pathetic single room studio. Red blood and viscera swathed over every appliance, wall, and cabinet like a botched and unfinished painting job. There was one bed and one chair, both piled on with a random assortment of clothing in different states of distress and damage. One wall was covered end to end in missing persons posters and article cut outs.



“Oh dear god- I-”



My eyes feasted on something truly terrible. A pile of bodies meticulously stacked to the ceiling in the bathroom. They had to fill up the whole bathroom. 



“I ripped the door off  its hinges to make space.” The neighbor said with a proud bravado. He tilted his head in my direction. “You’re gonna join em soon… but not for a few days I’ve decided. He needs more time.”



My head shakily glared in his direction, “Wh- what?” 



“Charles get down here!” He yelled. 



Down? I looked and a dark square hole had been cut from the ceiling. Then unsteadily and with a loud thunk, a short slender man fell from the hole and cried out once he hit the floor. The blood soaked carpet squelched when he writhed on top of it. 



“Micah, meet Charles. He’s going to eat you.” 

  


Charles rolled over on his back and I saw an emaciated man. I could smell his own soilage on him. Sores and blisters covered his skin head to toe. His hollow face and sunken eyes peered up at me. A despair I had never been witness to was on display with this man. 



“Now then, both of you up the hole.”



The neighbor slid a small ladder from under the bed. It too was covered in blood. Charles hurriedly climbed the ladder and clambered into the hole. I did the same with trembling hands and feet. I could hear the ladder rattling under my feet. I didn’t really know how to climb into the hole from the top of the ladder. When I tried to lift myself, my feet wavered and the ladder gave out from under me. My fingers strained against the plywood beam in the ceiling I was latched onto. My muscles gave way with a yelp. Right before I fell to the floor, Charles grabbed onto my arm with his hands and saved me. He hoisted me into the ceiling. The neighbor gave me an approving grunt. 



“By the way, my name is Monte. You will not address me, only I will address you.”  The neighbor said again in his authoritative tone. He waved his hand to the side and *shunk!* The hole closed and with it all light had recessed from our space. “I’ll be back soon, don't worry, I’ll know if you try anything.” He said albeit muffled by the drywall and plywood separating us. 



I could hear the door to the apartment open and shut with a slam. 



Where we were wasn’t really an attic, hell you could barely call it a crawlspace. There wasn’t even enough room for us to sit up. I was lying on my side just waiting and sweating. It was hot here. And I could smell the stench of not only the rot of this godforsaken apartment but also from Charles. There was no light and I could only hear the ruminant hum of A/C ventilation and his shaky breathing. After some amount of time had passed he finally spoke.



“I’ve- I’ve got something that will get us out. I’ve been waiting for this for a long time.” Then I heard the familiar flick of a lighter. “I stole it off some bloke that died here.” His thick cockney accent sounded out with a dried raspy voice. His treacherous breath was sickening. However, thank god for Charles. The lighter finally came to life. Our only hope was now illuminating Charles. His face had gone from being bleak with the washed out eyes of a lost soul to having a fiery passion for life. For the first time over the past day or so, I was finally hopeful.



“So what’s the plan?” I said with little hesitation.



“We’re gonna torch this place. The hole won’t open. Not without h-” I could see he began to gag. “Sorry it’s that- you know.”



It’s the spell or curse he put on me or whatever he did by doing that weird thing by grabbing my uvula. 



“Everyone has it.” 



“I know…I- I do.”



He picked the conversation back up after a brief pause of solidarity. “Anyways, we’re gonna burn this motherfucker down. So I’m gonna go down first. That's the routine.” He breathed in nervously. “You’ve gotta light it and use it on this. It’s some shit I picked up from some rats that nested here not too long ago.” He rolled over with difficulty and back over again and revealed a bundle of straw and detritus. “This’ll catch quick.” As soon as it does we run.”



“How do you know we won't be killed?”



“We’re far too valuable. It’s either we run or you die here and I get transformed into one of the pets.” 



“What do you mean pets?” I said anxiously.



“It’s best if you didn’t know… so are you down?”



“Yes.”



“Good. Now we wait.” 



And so we did. For a long time. I know I dozed off at some point despite my sweltering cramped residency. I awoke to his voice. 



“Micah, Charles. I’m home. I’ve been on a good hunt. Now then, Charles come down and display for Micah how we *clean* my kills.” 



The door swiftly opened up from under me and I fell to the floor from the hole. The plan was already getting turned upside down. 



“Gah! You fool. Stay down here then. Charles do hurry, I don’t want her getting cold.”



I lifted my aching head from the fall and saw a headless woman’s body being undressed by Monte. Blood was still pouring from the head to the floor pooling into the carpet darkening its already crimson color. I peered up at Charles. He was lighting the bundle as I looked up. I watched it catch light. I suppose Monte couldn’t hear it because of his bloodlust. He was taking his nail and pressing it in between the layers of skin to separate it from the fat and flesh. His face exclaimed his total enrapturement with his brutal task. Charles hid the small fire upstairs and we hoped for the best. He climbed down and he shuffled over to the body. He began holding the skin for Monte as he separated it from the body. It made me unimaginably sick. I threw up onto the floor and started to cry hard again. Who was that woman? Why her? Why bring her here? 



“I need more chow for my pups. But this one is could make an excellent stew, I mean, look at the size of those thighs!” He exclaimed with evil joy.



I couldn’t take any more of this. I looked up and the fire had grown to a small blaze and now smoke began pouring into the ceiling. 



*Sniff sniff* “What’s that I smell?” Monte tilted his head up and his eyes widened when he saw the smoke and he screamed when he saw the fire. Both in worry and in terror. Then his fear turned to fury as he glared at me.



“I knew I should’ve locked you in the body room!” He stomped his way towards me and before Monte’s already outstretched hand could grab me Charles rushed first. 



Charles shoved me into the poster wall but instead of brick or dry wall I fell through glass. The posters had hidden and blocked out the sunlight from a window. Shards of glass pierced my back and passed all around me with the shreds of the missing and dead. My hands reached for anything, but it was met with the cold grasp of the frigid air. My heart fell into my stomach with a deadly weight of despair and fear. My desperate gaze first met Charles’s face as he peered out the hidden window, which was filled with abject terror. Monte then speared him with his bare hand. Hot blood steaming and beading into sparkling clusters spewed into the air from his chest. The red gush was like paint spilling onto the black starless canvas of the sky. His long nails and palm were coated in the red and fleshy collective of Charles’s innards. The empty sky with its red blush was strangely beautiful. I wondered if this was truly what the gate to hell looked like. I plummeted to the ground with a flat slam that wracked every bone, muscle, and fiber in my body. My soul was  broken and lost- no, it is broken and lost; and now my body literally befell the same fate. Charles’s body fell soundless beside me with blood splattering across my face. My vision darkened from red to blackness as I felt every nerve in my body explode into tiny oblivions over and over again until I too, fell into the oblivion of unconsciousness

I woke up in the hospital. and I’m writing all this from my bed. There’s no sign of Monte. There’s no sign of danger. This might be my ticket out. I don’t know who or what the hell he is but I hope the fucker is dead.
