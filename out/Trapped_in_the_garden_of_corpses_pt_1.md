Good evening, hope yours is well because mine is not. I have been working for the Morris family for many years and yet one day they vanished, I awoke to find no one not even the old matriarch madame Sarah.

Then thats when she came, apparently madame Sarah had a cousin who's daughter had bought the house from them, according to the solicitor the Morris family had debts and fled to America; the land of the free and a fresh start for a vagabond.

I remember sitting at the desk with the Solicitor, A mister I.P Freely such a peculiar name indeed. Freely was a slight man with a balding head and a pointed nose, like a little rat, his voice however was akin to a lion.

I am not a particularly big man, I stand at only 5'9 but im broad and I am from all my years working here as both butler and gardener, this man was far smaller than I and yet i was afraid of him. The family solicitor was a man called Clark Clerkson who had quit due to stress and this man was his replacement.

As we sat in the old but clean, I take pride in my work, office of brown walls and furniture with the emerald green upholstery and the shelves where the family kept all their heirlooms the solicitor looked through his notes "good news for you Mr Dombrowski, you have been hired to stay as Gardner of the estate" his words brought me joy, a man of my stature in society would never find another job like this, but he continued "you must vacate the manor and move into the old shed out back, for the countess does not want you in her abode" he said commanding.

I felt saddened i had lived in the servants quarters for twenty years, my room was a comfort to me the only space that was mine, as I drifted into thought I could feel the rat-like man stare at me and came back "yes I understand an-" but before I could ask any question i was handed my orders in a little book.

It was a small tome of brown switched leather "within this are your orders, no man shall enter the manor house from this day forth, both you and I have 2 hours to leave before the lady of the manor arrives, am I clear?" His words brought me panic and I nodded profusely, shaking his cold boney hand "good god you are a sweaty man" he growled at me wiping the sweat on his tissue.

That was it I had moved my things into the shed, it did have a reasonable living quarters so it was doable. I awaited to be called to meet the countess. It wasnt until nighttime around 7 o'clock when the carriage came. I arrived to meet them, the carriage stopped and a figure dressed in black with a top hat was the driver, I couldn't see his face but his hands were like that of a skeleton but my minds was playing tricks I am sure of it.

Two attractive you women walked around from the other side, one was dressed in a kimono clearly a woman from the far east and the other was dressed as any servant from the time would be in modest black clothing, they opened the carriage door and a tall, voluptuous woman walked out; I panicked. 

The countess was breathtaking but there was no colour in her skin, she wore a long black form fitting gown and a fur coat to match, diamond and others precious gems covered her attire, she looked at me with a dismissive look, her eyes were red definitely no mistaking, she smiled at me and my blood ran cold "keepnthe grounds nice for me and do as I command and you will keep this job for as long as you want" she then walked past me.

I was stuck on the spot for at least a good half hour, the carriage been stabled and the maids had taken the luggage inside, I couldn't move, there was a miasma that littered the grounds, something wasnt right about this place, it wasnt the weather for it and yet the fog that rolled in wasnt... right.

Once I gathered my wits and realised how ridiculous I had looked, I went to work: first I checked the stables and found no sign of the horses or the rider, only a black bag sitting on the seat and a top hat on top. Secondly, I went to look around and make sure everything was as it should be, the plants had started to fade and were brown. Impossible I had taken care of them this morning.

The third thing I did that day was go to bed and rest, the nightmares I had were that of a horror novella, giant bats and ghouls and demons. The next day the plants were gone and one of the two servants had come to me, she had changed put of her kimono and was now dressed in a typical attire of a servants in England. She was quiet simply handing me a note, what it said made me confused, it said "read the book" and " go to the nearest cemetery for fertiliser" such a strange thing but before I could ask the servant ran off.

I decided to consult the book, inside was a number of rules and regulations:

1.the mistress is always right amd must not be defied

2.any screams from the manor must be ignored

3.women are the only guests allowed NO MEN

4.no religious items are allowed 

The list went on and on and by the 50th rule I learned what my job was, go to the cemetery and dig up a corpse, bury it in the garden NO PLANTS.

I was confused and I laughed it off, this woman is a strange one. I want to the bathroom laughing to myself, as I stood there taking off my belt to use the toilet I saw it in the mirror a woman's face covered in blood, she was naked and terrified looking. I jumped back as she screamed at me to help her, I panicked and grabbed the mirror throwing it out the window.

I cried, a grown man of forty years I cried hard. What was going on, I need to escape I need to leave before its to late. I grabbed everything together, all of my possessions and ran towards the gate, the estate was big and from the manor to the entrance was a good 20 minute walk, I ran which shortened it. 

As I ran i found myself follwed by crows and cats that watched my every move, upon noticing them I galloped like a two legged horse and reached the closed gate, there was no escape, I could see it the walls of greenery were to high to climb, I was trapped amd the cats and crows awaited for me to return to duty.
