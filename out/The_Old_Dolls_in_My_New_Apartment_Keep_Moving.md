I just moved into a new apartment last month. It’s a pretty old building actually built around the 1920s I think. My last place was too small so I was excited for the extra space. This apartment came partially furnished which was a nice bonus for my budget. Most of the furniture is vintage probably from the original tenants. There are these two old dolls on a shelf in the living room. They’re porcelain with cracked faces and glass eyes. A bit creepy but I figured it was part of the charm. I planned to put them away eventually but I’ve been busy unpacking. 

  
The first week was fine. Normal new apartment stuff. The floors creak a lot but that’s expected in an old building. I noticed the dolls had shifted a little after a few days. Their heads were turned slightly more towards the center of the room. I thought maybe I bumped the shelf while cleaning. I just shrugged and straightened them out. 

  
Then it happened again. About a week later I came home from work and one of the dolls had its arm positioned differently. Not just a little wiggle. It was distinctly bent at the elbow. I live alone so I know it wasn’t me. My heart started doing a little jig in my chest. I stared at it for a long time. Was I losing my mind? I carefully moved it back to its original position. I even took a mental picture of how they were placed. 

  
I was extra vigilant after that. I’d check them every morning before leaving. Every evening when I returned. For a few days nothing happened. I started to relax. Maybe it was just my imagination or settling of the building. Then I got a call from my sister asking me to babysit her kids. I was gone for two nights. When I came back the dolls were facing each other. Their faces were practically touching. 

  
I almost screamed. It was unsettling beyond words. It wasn’t a small shift anymore. This was deliberate. Someone or something was moving these dolls. I felt a cold dread settle over me. I tried to convince myself it was a prank. But who would prank me in my own apartment? I hadn’t given anyone a spare key. 

  
I decided to move them. I picked them up one by one. They felt cold. Much colder than the room temperature. I put them in a box in my closet. I taped the box shut just to be sure. I didn’t want to see those glassy eyes anymore. 

  
For a couple of weeks the apartment felt normal again. I still checked the closet door occasionally. Just to make sure the tape was intact. It always was. I started to think I had overreacted. Maybe the old building just had some weird vibrations. I was feeling a bit foolish about my earlier panic. 

  
Then last night I woke up to a faint tapping sound. It was coming from the living room. My heart started to race again. I told myself it was probably just the pipes. Old buildings you know. The tapping continued though. It had a rhythm to it. Tap tap pause tap tap. 

  
I finally crept out of bed. I grabbed my phone and turned on the flashlight. The tapping stopped the moment I entered the living room. Everything was still. The shadows played tricks on my eyes. I was about to turn back when I saw it. 

  
On the coffee table right in the center of the room were the two dolls. They were sitting side by side. Their faces were turned towards my bedroom door. One of them had a tiny porcelain hand raised. As if it had been knocking. The box in my closet was open. The tape was cleanly cut. I don’t know what to do. I can’t sleep. I feel like they’re watching me right now.
