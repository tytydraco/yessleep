Yesterday was a weird day, so weird I had to move into a new place in just one day. Here's what happened yesterday.

Strolling around the living room, my eyes land on this book on my bookshelf that I couldn't remember keeping there. *Weird, did dad keep it here?*

I opened the book to inspect it, the words seemed unfamiliar but not strange. *Did I pick it up from the bookshop haul last weekend?* The corners look ragged though, as if someone had torn slight shreds because of anxiety. I opened the first page to see a handwritten note, elegant and pretty words, ‘To my love, Goodbye.’ *Mom? Did she write this? to dad?*

Tears fill up my eyes. *Mom died a week ago.* 

*She fell from the stairs, that's what I was told at least*. I wasn't home when it happened.

I instinctively flipped through the pages and my eye caught a glossy piece of paper placed carefully in between the yellowish pages of the book.

I took it out, a photograph… it's me, standing between mom and dad in the living room, smiling at the camera.I stare at the image. *Good old times*. A sob ripples through my throat. *God, I miss her*. I shake my head to clear my thoughts and am about to put it back when I instinctively turn a few pages and see a white envelope peeking through the pages. I open it only to find a ward of cash sitting there, crisp 100s, about 7 of them.  

My heartbeat accelerates. Why is there a ward of cash hidden in a book with the last picture we took together as a family? *Something feels off.*

*Thud*.

The noise is so loud it jolts my entire body into a state of alertness, adrenaline pumping through each and every tiny vein of my body. I keep the book on the small coffee table beside the shelf and tiptoe towards the source of the thud, the garage door. 

I don't know from where did I pick the  knife but I did, and headed closer to the garage.

I approach the garage door and open it slowly, ready to stab whoever is in there. No one. Not even a cat that I could blame, just eerie silence. The window is locked from inside and so is the other door. I grip the knife harder and keep looking around but nothing. *Empty. Alone.*

A breath escapes my mouth. I don't know if it's relief or confusion but I turn around to go back inside the house, still peering back occasionally until I shut the door close.

I go back inside the living area only to notice another strange thing. The book is gone.

*Another thud*. From the store room this time.

*Shit someone is inside the house.*

I rush towards my bedroom and lock the door once I am inside. *Dad? I need to call him*. My eyes catch a glimpse of something under the bed. A small cardboard box, kind of like a delivery box. *Gosh! It's heavy.*

I open it to find a stack of letters, all in mom's handwriting. Loads and loads of letters that are all seemingly familiar but also fairly new to read at the same time. All in mom's handwriting, telling me how much she loves me and she was scared, saying she was sorry for leaving me alone with dad.

*But she did not leave. She fell*.

The ink on these letters is smudged. Something about them is familiar. *When did I put these here? Did I read these before?*

SHUT UP!!

I scream at noone in particular. *So noisy*. My brain is so noisy, it hurts. I hear shuffling outside, in the living room, *again*. 

*Someone's definitely outside*. I am scared. I don't know what seems louder in this moment, the thumping of my heart or the creaking of the wooden floor outside.

The creaking sounds careful, almost deliberate. I grab the knife I had kept aside when entering the bedroom, taking small steps towards the door, my eyes glance back once at the letters on the bed as I open the door.

The door opened in an instant and I feel a gust of air against my face as if someone’s moving in front of me, way too fast. I shut my eyes and swing the knife. It hits something, *barely*.

I am too scared to open my eyes and keep jamming the knife forward. 

*My mind fills with flashes of red.*

The hit seems to have taken all my energy, my body feels heavy, so rigid. It fills me with dread. I try but I can't pull the knife back and something about it’s weight feels awfully familiar. Eerie silence engulfs me and I slowly open my eyes.

*No person. No movement. No noise*.

But there's blood on my knife. It's stuck in a wooden frame. *Was the blood there this whole time?* 

My eyes travel across the entire living area. I suddenly feel the contents of my stomach rise up. My forehead is covered in thick sweat beads. My lungs are on fire as if I am breathing underwater.My eyes start to water as the taste of bile fills my mouth. I rush to the closest sink, in the kitchen, emptying the contents of my stomach.

*Rinse. Spit*. I keep cleaning my mouth to get rid of the taste, then I hear a distant shrill sound through the window. *Sirens, blue and red lights*, getting louder and louder. I see a reflection of myself in the window. *Red*. A splatter all over my face.

I frantically wipe it off. Blood. I have blood on my face. I might have cut myself with the knife. My eyes travel to the knife on the kitchen counter, same red blood. 

*It doesn't hurt though.*

I go towards the store room across the kitchen. I need first aid. The door opens with a click and a sharp smell fills my nostrils. It's rotten, rancid with a sweet undertone to it that makes it better somehow. I don't know why people can't stand this smell. I stare at the source for some time before calling out. 

*Dad?*

He doesn't move, still as a rock on the cold floor. Mom's book laying open beside him, covered in red, the picture sitting in the middle of the room, a large splatter on it covering everyone's face. The envelope is gone though. I pick up the picture, ‘*there you are*’, and wipe the red liquid with my sleeve. I walk  past dad to get to the first aid kit. 

In the living room I sat on the sofa, tending to myself as the shrill noise outside grows closer and closer.

*Thud. Again.*

I grab the knife again as I move towards the door.

I remember nothing after that. No pain, no noise but my wrists have red marks on them. *Did I stab the person trying to break in? Did he attack me?*

I am sitting in my new room now. The people here are nice, but they only like to wear white. They know my name. I came here yesterday, after someone broke into my place. This morning, I got into a fight with one of them when he said it's been weeks. 

Since then, they talk less, only when asking about dad or mom. They keep their heads low and scribble in their tiny notebooks, all day long. *Boring*
