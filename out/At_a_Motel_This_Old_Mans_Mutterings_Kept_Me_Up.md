I was driving back from college for the holidays when I decided to stop in for the night at this motel. I go to college a couple states away, so it takes about two days driving to get back. This motel was in a terrible state, like total disrepair. It looked as though whoever owned the place didn't have any intent in repairing it; that they would rather keep it open taking all the dollars they could until one day the structure would cave in and they would just pack up and go.  I initially considered moving on and trying my luck at this other place another two hours down the road, but my exhaustion got the better of me, and I didn't think it was safe to keep driving. 

 

 

This disinterested teen, probably the daughter of the owner, checked me in and handed me the keys. The room was the furthest to the right up on the balcony level and on the way up the stairs I got stuck behind this old guy carrying two bags of ice.  The guy was pretty feeble looking and was clearly struggling so I offered to help, mostly out of agitation than any kind of altruism.  Rather than turning to face me he just sort of sent me an embarrassed look over his shoulder  

"No no that's ok" he said and kept on his way seeming mostly perturbed by my bothering him. 

 

 

Turns out he was in the room to the left of mine so I just kind of awkwardly ambled behind him until we reached the end of the balcony. When we got to our doors, I got my first good look at him as he tried to fish his room key from his back pocket and floundered with the ice bags. He was a relatively short man with a sizable pot belly, despite his thin frame, which popped out from the filthy white vest he was wearing. He looked all round unkempt with this horrid thin hair running down from his scalp over his neck. His eyes were frazzled and gave him this all-round panicked expression. I remember thinking he looked like the kind of guy to keep a box of boutique porn mags in the woods. Aware at how long I had been staring I quickly handled my own keys and went inside, locking the door behind me. 

 

 

Inside the room wasn't much better than the outside. The room was lit by this dim solitary bulb lackadaisically hanging from the roof and every item of furniture seemed to have its own disquieting stain. Most notably there was a slight smell like rotting food that had probably been trampled into the carpet. The fact the smell was only faint seemed to irritate me more; I would soon forget the smell only for it to come back like a wave coming in and out with the tide. The bed itself was stiff and irksome and I laid restlessly for quite some time before I began to drift.  Though soon after as if by fate a low rumble began to emanate from the vent above my head rousing me once more.  The noise was not a continuous drone as one may expect from a fan turning on but rather it seemed to fade in and out with changing pitch. It was a spell before I realized that it was in fact a voice. My bed leaned against the wall adjoining my room and the old man I had met just the moment before, and it seems the vent connected between them. It was another while longer before I could make out what was being said - like I was tuning into a radio station. From what I could make out the man seemed to be talking to himself, a trailing mumble which made it hard to catch onto what he was talking about. 

  


"I've been here too long... where I lay now you lay and now I lay dead... I am not myself... If a man is not his mind then where is the man... I feel now... Every twist of muscle... Every creak of bone... Every pump of blood... If I am here and there in equal measure then what is here and what is I... I've been here too long... If God sees me and says that I am me then me I am... But if God sees you and says that you are me then who is me, who is you and who is he... I've been here too long... If you are not your body then your body I will take... I feel now... Every twist of muscle... Every creak of Bone... Every pump of blood... I've been her to lo-" 

 

 

THUD! A monstruous impact comes from the adjoining room and seems to shake the wall between us. In a panic I spring from the bed to my feet in almost one smooth motion. Silence now from the other room.  

"He must've had an aneurysm or something" I thought - heart a whir. 

"Probably taken some dodgy gas station boner pill and burst a vessel in his brain". 

 



Now standing, I'm face to face with the vent; from inside a light radiates. The same dim light as my room. I press my face almost against the grate and squint, straining my eyes in hopes to catch any sign of movement from the opposite room. Somethings blocking the view however, as if an ironing board or something has been placed in front of the vent in the other room. I turn to go when suddenly- THUD...THUD...THUD! The same crashing from before, three times now. Again, I press my face to grate and squint, taxing my eyes. I'm there a minute before I see it. The face staring at me from the other side. 

 

 

It had been no ironing board at all; he too had been pressing his face to the vent. I could now see the old man's bug eyes staring back at me, a clownish smile on his face. He had been staring at me the whole time. THUD... THUD... THUD! The banging again. It's him; he's knocking on the wall. 

 

 

I stumble off the bed and fall flat on my ass. I scramble up to my feet and rush to the door grabbing the keys as I go. My hands are shaking too much; I can't unlock the door. Stood there fiddling with the keys, I know on the other side of that wall he's at his door to, he's coming to get me. Finally, I swing the door open – so hard it comes crashing against the wall. I break from my room, but I don't turn towards the stairs, I'm too scared to pass by his door. Instead, I swing my leg over the balcony and scramble over. Now, hanging from the railing as low as I can I drop onto the tarmac below.  Pains shoot up my legs through my knees and again I'm on my ass. Without turning to face what's chasing me I once more surge to my feet, breathing through gritted teeth at the pain. My bare feet slap against wet tarmac as I make it to the car. Clambering in and locking the doors I finally take a moment to face my pursuer. Nobody's there. Instead, looking up to the balcony I see his door hanging open and inside his silhouette. He just stands there watching me as I start the car and fire onto the road, hitting the curb as I go. 

 

 

I don't stop driving. I even blew straight past the motel two hours away – it's not far enough. I only stopped when in my exhaustion I came close to hitting a car merging from the other lane. I ended up sleeping in my car that night.
