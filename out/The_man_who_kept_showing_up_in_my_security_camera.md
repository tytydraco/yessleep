I am a single mother and my son and I just moved a couple of states away to get a new start. His father passed away. So I thought it would help our son to get out of that small town and get to see a new side of the world.

We get into our new house and it is stunning.. My husband had left us a great inheritance and the first thing I wanted to purchase was our forever home! I ended up with a lovely victorian style house, 2 stories, 4 bedrooms and a beautiful kitchen! I do love to cook.

Being a single momma, means I need to protect the house. I had cameras around the house installed as well as the outside of the house. They have motion detection, night vision, sound, the whole nine! I have never had cameras in or around the house before so I was excited to play around with my new "toys" and have my son walk in front of some to see how fast the detection went to my phone. Pretty stinking fast, I'll tell you!

Everything was perfect, good neighborhood, great school district, lovely home. Although I was missing my husband terribly and my son, his father... we were making the best out of it.

Then came the same notification at 3:42 AM. It started with just once a week and I was thinking a stray cat... but then it happened again and again and again. The only reason why I even noticed that it was strange was because it was then EVERY NIGHT AT 3:42 AM.

The next night I decided to stay up (I usually am asleep by 9) although it was really difficult. I looked when the notification popped up and I could see... nothing... absolutely nothing. I even looked out the window next to the door and nothing!

The next night I did it again, and I faintly see a man in the shadows across the street.. fucking weirded me out.. but he wasn't moving or anything so I'm thinking *what in the actual hell is going on here...* He was motionless and appeared to be staring straight at my house. It happened again night after night.

One night I had had it.. I took my late husband's gun out of the safe and I was going to confront him.. when I noticed he was holding up a sign.. *what the fuck?*

Before I get hurt I look into the camera and I zoom in to try to read the sign.

"**PLEASE STOP LEAVING YOUR TRASH CAN OUT, I'M TRYING TO SLEEP."**

I almost lost it, I was thinking it's a fucking neighbor?? But I did think it was some elaborate prank so I checked again the next night... something else is written on the sign

**"SERIOUSLY, TUESDAY IS TRASH DAY."**

Then..

**"I'VE ASKED YOU NICELY 6 TIMES."**

Eventually, I've had enough of this.. I grab the gun and go to confront him. He looks at ME horrifyingly and says "Holy shit you can see me?"

HE IS A FUCKING GHOST.

And he's been haunting the wrong house for 14 years because **my house number is one digit different from the house he died in.**

Now he's furious because I've been leaving my trash cans out all week and **“the neighborhood has standards.”**

You gotta be shitting in my mouth right now..  no way am I being haunted by an HOA ghost.

I check the camera the next morning.

He's standing there again.

Holding a sign\*\*...      “Also, your grass is getting ridiculous.”\*\*
