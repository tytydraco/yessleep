Never. Do you hear me? NEVER buy old computers at flea markets or from random sellers. And it doesn't matter where. It can be dangerous.

But I guess I should start from the beginning.

Hey everyone. My name is (redacted), and recently I went to a flea market where I noticed an interesting old computer. It caught my attention because it looked like it was made sometime between 2007 and 2009.

I thought it would be nice to buy it and maybe resell it for a higher price later.

In the end, I managed to get it for just $5, even though the seller originally wanted $30. After I talked him down, he even threw in a monitor and a keyboard. I thought that was pretty generous of him.

But the strangest part was that when I offered him $5, he didn't even try to negotiate. He just agreed immediately.

Honestly, I think I understand why now.

When I got home, I decided to turn the computer on and check if everything worked. I connected the computer, monitor, and keyboard. Basically, I set up the whole system the seller had given me.

At first, everything seemed completely normal.

Then something happened that I still can't explain. And honestly, it was absolutely terrifying.

When the computer started up, the familiar Windows 7 boot screen appeared. Nothing unusual, just the standard startup animation.

But when the desktop finally appeared, the monitor suddenly started flickering.

Some kind of error messages began appearing all over the screen. One after another. And then strange things started happening around me.

Objects in my room seemed to shift out of place. It was as if everything around me was glitching, distorting and moving on its own.

The screen kept getting brighter and brighter. And before I could do anything, I was literally pulled inside the monitor.

I don't know where I am now.

It's incredibly bright here, and completely empty. There isn't a single soul around. It feels as if I've somehow ended up in the future, but there's absolutely nothing here.

And there's something else...

When I tried to take out my phone, I noticed it was gone. Instead, there was a completely different device in my pocket. An old touchscreen Android phone, similar to the models that were popular sometime around 2010–2012. It has a small screen, thick bezels, and a pretty outdated interface.

I'm absolutely certain I had a different phone before.

The strangest thing is that this device still works. It says the battery is at 15%, and somehow it still has internet access. That's the only reason I'm even able to write this post.

I don't know what to do.

Please, if anyone sees this post, help me.
