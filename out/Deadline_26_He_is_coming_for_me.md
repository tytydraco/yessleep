The last days are pacing towards me now. I must survive; I must cling to hope. But His prey can’t run, nor can they hide. And I have been chased for a long time. He is everywhere. Mind and matter. He stands amongst the shadows, the corners of my bedroom at night, hiding in the blackness of my house where light does not reach, I see a figure, darker than the night itself standing in the hallways, in the rooms I call home.  
Sometimes I wonder if it’s in my head, and sometimes I pray it’s all in my head, if this is all some crazy effect of some kind of deterioration in my health, but now I think about it, the breaking of my mind and body came only when He did.  
I’ve done my research, I’ve learnt what I can, websites people shouldn’t visit, vast records and documents erased from the face value internet. He doesn’t want to be found, He doesn’t want to be known, not truly. Ive heard of beings such as Shadow People, The Hatman, The Stickmen. Its familiar, but not right, He is different.  
Ive read of people, brave or foolish enough to strike their skull with metal and electricity, desperate to erase things from their minds, things like the carnage He brought. But even those who forget will not escape when the end comes. Every loose end is tied and then burnt. He doesn’t let go of his prey, He has His scent on us, tainted and tortured.  
It doesn’t matter how far you go, how long you wait, how much you forget, He is there. Don’t bother playing some stupid waiting game, I’ve learnt that time means nothing to Him, He can transverse it, He can walk on it like a bridge. There are memories I have of Him from my childhood that I never had before. Even good memories I once had, a strange figure is always watching and waiting. Memories once filled with joy and love, replaced by His presence and His form. I don’t know what He is, if it’s even a He, where He comes from, why He is doing this to me. I might never know. But now I know He can find me, not just wherever I go, but “whenever” I go.  
What if He could use me for something? What if I could avoid whatever awaits me by bowing down at His feet? I think it is more akin to slavery than service. But I might be spared. For now at least... But how I could leave everything and everyone behind? I have no friends anymore, but there are still a few family members who stand by me, ones I love, even if they look at me like I’m insane, like some kind of nut. But they dismiss all of this, this fear, His presence. To them He is just a hallucination. Even those stupid doctors said He is just an embodiment of my shut up anxiety, but I never had anxiety or the other host of problems until He came into my life.  
Maybe even if I refuse to bow down, it won’t make a difference. He might still decide I am worthy regardless, to be taken and twisted, moulded into some kind of husk, something mindless and pitiful, a shell of flesh and bone with no choice or even awareness, just an extra limb for His use. It sounds familiar, too familiar. It reminds me of when they put me on that ward long ago, nothing but a drugged-up zombie. Maybe it wouldn’t be so bad. Maybe being unaware was a mercy.   
But there were stories of those who were “finished with” after His sick little hunt, with no potential, no use, no fun left to be had out of them. People who managed to survive too long. But the stories of what happens to them can’t be true, it is impossible, but then again, I have learnt that reality is barely a structure now. just a line we all pretend exists to help us sleep.  
There are whispers of flesh ripping like paper, arms and legs flayed open for maggots and flies. There are talks of rusted metal hooks and sharp grapples that dig into muscle. There are talks of limbs bound inside and out, coarse ropes, sharp wire, finding their way both through and around the body. But that is not the end, it should be, it must be, but it isn’t. It drags on and on, because He doesn’t play by our rules, He doesn’t make it quick, you will bleed out, until your body crumbles, feeling every chunk of flesh decay and drop to the ground, piece by piece. But it is not the end.  
Into the branches you will go, into the wood, into the bark and the sap and the moss and the roots. But that is not the end. You will be reduced to nothing more than a immobile soul crying out into nothing, piled and crushed on all sides by others who died here before you, and they will scream, they will cry, they will call for their mothers, their fathers, brothers and sisters, their lovers and friends. And you will start screaming, joining the others around you in some impossible place.  
But after time you will forget yourself, your name, your job, your hobbies, your interests, your memories, everything that makes you human and even those closest to you. But they would be long dead by now. Mercy comes only after ages upon ages, all that will be left of you is a dead leaf on a crooked branch.  
I have lived like this too long, I am scared of everything, I am scared of the endless voices that speak to me in the dark, I am scared of the knocking on the window and the doors and the mirrors, I am scared of the nightmares and the day dreams. I am scared of everything. And yet I know its not even over yet, if anything it has only just begun.  
I don’t know what to do, I feel Him everywhere, I feel His eyes on me everywhere, I can feel the fear spread the walls of my home like some kind of unseen mold, I can hear the sound around me fade like my only sanctuary is being cut off from the rest of the world. I will die alone, but death is not the end. I’m sorry Mum, I’m so sorry, I should have listened to you, I should have stepped carefully, I am sorry.  
And you… reading this… I am sorry, because now you know things you should never know, and He has smelt you, and He isn’t far away from your trail now. Who knows, maybe He will take you instead of me… maybe I have proven myself to His side, a quicker death.
