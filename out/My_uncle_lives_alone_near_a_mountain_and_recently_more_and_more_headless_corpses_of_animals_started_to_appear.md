TW: Slight instance of self harm and animal abuse.



As the title says, my uncle lives alone near a mountain, and recently more and more headless corpses of animals started to appear. I post this to ask if anyone has seen or knows why this happens, as it has me worried. I already posted on other subs, but I am guessing people here will appreaciate to read this chain of events.



For some context. He decided to live alone, isolated from everyone, after my grandfather died, not long after my grandmother did. Some said he died of sadness, others, that he just went to sleep and decided to see her again. In any case, this broke my uncle and he decided he needed some time for himself. My grandfather was quite the wealthy man, and with the cut my uncle got from the will he built a cabin near a mountain. He never married and had no children, and since he had enough money, he didn’t really need to work again, so it was no problem for him to leave everything behind.



 My father was the only person he kept contact with. Every month my uncle would drive to the nearest town 4 hours away to buy groceries, and while there he would pass by the post office to take from a security box a letter that my father sent. My uncle would then write down a response and hand it to the same post office to be delivered to my father. They kept this cycle for many years, a monthly cycle of sending and delivering letters.



Well, now that my father is not here anymore, I am the one in charge of receiving and responding to those letters, even if my uncle does not know yet it's me.



This started to happen like 6 months ago, but my uncle didn’t tell me until the next month. He went to the mountain for a stroll and found a headless bear. It seems that the “hunt” was recent as the blood leaking from the hole in the neck was still red. The way the head was separated from the body, according to my uncle, was done by someone with the knowledge and the tools of a master as it was a clean cut, the bone separated right on the joint of the neck. It was clear this wasn’t a wild animal attack, otherwise the carcass would have been mangled and everything but the head would have been gone.



Initially my uncle believed it was a poacher who wanted a nice trophy, who after shooting the bear they just removed the head and left the rest. It didn’t sit well with my uncle that the rest of the body was intact since it meant the shot that killed the bear landed on the head, and someone that wanted a head as a trophy would surely shoot anywhere but the head. He also found it weird to see the corpse of a bear so close to his home, as in all his time living there none have ever approached that much.



But with no other clue my uncle just assumed the poacher somehow got the bear to eat some poisoned meat, and in pain the creature just wandered aimlessly, and that was it.



That’s mainly the reason why my uncle initially didn’t tell me about it in his monthly letter as the situation was otherwise uneventful. But over the course of the next week, he found another corpse, this time of a bison. And the same thing, the head was gone, the cut clean, and the blood was still fresh. This made him more alert to his surroundings on his daily strolls, trying to see if he could spot more of these headless animals.



According to his next letter, my uncle found more corpses, all headless, and one of them, this one a bald eagle, had purple goop on the hole of the neck. The substance seemed viscous and a bit sticky, though he decided to investigate further in fear it was some kind of poison.



The liquid was clearly not natural though, and since that was the first time he encountered it, my uncle came to believe this was used on all previous corpses, maybe there to help with the removal of the head, and that always the poacher would remove any trace of it before leaving, that time them getting lazy and forgetting to wash it off entirely.



As of now 9 corpses have been found. The bear, bison, and the bald eagle previously mentioned, alongside a goat, a caribou, 2 frogs, one green, the other brownish, a salmon, and a wolf, this last one I’ll say more about in a second. And considering these are ones he finds on his casual strolls it’s likely there are many more out there.



As every animal my uncle found has been a different creature, outside of the frogs, my uncle was set on the theory of the trophy hunter trying to seek one of every creature in the mountain.



That theory lasted until his last letter I got.



On his daily stroll, this one a bit later than usual, he heard a sound of pure anguish, a mix between a howl and a cry. My uncle originally wanted to run back home, but after thinking about it for a few seconds he decided to go investigate. His house is on open view, so if there is a poacher nearby, they would be aware my uncle lives nearby, and if they haven’t done anything to him yet, they likely wouldn’t do anything now. I don’t think I can do justice to what happened if I paraphrase it, so I will just add a strip of his letter.



“*And with the confidence the supposedly poacher would not harm me, and the revolver packed on my holster, I decided to go see what that sound was. As I got closer to where I heard the sound, the first thing that hit me was the smell. Horrible, putrid. Remember when I forgot about some scrambled eggs I left on my lunch box all summer back in high school? That aroma was foul, got deep into my nose and left a burning sensation that went all the way down to my throat, and let me tell you brother, I would rather wear that aroma as a perfume than take another sniff of the aroma I smelled out in those woods.*



*I was almost ready to walk away, but the idea of finally confronting this poacher for all the corpses they were leaving behind was enough motivation to keep going. So I convinced myself this smell was some kind of bait with a strong odor to attract prey from far away and kept moving. Under the faint light the moon gave, as I approached those cries I saw this wolf, scratching its head against a tree. The act seemed desperate, the whole body of the poor thing shaking, leaving chunks of flesh and blood on the bark.*



*Remember the theory I gave you about the poacher leaving poisoned food? This made me believe that was the case, that I was witnessing a confused animal that just knew everything hurt and was trying to get rid of that pain in any way.*



*And then it turned. Maybe I did some sound, I don’t know, but its whole head snapped, its body following. And as I saw it directly, I noticed this purple dot on its snout, and every second that me and the wolf stared at each other more and more dots appeared on the fur of the beast, which would pop like bubbles, purple goop sliding down its head, in an abnormal way as it was setting around the base of its neck, not moving an inch down, forming some sort of perfect circle around it.*



*After the initial surprise, I tried to walk away, but the wolf turned aggressive, growling at me, its body setting lower, almost like a spring that was ready to fire any second. My first idea was to make a loud sound and extend my arms to make myself look bigger, but this seemed to just trigger it as the next second, the wolf was sprinting towards me.*



*I tried once more to scare it but as it was getting closer and it was clear it would not back away, with the creature just a few meters away from me I pulled my revolver and shot the wolf in the head, the bullet spreading its blood everywhere. Some of it fell on my index finger of my right hand, and instantly felt a sharp pain, as if a bunch of mosquitos decided to feed off my finger.*



*The worst part is the pain felt all around my finger, not only in the exterior, I was feeling this burning sensation all the way down to my bone.*



*I decided to walk back to my cabin to search for some pills for the pain but as I was going back the pain got worse and worse. It went from a bunch of mosquito stings, to feeling like my finger dipped inside boiling water. Remember that time when I fell from that tree and landed on a branch, groin first? That pain was nothing compared to this.*



*As I was making my way back, I stumbled on some roots and fell to the ground. The pain was entirely secluded to my finger, but it was so nauseating it made me dizzy, and I felt as if I would puke any second. In an act of desperation, I pulled the pocket knife, the one you gave me as a present a couple years back, and started to press it to my index finger trying to amputate it, the motion no different than someone trying to cut an apple. I barely felt the blade, whatever pain it caused was nothing compared to this burning sensation that enveloped my finger. It was easy to cut flesh and muscle, but once I got to the bone the knife stopped sliding back and forth smoothly. It wouldn’t bulge, so I did the next best sensible thing. I picked a rock and started to smash my finger with it. Let me say it again, the pain from this attempt to remove my finger was barely there, overshadowed by the more present pain, now of it being chewed all over, swimming inside my finger. As I heard a crunch, and noticed how my index would bend unnaturally backwards, I picked back the blade and finished the job. The following thing was relief, as that pain was gone.*



*Now, I did still feel some pain, a result of my botched attempt at an amputation (should have study medicine like father wanted), but compared to how it felt a few seconds before, it was as if I was back on that spa our mother brought us to, were all I could feel was nothing but bliss in all my body.*



*Yet the reality that I had a bleeding wound settled in so I stood and kept walking to my cabin. There I bandaged myself as best as I could and went to sleep.*



*Today, I decided to go grocery shopping earlier, since I was planning to go see a doctor.  They did something to close the wound and bandaged the area, and it seems for now I am in the clear. As I write this letter the pain is a bit there, but compared to yesterday this is nothing.*  



*Though, I do wonder, what happened yesterday, what it was that I saw? I honestly have no clue. My best bet is some kind of poisonous mold, but I am not entirely sure. Maybe I will go back and try to find another of those headless corpse to see if any has purple goop. If I can collect some in a jar maybe I could send it to some laboratory. You know more than me in that regard, brother, so maybe you could come help me here. When you can, of course. I know you have your own life, but I would appreciate the help here.*”



And following that he went on to recall a story about my father and him when they were young and would steal apples from the neighbor.



With what I have read I find it impossible to tell what is actually going on. I have tried to search on the internet for some answers but nothing.



The theory of my uncle, that it is some kind of mold, I find it hard to believe. So, I will ask, has anyone had any experience like this in the past? Does anyone know what that purple goop may be?



I am a bit worried for my uncle, as this letter I shared was sent 3 months ago. Receiving his letters monthly has been the only constant that I could count on in my life. I am guessing the wound from his hand got infected and these past months he has been lying on some hospital bed. That is at least what I prefer to believe over any other alternative. I had some stuff going on with my life, but I am planning to go give him a visit as soon as I can.



So, any kind of help is appreciated.
