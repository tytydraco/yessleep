It was almost two in the morning when that idiot tailgated me on the country road, leaving barely two meters between our bumpers. His high beams burned through my rearview mirror right into my retinas. My nerves were already shot after a twelve-hour shift. When he finally passed me on an unpredictable curve, honking his horn, I lost my temper. I rolled down the window, thrust my arm out into the cold night air, and flipped him the middle finger.

The driver of the black mid-size car didn't slow down. He pulled ahead, but as his car was side-by-side with mine, the passenger turned toward me. In the dim light of the dashboard, I saw his face. He was smiling. Not a malicious grin, but a gentle, almost affectionate smile. He slowly raised his right hand, folded his fingers into a fist, and extended his middle finger back at me.

His hand was unnaturally pale. And his finger didn't stop extending. It grew inch by inch, thin and bony, until it nearly touched my car's window pane. I slammed on the brakes in sheer terror. The black car vanished into the darkness.

By the time I got home, my hands were shaking so violently that it took me three tries to unlock the front door. I didn't want to wake my girlfriend, Elena. She was already in the bedroom, fast asleep. I undressed in the dark, slipped under the covers, and snuggled close to her back. The scent of her lavender perfume slowly calmed me down.

I felt Elena turn toward me. Her hand slid warmly across my chest, traveled slowly up my neck, and rested at the corner of my jaw. It was a tender, familiar gesture. I closed my eyes and breathed a sigh of relief.  
"Are you awake?" I whispered softly.

She didn't answer. Instead, her hand began to apply pressure. It wasn't an aggressive strangle, but a slow, continuous force. Her middle finger rested right against my jugular vein. The tip of the finger suddenly felt freezing cold instead of warm.

I reached for her wrist to push it away, but her skin felt rough and dry, like old paper. The finger pressed deeper into my flesh.

A faint shaft of moonlight fell through the gap in the blinds onto the bed.  
Elena was lying on the other side. She was fast asleep, facing the wall, her steady breathing softly audible in the room.

The hand at my throat didn't belong to her. It was reaching up from below, directly out of the hollow space under my pillow, gripping my neck.

I wanted to scream, but the unnaturally long middle finger wrapped itself heavily and ice-cold around my throat from the outside. Then I felt a searing, burning pain right under my lower jaw. The bony finger wasn't just pressing against me—it was noticeably piercing my skin. I heard the faint, wet tearing of tissue as the blunt tip punctured the skin layers from the outside, forcing its way brutally upward through the flesh beneath my chin.  
Warm blood ran down my throat as the finger shoved further upward, bursting through the floor of my mouth and shoving my tongue aside. I instantly tasted raw iron and my own blood.

At the same time, a silhouette detached itself from the darkness in the corner of the room. It was the passenger. His arm was unnaturally long, stretching from the shadows of the wall, across the floor, and deep under my pillow. He stood motionless, his other hand folded peacefully over his stomach. His smile remained gently unchanged.  
He leaned forward slowly, placed his free index finger over his lips, and whispered softly, "Shh."  
The finger that had breached the skin beneath my chin pressed relentlessly upward. It levered my teeth apart and thrust bloodily out into the open through my shocked, gaping lips.
