This is a true story that happened to some of my friends, and honestly, I wish it wasn’t.  
My friends had a dog that was taken from their backyard.  
At first, they had no idea where she had gone. They searched around the area and eventually put up a post on a local community page, hoping someone had seen her or knew where she was.  
While the wife was scrolling through the page, she suddenly came across a post with a photo of a dog.  
It was their dog.  
The fact that she had been taken from their own backyard made the whole thing immediately feel much more sinister. Someone had taken their dog, and now she had somehow ended up with a stranger.  
They tried to make contact with the person who had her. The response they got was:  
**“I don’t have a phone, let me go to a payphone.”**  
Obviously, that didn’t make much sense.  
The dad started keeping an eye on the community page. He went back through the woman’s previous posts and found a photo she had already posted that showed part of what appeared to be her balcony.  
He started cross-referencing the details in the photo with apartment buildings in the area and eventually managed to identify an apartment block.  
He went there himself and started knocking on doors, trying to find the woman and, more importantly, trying to find his dog.  
Eventually, he found a guy who knew her. They both went to her apartment and knocked on the door.  
Nobody answered.  
The guy then told him, **“I’ll get her from the basement. Give me your contact details.”**  
Later that night, the dad received a text.  
**“I’ve got her.”**  
His wife wouldn’t let him go alone, so he called a friend to come with him. They brought metal pipes with them for protection in case things went bad.  
When they arrived, the man had the dog.  
He handed her over and apparently just said:  
**“Take her now and never talk to me again.”**  
Then they saw what had happened to her.  
The dog was covered in glue.  
She had bruises all over her spine.  
They took her straight to the vet. My friends said she was incredibly timid and frightened after being brought home.  
They contacted the police and reported what had happened.  
According to my friends, they were told that the police wouldn’t do anything because they only dealt with “important human matters.”  
I don’t know exactly what was said during that conversation, so I’m not going to pretend I do. But my friends were left feeling like nobody was going to hold anyone accountable for what had happened to their dog.  
And unfortunately, the story doesn’t end there.  
The dog was later diagnosed with lung cancer.  
Eventually, her condition became bad enough that my friends had to make the horrible decision to put her down.  
After everything she had already been through, that’s the part I keep thinking about.  
I have no idea whether the cancer was connected to what happened to her. There isn’t enough evidence to say that it was, and I don’t want to make an accusation without something medical to back it up.  
But given that she was taken from their backyard, returned covered in glue and bruised along her spine, and was later diagnosed with lung cancer, I really hope my friends consider having an autopsy performed if they can afford it.  
Maybe it would provide an answer.  
Maybe it wouldn’t.  
But at the very least, they might finally know what happened to their dog.  
She was taken from her own backyard, survived whatever happened to her while she was gone, and eventually came home terrified.  
She deserved so much better.  
I know the persons name and I can probably find the address but their have been other disturbing disappearance of dogs in that same area so this could be a serial offender. It just makes me feel so sick.
