“How long until I come down.”

I can't tell you how many times I've asked this in my life. One of the things they tell you about having a bad trip, is that you just need to remind yourself that it won't last forever, and it will end eventually. Maybe this is good advice for some people, but all it did for me was make me stare at the clock too much. I would concentrate on how slow time was moving, and I would end up slipping further into the bad trip.

I didn't know what to tell Charles. I felt like the truth was the last thing he needed to hear right now. I didn't even know how to tell him that he was never going to come down, and that he just needed to try and adjust to this new life.

“Let me get you that water.”

His eyes were locked forward. He barely moved except for the pinky that he kept shoving into his ear. When I came back with the water, he looked exactly like how he looked when I left him.

“Here you go.” I reached out to hand it to him, he didn't budge.

“Hello Charles, water.”

“If you stick your hand into a fish bowl, wouldn't you say that you still exist outside the fish bowl?”

I just answered my own question from earlier. When They told me They couldn't fit in my room.

“I'm going to take a nap.”
