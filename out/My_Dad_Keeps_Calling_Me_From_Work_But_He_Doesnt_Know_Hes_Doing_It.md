My dad works night shifts at a warehouse about 30 minutes from our house. He's been doing it for years, so nobody in our family thinks much about his schedule anymore.

Last Sunday, something strange happened.

At around 11:40 PM, I got a call from him.

He asked if I'd locked the front door.

I said yes.

He told me to check again because he'd forgotten something at home and didn't want anyone getting inside.

I thought it was a little weird, but I checked anyway.

The next morning, I mentioned the call to him over breakfast.

He looked confused.

"What call?"

I told him he'd called me around midnight.

He laughed and said he hadn't touched his phone all night.

I assumed he just forgot.

Two nights later, it happened again.

Same number. Same voice.

This time, he asked whether Mom was asleep.

I said yes and asked why.

He went quiet for a few seconds.

Then he said, "Good. Don't wake her."

I asked him what was going on.

He hung up.

The following morning, I checked his phone while he was watching television. There was no outgoing call to my number that night.

I showed him my call history.

He didn't laugh this time.

He told me not to answer if it happened again.

I asked why.

He said he'd explain when he got home.

That night, at around 11:40 PM, my phone rang again.

Dad.

I remembered what he'd said, so I let it ring.

A few seconds later, I received a text.

"Pick up. It's important."

I replied, "Dad, you're supposed to be at work."

He answered almost immediately.

"I'm outside."

I looked through the window.

There was a car parked across the street.

I couldn't see who was inside.

Then another message came through.

"Don't open the door."

I stared at the screen.

Before I could respond, someone knocked.

Three times.

My mom woke up and asked who it was.

I told her to stay in the bedroom.

I called my dad using the number saved in my contacts.

He answered on the first ring.

I could hear machinery in the background.

He sounded exhausted.

"What's wrong?"

I told him someone was outside pretending to be him.

He went completely silent.

Then he asked me to describe the car.

I did.

He told me to stay away from the windows and call the police.

The knocking stopped.

A minute later, the car drove away.

When Dad finally arrived home, he looked genuinely frightened.

He checked the entire house, then sat down with us.

He told us something he'd never mentioned before.

About a month ago, someone had been calling the warehouse pretending to be him, asking about his work schedule.

Management initially thought it was a prank.

Until someone started showing up outside the building.

The police were informed, but nobody was identified.

I asked Dad why he hadn't told us.

He said he didn't want to worry us.

I thought that was the end of it.

Until this morning.

I was going through my phone and noticed something I hadn't seen before.

The number that called me on Sunday night wasn't my dad's.

It was a different number.

I searched it online.

It belonged to a local phone repair shop.

I showed Dad.

He stared at it for a long time.

Then he asked me something.

"Did you ever tell that person what time I usually come home?"

I said no.

He nodded.

Then he opened his phone and showed me a message he'd received yesterday.

It was from an unknown number.

It contained a photograph of our front door.

Taken from inside our house.

The message underneath said:

**"Your family is very polite. They almost let me in."**
