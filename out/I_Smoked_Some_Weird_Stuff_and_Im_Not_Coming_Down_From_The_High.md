Let me start by saying that I am NOT a stoner. I’ve smoked weed only a handful of times in my life since my early twenties. A lot of people seem to think I'm a stoner because of the people I’m friends with but that simply is not the case.

My best friend my whole life, Tommy, is the most stereotypical stoner dude in history. That guy has been high out of his mind since we were in middle school. He has always tried to smoke me up but it’s rare that I cave in to the peer pressure. I’m a very anxious person and 9 times out of 10 the shit he smokes tends to give me one hell of a panic attack.

So last weekend I'm over at Tommy's house, we’re hanging out, I'm two beers in, and he pulls out a joint. “You smoking with me tonight Nate?” he asked.

I looked at the joint in his hand. Something was off about it. It wasn’t the normal shade of green you’d expect from weed, it was much lighter, like the color of a granny smith apple. “Sure man.” I said, already feeling anxious “Is this more of the shit you grow yourself?”

“You know it bro.” He said, pulling it to his lips and lighting it. He took a big long puff and passed me the joint. 

I stared at it for a moment before bringing it to my lips and taking a long puff myself. I instantly began to cough which prompted a short quick laugh from Tommy. My coughing didn't stop and was soon accompanied by spit flying from my mouth. “Oh, oh dude, gross. Step out, this is a nice carpet.” said Tommy.

I forced myself to my feet and stumbled to the sliding glass door, sliding it open quickly and stumbling outside. I leaned over the edge of the balcony and I emptied the contents of my stomach onto the grass below.

“Dude.” Tommy said, stepping outside behind me. “Not a fan of my new stuff I guess.” he joked to himself.

I looked up at him with watery eyes as a few more coughs forced their way out of me. “Wha-” I interrupted myself with a big dry heave which turned into me puking over the edge of the balcony yet again.

Tommy had the biggest shit eating grin on his face when he spoke next. “I thought you could handle something a little stronger.” he said, “that’s my bad man.”

“You what? What did I just fucking smoke?” I asked with a sniffle.

“I honestly don’t know man, some shit I bought outside a China shop. The guy called it the mow-guy or something.” He said, rubbing the back of his head, maybe starting to feel bad.

“Mow- dude. Mogwai? That’s from Gremlins dude. That’s from the movie Gremlins. You got scammed, we probably just smoked lawn clippings, you moron.” I said, my anger increasing with every word.

“Damn I knew it sounded familiar.” he replied, sounding confused. “But like, he sold me the seeds, I grew the plant myself, it was crazy looking like nothing I’d ever seen.”

“Wha- and we just smoked it?” I wheezed hard after speaking. 

“Yeah, but it’s not my first time, the high is good trust me.” he pleaded. 

It was at that moment that I started to feel the high of whatever the hell was in my body. My toes and my fingertips felt numb, my arms and my chest felt cold, but it was nice. It was a good cold, like sitting in front of a fan on a hot summer day. “Woah.” was all I could say as I composed myself and wiped my mouth with my sleeve.

“Yeah dude, woah is the perfect word to describe this, and that’s after one puff, we got the whole joint left brother.” he spoke to me like the snake spoke to Eve in the garden of Eden.

Before I knew it we were back on the couch choking down the rest of the joint. To say I was high would be the understatement of the year. I was on another planet. Nothing felt real. Every word uttered by Tommy or by whatever the hell he put on TV just went in one ear and out the other. My head was fuzzy and everything sounded like the parents in Charlie Brown. Wah wa wah wah waaaah wa wah.

This is where it gets crazy. At some point during it all I fell asleep on the couch. When I woke up it was two days later. Two. Days. Later.

We smoked on Saturday night sometime around eight-ish and now it was 9 PM on a Monday.I had dozens of missed calls. I felt like shit, Tommy was nowhere to be seen, and I had missed a whole day of work. I rushed to the door and I rushed to my car. I sat down and shot Tommy a quick text that said something like ‘thanks for waking me up asshole’ and I started driving home.

As I started driving I noticed I still felt a little off. My hands felt freezing. I cranked the heat up and peeled out. 

A mix of anger and frustration had me driving as fast as I could to get home. I was flying down the back roads. However, somehow, there was some speed demon in a pickup truck riding my ass. Soon he was flashing his high beams and blaring his horn. What the hell? I thought. I was going so fast, how was this guy keeping up? And how was I going too slow for him? 

I glanced at the speedometer, I was going 12 miles an hour. I pulled over in a state of shock. I watched as that pickup truck flew past me as the driver flipped me off. I was so confused. Was I still high?

I started driving again, this time watching the speedometer, trying to stay above 20 miles an hour, but anything above 15 miles an hour just felt far too fast. I was scared. I eventually made it to my apartment after moving at a snail's pace the rest of the way. 

On my door was a notice of my rent being due. I knew it was due soon, but I thought it wasn’t due until the last Friday of the month. I grabbed the notice and went into my apartment. I felt so wonky. Everything felt so fuzzy.

I laid down in bed and thought about what excuse I'd make at work for missing today. I glanced at my phone and saw that they had called me almost ten times. That was odd, I thought. Then I had a shocking realization. It was the following Monday. I hadn’t slept on the couch for two days, I had slept on that couch for NINE DAYS. In my haze I hadn’t checked the date, I just saw that it said Monday on my phone and I assumed it had only been two days. It never crossed my mind that I could’ve slept away nine days.

That doesn’t even make sense. Nine days? Shouldn’t I just be dead?

I instantly called Tommy. “What up dude it’s Tommy, I’m busy so leave a message, or just text me like a normal person.” His voicemail taunted me. I called again, voicemail, I called a third time, voicemail. Fuck.

I rolled over and suddenly froze. I felt my ribs shift, my stomach fat move, I could feel too much. I could feel my lungs fill with air as I gasped, I could feel my heart pump, I could feel blood flowing through my veins. Suddenly I could feel every function of my body, even those you aren’t supposed to feel.

Blinking felt so strange, I could feel my fleshy eyelids slide over my wet eyeballs. I could feel my tongue resting in my mouth. I swallowed my spit and I felt it slide all the way down my throat and into my stomach. My stomach felt so empty. I hadn’t eaten in days. I couldn’t move. My limbs felt cold. I was tripping even harder. 

I coughed. I felt my lungs inflate and quickly deflate, I felt each individual rib in my body move with them. I felt mucus detach itself from my throat and land in my mouth

I couldn’t do anything other than lay there, grip my blanket, and try to breathe. It felt like I had been there for hours. I glanced at my phone. Only a few minutes had passed. 

I debated calling my mom for a while but I decided not to. Maybe I’ll regret that. 

After what felt like days of staring at the ceiling and trying to breathe I could see the sun starting to rise. I made it to morning. I tried to sit up but It was so difficult. I could feel my muscles moving my bones. After a few moments I finally did it, I had sat up, but not without the cost of me pissing everywhere. I didn’t even realize I had to piss through all of this sensory overload. 

I spent the next hour or so getting to my feet. I stumbled to my couch and sat down. With great effort I turned on the TV. No matter what I put on I just couldn’t focus. I kept focusing on every function of my body.

I used my phone to order food. Another herculean feat. A cheese pizza. I tipped really high I think. I’m not completely sure.

I tried calling Tommy five or six more times. I just kept getting sent to voicemail. I was getting more used to controlling my body as time went on.

When the pizza arrived I was still in my piss soaked pants. I brought myself to my feet with great discomfort but relative ease. I opened the door. The guy handed the food to me. “Have a good day man, thanks for the tip.” he said.

I tried to respond but I just sort of clunkily opened my mouth and groaned. The guy nodded and smiled and walked away. I slammed the door closed and stumbled back to the couch. 

I opened the pizza box and brought a piece to my mouth. I bit into it and began chewing. Soon I bit the inside of my cheek hard. I let out a screaming groan that sounded like it came from Night of the Living Dead.

I swallowed the half chewed ball of pizza. I felt it slide all the way down my throat. I then could feel it sitting in my stomach. I could feel my stomach acid starting to break it down. My appetite was ruined, but I was so thirsty.

I got to my feet and walked over to the sink. I turned on the cold water and put my face under it. I began to take gulp after gulp. I could feel the chill go down my throat and into my stomach. I groaned loud again. As I pulled myself away from the sink.

I fought my way back to my couch but before I sat down I glanced out the window. I swear I saw Tommy’s car parked next to mine. I tried to yell Tommy's name out the window but what came out was a groan that sounded more like ‘mommy.’

I could see someone sitting in the car but I couldn’t tell if it was Tommy. I called him on my phone, voicemail. I rushed out the door of my apartment, leaving it open. I had to get to Tommy, he’s the only one that had any idea what we smoked so I felt he was the only one that could help me.

I lived on the third floor of my apartment building. I rushed to the elevator and began to smash my thumb into the down button hard. I felt my thumb snap. I looked down and my thumb was clearly broken, but it felt normal again. I couldn’t feel the bone or the muscles or the ligaments or whatever anymore. I just felt like my thumb.

The elevator was taking too long so I ran to the stairs. This was one of those ‘butterfly effect’ decisions. As I was rushing down the stairs I rolled my ankle and fell. I tumbled down a whole flight of stairs and landed on my left arm hard. 

Whatever two bones are in the forearm, I had broke them both. My arm was shaped like a zigzag. I used the stairs to stand up. Again, same as my thumb, my shattered arm felt normal again, I felt like I was supposed to again, just only in my left arm and right thumb. 

I made it to the front door. An older woman was bringing in her groceries and she gasped when she saw me, dropping everything. I must’ve looked terrible. She went ghostly white. “A-a-a-a-a-are you o-o-o-okay?” she stuttered. I shoved her to the side and stepped outside. 

“TOMMY!” I groaned. I sounded like Frankenstein’s monster. I ran out back. Luckily his car was still there. 

I ran to the driver's side door and flailed my broken arm into the window to get his attention. I was met with the blood-curdling shriek of a young woman. I looked inside and it was not Tommy. It was a girl. Maybe in her teens. Someone I had never seen before. 

She quickly shifted into reverse and drove away fast. She ran over my right foot when she did. I’m sure it broke in several places. It felt so good. 

By then a crowd had started to gather. A handful of my neighbors were standing around me. I groaned again, trying to explain myself but only noises were coming out. I probably looked like a zombie, or like some kind of tweaker. I guess I was some kind of tweaker. I ran forward into the woods.

I ran until I collapsed. I pulled myself up so I leaned against a tree. I can hear sirens in the distance. I looked at my broken foot and I wiggled it as much as I could. It felt normal. It felt like I was getting the best foot massage I could ever imagine. It took so much energy to type this out, but I had to leave a log, I can't just die without anybody knowing why. Now as I lean against this tree I have a big decision to make. Would it feel better to jump off a bridge or run in front of a semi-truck?
