I never thought I’d spend my first few days as a twenty-three year old being stalked by a Lovecraftian horror, but here we are. It got in last night. I don’t know how long it was waiting out there. Had it been holding its breath till it got closer? Given the volume of its breathing, I should’ve heard it coming. 

Also, I moved locations, I’m no longer in the old ranger station. A lot has happened since last night. To get to the ranger station initially I had to back track back up the mountain road about half a mile. I knew I was just adding distance between myself and civilization but it was the only solid structure I remember seeing the whole way up here. I figured it be my safest move, and I think I was right. The temperature was already starting to drop when we crashed and the nightly snowfall would have slowed me incredulously.

Last night after I powered down the phone, i stayed in the abandoned office in the building next to the candle. The second it was reaching its last moments of light, and began to flicker out was when that thing started to make that awful wood winding up sound. The front doors had started banging furiously. When I first arrived I had dragged every spare chair and filing cabinet over in front of it, hoping to bar that thing from entering but my efforts were futile. Eventually it burst through. I could hear it maneuvering through the building searching for me. It took its time but was Rageful and filled with malice. With the distance it crossed in the building, only more destruction and catastrophe followed, and it was getting close fast. I didn’t know if it primarily relied on sense of smell or  hearing, but I was banking on it not being able to smell me only hear, remembering it turning around after the gunshot and Ricky’s screaming, so I paid mind to staying as silent as possible.

That’s when I had the craziest but possibly most live saving idea in my life. I still had lighter fluid on me for my zippo. The thought of the creature waiting till my candle burnt out stuck with me, what if even though it couldn’t see the flames it could maybe sense them. Maybe it also held the primeval sense of fear of fire along with most the animal kingdom. I was going to take my chances. My stomach refused any of the food I tried to eat today and I knew making it back through the snow and all the way back down the mountain was going to get harder and harder. And if this thing was going to keep coming after me, then maybe slowing it down would increase my chances of survival. By the time I had managed to get to the other side of the room, laying on my belly and practically slithering like a snake, I had left a massive mess of lighter fluid all over the floor, trailing behind me like a blood trail. By the time I got the window open it burst through the last door into the office. I stood there frozen, I held my breath, I didn’t even blink. It rose its narrow traffic cone shaped head towards the ceiling and opened it jaws,   
“Cluuaaakkk Cluuaaaakkk Cluuaaakkkk”  
I flicked my zippo open.  
It’s necked snapped towards me head craning in my direction. I was staring it right in the face. Its eyes, or where eyes should have been, were deep swollen pits, leaking black liquid.   
It began making the wind up noise again. I flicked my lighter and dropped it to the floor.  
The room was engulfed in flames immediately. The fumes from the fuel erupted into a massive heatwave, I had to throw my forearms in front of my face to protect myself. 

The thing started to scream uncontrollably, it swung at me but I had jumped out of its reach right after the initial burst took place. It was confused and tried to grab at every angle I could’ve been. It behaved maniacally, wriggling and heaving up and down, still screaming and trying with all its might to make the winding sound. There was a moment it leaned onto the wall giving me clearance back into the hallway so I took it. I jumped through the flames and crashed onto my side on the floor. A massive mangled and charred arm slammed into the tiles next to me. I got up and ran towards the front, with this thing careening around corners trying to reach me through the smoke. Its scream was so loud I don’t remember hearing the sounds of the flames themselves at this point, even though half the station was on fire. There was a small T intersection in the hallway, as I made my last turn towards the entrance of the building I pulled the spoon I found last night out of my side pocket and through it in the other direction. The clattering metallic sound was loud enough for the creature to discern over its own screams. It took the bait and I took my chance to leave.

As far as I could tell it was still trying to murder that spoon in the corner of the hallway when I left. Flesh melting from the heat as it thrashed in the corner, still howling, calling out in pure hate, pain, and rage. The distance between us silenced the noise. I ran so fast down the road after that, I don’t really know how much ground I covered. As of right now I’m hiding out in a culvert drain, and the phones dying again. Hopefully I can finish this fucking trip tomorrow, I haven’t heard that thing again either and I hope I don’t. I’m too exposed tonight and I’m too tired, cold, and weak to defend myself against it again. My arms got pretty burnt in the fire, I didn’t notice till a ways done the road. I’m at two percent so I’ll update again tomorrow, wish me luck boys. I’ve made it this far, I’m making it home.
