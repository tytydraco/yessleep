The worst sound in the world isn't the wet crunching of a hammer hitting a skull. It's when that crunching gives way to the hammer striking concrete because the person swinging just can't stop. I learned this while cowering behind the curtain of the locker room showers, where I'd been since the attack started.

That horrible ringing sound is all I can hear now. It cut through the sound of the men rushing to grab the kid swinging that hammer. It counted time as I lied to investigators about what I knew. It rings out now as I write this confession. Mom and Dad, I'm sorry.

The coaches really don't watch us on those long runs. They just make us stretch and then give us a time to be back. They tell us to “take it easy” that it's a “long slow run” and we should make it long. That's what made it my favorite. Just the guys out on those trails talking about grades, games, and girls. 

Of course we kind of naturally split up, y'know? It wasn't really on purpose, but some kids just are slower than the others. Usually it was just the varsity guys ahead and we kind of left everyone behind. I don't know how Jeff caught up if I'm honest, but he was there that day.

We'd picked a different path than usual. One we'd never seen before, but it looked normal for a long time. Just before we were going to turn around we saw it though. That fucking church.

I mean it was normal at first, normal for an old building at least. It was creepy but in that Halloween haunted house way, not the serial killer lived there way. Still, no one wanted to go in. We kinda just stood outside and made up stories about it.

Like someone got crucified in it or they all got raptured or they all drank the Kool-aid. I think Anthony said it was probably a puritan place. Sam said it was satanic now. Arch told us he'd take Kathy McGuire there and we all gave him shit for that. Jeff told some kind of joke. I don't really remember because we were too busy trying to tackle Arch.

“Let's go in.” I don't remember who said it, but I remember the idea sticking immediately. Anthony said I should go in, but I told him off. Then Jeff said we could all go together, but Sam told him that would ruin how creepy it was. I think everyone wanted it to be a test of courage, but we were all too scared to take it.

Arch didn't let up. You know how he is. When he sees a sore spot that's what he pokes at. He brought up that time I peed watching Lost Tapes at his 12th birthday sleepover. Said he knew I was too scared. I really thought about going after that but turned it on him instead. 

Told him if he was so happy to take a girl in there, he should find out where he was going to lay down with her. Sam said he was going to take Arch’s mom in there, and that set Arch off in the way it always did. 

While they were fighting Jeff tried to get Anthony and me to go together. Anthony said fine, but Arch was worked up. He shoved me and told everyone I was challenged first and I had to go. 

Jeff said he'd go with me so I turned it on him. “If you wanna go so bad, do it. You want to run with us anyway, prove it.” I wish I hadn't said that. 

He said no. I mean, we all kinda had. It's just hard to say no when everyone starts piling on. Jeff said we could go together, but I'd already given Arch a target, and Arch was mad enough as it was. Sam tried to calm him down but couldn't. 

Arch didn't mean to hurt him. Just talked about running stuff. Jeff really was a weak runner. Kind of weak overall, that's all. We just joked about that. All of us. Until Jeff said he'd prove he could hang and he'd show us we were scared of some empty building. 

We all laughed, but the laughing stopped when Jeff stopped at the door. I tried to give him a thumbs up but I don't think he saw it before he went inside.

It took a long time for Jeff to come out. Long enough for me to pee and for the guys to start throwing rocks at the crooked cross above the door to try and knock it down. When we couldn't find any more rocks Sam said someone should go get Jeff and Anthony said we would all go. Just before we all got to the door he came out.

He wasn't different or anything. He just shrugged and said, ‘Just a dumb church.’ If we had more time we would have probably gone in, but we had to get back or we'd have to do extra runs later. We agreed to go back next long run but I'm really glad we didn't.

Sam patted Jeff on the back and we all took off. While we were jogging we had a million questions. Jeff told us all about the broken pews, the glass everywhere, the Bible on the pulpit. Said he thought about bringing it, but thought it was more important to find a spot to take Arch’s mom. We laughed and Arch punched his arm, but it was all fun.

It was all fun for a while. Jeff could keep up with us most days and he was getting better at joking around, so we just let him come along with us. Sam even started acting like an older brother. He protected Jeff when he called Arch's mom a cow. Arch might have bitten his head off had Jeff not made a joke about turning vegetarian.

By the end of that month he started beating Anthony in sprints. Sometimes he'd even beat me. He was getting better. Changing, too. I swear he looked a little taller, but maybe he just stopped slouching.

There were a few other things I guess we could have seen. Jeff stopped showering with the rest of us. He'd go into the handicap stalls with the curtains. He also started eating more, like a lot more, but that was normal we thought. 

The coaches noticed Jeff changing too. Started egging us varsity guys on. Telling us they might need Jeff to trade spots and this and that. Coach stuff, but I can't say it didn't work. All of us tried harder, started running extra, even though we knew coach wouldn't actually do it.

We'd break off from the big group to run further and faster. Started leaving the juniors in charge of the younger kids so we could focus. Jeff was one of those juniors. We thought he was cool but we found out that he was being mean to some of the freshmen on the team too, little pranks like stepping on their shoelaces or bumping them off trails. That got back to Coach, and we got rewarded with a lecture on ‘brotherhood’ and ‘responsibility’ and ‘setting an example.’

We were panting after practice one day when Jeff made a joke about replacing Anthony. Saying maybe if he didn't have “ratty ass shoes” he could run faster. It could have been harmless, but you know Anthony's family. How he couldn't really do that. How much he needed the chance at a scholarship. 

No one wanted to explain that to Jeff, and no one was going to tell him we saw Anthony crying. After that Anthony didn't really wanna hang out with Jeff anymore. I think we all needed a break from Jeff. Even Sam kinda ignored him for a bit. 

At least until the fight. Well, “fight.” We all took off running when we heard Jeff was getting into it with Scott, the big football guy. Later we heard that Scott had said something about Jeff being a virgin and Jeff told Scott he was a cuck because his girlfriend, Kathy McGuire, had slept with the whole team. Then they started scrapping.

When we got there it was towards the end. Sam was yelling for Jeff to stop as we fought our way through the crowd. Arch was yelling that he'd kill Scott. Everyone was cheering. Until the snap.

When the screaming started the crowd broke apart and we saw what happened. Jeff was sitting with a crooked nose on top of Scott's back. He was pulling Scott's arm back toward him, twisting it at the wrist, elbow, and shoulder. Like a fucked-up spiral.

When the teachers got there Scott wasn't the one crying. He was just staring at his arm. Jeff was all blood and snot and tears. We didn't get close to him, but we could tell he was freaking out. We all were.

That was the day we had that assembly on conflict resolution and when we all got dismissed early. Scott and Jeff got a week suspension so they could “investigate what happened.” I don't think Scott has been back to school though.

The next Monday, we tried to go back to normal. Running, class, hanging out. Jeff was still suspended, and for a while we forgot about him. When he finally came back, he didn't make some grand entrance or anything. He was just kind of there.

He was a lot quieter, which made a lot of sense considering everything. I wouldn't even know how to tell you guys if I got suspended, much less what I'd do with myself for a whole week. Jeff must have had a bad time with it though, because we couldn't get anything out of him. 

He kind of faded into our group. Like he was still laughing and running with us, but he was dimmed. Even when Arch would take shots at him, he would just laugh. When they started construction on the regular showers and we could only use the private showers he always went last. 

I know it's weird but we waited for him one day. Just kind of trying to hang out and see if he was alright. We didn't peep on him or anything weird, but the way he got out and covered his body made me feel like a creep. I'm glad we did though because we saw that rash.

It was all over his chest and ribs. Red and ugly and you could tell where he scratched until it bled. It was gross, and Arch asked if he had AIDS, but no one laughed. We were being jerks and Jeff told us so. He was pissed and yelling about trust and friends and privacy. Saying ‘I thought you guys were my friends!’ He didn't stop until the coaches came out to make sure we weren't fighting. After that everyone but Sam shuffled to class. He probably stayed to apologize.

I saw Jeff later sitting in the courtyard when I left class to pee. He had his head down, staring at his hands. Praying, maybe. Or just talking to himself. He didn't see me so I thought about just leaving. But I came up from behind him and almost put my hand on his shoulders. Before I could, he whipped around and looked pissed.

I fumbled some small talk, then fumbled an apology about that morning even harder. When I finished saying sorry, he didn't even look at me. Just shook his head and told me to ‘Fuck off.’

He didn't run with us the next day. I got everyone to agree to get him some donuts and apologize when he came back. That was the plan at least, until he came back like a jerk. He wouldn't talk to us, wouldn't listen to us. Sam tried but couldn't get anything out of him. 

Arch decided he didn't care about apologizing anymore and tried to get him to respond at all. Started poking at him, saying he wasn't really being a friend himself. We tried to tell Arch to knock it off but he started calling him a freak for what he did to Scott. 

That got his attention. Jeff got up in Arch's face telling him his mom was a whore and that Arch's dad wasn't actually his dad. You know how Arch gets about that. So they went back and forth before Arch shoved Jeff. 

Jeff shoved Arch hard. Really hard. Arch tripped over those tools they'd been using for the showers. We were trying to stop Jeff but he pushed us away way harder than he should have been able to. Jeff was on top of Arch, punching him. Arch was apologizing. Jeff just kept punching.

Anthony ran to get the coach. Sam went to Jeff, tried to pull him away, to talk some sense into him. Jeff grabbed one of the hammers that had fallen. When he swung it down, Sam screamed. He was holding onto Jeff. I ran into the showers and hid. Covering my face and crying. 

I hid until the sound stopped. Until the coaches pulled Sam away from Jeff and dragged Jeff out. Until they came and got me. I think in my head I was still hiding until now.

I didn't tell the police about the church, about how Jeff changed. I didn't try to lie to them, I just didn't want them to think I was crazy or to think I was making something up. 

You guys were the best through all of it. Mom, thank you for holding me while I cried. Dad, thank you for sitting and watching the game with me. I didn't like the beer, but it meant a lot getting one from you.

School still hasn't returned to normal. Everyone says we'll still graduate on time. Honestly, it doesn't even matter anymore.

There's a ton of people posting online about Arch like they knew him. Saying there should be a 5K dedicated to his memory. It pisses me off. They didn't know Arch like that. Not like we knew him. 

I wanted to do our own run for him. To go out to the woods and just go until we couldn't. Sam was down for it but Anthony never replied. He's still not texting us and his parents don't want us going over. He'll come around though, I know it.

Thank you guys for letting Sam stay with us as often as you did. Those late night talks were more important to us than we could ever say. I'm sorry I lied, we were smoking weed that night you caught us giggling. It was probably the first time we'd laughed in a bit. 

Sam was funny like that though. Even before everything he was able to be laughing one second and then sad the next. In one of those talks he told me he had been diagnosed with depression. I probably should have reacted better than a stupid “What the fuck?”

It just caught me off guard. I felt bad I didn't know, and then I was mad when he told me his dad called it “Pussy Disease.” 

He talked a lot about his dad. Even now I hate that guy. I hate the way he treated Sam. Our Sam. My Sam.

The same guy who fought us all to order pineapple pizza. The dude who was the first to sign my cast when I broke my arm.

When he showed me the bruises on his ribs I almost went over to his house by myself. Sam wouldn't let me. He told me “bruises heal” like some sort of wise man. He showed me when the bruises stopped being that ugly purple. Then he showed me when the rash started covering them.

I asked him about it. He said it started when he started having the dreams. 

He said he was dreaming about what happened, and I told him I was too, but he said he was dreaming about what Jeff said. He said when Jeff was hitting Arch he was talking about the church. 

I hadn't thought about that church with everything going on. I asked Sam if Jeff was really still upset about having to go in the church. Sam said that wasn't it. Jeff had been crying and whispering that he wanted to go back.

Jeff had said it was a normal church. I didn't get it. Then Sam started saying it wasn't. He would scratch his side and tell me we should have gone in.

I told him it was “bullshit” and we couldn't know what was gonna happen after he went in. When I would tell Sam that Jeff just changed he would get mad. He started getting mad a lot. He started scaring me.

He was scratching like Jeff, and he was mad like Jeff, but it wasn't the same. Sam would brood, he would give me the cold shoulder. He started talking about how shitty his dad was and how he just wanted him to stop.

The last time he came over I held him. I was holding him as he cried and shook and whispered how much he hated his dad and everything. I tried to ignore him when he said those things. Especially when he was talking about his dad and ending it all. Especially when he started crying about the church.

I wish I had never let him go. I wish I had made him stay. Most of all I wish I was a better friend.

He would still be here if I was.

I remember where I was when I found out. In my room, doomscrolling, when I got a text telling me to check the news. They said there was a shooting and that two people were dead. I had to watch the video three times because I was sure I heard the news guy wrong. I couldn't understand why he was saying Sam's last name.

I tried to call him, I was trying to call him when you guys came in. I didn't get it when you guys started apologizing or when you guys were crying and hugging me. I didn't want to get it. 

He was there two nights before. I held him. I listened to him say he wanted to end it and I just thought it was his emotions talking. I didn't know he meant it.

The news said it looked like a fight. That Sam was just trying to defend himself. That he'd been beaten until he broke. I believe that. Everyone does. What he did to his dad had to be done. But what he did to himself? I still haven't figured out how to forgive him for that.

I don't think I've been okay since. I'm still mad at him. I'm mad at everything lately. Like there's an itching coming from inside my bones. Like my marrow is boiling. 

I feel it when I wake up. I feel it in my dreams. Dreams where I see the church. The church that rings and tells me that I am a fuck up. That I'm a monster. That I'm the reason my friends are dead.

I can't stop itching myself. I scratch and scratch and my fingers come away from my sides bloody and aching. 

I'm scared. I don't want to hurt you guys. I don't want to hurt anyone. I want to run away. I'm going to that church that took my friends. That took everything.

I'm so sorry. I won't tell you where it is. I love you.

I'm going back, and this time, I'm going in.
