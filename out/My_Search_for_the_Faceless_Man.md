Back in 1962, Thalidomide - the drug that devastated countless families - was taken off the market after the outcry from the general public became overbearing. How does the saying go? Oh yeah, regulations are written in blood. Now, what the fuck am I talking about this for? Well, most of the children had become accounted for in some way: raised at home, adopted, institutionalized, or unfortunately perished due to their congenital defects. Now, realize that I said most, not all - most. 

In 1972, an orphanage on the northern shore of Long Island had burnt down, with the fire taking all of its records in the blaze. One thing is known, though: it was home to one of the few thalidomide babies born in New York State, and his body was never recovered from the wreckage. Now, a body not being discovered was VERY unfortunately par for the course for a situation like this: the orphanage wasn’t just for orphans, it was for orphans with deformities and mental illnesses. Home for what those back in the day viewed as the “dregs of society”. So who was REALLY looking for them?

I am. Specifically, for this thalidomide boy. There’s something special, alleged about him. The final worker who survived working at that orphanage, one of the few workers who escaped the blaze, claimed they housed a “faceless thalidomide baby”. No one believed her, but I do. So, I sat down with her for an interview at her house. It was an old, raised ranch style home with antique furniture and no electronics in the home besides an analog television, not even cable. The ticking of the large grandfather clock took over the quietness that loomed within the home as she thought of answer. 

“Miss Rosenthal,” I said, “you can take your time. But I have to know, how the hell did this happen?”

Miss Rosenthal put her hand up and said, “I’m… I’m thinking! I don’t know how he became faceless… but he survived. A tracheostomy was performed to help him breathe, along with giving him a G-tube… He thrived, but was put down by so much staff… I worked in that place because I felt for those children. They were neglected and abused by EVERYONE: from their parents who gave them up, to the staff at the orphanage. I couldn’t stand to see it, but I was one person…”

Miss Rosenthal began to tear up, so I pushed the tissue box closer to her. She sniffled a bit and wiped her eyes, then continued: “I don’t know how, but it was as if he could ‘see’. I know he could hear, he had ears… but no eyes, obviously. How the hell could a boy like him see? Three days before the fire, he took a piece of blank paper from the stack I was holding and drew, using black crayon, some kind of angel… a winged creature. And he pointed to where his eyes would be if he was born with them. Then… the fire. I caught him with a box of matches. He set the fire and then carried me out of there. He was ten years old, strong as a bull, and was able to carry me out. He spared me.” 

“You showed him kindness… mercy. Treated him like a human,” I said, writing notes down on my pad. Miss Rosenthal nodded as she looked down at the table, both regretful and defeated. She rubbed her eyes and stood up, beckoning me to leave. Before I left, I asked: “Have you ever seen him again?” 

Miss Rosenthal said, “His name is William… Billy I called him. The only place he knew as home is where you may find him. He wasn’t there for years, but I get news from some people around that neighborhood. I used to live near there. Some of the people, they know me, I have friends there that still live there after all these years. The rubble has recently been cleaned, and they’re building a school there. He’s been seen at a distance at night. But please, don’t go. He’s a very angry man…”

I thanked Miss Rosenthal, reassured her, and was on my way. Of course I went looking for him. Every night for a two weeks I went to the build site and waited for hours, one time waiting till the sun came up. I’d sit on the same folding chair for hours, sometimes seeing a mirage in the distance, causing me to grip the edge of the seat so hard my knuckles would turn white, and my heart would pound into my throat. But then I’d see it was nothing, a fat fucking waste of another night. 

Then when I would nearly quit, I saw him. Sitting at the building site on a Thursday at 11 pm, he came crawling from what looked like a makeshift tunnel. I could vaguely see his featureless face: he had hair, ears, a chin, but where his eyes, nose, and mouth would be was just a shapeless slate of skin. He put on a baseball cap, fastened his sweater, and began to walk around. I stayed in my seat and lifted up my phone to try and get a photo, turning off the flash as to not disturb or alert him. But before I could even pick my phone all the way up, I saw him turn around and make a run for me. I got up, nearly falling over the chair, and just ran, leaving the chair and my bag of food behind. I sprinted like I never had before, but it wasn’t any use. His feet slammed on the ground with every stride like thunder, and he caught up next to me in seconds. He reached out his hand and grabbed me by my neck, throwing me down on the ground. He flipped me over and slammed his fist onto my face. 

I woke up in what seemed to be an abandoned rail station. There was the sound of dripping of water in the distance, random creaking noises, and the smell of mold and smoke. Looking around, I saw rotting walls and rusted pipes. A light came in from an adjacent room. I tried moving but realized I was chained to a wall. I started shaking the chains and screaming as I struggled. It was no use, I couldn’t get out. A shadow looked at the entrance, followed by its owner: The Faceless Man. 

I looked at him and said: “W-William?”

A voice echoed in my head: “SHE TOLD YOU THE NAME.” 

I winced in pain. Fuck, FUCK! It felt like someone cracked me upside the head with a fucking hammer. My nose dripped blood. 

The voice was quieter, softer now: “You aren’t used to it. My bad. But she told you. Rosenthal.”

“H-how the fuck are you doing this?” I asked him. 

“The angel imbued me with sight and this power. I am chosen to bring forth retribution against humans.” 

“Retribution? That’s…”

William got right in my face. I could see the hole where his trach was… I could hear the air moving in and out of it as he got in my face. 

The voice continued in my head: “Crazy? Was that the word you wanted to say? How crazy am I. I and many others get deformed from a medication. A medication that the company lied to save their ass, and to save the Nazi prick who made the fucking thing. And if I’m so crazy, HOW DO I HAVE NO EYES BUT SEE? HOW DO YOU HEAR ME?”

My nose bled more, and I passed out from the pain… the thunder in my head. 

After an unknown amount of time I woke up. I looked down and saw dried blood on my shoes, and smelled the wet copper smell of my blood. William walked into the room, carrying someone over his shoulder. It was Miss Rosenthal. He threw her body down on the concrete floor, a groan of pain emanated from her bruised mouth. 

“Wh-what the fuck?” I said groggily, using all my energy to push the words out. 

The voice in my head came in again, this time in a hushed tone: “She gave my life away as a fucking sensationalist story piece… turned me into an urban legend… a fetish for your shitty journalism career.”

“H-how do you kn-know?” I asked. 

“I watch all that I can. I should’ve never saved her. You’re all the fucking same. ALL OF YOU!” 

The thunder rattled my head from the inside out. I winced in pain and shouted for him to stop. He laughed. I could hear him laughing in my head. As he laughed, he picked up Miss Rosenthal and twisted her neck. The audible snap and the quick pooling of blood at the base of her neck made me vomit all over my shirt. He kept fucking laughing. 

“Swift judgement. But for you, you took a while. So let’s make sure your punishment, your judgement, does too.”

He grabbed my arm and yanked. I cried out in pain, but asked, “WHY? How do you know it’s not a demon fooling you. She told me it didn’t look like an angel!”

William slapped me and grabbed my neck. “SHUT THE FUCK UP!” his voice boomed in my head. 

He grabbed my left arm and yanked and twisted it. I screamed and cried in pain, but he didn’t stop. The laughing began in my head again. Then my arm came off. My vision began to go blurry as blood poured from the socket where my arm was. He threw my arm on the floor and went to the other one. Just as he began yanking, I heard footsteps. 

“The screaming’s in here!” 

A group of armed SWAT officers came running into the room. William turned around. One of the officers screamed “WHAT THE FUCK!?”. William jumped up and pounced on the officer. They raised their guns and opened fire on William. Then it was over. As William’s body fell limp, full of bullet holes and torn flesh, my world went dark. 

I woke up in the hospital after being unconscious for three days. Supposedly I needed a blood transfusion, tetanus shot, and so much more. I looked to my left and the absence of my arm crushed me as I realized just how real this all was. 

My three year long search had ended. I had the story, the police to back it up… but it didn’t mean anything anymore. William was right, all people saw was a sensationalist story, but overlooked the dark nature of humanity. A boy was wronged, deformed by a drug… a drug that the company that made it denied the detrimental effects of. The drug made by a eugenicist, a Nazi. 

We’re all fucked up and maybe deserved some sort of retribution. Maybe William wasn’t just a victim, but a reflection of our base nature: a sad, twisted, angry species hellbent on revenge and power. 

Or maybe it’s none of that. I don’t know. All I know is that I need sleep, and a new fucking job.
