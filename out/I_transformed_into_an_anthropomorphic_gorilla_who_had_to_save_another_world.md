That night, as usual, I turned off the lights, lay down in bed, and tried to fall asleep. I usually get lost in my thoughts before going to sleep. Sometimes these thoughts are frightening, and I try to suppress them and replace them with something else. This time, my mind was filled with all kinds of things: strange paranormal stories, conspiracy theories, political news, and so on.

Eventually, after about half an hour, I managed to fall asleep... although I'm not even sure whether you could call it sleep. I dreamed that I was being pulled into an indigo-colored vortex that seemed to be made of energy. Suddenly, the dream ended and another dream, or maybe some other reality, began.

I found myself in a dimly lit, old place with decaying, rust covered steel walls. It looked like an abandoned factory. I had a strange feeling, as though I was no longer human. I looked down at my hands and saw that they were enormous and covered in thick, dark gray fur. All of a sudden, my perception shifted, and it was as if I could see myself from a distance. I had transformed into a tall, gorilla-like humanoid creature...

In fear and amazement, I stared into my own green eyes and touched my face. I was some kind of bizarre wild beast. A gorilla? Bigfoot? I had no idea! What was even stranger was where I was and why I was there. Why didn't this feel like a dream?!

My perception returned to normal, and I noticed something resembling a portal made of white light suspended in the air. I raised my hand to shield my eyes from its intense brightness. Gradually, a girl began to appear inside the portal. She had pale, almost ghostly white skin and long, dark blonde hair. She was wearing a long white dress, and her eyes were a clear sky blue, completely devoid of pupils. She looked about twenty to twenty-two years old.

She stared at me in silence for a few moments, then broke into a hideous, unnerving grin that stretched across her entire face. It was obvious she didn't know how to smile and was merely trying to imitate one to make me feel more at ease. She stared at me with that terrifying grin for about ten seconds before speaking. Parts of her face seemed to be melting like liquid as she said, "I summoned you. She is looo...oooking for the savior..." Her voice seemed to glitch as she dragged out the word. "...Goo...ooo to the mansion and save the savior, or evil will consume everything!"

Apparently, I had been summoned to a parallel world to save someone who was referred to as the savior. I couldn't even think logically about what was happening, let alone object to it. It was as though some force or perhaps my own instincts was guiding me. In that state, I was hardly human at all.

"Who are you?!" I asked.

The girl replied, "I am the goddess of loooooove!" Her voice glitched again. "Go to the mansion, the one in the middle of the city. I will support you."

She then stared at me silently for a little while longer, wearing that same terrifying smile, before vanishing along with the portal. I spent a moment thinking about her and her robotic voice, then began searching the factory for an exit. I eventually found a door and stepped outside.

I found myself in an incredibly beautiful place. There were charming, traditionally designed houses surrounded by gardens full of flowers and trees. But something seemed terribly wrong. There was absolute silence everywhere. I couldn't even hear a single bird. I looked up at the sky. It was daytime and everything was bright, yet there was no sign of the sun.

I scratched my cheek with a finger, and heard something that sounded like whispering. I looked around and noticed two figures wearing hoodies, one white and the other gray. They looked somewhat human, but it was obvious that they weren't actually human.

The figure wearing the white hoodie was a young, pale-skinned boy with black hair. There was just one problem: his left eye was located in the middle of his forehead, and his mouth was on his left cheek. The other figure was dark-skinned and had no mouth at all; where his mouth should have been, there was nothing but bare skin along his jaw.

The boy in the white hoodie muttered something incomprehensible, pulled out a switchblade, and started walking toward me. Concerned, I prepared to defend myself. He approached several times, and each time I backed away. He kept trying to stab me with the knife. Eventually, he lunged at me, and I struck him hard across the face with my right palm. The blow sent him flying, and his head slammed into a rock. Blood splattered across the stone, and he fell motionless to the ground.

The other boy, who had a set of brass knuckles on his hand, rushed toward me and landed two powerful punches. They left me somewhat injured. I let out a growl like a wild animal, grabbed him by the throat, lifted him into the air, and hurled him several meters away. He crashed into the wall of a house with such force that the wall cracked.

Panting heavily, I noticed an orange cat staring at me. I looked in the other direction and saw an enormous building. It was probably the mansion the strange girl had mentioned.

Just then, I became aware of some noise and noticed several people watching me from a distance. I also saw that the cat's head had somehow transformed into the head of a bald man. I gathered all my strength and began leaping away from the scene, covering six or seven meters with each jump, heading toward the building.

I reached the mansion, an enormous old house with ivy growing along its walls. I was about to open the large wooden front door when I noticed it was already unlocked, so I stepped inside. I looked at the walls and noticed several paintings depicting incredibly strange surreal scenes or perhaps cosmic horror would be a more appropriate description. They gave me an intensely disturbing and frightening feeling. In one of the paintings, a horrifying red octopus deep beneath the ocean seemed almost alive, as though it were watching me.

As I walked through the hall, I heard a noise. I cautiously peeked around the wall and saw two people in a room filled with enormous metal bookshelves packed with books. One was wearing a long silver robe and a bird-like mask resembling those worn by medieval plague doctors. The other was a bald, muscular man wearing a black tank top. He reminded me of the wrestler Alexander Karelin.

I heard footsteps, and a little girl approached them. She was wearing a dark gray jacket, had Japanese looking facial features and long hair, and her eyes were completely white, without pupils. She began talking to the two men. Although I couldn't understand what they were saying, it seemed as though their conversation and intentions were being transmitted directly into my mind in the form of telepathic images. Perhaps the goddess of love was responsible.

I saw an image of the savior. He was a newborn baby who was being kept on the mansion's top floor. Little girl seemed to be an evil witch, her intention was to trap him inside an eighty-year-long dream. It was an unimaginably long, multilayered dream from which escape was impossible, and anyone who entered it would die. I also learned that the little girl or, more accurately, the witch had previously killed other children in the same way.

The telepathic images suddenly stopped, and I realized that the three of them had noticed me and were staring in my direction. The witch gave me a malicious grin, burst into laughter, and quickly ran away, heading up the mansion's staircase. The plague doctor and the muscular man charged at me, but I let out a roar, leaped forward and hurled myself into the bookshelves, bringing them crashing down on top of them.

I was about to make my way toward the stairs when the muscular man, having crawled out from beneath the fallen shelves, let out a scream and rammed his shoulder into my back, knocking me to the floor. I felt an excruciating pain in my back, and it seemed that my spine had been injured. He began screaming and repeatedly punching me in the face, leaving it covered in blood. It felt as though my nose and several of his teeth had been broken.

Knowing that losing this fight could mean my own death or the end of an entire world made me fight with everything I had to avoid passing out. I summoned all my strength and slammed my head into his face. The sound of his skull cracking echoed through the room. He stared at me for a moment, his nose bloody, before collapsing motionless on top of me.

I pushed his body aside, got back to my feet, and, despite the terrible pain in my back, raced up the stairs as fast as I could.

Yes, this was a strange and horribly world, even worse than my own. It seemed that corruption had consumed most of it. I hadn't encountered a single pleasant being there, and it was as though everyone except the goddess of love wanted to destroy me for some reason. Still, I believed that everyone deserved a second chance. Everyone deserved a savior.

I reached a room with a beautiful blue door and stepped inside. A baby was lying on the bed, and the witch was approaching him. At the sound of the door opening, she quickly turned around and stared at me.

"Why?" I asked.

The witch replied, "Love game."

I frowned. "What?!"

The witch hurled several pieces of paper covered in strange symbols at me. They stuck to my body.

"Would it make any difference if I actually had a reason for my evil deeds?"

The places where the papers had attached themselves began to burn with an unbearable intensity. I screamed in agony, as though the nerves throughout my body were being set on fire.

The witch continued, "Politicians in your own world have reasons for committing mass murder. Do you think they're any better than me?"

Overwhelmed by the pain, I dropped to my knees. After a few moments, I managed to say, "No..."

She approached me and placed another piece of paper on my forehead. An unimaginable pain flooded my head. I felt as though my brain would burn if I didn't somehow escape this situation. I struggled to move, but it seemed that the papers were draining my energy.

The witch pulled a pair of scissors from her pocket and slowly began raising them.

I looked at the baby and saw that he was staring back at me. He seemed like the purest thing in existence. His eyes began to glow, and I watched as the symbols on the pieces of paper faded away. The papers lost their power and fell helplessly to the floor.

The witch was visibly shocked by what had happened. She glared at the savior with an incredibly twisted, furious expression.

"You little bastard! I'll send you to your mommy and daddy!"

With a scream, I punched her with all my strength. The impact sent her flying into a wall before she collapsed to the floor. I got back to my feet and saw that her face had transformed from that of a little Japanese girl into that of an ugly old woman with a huge nose. She coughed up blood several times and struggled to stand. This time, her face changed into that of a young man with long blond hair. She wasn't a child, a woman, or a man... She seemed more like the embodiment of evil itself.

I approached her and was about to strike again when I felt something pierce my leg. I looked down and saw that the plague doctor had entered the room and was injecting my leg with an enormous syringe filled with some kind of green substance. The substance was melting and eating away at my leg. I let out a scream of pain and punched the plague doctor so hard in the head that his steel mask shattered. He was thrown out of the room and tumbled down the stairs.

The witch, whose head now resembled that of a deer, wiped the blood from her mouth and transformed her face back into that of the little Japanese girl. She looked at my left leg, which had been completely melted away, and said, "Darkness always triumphs over light."

Her face split open, and long, black tentacles emerged from it. A mixture of male, female, and children's voices, along with animal sounds, filled the room.

I stared at her for a moment, remembering my own traumas, then said, "Maybe you're right, but darkness must be held accountable for its actions!"

With the last of my strength, I lunged at her, grabbed her tightly, and hurled her against the window. The glass shattered, and we were both thrown out of the mansion. We fell from a height of several dozen meters and crashed to the ground.

I suddenly jolted awake and found myself in my bedroom. I was terrified, and my heart was pounding. Anxiously, I touched my left leg, and when I realized it was still there, I breathed a sigh of relief.

Had it all been a dream?! But it had felt far too real...

Suddenly, I heard the voice of the goddess of love saying:

"Thaaa...aaank yooo...ooouuu!" Her voice glitched as she spoke.

At that moment, I realized that what I had experienced wasn't a dream...
