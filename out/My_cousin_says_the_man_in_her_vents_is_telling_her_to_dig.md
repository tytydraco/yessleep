My grandma renovates old homes and rents them out. My grandma had 7 children and they'll usually help out on the side for some extra money. We come from a small town where the houses are pretty old, run down but cheap. I've spent some summers helping out, she'll put you to work but she pays you really good too. She's a fiery spirit, never a dull moment with that woman. I've never experienced anything really, I honestly believe ghosts don't like me or something. But there were a few times where I'd be in a house alone. The work kept you busy and your mind away from it, but then the work would slow down and you'd be forced to face the atmosphere that was so thick you were choking on it. I'd usually just sit outside if it was warm out or sit in my car if it was cold to take a break away from it.

My grandma would rent houses out to her kids, usually for a good deal. If you go past our town, there was a big stretch of farms and forests. You still got to go to the nice schools in our area but you had to drive a bit to see the heart of the town. There was this old house in the middle of that. It was huge. My grandpa is a very sweet man who is tough as nails. He never spoke to me too much but if he had to give you bad news he'd stare at the ground and take off his hat and wring it in his hands. He is **very** smart, he could build you a 5 door refrigerator with a toothpick and some dental floss. If something was wrong with your house, your car, your marriage, you'd call my grandpa and he'd fix it.

Him and my grandma were a dream team when it came to renovations. My grandma thinks quick on her feet, she's good with people too. Naturally charming and knows how to strike up a good deal. My grandpa knew how to fix any problem. They'd tear through an old house and build it new in less than a year. When they finally got their hands on this house they were ecstatic. There was something about this house that stood out to you. It looked like an attractive older man in a suit. It was sleek but impressive. Didn't help that it was the only house for a few miles. My grandma loved that house, it had been a goal she had been working towards for a while and she finally got it. They didn't waste anytime and got to work immediately.

The house had been abandoned for a while, they expected there to be a few problems with it. There was mold, a caved in part of the ceiling and holes in the walls. Didn't matter, they could fix it. Some animals or something chewed through the wires in the house and there were leaky pipes, didn't matter they could fix it. The roof was old, worn and leaking and it didn't matter, until my grandpa fell off his ladder. It's a scary feeling when a man made of steel begins to wear and tear. He was rushed to the hospital, my grandma made sure they ran every test they could. Nothing was broken, just a few bumps and bruises but he wasn't ok. My grandpa had cancer before, beat it in a year. But this time it came back with a vengeance. His white blood cells were incredibly low, there was hope but he had to fight.

My family took this news very hard. My grandpa was a superhero to his kids and grandchildren. He had to rest now, which was the hardest thing for him to do. My grandma is tough, I've only seen her cry once and that was years after this. She didn't let anyone see her nervous. She put her head down and went to work, she found my grandpa the best doctors, every meal was packed full of superfoods and vitamins that he needed. She had hope that spread through all of us and she wouldn't rest for a minute until he got better.

Even while he was sick, my grandma wouldn't stop working on that house. My grandma had 3 sons, 3 son in-laws and countless grandchildren at her disposal, my family is built to work. But without my grandpa around, it slowed down a lot. It was early fall when he got hurt and soon it was winter. That house just did not want to get fixed. Brand new pipes would burst, the heating and air conditioning gave up and a few times the mesh lining and joint compound over holes in the wall would just fall out. The house stressed my grandma out a lot. She had spent so much money paying people to fix stuff that the once charming house was turning into a black hole.

But she got lucky, one of her daughters was infatuated with the house. She wanted it, bad. She talked to my grandma about it and they made a deal, My aunt would help pay for some of the remodeling costs and her husband would help fix it for free. In return the house was theirs with a very affordable monthly rent. My uncle lost his job a few weeks prior to this deal and was happy to have something to do, my aunt fortunately had a cushy job that made this dream a real thing. My grandma was relieved that the house wasn't a complete loss, so they pushed on.

By the beginning of spring next year, the houses interior was finally beaten and polished into submission. All the house needed was a fresh coat of paint and some new windows. My grandpa was doing a lot better so he was able to help out and finish up any loose ends. Soon they were done and they could finally move on to the next project. My aunt and uncle moved in with their daughter. This was their dream home. On the second floor there were 3 bedrooms, my aunt and uncle took the biggest one while my cousin had the second biggest. They turned the 3rd upstairs bedroom into my aunts office. The master bedroom was downstairs but it was turned into my cousins play room.

They hosted a few parties in their house, it was big enough to hold my whole family and some of their friends. It fit 30 of us comfortably. I was born at an awkward time, my older cousins were 4 years older than me and my younger cousins were 4 years younger than me, I used to joke that my family had litters and I was born in the middle of them. I was pretty weird too so I normally just kept to myself and would watch tv. I liked to walk around daydream too so I always just looked kind of lost.

When my aunt hosted her first party it was on New Years eve, everyone gushed about how beautiful the house was. I remember it well. It had tall ceiling and was decorated perfectly, my aunt had very good taste. It was rustic with wooden tables and countertops with dazzling lights thrown around the house, everything was from antique stores and boutiques. It came together to create almost a nest like ambiance when you first walked in. I remember looking around, looking at all the knickknacks and pictures. The party took off and soon everyone was a little tipsy and separated into groups.

Since I was the odd one out, I floated around to all of these rooms. There was something about me that was always just very awkward, my family didn't really like to talk to me but I don't blame them. I kind of just made problems and started arguments so consequently I was left out and got used to it. First I hung out with my older sister and my cousins, it was short lived though because she shook me off as their conversations diverged into drinking and smoking weed outside while the adults were to busy to notice. I followed them to the front door but I wasn't allowed outside because it was "too cold". I found my mom in the dining room next, she was half way through a conversation about people I didn't know so I just sat down next to her and ate a cupcake. They decided to play a board game together and the rules were too complicated for me to understand so I went to go find my dad. He was with the other guys in the side room watching a game on TV. I sat down next to my dad but this room wasn't my favorite. The whole room was grey with no windows. The house still had electrical problems and it didn't matter if all the lights were on the house would swallow it whole and make it dark. The room had this weird old smell that I couldn't get past and all the men yelling at the screen made me uncomfortable. As the men fell into their bottles of beer I slipped out.

My brother is my favorite family member. I honestly think I have the best little brother in the world, but I didn't always feel this way. When I was a kid I think I was jealous of how everyone loved him but would look the other way when I came around. But eventually I found him in the play room with the other kids. They liked to play with me because I had a pretty impressive imagination, I could make good stories. But today they already found a game that they liked, so I tried to join in. There was only one overhead light that lit up with room and it would flicker every minute or so, the room felt risky to me. Like at any moment the bulb would give out and we'd be surrounded by the darkness that consumed the house. The play room was connected to their laundry room. There was no light coming from it. The darkness crept into the playroom when I stared at it, and I felt like it was staring right back at me. I didn't understand how the younger kids weren't scared by it. I stared into it, falling out of the game we were playing. For some reason it demanded my whole attention. I heard stuff coming from there, weird sounds like traces of something moving. It freaked me out so I didn't stay there for too long.

There was something about the house that made it feel like the light was important. Like it was a scarce resource that kept something at bay. None of the rooms were bright, even during the day. The thing that lived in the house with them took owner ship of the dark corners and the spaces in the corner of your eye. I felt like I was being watched by a cruel old soul that didn't like me being there, I wasn't invited or wanted. After I left the playroom, I went to the bathroom that was across the hall. I sat down and soon the light completely cut out.

I sat there with my heart beating out of my chest. Too afraid to move or something might know I'm here. I felt pins pricking my eyes that were shot wide open trying to see anything. I could only vaguely see my own reflecting in the mirror ahead of me. Before I could cry the light turned back on half heartedly, flickering. I tried my best to get out of there quickly. They had one of those Christmas toilet paper things that plays a song when you roll it and it scared me pretty good. I didn't wash my hands and left the bathroom. In the hallway I felt odd, I wasn't afraid of the dark. Why did this house scare me so bad? I chalked it up to my imagination and decided to explore the house a bit more to waste some time.

If you went into the dining room where my mom was, there were two doors to your right. The first door was the side room where the men were watching football, and I didn't know what was in the other door. The women were still involved in their board game so they didn't notice me slide in. The room was dark but as my eyes adjusted I walked around, it was mostly empty. There was a window to the left where I could see my older cousins huddled up and laughing so I watched them for a little bit. Behind me was a staircase, I remember turning around looking up them and getting a strong feeling. Something was up there. I put my foot on the first step as the curiosity itched in my mind. My eyes were glued on the top as I slowly walked up them. The stairs were loud and old. On the third step my aunt heard me and with a sharp call of my name I was informed I wasn't allowed up there.

At the end of the night I ended up liking the front room the best. One of my aunts was in there charging her phone and she was cool to me. She hung out with me and didn't treat me like a stain. She turned on the TV for me and we sat next to each other until I fell asleep. I'm pretty sure I missed the ball drop because the next thing I remember was my mom waking me up saying that we needed to go home.

My aunt and her family only lived in that house for a year or two before they moved out. I remember not really asking much about it because I didn't care. My family and I had some rough years. I struggled a lot with my mental health, I held some resentment towards them and went my own way. Recently however I've slowly started talking to them again and I found out why they left the house so quickly, this is the story I was told.

While my aunt and uncle loved the house, there was something about it that made them all uneasy. My uncle wired the house himself, my grandpa checked it too. But the house would burn up lightbulbs like crazy, they wouldn't even last a full month. The lights would flicker no matter what they did, the TV would randomly cut out and parts of the house would lose power constantly. They called every possible number that could help and they never found the source of the problem. They even tried an array of different light bulbs but none of them would last. There was also a problem with the heating and air-conditioning. In the winter, the house just couldn't hold heat. They would wake up in the middle of the night and could practically see their own breath, they had to spend a pretty penny to get a few of those standing heaters to keep the house livable. The summer wasn't easy either, the house would generally stay at a comfortable temperature but this ball of freezing air would randomly appear. You could be in one room and be fine, then an icy breeze would blow through you.

The house was just generally creepy as well, we all felt it. But the person who got the worst of it was my cousin. She was young, kindergarten age. When they moved in she changed a lot. She's a kind hearted and smart kid, she makes friends quickly and is just the kind of person you want to be around. She's always been like that, but when they moved in she sort of pulled back and regressed into herself. She was always tired and seemed more fussy than usual. Her parents blamed it on the move being stressful but this was way out of the norm for her.

It wasn't uncommon for my aunt and uncle to wake up frequently during the night in this new house, you just couldn't get good sleep there. They thought it was just them until they heard a small voice coming from down the hall. They both got up and immediately followed it until they realized it was coming from my cousins door. They go in and see her sitting upright in her bed, she turns to look at them and rubs her eyes. The ask her why she was up and who she was talking to. She doesn't say who but she points to a dark corner of the wall. My uncle checks her room then the rest of the house while my aunt sits with her trying to get her to go back to sleep. My uncle didn't find anything.

My aunt and uncle got used to hearing my cousin talking to herself in the middle of the night. The fear that was once attached to it soon melted off as it was chalked up to an overactive imagination. My cousin was doing great in school, made a ton of new friends and was involved in an after school dance group that she loved. By all means she was growing up normally, an imaginary friend didn't change that.

They were sleeping one summer night when they woke up to a horrible feeling.

My aunt gets up first, call it mothers intuition but she knew something was wrong. She goes into my cousins room and finds nothing. Her daughter was gone. She gets my uncle and they run around the house checking everywhere while calling her name, but she's nowhere to be found. My aunt calls my grandma as she runs outside but as soon as she opens the front door she sees her. My cousin is on her hands and knees crying while desperately digging at the dirt around the side walk. My aunt runs to her and grabs her demanding to know why she was out here so late at night. Through tears my cousin says,

"The man in my vent won't let me sleep unless I find his treasure."

My cousin was very upset and kept crying for a while, while my aunt stood outside with her daughter trying to wrap her head around what was going on. She goes back inside and sits my cousin down at a table while she calls for her husband and tells her what she just said. While my uncle goes around the house looking for this man, my cousin finally tells her mom what's been keeping her up at night.

She tells her that every night there is a man made of mist who crawls out of her vent and stares at her and won't let her sleep. He used to just stare and shake her bed but soon he started talking to her. He'd tell her to come outside with him and she'd always say no. At first the man understood and would do funny things to make her laugh, but as time moved on he began to get angry and it scared her. He told her that there was buried treasure outside and she needed to go dig it up, for him. She said no countless times but this night she'd finally said ok. He took her by the hand and led her downstairs. He unhooked the latch on the door that was out of her reach and walked with her outside to a specific place. Then he pointed at the ground and told her that's where the treasure was buried. She started digging but he kept telling her to go faster and that someone was coming.

That someone was fortunately my aunt who was now seated at a table with her crying, terrified child. My family doesn't take chances, were skeptics but we do believe in the paranormal. My aunt did not want to find what ever was buried out there, she wanted to make sure he daughter never saw the man again. My cousin slept in bed with her parents until they moved out. She slowly started to open back up and began telling them more about what she was seeing in the house.

She said there was a woman that lived in the mirror with no hair, but she wasn't afraid of her though and they'd stare at each other and the woman would make funny faces. She also said that there was a tall skinny man that lived in the laundry room and would get stuck in the walls and would cry a lot, but that he couldn't see her. The only one that scared her was the man in the vent. After she told my aunt and uncle this, the house got worse.

My uncle started seeing things out of the corner of his eye, mostly the bald woman. He'd be shaving in the mirror when she'd peek her head out from behind him. My uncle would turn around and look but she'd be gone.

After my cousin told her parents about what was happening she was never left alone, not even for a second. My aunt was with her daughter in the play room when an empty laundry basket was thrown out of the laundry room missing my cousin head by inches crashing into the wall in front of her.

My aunt started to see things too, she'd go downstairs sometimes and as she was in the dining room heading to the kitchen she'd look up and see a tall shadow. The shadow looked barely human, with arms bent in strained positions. But then in an instant it would be gone. One night while she was sleeping she woke up to all of the air being pushed out of her. She tried to open her eyes and move but she couldn't. She smelt this disgusting putrid odor as she struggled to take control of her body. She was finally able to open her eyes when she saw an old man lying on the ceiling above her staring at her. His mouth was moving quickly as if he was talking but she could only hear the sound of her own ears ringing.

They sold the house and got out of there as soon as possible. They lived in a nice apartment complex near by for a bit but then they decided to move to North Carolina with my grandma and cancer free grandpa. My cousin is doing great, she's a cheerleader now. I haven't seen her in ages, but I do know, she remembers everything.
