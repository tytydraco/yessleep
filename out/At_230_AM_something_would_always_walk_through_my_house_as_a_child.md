I remember this happening to me when I was 16, still living in my parents’ house. My room was right next to my parents’. Their room was quite large, with a king-size bed in the middle of the room. My room was smaller, with a single twin-size bed to the right of the room. When I laid a certain way, I was facing the door entering my room. My bedroom and my parents’ bedroom doors both went into a long hallway, which led to the living room, kitchen, and dining room.

 

I remember every night, starting from when I was 5, a single occurrence would play out. At 2:30 AM, I would hear thumping footsteps exiting my parents’ room. They would then walk halfway down the hallway, pause for a minute, then walk the rest of the way into the living room. They would not walk back. This would happen every night, without fail. Sometimes when I would wake up before my parents in the morning, I would walk into the living room and see no one there. I would even check my parents’ room, and they would both be in bed, still asleep with no sign of them moving at all.

 

We never owned any pets, so this was perturbing to me, and eventually I decided to find out what was going on. One night when I was 15, I lay in my bed, awake, waiting for the moment at 2:30 AM. Eventually, I heard the noises. First, I heard what sounded like a creaking coming from my parents’ room. Then I heard the footsteps, slowly walking. What I saw next was chilling. Something extremely large passed by my door. It was shrouded in darkness so I couldn’t get a good look at it, but from what I could see, it was way too tall to be either of my parents. After it passed my room, the same thing happened. It paused halfway down the hall, then walked into the living room, where the thing stayed for the rest of the night. I wouldn’t get any sleep that night.

 

I told my parents what I saw the next morning over breakfast. After I said what I saw, my dad completely froze for a couple of seconds, before continuing to eat. My mom, after a long pause, told me that I must have been dreaming. “Monsters aren’t real”, she would tell me, the same thing she’d hammered into my head since I was 3. She’d tell me a lot of weird things when I was a kid. She’d tell me monsters weren’t real, even when I never asked her if they were. She told me never to look under her bed, because she has a lot of “private things” under there. She was really strict about me staying in bed, too. She was always telling me that no matter how bad I wanted to get up in the night, I must never leave my bed until morning. I never thought anything about it.

 

I should have.

 

I started to sleep through the occurrence at 2:30, not paying any mind to it. I began to write it off, thinking it was probably just one of my parents or a reoccurring dream.

 

When I turned 16, a couple days after my birthday, I couldn’t fall asleep one night. I spent all night tossing and turning in bed, but for some reason, I just wouldn’t fall asleep. At 2:30, I heard the creaking. I froze for a second, I had completely forgotten about the 2:30 occurrence. As usual, I heard the footsteps exit my parents’ room, pause down the hallway, then continue into the living room. I lay in my bed, thinking about what I could be hearing. I ruled out the possibility of this being a dream, since I was wide awake the whole night. I decided I was finally going to see what was walking out of my parents’ room every night.

 

I slowly got out of bed, being careful not to wake my parents. I inched my way down the hall and saw something that made me freeze. I saw large, dusty footprints on the ground, too big to be human. They led into the living room. I grit my teeth. There was no going back now. I needed to know. I crept down the hallway and peered into the living room. There was something standing in the middle of the room.

 

It’s hard to describe whatever the hell I was looking at. I couldn’t see too well, but I could make out the thing much better this time. It was too tall to fit in the room, so its neck was bent at an unnatural angle. Its knees were bent, so it was much taller than I originally thought. Its arms were also long, reaching down so far that its palms were resting flat on the ground. Its hands were made up of three clawed fingers. The thing looked incredibly emaciated, I was able to see its skin tightly wrapped around the shape of its spine and ribs on its back.

 

When I saw the thing, I slapped a hand over my mouth to keep from screaming, but a small yelp still escaped my mouth. The thing turned around, and I was able to see its face. Its face was the most horrifying part. Imagine a normal human face. Then imagine it twisted. Like, twisted into a swirl, so much that it barely resembles a face anymore. That’s what the thing’s face looked like.

 

The thing opened what I think was its mouth and let out a gurgled cry. It sounded like a mix between an elk and a human scream. Some sort of black liquid burbled and oozed out of its orifice, then dribbled down the side of its head and to the ground. It sizzled when it touched the floor. I fought the primal urge to scream in terror.

 

Just then, I heard footsteps coming down the hallway. I turned around to see my father standing in the hallway. He had something in his hands, what looked like a long pole. The thing locked onto my father and let out a deafening screech, the black liquid that was lingering on the corners of its mouth spewing out in every direction. A drop landed on my hand, which started to burn like I just poured boiling water on it. I gripped my hand and screamed, causing the thing to turn towards me again.

 

My father took the opportunity to rush at the thing with the pole, which was now brightly flickering and loudly crackling at the end, the end which he jabbed into the side of the thing. The thing screeched again and spasmed, then dropped to the floor, seizing and contorting, before slowly stopping moving all together.

 

My father, paying no attention to me, walked over to the creature and picked up one of its arms with a huff, dragging it to his bedroom. I followed him and watched as he lifted the dust ruffle under his bed. I saw a large hole under the bed. I guess that’s why my mother didn’t want me to look under there. When he finished stuffing the thing under the bed, he finally turned his attention to me. He walked over to me and held out his hand.

“Give me your hand. Let me see the burn.” He said, in his usual tired tone. I gave him my hand, which I noticed had a large burn mark on it.

“Come to the closet. Let’s get this fixed up.” He practically drags me to the closet in the hallway, where we keep a large first aid kit.

As my father treated my hand, I asked the question. “What was that thing?”

He didn’t answer. He seemed almost hyper-focused on fixing my hand. He was quickly disinfecting the burn and wrapping a bandage around it.

When he finished, he started to hurriedly pack the first aid kid. “You need to hide this injury from your mother. Wear long sleeves, gloves, whatever. She can’t know you saw it.”

“Saw what?” I whisper-screamed. “What in god’s name was that thing?”

After a long pause, my father just said “It’s best if you don’t know.”

 

That was the last time I ever heard about the thing from my father. I still have the burn scar on my hand today.

 

I rarely see my parents anymore. My mother recently developed Alzheimer’s, which has left her in a broken state. She resides in a healthcare facility, in the memory ward. Every so often, I’ll go visit her to make sure she doesn’t forget me. My father comes sometimes too. However, every time I visit my mother, when she sees my hand, the one with the scar, she panics. She starts babbling and convulsing, screaming words like “hole” and “scourge”, but one word she always says is “venator”.

 

I got curious and I looked up what a “venator” was, and it led me to a translation. Venator apparently means “hunter” in Latin.

 

I think my mother was screaming the name of the creature.
