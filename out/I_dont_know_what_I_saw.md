I used to have a friend who had a house literally in the middle of nowhere, no neighbours for miles and woodland and fields surrounding it. I remember him inviting me over for a sleepover one night and as I was going through some personal issues at home, I said yes.

Everytime I pulled up to his house whether it be light or dark outside, I instantly got chills. It was an old victorian house in the middle of a field/wooded area, its enough to give anybody shivers. I remember walking in and being greeted by his parents and having a little catch up before going to my friends room. I hadn't seen them in about 2 months due to my friend being ill so it was pretty nice to talk to them.

Whilst walking up the stairs to my friends room, I felt a shiver down my spine, like something had walked straight through me. I shrugged it off thinking it was just a room and finally entered his room. I greeted him and caught him up with all my life drama. We played games and had a really fun night, until it was time to sleep.

My friend's household were all early sleepers, making me quite uncomfortable as my household were very late sleepers in comparison. My friend went to sleep way before me so I was just left awake. I stayed up on my phone surprisingly reading stories from this exact sub reddit. This is the 'fun' part.

It was around 2 AM, and I remember closing my eyes to atleast try and go to sleep, and then I heard something coming from the woods. I still don't remember what it was exactly but it sounded almost identical to a chainsaw. I felt uneasy in this moment like any other person would, just remember, I was in the middle of nowhere, with barely any signal. I went to look out the window and noticed my friend had a backyard light, it was still too dark to see further than the treeline but still pretty good visibility.

I remember seeing something moving right at the end of the yard, but brushed it off as an animal or something. Even though I thought about it being an animal, I woke my friend up. I told him what had happened and his tired expression turned into a worried one. I asked him what was wrong and he responded with possibly the most chilling sentence I've ever heard. "We try to keep him away." I was obviously confused and asked him to tell me more, but he never answered and woke his parents up.

His parents asked if I had my house keys and when I said yes, they told me to gather my things and drove me home. The car ride back was silent. I still don't know what happened that night or what it was that I heard and saw, but I havnt heard from that friend in about 5 years.
