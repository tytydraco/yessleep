I have a movie club with my aunt. We both love horror and even though I’m only 14, she lets me see some really fucked up movies sometimes. I love her and our club.



The rules of our club are very simple.

1.       She picks the movies.

2.       We can jump, we can cover our eyes, we can hold hands, but absolutely no screaming.



We call it the No Screaming -club and we meet up at her place once a month, when she has the night free. We had already agreed on the next meet up when she texted me to let me know that her friend would be joining us. Like I didn’t know what “friend” meant. But it was cool because my aunt is cool and I want her to be happy. And nobody else has been allowed to meet her “friend”, so that felt special.



When I got over to her place, she had the movie all set up with popcorn in a bowl and the light dimmed down low. She said her friend would come by later so we could start up the movie. We got comfy on the couch and she turned the movie on. About 20 minutes in, when I had already forgotten that the friend was even supposed to come over, there was a knock.



My aunt got up and looked down at me, smiling.



“Club rules, remember,” she whispered.



That was definitely odd, but I figured it was just that the rules applied to her friend too and I was allowed to point that out if the rules were broken. It made some sort of sense.



Then instead of heading to the door, she went to her bedroom. Again, definitely odd, but maybe she had something there she wanted to give her friend the moment they got there?



A moment later, she came back, looking at me and touching her finger to her lips with a sneaky smile on her face. Trailing behind her was something huge!



I don’t know if I can quite describe it. It looked a bit like a bat. It had huge pointed ears sitting almost on the top of its head, brushing the ceiling and making a soft shushing sound. Its head and torso were covered in grey-brown fur, sticking out in tufts in some places, like someone had ran their hands through it just a moment before. It walked a bit hunched over, with long arms barely off the ground. I couldn’t see its feet, but there was no thump of a shoe or soft pad of a sock, but a click. Like a dog’s nails on hardwood.



I did not scream, club rules, but I must have been staring, my eyes huge and my whole body shaking. I could feel the sticky cold in my armpits and down the back of my neck. There was no way the thing didn’t smell the absolute dread that washed over me.



My aunt casually sat back down on the couch, squeezing me close to the armrest and leaving space on her other side. She patted the cushion almost playfully with excited little taps and the thing lowered itself there, the couch creaking under its weight.



I forced my head back toward the screen, but I was not watching the movie anymore. I stared at the dark, crooked legs I could see just past my aunt’s, up to their knobbly knees. The legs looked almost spindly and I wondered how on Earth could stick like legs like that carry such a hulking form around. How it could even balance with only two toes, each ending in a sharp, pointy nail, with the heels looking much the same.



From over the top of my aunt’s head, I could see the wolf like face of the creature. Covered in thick brown fur, it had a snout and sharp yellow eyes. And when it smiled at something on the screen, I could see fangs. I must have reeked by then, my t-shirt clinging to my skin, my brain screaming to get up and go, but my body frozen. My aunt must have smelled me by then too.



But she didn’t seem bothered at all. She was leaning toward the thing, almost cuddling, and she kept whispering to it. And it whispered back! Not words I could understand, but something low and garbled. They seemed almost lost in their own world, not even glancing at me, their eyes on each other and only occasionally drifting back the TV and the movie still playing there.



I almost could have talked myself into believing that I was seeing things, that my aunt was playing a trick and it was just an elaborate costume, but then the creature twisted its arm around my aunt’s waist and its talons brushed me. And they were cold, hard and definitely real. They looked deadly, but my aunt reached out and wrapped her hand around them like it wasn’t a big deal at all. Like she did it all the time and knew just how to slip her fingers around the sharpness.



They sat there, cuddled together, holding… hands? for the rest of the movie. When the credits started to roll, the creature creaked itself off the couch. It stalled a moment, meeting my nervous eyes, and groaned something softly, exposing its fangs. I could not make even a guess at what it said, because my ears were ringing with my pulse as my heart tried to beat itself straight out of my chest.



Then it turned and headed for my aunt’s bedroom. She trailed after it and I could hear some distorted voices before she returned to the living room alone.



“I’m so glad you got to meet her,” she smiled, totally oblivious to the pure terror her friend had struck in me. “She really likes you and wants to come to the next club meeting. Is that okay?”



I didn’t know what to say, so I just nodded a little. Because I want my aunt to be happy and if bringing a freaking monster to movie night makes her happy, I guess she can do that.
