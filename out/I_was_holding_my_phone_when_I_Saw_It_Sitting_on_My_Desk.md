I'm  in my bedroom.

I'm playing my game while lying on my bed.

My phone was still in my hand.

The sound from the game played quietly through the speaker.

Footsteps.

Game effects.

The occasional tapping of my fingers against the screen.

I had been playing for quite a while.

Then—

I stopped moving my finger.

I heard something.

The sound of a game.

I slowly lifted my head.

The sound was coming from the desk.

I went still.

Listening.

The game sound came again.

It sounded exactly like the game playing on the phone in my hand.

I stared at the desk from across the room.

There was a small glow coming from it.

A phone.

Its screen was on.

I was still holding my own phone.

The screen was facing up.

The game was still open.

I looked toward the desk again.

The game sound played once more.

I frowned.

“What the hell...?”

I didn't get up right away.

Maybe I'd forgotten to turn something off.

Maybe there was a video playing.

Or maybe—

there was another phone.

Eventually, I sat up.

My phone was still in my hand.

I listened again.

The game coming from the desk continued.

Even the sound effects were the same.

The sound grew clearer as I got closer.

At first, all I could see was the glow.

Then the shapes on the screen began to come into focus.

I stopped.

My eyes narrowed.

I recognized the screen.

My character.

The menu in the corner.

The exact position of the game.

I stared at it for several seconds.

Then I slowly looked down at the phone in my hand.

The same game.

I looked back at the phone on the desk.

The exact same game.

Not a similar game.

Not some coincidence.

It was the exact same game.

Even the game itself was at the exact same point I had just been playing.

I froze.

“...What?”

The sound from the phone on the desk continued.

The same sound.

The same effects.

Even the same part of the game.

I looked at the phone in my hand.

Then back at the phone on the desk.

My hand slowly dropped.

“Whose phone is that...?”

I kept staring at it.

Then I looked down at the phone in my hand again.

For a few seconds, I didn't do anything.

Then I placed my phone on the bed.

The screen was still on.

The game was still running.

I even looked at the screen for a moment before turning away.

The phone was clearly on my bed.

Only then did I walk over to the desk.

I picked up the phone that had been sitting there.

I looked at the screen.

At first, I could only see the glow.

Now the shapes on the screen were clearer.

I stopped.

My eyes widened.

I recognized the screen.

My character.

The menu in the corner.

The exact position of the game.

I stared at it for several seconds.

Then I noticed the case.

The small scratch in the corner.

The wallpaper.

The app icons.

Everything was the same.

I turned the phone over.

Then looked at the screen again.

I froze.

It was my phone.

“This is my phone.”

I stood there in silence.

Then I looked back toward the bed.

The phone I had just left there should still have been sitting on it.

**But the bed was empty.**

I stopped.

I stared at the mattress.

Nothing.

I lifted my pillow.

Nothing.

The other pillow.

Nothing.

I pulled back the blanket.

Still nothing.

I looked underneath the bed.

Empty.

I slowly stood back up.

The phone in my hand was still on.

The game was still open.

I stared at the screen for a long time.

Then I looked at the bed.

Then back at the phone.

My mind started searching for the simplest explanation.

Maybe I had moved it to the desk.

I tried to remember.

But the memory immediately rejected that possibility.

Because I was still holding my phone when I first heard the game coming from the desk.

I was still holding it when I saw the light.

I was still holding it when I walked closer.

And I was the one who had placed it on the bed.

I rubbed my face.

“I was still holding it...”

I stopped.

My eyes went back to the phone.

“Then what was the one on the desk...?”

There was no answer.

I stared at the game on the screen.

The progress was the same.

The game was the same.

The phone was the same.

Everything was exactly the same.

Too exact.

I slowly turned toward the bed.

Empty.

Just the bed.

There was no second phone.

No evidence that I had ever put anything there.

I stood completely still.

Then a thought came to me.

And this time...

it was far worse than the possibility that there had been two phones.

If the phone in my hand now was the same phone—

if it really was mine—

if it was the phone I had been using to play for almost two hours—

then...

what had I been holding for the past two hours?

I didn't move.

The game was still playing from the phone in my hand.

Exactly the same sound I had heard coming from the desk.

I stared at the screen.

For the first time that night...

I didn't know which one to trust.

My memory—

that the phone had been in my hand.

Or reality—

that the same phone was now in my hand...

after I had already seen it sitting on the desk.

I swallowed.

“...Which one?....

I looked at the phone in my hand.

I knew it was mine.

I knew I had been playing that game.

I knew I had placed the phone on the bed.

I knew all of that.

But suddenly...

I wasn't sure if I remembered it—

or if I only remembered believing it....
