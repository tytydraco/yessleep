When I was a child, I easily believed ghost stories. I spent a lot of time watching scary stories on Reddit and YouTube and always thought, "I hope this never happens to me." These stories scared me so much that sometimes I had anxiety attacks and couldn't sleep. Until one day, it happened.

I had arrived home from school completely exhausted. I just wanted to turn off the lights and sleep. Back then, however, I always left the light and the television on. It was a kind of security for me. I didn't like being alone in the dark. Around midnight, I heard a whistling sound. I thought it was something from the neighbor or even a sound coming from the television.

Then I heard it again.

And again.

The whistling was coming from the living room.

I started thinking, "Am I imagining this? What if it's something supernatural?"

That's when I heard footsteps. Slow ones.

They sounded like the footsteps of someone barefoot walking across the floor. My bedroom door was open because my father worked nights and had asked me to leave everything open so he could come in when he got home. I was lying in bed, completely still, with the blanket over my head. I was very hot, but I couldn't take the blanket off.

I pretended to be asleep.

The footsteps kept getting closer.

And, along with them, the whistling.

With each passing moment, they seemed closer and clearer.

Until they reached my room.

I was sweating cold. My body seemed unresponsive, and my vision was blurred. I could only stand still, trying not to move. Then the whistling got even louder. For a few seconds, I had the impression that there was something inside the room with me.Then, the whistling began to move away. That thing—or whatever it was—seemed to have left the room. I couldn't take it anymore. I jumped up and ran to my brother's room, who was also home and needed to wake up early for work.

Completely terrified, I said:

"Someone's whistling inside the house."

He thought it was nonsense, but he let me sleep in his room. With the door open.

And that's when the whistling returned.

Loudder.

I tried to warn him again, but he just told me to go to sleep.

I didn't know what else to do.

I went back to my room, closed the door, and, for the first time that night, said a prayer before bed. Even so, the whistling continued for a while. I tried to ignore it. At some point, I managed to fall asleep. When I woke up, it was morning, the whistling had stopped. But it didn't end that night. For some time, I occasionally still heard the same whistling during the early morning hours. I simply tried to ignore it. Years later, I began to move closer to Christianity. I started praying, bought images of the Virgin Mary and Jesus, a rosary, and some religious paintings. After that, I never heard the whistling again; nothing similar ever happened again.

To this day, I don't know what really happened in that house.

As a theology student, I know there's no biblical teaching saying that demons whistle. However, there are accounts in spiritual literature about manifestations associated with sounds, such as footsteps, knocks, voices, and screams. Therefore, at the time, I interpreted it as something possibly supernatural. Or perhaps it was something I simply can't explain. I believe it was a demon. I only know one thing: I was 12 years old, alone at home in the middle of the night, and I heard whistling that seemed to walk through the house until it reached my room.

To this day, it gives me chills.

I still sleep with the light on because of the trauma.

I just don't leave the television on anymore because the electricity bill is unforgiving. And honestly, I hope I never have to find out where those whistles came from.
