I think we got the sanitized kids' version.” He laughed, but then stopped when he couldn't hear me responding. He cleared his throat and continued.

“Well, the less happy version was that the first person who used the tree was a girl named Madeline.” My heart froze when I heard the name, and a dull ringing replaced Josh’s voice for a moment before I heard him continuing with the tale.

“She had asked her boyfriend to meet her there one evening at that site in the woods. She had proclaimed her love for him to nearly the entire town beforehand and thought that beautiful spot was the perfect place to cement their relationship and ensure they would be together forever. The feeling was not mutual, though. The special place she had found was already used by her boyfriend to bring many different girls there before her. And worse, he had brought another one there earlier the same day she was planning to declare her love for him. When Madeline arrived early and found them both there. She was devastated and was about to leave until she saw her boyfriend carve his name alongside the other girls onto the tree. Apparently it was too much for her, and she snapped. They say she killed them both, buried their bodies at the base of the tree, and used their blood to cover up the other girl's name, then disappeared."

"From that day, the story had changed; some people believed that a tree existed out there that would grant true love to whoever kissed under its branches; others say that the tree once held that power, but was a curse now. They say that the reason the people who fall in love are never seen again is that Madeline comes for them. She still exists there, haunted and alone, preying on the lonely people who discover the Kissing Tree. She continues seeking the love that no one will repay, convincing those she lures there to kiss her and carve their names into the tree, promising them to her forever. Once the promise is made, she will never stop pursuing her love until she can spend eternity with them as well, longing to feel a love which she is forever denied.” There was a long pause, and I absorbed the horrible implications of the story, and then Josh continued,

“But I’m guessing since you are talking to me that you didn't end up going, right? I mean, honestly, I didn't believe you saw the tree, kind of figured you were messing with me.” He laughed, and the sound felt a hundred miles away.

“How did things go with Alice?..... Aaron, are you still there?”

I let the phone fall to my side and ended the call even as Josh was still speaking to me.

Unable to process the gravity of what I had unleashed.

Since that terrible day, I have been unable to resume my life as anything resembling normal. I managed to escape her, but only physically. I see her every night now. She speaks to me when I sleep, calling me to join her.

Even now, exhausted as I am, I fear what I will see when I close my eyes. I have to secure myself to my bed, hoping the bindings will prevent me from moving when sleep does take me. I pray every night that I won’t wake up in front of that accursed tree again. Pulled back into that nightmare to fulfill the promise I made under the light of the moon to that restless spirit.

As hopeless as it all feels, I have to try and find a way to break the curse that I have brought upon myself. It might be impossible, but I have to try. I leave this message as a warning to others that the tale of the Kissing Tree is true. It's out there, and if it calls you, heed this account and run!

It might be too late for me, but I won’t give up. If I learn more, if I can survive, I might find a way out of this nightmare, out of all of this. All of this madness, just because I was lonely. All of this insanity, just because I wanted love...
