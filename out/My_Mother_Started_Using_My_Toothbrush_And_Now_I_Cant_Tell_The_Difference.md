Before I start the story, I need you all to know that when my mother was my age (16) she got pregnant and had me, my father wasn't around and she raised me pretty much alone. Me and my mother have always been super close since we look similar, same nose, same eyes, same build; the only difference between us is that I've had the same blonde highlights since I was 14, my mother's hair has stayed our natural brown color that whole time.

About 4 weeks ago on Saturday, I woke up later than usual, when i went to brush my teeth, I had found my toothbrush sitting there, already damp. Disgusting, I had thought as I reached into the cabinet to grab a spare one; at the time I assumed my mother just got our toothbrushes mixed up while she was half asleep. I thought nothing of it until I had gone down stairs and saw her leaning against the stove making breakfast, she was wearing my lululemon set.  
“Hey honey” she said as if nothing was wrong “I made pancakes if you'd like some” I stared blankly at her, I could tell she could read the expression on my face “ha-ha, you noticed the outfit, right?” she put down the spatula and did a little spin “I wanted to try something new today”  
“Right.” I replied somewhat annoyed “it's a Saturday, why are you up and ready so early?” I asked, hoping she had an answer that would get her out of the house long enough for me to do my discord moderating in peace “oh! I forgot to tell you; I have a hair appointment today”  
“Finally decide to switch things up?" I replied, shoving a pancake in my mouth “you could say that.”  
Her answer seems strange now that I think of it but at the time I was too distracted by the delicious taste of pancakes.

Later that day when my mom came home and wanted to show me her hair, I was surprised to see my exact medium length layers and blonde highlights on her, what shocked me even more was when she smiled at me and I realised she had her makeup done the exact same as I do mine “wow” I was at a loss for words “you like it right?” she asked me, her confidence was clearly high and I didn't want to ruin it for her by accusing her of copying me “wow mom, you look great!” she smiled and went to her room. Later that night I woke up to a faint sound coming through the walls. It was clearly my mom speaking to someone, or maybe just speaking in general? I strained my ears to listen to her, she was whispering the same phrase over and over in different tones “hi, my name is \[OP\]” over and over for hours until she got it in my exact voice. I think the scariest part about this whole situation is the fact that I got rid of my highlights and went back to brown, started dressing like my mother used to and sounding more similar to her and what i find is arguably the scariest part is that this morning when I went to brush my teeth, I reached for her toothbrush.
