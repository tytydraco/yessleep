I used to live in a 900 square foot house. Really small, I know. We only had two bedrooms and one bathroom, and I had three siblings (at the time). One day my neighbor came over and showed me this cool ghost hunting app on her phone. I knew it was fake but we were just dumb kids and so we decided to play around with it and see what happened. The algorithm worked something like this: the app would access your phone camera, and the app would add in these multicolored dots, signifying the ghosts. There were three colors: green, which meant a good ghost, yellow, which meant basically a chaotic neutral ghost, and red, which was the equivalent of pure evil in my eyes as a ten year old. My neighbor and I walked around the house, flashing the camera into certain rooms, and laughing about the ghosts that we saw. We saw a yellow ghost in my mom's car, a green ghost in the bathroom, another yellow one in the kitchen. It was just two girls having a fun time being ghost hunters together.  AT the end of our mini tour, we came to the last room of the house. My room. IN this room was a triple bunk bed (picture a normal bunk bed with another bed on top) and one bed meant for a little kid, my youngest sister. The room had a creepy vibe to it, especially when the blinds were closed. At the time, my siblings and mom were outside doing stuff, and my dad was at work, so it was just me and my neighbor in the house. I remember that day, after we'd finished going through the house with the camera, almost feeling a slight sense of dread as we walked up to the door of my room. I opened the door and we shined the camera in. It was dark, and we could only make out the shapes of my bed and my shelves, and the door rested open about three fourths of the way. The second the camera began scanning up my bed, we heard a loud thump, and something fell out of the top bunk. I screamed, and my neighbor and I bolted, slamming the door behind us. Now, usually I wouldn't have been that scared, because after all, I'm a bit of a true crime/horror junkie, but the loud crash came from the top of the bunk. My bed. The thing that fell was a small stuffed animal, a pink giraffe with a music player in its head. It was an old kid toy that helped me fall asleep. Now originally I thought "oh, the wind must've knocked it over," but the vent was on the floor on the opposite side of my bed, which was turned to face the window if you were laying your head down on the left side. This is where I slept, and the creepiest part of this entire experience was the fact that I didn't have a pet that could have knocked it off, and even if I had the stuffed animal was on the wrong side of my bed, because all of my toys were on the left side, where my head would be. As soon as we reached outside, also, my mom was sitting on our front porch, texting someone. My neighbor and I tore out of the house, and frantically told my mom what had happened. She rolled her eyes and checked my room, but shrugged and said it must've been the wind.  Later, when my neighbor and I were alone again, she turned to me, and told me that on the app, before the toy had fallen, she'd seen a red dot on the top of my bed, which basically signified demonic activity. I freaked out again, but we lived in that house for at least another six months before packing up and turning it into a rental while moving to a new neighborhood in the same city. Since that first time, I've had two more encounters with supposed demonic activity, and both have scared the living crap out of me, more so than the last. I'm religious, and part of me says this proves ghosts, but the other part of me isn't so sure. That other part of me also believes that maybe, someday in the near future, something may come out of the darkness again, and I don't know how to react if it does. I'm not the scared kid I used to be, but part of me still wonders if I would've turned out the way I am today if I had only just not opened my door.
