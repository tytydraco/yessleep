On November 14th, 1999, at 8:17 a.m., Blackridge Primary School opened its doors like every morning. Nothing suggested that this day would become one of the darkest in the town’s history. Twenty-five minutes later, at 8:42 a.m., emergency services received a frantic call. On the other end of the line, they could hear screams, children crying, the sound of furniture being overturned… then the call was abruptly cut off. When police finally entered the building, they discovered a massacre scene. Classrooms were covered in blood, several doors had been barricaded from the inside with desks and chains, and dozens of victims lay in the hallways. The few survivors all identified the same man: Elias Voss, a school monitor who had been hired only a few weeks earlier. He was quiet, avoided eye contact, and always kept to himself. What many remembered most was the deep scar that distorted his mouth up to his cheek, as if his face had once been torn apart and stitched back together. When the authorities searched the school, Elias had disappeared. For weeks, searches were conducted in the forest, nearby buildings, and sewers. No body was found, and no trace could explain where he had gone.  
The case was eventually closed due to lack of evidence. The school was condemned, sealed, and left abandoned. Officially, the killer had escaped. Unofficially, no one ever knew what had become of him.  
Twenty-seven years later, on June 27th, 2026, Nathan Carter, twenty-two years old, made a living from urban exploration. His video channel had already taken him into abandoned factories, old hospitals, derelict asylums, and all kinds of supposedly haunted places. So far, nothing had truly frightened him. A few days earlier, an internet user had sent him the GPS coordinates of an old school lost in the middle of a forest. The message contained only one sentence:

“If you hear someone breathing… never answer.”

Nathan laughed when he read it. He thought it was a bad joke meant to attract thrill-seekers.  
From the moment he arrived, however, something felt off. The building was enormous. The windows had been sealed with rusted metal sheets, while paint peeled off the walls. In several classrooms, notebooks were still open on desks, schoolbags lay scattered on the floor, and children’s drawings still hung on the walls. It looked as if everyone had left in a hurry, never to return. What disturbed him even more was the state of some corridors. They were almost completely free of dust. Several doors creaked on their own despite the absence of any wind. Even stranger, an old lightbulb hanging from the ceiling flickered faintly. Yet the electricity had supposedly been cut decades ago.  
On the third floor, Nathan noticed a heavy metal door that did not appear on any map posted at the entrance. Behind it was a small windowless room that seemed to have been used as a hiding place. The walls were completely covered in phrases carved into the concrete:

“THEY FORGOT ME.  
YOU HAVE TO EAT TO STAY ALIVE.  
I CAN NO LONGER HEAR MY OWN VOICE.”

Below them, a newer inscription read:

“THERE ARE STILL PEOPLE COMING HERE.”

Nathan felt a chill run down his spine.  
That was when he realized something that had never been explained in the official Voss case file. Elias had not escaped. He had stayed.  
According to never-released documents and several contradictory testimonies, the first search teams reportedly heard noises inside the school walls after the massacre. Scratching sounds, shifting movements, as if something was traveling through the technical ducts. No one took it seriously.  
But Elias had discovered those passages. And for years, he had survived inside them. In the ducts. The false ceilings. The hollow walls. At first, he fed on whatever he could find inside the school. Then… nothing. Unofficial reports mention disappearances of stray animals in the area, followed by people who came to investigate despite the ban. Nothing was ever officially confirmed. What is certain is that Elias never came out. Or rather… something never came out.  
That was when Nathan heard a noise. A crack, then footsteps. Slow. Regular. They seemed to echo just above him. Nathan immediately raised his flashlight toward the ceiling. There was nothing. Yet the footsteps continued, as if someone were calmly walking on the floor above, fully aware of his exact position. Later, when he checked the building’s old blueprints, he noticed something that did not appear in any official report. A vast network of technical ducts connected nearly every room in the school. Someone could move from one end of the building to the other without ever appearing in the hallways. But the blueprints showed something else. Modifications. Recent ones. As if something had expanded those passages from the inside.  
That night, Nathan never returned home. His camera was found near the entrance of the school. The last twenty minutes of footage were heavily corrupted. The image kept cutting out, and only a slow, almost animal-like breathing could be heard.  
For a few seconds, an extremely thin figure appeared at the end of a corridor. Its skin looked greyish. Its face was so deformed it no longer seemed human. Its jaw hung unnaturally low, revealing broken teeth, while its eyes, completely white, gave the impression that it was blind. Experts concluded that the footage had likely been tampered with.  
Yet one detail remains unexplained. In the final frame, the creature looks directly into the camera. Then it smiles.  
Today, that figure is known by another name.

**The Tenant.**

Because he never escaped.  
Because he never left the school.  
Because he lived there long enough to become its only inhabitant.  
According to numerous urban explorers, he is no longer tied only to Blackridge School. Similar accounts report sightings in abandoned hospitals, empty factories, old boarding schools, and forgotten tunnels.  
The stories are always the same. At first, he remains completely still, hidden in a dark corner or at the end of a corridor, making no sound at all. Many claim he never approaches as long as he is being watched. It is only when someone looks away that he begins to move.  
And if one day you hear breathing behind you in an abandoned building… do not waste your time trying to find where it comes from. If you can already hear it, it is probably much closer than you think.  
And it may have been following you for far longer than you realize.
