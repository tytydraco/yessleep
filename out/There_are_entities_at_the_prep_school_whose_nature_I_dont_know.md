Because I was doing poorly in my schoolwork, my family enrolled me in a private tutoring center I won't name it here.



At first, I agreed because it was close to home and my friends were there, too. Little did I know what was in store.



For the first three weeks, everything was fine the teachers, the atmosphere, and my friends were all great but when we reached the fourth week, I started noticing strange things.



For starters, the place was a converted apartment building; they had simply taken an apartment block and turned it into a tutoring center. The cafeteria scared me the most; it was in the basement, and the lights were slow to turn on, which always made me uneasy. Even today, I feel scared whenever I think about it. Food was sold in a small room within the cafeteria, while the larger outer room was where kids a year younger than us used to hang out. One day, I arrived very early and decided to check if the cafeteria was open before class started (please don't judge me). It was open. When I stepped inside, a strange dread washed over me it felt as if something was telling me both to leave and to stay. I hung around for a while until my friends arrived; I was scared because it was rather dark, but at one point, I heard a tapping sound. It wasn't just a random noise; it sounded rhythmic, almost like someone keeping a beat. Finally, through the basement window which barely let in any light I saw my friends arriving and ran out. Nothing else happened that day.



On Tuesday, a second incident occurred that terrified me even more. Our classroom was the only one on the top floor. As I was heading up there, I saw something tall enter the restroom. At first, I thought it was my own hair, and then I assumed it was a teacher but the figure was taller than any teacher should be; it looked as though it had to stoop to avoid hitting its head. Besides, why would a teacher go into the student restrooms? After thinking it over for a moment, I decided to go back down to the reception area. As I was heading down, I heard footsteps coming from a ventilation-like box at the top of the stairs; I shouted "Hey!" to figure out what it was, and my own voice echoed back, sounding much deeper. My heart felt like it was about to jump out of my chest; I immediately rushed down to the reception floor and didn't even set foot on the stairs again until my friends arrived.



The story I’m about to tell now might be even scarier than the others or the ones that followed. Another day, we went down to the canteen, chatted with the kids there, and stepped inside to grab some food. We picked out what we wanted, and I reached into my pocket to pay; I pulled out the money and handed it to the woman behind the counter, but something was off. Instead of the face usually found on the bill, I saw an image that looked exactly like a photograph taken at the precise moment I had stared at that thing in the restroom pasted right onto the money. I quickly shoved the cash back into my pocket and put the items I was holding back on the shelf. The strangest part was that when I tried to take the money out later that evening, I found nothing but sand in my hand.



I forgot to mention until now just how unbearable the canteen’s stench was; it was bad enough to make students complain, and the walls were coated in a substance resembling a mix of moss, sand, and blood.



I have long hair partly because I listen to a lot of grunge and rock music, and partly because I want to hide my acne; you’ll understand why I’m mentioning this in a moment. One day, I needed to use the restroom before class. Because of what I’d seen earlier, I went to the second-floor restroom instead. I finished up and headed back to class, only to find my friends staring at me as if I were a supernatural creature. When I asked what was wrong, they said, "Someone who looked just like you but with short, blonde hair came by a moment ago. We chatted for a bit, then they said they needed to use the restroom and left." I was shocked, because my hair is long and black. Who knows? Maybe it was another student, or... someone else.



Now, I’m going to tell you about the incident that caused the prep school to shut down. One Friday, while hanging out with friends in the classroom, we heard a strange banging sound coming from the restroom and went to investigate; we assumed a student was locked inside and trying to call for help. We tried forcing the door open while simultaneously calling out to the person inside, but eventually, we went to get a teacher though one of my friends chose to stay behind. When we returned with the teacher, the door to the last stall had been forced open and broken; our friend who had stayed behind was gone. In their place, there was blood on the floor, along with a piece of flesh that looked like an Achilles tendon.



We never heard from that friend again. It was as if the security cameras had short-circuited after we left and started working again only when we returned. The strange thing was that the blood was confined to the restroom as if, following a struggle, both individuals had simply vanished from this world. Stranger still, despite being just one floor down when it happened, we hadn't heard a single sound or scream.



News of the incident eventually reached the police; the owners of the prep school were arrested, and the school was shut down.



My friend's family filed a lawsuit against the school. I don't know the status of the case, but I think these events and that school will haunt my nightmares for the rest of my life...
