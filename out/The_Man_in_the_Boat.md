I remember when I was about 8 years old, the adults of my home had very few tales. No one claimed of fairies, spirits, or the like. The only one ever told was of the Man in the Boat.

He was old and young, tall and short, strong and feeble, whatever suited him at the moment. He never sought anyone out, but it felt like everyone found their way to him somehow. He offered a ride with his small boat, always large enough for the person he asked to come with him, but never any bigger. His voice was always that of the last person he met, even if that voice would be impossible for his size.

The boat ride he offered was an impossibility. He would show up on the smallest creek and take you to the most fantastical places you could imagine. Towering cliffs would surround you as the water rushed below, then a great forest shaded you, as many things swam next to you in the water below.

His only fee was a memory. One that means something important to you, no matter how small or big. The memory of your first taste of your favorite drink, or the memory of the joy of your first child. A memory for a memory. A piece of yourself, taken for a new one. 

For the longest time, I thought of him a myth. He left my mind when I moved out for college, got a girlfriend of three years, and decided to show her my small hometown. That was when He finally showed himself to me, when I had gone for a nostalgic walk in the woods alone.

"Hello." he said, in a young girl's voice. "It is a pleasure to finally meet you, after all this time."

I was initially startled, but he continued on.

"I would love to show you something truly incredible. If you are willing, of course. The choice is yours. All I require is your attention."

Of course, when a strange man with the voice of a tiny girl appears out of nowhere, you're going to be intrigued. That's just human nature. After some though, I agreed, and he led me on a fantastical journey on boat throughout a great forest and a beautiful frozen land. When we returned, a girl was waiting for me.

"Come on, Bruce. You've worried me sick. It's almost night, and you promised me the "best seafood in the world. I would like to see that."

I have no idea who on earth she is, so I asked her what her name is and how on earth she knew mine.

"Uh, Bruce, it's me. Alissa. Your girlfriend."

I have a girlfriend? How odd. I do remember coming back here with someone, but I'm blanking. I think it was just an old friend I coincidentally met. I don't think I could ever get a girlfriend, let alone one like this "Alissa".

"Bruce. Come on. Stop playing dumb, you've been with me for 3 years. You know me."

Did I? Perhaps I did, perhaps I didn't. I decide to go with this strange girl, but beforehand, I look back, to see if the Man was still there. I could swear I saw a smile in the woods. I hope it was the Man. It would not be fun to deal with the animals here with this girl around, after all.
