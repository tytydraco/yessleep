I've loved trains for my whole life, and i know many people did, but i loved them more than anyone else. I still remember all the train toys i had that would take up 3 Boxes in my closet. Living in Miami i saw a decent amount of them growing up, and I've ridden several around the country. Recently I've grown tired of this place. I've never liked the weather down here, and i've never been a fan of beaches due to, personal reasons. But i've been to Canada several times and, i've always preferred the colder weather, epically up north. So when a opportunity for my dream job as a Train driver came from in Manitoba, i took it. 

Where i work is, very different from Florida. I don't want to say exactly where i live but, its far from Winnipeg, were a bit of a Central hub in my area. Now as for my Railroad-er, railway, were rather small, even though we have a lot of track we only have a few locomotives. We really only do one main thing, a train to and from another town up north, serving some isolated communities along the way. It takes a good while actually, about 7 1/2 hours each way. Now i don't operate this one, not usually anyways. I stick to our main base in the south, usually in the yard doing anything that might need doing, sometimes i take to occasional freight train up north about a hour but, nothing much past that. We have a few other freight trains that pass through going up the mainline, and there is one passenger train that takes a rest stop here. 

  
My first strange experience was about a month after i moved here, it was pretty cold October, around 9PM so i stayed inside the cab of my locomotive, i had been moving some cars around the yard, and everything was sorted, i just wanted to take a warm break in the warm cab before i went home for the night. Then i noticed something, my headlight was dim but just barley, i could see someone standing at the edge of the yard, about 40 feet away i'd say. he wasn't doing anything just, standing there. I turned the headlight on bright to see him, and i swear to you, that thing looked to zip away before that light fully reached him, he didn't just vanished i saw that. Shadow or whatever it was, zip away at insane speeds, after that i shut down my locomotive and just went home, in that moment the comfort of my house sounded much more appealing than before.

  
My second one was a few weeks ago, I've been here for about 7 months now, this day i was helping with some general maintenance to the line, we wrapped up our section at around 6:30, and we were just getting ready to head back to town, for context we had 3 main vehicles, one is a large pickup truck with some wheels for operating on rails, and the other 2 are for maintaining the track directly, to a outsider they look quite strange. I was just about to load the last of the equipment into the truck when i saw something in the distance, it was just on the edge of the tree line. It looked almost like what i saw several months ago but, it was different. Now i dont know if it was the sunset playing tricks or what but, it was i'd have to see 10 feet tall, probably more like 15, it arms were just a little bit too long, and it was almost pure black, but almost, transparent. I called out to my coworkers to point it out but when i looked back. It was gone, just like last time, within a few second it vanished. Now i expected them to be confused, probably look at me funny, but they just gave a look of.. understanding, nothing more was said, i finished packing up and we headed off back home. but when we did, one of them pulled me aside, he asks if i saw a long, human-like shadow figure earlier. I was surprised he was so spot on, but he explained that they had all seen it, even some of the isolated people they talk to have seen it. He said its always from a distance, never more than around 30 feet. Its something they see every now and then and, they've pretty much gotten used to it, since it never does anything they don't worry about it, they just.. let it be.
