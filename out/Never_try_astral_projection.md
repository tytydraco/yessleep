I didn't believe in any of this stuff at first.

I was just a regular high school girl living in Florida, bored and curious, who stumbled across threads about "astral projection" on Reddit . 

With enough practice, I actually got good at it. 

I learned how to separate my mind from my body, travel around, and see weird, distant places. 

I thought I was totally in control... until that one night.

It was raining heavily outside, with loud thunder shaking the entire house. 

The mood felt unusually heavy and unsettling. 

After everyone in the house went to sleep, I lay in bed and started my usual routine to leave my body.

Right as my consciousness was about to fully detach, everything went dead silent.

The sound of the pouring rain and thunder vanished in a split second. 

And in that heavy silence, I heard it.

A deep, sinister voice right next to my ear whispered:

"Yes... so beautiful. 

Keep going, little girl... keep going. I will always welcome you into my world."

Before I could even react, heavy, thick, hairy fingers wrapped tightly around my neck. 

These big, hairy hands squeezed down with terrifying force, pinning my soul down and refusing to let me return to my body. 

I was choking in the dark space between worlds, unable to scream or move.

Suddenly, a massive bolt of lightning struck right outside the house, shaking the room with a deafening boom.

That loud crack broke whatever hold it had on me.

I woke up gasping violently for air.

Outside, the air was dead quiet and stifling. 

There was no trace of the rain or wind that had been raging just minutes before. 

I was struggling to catch my breath, my eyes locked on my open bedroom door. 

The hallway to the right was pitch black—a thick, unnatural kind of darkness.

My whole body was shaking. 

I could feel a presence standing right out there in the dark, something wild and dangerous, waiting to attack me at any second. 

I kept my eyes glued to that darkness all night, too terrified to move.

In the morning, my dad walked into my room to wake me up for school. 

But the moment he saw me, his face went completely pale.

He rushed me straight to the hospital. 

I was severely dehydrated, and my bed was soaked through with sweat. 

My eyes were wide with panic and my lips were completely white. 

After two days on IV fluids in the hospital, I finally went home.

From that night on, things changed. 

My dad refused to sleep in his own room. 

For months, he slept in my room, keeping a light on and staying awake most of the night, visibly terrified.

Years later, when I was older and thought everything was long behind us, I sat down with my dad and asked him, "Dad... why did you sleep in my room for all those months? Why were you so scared every night?".

He looked at me with a heavy expression and asked softly, "Promise me you won't freak out?"

I shuddered a bit, but nodded and told him I could handle it.

He took a deep breath and said, "When I walked into your room that morning to get you for school... I saw tracks on the floor. Animal prints with hooves."

He paused before continuing, "They were clear, muddy prints.

They didn't come from downstairs. 

They started right at your bathroom door, walked step by step, and stopped right at your bedroom doorway, facing you directly. 

Based on the prints, whatever entered stood right there watching you for a long time... then turned around, walked straight back into the bathroom, and vanished. 

I was convinced whatever came out of that bathroom came for you, but backed off for god knows why.

I stayed up every single night after that to protect you, terrified it would come back."

Hearing those words made my blood run cold.

Everything that happened was my fault. 

I should have never messed with astral projection. 

When I left my body that stormy night, I left a door open behind me... and gave something terrifying a way into our world.
