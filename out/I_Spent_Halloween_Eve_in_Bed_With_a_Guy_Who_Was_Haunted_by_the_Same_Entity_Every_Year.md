This happened about six years ago, and to this day, I still can’t fully explain what the fuck happened. I also still get the creeps when I think about it, which is probably why I haven’t forgotten it.

Soooo it was the day before Halloween, and I was talking to this guy I’d met online. We’d been talking for about a week, and I decided I was going to drive two hours out of my way to spend the night with him.  
I know. 2 hours? Survival instincts went straight out the window.   
Before I went over, he told me he had something important to tell me.  
Naturally, I assumed he had a girlfriend or a wife or something. Instead, this man tells me that every year on Halloween, he’s visited by the same spirit that’s been tormenting him since he was a teenager.  
I laughed because I genuinely thought he was fucking with me.  
He was not laughing.  
He seemed completely serious, and looking back, I think he was genuinely scared of being alone that night. I got the impression that he didn’t really want to tell me because he thought I wouldn’t believe him.  
So being the woman I am, I decided to go over there regardless.

When I got to his apartment, he was waiting outside. Dark hair, dark eyes, tattoos everywhere, black jeans, leather jacket, black tank top, chain around his neck. Basically, exactly my type.  
And the chemistry was IMMEDIATE. I don’t know how to explain it, but the second I saw him, I was ridiculously attracted to him. It felt intense, like I was being pulled toward him.

Walking to his apartment was kind of strange. You had to walk down to the lower levels of the building to get to it, almost like going into a basement. And he was the ONLY apartment on that floor. I remember thinking the location was kind of creepy, but I didn’t really care.

When we got inside, his cousin was there, hanging out on the couch and drinking beer. I got the impression that he’d come over partly to keep the guy company, but also possibly because he didn’t want to be alone that night. I can’t say that for sure, though.  
The three of us hung out for a few hours, talked, and watched TV.   
Meanwhile, me and this guy were acting like crush filled teenagers. Holding hands, kissing whenever his cousin went outside to smoke, immediately stopping whenever we heard him coming back in.  
Like typical high school romance bullshit. 

Around nine, we got hungry, so he offered to drive my car and pick up some food. That turned into a little detour behind an old building, where we decided we had other things to do before getting dinner.  
I’ll leave the details out, but let’s just say we weren’t exactly prioritizing the food. 😂😂🫣

Eventually, we got back, ate, and put on a movie. I think it was a horror movie, which is ironic thinking about it.  
His cousin eventually passed out on the couch around eleven, and me and the guy took this as our cue to move things to his bedroom.

We talked for a while, cuddled, kissed, and, picked up where we’d left off in the car.  
Everything was completely normal until afterward, when he suddenly asked me what time it was.  
I checked my phone. It was a few minutes before midnight.  
I told him, and he got quiet.  
Then he said, “It’s almost time.”  
I was like, “What do you mean?”  
He looked at me and said, “Remember what I told you earlier?”

Y’all. 💀  
I had completely put the whole spirit thing out of my mind because, again, I thought he was joking.  
I was like, “Wait, you’re actually serious about that?”  
He didn’t answer me.  
And then i’m not even playing. At Midnight, his bedroom door swung open by itself.  
I’m not talking about it slowly creaking open. It slammed open. HARD. 

I immediately assumed he was fucking with me. I asked if his cousin was doing something, got out of bed, and went to check the living room.  
His cousin was still passed out on the couch, snoring like a fucking chainsaw.  
Nobody else was there.  
So I walked back into the bedroom.  
And that’s when I saw it.  
There was this dark mass in the corner of the room.  
I genuinely don’t know how else to describe it. It wasn’t like seeing a person standing in a shadow. It looked like a patch of darkness that was somehow darker than everything around it.  
My brain was desperately trying to come up with literally ANY explanation. Maybe it was clothes. Maybe it was a weird shadow. Maybe my eyes hadn’t adjusted to the room.  
I just wanted the lights on.  
So I looked at him and told him to turn them on.  
He looked at me and asked, “You can see it, can’t you?”  
I didn’t even answer. I just wanted the fucking lights on.  
The second the room lit up, I thought whatever I’d seen would turn out to be nothing.  
Nope.  
Something started BANGING on the inside of his closet door.  
Hard.  
Then things started flying off his dresser.  
Not falling over. Not slowly sliding off the edge. I mean, objects were being thrown across this room.

At this point, I was absolutely terrified.  
I looked at him and asked, “What does it want?”  
He paused for a second and said  
“Me.”  
And THAT was when he finally explained what he’d been talking about earlier.  
According to him, when he was around thirteen, there was a girl he liked who was being tormented by this entity. He claimed things had gotten so bad that she’d been hospitalized MULTIPLE times.

He was a teenager and wanted to help her, so he told me he’d made some kind of pact with the entity.  
His understanding was that the pact caused it to attach itself to him instead of her.  
And ever since then, it had supposedly returned every Halloween to torment him for the night before disappearing again until the following year. I know. It’s fucking insane. 

I’m just telling you what he told me.   
To be fair, he did try to warn me and I just didn’t believe him. Needless to say, I was completely spooked out of my mind.

I’d driven two hours to meet a guy I’d known for ONE WEEK. I was in an apartment near the basement of a building, in a bedroom where shit had just started flying around, and this man was calmly explaining that he’d been dealing with some dark ass entity since he was thirteen.

What the fuck was I supposed to do with that information?  
I was scared to drive home alone in the middle of the night, and I honestly didn’t feel comfortable leaving the apartment by myself, either.  
So I stayed.  
Yes. I stayed.  
I know. Please don’t ask me why. My decision making skills were clearly lacking.  
I slept with every single light in that bedroom turned on.  
The apartment was also ridiculously hot. No AC, no fan, nothing. I was sweating my ass off under the blankets, but I had this overwhelming feeling that I shouldn’t come out from under them.  
I can’t explain it. I just felt like I needed to stay covered up and wait until morning.  
So that’s what I did.  
I eventually fell asleep and woke up around five in the morning.  
The night was over.  
At least, as far as I could tell.  
And because apparently I had learned absolutely NOTHING, me and this guy fooled around again before I finally decided to leave.

Afterwards I got in my car and drove home.  
But here’s where things get even weirder.  
The entire drive home, my car smelled like sulfur.  
And when I say the entire drive, I mean I noticed it immediately and I assumed something was wrong with my car or that I was driving through an area that smelled bad. But guess what? Even on interstate.  
It STILL smelled like sulfur.  
I couldn’t get rid of the smell, and I remember feeling really unsettled by it. I noticed that the further I got away from him the smell started to slowly dissipate. About 30 minutes away from home the car actually smelled fine and normal.   
   
When I finally got home, I had this strange feeling that something about me had changed.   
I don’t mean I saw a shadow following me around or anything like that. Nothing so obvious. It was more of a lingering feeling that I’d brought something back with me from that apartment.  
Almost like an imprint or a piece of this thing’s energy managed to latch onto me. 

The guy eventually told me he’d managed to chase the entity away.  
I was happy for him, obviously, but I couldn’t stop thinking about that night.  
What the fuck did I see in that bedroom?  
What was banging on the closet door? Why were things flying off the dresser? Why did my car smell like sulfur for the entire drive home?

Was his explanation about the pact actually what he believed had happened? Was there some other explanation for what we experienced? I genuinely don’t know.  
And I still can’t explain why the whole experience left me feeling so strange afterward.  
It’s been six years, and I still think about it.  
I’m not saying I can prove I encountered a paranormal entity. I can’t. I’m just telling you what I remember happening, and it was one of the strangest nights of my life.

The funniest part is that I went there expecting to hook up with a cute tatted guy, maybe have a little fun, and see where things went.  
Instead, I got a bedroom poltergeist, a sulfur scented drive home, and an experience that still creeps me the fuck out years later.  
So if a hot guy tells you he’s been haunted by the same entity every Halloween since he was thirteen, maybe don’t go spend the night with him. 😂😂😂  
Or maybe do… but if you do… At least bring your own fucking Alice Box.
