

I woke to the cries of my little brother Garris. I wipe cold sweat from my brow as I sit up in my bed. The moon light from my window acts as a spotlight, showing me my black sweatshirt still stained on the cuffs crimson. I still recall that bout, my first amateur fight. My uncle was so proud of me that day.

My uncle Jon has been a father to me since mine ran off when I was six. I remember little of him, besides the fact that he was a my junky. Damn, I can't believe it's almost been eleven years now. My uncle took me under his wing at six, and at seven I began training. Ten long years of boxing, over fifteen bouts. I go back to a time when ...

Garris cries louder.
" Son of a bitch" I whisper rising to my feet. I throw on my sweatshirt and make my way to my bedroom door.

As my hand reaches for the door knob I hear the microwave turn on.
"Goddammit Ma'" I say stepping into the hallway.

I told her a dozen times not to put the baby bottles into the micro. I move through the hallway but stop once I see a familiar coat laying on the floor acting almost like a sentry keeping me from the kitchen.

James, my mom's junky boyfriend. My stomach drops as I step over his coat. I hate James, always on edge and always picking a fight. He's twice my age and he had fractured one of my ribs before in drunken drugged state. My mom really has a type, trash.

"Ma', don't microwave the milk." I say to an empty silent kitchen. The only sound in the air is that of the wurring of the microwave. I turn from the kitchen to peer into the living room. I see my mom passed out on the floor. I step out of the kitchen and see, I see the rubber band and syringe on the floor next to her. Heat rises through me like a volcanic erruption. Hate, malice, and rage permiated in me. My face turned a shade of red. 

"Fuck, Ma' you said you'd stop that shite!" I yell at her unresponsive body .
"MA'!" I scream

As I ready myself to begin a rampage, a loud pop came from the microwave. I jump at the sound startling me, I aboutface and see a red thick liquid splat staining the microwave window.

A toilet flushes.

I go over to the microwave and open the door to see...
I see Garris.

"NO!" I cry

I spear my hands into the hot summer storm that is the hell inside the microwave. My hands blister as I pull the malformed, melting, bubbling body of Garris. His skin was peeling away from him like pulls of gum. His body was so burning hot that I dropped him to the floor. God I dropped him. Tears followed him to the floor.

"Please..." I said to my baby brothers little dead body.

Foot steps approaching.

" He finally stopped crying." Came the voice of James .

Rage filled me, heat in my core. Through my sadness and grief, I met eyes with James. His eyes looked glazed over. He saw Garris, and in the instant it took me to close the distance between us. I saw a look in his eyes that haunts me, I say regret.

With all my might I threw my right fist into his left cheek breaking his jaw. Picking up his hands in response and stumbling back into the hallway. I persued twisting my hips and throwing a left hook into his side breaking his ribs. I follow it up with a straight right into his solar plexus that sends James to the floor gasping for air. I mount him, gripping his neck with my left hand choking him while I strike him over and over again and again with my right. I stop when I realize I'm only hammering wet mashed bone into the carpet. I dismount James' corpse. Full of blood and tears I hear a groan from the living room. An annoyed groan from my mom. I make my way to her spot on the floor, grabbing a pillow. I loom over her gripping the pillow with my fists clenched.

As I begin to mount her I catch my reflection in the large window that peers into my living room. I was smiling in the window, but my eyes. My eyes had no pupil, they shined white like. Like twin pearls in the moonlight. My reflection spoke to me as my eyes met it's pearls.

"Do it" it whispered in my ear.

I fell back and threw the pillow shutting my eyes. Opening them to see my reflection returned to normal. I wept, I wept into my hands. I called my uncle and an hour later I was sitting in the back of his car while he spoke with the police. My mom was arrested and I moved to my aunt's in the Midwest.

Life has somewhat returned to normallacy. The only thing that unsettles me is at night when the moon is full. I hear that whispering voice calling me from my restroom. I follow it and I see those twin pearls shining back at me from my reflection in my restroom mirror.
