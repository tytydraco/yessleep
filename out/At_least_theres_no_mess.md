I don’t mean to waste anyone’s time before getting to the main point of my little post here, but I feel like this requires a bit of backstory.  
My father passed away before I was really old enough to know him. 

Don’t go feeling sorry for me. According to my mom, he wasn’t a very nice man, especially after he got back from wherever he was stationed in the Middle East.

I’m going to try not to go into too much detail. Long story short, he abused my mother and made her life shit. My mom kind of just took and explained he did it “due to his PTSD.” That’s what she always said, but I’ve always believed part of it was because of me. She didn’t have much of an education and hadn’t worked in almost 10 years. Without him, she probably didn’t have many options when it came to providing for herself and a kid. So she stayed with him.

He drank heavily. Drunken nights were the best nights according to my mom, he’d always drink enough to pass out. Cleaning up piss, shit, and vomit (whichever one he chose, sometimes all) off my dad paled in comparison to how a night with his sober angry self would usually go.

Eventually the drinking caught up to him, and he died before I got to really know him. Most of my memories involving him are about his unintelligible arguments with my mom and him standing over my crib for hours at a time at night. How no matter the situation he always had a bottle in his hand.

So yeah, that’s basically my father. Sorry, that was a bit of venting. But now you have the whole picture. There wasn’t anything crazy or paranormal about him.

So why did this happen?

A few nights ago, I got a package. Nothing really weird about the box itself. Just a normal cardboard box with my name and address on it. I didn’t recognize the sender and there wasn’t a return address, but I order enough shit off Temu that I figured I’d forgotten about a package.

There was an urn inside.  
It was a shitty faux gold color, with an engraved pattern around it. Shit looked like those old lego chrome pieces from the early 2000s.

After turning and inspecting it I noticed some numbers engraved in a font made to resemble the engraving pattern.  
1976–2009.  
It took me a second.  
Didn’t know when my dad was born but I was sure that was the year he died.  
I just stood there and looked at it. I didn’t immediately jump to it being his urn because that would be ridiculous. I figured maybe it was some really fucked up coincidences, or maybe my mom had ordered something related to him and used my name for whatever reason.  
But the longer I looked at it, the worse I started feeling, I put it down on the table and took a moment to really feel how fucked up I was.

I don’t know why but I just started freaking out. My stomach felt like I swallowed a handful of needles and my mouth got that watery feeling you get right before you throw up. Then I smelled something.   
An absolute assault on my sinuses. It was strong enough that I immediately thought there had to be something dead in the box(other than pops lol). 

Closest thing I can compare it to is when I accidentally ran a skunk over and the smell invaded and hotboxed my car.

I reached for a mug on the counter and went for the sink because I needed water to swallow down whatever I was planning on throwing up. Somewhere in that panic, my arm knocked the urn.

I watched it fall off the counter. What followed was me flinching with the expectation that it would hit the tile and crack, exploding into a cloud of ashes.

I didn’t even hear it hit the floor. I looked down, there was nothing there. No shards of ceramic. No ashes.  
I checked inside the cabinets. I moved the trash can. Checked behind it. Checked the other side of the counter even though there was no possible way it could’ve gotten over there.  
Nothing.  
The weirdest part was that the smell was gone too. It was an instantaneous switch.  
I went back to the box thinking I had somehow daydreamed and it was still in there. I pulled all the bubble wrap out. Flipped the box over.  
I even checked the shipping label again because at that point I was seriously wondering if I had just hallucinated the entire thing.  
But the box was real.  
It was addressed to me.  
I still have the box.  
I have no fucking idea where the urn went.

Thats the whole story, not very exciting compared to the crazy shit I see on here usually but up until now this is like, the weirdest thing that has ever happened to me in my life.
