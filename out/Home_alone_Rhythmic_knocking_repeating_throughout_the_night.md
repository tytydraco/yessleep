This happened when I was a teenager still living with my parents, so a long time ago. 

I should preface this by saying that the household consisted only of myself and my parents and that none of us used "special knocks" to be let in (besides, my parents always had their keys).

We lived in a very quiet, peaceful suburb. Mostly old people lived there. Growing up there, I was able to leave my backpack out in public sometimes without it being stolen. That's the type of place it was. Nobody was ever loud, or hooting and hollering outside for no reason. Especially since, as I said, we didn't really have 'neighborhood kids' - I was one of the only minors in the whole town at the time. 

I'll also say that I have no schizophrenia or other conditions that cause hallucinations, and that at the time this happened, I had never so much as touched weed, let alone anything else. I was completely sound of mind. 

I'd been home alone all day and into the night - I don't remember exactly where my parents had gone this time, but I think they'd gotten caught up in traffic a few hours away after some concert or get together with friends. 

I was bedrotting, scrolling on my phone, in a generally good mood because I liked having the house to myself. I was still awake and alone at around 1 am, when I heard a sudden knock following a specific rhythymic pattern on my front door. I ran down to see who it was, but couldn't make anything out in the dark. I admit I was too scared to go outside.

I went back to my room because I sort of wanted to be out of view in case someone was out there. None of the people our family knew would ever show up unannounced - in fact, none of them even really lived in walking distance. All our friends and family lived at least 30 minutes away by car, hours by walk. Judge if you want, but we'd never gotten to know our neighbors, and they almost never left the house. 

I was also worried because I knew that my mom had once seen an unknown man hiding in our bushes, though that had been years earlier. 

The knock repeated again about ten minutes after the first time. I started texting my friends and parents what was happening, but it's not like any of us could do anything. Five minutes later, there was a new pattern of knocks on the front door, and then the person repeated that new pattern two more times.

For about two hours, these knocks happened about every five to fifteen minutes. At this point, I really thought whoever was out there was not someone I should expose myself to. I didn't call the police because I thought there was no crime to report. Thankfully, it did eventually stop, and never happened after that. 

Maybe I'm wussy, but that was one of the most tense and scary nights of my life
