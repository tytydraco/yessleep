I’m going to start this off by saying there are going to be a couple of posts based of a couple of experiences I’ve had in previous years camping. 

A few years back me and my friend decided to go camping on the wildlife preserve near our neighborhood(it’s an old farm that got converted). This preserve is absolutely massive and if you don’t believe me you can look it up. It’s the J.B starkey wildlife preserve. 

We found a decent spot on one of the maintenance trails next to a palmetto field. Now the fields are riddled with coyotes and we knew that which is why we went out sooner than later to set up our tents and stock up on firewood for the night. 

We ended up not being set up until the sun was touching the horizon. This wasn’t a big deal since we were only about two miles from the road and knew the woods like the back our hands from hiking it during the daytime. Well we ran out of firewood this time and didn’t want to walk to gather more alone due to the coyotes. On the walk we started to hear rustling in the bushes from what would assumedly be small animals like rabbits or squirrels, but it sounded like it was circling us. It always seemed to be moving around us rather than running away and we did start to get a little spooked. We hurried up getting more dead branches and cut up a smaller fallen down tree. On the way back to our site we started hearing what sounded like running. It was no longer little noises around us…it sounded like someone or something was running up to us. We stopped dead our tracks. The movement stopped slightly afterwords. Now we knew there were hogs and coyotes and our best guess was to make ourselves known and make noise to scare off any animals that may be curious or attempting to stalk us. We started shouting and barking hoping it would deter whatever it may be and it seemed to do the job. 

Fast forward 30 minutes and we’re sitting by the fire cooking some cheddar cheese glizzies from Publix when we started hearing what sounded like a small child laughing. Now we were out about 2 miles from the trailhead/road. So we really started freaking out.  On top of that we knew we were out there alone. We don’t pass anyone coming out there and no one would walk around that far into the woods at night unless they were hunting and it’s only open to hunting 2-3 days out of the year. We both shut up real fast and grabbed our flashlights from the tent. We started shining them in the direction of the laughter but there was no one and no animal there. We both mutually agreed we were paranoid from earlier and our minds must be fucking with us like Mandela effect. It was odd that we both heard it but brushed it off. At the end of the night we both headed to our respective tents. At about 2am I suddenly awoke and saw that my friend had texted me which was odd since he was just across the site.   
I looked at the text and my heart dropped. He said there was someone in our site and they were in front of his tent almost staring in. I grabbed my flashlight and shined it through the tent to see the silhouette of what is assumedly either a small man or large child in the middle of site by the fire. I screamed that we were armed(we only had a hatchet and 2 machetes) and they needed to leave before they get hurt. Whatever it was seemed to slowly walk away and I got out of my tent and went over to my friends to find him wide awake looking like he’d just seen a ghost. Needless to say we both slept in the same tent that and as soon as the sun rose we packed up and walked back to our cars.   
   
We haven’t camped in the spot ever again and after the last trip out there I don’t think I’ll ever camp there again.
