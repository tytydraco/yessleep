My wife said the voices in her head started as a whisper.

“Punish Him.”

We were at the Dairy Queen with my softball team that night.  A tradition after a big win.

Our captain is a bit of a blowhard named Carl and he was recounting a successful hunting trip to the wide-eyed, Johnny-come-latelies on the team…

“I tracked that bitch for about an hour.  Owls are greasy.  You look left too long, and BAM, they’re gone.  But when you seasoned like me…you know when that bitch is screechin’ back to her babies in the nest…so I found that nest”

My wife Peachy was always none-to-a-fan of Carl’s and by the look on her face this was no exception…his front tooth protruded over his bottom lip as he snickered at his own story.

But suddenly…she gasped…and YELLED…

“WHO?”

Everyone turned and looked at her, I could tell she was uneasy.

Of course, Carl just thought she was reacting to him.

“Hoo! Hoo-hoo, is right!  Exactly what that bitch sounded like…”

Carl chuckled and choked on his soft-serve and carried on as everyone turned back to him.

I put my hand on her shoulder.  I know when my angel is not feeling right.  She looked up at me with genuine fright in her eyes.

We have a love built on acceptance, so I asked straight that night as we got into pajamas…

“What was going on in that head tonight, my Peachy Queen?”

She paused for a moment and took a deep breath.

“I’ve been hearing voices…”

“Voices?”  I’m not proud of how shocked I sounded.

“A woman has been speaking to me…at first it was just a whisper, but now it’s louder…her name is Mother.”

I was plum confused on how to handle, so I tried rational thought…”Well…sweetheart, your Momma has been deep in the dirt for four years gone…”

“NO…not my Momma.  She is Mother.”

“Oh…okay...” I tried to put on an air of understanding. “What does she say?”

“To punish them.  To punish all those deserving…”

Maybe I shouldn’t have encouraged it.  I could tell she was starting to sob lightly, but I kept the questions going…

“Who are those deserving?”

She hugged me tight and rested her head on my chest as she has done so many times to find comfort.

“People who hurt animals.”

“Oh…”

I held her tight and nodded.  I knew what was happening.  Peachy works at the local Feed N’ Seed and loves critters, all alike.  I just knew that one of her coworkers — who are all a bit nutty — got into ear and start spouting stuff.

“How ‘bout we just read a bible verse and get a real good night sleep…I’m sure it will be better tomorrow.”

And so we did.

Let me tell you she was right as rain next morning.  Chipper and happy as clam on a Tuesday.

BUT THEN…

I came home to dinner that night.

Carl’s truck was in the driveway, which I found peculiar.  He normally is at the Billiard hall at that time of most nights.

I walked in and there was Carl at my dinner table, eating freshly baked apple pie with my darling Peach.

There was laughter and warmth, which I found shocking because again Peachy did not like him and Carl…well, I’m not sure what he likes.

“WELL GODDAMN, look what the wind blew in!” He shouted at me as I walked through the door.

“Hey Carl…whatcha doin’ here?”

Peach just smiled endlessly, albeit strangely at me as I walked to the table.

“You got a goddamn bite of gold right here, Paul...”, he took a bite of the pie and then pointed at Peach.  “And the wife ain’t bad either.”  They both died laughing together.

I was at a loss for words, but as usual Carl was not.

“I was at the Feed N’ Seed today and there was Peachy haulin’ grain and she got me to regale that owl huntin’ story for rest of the girls there...then sure as shit, she invited for pie tonight.”

I think my face dropped a mile down.  To say I was nervous, would be underselling it.

“Honey…” I said to Peach.

She saw my worry and quickly sat me down and gave me a slice of pie.

“You must be famished, darlin’!” She said as she put the plate in front of me and hustled into the kitchen, yelling back to us. “Carl, tell Paul the ending again.  I don’t think he heard it the other night!”

Carl was quite happy to.

“Well, I tracked that bitch back to her nest and she’s feedin’ the babies…I got my cross bow sight on it, I lined her up…and POP! Straight to the heart!”

Carl slapped me in the chest proudly.

Peach yelled from the kitchen, “Tell him why the heart!”

“Oh, well, it’s better for the taxidermy.  Prettier when I hang her on the mantle.”

My heart started to race as I saw Peach come out of the kitchen with a butter knife and an egg timer.

I quickly stood up…”Maybe we should go for a walk…” I tried to speak ‘Carl’, “you know stretch those goddamn legs?”

“Not when the pie is this goddamn good…” Carl said as he stuffed another massive bite in.

Peach sat down next to him and put her hands in her palms, smiling endlessly at Carl.  I tried to get her alone…

“Peachy, sweet heart…could we…”

But she just focused in on Carl…

“How long after you shoot her in the heart do you think it takes her to die…?”

I didn’t know what was happening, but I didn’t like it…I was shaking…”Peachy, honey…”

No one was listening to me.

Carl considered it for a moment.  “I gander a guess at…five…ten minutes.”

WHEN….

I noticed that Peach was smiling as she nodded, but at the same time tears rolled down her face.

My voice quivered, “Ppp…eeeeaa….ccchhh…”

SUDDENLY…

**Peach grabbed the butter knife and SLAMMED it into Carl’s chest straight into his heart.**

**I SCREAMED.**  I flailed backwards, banging up against the wall and falling to the ground.

“OH MY GOD, OH MY GAWWWD!”  I covered my mouth, trying to find some sort of release…

I could hear Carls gasps for breath as he stared wide-eyed at the butter knife in his chest.

Peachy put down the egg timer in front of him and set a countdown for 10 minutes.  She hit start and smiled at Carl with a sinister love that I’ve never seen.

“I know it ain’t apples to apples, but let’s see if it’s five…or if it’s ten…”

I screamed and clawed my way up the wall.

Just then…

“Honey-bunny…”

I turned. Peach looked right at me.  She smiled…and I melted.

It was the same girl that I fell in love with all those years ago.

Love is built on understanding.

I hope I can find grace in that, as Peach and I move down our new chapter with Mother in our lives.
