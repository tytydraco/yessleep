Guys, it has been a long time since I last wrote here. I needed some time to process everything first. I will start right where I left off.

After I got back home, I came to the conclusion that it was really just my imagination. Konrad and the dog were not dead, it was simply the lack of sleep. I lay down and closed my eyes. I was able to fall asleep and I dreamed of Alina, how she said to me, John come here come here John come to me. It was a beautiful dream.

But when I woke up, I could still hear Alina’s voice. My eyes followed the voice all the way to the open window, and there it stood right outside my window. I wanted to run, but my body felt like lead. The worst part was, how on earth did it even get up there? I live on the 4th floor! The dog opened its mouth, its lips or its mouth did not move, yet out of its mouth came Alina’s voice saying, here come here John come to me. Because I could not run away and I was terrified that the dog would step right into my room, I grabbed my alarm clock and threw it straight at the dog’s head. The alarm clock hit, but there was no reaction. The dog just kept staring straight into my soul. But before it disappeared, it said in Peter’s voice, hello John. Then it vanished. I looked out the window. My stomach turned sick because either that dog can fly or it climbed straight up the house wall.

I thought things could not get any worse, but then it hit me, if the creature knows what Alina sounds like, then it also knows where Alina lives. I rushed into my basement, grabbed my grandpa’s machete, threw it into my backpack, and ran to the bus. Luckily, the bus arrived just in time. I screamed at the village bus driver, Frank drive drive goddamn it! My entire body was pumping with adrenaline. I just wanted to kill this creature before it takes Alina, just like it already happened to Konrad.

The bus driver stopped in the village. I felt the gravel beneath my feet as I stepped out. This time, I was not running for my life, I was running for Alina’s life. I sprinted to her house. My heart was pounding like crazy, my spit had developed that iron taste of blood. I arrived at her house, she had her window wide open and I thought she was dying because I heard screams coming from her living room. I pulled out the machete, knocked and yelled, Alina Alina! I kicked the door open, ran into her living room, and screamed, Alina where is it where is it?

My beautiful Alina was just sitting on her couch and looked at me completely bewildered, honey what is wrong put that thing away you are scaring me. I answered, still hyper but a bit calmer now, I heard screaming coming out of your window. She answered calmly, oh honey I was just watching a horror movie, you know my favorite movie The Thing.

She wanted to hug me, but I still had my machete in my hand and said, wait, if it is really you, when is my birthday?

She said, it is obvious, 29.06.2000.

And what is my mother’s name?

She answered, oh honey is this really necessary?

I screamed, answer me!

She said startled, Lisa.

Last question, when did we become a couple?

She said with a soothing voice, I could never forget that, 23.05.2024.

I fell into her arms and said, it is really you, and started crying. She said, babe everything is fine. I smelled her perfume, which as always smelled like lavender and vanilla, and thought to myself, it is truly her. She stroked through my hair and said, everything is fine come let us go to the window and get some fresh air.

We went to the window. I took a deep breath and felt the cool evening air fill my lungs. But at the exact same time, my nose filled with the stench of a wet, musty dog and rotting meat and blood. I smell it. My eyes were closed and I thought to myself, no no no it is here.

I snapped my eyes open and there I saw it standing in the garden, the dog that had turned my days into hell. Alina said very calmly, a bit too calmly, look it is Sammy. He looked at me, but next to him lay something. At first it looked like a pile, but then I looked closer, small thin brown hair, Alina was lying there. But she looked different, as if her body had already been buried for two months. It felt like someone tore my heart right out of my chest. If Alina is lying out there, who is this next to me, or rather what?

I turned around as fast as I possibly could and backed up against the wall. I gripped my machete even tighter and stared at it. Now she still looked like Alina, but she looked at me like I was prey. Her teeth sharper, her nails longer, almost like claws claws. And out of her wide open mouth came, come here John come to me. I screamed, no no no what have you done what have you done what have you done! I swung my machete to strike, but she or rather it screamed, I know who you are I know what you have done I know it I know it you deserve this give up this is not you you are weak you would never hurt little Alina.

I wanted to strike, every single fiber in my body burned to kill this thing, but my heart just would not let me. My love for Alina, the thought what if I am imagining all of this, this feeling of loneliness of helplessness. In that exact moment, it lunged at me, as if the thing could smell my emotions. I dodged. The thing slammed against the wall and almost brought it down, but one of its long, razor sharp claws caught me on the arm and left a deep cut. I ran out of the living room, locked the door behind me, and heard, come here John come to Alina.

I sprinted out of the house. I saw Peter walking towards me with fast paces. He said, John what have you done put the machete away! I did not know if it was Peter or a thing, but I did not even care anymore. I grabbed him and slammed him against the frame of the entrance door. He collapsed unconscious. I saw Peter’s black Chevrolet, but then I felt a massive impact from the side. The Aussiedoodle lunged at me and the force threw me back a few steps. He was growling like crazy. He leaped into the air to attack, but mid air my machete sliced his neck and the head rolled down the little hill. I stood there in shock for a second, wondering what I had just done, but the only thought playing in my head was escape. I ran to the car, sat inside, the keys were still in the ignition, typical Peter. I started the engine and in that moment I saw in the rearview mirror how that thing, which pretended to be Alina, broke through the front door, focusing only on Peter’s car with me inside. It simply trampled right over Peter. The thing looked twice as big now. I was so shocked that I forgot where I was and what I was supposed to do. With a sudden jolt, I snapped back to reality and hit the gas.

I sped out of the driveway and onto the country road heading towards the forest path. The thing was still right behind me, screaming in a distorted voice, Jooooohn! I drove even faster into the forest path. The thing was almost scratching my car. I thought things could not get any stranger, but I was wrong. Left and right, literally every animal from the forest was standing there. Deer, wolves, squirrels, hares, birds, foxes, even a goddamn bear! They were just standing there on both sides of the road, staring at me I had the exact same feeling as with the Aussiedoodle, as if they were staring right into my soul, all along the forest path, while this thing was still on the verge of smashing the car.

Ever since I was at Alina’s house, the sun had gone down, which made the forest look even darker than usual. And then I saw it, and a tiny spark of hope ignited in my chest. I saw the streetlight at the end of the forest path. That exact streetlight marks the beginning of Alpirsbach. I pushed the gas pedal down even further, I felt like I was going to ram it right through the metal floor. I looked into the rearview mirror one last time and looked one last time into the eyes that I loved and still love. It was not Alina, and all these months it had not been Alina, yet those were the eyes I loved. I counted down in my head, 3 2 1 I blasted out of the forest path at over 100 km/h into the first street of Alpirsbach. I looked back in the mirror and saw the thing turn around, slowly strolling back into the woods. I had escaped. There were too many people in Alpirsbach, it could not follow me without being detected.

I felt too many emotions all at once, tears in my eyes from sorrow because I lost the love of my life, the girl who was my entire world, a smile of pure gratitude because I escaped. But I felt another emotion, hate. Just pure hate that she was gone. This hate felt like a black hole that simply sucked in every other feeling. I did not stop. I did not give a shit about my things back in Alpirsbach. I drove north through Karlsruhe, Mannheim, Mainz, Koblenz, Köln, all the way to my hometown of Essen. I pulled hard into the driveway of my parents’ house and stopped.

My father came rushing out, tall athletic and with a serious facial expression, but he had a soft core. I got out and he yelled loudly, John is everything okay? Wait, is that a 67 Chevrolet Impala? Before I could answer any of his questions, I collapsed from pure exhaustion because I had not eaten anything, barely drank, and had barely slept at all over the last few days.

But I did not dream of anything. It was just me and a black space. The dream felt like emptiness, like nothing, like days spent without a single thing. But then I snapped my eyes open. I was completely drenched in sweat, tried to sit up, but I was still too weak for it. Then I recognized where I was. I was in my parents’ guest room, and for the first time, I genuinely felt like I could breathe deeply without any danger. But then the memories of Alina and what happened came rushing back. I started crying because she was gone and she would never come back.

A rough, older hand opened the door. When I saw it was my father, I wiped the tears from my eyes. He said calmly but happy, great you are awake, that took a long time.

I answered quietly, because my voice was still gone from all the screaming, how long have I been asleep?

He answered with a short laugh, 46 hours my friend. Do you care to explain where you got the Impala from and what happened?

I said quietly, nothing. My father was a man of facts, someone who does not believe in the supernatural, he would just write me off as crazy.

He said, for nothing, that wound on your arm is pretty big, the one I stitched up. You know what, tell me some other time, rest up first I am going to make some food okay? And with that, he closed the door.

I closed my eyes, tried to sleep. It did not work anymore, I was wide awake now and full of pain. As a distraction, I grabbed a newspaper that my father kept in the guest room. I took the newest one, and what I read in there made my heart drop straight into my pants.

3 dead bodies found within 50 meters in the district of Alpirsbach, including 2 older men, still unidentified, a young woman named Alina Meyer, and a dead dog with a severed head. Furthermore, the property of the Meyer family lies in ruins, police are still investigating.

I slammed the newspaper against the wall, I did not want to believe it. All because of this thing, it destroyed lives. Why why? Then something came to my mind, something I had forgotten long ago, a story from a children’s book that my mother used to read to me, the story of the wanderer in the woods. A creature that does not just want to eat you, it wants to hunt you for fun. It wants to terrify you because your meat tastes better that way. Before I could think any further, my father called me for dinner and I walked over with slow steps.

It has been two weeks now since it happened. Physically I am feeling better, but mentally I am not. I feel cold, abandoned, and lonely, and I blame myself for not killing that thing. It is probably out there in the woods right now, doing the exact same thing to someone else. I try to distract myself with my old hobby, taking photos of liminal spaces. It works a little bit. I do not know what to do now, how my life is supposed to go on, but something keeps pulling me back down to Alpirsbach. I do not know if it is the desire to kill the creature, or simply to see my friends and nature again. I know I will see this creature again someday, but this time, I will be ready.

PS Trust what you can see, but do not always trust what you feel.
