I’ve always been a skeptical man, no superstitions or any belief in the paranormal. That was until my daughter got sick suddenly last month. It happened so fast I didn’t understand what was happening to my child. Nothing could’ve prepared me for what was to come. 

It happened two weeks ago I remember it vividly, Chloe walked in with her mother and happily greeted me jumping up and down out of excitement.  “Daddy I got a surprise for you!” She shouted excitedly. “Oh yeah?” I said smiling big and glancing at Darlene my Ex wife. “Here ya go daddy!” Chloe said ecstatically handing me a termite looking creature made out of clay. It was a dark grey but the head was painted in a rainbow of red, blue, purple, and pink.   
I glanced at Darlene and she looked at Chloe and gave her a kiss, “I gotta go hunny.” She said clearly not wanting to be here any longer. I didn’t blame her or anything I understood it was awkward to just stand in your ex husband’s house.   
As she walked out the door I noticed something a little odd, what looked like a beetle of some sort on her neck, but when I looked closer and tried to adjust my eyes it was gone. I ignored it and immediately spent the entire evening painting, coloring, and doing all sorts of activities with Chloe until she was tired. 

Later that night as I was sitting in the living room after just tucking her in, I looked at the wooden coffee table that was sitting just a few feet away, I saw what looked like a small black beetle crawl onto the table and just stay there. I looked at it trying to wrap my head around what I was seeing, the thing looked like a normal beetle but something was wrong. The beetles legs twisted and there were jagged tiny  protrusions on the sides, strange blue markings worked its way up from the back of the beetle all the way to its head.   
As I studied it I came to a realization that it looked exactly like the beetle that I saw on Darlene’s neck. I was perplexed I didn’t know what was going on, my thoughts were lost immediately as I heard Chloe violently coughing. It sounded awful like a dog’s bark mixed with a screech of a dying crow.   
Running into her room I turned on the light and saw my daughter coughing up a viscous black liquid. It horrified me and what horrified me even more as I ran to her side was what looked like little *bugs?*   
They were crawling inside of the liquid and some of them stopped moving as they seemingly lied still and died.   
I grabbed Chloe holding her tightly in my arms running towards my car. We drove to the nearest emergency room and as we walked up to the entrance of the hospital Chloe asked me a strange question. “Daddy why do bugs like to crawl around me when I’m with mommy?” I glanced at her and dismissed the question and said “Hun you’re gonna be okay I promise you.” As we walked into the emergency room. 

It was about two hours later after extensive tests, X-Rays and the doctors being stumped as to what was happening when the main doctor walked in. “We have yet to come to a conclusion on what’s going on with your daughter, but we’re prescribing her some antibiotics and anthelmintics if it is the parasitical infection we think it is.” He said looking at me with a grim sort of expression. “Parasitic infection?” I said as Chloe frowned and asked the doctor “Are there bugs in my tummy?” He looked at her with a soft but fake smile and said “No need to worry, the medicine we’re giving you is gonna make you feel much better and no there’s no bugs in your tummy alright?” Chloe seemed content with that answer as she walked over to me and held my arm. 

On the drive home my brain was reeling, I was so confused on what was going on. I wanted my daughter to feel safe and not whatever was happening right now to her. Although she seemed happy and cheery so that sort of eased my anxiety. I called Darlene later that night as well, Chloe asleep in my bed as i stood in the kitchen. “Yeah I know that’s what the doctor said though Darlene.” She was prying trying to get all the information she could and I was being truthful. I didn’t have the information she wanted clearly so she hung up angrily.   
I sighed running my hands over my face and trying to come to terms with the fact my daughter is dealing with some type of infection? Some parasite? If that’s what it even was. 

The next day my daughter woke up as usual, no fever, no cough or anything that seemed out of the ordinary. Darlene had texted me saying she was on the way since we had split custody. I walked over to Chloe and made sure she had everything she brought from her mother’s and she had piled her backpack and a couple of toys on the couch in the living room. She looked at me smiled and said “I’ve got a lot of friends inside me.” I looked at her perplexed as to what she had just said and before I could question her there was a knock at the door. It was Darlene and I told my Chloe to grab her stuff and gave her a kiss on the cheek as she headed to the car. I gave the prescribed medication to Darlene as she said “I really hope they figure out what’s happening to her soon.” I nodded my head and said “Yeah me too.” But before she turned around and headed away I saw something that haunts me now more than ever. A little movement under her left eyelid as a little black leg came from under it as she turned around and went towards the car. I should’ve said something then but how could I? I would’ve sounded insane. 

It was only three days later when I got a call from Darlene’s mother, I was shocked because I knew she had always held a sort of disdain for me. “Hey I know i’m calling out of the blue but I haven’t heard from Darlene in two days and i’m sort of getting worried, I was wondering if you had heard from her?” I listened intently and frowned. “No.” I said “I haven’t heard from her either but she told me she was going to be driving up to her cousins for her birthday.” The woman sighed and asked “Can you please check up on the both of them?” I nodded as if she could see me. “Yeah i’ll go right now.” I said already running to grab my keys and a jacket, it was pouring outside and Darlene’s mother had hung up. I hadn’t called Darlene because I knew she was busy but at the same time I had an awful feeling in my gut. 

It’s a fifteen minute drive to Darlene’s house but every minute felt like it was hours long. As soon as I pulled up to the house I noticed something odd, all of the lights were turned off and the curtains closed. I jumped out my car into the freezing downpour and rushed up to the front door. Knocking twice I waited for a couple moments then knocking again and calling out for Darlene. I tried the doorknob and it was unlocked, an immense feeling of dread washed over me in that moment It felt as if the universe was telling me something’s wrong. When I walked inside I immediately was hit with an awful smell, it was a rank pungent odor that smelt like cat piss mixed with the most foul of excrement. I covered my nose as I ran throughout the  house shouting for my daughter or anyone that would hear me. 

The house was in disarray, garbage bags set everywhere and plates and dishes piled in the sink with grime covering the countertops. The fridge half way open with a foul sludge falling out of it. “Oh my god.” I said, this place was a wreck and my heart was pounding. My instincts kicked into overdrive and I ran to my daughter’s room and the door was shut. I reached for the doorknob and when my fingers brushed against the metal it felt gooey. I almost gagged but I threw open the door and what I saw was the most grotesque thing i’d ever seen. Darlene was on the ground sprawled out her face opening and a pair of bulbous eyes attached to slug like eyestalks protruded from her mouth. Her lower half melted into a black liquid substance that I immediately recognized, it was the liquid   
Chloe coughed up. 

They eyes turned around as a loud squelch came from darlene’s form, it moved across the ground towards me slowly. I almost threw up, I couldn’t comprehend what I was seeing, then tons and tons of those beetle like insects poured out of the back of Darlene’s ravaged back.   
They started crawling towards me and I panicked stomping on them and closing the door as I heard a voice from the next room. “Daddy! Daddy! please help me!!” It was Chloe. I ran towards the sound and I found my daughter inside of the bathroom, she had locked herself in there and I yelled for her to come out and that we have to go. “Cmon baby we’re going now!” I screamed as she unlocked the door, jumping into my arms. I ran outside and ignored the bugs that were now crawling all over the ground, walls and the ceiling. I put my daughter in the car and started dialing 911. “I- I hello please send help- Th-There’s something wrong with my ex wife.” I said struggling to get the words out. The operator asked questions and I responded to the best of my ability, I didn’t wanna sound insane but how would I explain what I had just saw? I waited outside for the police to arrive and they came quickly. 

After the ordeal the police had told me that when they went inside they found no sign of Darlene but the mess was there. All of the bugs that I and my daughter had seen were apparently gone. I’d been questioned extensively and I feel awful because I can’t say exactly what I saw without feeling insane. My daughter now lives with Darlene’s mom and I still get to see her sometimes, but after everything I tried to tell the police they didn’t believe most of it and tried to pin her supposed disappearance on me. I am currently in the process of trying to file for custody for my daughter once again. I have a feeling that whatever happened to Darlene will happen again but I do not know to who or when it will happen again. 

Im starting to have odd dreams now, they start seemingly normal but they devolve into a nightmare of chittering bugs and the thing that I saw come out of Darlene that dreadful night. I’ve tried to block all of it out, i’ve talked to therapists but nothing seems to work. I’m currently sitting inside my apartment writing this letter because I feel something odd. There’s an intense feeling of nausea and fatigue that i’ve been feeling for days. Something was under my skin yesterday and I brushed it off as my imagination but deep down I know that i’ve been infected with whatever was inside Darlene and my daughter. I’m starting to see little bug like creatures outside my peripheral vision, I don’t know what’s going to happen to me but If you find this letter please know i’ve tried everything I can to keep myself from getting in contact with others. And too my beautiful daughter, I love you so dearly and I hope you can one day grow up to be a beautiful person.
