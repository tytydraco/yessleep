I first saw the Tree Lady on a camping trip with a couple friends in the summer of 2016. That is what we called her and what I came to know her as. John and I were 18, and Terry was 20. While Terry had been held back once or twice, he fit into our trio like a glove. Let me gather my thoughts for a second... yes, the Tree Lady.

We had finished setting up the tents and were plotting our course for the next day. We agreed that the river was the perfect spot for fishing in the morning. 

"Dude, I am not kidding, the amount of mosquitoes tells me that this spot is live!" Terry exclaimed before John patiently corrected hm.

"Yeah, the *number* of them, yeah," he elbowed Terry playfully.

"No, I meant what I said ya bitch," Terry shoved John back towards me.

"Guys..." I recollected myself before stopping dead in my tracks. My friends each turned towards me to ask what was up but froze when they saw my eyes were straight ahead. "What the hell is that?" I half wheezed. 

John was characteristically first to turn and try to make out what I was seeing. He must have seen just a tall tree at first, because he looked back at me before Terry jumped back. "What the *fuck?!"* John looked back pale as a ghost.

Before us stood a tall, gaunt, and hunched woman, her robe concealing her torso. Her hands were man sized (about 6'0") and she must have been 20 feet above the ground. Her mouth was wide open under a catlike maw. Her eyes were long and unreadable in expression. I stammered, "*guys, you guys, you seeing this*?!" Reading each other's expressions told us everything we needed to know. 

Terry bolted back to the tent and John quickly followed. "Yeah, I saw- " John spat out and I turned around to see the rake we were using slap him in the mouth. Blood flew and I grabbed him by the shoulders and led him back to the other tent. Somehow, in our confusion, we all ended up in Terry's. We spent the night measuring the distances from the base of the Tree Lady up to the top of her head using a laser measure. She didn't move once during our hushed discussion. I asked John about why he had stepped on the rake.

"Oh you know..." he looked down in shame, "I am sorry, yeah I did that on purpose". When Terry asked why, all John could say was, "If there is a God, I wanted to make him laugh". The next thing I remember was the morning.

Terry had dragged John from the lake and set him on the ground next to the tents as he wept. I looked at my best friend and knew he was gone. Apparently, they had gone to the river to set up the fishing poles before I woke up. John had been dipping his feet in the water when the Tree Lady appeared behind him, jolting him into the water as Terry watched helplessly. John looked up as she slowly opened her coat. A large beehive concealed the rest of her body and they swarmed John. By the time she disappeared, Terry had lost the EpiPen that John had just lent him. John floated in the river before Terry had dragged him onto the grassy shore, swearing the whole way back.

John was allergic to bees. I am not sure what happened that morning, but I wept continuously all the way home. I only bring this up because there is a new tree on my lawn that wasn't there last night. 

Has anyone else here ever seen the Tree Lady?
