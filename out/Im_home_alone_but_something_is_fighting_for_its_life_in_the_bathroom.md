Dude, who or what is in my freaking house?  
The only reason why I haven’t called the police yet is because whoever or whatever it is, it is fighting for its life in the bathroom and I have better respect than to interrupt someone in the midst of a battle.  
Let’s start at the beginning, I’m housesitting for my parents while they go on a cruise. I recently was let go from my job so I have the time to do so. It sucks that it happened to me but I’m trying to make the best of the situation.  
My parents have a one level home with 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. They have a deck in the back-ish area of the house with a glass sliding door that leads into the kitchen/dining/living room area (I know they are kinda of smushed into one, I didn’t build the house).  
When you enter the home from the glass sliding door beyond the initial amalgamated space you’ll see two guest bedrooms (one being my makeshift bedroom for the time being) then immediately to the left of the second bedroom is a bathroom and at the very end of the hall is my parents bedroom which has a private bathroom inside.  
Another thing you need to know about the layout is that if you were to immediately to step out of my parents’ bedroom, you would see the front door. A regular door to describe it so elegantly. It’s perpendicular to the glass sliding door and only a couple of feet from the guest bedroom I’m in.  
I guess there is one very last detail I should mention just so you understand why I said “whatever it is” later on in the story. With the exception of the kitchen area, bathrooms, initially entry area to the front door, and the minimal dining area. Everything is carpeted.

So it all began about 2 1/2 hours ago. I had just gotten ready for bed, I was cozied up in my bed under the sheets. I was watching YouTube videos on my phone slowly dozing off.  
CRASH.  
I heard the glass of the sliding glass door shatter and fall to the ground along with the thudding of what I guess to be a rock of some kind.  
I shot up from my bed like a deer reacting to the sound of a crunching leaf.  
“Uh oh” I heard a voice say.  
The voice sounds unnatural, I realize how odd that sounds but what I mean is the voice is human but very much a forced tone like when someone who is clearly angry tries to sound happy but isn’t hiding it well.  
At this point, I quietly arose from my bed and pressed my ear to the door (thank god for my paranoid habit of my always locking bedroom doors before bed, no matter where I am).  
What really freaked me out was the footsteps, what should have sounded like thudding tennis shoes against hardwood floor instead sounded like crushing styrofoam packaging in your hands with each step.  
I could tell because the sound would seem closer at some points to my door and farther at others. I heard whatever it is walk into kitchen and I heard the fridge door open.  
“Who buys 3 gallons of skim milk?” The voice commented.  
Rude. Mind your business.  
I could only conclude what happened next based off the sounds I heard.  
I heard the whining of metal, a high pitched yet forceful sound. I think it bent the fridge door in half. For all I know it could have been eating. I am not about to go check.  
“Ooo Taco Bell!” It said, as I heard the crinkle of the paper bag.  
Dang, I was saving that for later.  
I heard whatever sounded like the fridge door closing followed by a metal thud, I think the door fell off onto the ground.  
That’s where the next odd sound comes in.   
That crushing styrofoam sound passed close to my room and when the thing stepped on where I estimate the carpet in the living room area should be, its footsteps began to sound like coins being shaken in a glass jar.  
From there, I could surmise it sat on the couch based on the sudden moment of silence.  
“Grub time!” It exclaimed.  
The noises it made while eating my half eaten burrito sounded like a wet mop being slapped against linoleum.  
At this point 30 minutes had passed, I was monitoring the time on my watch. The thing scarfed down my burrito. A moment of silence.  
“Why is your heartbeat so loud?” It asked.  
I felt a pit in my stomach, I reflexively covered my mouth with one of my hands.  
“Tachycardia is a symptom of many things including hyperthyroidism, heart issues, fear, or anemia. Let’s see.”  
I heard sniffing noise like that of a dog sniffing chilly air.  
“Nope, you got plenty of red blood cells. You do have a spike in adrenaline. Care to tell me why?” I heard the sound of coins in a jar get closer and closer until I heard odd breathing outside of my door.  
It was not the sound of something needing to breathe, rather something trying to breathe. It was quick breaths. Whatever it was it was practically wheezing with strange giddiness.  
I timed it.  
15 minutes of the wheezing becoming faster and faster until it sounded closer to a buzz than a panic attack.  
Silence.  
Then the sound of…cats hissing?  
“Oh dang it! Where’s the bathroom?” The voice said panicked. The sound of coins in jar went from close to distant before I heard the bathroom door slam.  
I then heard the most human sound in the world.  
I don’t think I need to describe it but you know when nature calls.  
I heard grunting and groaning. I still do I type this. Followed by long sequences of bombs being dropped and the flushing of the toilet. It’s been in equal intervals of 10 minutes.  
Now we get to me typing this, it has said a couple of strange things during their bathroom battle. I will list them out below.  
“Is this my blood or theirs?”  
“Is it true helium will be gone in the next century?”  
“Why do my children stop crying?”  
“Does Mickey Mouse wear socks?”  
So, I’m posting this on here both as evidence if I am found murdered/missing AND to get advice on what I should do? I mean it will know if I call the cops…I think. Do I really go to the low of attacking something on the porcelain throne?   
I will literally take any suggestions at this point.
