Remember when dogs were our friends? When they couldn’t harm us as pets? It’s starting to get hard to believe now. 

I remember when I had this one dog, a German shepherd; I named him Shield. They were supposed to protect us; we had them trained to do so, so we wouldn’t be harmed by outside forces. I had loved Shield, even when he did stupid things that dogs do. I’ve had him since he was just a puppy; I never knew I would lose him. Not when I had him so long.

For context, it was just a regular Halloween day. I had gone out to work for a few hours filing paperwork; it was honestly boring just doing the same thing over and over. I never had the patience to fully do it, so I copied what I did for every piece and put it on a stamp so I could use it without writing. My friends had put me into the job; I didn’t want to, but they had convinced me. After my day's work, I went home, hung up my coat, kicked off my shoes, and went upstairs to chill for a few more hours. I needed to relax after all that boring stuff. I watched my favorite Halloween/Christmas movie, ***The Nightmare Before Christmas***, and sat on my bed scribbling notes I hadn’t understood on torn pieces of paper. I gave Shield plenty of food before I left, so I was confused when I heard him barking. I paused the movie with my remote and went downstairs to check on him; he was barking at the glass backdoor into the backyard, like something was there. I opened the glass door and let him out into the backyard before going back upstairs to continue watching my movie.

The movie was great, and I had decorated all of my house, inside & out, with Halloween decorations; if you went to my house that day, you would have thought it was an attraction.

I went back downstairs to go fix the lights and make dinner for myself and Shield. I first put Shield’s food in his bowl, then I started making my dinner, which was just some good, cheesy mashed potatoes. Anyway, once I did, I looked out the window and saw that the moon was red like a blood moon. It was a good Halloween setting, I thought to myself. I put my mashed potatoes in my room and headed down into the basement to grab some extra lights. But then, out of the corner of my eye, I saw it: a shadow staring back at me. I grabbed the box and ran out of the basement, thinking it was someone who had broken in. I barricaded the basement. I tried calling the police; they said they’d get there in 5 minutes. I hung up the phone, waiting for them to get to the house as quickly as possible, but then I heard Shield whining, and I went into the backyard. Shield was covering up a grave; it didn’t scare me at the time, considering I had lots of fake grave decorations, but when I took a closer look at the grave, ***it had my dog’s name, Shield, engraved into the stone.*** It also had this strong odor to it, as if someone or something had died there. I didn’t look at Shield’s face, but I was incredibly worried. I picked up the phone again and told the police what else had happened; they said they would be rushing to get there now.

I went to try to comfort Shield just in case he was worried about it, but when I looked at his face, he was smiling unnaturally at me; it looked like he had human-like teeth, and his eyes were pure white. I realized that wasn’t my dog. It was something else, something not human, ***something pretending to be my dog.***  
The police rushed in, and I opened the door for them. They asked where I last saw the “intruder,” and I said in the basement. They checked it thoroughly but didn’t find anyone. Then I told them about the grave and how that dog out there wasn’t my dog, but they checked the backyard. The entity wasn’t there, and when they dug up the grave, they found my dog’s dead body lying there; he’d been dead for days. They took the body and got rid of the grave in my backyard, instead taking him to an actual cemetery and leaving him there so I could still remember him. I placed a rose on his grave as a gift to him; I’m sure he’s in a better place now.

Months later, I moved to a new house, so I wouldn’t have to ever think about it again. And now that I’m talking about it, I get what happened to Shield. 

The ***entity*** was the shadow I saw in the basement, and it ***killed Shield.***
