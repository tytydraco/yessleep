It started two weeks ago; he just appeared.

I was doing the dishes, and a person came behind me and set a plate in the sink and said, "Sorry, I just finished eating."

I live alone.

I turned around, and there was this basic-looking guy, eyeglasses, brown hair, suit and tie, looking like he was going to go to work.

I obviously freaked out; I've never met this man before in my entire life, and here he was with one of my plates in the kitchen of my apartment acting like nothing was wrong.

He then looked at me like I was crazy.

“Woah, what's wrong? Did I scare you that badly?”

I insisted he leave, then I grabbed a knife to defend myself, and he backed up.

“Hey, just calm down. Hold on; let me go get someone. Just breathe for a second, okay?”

He quickly booked it out the door; he was so fast I could barely get out the door to see him turn down the hall.

I sat there in shock for a few minutes before I grabbed my phone and went to call the police, but then the landlord came and knocked on the door.

“Hey, Tim said that you threatened him with a knife. Is everything alright?”

“Tim” lurked behind the landlord, worry plastered upon his face.

I said Tim, whoever the hell he is, should be kicked out for trespassing.

The landlord looked at Tim and said, “Goodness, what did you say to her this time?”

Tim responded with a nervous chuckle before answering with, “I guess I should have given her my dishes sooner."

They both shared a laugh while I stood there befuddled.

I asked the landlord, "Who is this and what the hell is going on?"

And he answered with, “Uhm, your roommate?"

I have never met Tim in my life, not once, not even on the street.

But somehow, he has become my roommate; moreover, he says he's been living with me for three months.

Obviously that should be easy to disprove; however, any evidence that he was lying did not exist.

First, his name was listed on the paperwork for the apartment, signature, and same date I arrived here.

Second, I asked the neighbors; they all know him. More than that, they love him.

And the thing that scares me the most and that I cannot explain is that he has a room in my apartment.

After the argument with Tim and my landlord, I wanted to go into my bedroom for some privacy and to make sense of things, and then I saw a new door in my apartment.

I opened it, and it was a bedroom, with a guitar in the corner and posters and items; some things were even covered in dust that had to have been at least a week old.

There was no sign any of this was built recently; there was nothing to indicate this person wasn't my roommate except for my memories.

I left and stayed at my best friend's house. I told her that some strange man had been living with me, and no one seems to think it strange.

I told her he had a room and that his name was on the paperwork and that he needed to leave; I was an absolute mess.

She said, “Are you sure you're not talking about Tim?” seemingly as a joke.

I stared at her in disbelief.

“You are talking about Tim?” she said. "Lord, you haven't treated him like this since he crushed on you in high school."

I ran upstairs and tore open her closet and got on my knees and dug around until I found our yearbook.

I flipped through to our class, and then, I saw it: a younger-looking Tim, right beside people I actually knew.

I stayed with my friend for a few days, mostly locked up in her guest room. After doing some research, I found him online. He has an Instagram and a Facebook. More than that, he has a LinkedIn showing his resume.

He is a guitarist for a band that I've never heard of called “The Thought Forms. "I looked them up, and they have music on most platforms.

I felt like I was going crazy.

I decided that I needed to get to the bottom of this, so I moved back in once I got my nerves steeled.

I came home to him practicing guitar in the living room.

The moment I saw his face, I wanted to call the police, or someone, to please just take him away, but I've called; they don't believe me either.

All I did was glare at him, and he stared at me, mumbling a few apologies for "whatever I did."

I lived with him for a couple of days; I followed him, and everywhere I went, people knew him. He had an entire life, even though it feels like he appeared out of thin air.

The bar his band was playing at knew him and said they booked the gig two weeks ago.

There is not a single crack or flaw in any way, shape, or form in his life. It's like everyone has implanted memories of him, and for some reason I didn't get the memo.

I'm talking to him more now, and he laughs and jokes, but whenever he thinks I'm not looking, his face goes blank, like he's taking a breath between scenes in a stage play.

Same thing happened when I caught him awake last night. He was sitting in the living room, emotionless, dressed for work at 3:14 am, staring at the front door.

I took a photo of it, but when I checked it to post it, it was gone.

I'm worried for my safety; every night I stay at that apartment, I know he's watching me. Every night I hear the door creak open, and I know if I react to him, he's going to try something.

Like he's mad I don't agree that he’s a person I've always know. Someone whom I've gone to school with, whose music I listen to, and who's gotten drinks with me and my friends before.

And the worst part is, I'm starting to remember him too.

Little bits and pieces of the lies I've been fed have been appearing in my mind when I look at him.

Memories of us hanging out, talking by lockers, and songs I've never heard before playing in a loop in my brain.

I'm scared, and I'm going to put this post out there to try and get help and to help myself remember he's not real.

I’ll make another update post soon when I figure something out. I'm not sure what I'm going to do, but I have to do something.

I just hope this post doesn't disappear.
