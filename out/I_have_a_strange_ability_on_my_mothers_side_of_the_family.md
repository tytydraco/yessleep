Hello everyone. Recently I (24M) might have discovered something that, quite frankly, disturbs me. But I need you to understand how it came to be. You see, I was raised mostly by my mother and her side of the family. She and my dad have been divorced since I have memory, and by the way the rest of the family talks about it, it wasn't pretty. They are a relatively normal Latino family; strict, but loving. However, there was one rule that I was taught frequently: I must watch the way I speak. I don't even mean cussing; again, as a Latino family, cuss words were a second language. It wasn't even about respect or the volume of my voice. I was simply not allowed to wish ill will on anyone. I couldn't even wish they had "the day they deserve". I always thought we were just a very respectful, Catholic family. But now, after recent events, I've been connecting dots, and the picture seems quite disturbing, and even impossible, so I ask you to please hear me out. 

The first time I noticed it, I was a teenager. Maybe 12-13 y/o. Somehow, the topic of my dad's side of the family was brought up. You see, my mother never had a problem with my dad, it was his family that caused all the problems, from what I've been told. My mother would sometimes shit talk them; calling his mother a crazy woman who overprotected him, and calling his sisters a bunch of hypocritical prudes. That time, however, something was different. "They keep judging my decision to leave your father because it goes against God's word, as if his sister wasn't sleeping around with any man she met. I wished she became pregnant. I'm sure her fucking mom would have a heart attack", she said. Immediately after...silence. Everyone at the table became tense. Even the air felt heavy, as if she had done something wrong and everyone was silently judging her. But they just went back to eating. I was so confused. Time passed and I simply forgot about it, simply an awkward family moment. 

One day, however, my mom received a call from my dad. I couldn't remember the last time my dad talked to my mom, so I immediately knew something was wrong. She hung up the call, and gave me a look that I had never seen before. I used to think it was sadness in her eyes, but now I know; it was guilt. "Your grandma...she passed away", she told me. My grandma died from a heart attack; turns out my aunt had been hiding a pregnancy from her, and when she found out, they had a heated argument ending on the heart attack that killed her. I couldn't believe it...It was a coincidence, right? My grandma was old, and let's say my mom wasn't lying when talking about my aunt. It was a matter of time before something like this happened. Still...it felt wrong. 

Years later, I convinced myself it was just that, a crazy coincidence. The kind of story you tell your drunk friends and say "weird, right?". But then, it happened again. Only this time to me. You see, my father became chronically ill. I won't go into details, but it was the kind of disease where, as grim as it sounds, keeping the person alive was a form of torture rather than healthcare. He slowly lost the capacity to do many things, to the point I had to move with him to become his nurse, essentialy. It was a tough labor; cook for him, bathe him, change him, drive him to medical appointments, staying up at night in case something happened. On top of that, I was still a uni student. Both of our lives were becoming hell due to this disease.

It was an innocent comment. One of those you overshare when you're a couple of drinks in, with your best friend. "Can I be honest? Sometimes I wished he died. So he could rest." Then the same sensation. The air was heavy. As if the universe was judging me for what I had said. There was an immediate feeling of shame inside me, as if I had done something wrong. The very next day, I was at a class when I received a call. It was my mom, asking me to go home. It was bad news, clearly; my mom was the kind of person who couldn't give bad news on the phone. I just knew. She didn't need to tell me. I was just going there, taking the longest bus ride I ever had, to get a confirmation. My dad had passed away while I was at school. 

I keep thinking about it. It can't be real, can it? How many times have I said "I wish I was rich"? Never happened. It's almost as if this "ability" only grants ill will wishes. And my family...why not talk about it? They just said "watch how you speak", but never anything else. Did they think I wouldn't believe them? Is this some sort of family secret? Maybe I'm risking everything by posting this, but I need a way out. I need someone to tell me I'm crazy. Now I spend hours going through every single moment in my life, asking myself if it was my fault. Like the one time I was 12 and thought that it would be cool for classes to be suspended, only for my teacher to have a car crash that almost killed him. Was that my fault? "What about X moment? Was that my fault?" is the only thought in my head now. And now I'm scared. For those times I wished something bad happened to the people I love. Out of anger, out of heartbreak, just as a joke...I didn't mean it, obviously. I'm just scared this "ability" won't understand that.
