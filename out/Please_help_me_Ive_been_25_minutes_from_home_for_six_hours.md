I left my house at 9:57 yesterday morning to get a wart burned off my foot. Gross I know, lets not lose ourselves in that detail.

That was the entire event. I woke up. I helped my handsome husband who I’d give anything to speak to right now get our toddler son off to daycare. I waved them off, got ready and got in the car to head to the appointment.

I live in South Texas, and my podiatrist is on the other side of San Antonio, so I had about a forty-five minute drive.

I stopped for gas on the way, listened to a podcast, and complained out loud to nobody about traffic on 1604. 

The appointment took maybe twenty minutes, she cut my foot, froze it, I told an uninteresting story about my Bruja aunt who recommended at this point I try to “sell my wart”. My aunt said that the mind controls sickness. I joked who would want my wart. I just tried to distract myself from the doctor burrowing into my foot and crack some jokes.

The doctor froze the wart, told me it might blister, handed me a sheet of aftercare instructions, and said, “See you in four weeks if it’s still there.” 

I remember all of this because I keep replaying it, looking for the moment something changed.  But hey thats why you’re all here while I outsource my sanity and crowdsource a solution.

I walked back through the parking lot at 11:06 a.m. 

It was hot already. The kind of September heat we get here where the inside of your car feels hostile. It’s heavy and smothering, you can feel the air touching you all around.

 Only 4 more months until my husband and our family are restationed in Virginia.

Maybe that’s it, maybe I got in a car wreck and this hell.
