How’d you know? I can hear ya askin’, I can hear. But it isn’t so deep down as that, not upcreek. There’s ways you tell, thats what they was always telling me. 

Group of guys callin themselves Cheroques always talking about the skin walkers, old yellow eye hot eye bastards out deep wood. Old Stan says he can't trust a word out of any of them, but old stan lost his leg to the sugars last year and we were all surprised because his eyes didn’t go first. Jesus he could put back that shit he made up on the still like water and it was clear as water. Course when you shook it in the plastic the bubbles stayed longer so you knew it was true shine. 

Same way old dicky from down city could tell what was real whisky by shaking it and looking at the bubbles. He taught me the trick old dicky. Old dicky was older, but we called him old on the account that he was a bit nuts, always smoking plastic and running around chopping shit with his hatchet. Sammy two tone, but we called him Sammy Semaphore nobody could remember why, said that he thought Ol’ dicky was chopping shit with that old hatchet “hoping it might jump up and try and grab him.” Now I think Ol Dicky might’ve had the right idea all things considered. 

I wasn’t walking up creek you know, I was just letting the creek flow toward me in one of the chairs that folds up, the blue one that I keep for fishing. I wasn’t fishing today, but I wanted to. I was just smoking that old hot thing that momma made me keep from dad. The one dad made out the corncob. It works real good afterall for carrying me home when I’ve been working through a hard one. She smokes hot in the bowl, but it still works just fine. I was sitting in the folding seat and waiting for a reason to keep walking to strike me, probably something simple, letting getting a beer. 

Then I saw her, she was furry still, but in the wrong places. Furry on her hands, and you could tell far off from the shadows. The shadows in front never moved, but the ones in back did. I don’t know if that’ll make any sense to you. She was shaking too, and I think her eyes started glowing just a little, just a little at first then it started getting bigger. 

I think it might have been panic but right about here my heart started to beat so hard I could hear it from outside my chest and I was getting scared it might alert the creature. I was breathing pretty loud, probably a lot louder than my heart, but by god I swear if it was gonna hear anything it would be that damn heart beating half out my chest with every pump. She was on a mission I think, whispering and muttering to herself. First thing I thought to myself is “Don’t you do it” On account of the fact that I almost always do the stupidest thing I can think of, but here I was thinking “I could figure out what shes saying.” Plus, and if you tell anyone I’ll say you made it up, but I was thinking I might wanna see if it got the same parts as you and me have. 

I’m a prayin’ man but a man all the same and when you see something young and naked running around you struggle not to look. Most people can not follow though, I thought I was one of the ones who couldn’t follow but I left old foldy out in the moonlight thinking about proving old Stan wrong for ol’ dicky. That’s how old times were though.

I only ever saw the face in silhouette under soft moon but it had grown out like a dogs snout would. She was sniffing the air and whispering to nobody at all. Every muscle in my body pulled away but this damn bug I got in my brain that makes me do dumb stuff said “That tree is pretty close bud, take the walk forward, no going back now. No running anyway.” so I walked close and avoided every stick on the ground that could crack out of luck because I never looked at my god damn feet.

She was sniffing the air and I was trying not to make yellow or brown because I was so god damn scared about it all and the bug in my brain had finally shut up so I was on my own. I hid behind the tree and cursed myself for being so damn fat that the tree might not cover me on both sides and I tried not to breath at all which was hard because I’d been running and my guts felt like they might spill out of me if I didn’t hold onto them tight. I listened thought and she was whispering alright. “Epunaman” She was saying and I knew it wasn’t a language I should know right off because she had an accents like like Cochi has on the reservation. She said it a few more times “Epunaman, Epunaman” as if she wanted me to hear it. 

I waited by the tree until I sunk down to the ground and I sat there for a bit just thinking about it all and I don’t know if any of it was worth anything at all. Then I got up and got my chair and I went home thinking the whole way “don’t have any more, people won’t believe you if you have anymore” but i did anyway. I’m a scamp.
