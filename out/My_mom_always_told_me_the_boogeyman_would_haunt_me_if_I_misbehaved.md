I didn't believer her until... the first incident. When I was six, I accidentally dropped my milk on the floor. My mom looked like she was about to scream at me, but then quickly calmed down and told me that the boogeyman would have a visit. This was the third time this happened, so I wasn't to scared.

But this night, I heard something outside of my window, scraping against it. When I turned on the lights and looked outside, I saw something gray quickly flash over the fence. When I quickly told mom, she said it was just a raccoon, and there are lots of raccoons in our area. I calmed down and went to sleep soon after.

The nighttime events continued. I heard something going on around the house. In my parents' room, on the roof, in our backyard. Every time I asked my mom, she always said it was a raccoon. One time, I asked my dad. He said that it was probably a bird.

My dad didn't come home often. He was a construction worker, so he usually stayed nights. He was a great dad, always cracking jokes. He was also really different from my mom, but he also kept telling me to behave, or else the boogeyman would get me.

One night, I went to a sleepover with my friend, and while we were pillow fighting, I accidentally threw a pillow too hard and it hit my friend's mom on the face. I quickly apologized, and she said it was alright. But I was still worried. Before going to sleep, my friend told me why my face was so pale.

"Because the boogeyman will haunt me." I replied.

He responded with, "Wait, you still believe that shit?"

"I mean, not really, but every time I misbehave, there's always some scratching outside the window. And this is probably the worst thing I've done,"

"Uhh, okay. Well, see if anything happens tonight."

It took me an hour to fall asleep. But despite that, I didn't hear a single thing. And it wasn't like we weren't near a window, either. Hell, the forest is right outside my friend's bedroom. So, the next day, I told my mom that my friend and I misbehaved a bit, but nothing happened. No boogeyman.

"Well, sometimes the boogeyman can be busy and take a while to get to someone." She then laughed. I weakly laughed too.

The next day, at school, my friend came in looking like he didn't sleep at all. He hadn't.

"There was something outside of my room last night. It was tall and gray, and it had no eyes or mouth."

"Wha- are you sure you saw it?"

"When I told my parents about it, it was gone. They said it was probably just my imagination or a nightmare. But I still couldn't sleep at all."

Guess my mom was right about the boogeyman taking a while.

And the weird night occurrences continued to get stranger. Whenever I did something even slightly wrong, even not getting a 100% on a test, there was always some sort of weird shit happening. One time I thought I saw a long gray thing out my window, with no eyes or a mouth. I rubbed my eyes, and it was gone. I kept hearing things, and one time I even heard some say "huck" outside my window, barely audible. But the strangest thing is, not everything I did happened to attract the boogeyman.

One time I was talking in class, and the teacher got mad at me. Nothing happened that night. Another time, I kicked my friend a bit too hard for some reason that I forgot about, but nothing happened. But when I accidentally coughed all over my food, I saw the boogeyman outside my window.

As time went on, the boogeyman appeared less and less, because I misbehaved less and less. I made sure everything I did was perfect. I barely tried anything new, because then I couldn't misbehave if I didn't do anything. Then, one day I got a letter from a prestigious university on the other side of the country, saying that I was accepted. I couldn't believe it. I was finally getting out of the little town in the middle of nowhere, to a place where I could be admired. But my parents didn't want that. They wanted me to continue my father's stupid ass legacy of being a construction worker. We kept arguing and arguing, until the day before I was meant to go, they kept and hid the ticket I needed to enter the school. I couldn't believe it. My own parents, not letting me follow my dreams?

"We just want what's best for you!" said my mom.

"You don't know anything about what's best for me, you stupid bitch!"

I already regretted the words before they left my mouth. But it was too late. Before my parents could say anything, I quickly ran out the door and ran as quickly as I could to my friend's apartment.

"What's the matter?" they asked me.

After I told them everything, he said, "Man, that's fucked up. But, I'm sure your parents will understand. Maybe what you can do is apologize to your parents, try to see if you can get the ticket back, and if you can't, then come back and we'll see if you can get another way into the school."

"Well, it's pretty easy to get another ticket, but it's the principle of the thing, you know? Besides, my parents are not what I'm worried about."

"Then what are you wo- oh don't tell me it's that boogeyman bullshit again!"

"Yeah, I know it sounds stupid, but, I always have a feeling that something's going to get me whenever I do something wrong."

My friend sighed. "Look, I'll give you my Swiss army knife. Think of it like a parting present or something. And if that boogeyman tried to get you, stab his ass."

So I took the knife and I went back home and I apologized to my parents and they forgave me. They even gave me my ticket back. I kept it with me and within eye's view of my bed, and I decided to pull and all-nighter, and the sleep on the plane ride. It's a good thing I did, because at around midnight, I saw a long, spindly, gray arm come around the doorframe. I quickly pulled out the army knife out from under the pillow where I hid it, and pulled out the knife part.

It was the boogeyman. A long, strange gray thing that looked kinda like a xenomorph, but with no mouth, or eyes. The area where they should have been was a much lighter gray than the rest of its body.

Just when the boogeyman was about the reach me, I quickly lunged at it, and stabbed it, pulling down my knife through its body. I heard the ripping of thick fabric, and a lot of cursing. I saw some human skin under the gray parts.

"You fucking bastard, where did you get that knife from?!" It was said in a voice I knew all too well.

"Dad!?"

Then mom came it. 

"Honey, are you alright! What did this boy do to you!"

"He stabbed, me that's what he did."

I was astonished. I couldn't believe it. It was my dad all along that was tormenting me?! Him?! I was furious.

"You little shit, you lied to me all these years, and for what! To make me behave a little better!"

Mom responded. "Oh, my child, we just wanted the perfect son. But, I guess it didn't work out. Well, I guess there's always next time. I'll go get the rope."

Just as she left, I kicked my dad in the nuts as hard as I could. Then, I stabbed in his armpit. He screamed.

I quickly ran right past him, then through the hallway, pushing mom aside. I quickly unlocked the front door and ran outside, with my parents on my heels. Just as they were about to get me, a car suddenly drove right into my parents. I dove out of the way just before it hit me.

"Oh my god oh my god oh my god!!! What have I done! What have I- what the fuck?! What's that?"

The driver noticed me. I told her everything that happened.

The next day, the police told me that they had my mother, but my dad, and the suit, somehow escaped. I was worried, but it didn't matter anyway. I was going to a place on the other side of the country. There were people searching for my dad, so he would never get anywhere. My parents never even bothered to find out where I was going. I was finally safe.

I got a degree in nuclear physics, became a nuclear engineer, and moved to another country. I worked at a power plant, made a lot of money, and got a fiancee. I was finally safe.

Or, at least I thought I was. The day before my wedding, I found out that my car's tires were slashed. The facility that I work at was pretty far removed from the nearest city, but I could still get service. So I called my colleague friend, who left a bit before me. They'll come get me in 10 minutes. I don't know if that's enough time.

Right now, I can see something outside of my car window, a long, gray, bloody humanoid. It's in the bushes, and I can barely see it. But I can tell that it's waiting for the perfect moment to strike.
