I keep replaying the moment I woke up, trying to make it make sense, and it still doesn’t. My head was pounding, that dull kind of pain that sits behind your eyes and makes the whole world feel underwater. I was on the floor of our living room, and my body felt heavy in a way I didn’t recognize, like it belonged to someone else. Only one of my eyes would open. The other had something running down it, warm at first, then not warm at all.

When my head finally tipped to the side, things started coming into focus, slowly, in pieces. The light was low and yellow, the kind we used to leave on when we didn’t want to fully wake up the house. And there was music. It took me longer than it should have to place it, which scares me more now than it did then, because it was our song. The one from the wedding. I couldn’t understand why it was playing, or who had put it on, or how long it had been playing while I lay there not hearing it.

That’s when I saw it in the corner of the room. It was dancing. I don’t know how else to describe the way it moved except to say it was wrong, the way a kid moves when they’ve just started ballet lessons and think they already know more than they do, throwing their arms out like they’ve seen it done and are trying to prove something. Except this wasn’t a kid, and whatever was underneath the movement didn’t fit the shape it was making. I noticed the dress before I let myself think about what it meant. It was your wedding dress. For one second my body actually tried to respond to that, my leg twitched like it wanted to stand, though I’m still not sure if that happened or if I only wanted it to. I remember hoping, right then, that you were somehow the reason this was happening, even though I had no idea what “this” was.

It kept dancing for a while, until it drifted over to the shelf where we kept the photo. The wedding one, the two of us right after eating the cake, still in disbelief that it was real. The second it picked that photo up, something changed. Whatever presence had filled the room while it danced was just gone, like it had stepped out of itself entirely to be alone with that picture. I tried to hold onto consciousness, but the pain kept pulling me under, and I could feel my body getting colder, not just numb, actually cold, like something was leaving it.

Then it turned to look at me. After everything, I think that was the strangest part, how understanding its eyes were. Like it knew exactly what I’d lost and had lost it too.

“I miss her and her silly dance, from when she wanted to take a break from her life… from your marriage. How is It possible to miss something we always saw as so distant?”, It said.

It came toward me after that, unhurried, like there was no reason to rush anything anymore. It set the photo down on my chest. Then it pressed something into my other hand, and I didn’t have to look to know what it was. I understood everything the moment it met my eyes. After that, everything went dark again.

I woke up today in the same place. The sun was already coming through the window, but I still had our photo pressed against my chest, and the knife in my other hand. I get it now, my love. I think the both of us will finally find you soon.
