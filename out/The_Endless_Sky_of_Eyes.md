I was told by my shrink to write all of this down to come to terms with it or something along those lines but I don’t think it matters. I know it doesn’t matter. Nothing I do matters anymore. I doubt she believes me anyway. It’s not like I blame her, who would believe me? I know you won’t. I wouldn’t believe me either. What I’m about to tell you is pure insanity. I’m simply asking that you don’t write me off as a madman. I’ve never had any history of drug abuse, schizophrenic episodes, or any mental heath issues. I’m as vanilla as a person can be. The horrors I’ve witnessed have changed me forever. I’ve seen what lies beyond our reality. I’ve seen things I was never supposed to see. I’ve seen the most demented, fucked up thing I could possibly ever imagine.*

*I’ve seen the sky of eyes*.

I sat on my patio looking off into the sunset. I had just moved into to my apartment, still getting used to the new area. Being on the third story, I had a nice view of the complex. Everything felt so right. The warm air gently swaying on my face, the sun slowly going down reflecting the golden hour. It was beautiful. I had just taken up a new well paying job after college so I was living here comfortably. I remember thinking at that moment that everything was perfect. That nothing could ruin this for me.

I pulled out my pack of cigarettes and took one out. I put the cigarette between my lips and flicked my lighter and gently took a drag. I held in the smoke and blew it out watching the smoke slowly disappear into the air. I could feel the nicotine working its magic and just as I was about to take a second drag, I heard a knock at my door.

*knock knock knock*

“Not now.” I said to no one in particular.

I took a look at my cigarette, not wanting to put it down.

*Let’s hope this is quick.*

I lightly ashed the cigarette and walked to my front door. I slowly opened it and was greeted by a tall man in a black and white suit. He had at be at least 6 and a half feet tall. He wore a fedora and seemed to be in his mid 40’s. He was holding a suitcase in his right arm. He smiled when he saw me, but it wasn’t threatening in anyway. It was a friendly smile, like a new neighbor coming to introduce themselves.

I was going to greet him but was so put off by his posture I stumbled over my words.

“Hello” The man said and took off his fedora in a respectful manner and held it to his chest.

“Hello. Can I help you?” I said.

“Why yes you can! May I come in?” The man asked.

I was hesitant, I hadn’t expected him to ask this question. I didn’t speak and continued to stare at him, he was oddly mesmerizing in a way that’s difficult to describe. There was just something about him. Something off but I couldn’t put my finger to it.

When the man noticed my silence, he spoke again.

“Everything will make sense if you give me a chance, I mean no harm and that’s a promise.”

I didn’t trust this man, but I trusted that he clearly had something to say to me. But what could he want from someone like me? I knew nobody in the area and certainly didn’t know this man and he was not the type of person that you would forget.

I was very reluctant to let this man into my apartment, but he didn’t seem to be dangerous. He just seemed weird. I stared at him for a few more seconds before I opened my door wider and signaled him to come inside. The man’s smile widened and he stepped in.

The man looked around my apartment as if he were studying it and placed his fedora on my coat rack.

“Nice place you got here. Hope you don’t mind my fedora being put there. My name is Damien” He said in a friendly voice.

“Cut to the chase man what do you want.” I said sternly.

Damien’s smile inverted and turned into a snarl in a quick second. He walked closer towards me and jabbed a finger to my chest.

“Now listen here Joshua. You don’t know who I am so I’m going to let that go but I don’t take disrespect well. This will be your only warning got it?”

The tone Damien spoke in froze me in my place for a quick moment, but it slipped my mind after I realized he just said my name.

“How do you know my name?” I said trying to sound as dominate as I could, but failed. I suddenly felt very small and I realized in that moment this man could beat me to death if he wanted to.

“You don’t need to worry about that. Did you hear me or not?” Damien said in the same tone.

All I could do was nod. The smile returned his face.

“Great. Apologies for my behavior there, like I said I don’t mean you any harm. I just don’t like when I get treated that way, but you seemed to have taken the message. Now you wanna know why I’m here right?”

I nodded again.

“I would like to show you something.” Damien said while putting his hands behind his back and straightening his pose.

This immediately threw me off, what did he mean? I had never seen this man in my life but now he was in my apartment telling me he wanted to show me something? I should have kicked him out right there, but I was afraid. There was something about this man that told me I shouldn’t have let him inside, but it was too late now. I just wanted to enjoy my damn cigarette.

“What is it?” I said, hoping this wouldn’t be long.

Damien placed his briefcase on my couch and began to pull off the straps.

“Woah woah what’s in there?” I said.

He stopped and looked at me.

“If you would like me to leave just say it. What’s in this briefcase is what I would like to show you, but just know once I leave, you will never be able to see it.” Damien said with an annoyed look on his face.

He waited a few more seconds for me to respond but I didn’t say anything.

“What I do is show people things. Things that can change your life or the way you look at the world. I can change everything for you if you let me, but if you want to continue to live in ignorance with what you think you might know, then once again i offer you the opportunity to kick me out.”

I’ll admit this interested me, but I couldn’t get a proper read on Damien. I still didn’t trust him but he was peaking my interest so I decided to give him a chance.

“Alright carry on.” I said.

Damien looked back at his suitcase and continued to pull on the straps and it finally gave.

I don’t know what I expected to see, but it certainly wasn’t what he pulled out. Damien reached into his suitcase and I saw what it was. He held out a small glowing orb. Waves of different neon colors flashed rapidly with it and I could hear the sounds of electricity coming from it, like when you stand next to a working fuse box. Its glow was almost blinding and I had to shield my eyes from it. I was certain if I looked directly at it for too long I would go blind.

“What the fuck??” I blurted out.

I looked at Damien but his grin never left his face, however it no longer looked so friendly.

“Leave my apartment now. Leave and put that back!” I yelled at him.

Damien didn’t react, he slowly walked towards me and gripped my arm with a great force. The pain was excruciating. He slowly lifted the orb to my face and I was now screaming, I could almost feel my eyes burning in their sockets. I found myself unable to move, some unseeable force was keeping me in my place and I was starting to feel hypnotized. Then just as I was about to pass out from the pain, I heard Damien speak in the most inhuman distorted glitched voice I have ever heard.

“**TAKE US**”

I blacked out. I’m not sure how long I spent in the darkness but it must have been at least a few minutes, yet I remained conscious in my head. All I could think of was how poorly I handled the situation. I shouldn’t have let him inside.

Suddenly I began to feel wind against my body and I slowly opened my eyes. I was falling from a high height and eventually I landed on the ground with a loud splat. An unspeakable pain shot throughout my whole body but it became easy to ignore when I looked around and saw my surroundings.

What I saw was the most horrific thing I have ever seen, and I hope nobody will ever have to see what I’ve seen.

Red mist floated in the air and I could smell an overwhelming scent of copper and puke overtaking my nostrils. I looked up to see where I had fallen from and I froze in my place. I rapidly blinked a few times hoping I was hallucinating but it didn’t take me long to realize this was very much real.

What I saw in the sky were human eyes. Thousands if not millions of them all in different shapes and sizes completely overtaking the entire sky. Some were enormous and some were smaller. They all were looking around frantically as if searching for a way out. I could hear the sounds of their movement. It sounded fleshy and wet, like a loud smacking sound. Most of them in my sight looked down at me and stared. I could feel them on me. The feeling of thousands of eyes looking at me at once is something I can’t begin to describe. I could feel the weight of them. It was keeping me pinned to the ground. I tried to get up but felt a shooting pain throughout my entire bottom half of my body.

That’s when I noticed how the ground felt.

My feet felt like they were very slowly sinking, like gooey quicksand. When I looked down I screamed. The ground was made of what looked to be raw human flesh. It felt sticky yet slimy at the same time and they were all connected together with what seemed to be more eyes. The eyes were tied together like rope and were connecting to different pieces of organs and intestines to make ground.

I franticly looked around for somewhere to hide, and was forced to take in more horror.

I looked around and noticed there were hundreds of human faces towering over me growing from the ground. Each face was forcefully stretched upwards and almost looked like trees. Humans arms meant to resemble branches were at the very top of each face, starting from they're foreheads. All of them had the facial expression of a scream but they didn’t move or make any sound, they were completely frozen in place except for their eyes. Their eyes looked at me helplessly and I began to wonder if each face was still alive, but only able to move their eyes. I can't explain it but they felt alive. It was as if I was standing in the middle of some kind of demented forest.

It took every ounce of strength I had in my body to stand up but was again reminded of the pain I was in. Adrenaline wore off after I tried to stand on my feet but pain shot through my entire body once more and I fell to my knees. I began to weep. My understanding of everything I thought I knew about the world now gone and replaced with the impossible.

I was immediately determined to get out of here.

After a few moments I tried to stand up again but my legs still wouldn’t cooperate. I didn’t know what to do, I was completely helpless.

I don't know how long I sat there crying, but at some point I finally gave into my senses. I jolted my head around for any kind of direction to go in, but found nothing useful. The extended faces surrounding me all stared at me with unblinking eyes, they felt so alive.

I began to crawl, my hands gripping the wet flesh. The red mist was starting to blind me and it was hard to keep my eyes open but I kept crawling. I was desperate. The thought of staying here unable to escape for the rest of my life motivated me to keep moving. I tried to calm myself but I couldn’t. How could I? Just a couple minutes ago I was sitting on my patio in peace and now I was seeing the most unimaginable hell I’ve ever experienced.

I was lost in my thoughts but was interrupted when I heard what sounded like footsteps coming from my left. I panicked and crawled as fast as I could behind an extended face trying to stay out of view.

I heard the footsteps approaching around me and a woman came into view. She was drenched in blood and her right leg seemed to have been slashed but she was walking without any problem. She looked to be normal but I was cautious. When she noticed me she called out.

“Hey! Hey it’s okay. I’m a person like you.” She said.

I didn’t respond.

“My god what happened to you?” She looked genuinely worried.

I looked at her for a moment before I responded.

“Fell… from. the sky.”

My voice sounded raspy and it was difficult for me to get out the words. It was only then I noticed the pain in my chest. It hurt to speak.

She walked towards me, looked up at the extended face and back down to me.

“Can you walk?” She asked.

I shook my head.

She looked back up at the face I was leaning on and gripped an arm a few feet above me. She began to pull on it and the arm snapped off with a sickening crunch. She carelessly tossed the arm onto the ground and snapped another arm off. The sound was revolting and I had to hold myself back from vomiting.

“Grab my hand. You can trust me. What’s your name?” She asked holding out her hand.

“Joshua” I said with a grunt.

She tried to smile but her face wouldn’t allow her.

“I’m Amanda. It’s good to meet you Joshua.”

I took her hand and she helped me up. I screamed from the pain and she immediately slapped my mouth shut with her free hand.

“Be quiet. They’ll hear you.” She all but whispered.

I wanted to ask what she meant but I couldn’t speak. I looked at her with confusion and her face changed to a look of sadness.

“You’re new here aren’t you?”

I nodded.

“Did you see Damien?”

It was only then I gained the strength to talk. I needed some sort of explanation and I knew Amanda knew something. Anything at all about what this place was, or why I was chosen to be put through this nightmare.

“You.. saw him. too?” I said.

Amanda didn’t respond, instead she picked up the bloody arm she tossed on the ground and handed it to me, then the other. I didn’t want to take them. It was absolutely vile to see another person handle human remains with no hesitation. It made me wonder how long she had been here.

“Use these as crutches and follow me. You’re new so you still have a chance.”

I still couldn’t take them. Amanda could see the look of terror on my face and she started to look even more upset.

“Okay I’ll do it for you. I can understand your hesitation.” She said.

Amanda helped me up and I stood. She placed the two broken arms underneath my armpits and to my surprise they actually held me up. I tried to push back my disgust, I needed to cooperate.

I needed to get out of here.

“What. Is this. Place?” I asked, more pain shooting through my chest as I spoke.

“I don’t know. I don’t know where it came from or why it’s here or how it was created but I stopped trying to make sense of it a long time ago.” She said.

“How long.. have you been. Here?” I asked.

“Again I don’t know. But a long time. There’s no night and day here and nobody tries to keep track of time. But I’m grateful for it. I don’t think I want to know.”

She paused for a few moments before she spoke again. I could hear the hopelessness in her voice.

“There are creatures that wander around here and if they catch you, they do.. things to you. I don’t know what exactly. All I know is they somehow make you apart of this place, just like the huge faces you see around us or the eyeballs in the sky. They make you apart of it and once you are apart of it there’s no escape. You’re stuck.”

She pointed to the sky but didn’t look. I didn’t blame her.

“Every single eye you see up there is a person, or was a person. They are forced to stay up there for eternity. All they have left.. is their *fucking* eyes. All they can do is look around.”

I couldn’t begin to digest this information, this was impossible. Everything about this place was impossible. I wanted to convince myself I must have been in a horrific nightmare but I knew I wasn’t. I would take any chance to believe this wasn’t real, but of course I knew it was. I just couldn’t accept it. I was being overwhelmed with too much horrific information, I just wanted to go home, away from whatever this was. This all happened so fast.

Just as I was starting to take my reality in, we heard a blood curdling scream somewhere in the distance and the sound of hundreds of legs slapping against the fleshy ground. It wasn’t far. Amanda suddenly looked terrified.

“Follow me. Now!” She yelled.

I tried to move on my arm crutches but the ground was so slippery I fell down hard on my second step. Amanda swore in a panic and I couldn’t get up. My face fell hard into the flesh underneath and I tasted fresh copper and raw meat. I gagged so hard I thought my stomach would explode.

“I’m going to pick you up, okay?”

I didn’t bother trying to reply, I had never been in so much pain in my life. It took everything I had in my body to not pass out. I tried my hardest to keep my eyes open but it proved difficult, the mist still blinding my vision.

Amanda swooped me into her arms and I heard her grunt in a struggle. As soon as she lifted me up she began to jog.

“You aren’t.. dead so you can still go back to… your life” she said, I could hear in her voice how hard she was trying to keep me up.

Amanda limped and grunted while holding me, I could progressively feel how weak her arms were becoming. I could hear the footsteps behind us growing closer and Amanda screamed in my ear. After she screamed she seemed to move much faster, as she did the sounds of feet began to fade but she didn’t stop. I lifted my head and looked around slowly, the mist was more blinding than ever.

Eventually an extended face appeared out of nowhere in front of us with no time to react. Without warning, Amanda aggressively slammed into it and I dropped on my back. The fall didn’t hurt so much this time, but I could hear Amanda moaning in pain.

“yy..you.. okay?” I barely managed to get out.

“Yes. I’m alright.” She said in measured breaths.

“You said… I could.. leave. How?” I asked.

Now that we were safe for the time being, I wanted to ask her everything on my mind. While speaking was difficult, I felt I needed to so I could understand this place, plus she said I could get out of here and I needed to figure out how to.

Amanda seemed to take a few moments before responding, I let her take her time.

“We’re almost there. There’s a tunnel ahead that you can crawl through to get back to your life. Since you’re still alive you have a body to go back to. But if you’re dead like me you don’t have a body to go to, so it’ll be useless for me. It’s up ahead. Right there.”

She pointed ahead of us and just behind the red mist ahead, I could barely make out what looked to be some kind of hill or mountain structured with flesh. There was a small opening in front of it the size of a sewer tank, just barely enough to fit a human body through.

"You're.. dead??" I said in shock.

She let me look for a few seconds before speaking again.

“I need to tell you something.” She said with uncertain sternness in her voice. Like she was afraid of what she was about to say.

“Do you want to know how I ended up here after I died?”

I looked up at her, unsure if I wanted to know the answer. But her tone of voice made it clear I needed to listen.

“Damien came to visit me too while I was still alive, and I was in your same position. I didn’t have another person to help me get out of here but I figured it out on my own. I went back to my life but when I died I ended up here again. No heaven or hell. This is my afterlife.”

I didn’t understand what she was trying to say. I looked at her with pure confusion written on my face, but she answered all of my questions with one single sentence.

“This will be your afterlife Joshua.”

My eyes widened in fear and I shook my head desperately, no longer acknowledging the pain it brought me. The weight of her words slammed into my head with the force of a thousand suns.

This? This was my end? After everything I’ve done in my life and everything I’ve accomplished. It was all for this? She had to be lying, this couldn’t be true.

This could not be true.

“WHAT?” I yelled.

Amanda could do nothing but look at me with sympathy. I began to weep into my hands. My stomach was dropping so hard I finally vomited onto the ground. I never knew words could have such impact. My stomach kept dropping to the point where I was gasping for air. After sometime it finally stopped, Amanda never said a word throughout my breakdown.

I didn’t have anything left to ask, nothing left to say. I understood in that moment nothing I will do in my life will matter any longer. Nothing will hold any meaning.

This was my eternity.

Just as I was about to start towards the tunnel, we heard it again. Footsteps. Footsteps roaring towards our direction, hundreds of little feet violently slapping against the flesh underneath us.

They were approaching us fast.

I shot my head towards Amanda as I realized this, she looked just as panicked as I felt. Her face turned a ghostly white underneath the blood on her skin, and her eyes gave off an expression of absolute horror.

“**GO!!!**” She screamed at me.

I turned around and crawled as fast as my body would allow towards the tunnel. The footsteps grew closer behind me and soon enough I could hear them directly behind me. I slammed one arm in front of the other at a speed I was in no business to move at. The pain shook throughout my entire body with every movement and it took every ounce of strength I had to not pass out. The tunnel was slowly growing bigger in my sight, I was getting close.

I heard Amanda shriek so violently it shook every bone in my body. I risked a look behind me and saw the source of her screams. I wish I never did.

She was being tightly held together by two enormously tall creatures. Creatures unlike anything I’ve ever seen. They stood at least twenty feet tall. Their heads looked to be what I could only describe as giant fly-like heads. They had at least twenty small eyes with huge cavernous mouths filled with hundreds of tiny teeth. They had two sharp whiskers above their eyes, the size and sharpness of machetes. Their bodies were skinny and drenched in slime and blood, green and black skin extended up towards their heads and I could make out hundreds of tiny feet throughout the entire body, yet somehow it could still stand on two small red covered legs. They looked two like giant, tall centipedes. Almost as tall as buildings.

One creature was gripping Amanda’s head and the other was holding her legs and they slowly extended her body out to eventually rip her body in half. Her screams were cut off with a disgustingly sickening snap as her flesh poured down and emptied from her body.

Throughout all of this, I never stopped crawling. I snapped my head back in front of me, not being able to process what I had just witnessed. The tunnel was right in front of me. I gripped the inside of the tunnel and was greeted with a never ending blackness.

The tunnel was extremely tight. I forcefully pushed myself further inside to the sounds of more footsteps behind me. I squeezed my full body inside of the tunnel running off pure adrenaline and heard the footsteps behind me retreat but that did nothing to calm my nerves.

I couldn’t move my arms or my legs, I was desperately pushing myself further unable to see the walls around me. The smell of blood and copper was stronger than ever. I dry heaved with short breaths trying to escape the tightness of the tube. I felt bone on my right arm snap and I screamed in pain, but never stopped crawling.

Breathing became difficult. I gasped for air until my lungs felt they were on fire and eventually I felt another bone on my rip cage crack along with my gasps. My mouth quickly became full with blood and I violently vomited onto myself. I was crying now, overwhelmed by claustrophobia and pain but I had no time to stop. I screamed in pain, disgust and adrenaline as loudly and hard as I could, and finally just as I was about to pass out, I squeezed myself forward one last time and felt my body falling at a great speed.

*More darkness, but I couldn’t feel my body. No light came for what felt like hours. I couldn’t feel anything. I was lost. My thoughts being my only company. I tried to move an arm or a leg but found I couldn’t. I couldn’t even feel them. I searched for a taste in my mouth but found nothing. I was an empty, soulless being*.

*I was nothing.* 

After sometime, I was finally able to open my eyes. Light overwhelmed my vision and I shot my body up. I had a massive migraine and every movement felt like a stab to my head. I searched around frantically but I was able to calm down when I noticed where I was. I was laying in the middle of my living room.

At my apartment.

I was alive again. Damien was gone.

I felt no pain. I looked around my body but found nothing. No scratches or marks anywhere to be found. I was my normal, healthy self again. I sat there on my floor unable to stand up and cried for what must have been hours. The horrors of what I had just witnessed fresh on my mind.

How was I supposed to live now?

How was I supposed to go throughout my daily routine normally knowing what awaits me on the other side?

I checked my phone and saw the time. Only an hour had passed, I hadn't been there for long. While my time in that place was very limited, it was enough to permanently scare me. At first I tried to tell myself it was just a nightmare, but when I searched my apartment, I found Damien's fedora still sitting on my coat rack.

That was enough for me to push those thoughts out of my head.

That was a week ago now. I’m alive physically, but not mentally. I have given up on myself and my life. I still work at my job but I’m thinking about quitting soon. I haven’t reached out to my friends or family since.

Nothing matters anymore.

I have started seeing a therapist so I could try to live normally again, but found that doesn’t help either. Nothing helps. I’ve tried everything I could think of. I can't imagine what my therapist will think about this when she reads it, but I don't care.

I’m afraid of what’s going to happen to my soul whenever I’m not here anymore. Every hour that passes is another hour I delay the inevitable. I’m afraid to live.

I’m afraid to die.
