I moved into this apartment in June. Fourth floor, old building, cheap enough that I ignored the ugly carpet and the fact that none of the doors close properly.



Nothing weird happened for the first month.



Then one night I was walking home around 11 and looked up at my building because I'd left the bedroom lamp on.



My apartment has five windows facing the street.



That night it had six.



There was a narrow window between my bedroom and kitchen windows, maybe half the width of the others. Yellow light behind it, curtains partly closed.



I actually stood there counting because I thought I had the wrong floor.



Five normal windows.



One extra.



When I got upstairs, I checked the wall where that window should have been.



There's nothing there.



It's a short section of wall between my bedroom closet and the kitchen cabinets. I measured it the next morning because this bothered me way more than it should have. From inside, there's maybe three feet of space between those rooms.



From the street, the extra window sits exactly there.



I convinced myself it belonged to another apartment somehow. Old buildings have weird layouts.



Then I saw it again.



This time the light in the extra room was off, but the curtains were open.



I could see part of a bed.



My bed.



Same dark green blanket. Same stupid white pillowcase with a blue stripe.



I went upstairs and my bedroom looked normal.



After that I started checking almost every time I came home after dark.



The window isn't always there.



I've never seen it during daylight. Some nights it's missing completely. Other nights it appears for a few minutes and then is gone when I look back.



Three nights ago I saw someone inside it.



Just a person standing near the glass.



I couldn't make out a face from the sidewalk, but they were wearing a grey shirt and dark shorts.



I was wearing a grey shirt and dark shorts.



I didn't go upstairs.



I walked to a 24-hour cafe about ten minutes away and stayed there until almost 2am. I kept telling myself it was another tenant, reflections, perspective, anything.



When I finally went home, the sixth window was gone.



Yesterday I decided I was being ridiculous.



I came home around 10:30 and deliberately didn't look up until I was almost at the entrance.



Six windows.



The extra room was lit.



This time whoever was inside was sitting on the edge of the bed with their back toward the window.



I stopped walking.



A few seconds later they stood up.



Then they walked out of view in the direction of my kitchen.



I stayed outside.



Maybe thirty seconds later, the light in my actual kitchen turned on.



I could see it through the normal kitchen window.



Then my bedroom light turned on.



Then the bathroom.



One after another, exactly like somebody was walking through my apartment.



I called the police and told them I thought someone had broken in.



They checked the apartment.



Nobody was there. Door was locked. Windows locked. Nothing missing.



One officer asked why I'd thought someone was inside.



I didn't tell him about the sixth window.



I slept at my sister's place last night.



This afternoon I went back with her to grab clothes.



In daylight there were five windows, obviously.



But while we were inside, my sister asked why I'd moved the wardrobe in my bedroom.



I hadn't.



It was pulled maybe six inches away from the wall.



Behind it, near the floor, there was a little rectangle where the paint was a different color.



We moved the wardrobe farther.



There's an old metal handle embedded in the wall.



No door outline. No hinges. Just a handle.



My sister tried pulling it.



I stopped her.



I'm back at her place now.



It's 11:40pm.



About twenty minutes ago I walked outside and looked toward my building from the end of the street.



The sixth window is there again.



The curtains are open.



I can see my bedroom.



And someone is standing beside the wardrobe with one hand against the wall.
