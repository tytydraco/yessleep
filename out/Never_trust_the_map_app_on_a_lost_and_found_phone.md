I am currently typing this from my bathroom floor, or what I thought was my bathroom. This is my only way to contact the world I knew and I need someone to know what happened in case I don't make it out of this house. 

I usually work the late shifts at a warehouse. The pay is good and working until night time never bothered me. Last night I was loading up the last few boxes in the truck when I dropped my phone. The screen shattered before my eyes and along with it also my only way of getting home. This was not my home town, I just moved a few weeks ago to escape the city costs. It is a small town however the warehouse I work at is at an hour driving distance and with me not knowing the roads yet it is impossible to get back without my phone. Well, the GPS on my phone. The guys I worked the shift with just so happened to live in different directions. My luck wasn't looking too good, even more when I started thinking I had the next two days off. I even considered crashing on one of the chairs in our break room until Monday. Why Monday you might be asking. Cause around this area there are no busses on Sunday, which meant I would have to wait until the next working day to be able to leave with the public transport. Moments like this make me wish this workplace would cover the transport. 

My colleagues saw me frantically walking around and suggested I take the phone we found a week before and placed in the lost and found box. No one came to pick it up and we started debating whether someone actually will. I agreed and reluctantly took it. It seemed like an old model, some unknown small brand I haven't heard of. But it was unlocked and had a GPS map I could use to get back to my home. 

I was driving home when it started raining so hard I could barely see the hood of my car. Around 1:40AM the traffic completely stopped because of a wreck on the highway. The cars had to get towed. I was too far back to see the site of event. Thanks to the guy in front of me that came out in this rain to tell me what happened. I was exhausted. I couldn't wait to get home. Then, the phone lit up with a notification from the map app. A new route was available that will get me to the destination with a delay of 35 minutes. I accepted without much thinning. 

The app told me to turn around and take the next exit. I remembered hearing my colleagues speak about this road once. The road went past an old lumber warehouse and then comes to halt near a ditch. A dead end. But I was so tired I didn't care, I thought maybe they opened a dirt road or something. 

I took the turn.

For the first few minutes nothing unusual happened. It was just a normal dark road. My headlights were barely cutting through the rain and the trees on the sides seemed to grow thicker while the road was narrowing. After about 5km I realized something was wrong. I should have hit the dead end a long time ago. Then, the rain just stopped. Nor turning into a drizzle or slowing down but instantly off. The sky wasn't as dark anymore. It became a sickly gray color that did not resemble clouds, kinda like the type of blank gray you would see on an old TV when it is turned off. 

Suddenly my phone speaker cracked and the GPS voice came on. 

"In 100 meters roll up your windows and lock all doors."

A cold sweat broke out on my neck. I thought it was some weird glitch but then I heard a noise outside.  Loud thuds of something heavy could be heard dragging across the roof of my car.

A minute later the voice came back.

"In 200 meters turn off headlights. Do not illuminate the road."

I was starting to panic I didn't want to turn my lights off but what choice did I have? Whatever was outside was worse than a bit of darkness. I reached and switched off all headlights. Driving blindly in a grey glow sky was terrifying. Out of the corner of my eye I could see movement. Dark shadows were moving across the road. They were way too tall to be ones of an animal and their legs bent backwards when they walked. They didn't make a sound and neither did I want to . We were all in silence, the only thing I could hear was the sound of the engine. I gripped the steering wheel, praying I wouldn't hit one.

"In 500 meters do not look at the pedestrian on the left."

I tried to look straight ahead, I swear I did. My curiosity took over me and I glanced as I drove past. Standing in the dirt by the side of the road was what I assumed to be a guy in a blue rain jacket. When the light hit his face I could swear it was me. The guy had my face. I was looking at myself. Before I could fully process the GPS made its normal chiming sound.

"You have arrived at the destination."

Instantly it was pitch black outside. I couldn't be happier to be home. The deafening sound of heavy rain slammed into my windshield was here once again. The streetlights of my own neighborhood were shining though the glass as I pulled into the driveway.

I sat there shaking for a second thinking about what happened so far. I convinced myself it was the sleep I've been having, or not having to be precise, these days. My brain was just playing tricks on me because I was tired. Nothing will fix this except for a good 10 hour sleep I though.

I grabbed my backpack and ran through the rain to the front door. I reached to grab the door handle, but my hand hit the flat wood. The handle was on the left side. Weird. I could clearly remember it being on the left, even the hinges were backwards. I opened the door and stepped inside. Everything in the house seemed flipped. What was I in, an episode of the Truman Show? The stairs went up to the left instead of right. The coat rack was bolted to the wrong wall. The rooms were all flipped. 

My wife walked out of the kitchen. She was holding a mug of tea with the biggest smile on.

"Rough drive, honey?"

Her voice was too deep. It sounded almost like the voice of the GPS. I just nodded silently and walked past her. I locked myself in the downstairs bathroom. I am siting on the tiles now. Before I had looked at the shampoo bottles on the edge of the tub. All words were printed backwards. She had started knocking on my door and soon she will figure out that I am hiding.

I tried to dial 911 but there doesn't seem to be signal for calls to go through. The map app also would not close. It keeps saying I have reached the destination but the blue line is pointing straight out of the bathroom and the ETA is frozen.
