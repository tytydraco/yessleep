This story happened a few years ago when I was 19 years old, I live in a rural village in Transylvania with my parents, we live in a farming community where we raise cattle for milk, our small village has a long history and in that long history there are paranormal events, we once had a shaman I used to go sing carols to for candy that lived with lizards and snakes or two old identical sisters that lived on top of a hill that never married instead choosing to live with twelve dogs who would howl every night keeping me up. This story begins in autumn when me and my dad went into the forest near our house to gather wood for winter, during that time I kept seeing something out of the corner or my eye, making me feel uneasy, looking at my dad I can tell he was feeling uneasy as well, looking around himself every minute, after a while we gather the wood and leave, a few weeks later one our hearding dogs appears missing chain and all, when I saw this I knew one of my dumber cousin was the culprit who wanted to prank me by stealing my dog (I know dumb rural pranks).  I call him to ask where the hell my dog was, sleepily he answers with a joke “Out collecting wood” then I knew, he tied my dog near the forest. I hang up murmuring some insults at him under my breath before heading towards the forest, as I got closer I started hearing my dog barking, but not a bark he would use on a cow running away from the heard, he used a stranger is close type of bark. Once I go up the hill I see him tied to a tree at the forest entrance, I go cut him loose and when I do he goes full on running back to my house when usually this dog sticks to my side even when we get chased by a heifer with anger issues,before following him I take a look into the now darkening forest and feel a cold wind run down my spine my instincts telling me turn around and run faster then your dog, I compose myself and walk away steadily back home, after I had dinner with my family I went to my bedroom, my bedrooms window was left half cracked so that air circulated during the night, after 2 hours of watching a movie I decided to get in bed, after a few minutes of rolling around unable to sleep I start hearing something outside my window. My grandma used to tell me these folk stories about cryptids that exist in this world, about how bad people who die could come back from the dead as “Stirigoi” a type of vampires that feed on the fear, life force and even blood of their loved ones or the “Pricolici”, the soul of a very bad man that was so evil the devil made him turn into a werewolf to stalk and kill people or livestock. What I began to hear outside my window reminded me of a dying smokers death rattle on their death bed, a slow growning that made me freeze in place, my room feeling colder by the second, it lasted for 15 to 20 seconds before my body pumped with adrenaline jumped out of bed to the window closing it in a swift move through the curtains, I never saw what was outside my window and thankfully I never have since , might be because the following day a priest came to visit my grandma and she asked him to bless the house with holy water because as she would say “you can t be in the good Lords hands tight enough”. Now I’m away from that small village in a big city getting my masters degree and still wondering sometimes, what the hell was outside my window that night.
