I still don't understand what happened, but I'll try to explain. 

It started at my brother's wedding. It was like fourteen hours away from where I lived, but it was my brother's wedding, so I had to go. I stopped as few times as I could, but inevitably I had to stop a few times. 

The first few times nothing major happened, I either stopped for food or because I had to piss. But either the fifth or sixth time I stopped, I got an uneasy feeling. I had to stop at a dingy gas station with a motel attached at about 2:30 A.M. I didn’t want to sleep there, but it was either that or I slept in my old car with no AC. So I filled up on gas, locked up my car, and went inside the gas station to get a few things. The cashier was outside on a smoke break when I walked in.

“Carter?”

Hearing my name in the middle of nowhere made me drop my bag of Doritos. I turned around, picking up the bag, to see who was behind me.

It took me a second in my sleep deprived state to recognize who it was, but when I finally did, my face lit up.

“Mr. Weller!”

Mr. Weller was my high school Geography teacher.

“Oh please, call me Nick for Christ's sake, Carter.” He chuckled quietly.

“So what the hell are you doing out in the middle of nowhere, man?” I yawned, the sleep deprivation finally catching up to me.

“Oh, I’m on my way to my brother's funeral. He… passed away, or at least that’s what we think, a few months back. He went missing about three years ago, sometime during October of 2022. All that we found was a bowl of blue candy in his room.” He looked at the ground while he said this.

Something I can’t help but notice is the dates. I know it’s just coincidence, but the fact that his brother went missing in October of 2022 uncannily lines up with the month my brother got engaged. But I only noticed this in hindsight.

“Oh shit, I’m sorry for your loss man.” I said, trying my best to not yawn again.

He looked back up at me.

“Oh I’m sorry, you must be going somewhere if you’re out here. Don’t let me hold you up Carter.” His voice sounded detached from what he was saying, but we were both probably exhausted so it made sense.

We would exchange a few more words before we went our separate ways.



Not much else to say about that night, the motel was dusty and the bed was like lying on a bunch of erect dicks. What happened the next morning is what bothered me.  I woke up at around 6:30 and nothing was off at first. I grabbed my phone, threw on music while I showered, and grabbed my keys. But that wasn’t the only thing on my bedside dresser. There was also a small dish of blue candy covered in what looked like citric acid right beside my keys. I didn’t lock the door at night so it wouldn’t have been impossible for Nick to sneak in and place this here, but it really wasn’t like him to do that. Or maybe room service could’ve put this here. That was more likely. I reached into my bag to see if I had any baggies, I did, and thought about putting the candies in. I stood there for a few minutes before deciding not to bring any, I was only about an hour away from the venue and they probably had similar sweets, just without the sour powder. I have always hated sour candy.



I got in my car after grabbing a coffee from the convenience store. Nothing special happened on the drive there, just trees and more trees. Eventually, I pulled up to the venue. This almost perfectly circular clearing surrounded by a lush conifer forest. I assumed it had been cleared for the venue, but whoever did it had left almost no irregular edges. 

There were only a few people there, unsurprising considering the time. I got out of the car and stretched my legs, attempting to wake one of my legs up as quickly as I could.

“Hey Carter!”

I looked up, still stretching. My brother, Quinn, was walking up to me.

“Quinn! Fuck, it’s been a while, man.” I said as I pulled him into a hug.

We talked for about ten minutes before I walked up to one of the tables with snacks on it. I scanned my eyes across the table. It had cheese, caprese salad, chocolate fondue, and a bowl of blue candies… covered in a sour-looking powder. I blinked. I almost didn't process it. But I couldn’t ignore it.

“Hey, Quinn?” My voice sounded shakier than I wanted it to.

My brother walked over, confused.

“Yeah, what’s up?”

I looked at the candy for another second.

“Who brought the candy?”

Quinn looked at the candies and raised an eyebrow.

“I… I dunno. Maybe mom or dad.” His voice sounded just as confused as it sounded indifferent.



Hours passed as we waited for more people to arrive. As the last group of people arrived, I made a subtle headcount.

Around thirty people.

I looked at my brother. One of the other groomsmen was helping him with his tie. 

I checked the time. 3:15 PM, the wedding started at 3:30.

I made my way over to Quinn.

“You ready to be a dad and her daddy?” My voice cracked in the middle of the sentence.

Quinn laughed and slapped the back of my head.

“Fuck you, Carter.” He said, trying to slow his laughter down.



As I stood beside the aisle, watching my brother, I noticed someone take one of the blue candies. My stomach tightened, but I couldn’t quite explain why. I focused back on my brother, but I couldn’t take my mind off the candy. I turned my attention to my sister-in-law, Noelle, as she began walking down the aisle and noticed in the corner of my eye the person who took the candy go into a porta-potty. 



The ceremony was the one time I was able to take my mind off it. I never cry much, but this made me shed a few tears. No shame about it.

Nobody had come back out.

I hugged my brother and his wife before walking over to the porta-potty.

“Hey, you doing alright in there?”

“No… fuck… listen man, could you get me to the hospital? I don’t have anyone to take me and- fuck! Sorry. Just, please man.”

“Uh, yeah. Unlock the door and I can help you out. I’ll let Quinn and Noelle know that I’ll be back as soon as possible and then I can drive you.” I tried to keep my voice calm as I said it.

The person in the stall didn’t respond, but they slowly unlocked the door.

I immediately grabbed the door and opened it.

There was nobody inside.

I stumbled back, as if I saw some kind of monster.

I frantically looked behind me. More people were bent over in their seats, groaning. All the while more people grabbed blue candy.

I ran over to the table, trying to wipe dirt off my pants.

“Hey! Don’t eat that candy, please.” My voice was frantic.

“Carter? The fuck are you doing telling people what they can’t eat?”

“Oh my god what the fuck?!”
