In all my years, I was never one to have vivid dreams, mostly, I dreamed nothing, and if I dreamed something, then they would be a distorted memory of something that happened to me the day or the week or the month before, and I never got nightmares, not even as a kid, and most importantly, I never, ever, felt any kind of feelings while dreaming, it always happened as if I was watching a movie outside myself. Which is why whatever it was that my brain conjured the night before seemed so important.

I don't know exactly why, but the \*me\* of the dream was not me, looked nothing like me, yet knew it was myself, because both looked through her eyes and was also an incorporeal spirit outside her body which gave me the ability to see everything. 

I was surrounded by guards that vaguely resembled people, they were made of shadows, but their figures also tended to shift forms from time to time, to children with tentacles for heads, to monoliths that possessed burning yellow eyes, to soldiers back again. They were both male and female, even if they did not look anything like it, but that knowledge seemed intuitive to the me in the dream.

 Although was not in chains or retained in any way, I knew it to my core that I was trapped without escape. We were marching through a field of flowers on a cloudless day, and everything that was not my captors seemed to have this desaturated filter over it. It was daytime, yet there was no sun.

 When we got to the center of all, one of them had me kneel in the colorless grass and the flowers bloomed in reverse, being swallowed by the dirt, leaving nothing but spiky gray for me to sit on, and I couldn't help but lament that there were no softness to cushion my knees that were now getting stabbed like a billion needles. I thought that it must be part of the punishment, or ritual, but then it occured to me that these beings felt no pain or discomfort, so any sensation that I experienced must be entirely accidental and incomprehensible to them.

The one that had me kneel touched my shoulder, very close to my neck, and all the hairs in my body reacted to its ghostly touch. I felt nothing, I felt everything. Like pressure that was not there. I looked at it in the eye, even when I knew it was not allowed. I became it, my punisher, just in time to slit my own throat, and to see my own vessel fall to the  ground, as sheets of bright intense red escaped from it.

Having become one of the shadow people, their reactions were no longer a mystery. They all had been widely smiling all this time, jet black teeth in jet black gums in jet black lips in jet black skin, they were still shapeless, but now having their eyes I could recognize the difference of gradient in their monochrome color palette.

They had been mocking me, all this time, playing a game only they could understand or find amusing, showing me how to escape by shifting my form just like they would, fully knowing that was not an ability possessed. 

One of them grabbed the red cloth that had been produced with my death, and then fancied itself a dress. They spoke no words, but the knowledge vibrated into my boneless skull. 

It asked me, was it pretty? 

And I, now an impostor possessing one of their own, had to swallow my fury and my anger and my distress, so my own form would not distort and give my own nature away. I answered that yes, that it was beautiful. even if all wanted to do was rip it off of its skin.

It was my blood. It was mine. 

That thing was wearing me, and all I could do was praise it.
