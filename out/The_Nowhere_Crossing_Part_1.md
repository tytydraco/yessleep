Please forgive me, if there are any spelling mistakes, english isn't my first language. 

I live in one of the major Cities in Germany. 

I spent my whole life here an basixally know the City like the back of my pocket. I went to school and spent my whole childhood in one of the nicer parts of town, explored the more sketchy neighboorhoods during my teens. 

After my graduation i moved out of my parents appartement and moved into my own. It's only one room with a small kitchen and an even smaller bathroom on the 7th floor of the building with no elevator, but it's affordable. 

I work at a gas station and mostly take the late evening shifts because i don't like getting up early. 

It may not be the most glamorous job out there, but it pays the bills and the walk home is only 15 minutes.

I allways liked the walks back home at night. Especially in the summer. It's not as warm as during the day and there are much less people on the sidewalks.

At least until now. I had the closing shift, as usual. My colleagues already left, so i locked up and went on myy walk home. It has been a rainy, chilly week. A nice change from the heatwave that plagued us the weeks before. I enjoyed the cool night air as i noted something odd. I've just reached a crossroad, waiting for the traffic light to turn green and noticed how silent it has gotten. Usually, this shouldn't be weird. I know how quiet the City can get at night, but right there it was absolutely silent. I don't know why, but it gave me the creeps. I looked around and noticed how empty the streets were. No cars, no people, nothing. This crossroad is basically in the middle of the City, it's beating heart and yet it was void of any life. Not even a breeze. I looked at the buildings around me, looking for anything, even a single light would do, but there was nothing. At this point, i was freaked out enough to just ignore the red light and run across the street. But as i looked forward, i noticed that even the light of the traffic lights were gone. In their stead, only gaping blackness stared back. 

It felt like the world around me was slowly swallowed by nothingness like an endless abyss opening up.

I started sprinting across the street, along the sidewalk, not looking back.

I ran like a madman and just turned a corner as i bumped into someone. An older man, propably in his 40s. We both fell over and i felt my breath getting knocked out of me. 

After a moment, he slowly got up, cursing at me.

I've never been happier to be yelled at. 

I apologized and got up. 

As i continued my walk home, i asked myself if i just imagined all that. By the time i reached my appartement, i convinced myself that i was just tired and must have overreacted because of that. 

The days went by and i almost forgot the experience. Until tonight, it happened again. 

I reached the same crossroad, waiting for the traffic light and noticed this emptiness. This Void, closing around me. I didn't wait this time and immediatly sprinted back home, feeling the darkness creeping up behind me. 

I don't know what's going on and don't know what i should do. 

So please, if anyone has any ideas, please let me know...
