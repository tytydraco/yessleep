I don't know if this is just my brain messing with me or something else, but for the past 2-3 days I've been having almost the same nightmare repeatedly. The scary part is... it always starts normal. In the dream, I'm doing something random, like everyday stuff. Nothing creepy. The environment feels completely normal, like a regular dream.

But then suddenly... out of nowhere... it appears. It's not like it slowly enters the scene. It just spawns in the same place, like it was waiting there the whole time. I've never seen it standing. It's always sitting in a squatting position. That posture is burned into my mind because it looks unnatural and disturbing in that moment. The thing doesn't even have a proper face. It's like a black smoky body, almost like a shadow made of smoke. But it still feels like it has a shape.

Like a genie or some kind of dark mist that formed into a creature. The weirdest part is its eyes. Its eyes are glowing white, but not in a human way. There's no real face, just two glowing eye shapes, like white venom-like glowing eyes in the middle of darkness. Its hands are white too, but they don't look fully physical. They look like white smoke shaped into hands. Like they exist... but not completely.

When it's squatting, its hands aren't placed normally. It feels like the hand-structure is touching its leg, almost like it's resting or waiting. And the moment I look at it... It attacks. Every single time. It comes at me fast and grabs my neck. It starts choking me and pressing harder and harder. And that's when the worst part happens. I can feel the choking even after the dream starts fading. It feels so real that I can't move, I can't scream, I can't even properly breathe. That's when I realize I'm in sleep paralysis. It feels like the dream ghost killed me, and my body is stuck between the dream and reality. I can't move at all, and I can feel pressure around my neck and chest. Then I wake up suddenly. But today something even more disturbing happened. I had a dream... inside another dream. In that dream, I was playing with a bunch of white eagle babies. It was such a random but peaceful dream. Then suddenly...

the same entity appeared again. Same glowing eyes, Same smoky black body, Same squatting position, And it attacked again. I woke up... but I was still dreaming. And the scariest part is that I knew it. I knew I was in a dream, and I knew I had sleep paralysis in that dream. It felt like I was trapped in a loop. After a few seconds, I finally woke up for real. I don't know what this thing is. I don't even believe in ghosts like that, but the fact that it keeps repeating in the exact same way is terrifying.
