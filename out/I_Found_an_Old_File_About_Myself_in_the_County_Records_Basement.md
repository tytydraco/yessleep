I've worked as a records clerk for the county municipal office for 3 years. It's a small county, the kind of place where the building itself hasn't been updated since the 1970s and half the staff still keeps handwritten ledgers because the digital system crashes if you look at it wrong. Most of the job is routine. Property deeds, tax records, old permit applications nobody will ever look at again. You stop expecting anything strange after the first year. Then last month I went down to the basement to pull an unindexed box, and I found a file with my own name on it.

The basement holds everything that was never properly logged into any system, digital or otherwise. Boxes going back decades, some older than the building's last renovation. My supervisor sends someone down every few months to sort through what's left. That day it was my turn.

Box 41 had no label, just a number scrawled in marker on the side. Inside were maybe 30 folders, mostly what I expected. Old zoning disputes. A stack of birth certificates from the 1980s. Then near the middle, a thin folder with a name on the tab. My name. Full name, spelled exactly the way it appears on my driver's license.

I want to be clear about something. This box had not been opened in years. The dust on the lid was undisturbed. The tape sealing it was original, brittle, the kind that cracks instead of peels when you touch it. There is no way anyone recently placed anything inside. Whatever this was, it had been sitting in that basement long before I found it.

The folder itself was old. The paper had that soft yellow brittleness you only see in documents from decades back. No staples, just a single string tie holding it shut, the kind clerks stopped using before I was even born.

Inside was a single typed page. No letterhead, no county seal, no case number. It read like a personal profile. My date of birth. My address, correct even though I've moved twice since childhood, each old address crossed out in pen and the new one written beside it in a different ink, like someone had been updating it over time by hand. At the bottom there was a line that just said "status: active" and under that, in the same handwriting that updated my addresses, a date. Last Thursday.

I checked the date against my own memory. Last Thursday was an ordinary day. I went to work. I came home. Nothing happened that I could point to as significant. But someone, using a pen and paper, in a sealed box that hadn't been opened in years, in a basement in a county most people drive through without stopping, had written that date next to my name four days before I found it.

I brought it to my supervisor, a woman named Carol who has worked at that office for almost 20 years. She looked at the page for maybe two seconds before she told me to put it back in the box, seal it again, and never mention which box it came from. She said this the way you'd warn someone off a live wire, not scared, just certain. I asked her if this had happened before. She didn't answer. She just repeated, put it back.

I didn't put it back. I kept the page and left the empty folder in the box.

Three nights ago I found a second line had appeared under the first, in the same handwriting, on the page sitting in my own drawer at home. A new date. Yesterday.

I haven't gone back to work since. I don't know who is writing in that file, or how they're writing in a page that never leaves my apartment, but I know it hasn't stopped. Tonight the page is still blank under yesterday's date. I keep checking it every hour, telling myself the next line won't come. I don't think I believe that anymore.
