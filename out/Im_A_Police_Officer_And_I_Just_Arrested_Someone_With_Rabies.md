I'm unsure if I'm even allowed to speak about this publicly, but I'm worried about the future of humanity. I may loose my job, or even get arrested, but you people need to know. Rabies, you all (I assume at least) are familiar with it. It's a super deadly viral disease that spreads through animal bites and their saliva. It goes on to attack your central nervous system. Early signs are headaches, pain where the bite was, nothing really out of the ordinary for just having been bit by a wild animal.

But then your symptoms ramp up. You become more agitated, you become feral, you froth from the mouth, and most famously, you develop a fear of water. I've heard people often compare it to turning into a real life zombie, and that statement has never been more true. For as long as we've known it exists, rabies has always been 100% fatal with no cure or vaccine. Once you get past the early symptoms, you may as well dig your own grave. Now I know you're getting bored at my lecture, and typing this is making me feel boring, but there is a point to it.

Last night, I was out on patrol. I get called to a street that I frequent quite a bit, as it's known for it's insane amounts of overdoses and other activity that requires police presence. The call is for a group of three people who are being overly violent and are possibly overdosing due to frothing at the mouth and other various symptoms. *No biggie*, I thought, as I sped over with my sirens on. I had delt with these kind of people many times before. It's not that hard especially when you have someone else with you. Due to the fact that I might get in trouble for this, I'm just gonna call my buddy 'Q'. Me and Q met at the scene and started searching for the group.

Q and I had met in police academy, roughly about 10 years ago. We bonded pretty quickly, and he even set me up with my now wife. We'd go out drinking together, play golf, y'know, guy stuff. I don't want to go insanely into detail to avoid making him identifiable, but know that we were close.

We said some banter that I don't exactly remember, and then we split off. I remember thinking that this was gonna be easy, we're just looking for some cracked up meth-heads that couldn't have stumbled far. I still remember the harrowing call I got from Q over the radio. I heard the radio click on, and then off again, like he was struggling to hold down the button. My heart skipped a beat and I ran back hurriedly to our squads, looking around and hoping that my panic would subside as he strolled back into view, but he didnt.  
  
I yelled out for him, and then felt stupid when I realized that I have a radio. I clicked the button on the side.  
  
"Q? Can you hear me?" I let go.  
  
Nothing, except the occasional click, like he was trying to talk. I start running down the path he took, searching my surroundings with my flashlight. I took a second to stop, running so far and so fast in 80 pound gear isn't very fun or easy. I took this second to call in for backup. And as soon as I let go I will never forget what came through on Q's radio.  
  
I could hear as he screamed and begged for help, then the tearing of flesh and cracking of bones. I could hear joints come out of sockets with wet pops, and the squishing of something that almost made me puke. He managed to squeak out three words before his radio clicked off.

  
"They're eating me."

  
I felt dizzy, and like my whole world was crumbling down upon me. I tried my best to shake it off and I kept running in Q's direction. I'm going to do my best to describe the scene in as much detail as possible without being too gruesome. But you guys need to know what's happening here, and how serious this is.

Q's vest was taken off and thrown to the side, his shirt was torn and gave way to his ribs, cracked open and split. It was like his ribs were giant double doors and someone opened them. His jaw was completely broken and hanging off, barely being held on by a tendon. His arms looked like zig zags, his fingers were missing entirely. One of his eyes had been plucked out and squished, what remained laid next to my foot.

I was so shocked and out of it at what I was seeing that I almost didn't notice another detail. His legs had been picked almost entirely clean of the skin and fat that was on them. I hope you never ever have to see what it looks like for a leg to have no skin, no fat, and barely any muscle left. I hope you never have to see any of this in general, I have not slept since this night.

Before I backed away, I saw one of them. It was hiding behind a building, and peeking around the corner. Reviewing my bodycam later revealed he was there the whole time, watching me while I stood in shock. After I snapped out of it and took a few steps back, I raised my gun and aimed it at the 'person', light beaming off their eyes. I clicked the radio and yelled at dispatch. Something about telling backup to get their asses over here, I don't remember exactly what I said.

"COME OUT BEHIND THE BUILDING AND GET ON THE FUCKING GROUND!" I yelled, and some part of me knew it wouldn't listen.

Like a scolded child, it fled behind the corner and made a whimpering noise. I swung the corner wide, about 10 feet away. And in that short distance, the thing got on all fours, and closed it within 3 seconds. It lunged at me and let out a hideous high pitched growl. I managed to wrangle my gun underneath it's chin and blow the fuckers brains out.

I sat up, pushing the corpse off of me. I stared at Q's body, and began to silently weep. Backup showed up shortly after, and from there my memory is a bit foggy. Some of the backup when to search for the other two, and the rest comforted me while I sobbed uncontrollably on the sidewalk. What led me to make this post is a couple things. One, imagine my surprise when the day after, the fucking FBI show up and tell me to make a vow of silence. Two, my struggle with the thing didn't go as clean as I originally claimed. It managed to bite my wrist before I was able to kill it. And three, this isn't normal rabies, if you couldn't tell by now.

Rabies makes people aggressive, but not like that. The government is turning this into a giant operation that they want to keep 'hush hush' so that the public doesn't freak out or whatever. I don't trust them. Now the main reason;

They never found the other two things. They roam freely, and could be in another state by now. It was fast, so I don't doubt the others are too. This post is a warning, if you ever see someone that you assume is just a drug addict, turn the other way.

You don't want to be wrong.
