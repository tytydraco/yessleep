My hands are still shaking so badly I can barely type, and I haven't even washed the blood off yet. I’m just sitting in my car down the road from my house because I'm terrified to go inside.

Me and four of my closest friends were hanging out at my friend Jake’s place earlier today, just chilling in the lounge, when someone knocked on the front door.

Jake went to check it out and found a package sitting on the mat. It was wrapped up neatly with no name on it, so he brought it inside and opened it on the coffee table.

Inside was a box with a single button in the middle, with a card resting right on top of it. Jake picked up the card and read it aloud:

"You have 10 hours... press the button and one in your friend group will die today. Leave it untouched, and every person in this room will die."

At first, we thought it was a prank. Just a really sick joke from someone we knew. Nobody wanted to be the idiot who pushed a random button, and nobody wanted to leave the room just in case it wasn't a joke and walking out triggered something. So we just stayed put.

After a couple of hours, though, the atmosphere shifted. Nobody had come forward to claim the prank. The silence started getting heavy, and everyone grew paranoid. None of us wanted to kill a friend, obviously, but none of us wanted to die either.

By hour six or seven, everyone was losing their minds. We started arguing about what to do, and it got ugly fast. People started throwing out names of who should take the hit based on stupid things we had done to one another that under normal circumstances would never justify something like this. Just petty things from years ago, suddenly being used as a reason why someone deserved to die so the rest of us could walk away. Years of friendship went straight out the window.

Eventually, everyone burned out. Mary started crying, and we all realised how insane we were acting. We made a pact. We agreed we couldn't actually murder one of our own friends, and besides, it couldn't really be true, right? It had to be a bluff. So we decided to just let the clock run out together. The last ten minutes were pure torture. We were all just staring at the clock on the wall, waiting for the 10 hours to hit.

With just a few seconds left, Jake snapped.

He jumped up, shouting, "Fuck this and fuck all of you, I want to live and I won't take that chance!"

Before anyone could grab him, he lunged over the table and slammed his hand onto the button. He thought pressing it meant one of us would take the fall so he could walk out alive. Nothing happened to me. Nothing happened to anyone else.

Jake just froze. He clutched at his neck, his eyes bulging as realisation hit him, and a split second later, his head exploded, spraying warm blood, bone and gray matter on everything and everyone. 

We started screaming and all scrambled backward against the walls, completely losing our minds in shock and panic. Seconds later, right at the 10-hour mark, another card slid right under the front door.

One of my friends walked over, shaking so violently he could barely hold the paper, and read it aloud:

"...eventually. Now you get to feel alone, betrayed, and terrified every single day, just like you made me feel in high school."

The realisation hit us all at once. Every person in that room will die eventually. If Jake had just held out for a few more seconds, nobody would have died today. He tried to sacrifice one of us to save his own skin, and it backfired completely. Nobody said a word after that. We were sobbing, completely traumatised, stuck between grieving our friend and knowing he was willing to sacrifice one of us at the last second. Nobody could even look at each other after everything that happened. We ran out the front door to our cars and drove off in different directions.

I’ve been sitting in my car for an hour trying to figure out how the hell that happened, and it finally clicked. Last week, the five of us went to this new virtual reality place in town. We didn't even plan to go there, we just happened to get a free promo voucher for a group session. They had this whole setup where they put you under briefly to place a temporary chip in your neck for a better experience. They told us everything was removed before we woke up.

Except something else had been left behind. I keep thinking about the guy who set us all up in that room. The way he looked at us when we walked in together laughing, still close friends after all these years.

That's the worst part. Whoever did this to us, whoever watched us tear our friendship apart through the tiny camera lens I saw built into that button box, is someone we ruined back in high school. Someone we bullied so badly they spent years planning this, and we don't even remember them.
