This happened about three years ago when I was in college.

I was driving back to my apartment after visiting a friend who lived about forty minutes away. It was a little after midnight, and I stopped at a gas station because I was almost empty.

The place was pretty dead. There was one employee inside and maybe two other cars in the parking lot.

I filled up, went inside to grab a drink, and when I came back out, I noticed a dark SUV parked a few spots away from me.

I didn’t think anything of it.

I got back on the road and started heading home.

Maybe five minutes later, I noticed the same SUV behind me.

Again, nothing weird about that. There weren’t many roads you could take from that gas station.

But after another few minutes, I realized they were following pretty close.

I was doing about 55 and they were probably two car lengths behind me.

At first, I thought maybe they were pissed because I’d cut them off or something. I couldn’t remember doing it, but it was late and I was tired.

So I slowed down.

They slowed down too.

That was when I started paying attention.

There was a small residential area coming up, and instead of staying on the main road, I turned into it.

The SUV followed.

I made another turn.

They followed again.

At that point, I knew something was wrong.

I remember turning my music completely off.

I don’t know why. I guess I just wanted to concentrate.

I made a right onto a road that basically looped back around toward the highway. There was absolutely no reason for someone heading in the same direction as me to take it.

The SUV was still there.

I called my roommate and put him on speaker.

I told him what was happening, and he immediately told me not to drive home.

There was a 24-hour truck stop about fifteen minutes away, so I decided to go there.

The problem was that the fastest way there involved about ten minutes of pretty much nothing. Just woods and highway.

The SUV stayed behind me the entire time.

At one point, they got so close that I couldn’t even see their headlights anymore.

Then they backed off.

Then they got close again.

I was genuinely expecting them to hit me.

When I finally saw the truck stop sign, I sped up.

I pulled into the parking lot and parked directly in front of the entrance.

The SUV followed me into the lot.

That was the moment I really started panicking.

I didn’t get out of my car. I just sat there with the doors locked and my roommate still on the phone.

The SUV drove slowly past me.

There were two people inside.

I couldn’t really see the passenger, but the driver was a man. He turned his head and looked directly at me as they passed.

Then they drove around the building and left.

I waited almost twenty minutes before I moved.

My roommate convinced me to call the police, so I did.

I didn’t have a plate number. I could barely even describe the SUV beyond saying it was dark-colored, so obviously there wasn’t much they could do.

But the officer I spoke to told me something that made the whole situation feel worse.

Apparently, there had been a few reports in that general area of drivers getting bumped from behind late at night. When they pulled over to check the damage, the people in the other vehicle would get out and rob them.

He wasn’t saying that’s definitely what these people were doing.

Maybe it wasn’t.

But I kept thinking about how close that SUV had gotten to my bumper.

I stayed at the truck stop until almost two in the morning.

Eventually, my roommate drove out to meet me, and we went home together.

I never saw the SUV again.

The thing that bothers me is that I still don’t know when they first noticed me.

I always assumed it was when I left the gas station.

But every once in a while, I wonder if they were already sitting there when I arrived.

And if they were, I wonder how many people they watched before they decided to follow me.
