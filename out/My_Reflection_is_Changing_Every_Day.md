Part One

every morning, when I get ready for school (or just for the day if its the weekend) I wash my face in the sink, brush my teeth and hair, all whilst looking in the mirror. I have done this ever since I was in elementary as a basic morning routine. one day, I looked in the mirror and saw a zit. it wasn't big or alarming, just a normal pimple, I thought nothing of it until I felt my face trying to pop it. My skin was as smooth as it was the day before, I could feel no bumps or blemishes, as if the zit wasnt even there, "weird..." i thought to myself, I saw my mom walking in the hallway outside of the bathroom to get coffee. "Is this noticeable?" I asked pointing to my cheek where the zit was. "Is what noticeable sweety?" "The zit." I replied. "No, I can't see it so it must not be." she said before walking to the kitchen. Nobody at school mentioned it, not even my friends. I assumed I must've been tired that morning or everyone was just being overly nice to me since my birthday just passed durring spring break and it was the first day back. I simply walked home, did my chores, and ate dinner.

After dinner I had to go to bathroom, I looked in the mirror and saw two new pimples on my chin, the previous one looked larger and more irritated too, but again when I felt my face to feel them, it was smooth, no bumps or oil or anything. I once again asked my mom and she said she couldn't see anything "You've had mostly clear skin your whole life son," she said in a supportive tone while washing her dish, "a pimple here and there is completely normal for someone your age." "Dad wouldn't lie," I thought to myself "I'll ask him." I walked to the couch where he was sitting, "Dad, do I have acne on my face?" he turned the volume on the TV slightly down, grunted, then turned towards me adjusting his glasses "Hmm no" he grumbled tiredly, "looks fine to me." I walked away confused, but told myself acne isn't a big deal.

I took a shower and put on a new pair of clothes, I looked in the mirror once the fog from the shower faded and saw that the zit on my cheek was not only much larger but starting to leak oil, the skin around it now inflamed, the pimples on my chin now spreading to my neck, now red and irritated like a rash. My dad was passing out on the couch so I went to my mom, "Mom! It's getting worse, it's all over my face now!" My mom, now annoyed with my insistence, looked at me without a smile this time "Son, you're fine, your skin looks normal, you do not have acne, I do not know why you insist you do." She took a deep breath, "Maybe get some sleep, you have school tomorrow." I let out a quiet sigh and walked into my room, jumped into my bed, then fell asleep.

My alarm woke me up, I kind of forgotten everything about my skin that happened the day before as I got ready. my dad had to leave early for work and my mom was making the three of us breakfast. I got ready for the day and went out for breakfast. "Good morning mom and dad." "Morning son." my dad said going though the mail, "Good morning honey" my mom said, "I made you some scrambled eggs, and there's potatoes on the stove." "Smells delicious ma" I said, we made some small talk while we ate, they asked about school, I told them it was alright, so on so forth. After I ate and washed my plate I went to the bathroom to do my morning routine, I looked in the mirror and saw that my forehead was oily and also had a few pimples along the now receding greasy hairline, and the rash on my neck was spreading across the left side of my jaw. the zit on my cheek was the largest its been yet, practically popping itself. the oil leaking from it was now red with blood and pus. I freaked out and felt my face, placing my hands around my neck and cheeks. smooth. completely normal. I felt my forehead and hairline, it wasn't oily whatsoever, my hair was completely fine, not receding or greasy. This is when I knew with 100% certainty that my reflection did not match how I actually looked, and I cannot trust it. I didn't ask my parents or anyone at school about it, and nobody brought it up.

When I went home I didn't even want to look in the mirror. I felt awful because even though I knew it was false it was the only way I could see myself, and it was getting worse. At dinner my dad noticed I was quiet, "What's the matter son?"

"It's nothing dad."

"You know you can tell me anything, right?"

"Thanks dad, but it really is fine, I just, just had a bad day at school" I had to think of a lie because he would think I was crazy if I brought up the reflection, but at the same time maybe I am because my face feels seems to look normal to others. I had a feeling he was about to ask what happened but felt that it wasn't necessary, probably chalked it up to "highschool boy stuff." Curiosity eventually got the best of me and I looked in the mirror. the rash spread a little bit and looked a little more irritated, the zit on my cheek grew a second head, my hairline receded a little further, surprisingly nothing too crazy this time. I went to bed extremely unhappy.

Part Two

The next morning I decided to run tests;

Is it just this mirror or is it other mirrors too? Yes it's every mirror or reflective surface.

If someone else is present, do they also see it the way I do or do I still look normal? I tested this with my friend Dylan in the school bathroom mirror, and he said I look fine, then called me crazy for asking. shocker.

Does it show on camera or just reflections? That's the weird part... it works on camera until I take a picture, then I look completely normal. Likewise, when I'm recording a video I look grotesque while recording but as soon as I stop recording and replay the video, I look normal, I'm not sure what this is or what the rules of it are.

By the time I came home, I looked in my mirror and what I saw shook me to my core. My hairline was pushed so far back I was pretty much balding. The zit on my cheek has turned into a visceral infection, oozing pus and blood down my cheek and dripping down my neck, which, of course, I couldn't see or feel, it was only in the reflection. My skin was almost completely covered in either acne or the rash from my neck which now covered the whole left side of my jaw and a good bit of my cheek, now bleeding and extremely inflamed. Black heads covered my entire nose and most of my forehead, both of which practically dripping oil. what was left of my hair was pushed in chaotic directions due to my now unpredictable hairline, patchy and uneven with slick grease and dandruff making it look like an unkempt rats' nest. My swollen face forcing my lips, dry and cracked, to pucker. But of course, the moment I felt my face, I was completely normal. my healthy head of hair was clean and dry, my skin and pores were in pristine condition, and my neck had no rash. I had to hold in my cries, telling myself over and over again that I'm fine and that thing in the mirror isn't actually me, but it was taking a toll on my mental health, I wanted to see myself the way others did, but I couldn't, I could only see that disgusting vile creature.

I was once again quiet durring dinner, my dad didn't ask me this time knowing I would probably give him the same answer, which I can't blame him for. My mom though, she noticed it yesterday too, she sternly cleared her throat "What has been going on with you lately?" my dad didn’t bother to stop eating but my mother did and was now looking at me, tapping her nails on the table waiting for an answer. "n-nothing mom" I said then choked on my words knowing that was the wrong answer, "I'm fine." "Fine? Hon, you've barely touched your food and haven't talked at all yesterday or today." I paused trying to think of what to say that wouldn't upset her, "This is about you thinking you have acne isn't it?" she said before I could say anything "I don't know what has gotten into you, your skin is completely clear, there's kids your age, probably at your school, who would give anything to have skin like yours."

I sat in silence not knowing how to reply.

I went to bed earlier than usual that night. Almost slept in, finally got up after the third alarm on my phone went off, I went to the bathroom and got in my shower, avoiding the mirror like it wasn't even in there. I skipped my morning routine. that day at school was a blur, a filler day in all aspects, went from class to class hardly speaking a word to anyone. My friend Will noticed something was wrong durring lunch, I told him to meet me outside and told him everything, from when I first saw the zit on my cheek to how I see myself now. He was shocked because compared to most the guys my age at the school I had amazing skin, I'm just happy he believed me, he said he wished he could help but didn't know how, he hung out with me and the rest of our friend group for the rest of the school day. It got my mind off of how I saw myself, but when I went home I went straight to the mirror.

My left eye where the rash had spread was now red, I'd assume from bursting blood vessels. An oily sheen and blisters now covered the rash. On the right side of my face, what started as a simple zit was now a nasty boil the size of a quarter, the pus and blood now covering the right side of my jaw and leaving several streaks running down my neck and shirt. Every single pore on my nose was either a black head or inflamed cluster of pimples, my forehead was even worse, with mounds of pimples that formed underneath my forehead making it puff out unnaturally. My skin was a sickly olive color, with a thick layer of oil covering what little uninfected skin I had left. As if that wasn't bad enough, my lips were busted, oozing even more pus and blood down my face, revealing crooked teeth with caked on plaque barely holding onto discolored, swollen gums, I looked like a leper that crawled out of the grave, my ghastly appearance forcing me to regurgitate violently onto the mirror, covering my hideous reflection in my stomach contents.

My mother must of heard me, because she came rushing in the bathroom, "Honey are you alright?" I wiped the vomit off my mouth with my sleeve and bolted to the door, my mother blocked me, she looked at me with worried eyes, I flinched and covered myself, not because I thought she was going to hurt me, but because at that moment I was convinced I looked like my reflection, growing more sickly and infected every day. I began to cry, "don't look at me!" "It's okay honey, I'm here, everything is okay" she said in a gentle soothing voice, "your skin is pale, and you vomited, I'm worried about you" "d-do I.. do I really have clear skin?" I sniffled "yes honey, I tried to tell you, see? look" she took a picture of me, besides the pale skin, I looked completely normal, even though I was ugly crying in my mom's shoulder seconds earlier.

"Will texted me everything, about the mirror and how you look normal in photos, you've been in the bathroom for 15 minutes."

which was funny because I didn't think I was in there for that long.

"Your dad will come home soon, his shift was a little late today, he will drive you to a psychologist."

normally I would hate the idea of seeing a psychologist, but if there's any chance in hell that what's going on is in my head, and there's a way to make it stop, I'd take it in a heartbeat. It's been 2 months now, I look normal in the mirror now, thank fuck, but earlier today Dylan said "hey you got something on your cheek!" I felt it, smooth, then I looked in the mirror.
