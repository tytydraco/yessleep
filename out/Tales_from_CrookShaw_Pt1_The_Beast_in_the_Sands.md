I moved to the Arizona deserts of CrookShaw 6 months ago. I did so with my son and husband; we laughed and played in the sand, enjoyed the old-fashioned atmosphere CrookShaw held, and found ourselves to be the picturesque family I always wanted us to be.

Ever since we moved to this picturesque town, however, I've ignored weird sign after weird sign. First being the notebooks. Almost every older person I saw had one latched to their sides, large and leather-bound like an old tomb.

We had such happy times back then; it's terrifying to think of the ways this godforsaken town has twisted and pulled and torn into me. I remember our first day here; I remember the joy on his face when he saw his new room, painted blue.
