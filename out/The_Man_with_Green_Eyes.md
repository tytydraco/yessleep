Once per week, a man with green eyes stands outside my window. I don’t know what day it will happen. But without fail, one day during every week, he’s there. He stands motionless and looks into my room from out on the street. Those glowing eyes, shining brightly in the dark, pierce into my room as I’m sleeping. 

I’ve gotten used to it now, it might surprise you to read. I gave up trying to get rid of him a long time ago. I wouldn’t say I get easily scared. When he first arrived, I was more annoyed than anything. I thought he was some smartass, or a junkie, trying to screw with me one night. I very soon realised this wasn’t the case. 

The first night I saw him was a couple nights after I had broken up with Lyn. I still wasn’t feeling the best about it, as you can imagine, and I could hardly sleep. I thought I would handle all of that better but my mind was a mess. So, when I came back into my room after going out to grab a glass of water, I thought I was still just screwed up when I saw him. It was a man wearing a light grey suit, old fashioned, like from the 1940s or something, and an old light grey fedora. He was standing perfectly straight. He looked like one of those guys from a Renee Magritte painting. He was just watching me, in my room. When I first caught sight of him he was drowned in shadow, but I could make out his eyes. He had two beady green eyes, glowing deep and bright, breaking through the night sky. At first I thought they were weird gag glasses or something.

I kept looking out the window, and he didn’t move. I realised this was not my imagination. I got kind of angry. Who did this guy think he was? I slammed my glass down and went outside. 

When I stepped outside, he turned his head and looked at me. I shouted out at him.

“What are you doing mate?” “Excuse me! Who are you?”

He didn’t answer. My frustration grew. I stormed down my front walkway and met him on the street. He stayed standing in his place but followed me with those terrible eyes as I rushed up to him. 

“Mate, this isn’t funny, I’m trying to sleep.” I said when I reached him. 

He didn’t respond. I looked up at him. I saw he did not have glasses. Where his eyes should be there were two smooth green orbs, bright light bursting out from them. I was startled by this. He was a fairly short man, slightly shorter than me, maybe 5'7''. I noticed that he had a small polite smile. It was like a smile you would give to a work colleague who you didn’t know so well. He kept staring at me. 

“Alright, please leave, or I’m calling the police” I said to him sternly. 

Still he said nothing. I pushed him, pretty forcefully on the chest.

“Didn’t you hear me? Get out of here mate!” I was yelling now. 

He barely moved. Keeping his same calm smile, the man picked me up with ease and threw me backwards. I flew through the air and crashed onto the pavement. It must have been five metres or so back. I was bewildered and sat up. He was still standing there, with his stupid smile looking at me. I started to think maybe this wasn’t a man at all. He kept smiling. 

I rushed back into the house and called the cops. 

As the police car neared my house, I looked at him through my bedroom window. He kept staring straight at me, with that empty smile, and then vanished. He disappeared, suddenly and silently, as if he was never there. I couldn’t believe it. I had never seen anything like it in my life. When the police arrived he was gone. I rambled to them for a little while. They seemed slightly disgruntled at having to come all the way out for nothing. They left. 

This was the pattern for the next two times he appeared. He was there again the week after, and the week after that. I didn’t try to confront him physically those times. I called the police again both nights to try and catch him. But each time he vanished out of sight before anyone could see him.

Soon I started to grow afraid of him. There didn’t seem to be anything I could do. It bothered me, because apart from this I’m not really afraid of anything in my life. I tried filming him all night once, while he was standing there. But he didn’t show up in the footage. I’ve told friends and family about this man, about his disappearances even. And I reassure them I’m not crazy. Some of them have taken me seriously, some of them have laughed about it. But I’ve stopped trying to convince anybody now. I’m still stuck with him no matter what I try. 

And so here I am. It’s been years, three to be exact. I’ve stopped telling people about him. But I had to get it off my chest. I guess he’s become my secret. On random nights, never predictable, there he is. He’s out there on the street standing completely straight, staring into my room with those bright awful green eyes. 

I moved recently. It’s only on the other side of the city, but it’s far from my old neighbourhood. A few days went by, and I got a little relieved. I started to think maybe I had gotten rid of him. But last night I woke up in a sweat, I’ve had some stressful days at work. I turned to my right, looked out my window out of curiosity, and I saw two green lights, peering out of the darkness towards me.
