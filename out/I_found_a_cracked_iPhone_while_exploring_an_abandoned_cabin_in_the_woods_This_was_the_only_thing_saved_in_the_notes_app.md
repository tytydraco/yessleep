I do a lot of urban exploring, and yesterday I found a secluded, overgrown cottage deep in the woods. The front door was wide open. Inside, everything was covered in dust, but it looked like the previous owners had just vanished mid-meal. Wallets, car keys, and half-unpacked luggage were still scattered everywhere. While I was checking out the attic, I noticed a silver iPhone wedged deep between the floorboards. The screen was badly cracked and the battery was completely dead.

I took it home and plugged it in. There were no outgoing messages or calls, probably because there’s zero reception out there. But when I checked the notes app, I found a single draft titled "HELP" written a few weeks ago.

I don't know if this is an insane prank or if I stumbled onto a crime scene, but it gave me chills. This is exactly what was typed on the phone, typos and all:

**The Note :**

My hands are shaking so badly I can barely type this, but I need to get this out. I’m on my phone using the last bit of battery I have. If you are planning a trip anywhere secluded right now, just cancel it. Seriously. Just don’t go.

Five days ago, me and my friends, Mark, Sasha, Lucy, and Mina rented a cottage for a week-long vacation. Total isolation, no cell service, no emails. Classic cabin in the woods stuff. When we first pulled up, it honestly felt perfect. Mark killed the engine, laughing as he grabbed the cooler from the back. Sasha was stretching, Lucy and Mina were messing around arguing over who had to carry the heavy bags. They were just happy.

I grabbed my backpack and for some reason, I just got this awful, sudden chill. I looked past the cabin roof toward the tree line. She was just standing there. Deep in the shadows.

I completely froze. She was wearing this simple, pitch-black robe. Her skin was a sickening, pale gray color. But her head... man, it was messed up. She had long, tangled black hair, but it only grew on the back half of her skull. The front was completely bald and exposed. Her eyes were just pitch-black holes. But the worst part was her neck. It was impossibly, creepily long, tilting her head at this totally unnatural, broken angle.

The second I looked at her, a sharp headache slammed into my brain. I got this heavy wave of dread, and this weird whisper in my mind telling me I was just tired from the drive and losing it. I blinked, and she was gone. The trees were empty.

Lucy called out from the porch asking if I was coming inside. I forced a smile and followed them. God, I wish I hadn't.

Over the next two days, things started getting weird. At first, it was small. The hallway inside the cottage felt like it was stretching longer when you walked down it. The headaches got worse for everyone. Yesterday, we finally figured out her twisted rule: you can only see this thing from super far away, or very, very close. There is no middle ground. And god, she *plays* with you when she's close. Two nights ago, Mark was sitting on the couch when he suddenly went completely rigid. From the shadows behind the sofa, this long, pale gray hand with claw-like fingers slowly crept around his neck, gently brushing his cheek. He screamed and jumped up, and she vanished instantly. Then yesterday, Mina turned around in the dark kitchen and the thing was just *there*. Literally two inches from her nose, staring right into her eyes with those pitch-black voids for one horrific second before disappearing into thin air. Every time she does these split-second pop-ups, the headaches hit like a hammer, and you feel a piece of your sanity just rip away. She’s breaking us down.

Last night, Sasha went to the bathroom and didn't come back. We ended up breaking the door down. The room was totally empty, but the window was locked from the inside. We panicked and searched the whole place. When we went back to the living room, we looked out the front window. She was out there again. Same spot in the trees.

But her neck... it was even longer now. Sinuous and stretched out at a horrific angle. My head throbbed so hard my nose started bleeding. That's when I realized what she’s doing. She doesn't just kill you. She messes with your mind and warps reality to break your spirit, and then she *assimilates* you. She absorbs your mind to make herself stronger. Sasha didn't run away. She's inside that thing's head right now.

Right now, Mark, Lucy, and Mina are downstairs trying to barricade the doors. They’re crying and screaming at each other, totally losing it. Their noses are bleeding too. The house is completely falling apart, doors don't lead to the right rooms anymore.

I’m hiding in the attic. My head hurts so bad I can barely think straight. I just looked out the little window down at the trees. She's not there anymore. The spot is empty. She isn't far away. Which means she's close. She's very, very close. I can hear the attic stairs creaking. If anyone reads this, please
