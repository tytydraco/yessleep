This happened a few years ago, and I’ve gone back and forth about posting it because I honestly don’t know what to make of it. When I was younger, I had a best friend named Mason. We known each other since middle school and were basically inseparable for years. He was at my house constantly, knew my parents, and had been inside my bedroom hundreds of times. My parents trusted him enough that my mom eventually gave him a spare key to our house. He told her it was in case he ever needed to get something while we weren’t home. We were around 18 when we started drifting apart. Nothing dramatic happened. We just got jobs, doing our own things, and stopped hanging out as much. Fast forward a couple of years. I was cleaning out my childhood bedroom because I was moving, and I found an old phone in a drawer. It was an iPhone I’d stopped using years earlier. I charged it because I wanted to look through some old pictures. Most of it was normal stuff. Pictures of friends, random videos, screenshots, nothing interesting. Then I found a video that I genuinely don’t remember taking.

It was dated February 14. The video was about 20 seconds long. The phone was sitting somewhere in the corner of my bedroom, pointed toward my bed.

At first, I didn’t understand what I was looking at.

Then Mason walked into the room. He didn’t seem surprised to be there. He walked over to my desk, looked through a couple of things, and then sat on the edge of my bed. I was asleep.I watched the video again because I thought maybe it was some old joke we’d made. But I couldn’t remember it happening. I checked the date. February 14 was a Tuesday. I remembered that week because I was working late shifts and sleeping during the day.

I called Mason and casually asked if he remembered coming over around that time. He said no. I described the video without telling him everything. He laughed and said I was probably confusing him with someone else. I let it go for a couple of days.

Then I went back through the phone.

There were four more videos.

All filmed from different positions in my bedroom.

In one, Mason was sitting on the floor next to my bed. In another, he was standing in the doorway just watching me.

The third one was probably the worst.

He was going through the drawers in my desk. He pulled out an old photograph of me and my younger sister, looked at it for a few seconds, and put it back.

The last video was only about 10 seconds long.

Mason walked into the room, looked directly at the phone, and said something.

I turned the volume all the way up.

He said, “He’s never going to believe you.”

Then he picked up the phone and the recording ended.

I felt sick.

I called him again, but this time I told him exactly what I’d found.

There was a long silence.

Then he asked me how many videos there were.

That question bothered me more than anything else.

I asked him what he meant, and he just said he had to go.

He hasn’t spoken to me since.

I told my mom about everything a few days later. She looked genuinely confused at first, but then she remembered something.

Apparently, when I was about 15, Mason had asked her for a spare key to our house. He told her he sometimes came over to hang out when nobody was home.

She assumed he meant when I was there, too.

She gave him one.

She never asked for it back.

I ended up changing the locks.

I also went through my old pictures and found a few more things that bothered me. There were pictures of my bedroom taken from angles I didn’t recognize. I always assumed they were just random photos from when friends were over.

I haven’t talked to Mason in years.

The part I still can’t get over is that he wasn’t some random stranger breaking into my house. He was someone my family trusted. Someone who ate dinner with us, spent holidays with us, and knew where I slept.

I don’t know how long he was doing it.

And honestly, I don’t think I want to know.

One thing I still haven’t told my mom is that one of the videos was dated almost three months after Mason supposedly moved out of town.
