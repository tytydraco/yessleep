Being a prison safety inspector isn't a pretty job. I have the normal job of telling the cooks that "yes", gloves are mandatory, while also having to sweep through rooms for contraband. I have found Cocaine, Weapons, Sex toys. That stuff doesn't really seem to bother me, and for that reason I have been working around prisons for decades. It's good-paying money, due to how little people want to do the job. I have become well respected over the years. I assume that's why my higher ups contacted me for a special job. A one time visit to a new experimental prison outside the country. Why the prison couldn't get a local inspector is beside me. I just need to look around and report back if everything was "safe", and I would receive a pretty hefty paycheck. I can't discuss where it is (you'll see why soon), but when I flew out, I was told there would be an interpreter due to the language barrier.

I walk in, met by the usual friendly face, it's a woman with carrot top bangs and a big toothy smile. Even though we speak different languages, I can tell she's making a lot of effort to be polite. We spend a moment smiling at eachother before I look around for where the translator could be. Noticing my glances, she says something to me, then motions towards the door infront of us. When we walk through, all I can see is a ginormous hallway infront of us, and without skipping a beat, she starts power walking down the hallway, and I assume just to follow her.

As we walk together, I can feel myself getting alot warmer. I almost ask about it, then remind myself of the language barrier. I can hear a sound as well. It's faint but persistent, and I assume to blame it on this facility's clearly busted AC machine. I make a mental note to mention this in my report. Finally we make it to the end of the hallway, and she pushes through the double doors, where I am greeted by something entirely new. We're in another smaller better lit hallway, but this time having two glass walls on each side of us. This must be their cell layout. Bangs seems to have kept on walking, but stops when she sees me looking at the right cell.

It's nothing like I had ever seen before, the room is completely sterile of anything, even color, with white padding everywhere. The only thing in there is a slim man attached to a chain in the middle. I go up to the glass to get a better look. He's lying there, refusing to look at me. He has brown scraggly hair that dips into gray. I can't tell how exactly old he is, but he seems to be on the older side, due to the multiple fading tattoos (he seemed to be quite the wolf fanatic). I notice his clothes are particularly raggedy, resembling streetwear much more than a jumpsuit. Getting closer, I can also see a small food bowl on the floor, it's full, but seems to be far away enough were I'm not sure if the man can access it. I know culture is diffrent over seas, but this seemed disgustingly cruel. I have seen dogs being treated with more respect. Appalled, I start walking to the next set of doors, assuming correctly that my guide will follow me. I'm gonna have to make alot of changes around here. I made a mental note to stress the importance of humane cells. The noise seems to be getting louder with every door I open, but I still can't tell what exactly it is.

I instinctualy look at the next set of cells, but was met with a completely different view. Instead of a chain and food bowl on the ground, one of the walls in the cage not facing us had a huge hole in it. The hole was almost the size of the room itself, and coming out of the hole was railing that attached to the ceiling. I tried to angle myself to see down the hole but it seemed to go too deep for me to see. I wonder what was stopping him from just going into the wall hole. 

This time the prisoner was looking at me directly, his head tilted down, with tears streaming down his face. He seemed to be taking me in. It was weird to see a prisoner this close up, we must have been less than a foot apart, with the only thing separating us being the thick piece of glass. I learned early to not make direct eye contact with prisoners, you ruffle the least amount of feathers that way, but this guy seemed different. He also wasn't in a prison jumpsuit, instead a shirt with some sort of cheesy joke on it, some cackies, and some socks and sandals. He had blonde choppy hair, some scruff on his face, and was probably a little too chubby to have what's usually deemed a dad bod. I frowned when I looked down and saw a wedding ring. I made another mental note to mention how prisoners should also have normal prison jumpsuits for the safety of sta-

BANG

A CAR COMES RAILING OUT THE HOLE, AND HITS HIM STRAIGHT INTO THE OTHER WALL. 

I physically fall back and land on my ass. The man a foot away from me is now unrecognizable, his face bludgeoned in by the sheer force of the car, slamming him into the wall. I scream, more than I have ever screamed in my life. The car slowly retreats back down its hole, mechanically being moved back by the ceiling rail. I feel a warm hand on my shoulder, which I quickly slap off.

WHERE THE HELL AM I?!?

I get up and run to the nearest door. When I open it, it's just more cells. I run through door after door. I had gone the wrong way, but there's no turning back now, their must be an exit eventually. Every cage has a bizarre new thing, or item, or gimmick. The noise keeps getting louder, I finally hear it clearly. It's screaming, screaming from the people in cages ahead of me.

I must have gone through atleast a hundred doors, I'm out of breath. I look to the left of the room I needed to stop in, its a pitch-black room, the torture not so valiantly shown off like the rest of this facility. All I can hear is screaming coming from inside, but a different type of screaming? It has a sign up by the glass that I can't read because of the language barrier. I look to the right, and I see an older woman, shes staring at me, this time smiling. Before I know it, flame rises from above the ground in her cage, and I turn away before seeing another thing.

I go to therapy three times a week now. I have received many apologies and gift baskets from my bosses. All with notes, apologizing for not better preparing me for what I was signing up to see. Bastards are lucky if I don't sue, I'm actually looking into it with a buddy. Although, I like to believe I know the law as a civil servant, we're looking into the paperwork I signed before working this case, if there's any loophole in there that lawyers could exploit. It seems I have been blacklisted from inspecting even normal prisons, so I'm hoping the settlement money can take me through the end of my life. During this research, I found out more about that prison. The country I still can't say the name of (let's see if I can after I sue), is big into the idea of karma. So inspired by that, every "Prisoner" there is being punished, specifically for whatever crime they committed. An eye for an eye approach to punishment.
