Ever since I was a child I remember sleeping with a night light on all night. Even now I sleep with LED lights on that go around my whole room and there is a reason for this.

  
Back when I was 8 years old I used to not need a night light, I would turn off all the lights and just sleep peacefully. This all changed on the 7th of October. I still remember the date, I think I always will. 

  
I was in bed looking at the ceiling when I heard noises under my bed. I started sweating profusely, I just kept staring at the ceiling not moving a muscle as I heard scratching and something dragging itself on the laminated floor.

As I listened to this thing drag itself along the floor I could hear it breathing. It sounded exhausted as if it was tired and using all its strength to pull it self along. I shifted my eyes to the side to take a look at the floor and I saw its enormous fingers that were razor sharp. 

I started crying and the thing heard me and began wheeze laughing, which made me scream out in fear. It pulled itself all the way out from under my bed and slowly stood up. This thing was tall, like really tall. It had to bend its head down just so it didn't hit the ceiling.

It looked down at me with really beady eyes and a ear to ear grin. It kept breathing heavily and staring at me as it began to move closer to me. When it was a few inches away from my face i could smell its breath and i can't explain the smell except it smelled something like a dead fish that's been left out in the sun that's been covered in rotten eggs and spoilt milk. It was disgusting.

That's about the time I heard my father sprinting through the house, turning lights on and smashing through my door. As the light spilled in the thing began to scream and the only way i can describe the scream is it sounded like a denizin from the black ops 2 zombies map tranzit but higher in pitch.

My dad tried to attack the thing but when the light from the hallway spilled in the thing disappeared. My dad looked at me and i could tell he saw it too. Knowing that I wasn't going crazy and that someone else had seen this thing gave me a small amount of relief.

I slept in my parent's room that night and my dad kept the tv on so there would be light in the room. As soon as morning came he took me to the store and had me choose a strong night light. When we tried it that night it covered the whole room and i felt a little bit better knowing this thing couldn't get to me anymore. 

I fell asleep in a room that was barely dark and I found myself in the same position, staring at the ceiling and waiting for sleep to take me. 

That's when I heard that breathing again, it was under my bed again. The light didn't reach under my bed. This time I jumped out of my bed and turned my main light on. I could see it under the bed, that disgustingly long grin still on its face.

I ran to my dads room and told him what happened, he grabbed a flashlight and ran into my room, he shined it under my bed and the thing screamed again and disappeared. We bought a bunch more night lights and made sure every corner was covered in light.

I have seen the creature since then when I drive down a dark road, I'll see it out the window, it's too slow to catch up to me and I don't wanna know what could happen if it did catch me.

I now sleep in a room covered in LED lights and it looks like a tiktokers room but i as long as it keeps me away from that thing i don't mind. I keep flashlights in my night stand drawer just in case my power cuts out or something happens to my lights. I hope I never have to use the flashlights because I think the creature isn't too happy with me and it won't hesitate to attack first.
