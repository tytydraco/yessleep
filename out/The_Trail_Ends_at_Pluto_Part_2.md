**THE SUN**




My alarm went off at 2:15AM, I got dressed in my running shorts and t-shirt, grabbed my phone, and went downstairs. 


I followed my usual pre-run ritual; I ate a banana, drank a coffee, filled up my water bottles, packed a couple running gels, and went to the bathroom. Grabbed my headlamp, hydration vest, shoes and then drove to the trail. 


At 2:56AM I was standing beside the Sun sign. I’d passed this sign hundreds of times but this was the first time I stopped to read it. Every meter on the trail is equal to roughly 400,000km in space. The sign explained the distances better:


* Mercury: 150M

* Venus: 270M

* Earth: 374M

* Mars: 570M

* Jupiter: 1.95KM

* Saturn: 3.6KM

* Uranus: 7.2KM

* Neptune: 11.25KM

* Pluto: 14.8KM


Looking forward my headlamp showed a narrow tunnel of gravel disappearing west between the trees. Everything past 20 or 30 meters was pitch black. 


I started my watch: **2:59.47**


Thirteen seconds to go. I put my palm against the sign. 


Nine. I remember wondering how many people have actually done this. 


Five. Whether that westbound3am guy had actually – 


One. I touched the Sun and started running. 




**MERCURY** (Distance: 145m)  




Nothing spectacular happened, something moved in the woods. My headlamp caught a pair of eyes in the forest, a skunk or racoon perhaps. I laughed and Mercury came and went almost immediately, A light tap and I was off. 




**VENUS** (Distance: 125m)  




Another minute came and went, my legs starting to feel a little awake and my breathing settling into a nice rhythm. 


The cool air, quiet sound of gravel moving beneath my feet, and dark trees were almost calming. I slowed down again to touch the Venus sign and felt ridiculous. 


What was I even doing touching these metal signs because I saw something on the internet? Either way, I’m already here.  




**EARTH** (Distance: 105m)  




The Earth sign was barely further than the Venus sign, around 100 meters. Three markers within the first few minutes. 


I touched it and started to just think of my pace instead of ghosts or anything scary. 


My mind settled into keeping it easy, not burning out, and keeping a normal pace now. Normal long-run thinking.  




**MARS** (Distance: 195m)  




Now settling nicely into a normal pace I saw the first thing that genuinely unsettled me, it was the shadow of a man about 70m ahead of me walking in the center of the trail. He wasn’t doing anything out of the ordinary if it were a sunny day, he was walking west, no flashlight, no reflective gear, just… walking. Slowly. 


As I approached him I remembered Rule 6, *“Do not speak to anyone you encounter”*. I would normally say “on your left” but I just moved as far left as I could. 


As I passed the man he kept his face pointing forward. I could feel him behind me as I continued, the physical sensation that you know someone is looking at you. I wanted to turn around to see if I was in any danger, but again, I remembered Rule 4, *“Between Planetary Markers, Never Turn Around”*, I wasn’t sure if this was physically turning around or just looking behind me so I continued. 


Once I reached the Mars sign I couldn’t take the questions in my head any longer and I turned around. He was gone, it was an empty trail. 


Again, I feel ridiculous. 




**JUPITER** (Distance: 1.38km)  




The “inner” planets were behind me now and the distances opened up a bit, at least I won’t have to be slapping signs every 1-2 minutes. 


I had normalcy for a few minutes before the next encounter, the sound of an engine running. My mind was starting to panic again about something that would be ordinary during normal circumstances. 


As I approached I saw an ATV idling on the side of the trail, engine on, lights off, and no one around to claim it, at least no one within my very limited eyesight. I figured it was a hunter getting ready for an early day or perhaps some kid going for a late-night joy ride. I kept running past the ATV. 


Several minutes later I heard the ATV again, this time roaring loudly and the sound getting closer. The headlights now on and the light getting closer and brighter every second. “This crazy bastard is going to run me over”, I thought as I started to panic and heart started to race. 


I picked up the pace and started sprinting hard, I saw the Jupiter sign and went to place my hand on it. Right as I did the ATV turned hard into the forest, the sound and lights getting quieter and dimmer until it was just a memory. 


“There was no way this was coincidental, is it?” I thought, as I started truly believing that this “ritual” was more than just a creepy story some asshole on the internet made up. 




**SATURN** (Distance: 1.63km)  




Getting my heart rate back down to a normal took a while, I slowed my pace down to a near walk for this segment until I could settle it back to 150bpm. I was continuing along when all the sudden a cyclist rang their bell when they were practically beside me. This startled me, but to be fair this startles me in the middle of the day, there’s something about cyclists speeding past me without warning that both makes me jump and pisses me off. 


Dim front white light, bright red back light. Well done buddy, must be some commuter. Dressed in all black with a bright yellow helmet. He must have been going 30km/hr because he was gone in an instant. 


2 minutes pass and I heard the bell again, this time the cyclist was even closer to me and going faster. Probably riding with the person up ahead and they’re just catching up, although the spacing was odd. Same attire, dressed in all black with a bright yellow helmet. “Strange”, I thought. 


Another couple minutes pass and sure enough a third cyclist passes me, doesn’t even ring their bell this time, going just as fast as the last person. But same black attire, and same yellow helmet. This couldn’t be the same person, the path runs West to East, it would have be impossible to circle around. 


As I come up to the Saturn sign my headlamp illuminated something. Right beside it was the bicycle, laying down on it’s side, no rider in sight, with the back wheel spinning. Just the soft sound of it’s back tire. 


*Tick*


*Tick*


*Tick*


I placed my hand on the sign and kept going. 




**URANUS** (Distance: 3.59km)  




Still on edge from the past half hour of events I just focused on moving forward and thought my usual running thoughts like what I was going to eat after, counterpoints to hypothetical arguments, or movie ideas for a script I was never going to write. 


I must have been deep in thought, or my limited vision got the best of me, because for the first time in years, I fell. Hard. 


My foot caught the side of a gravel pothole and sent me onto my side and I skidded down the path. My right knee must have caught a rock for it to bleed this heavily, and both of my palms were ripped up and stung in the cool air. 


I got up, checked nothing was broken, and then remembered Rule 7, *“Do Not Stop Moving for Longer than One Minute”*. I checked my watch, I’d been stationary for 26 seconds. Just enough time to shake it off, make sure no bones were broken, and kept going. 


The sensible thing would have been to turn around at this point, but I figured I was already out here. My bones and muscles feel good, and if I don’t do this today the odds are I won’t be able to later anyways. 


Two kilometers later, I saw an older man sitting on the side of the trail. He had a backpack, filthy jacket, and looking a little worse for wear. 


*“Hey.”* The man said gently. 


I kept running, Rule 6 entering my mind again. 


*“Hey, buddy!”* He said louder and with more meaning. 


I kept running. 


*“You got a phone?”* he said as I passed by him. 


*“DON’T FUCKING IGNORE ME”*, the man shouted terrifyingly. I didn’t turn around, but I didn’t need to because I heard footsteps start behind me. 


*“DON’T YOU IGNORE MY YOU LITTLE FUCK”*, he was getting louder and more visceral, 


The footsteps were getting louder and the steps sounded like they were getting faster. 


“I’LL RIP YOUR FUCKING HEAD OFF”, he screamed from what sounded like 5 feet away.


I saw the sign ahead, I ran faster but heard the steps get louder and closer every second. 


I touched the sign and immediately turned around. 


Nobody.  



**NEPTUNE** (Distance: 4.08km)  


After the last experience I was on a permanent edge, there was no way this could have all been a coincidence. I tried to calm myself down as well as I could. I ate an energy gel to get some carbs and caffeine in me, perhaps that would settle me. 


For nearly fifteen minutes, nothing happened. That’s what finally frightened me. 


There were no animals, no ATVS, no cyclists, or footsteps. There was nothing, 


Not even the wind or sounds of nature, just the sound of my breathing and the cone of my headlamp. 


A few minutes later I noticed a light behind me. Tiny and white, it must be someone with a flashlight. 


I kept moving. Five minutes later it was even closer, I increased my pace and then noticed the light disappear into the forest. 


Minutes later it appeared again, this time bouncing up and down. Whoever was holding this flashlight was now running. 


I pushed harder, sprinting towards Neptune.


I touched the sign and looked back. 


Darkness. Nothing. 




**PLUTO** (Distance: 3.55km)  




Three and a half kilometers left. My knee starting throbbing and my palms stinging. I’d already run too hard the past 10k, I took another gel, drank some water, and kept moving. 


For 10 minutes there was silence, then the light appeared again. This time it was much closer, 


I started running quicker again, but this time if followed me closer. The light was bouncing behind me. 


My watch beeped with a notification that I just passed 14k. 800 meters to go. 


Closer. 


I stopped checking and starting sprinting, exhausted and terrified I saw the Pluto sign. I reached out and slapped it with excitement that I made it to the end. 


I looked back and the light was gone. I was alone. 


I bent forward, and placed my hands on my knees to rest briefly. 


**14.76km.**
 

I had made it, I remember smiling, actually smiling. The stupid ritual was over, I was done with this shit. 


Then I remembered Rule 9: *“At Pluto, Turn Around and Return to the Sun”.*


I then got another notification on my watch 


“GPS Signal Lost”.
