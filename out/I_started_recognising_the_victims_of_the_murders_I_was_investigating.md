I'm not sure how to explain this, so I'll start from the beginning.

For weeks, a string of gruesome murders gripped the city, people would be found with their bodies desecrated, there limbs ripped off, much of their flesh missing, I was suddenly assigned to the case after the detective assigned to it became one of the victims.

It was the night when had I had received it, I was looking through the victims photos, when I felt an uneasy sense of daja vu, like I had seen them before. I mostly ignored it as I continued looking through the case files, after all, it's probably nothing.

As I drove home, I thought over the case, the murders had no rhyme or reason, there were dozens at this point but I would figure it out, who would do such a thing, the mere thought sickened me. I pondered it as I fell asleep.

When I left the apartment, I saw some coworkers just outside, as well as a forensic team, I was shocked, I never would have thought my neighbour would be a victim in a case I was investigating, she was a good person too. Apparently it had happened last night.

I went back inside, shaken, this was probably a message from the murderer, me and the rest of my family would have to move, I wouldn't be safe, I sighed and sat there for a bit, contemplating what happened 

Sharon, an officer I knew well, knocked on the door. She asked to come in, I moved to let her, then had the sudden realisation that I couldn't, or she'd see the... something, I couldn't think what she would see, but I knew she couldn't come in.

I walked outside and she asked me a few questions, she asked if we wanted to go somewhere more quiet, I nodded sheepishly, we went over to her appointment, nobody else lived there.

When  the door closed, that was the first time I felt the hunger. Or at least the first time I remembered feeling it, it was an all consuming, ravenous hunger, and all I could focus on, was her, she didn't even have enough time to scream.

I devoured her, and just like that, she was gone, nothing but a carapace that I recognised all too well, I immediately left, racing home, I made some kind of excuse about her not feeling well.

That night, I lied in bed, horrified. And as I lied there, I felt it... A dealing deep within my mind, and I could feel it growing, as it spread down my body, that feeling transitioned into pain, my nails twisted to claws, my skin to scales

I stood up, looked around, I looked in the mirror and could see myself without light, I was hideous, no I was powerful, I looked down at my wife.

Then, I feel it again, that writhing, demanding ravenous hunger, my wife was still asleep, my youngest tended to sleep with us, so he too was lying there, in that bed, I tried my hardest to resist, pushing back with all my will, I... Couldn't... Kill... Them... I had to resist, these were two of the people I loved most in the world, it was instinct, when I striked at them.

I watched, in slow motion, as my hand, no claw, moved towards them, it reached there face, and passed right through the crazed delusion. The stench of rot filled my nostrils. Suppressed memories filled my mind

Each night, my form would shift, become more monstrous, become a hunter, I recognised the previous detective was coming close, so I solved the problem, it smiled in my mind "accept it" I am a monster, I kill, I slaughter, it smiled again, I laughed, nobody stood a chance.
