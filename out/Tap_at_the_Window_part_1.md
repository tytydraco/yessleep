“Tap,tap,tap.”

I awoke to the sound of tapping, it seems it’s coming from my window

“It must be that dumbass tree again” I said as I mossied out of bed.

“It does this every time it’s the slightest bit windy”. I grumbled as I waddled over to my window.

I opened the window so the branch would stop hitting it. Right at that moment I shook off my grogginess and remembered I cut that branch down last week, so what was tapping the glass? My neighbor did mention about a month ago that he had a woodpecker in his yard, maybe it's that. I poked my head out of the window and looked around. When I turned my head to the left I saw two beady eyes, soulless and hollow. The eyes moved further back and promptly flew away into the night out of view.

“Thank God, it was just a woodpecker, I thought some creature was outside of my window”. I said with a slight chuckle of relief.

“Tap, tap, tap.” in my room this time

I pulled my head back into my room and reached over to my nightstand to turn my lamp on. Before I could pull the string I saw something in the corner of my room, a man, a man freakishly tall with ripped and tattered clothing. You know how they say there’s fight or flight, well there’s a secret third option, freeze and that's what I did. I couldn't move, I was paralyzed with fear, then I heard it.

“Tap, tap, tap.” right behind me it startled me and I instinctively turned around to look. There was nothing, I realized what I was doing and turned back to keep my eyes on him. He wasn't there, where the fuck did he g-.

I took a sip of my coffee and said “So you said it was a home intruder that killed him”.

“Yep”. my partner Paul said casually while taking a bite of his donut “snapped his neck clean round, one hand too”

“One hand, ya right” I said in disbelief

“No seriously, there were indentations and a red mark of a hand right there on his face, I saw it for myself I did”. His country accent was showing, he usually hides it. “If you don’t believe then go to the morgue and check for yourself”.

I still didn’t believe him but curiosity got a hold of me. “fine I’ll go down there, only if you do too You know the place gives me the creeps”. We went down to the morgue after I finished my coffee. 

“Look Barnes, it’s a gruesome sight, even I threw up in my mouth a little bit”. Paul is the type of detective to brag about his kills, it’s a rare sight to see him distraught. “It’s right over here”. His voice had softened slightly.

“I thought I was the one who needed company, it sounds like you’re about to cry”. I said jokingly, I didn’t actually mean it of course.

“I’m not! It’s just disturbing; I can joke about stuff like this up there, but down here is a different story”. He actually does sound disturbed. He reached for the cloth and pulled it off of the man

“Oh my God!” It didn’t mean to say anything, it just came out. “Clean round is an understatement”. I was struggling to keep my breakfast down. His neck wasn’t just snapped backwards, it looked like it’s done a full 540°. His jaw was broken, both his eye sockets were caved in, and there was a large, I mean LARGE bruise that resembled a hand. “Are you sure it wasn’t a bear?”

“No, the coroner said it was definitely a man’s hand”. He said with full confidence. “What’s weird is there’s no fingerprints, no hair, no DNA whatsoever. From what I’ve heard it’s the number one case in the county”.

“I just can get over how big the hand is, how tall did you say he was”. I asked while examining the corpse.

He was staring at the corpse too, looking for tiny details we could have missed. “Coroner estimates about 8 feet”

I was in shock an 8 foot man with the strength to unscrew a man’s head with one hand “I think we should leave, I’m about to be sick”

As soon as he heard that he covered up the corpse as if he was thinking the same thing. “Ya, good idea”.

As I turned around I heard something.

“Tap, tap, tap”.

“Did you hear that?”. I said with discomfort, eager to leave.

He looked at me like I’ve gone crazy. “Hear what?”. 

“That tapping, how’d you not hear that?” Just as I was saying that I looked over at the coroner's office window, and heard it again.

“Tap, tap, tap”

The coroner took a sick day today so no one should be in there; so why is there a tapping coming from his office window? Right as I thought that I saw the face of an old man in the darkness of the office, he was severely bent over but still taller than the average person. He had tattered and ripped clothing just barely hanging on to his tall and frail body. 

“What the fuck is that!” I yelled at the top of my lungs while pointing. I blinked and he was no longer there, just gone, like he never existed in the first place

He looked at me like I just insulted his grandmother. “What the fuck is what? Are you alright Barnes?” He said while trying to see what I was seeing.

“I-it was a m-man! There was a man standing in the coroner's office tapping on the glass! How did you not see him?” I was yelling in a frenzied panic.

He grabbed me by the shoulders and yelled at me “Barnes! There’s nothing there, calm your ass down! There’s no tapping, and there’s definitely no man!” He walked towards the office and opened the door, showing me there’s nothing. “See, nothing, absolutely nothing” maybe I was just seeing things because of the body. I’m sleep deprived, distraught, and probably dehydrated, ya I’m just seeing things.

I started to walk “O-ok let’s go back upstairs I’m starting to hate this place even more.”

“Good idea, I'm getting cold anyways.” Paul said with his hands in his pocked probably from the chill. 

“You know Barnes you should take the day off after that little event” he looked me up and down. “You look like shit anyways.”

I sighed “I guess you’re right, I’ll talk to chief about it” I gathered my things and walked off. While walking I kept seeing the man in my peripherals and behind doorways. I began to walk faster into almost a run, I could no longer see him so I looked behind me. Nothing, I turned back around and bumped into something. “Ow, shit!” when I bumped into it I fell and got the wind knocked out of me. I rubbed my eyes and looked up, it was him, he’s real? I thought I was just seeing things. He was staring down at me almost with a sense of curiosity, his mouth slouched open and his eyes bulging out. I blinked and he was gone, it was just the police chief standing there now.

“Helloooo? You ok Barnes? Ya bumped into me and zoned out for a second” he said in a concerned tone.

“Ya, I'm fine.” I said while rubbing my eyes and standing up. “Hey chief, can I take the rest of the day off? I just, I just don’t feel right.” I'm fully stood up now.

“You do seem off, You have no open cases and I need my detectives in tip top shape. Sure, you can go home, but I expect you to come back well slept and at the top of your game.”

“Thank you sir.” I left the station and went home. I live alone, I'm not the family man type, I don't even have a goldfish, just me and the mildewed air of my crappy apartment. I threw all my things on the dining table and fell face first on the couch and let out a groan of relief. “Uuuuuugh, finally home. Sleep, bed, now.” I could barely speak I was so sleep deprived. I wanted to get in my bed to rest but I couldn't move from the couch, I felt myself quickly fall into a deep sle-.

“Shit! How long have I been asleep?” I checked the time and I’ve been asleep for about sixteen hours. I got up off the couch, cracked my back in pain, rubbed my eyes, and made some coffee. I checked the flask in my jacket pocket and shook it “nice, there’s still some left.” It’s a nasty habit but I’ve started to put whiskey in my coffee to keep me up for those extra long shifts, as in every shift. The whiskey makes time generally move faster too, so that’s a bonus. My landline rings and I rush over to pick it up

“Barnes, we need you back at the station NOW!” It was the chief, he sounded like he was in the middle of a subway with how loud and rushed the background was.

“Got it Ron.”I struggled to say, holding the phone between my neck and shoulder while trying to put my jacket on. “I’ll be there in 15”

“No you’ll be here in 10.” He seems very serious now.

“Ok Ron, I’ll see you at the station in 10.” I hung up the phone and finished making my ‘black coffee with no whiskey’. I made it to the station and everybody was rushing, even Paul who has a stocky build was jogging into the conference room.

The police chief poked his head out of his office and yelled to me “Barnes! Conference room Now!” I rushed into the conference room and looked around. Every detective worth a damn was in there, even Paul who is ‘ahem’ not the best. Ron walked in with a large folder and started to speak.

“Everyone be quiet. QUIET! I’m sure you’re wondering why I called you in here in such short notice, well, it’s starting. For the first time in 25 years we have a serial killer in the tri-country area.
