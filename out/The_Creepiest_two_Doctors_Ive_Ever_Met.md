When I tell people my story, they usually have a hard time believing me, and I can’t blame them. Sometimes, even I wonder whether I imagined the whole thing or if what happened to me was real.

It all began one night as I was driving my truck home along an empty road, with fields stretching out on either side. I had some jazz playing in the background and was smoking a cigarette when I noticed two figures in the distance, carrying something toward the fields.

As I squinted to get a better look, I realized they appeared to be wearing doctors’ clothes. They were carrying what looked like a medical stretcher, with someone lying on it. I couldn’t make out the person’s features from where I was.

I watched them until they disappeared into the fields.

*What are two doctors doing in a place like this?* I wondered.

It struck me as odd, but I soon forgot about it.

A few minutes later, the two doctors emerged from the opposite side of the fields, carrying the same person on the stretcher and running through the grass. I found myself watching them more closely, growing increasingly curious about what they were doing.

I was so distracted by them that I nearly crashed into something.

After managing to regain control of my truck, I looked at whatever it was I’d almost hit.

To my astonishment, it was the same two doctors.

My eyes widened as I glanced from the fields to the men standing right in front of me.

*They were just over there. How the hell did they get here so quickly?*

They apologized repeatedly before continuing on their way.

That was when I finally got a proper look at the person on their stretcher. He was an incredibly frail old man, so thin he looked as though he’d been starving for months. His hand was bleeding, and he was writhing in pain.

I also noticed that the two doctors looked remarkably alike. They had to be twins.

For the rest of the drive, I kept seeing them again and again. Sometimes they were on one side of the fields, sometimes they appeared directly in front of my truck, and other times they were behind me. They would vanish without warning, only to reappear somewhere else along the road.

Their repeated appearances began to make me nervous. I couldn’t shake the feeling that something was terribly wrong, and I started to worry that they might be planning to hurt me. I moved my weapon closer, just in case things took a turn for the worse.

Then they appeared again.

This time, they were right beside my truck.

When I looked out the window, I saw them running alongside it, matching its speed perfectly. No matter how fast I drove, they kept pace with me.

It was a bizarre sight. They continued running beside me for several minutes, and I grew more and more uneasy as the seconds passed. I kept waiting for them to fall behind or turn away, but they showed no sign of stopping.

Eventually, I worked up the courage to do something about it.

I pulled over and called out to them, telling them to stop. To my surprise, they did.

I asked them what was going on. They looked at me, then lowered the stretcher to the ground.

One of them spoke.

“Oh, we’re sorry if we’ve disturbed you. We’re just looking for an ambulance.”

“An ambulance?” I asked, bewildered.

“Yes. We’re doctors, and as you can see, this old man’s hand is badly injured. He’s most likely going to lose it, so we’re trying to find an ambulance to take him to the hospital.”

“But how have you not found one yet?” I asked. “Surely one would have arrived by now, considering how long you’ve been looking after him.”

The other doctor replied, “I understand what you mean, but things don’t always work out the way we’d like.”

He then glanced at my hand.

“This poor man is going to lose his arm. It’s a terrible thing to happen to anyone. Nobody wants to lose a part of their body…” He paused, studying my hand. “You’ve got big, strong hands, sir. Take good care of them.”

He looked away and continued, “Anyway, we’d better get going. We still need to find an ambulance.”

I glanced at the poor old man, who was still writhing in agony, and felt a pang of sympathy for him. Before the two doctors could leave, I stopped them.

“Can I help you? I don’t think wandering around looking for an ambulance is doing him any good. His condition could get worse the longer we wait. Let me take you to the hospital.”

The doctors smiled gratefully and thanked me profusely. I told them to climb into my truck. They placed the old man in the back while the two of them settled into the passenger compartment beside me.

We drove in silence.

There were so many questions I wanted to ask them. The whole situation was bizarre, and I couldn’t make sense of any of it. Still, I decided it was better to keep my mouth shut.

We’d been driving for about twenty minutes when a sudden, loud thud came from the back of the truck.

I immediately pulled over to check what had happened. The old man was back there, after all, and I was afraid he might have hurt himself.

I got out and headed toward the rear doors.

Before I could open them, something grabbed me from behind.

I turned around.

It was the two doctors.

Before I could even utter a word, the truck’s rear doors swung open, and the old man stepped out.

He began walking toward me slowly, one arm outstretched. A wide, horrifying smile spread across his face.

I stood frozen, unable to comprehend what was happening. He kept coming closer until he finally grabbed my hand.

And then, suddenly…

Everything went dark.

When I opened my eyes, I was staring at a ceiling. I looked around and realized I was in a hospital.

I had no idea what had happened. My family was gathered around my bed, their faces heavy with grief. Some of them were crying.

I must have looked utterly confused, because they weren’t looking at my face. Their eyes were fixed on my left hand.

I looked down.

It was gone.

My left hand was missing.

My eyes widened in horror. My breathing quickened, and my heart began pounding violently against my chest. I let out a terrified scream, and nurses rushed over alongside my family, trying to calm me down.

Eventually, they managed to settle me. I lay there in silence, barely moving, for some time.

Then, all at once, I remembered the doctors. The old man. The stretcher.

I remembered everything.

The memories shattered my silence, and I began screaming.

“The doctors… and the old man… they’re responsible! They did this to me!”

I kept repeating the words in a state of sheer panic until my family gradually managed to calm me down. Once I was able to speak coherently, I told them everything that had happened to me on that road.

They urged me to report what I’d seen to the police, explaining that identifying the people responsible might help the investigation.

And so, after being discharged from the hospital, I gave my statement to the police. I described everything I could remember about the two doctors, the old man, and the events of that night.

An investigation was launched, focusing particularly on the area where the incident had taken place. But after a lengthy search, the police failed to find a single trace of any of them.

Eventually, the case was closed with no suspects identified.

The incident became a memory that would remain etched in my mind for the rest of my life. It was something I knew I would never forget.

As time went by, I began hearing reports of other incidents along that same road. Their details were disturbingly similar to my own experience.

People were being found abandoned on the side of the road, each missing a body part. Sometimes it was a hand, sometimes a foot or an entire leg. In other cases, they were missing fingers or even other parts of their bodies.

What made these incidents even stranger was that the survivors all seemed to tell almost exactly the same story.

They spoke of two doctors carrying a medical stretcher with an old man lying on it.

The only difference was the old man’s injury. In one account, his hand was badly wounded. In another, it was his foot. In others, the injury was to his neck or some other part of his body.

To this day, neither the police nor the investigators have managed to find any trace of these people.

And so, the mystery surrounding them remains unsolved.
