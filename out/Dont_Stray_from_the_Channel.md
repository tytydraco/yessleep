I write to yeh wit little hope it may help me survive.  

I am locked in her barn…her barn that sits on a barge…her barge that she has anchored in the middle of the mighty Choptank Sea.  

She calls it the Isle of Abigail and it rests within a cloud of fog that seems attached by the hands of the Lord.  I drifted from the Channel of Goodwin after my propeller broke on the top of a sea turtle…the same sea turtle that then dragged my boat — The Happenstance — for what felt like hours, through the fog, to her barge.

Music played from a Gramophone as we approached and she rocked in a chair, having a cup of tea.  The turtle died almost instantly as my boat hit the barge, almost as if she persisted only to complete the task of bringing me here.

I consider myself a man of firm sand, but I am shaken from what then set forth.  

“My name is Esther.  Join me for tea, won’t you?” It’s then that I saw she had only one arm as she waved me over with it.  

What was I to do?  I took tea with her.  

She had a small cabin, a barn and a greenhouse on this floating bastard.  Do not think me a liar when I tell you that there were three animals roaming about…beagle dogs, large alligator snapping turtles and poisonous dart frogs.

I sat down and she explained that she has prepared for the end of Man and built this barge to withstand Mother’s great reckoning.  Befitted with the creatures to help sustain life afterwards.

She poured milk in my tea and I took a sip as I tried to humor her while my mind raced to find some advantage.  She must have a boat, or some vessel to move about more easily, tied up somewhere.

THEN…it hit me.

“Why can’t I feel my legs?” I said.  I don’t believe I hid my fright any longer.

She was endlessly joyful as she picked up one of the frogs and held it in her hand.

“Oh, darlin’…you see many milking cows around here?  You know if one of these hound dogs ate this frog they’d be dead as a door knob…but…if you start feeding them these here frogs in tiny bits…well, slowly but surely, they build immunity to that poison and they can eat as many as they gosh darn want.  Easy food for the hound, especially these Momma’s who are feeding their babies...but it does mean their milk is as potent as these darn frogs”

I could no longer move my arms, my chest heaved in panicked breaths.

“It was turtles that I couldn’t figure out.  They don’t eat often and when they do they’re picky.  They wouldn’t eat the frogs, I think they scared of the hounds…but they did take to human flesh.  I started with my fingers….moved up my arm…and now they got real taste for…but that’s all the supply I am willing to give them”

I felt the tears starting to roll down my face.  I knew it was no use, but I belted out a sad…

“HeellllPP!”…It made her laugh.

“Oh honey….”

I write to you from her barn.  I am tethered by a chain and there are five alligator snapping turtles with a taste for a man’s flesh slumbering before me.  I fear they will wake up hungry.

I beg yeh, don’t stray from the channel.
