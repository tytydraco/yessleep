I live alone in a six-story building. Every apartment has an intercom connected to the front entrance. When somebody downstairs presses your number, the handset in your apartment rings, you answer, and you can buzz them in.



Pretty normal.



Three nights ago, mine rang at 2:17 AM.



I wasn't expecting anyone, so I ignored it. It rang again maybe twenty seconds later.



Then again.



I finally picked up because I figured somebody was drunk and pressing the wrong apartment.



I said, "Hello?"



Nothing.



I could hear the faint buzzing you normally get through the system, but nobody spoke.



Then I heard my own voice.



Not a recording. At least it didn't sound like one.



It said, "Hello?"



Exactly the way I'd just said it.



I hung up.



I stood there staring at the handset for a while, trying to convince myself there was some kind of feedback issue. Then it rang again.



This time I didn't answer.



The ringing stopped after four or five seconds.



A minute later, someone knocked on my apartment door.



Three slow knocks.



I checked through the peephole and the hallway was empty.



That was enough for me. I locked the extra chain, turned on every light in the apartment and stayed awake until morning.



The next day I tested the intercom with a neighbor. Everything worked normally.



I also went downstairs to look at the entrance panel.



That's when I noticed something I should probably explain.



Our intercom doesn't show apartment names. It has numbered buttons. Mine is 42.



When somebody rings me, the little display on my handset shows the number of the entrance panel. It always says "ENTRANCE."



I had been half asleep the first night and hadn't looked at the display.



Last night it happened again.



2:17 AM exactly.



The handset rang.



This time I looked before touching it.



The display said:



42



My own apartment number.



I actually laughed when I saw it because it was so stupid. There is no second intercom inside apartment 42. There is nothing that should be capable of calling mine from that number.



I let it ring.



After a few seconds it stopped.



Then my phone rang.



No number. Just "Unknown Caller."



I didn't answer that either.



A moment later I heard three knocks from the hallway.



Same rhythm as before.



I went to the peephole.



Nobody.



Then the intercom rang again.



42.



I don't know why, but I picked it up.



I didn't say anything.



For about ten seconds there was only static.



Then a voice whispered, "You looked."



It was my voice again.



I dropped the handset.



Almost immediately, something knocked three times on the other side of my bedroom wall.



That wall doesn't connect to another apartment.



My bedroom is on the corner of the building. On the other side is open air.



I slept on the couch with the lights on.



This morning I unplugged the intercom handset from the wall. I pulled the cable completely out.



I figured whatever electrical problem this was, at least I wouldn't have to hear it tonight.



About an hour ago I came home and found the handset ringing.



It was sitting on the kitchen counter where I'd left it.



The cable was still disconnected.



The display was lit.



42.



I didn't touch it.



It rang for almost a minute and then stopped.



Nothing else happened for a while, so I started writing this because I honestly don't know what else to do with myself.



It's 2:11 AM now.



Six minutes to go.



The handset is still unplugged.



I moved it into a kitchen drawer and put a towel over it because seeing it was making me nervous.



At 2:08 I heard something knock once from inside the drawer.



At 2:09 it knocked twice.



It's 2:12 now.



I think it's counting.
