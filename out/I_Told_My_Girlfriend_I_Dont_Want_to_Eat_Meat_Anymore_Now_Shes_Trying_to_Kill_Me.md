Emphasis on “saw”. I’m not so sure anymore.

Now, I’m not a squeamish person. But the sight of all that blood. I just… It’s too much. So, I decided to try out cutting meat from my diet. Just for a bit. You know? Try it out.

That’s what I told my girlfriend that night at dinner. We were at a restaurant and I told her I was considering going vegan.

“Vegan?” she said.

“Yeah?”

“But I love meat!”

Okay? I was a bit perplexed at the time. I told her she doesn’t have to be part of it. We do make our meals together, but we can just cook separately. I didn’t think about it at all but then a week later she did something weird.

She got home from the supermarket with tons and tons of grocery bags. I asked her what they were and she just plopped the most insane amount of food I’ve ever seen in our kitchen counter. There was like, fifty pounds of it.

It was all meat.

“What the fuck is this?” I asked her.

She just looked at me. Like I asked the dumbest question on Earth.

“It’s food.” she replied.

I’m not sure if this is some sick joke over my “going vegan” choice. Or if she now finally feels free to eat how she always wanted. But it was really odd, and it was the start of our relationship getting sour.

That was just the beginning.

Every single meal. Every single god damn meal. Every time, every day. It was just meat.

Every day she would just eat meat.

Hamburgers for lunch, steaks for dinner. Tenderloins for breakfast, a bit of liver for a late-night snack.

When we ate together, I’d offer her some of my food. I’ve tried giving her some greens, mashed potatoes, some cauliflower. Hell, even french fries. She rejected it all.

She started eating her meals with side dishes as any normal person would. But as the week went by, she ate fewer and fewer sides.

Every meal was just meat. A huge steak, some ribs, you name it.

Sometimes she wouldn’t even season it. She’s never been the best cook, but she’s not that bad. Lately she’d just put a slab of meat on a pan and cook it. Then she’d eat it. No garnish, no seasoning, nothing.

She would just look at me and smile while she did it.

Creeps me the fuck out.

Sometimes she sends me texts with pictures of what she had for lunch at work. You know typical girlfriend stuff. It’s always a picture of her eating a massive slab of meat with a text that reads:

“I love you, Chris. Look what I had today!”

It gets harder to say “I love you” back. I just ask her to not send me food pics.

The other day we were going to watch a movie in the living room. I got some popcorn and chips and sat down. Typical movie night, right? As soon as I sat down, I offered her some. She looked at me with disgust.

I noticed she was already eating something, from a bowl.

You know mincemeat? Imagine mincemeat but in clumps and badly cooked, like half raw.

That’s what she was fucking eating.

The smell… I just couldn’t take it. I walked away. This obsession of hers with meat is ruining our relationship.

You just eat meat. All the time. I thought I understood what was going on. That was clearly what she was doing.

I guess I shouldn’t talk. I decided to go off meat, I guess she can decide to just go on meat.

But Jesus Christ, some moderation maybe?! I was considering just posting this on some diet forums but things have escalated.

I went to the toilet and passed through the kitchen. That’s when I saw her.

She was standing in front of the fridge. All I saw was the little light of the fridge illuminating her face. Jesus Christ.

She was eating raw meat. Straight from the freezer.

Just gnawing on whatever she could grab. I think there was nothing unfrozen for her to eat. So, she just settled for frozen, raw meat.

I still remember her teeth scraping against the frozen slab of steaks. Trying to rip chunks off it. I never wanted to run away as fast as I did there.

“Want some?” she said.

God.

I can’t live like this anymore. She’s a normal person most of the day. But every meal, every time we eat together. It’s like she’s a different person.

Like an animal.

There’s so much blood everywhere. It’s revolting.

This woman. I wanted to marry her. But now I think I might have to leave her. She’s clearly going insane.

“It’s what I like, silly.”

I don’t know this woman anymore.

She has a pretty smile. She’s always had a pretty smile. It’s one of the reasons I fell in love with her. Her cute face. Her pearly white teeth.

I never expected to see them red.

There were five dead birds on top of me.

Like, dead small birds. All mangled up and half bitten. I freaked the fuck out.

“I brought you breakfast.”

She’s completely losing it. I don’t think a shrink is enough. Something in her broke. I can’t take it anymore. I told her I was fucking leaving. Her parents can take care of her, this is too much.
