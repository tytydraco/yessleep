I work later than most people in my area, so for the past few months I've been catching the same bus home at around 10:40pm.

The stop is on a fairly busy road, but by that time most of the shops are shut and there usually aren't many people around.

About three weeks ago, I noticed a man standing at the stop.

There was nothing particularly strange about him at first. He looked to be somewhere in his forties, maybe early fifties. Dark jacket, jeans, trainers. He was standing a few metres away from the shelter looking down the road.

The bus came.

I got on.

He didn't.

I remember thinking it was a little odd, because it was the last bus going in that direction for quite a while, but I didn't think much more about it.

Two nights later, he was there again.

Same place.

Same clothes, as far as I could tell.

Again, the bus arrived and I got on.

Again, he stayed at the stop.

This happened another two times that week.

By the fourth time, I'd started paying attention to him.

He never looked at his phone.

Never checked a timetable.

Never seemed annoyed when the bus went past.

He just stood there.

And whenever I got onto the bus, I could see him through the window watching it leave.

I told myself I was being paranoid.

Then something happened that made me change my route.

Last Tuesday, I finished work slightly earlier than normal.

As I walked toward the bus stop, I could already see him standing there.

Except this time, when he noticed me coming, he stepped behind the shelter.

Not into it.

Behind it.

I stopped walking.

For a few seconds, I couldn't see him at all.

Then his head appeared around the side.

He looked straight at me.

I turned around and walked back toward the row of shops.

I went into a takeaway that was still open and waited inside for about twenty minutes.

When I eventually looked outside, he'd gone.

I called my boyfriend and told him what had happened. He offered to come and get me, but by then I was embarrassed about making such a big deal out of it.  
So the following night I decided I'd take a different bus.

The alternative stop is about a ten-minute walk from my usual one and takes me slightly further away from home, but I figured I'd rather walk an extra few minutes than see that man again.

I didn't tell anybody which route I was taking.

That part is important.

I left work at around 10:25pm and walked in the opposite direction.

When I reached the new stop, nobody was there.

I actually remember feeling stupidly relieved.

I stood under the shelter and started scrolling through my phone.

Maybe two minutes later, I heard footsteps behind me.

Not coming along the pavement.

Coming from the little side road directly behind the stop.

I looked up.

It was him.

I recognised the jacket before I even saw his face.

He walked straight toward the shelter.

I didn't know what to do, so I picked my phone up and pretended to be calling somebody.

He stopped just outside.

Neither of us said anything.

Then he smiled at me.

And said:

"Different bus tonight?"

I can't explain how scared I felt hearing that.

I immediately walked away.

He didn't follow me.

At least, I don't think he did.

I called my boyfriend and stayed on the phone while I walked toward a petrol station about five minutes away. He drove there and picked me up.

I haven't used either bus stop since.

But that isn't the reason I'm posting this.

Yesterday afternoon I went back to work.

Broad daylight. Loads of people around. I felt completely fine.

My manager called me into the office when I arrived.

He asked whether I knew a man called "Martin."

I said no.

Apparently a man had come into the shop about an hour before my shift.

He'd asked whether I was working.

My manager wouldn't tell him.

The man apparently said he was a relative and needed to speak to me.

My manager asked for his name.

He said Martin.

Then he left.

My manager showed me the CCTV.

It was him.

The man from the bus stop.

I've never spoken to him.

I've never told him my name.

I've never told him where I work.

And until yesterday, I had never seen him anywhere except those bus stops.

I went to the police and gave them everything I could.

They've told me not to travel home alone for now.

Tonight my boyfriend picked me up from work.

When we got back to my house, there was an envelope pushed through the letterbox.

No stamp.

No address.

Inside was a bus timetable.

My usual route had been circled.

So had the alternative one.

At the bottom, somebody had written:  
"You forgot another route."

I haven't figured out what that means yet.

But I'm definitely not going to work tomorrow.
