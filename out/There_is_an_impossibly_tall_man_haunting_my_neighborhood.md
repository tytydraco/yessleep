I was out for a walk one night recently, around 6pm. It was already dark, so I had a flashlight to make visibility easier.

The route I take just goes around my block. It’s about a mile, and takes me anywhere from 15-20 minutes depending how fast I walk. I’ll often throw on an audiobook and just let muscle memory take the wheel.

I was about halfway through my walk that night, when a shadow crossed my path as I walked under a streetlight. Like most shadows, it was tall and thin. 

For some reason, I looked up. 

Something stood in front of me, not particularly wide but just tall enough to block out the moon.

I thought it was just another streetlight at first, and was about to move on when I saw it blink. Not a flicker, like when the power briefly goes in and out. It was as though someone had given a streetlight two metallic eyelids.

And then it walked into the light. 

Or maybe I should say “he”, because it was a man.

Maybe it would have been better had he looked off, had the pallid skin and unnaturally wide smile you get in any “uncanny valley” story.

But no. He looked just like a regular guy. Maybe a little malnourished on account of being probably upwards of 10 feet tall, but as normal as one could be apart from that.

We looked at each other. Sort of just nodded. I think he could tell that I was trying to make sense of what I was seeing.

And then he did something very odd. He lifted a finger to his lips, smiling mischievously, as if to tell me to keep quiet and not tell anyone about our encounter. His left hand had a leather glove on it, which also struck me as strange, as it couldn’t have been cooler than maybe 65 degrees. His lips were also very red, and very, very wet.

And then he silently faded back into the darkness behind the streetlight, and walked away into the night. 

Finishing my walk would have taken me in the same direction, but the interaction had left me so unnerved that I just turned around and walked back the way I had come. 

I tried to put my audiobook back on, but I couldn’t concentrate on it. I wound up walking home at a significantly more brisk pace than I had previously.

I haven’t been able to go out on my lifetime walks in a while. Even if I could, though, it’s going to be a long while before I go back out there at night.

Three pets in the neighborhood have gone missing since that night, all while let out unsupervised into their respective, fenced-in backyards. Then two days ago, an elderly man managed to slip out through an open front door, left ajar while his daughter brought bags in from an evening trip to the grocery store. 

Last night, as I was getting ready for bed, I turned off all the lights and went to shut the front blinds.

Right before I did, I could have sworn I saw one of the streetlights blink.
