I don’t know how much time I have, and I don’t know what’s going on or where on earth I’m going. It happened so fast I almost don’t even want to believe it’s real. It can’t be real, this doesn’t happen here, does it?

I was just going on my morning run with my dog. I do this every morning since I moved here fifteen years ago. Molly joined me five years ago and hasn’t left me since. Out of nowhere two men came up to me, they looked friendly enough, but I’m not stupid. Maine is a really nice state, it’s not innocent of all bad things, but it’s generally pretty safe. Still, I kept my guard up. My dog, Molly, was close to my side, studying them as they came near. Where did they come from anyway? They approached me a little too confidently, which stuck out to me, and asked me for directions. It’s not the first time I have been asked where something was. I figured they must have been camping, and had gotten turned around, but their vibe seemed slightly off. I wanted to excuse it to drinking, but something kept telling me to stay cautious. All they wanted was directions to a nearby store, which I thought was reasonable. I pointed over my shoulder, I quickly told them it was a few miles down the road and went to move around them tugging Molly along. I looked down and saw her ears back and teeth bared, that’s when everything told me to get out. I turned my back for one minute and that’s when I was grabbed by one of them. The other one pulled Molly away from me while I struggled against the bigger one, He began dragging me towards a van that was parked in a small clearing. Why didn’t I see that? I could hear Molly barking but I couldn’t see her. I tried to reach out for her, I almost broke away when I felt a sharp sting in my neck. My body began to feel limp, my vision blurred and I felt hot.

I must have passed out because now I’m awake and I’m in this dark box? I think? I’m still in the van, but it’s dark and cramped. They shoved me into something. I have no idea how long I was out, I don’t know how far away from home I am. I don’t know how they missed my phone. My calls won’t go through. I don’t even know if this post will go through, I can only hope. I’m trying everything right now. My head feels so foggy. I was just on a run with my dog. I don’t hear her. Did they take my dog? Did they hurt her? What did they do with her? Where is my dog? If anyone sees a black shepherd off of Route 1 she’s super friendly. Her name is Molly. Her eyes are a piercing blue. She’ll come right up to you if you’re gentle and quiet. I think we’re coming to a stop. I can feel the van slowing down. I have to end this here. I don’t know what they plan on doing, I don’t know if I’ll still have my phone. If this is the last time I’m ever heard from, whoever finds my dog, please take care of her. Tell her I didn’t leave her. I don’t even know if she would understand, but I need her to hear it. Please find Molly. I hope I can update, I hope I can get out. I’m so scared. I’m so scared, but I’m also really, really angry.
