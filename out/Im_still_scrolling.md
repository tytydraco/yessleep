I never wanted to be online, but my friends Jordan and Richie convinced me to create a Branched account. It was fun for the first few weeks. I didn't want to be isolated forever. 

I should've stuck to my guns like my grandma with technology. It's 2047, and she still doesn't have a cell phone. She says that technology changes you, and now I believe her. 

\*\*\*
I don't have a lot of friends, maybe two that I used to text on a regular basis. You can say that I don't like people, but it is what it is. I guess that made me more susceptible. I might not smile a lot, but I'm a nice guy.

\*\*\*

I met Jordan and Richie at a Tekken tournament at the arcade five years ago. We all lost to the same guy fighting as Jack and became friends while we were licking our wounds. Jordan won the same tournament the next year fighting as King. Richie didn't even show up. 

\*\*\*

The last time I saw Jordan was two years ago at a Mortal Kombat tournament at the local arcade: he wouldn't shut up about Branched. He said he never gets bored anymore. He used to come over to my house, and we played Madden for hours. I guess that season of life is over. He always played as the Packers, and I was the Ravens. I never got my win back. 

On to my other friend: I'm not talking to Richie, because he played a practical joke on me last year. I didn't realize what he was doing when he handed me some purple wildflowers after we got done playing basketball. While I was resting, three or five bees started flying in my direction, and I took off screaming.

He was crying laughing, knowing I'm terrified of bees. I almost peed myself! I couldn't sleep right for three days. It's not funny. But it was just a joke. Maybe I shouldn't hold a grudge: life is too short. I was planning to see if he'll answer my calls later. 

\*\*\*

Anyway, one Monday night, I created my Branched account and immediately found Jordan's profile. I followed him and started watching his reposted videos. 

They were skits about crazy gardeners. Those were hilarious! In one of them, there was a heavy-set White guy rolling around in the soil. I'm crying. 

But shouldn't I be doing something productive?

\*\*\*
Wait! Where did the time go? I've been scrolling for two hours.

What if I miss something funny? Maybe just a few more minutes. 

Thirteen minutes later, I pulled myself off of the couch, leaving behind my print. I got ready for bed and placed my phone on my dresser. 

That's strange. I smelled dew, but there wasn't any rain that day. My left eye was itching, but I thought it was because I had been staring at the screen for too long. I wonder if I could've stopped it then…

\*\*\*
That night I had a dream that I was eating from the sun in the middle of a garden. And no, I never smoked weed. I woke up with a smile and wanted to return. 

The next morning my left eye was still itching, and I put eye drops into it. I can't explain the feeling, but it felt like the drops refreshed my pupil. I started seeing everything bigger and clearer.

Before heading out, I took a profile picture. It was me in an Atlanta Hawks jersey and black sneakers. I usually don't smile, but Branched suggested that I don't scare people off. My smile lasted long after the picture because I received some likes. 

I felt rejuvenated as I got ready for work. I checked my phone one more time and walked out of the front door. 

\*\*\*

I'm sorry I forgot to tell you: I'm a salesman at the Foot Locker two streets from my house. It's an easy way to make money, but I wanna take whoever came up with the saying “the customer is always right” and give him or her a wedgie. Did I mention I don't like people?

\*\*\*

Anyway, I scan myself in at work and it's a normal, boring day. At around 10, my left hand starts itching while I'm helping a customer try on black sneakers. I was busy, so I ignored it at first. I looked around and noticed everyone was drinking from a water bottle. Then the itch intensified, and I couldn't take it anymore. 

While fitting her left foot into the sneaker, I reached back and scratched my left hand with my right. It felt euphoric. I couldn't stop. I think I even started drooling. 

\*\*\*
Three minutes later, my hand stopped itching, and I realized what I'd done. When I reached back, I knocked the customer over, and she hit her head on the shoe boxes behind her. She was bleeding. My manager walked over and yelled at me to apologize. 

I did, then walked away. My manager followed me and she said, 

“If she ends up suing us, that's your job.”

I explained, 

“But it was an accident.”

She sucked her teeth and replied, 

“I don't care. I don't need that bad publicity on my name. Now, you're on thin ice.”

She walked away, and I went to the time station to scan myself for my lunch break. 

\*\*\*
I was supposed to be eating my turkey sandwich with Bugles, but I was occupied with scrolling through Branched. I followed a few people who seemed to like AEW Wrestling also. I even saw a Foot Locker commercial, and that guy looks eerily like me. 

Am I sure I didn't take anything? Grandma warned me about them spying on me. I'm just overthinking. 

That wasn't me. 

\*\*\*

I refreshed the page looking for the ad and got nothing. The time monitor beeped, and I walked over to clock myself back in. It's like my phone was stuck to my hands. I could feel my manager staring holes into my head. 

She yelled, 

“Get off your phone, and get back to work. You're already on thin ice. Do you want it to crack?”

I walked up to the scan, but it beeped once it detected my phone. 

My manager almost lost it and yelled, 

“Either get off your phone or leave early.”

I tried pulling the phone out of my hand, but my thumb kept on scrolling. My manager didn't notice my struggle. She must have thought I was being defiant.

She yelled, 

“You have five seconds to get off your phone, or you'll be sent home WITHOUT pay! One!”

I was trying to yank my phone out of my hand. Sweat was pouring from my forehead. My index and middle finger started bleeding as I yanked harder. I used my teeth to pull at my phone, and two of my bottom row teeth started bleeding. 

She yelled, 

“Three!”

At this point, my whole hand was on fire. Foolishly, I tried using my right foot to separate my hand from my phone. I tripped and hit the back of my neck on a shoehorn.

I looked up at my manager, who motioned for me to leave. 

\*\*\*
I walked home. I kept my left hand under my shirt and kept looking around. On my way home, an elderly White woman knocked me over, and my lunch fell out of my backpack. 

I was near a garden so some soil fell in my open Bugles bag. I looked up, and the woman was gone. I tensed up when I heard what sounded like water dripping from a hose. I grimaced and continued home. 

I gathered my keys with my right hand and mouth, and stumbled inside. I couldn't lock the door, so I pushed my loveseat in front of the door. My breathing calmed as the dripping stopped. The room started shifting around me, and I smelled dew again. I passed out on the floor. 

\*\*\*
Two hours later, I woke up to my left hand scrolling. My hand looked darker than usual. I screamed as I felt my knuckles cramping up.

What in the—

\*\*\*
I walked to the bathroom and rinsed my mouth out. I looked up and tried focusing on my left hand, but Branched opened up a video of a countdown of the best finishers. I started rapping the Hurt Syndicate's theme song until reality kicked in shortly after. 

I walked over to my couch and sat down. All the while, my left hand was still scrolling. I went to exit Branched, and something was ripping off my index finger. I stopped and did several breathing exercises to calm down. I slapped myself across my face three times to make sure I wasn't dreaming. 

After three hours of scrolling, I started to gather up the courage to try and exit Branched again. After nine minutes of trying to build up enough strength, I finally hit the overview button to minimize the app. It actually worked. 

My fingers were sore, but I had to continue on. Some time later, I was able to pull up my call log. Richie was the first number that came up. I took a deep breath as I called his number. Blood was dripping now, and my fingernails were being torn off. My middle fingernail just fell onto the couch.

After five rings, Richie's voicemail picked up. Well, maybe he's busy. I ended the call: I'm not talking to a machine!

\*\*\*
Before I could look for Jordan's number, Branched opened itself back up on my phone. It went to Richie's profile. His profile picture was a… wedding photo with a woman who looked like his wife. She was a skinny, brown-skinned woman with braids. Her smile was gorgeous. I almost shed a tear. 

Why didn't he invite me to his wedding? I guess we weren't as close as I thought. We were like brothers. I don't need him. What kind of friend was he anyway?

\*\*\*
I tried to slam the phone down, but almost fell over. My phone was still glued to my left hand. I couldn't feel my fingers anymore. They must be running on muscle memory. 

I pulled myself up, walked over to my backpack, and took out my turkey sandwich with Bugles. As I was eating, I tasted some soil and remembered my backpack falling into that garden earlier. I gagged at first, but it traveled down my throat. I struggled to stand, limped to my trash bin, and went to throw the Bugles bag away. 

Before I did, I licked my teeth and savored the sweet taste of soil. My stomach wanted more. 

What? Why?

I emptied the bag, looking to find more soil. I didn't find any and threw the bag down. I didn't feel like moving my loveseat, so I stayed on the couch. I fell asleep right there while my left hand was still scrolling. 

\*\*\*
That night I had a dream that my grandma's face had turned into a rotary phone. A black hand was spinning the dial over and over. A black plant with a disgruntled face glued to it turned in my direction and kept laughing. 

She kept telling me, 

“Stop scrolling.”

I was listening to her and laughing while tending to the roses in my garden. I looked at my left hand, and my phone was no longer attached to it. 

\*\*\*

On Wednesday morning, I woke up with my seat cushions drenched. Now, I know I didn't pee the couch. I checked myself, and I was dry down in my crotch. 

Gingerly, I stood up and placed the cushion in my washing machine. I walked to the bathroom. While washing my hands, I noticed a leaf growing out of my left palm. 

What the…

I tried to investigate, but I got a notification that this cute girl commented on my profile picture. She was a Latina with a mole on her left cheek and wearing a silk, brown dress. I can't miss this chance!

Immediately, I messaged her, and we entered into a conversation. She likes wrestling also! My smile grew wider with every message.

This might be the beginning of something beautiful. 

I was giggling like a schoolboy. Maybe we can go to a show together. I wonder who her favorite wrestler is. Then the ball dropped when she said she needed money to pay her light bill. I snapped out of my trance, went to her profile, and saw that she was living in another country. I cursed myself out and immediately blocked her. 

You're not meant to be loved! Remember that. 

Almost five seconds later, a notification popped up about Branched Dating. Are you kidding me right now? I had enough of this stupid spying app!

I put my left hand down and studied my palm. It looked like a bud was there! I got into the shower and scrubbed my body intensely, but it was still there. Taunting me. I also noticed that I had roots in my veins. 

\*\*\*
The hospital was too far away to get to without a car, and I didn't have enough money for Uber. I hadn't talked to my neighbors since I moved in. I walked outside and knocked on my neighbor's door to the right. No answer, and all of the curtains were closed. 

The same thing happened at the house to my left. I'm not fit enough to do a twenty-mile walk. I thought about hitchhiking, but people were too crazy. They would've laughed if I didn't at least offer gas money. Or do worse.

I walked back inside and sat on my couch. My left hand was still scrolling. 

\*\*\*
If I sat there, nothing would have changed. Determined, I held my head up and managed to go to my contacts list. I called my grandma, but I kept getting a busy signal. On the third try, Branched opened up with Richie's profile. Two minutes ago, he wrote a post about some conspiracy theory called “Dead World Theory”. Whatever it was, these posts were too long, and I didn't feel like reading. 

I wish I had read it.

\*\*\*
I tried to message him, but his Branched inbox was full. I tried to comment on his post, but it was deleted for violating community standards. Annoyed, I went to the news feed.

While I was scrolling, an ad for a fast food restaurant popped up with the tagline, “Feeling full?” I stood up, went to the kitchen, kept punching the wall with my right hand, and screamed. 

\*\*\*
After standing for what seemed to be two hours, my legs grew tired, and I sat back on my couch. I gave myself a little break and tried to play WWE 2K. With my left hand busy, it was really hard to execute the moves. After three or four minutes, I threw my controller down. 

\*\*\*

I inhaled, almost ripped my pinky finger off, managed to reach my contacts again, and called Richie three times. I got the dial tone with him as well. I tried texting him, but every time I tried, Branched opened itself up with another video. I tried to delete Branched, but it said that my phone didn't have permission for that activity. 

\*\*\*
Soil, soil.

My mouth became dry. I walked to the kitchen, got water from the faucet, but it didn't quench my thirst. My stomach was craving some soil.

I grabbed my keys and walked out of the front door. I walked through my yard, crouched down, and dipped my right index finger in the soil when three bees flew around my face. Immediately, I ran away and entered my house. I shut my door and ran around the house until I didn't see them anymore. I closed every window. 

\*\*\*
I sat on the couch. I looked at my phone and noticed something. Over these past few days, I hadn't received any calls or messages. 

Who needs them anyway? This was the season of me loving myself. I was destined to be a loner: I was born alone. 

As I closed my eyes, an image flashed in my head of each of their bodies stacked together in my garden dipped in a pool of blood. At first, I flinched, but slowly a smile warmed my face.

\*\*\*
I opened Branched to unfollow Jordan and Richie when I saw that they were already removed from my page. I typed their names, but their profiles didn't come up.

Did Branched do this? This is enough!

I walked up to the kitchen table, grabbed some paper towels along with Band-Aids from the drawers, and sat down. With my right hand, I began to pull my phone out of my left hand. With each pull, my fingers bled more. I turned my left hand over and noticed that the dial pad's numbers were stitched to my palm.

I kept pulling until I heard a bone crack. I had never felt pain like that. I pulled one last time, and my ring finger was yanked off. It was sitting on the floor. I looked to my left, and my thumb was still scrolling through Branched. 

Exhausted, I sat down on my couch. 

\*\*\*

Three hours later, my phone buzzed with a text from my boss,

“Hey, so the customer is going to be suing us for her injuries. You already know by now why I'm texting. You're fired! Turn in your uniform and whistle in by Monday.”

I just stared at my phone screen. Two minutes later, Branched opened up and played a video of a funny wrestling botch. The guy went for a frog splash and got tangled in the ropes. I laughed and leaned back on my couch. 

I gotta look for another job. 

\*\*\*
Two hours later, I tried to go to a job search site, but Branched always opened up a different funny video that caught my attention. My neck was getting sore anyway. 

\*\*\*

Well, it has now been two weeks since I lost my job. There's no happy ending, because that's it. My body has now grown branches, and I make sure I get my sunlight and water every two days or so. I hope I don't overgrow my house. 

It's too difficult to stand up with all this weight around me. Without any money coming in, I don't know how long I can stay here before my eviction. Maybe the landlord knows a good gardener. 

If you're looking for me, I'll be sitting on the couch, my left thumb still strolling.
