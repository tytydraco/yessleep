My father has been a pastor my entire life.  My parents protected my brother and I from most situations regarding patrons of the churches my father pastored at. Though some situations were unavoidable. This situation seemed avoidable but inevitable given the nature of my father’s profession. 

It was a normal day until my mom picked me and my brother up from school. She worked an hour away from our town at a hospital so she never picked us up. Besides being a pastor my dad also drove a school-bus (and still does he’s retiring this year, GO DAD!!) Anyways, we usually hopped on the bus with dad and rode the route with him then we’d stop for ice cream on the way home. 

The ride home with mom was quiet. I could tell she was worried and this was before cell phones. When we got home mom told me and my brother to go to our rooms so she could call my dad. Of course i had to listen through their bedroom door. 

I heard her say, “i cant get a babysitter right now.” I could tell by her tone that whatever she was talking about was urgent. “Ok ok ok i will be there as soon as i can.” I heard the phone slam as i was running back to my room. 

My mom went to my older brother’s room first. “We have to go to Monticello for awhile get your brother (me) and come to the car.” 

The car was running when my brother locked the house door. Mom had both hands on the steering wheel, gripping it hard. She was not relaxed. Me and my brother both got in the backseat and buckled up. At this point i was starting to get nervous but we never questioned our parents. 

The ride was an hour away. I knew this because it’s where we used to live. I played pokemon on my gameboy color for most of the ride. My brother watched out the window. Looking at my mom every couple minutes. 

We arrived at a mobil home in the middle of nowhere. I saw my dad’s truck and about six other vehicles parked outside. Before i could unbuckle my seat belt my mom turned around and told me and my brother to “stay in the car no matter what you hear, do you understand me??” “YES MA’AM!” 

Years later after i turned 16 i asked my mom about this and what had happened. This is what she told me. 

“I wanted to wait until you were old enough to tell you this. That day your dad called me at work and told me i needed to pick you and your brother up from school. So i left work early and i had planned on getting a babysitter to watch you guys. When i couldn’t i called your dad and he said to just come on and bring you two. He needed me there and it was important. When i told you two to stay in the vehicle i went inside the house. As soon as i walked in the mans teenage girl looked at me with the evilest grin across her face and said, “oh youre here and you brought the boys.” I looked at her and yelled YOU STAY AWAY FROM MY BOYS!” 

The girl was possessed by a demon. She was a girl in the church we had previously went to and had no idea my mom was bringing me and my brother. Take this story as you will but it’s always been with me and has always creeped me out.
