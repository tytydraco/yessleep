My heart is about to beat out of my chest as I prepare for my shift. I apply my lipstick and tie my apron tight around my waist. Have I got enough pens? One to use, one to lose and one to borrow. Check.  

It’s 16:50 just before the evening rush. I tentatively check with dread which section I’m in tonight. With any luck I’ll have an easy two-top section. If luck isn’t on my side I’ll have a section of parties and 6-top booths. 

It’s kind of the best of both worlds. I’ve got a section of 3 six-tops and 3 two-tops.

I cautiously walk onto the floor and we are SLAMMED. Servers gliding with a staccato punch between tables and tables and tables. One manager’s just gone home sick. We are overwhelmed - there’s no doubt. 

I move to my section ready for handover with Stacey who’s been trying to handle the day shift as best she can. She looks stressed. 

“Ok ...”  she says about to get the hell out of here. 

“Table 30 have been waiting over an hour for their appetisers, they are pissed off. You need to chase that up with the kitchen. 31 are ready to pay, they’ve been ready for 15 minutes I just haven’t had time to deal. 32 need their order taking they’ve been here 45 minutes. I just haven’t had the time. 33 are waiting on cocktails the bartender just can’t seem to get to their tickets. 40 need a check back, 41 need a check back, 42 just got here and need drinks orders taking and 43 are waiting for a manager - sorry I have to go - it’s hell”. 

As I took a deep breath and got ready to address everything, Sam in the section next to me appeared to be having a breakdown. 

“I can’t! I just can’t! Please take my last 3 tables” and he fled the restaurant floor in tears. 

I surveyed my section. Ok. I can do this. Tables 30, 31, 32, 33, 40, 41, 42, 43 and now 34, 44 and 35. Wow. 

Where to start?

I went to the bartender to check the status of 33’s cocktails. 

“There are 19 tickets before this hon” said Davey the bartender. “You just need to wait” 

Ok - 30’s complaint. 

I find a manager “we had no idea they complained .... I’m busy come and ask me in 10 minutes” 

Let’s get 31 paid and out of here. Easy. Sure they’re pissed off but as the busser rushes to clear the table I beg him to make the host wait 20 minutes before sitting me again. 

32 need their order taking. 

“Not very good at this are you?” Barked the Karen sat by the window. “Maybe you should look at your time management and maybe - you know - get a clue?! I want a gluten free menu and I want it in less than 30 seconds”. 

I rush off and the woman at 42 yanks my elbow.   
“Excuse me we’re ready to order” 

I can’t cope. I cannot cope. I can’t deal with my own section let alone the three extra tables Sam left me. There are no managers to be seen. 

Laura comes down from upstairs in tears “I’m done please take my tables - please!” 

Before I can even protest she is out of the restaurant and still I can’t see any managers or anyone to help me. I run upstairs to see the damage. 

She has 7 tables of 2  and a party of 18. My heart sinks and beats uncontrollably. I am out of control. I can’t see where I can start. 

I spot a manager who shouts at me “get your shit together!” 

I can’t. I can’t cope. I don’t know where to go next. I am losing my mind. Have you ever felt like you’re losing your mind?! 

The host approaches me, clearly pissed off.

“40 and 41 have been waiting to pay for 15 minutes and just walked out. You owe us!” 

I panic. I return to my section and I have 3 new tables plus Sam’s irate section he abandoned. 

Karen yells from 32 “HELL-Oo?! Where’s my gluten-free menu?!” 

I want to walk out but I can’t. Please somebody help me, please. The host starts putting together tables in the section next to me. “You’re getting an engagement party of 15 at 18:00” she said. My heart pounds and tears fill my eyes. 

I’ve got my section, Sam and Laura’s abandoned sections and my incoming table of 15. 

41 need refills so i take their Coke glasses and fill them. I return. 

The entire restaurant is empty. 

Nobody. 

No-one is here. 

Eerie silence fills the restaurant. 

Empty section, Sam’s tables are gone and the tables are clean, Laura’s section has been closed and I have no party. 

No manager. No host. No busser. No other servers. 

I run to the kitchen. 

8 chefs stare at me. 

Staring. Staring. Staring .... one holds a pan and stares. One holds a knife and stares. 

Motionless. They all stare. 

I return to my section. Just the engagement party settling into their seats. 

The party organiser smirks at me. 

“Pretty dead in here tonight, huh?” 

I swallow hard. 

“Yup. Sure is .... dead in here. T-tonight”.

I take their drinks orders and ring them through but when I take my tray to the service bar to collect them, there’s no bartender. 

I run up to the staff room to see if Davey’s on a break. He’s nowhere to be seen and strangely the pegs in the changing room  
are completly empty. No bags, no coats, nothing. 

Great. Ok - I’m not trained on the bar but you can do this. YOU CAN DO THIS! Come on, girl.

Karen’s back. “HELLO?! My gluten free menu! I want to speak to the manager”. 

I am \*this\* close to breaking down and walking out - but I need the money. 

My engagement party have walked out - at least I don’t need to get their drinks. One thing at a time, you can do this. Right - Table 30 ....
