I have been wanting to share this story on Reddit for a while but was always cautious that people would immediately dismiss it and just say I’m spouting bs however its late at night and I’m bored (lol)

It was May 2022 5 days after my 15th birthday to be exact . I remember everything about it so vividly and clearly, I have no way to explain this feeling of remembrance however to put it for people to understand it’s kind of like a song you have known off by heart ever since you were a young child. 

It was exactly 11:32 Pm and my mom had just told me to go to bed after i had woke her up playing the piano (I had got it 5 days prior as my birthday gift) I thought nothing of this and as a young teenager I ignored this request and simply plugged my headphones into the keyboard/piano . I turn off the lights because that’s tend to what I usually do as I like the dark .

My door had been broken 2 months prior and would not shut due to me slamming it too hard lol . 

As I’m searching up YouTube tutorials on “how to play the piano” my phone completely powers off. I found this very strange as my phone was on around 45-50% charge and was literally plugged in.

I’m currently just sat in pure darkness and only the blue screen light thing on the piano was reflecting on my wall. I suddenly hear my door creek open slightly more than it already was and assumed it was my mom telling me to quiet down.   
I quickly rush into bed to make her think I was never up.

As I rush into bed I got a glance of some sort of strange silhouette . The pianos screen had left some flicker of such. At this point I’m still calm. I thought well my mom’s quite tired and sometimes when she’s just awoken she can look quite scary?

However after a while when I still hadn’t heard any words from her or her footsteps walking out of my room . I hear the faint sound of my mom snoring upstairs (she’s the direct room above me and the floors are quite shit)

My heart sinks as I bite my tongue and stare directly at my piano which is directly next to my bed . I slowly feel this “silhouette sit down directly on the end of my bed and could hear it rocking back on forth while repeating some freaky religious bs and noise

After like 50 seconds it suddenly stopped . I don’t hear it leave , I don’t hear a sound? After 30 minutes of feeling like I’m paralysed I slowly look up and see nothing. As you can imagine I did not sleep that night but managed to fall asleep for 30 minutes at around 4:45 however when I woke up in the morning I felt nauseous af , to the point where I genuinely could not move . 

I never told a soul about that night up until now . Luckily nothing else ever happened however I looked up the house a few months ago (we’ve moved out since then) it turns out it was built over an old nunnery from the 1400 😭?   
This shit fucks me up till this day
