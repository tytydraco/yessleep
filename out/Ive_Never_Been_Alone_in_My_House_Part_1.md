I don’t know why, but the paranormal has followed me for most of my life. Ever since I was twelve, things around me never made sense in my house. Some people in the world have had experiences with the paranormal. I am one of them. But instead of turning you into the protagonist of some bestselling horror story, it actually makes you more sarcastic and more rational. Naturally, it scared me at first, but as I got older, my fear developed into sarcasm.

Maybe it’s because the human mind tends to use comedy as a way of coping and making sense of things, while rationality takes over as well. I don’t know. I’m just sharing what I know.

Let me tell you this: the paranormal isn’t always so scary. Seriously, so what if you hear footsteps upstairs? No big deal. I mean, if this ghost or demon could actually hurt you, why would it flick the lights or randomly open doors at three in the morning? Right? It doesn’t make sense at all. It almost sounds like trolling, but it’s true. At least, that’s what I thought.

The thing is, the paranormal can be different for every person. It can go from a classic scary “boo” to an actual mind-breaking scene. That’s what I wanted to clear up first. So be careful.

Anyway, the first time it happened to me, I was twelve. My parents went out for dinner and left me home alone with everything a twelve-year-old needed for entertainment and unlimited soda. Yeah, my parents bribed me with sugar and TV before they left.

As I was watching TV, I heard footsteps upstairs. I went up to check, thinking it might be my dad, but then I remembered he was out. Naturally, I found nothing, so I went downstairs. Then I heard footsteps again, but this time they were heavier and faster, as if someone were coming down the stairs chasing me. I spun around, scared, only to find nothing again.

I didn’t go back upstairs to check. I was twelve, not an idiot. I just pretended it didn’t happen and resumed watching TV until I convinced myself that nothing was wrong. A few hours later my parents returned home and I told them everything. Naturally, like all parents, they thought I was just nervous or scared for being home alone like any kid at my age.

That was my first experience with something weird, what followed the years after was straight up unfiltered paranormal.

One time I was also home alone in my teens during a winter, I was asleep in my room. And heard a voice whispering my name right next to my ear. I jumped out of bed with my heart pounding like a rabbit running from wolves. I looked left and right, up and down, the room was empty. Nothing. Just me holding a warm blanket and sweating in my pajamas. I didn’t sleep in my room the rest of that night. Couch in the living room felt safer thanks to the light from the TV. What scared me was how it felt. I felt like someone was next to me, I felt hot breath hitting my ear as the voice whispered my name, it was scary as hell. I thought I was dreaming and simply moved on from it.

And things continued like that for me for years. Whenever I was alone in the house things like that happened. So, like a healthy human being I developed the ability to use sarcasm to calm my nerves by mocking anything strange around me and use rational thinking to make sense of things. Yeah, and I did it for years.

If the lights flickered, I would simply think dad needs to call an electrician or just go “wow. Very original.” and continue doing whatever I was doing. If a door opened randomly, I would simply close it believing it was the wind and mock the ghost’s efforts to scare me. If my peripheral vision saw something move quick I wouldn’t even think about it. I got used to it real good. My idea was “if it can hurt you, why the wait? And if it wanted to scare you, why wouldn’t it do something scarier? And why would it budge scaring you in the first place?”

I was in between, I never believed the things that happened was truly paranormal, but I also never believed the otherwise. I just simply moved on and life has its ways making us people move on. Friends, parties, school, homework, career, bills, ambitions, getting your first car…...and such.

But the thing that happened recently made me believe in the paranormal shit and turned me into a sleep deprived twenty something year old man with nicotine addiction like a detective in a noir film. Except I ain’t dealing with moral ambiguity while solving a case but trying to understand why the thing in my house turned from trolling me the past years to actually hurting me. Yes. It did hurt me. And it was scary as shit. It wasn’t psychological, it was physical.

Keep in my mind that the current me never cared about the spooky lights flickering or the doors randomly opening or footsteps. I got used to it at this point. Even the scary events that involved hearing a voice whispering my name in my sleep. I just waved my hand in the thin air dismissively knowing there’s nothing there. Sleep paralysis? I got used to that so much I didn’t budge waking up frightened or even shocked, but laughing and feeling refreshed after sleep.

Not so long ago, I had one week off from work and parents went on a trip. I had the house for myself and peace to enjoy. I liked being alone since my mom nags a lot which me and dad had to tolerate. It was a good week. I ate wherever I wanted without causing a mess, I played video games all day, I went to hang out with friends till night fell, and I slept without worrying about the damn early morning alarm. And I repeated this routine everyday. At one day, I was asleep on the couch after playing “Silent Hill 2” on my console all night. It was somewhat early in the morning, the time where it’s cold and your house looked blue, not the decorations but like the air or something. You know what I mean.

Just as I wanted to get up to get a sweet morning drink, I couldn’t move. I couldn’t fucking move. I started to panic because this felt real. Actually real and not a sleep paralysis because everything looked perfect and if it was a sleep paralysis it wouldn’t have taken this entire time since it only lasted seconds for me.

My eyes darted looking around, I tried speaking and all I managed was a scared sound. And my eyes saw someone standing right next to me. They weren’t there when I opened my eyes. As if they spawned there. I screamed and managed more frightened sounds, even my fucking voice felt paralyzed. It was a woman’s figure, wearing dark robes that covered her from head to toe, only her mouth was visible, body swaying slightly. Her robes didn’t feel like robes because when she swayed the robes never moved. They weren’t fabric. She lifted her right gloved hand, holding something yellow in two of her fingers, her fingers had slight blood on them. The yellow thing was a tooth.

Next thing I know, everything was gone. The woman, the fear and dread, the paralysis. I was sitting up straight holding my head. I didn’t remember waking up jolting. I was scared as hell. It was the scariest thing that happened to me. As I swallowed, I tasted blood. My damn blood. My tongue felt a missing molar tooth, blood clot formed on the socket. And on the table was the molar tooth, right in front of me. the table had tiny blood drops surrounding it. I screamed horrified as stood up on my feet.

“holy shit” I repeated to myself till I ended up screaming to the top of my lungs as the realization of the realness of this settled in my mind. I was panicking, my breathing was uneven, not to mention the pain in my lower jaw. Every time my eyes landed on the molar tooth I didn’t feel pain in my jaw, but I felt fear as I remembered that woman.

My mind screamed for me to leave the house right at that instant, and that is what I intended. “Absolutely not I am dealing with this shit!” I thought.

I was trying my best to remain calm as I moved. I bolted to my room upstairs and began dressing in my clothes to leave the house. As I wore my clothes and shoes, picked up my phone and called my parents. I headed toward the bathroom to wash my mouth real quick. I was shaking, scared out of my mind, but as I remembered I was now leaving to a friend’s house and about to call my parents I relaxed a bit. Even though what was going on was mind-breaking.

I washed my mouth with the cold water, it eased the pain in my jaw. I didn’t care about the molar tooth, or the pain, I cared about the sheer insanity of whatever happened. As I spat the blood in the sink and washed my face I moved instantly. Didn’t want to look at the mirror, but I stopped as I realized something. My reflection, I didn’t see my reflection in the mirror. I didn’t see it because it wasn’t there. I turned around facing the mirror, no reflection of me. Nothing. I stared shocked and began hyperventilating terrified. I didn’t try to understand it, I wanted to leave. And the mirror pulsed. It pulsed like a living organism and I backpeddled from it. And it pulsed again. And I ran. Just as I ran I heard the mirror shattering behind me.

I was at this point running away like a frightened animal. I barely got down without falling down the stairs. As I reached the outside I was standing near my car, keys in one hand, phone in the other calling my mom’s number. She answered and I yelled out to her on the phone, trying my best to talk to her. “mom! Please listen something happened! I dunno what but something happened and I am scared! I’ll call you in a video call to explain! But I’m not staying in our home and I’m going to a friend’s house! Can you and dad come back sooner please?!” I didn’t feel like a scared kid calling for his mom, I felt like a helpless man who thought he figured out life but got slapped by reality. Mom didn’t answer right away, she was silent for few seconds. And then she answered.

she answered with a calm voice, her exact voice “sweetheart, I am right here. Me and your dad are right here with you. Come inside”

I stood frozen in my place. I knew this was something crazy again. So I hanged up the phone and got inside my car and started it. I was looking at the front door of my house from my driver’s seat, and there she was, my mom. Standing, looking at me, with a wide wide smile that stretched too far, and her eyes were bleeding. Blood trickling down her cheeks as if weeping blood. Her mouth was stretching down the longer I stared but I drove away fast. Didn’t want to look at the thing. A smart decision.

As I was driving on the rode my tears didn’t stop. My eyes kept looking at the rearview mirror, checking the backseats feeling scared something might be there, but there was nothing. Thank god for that. As I realized my reflection on the rearview mirror was there I felt relieved. I turned off my damn phone, didn’t trust that fucking thing. I drove in silence heading to a friend’s house, I usually drive listening to my favorite music, but not this time. The constant thought that filled my mind was “I am not crazy. This is real.”

I understand why this paranormal shit happens. Why it happens to people and they get called crazy for it. Because they were alone.
