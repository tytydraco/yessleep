So I live in the Tampa area, and sometimes I hang out at local dive bars where fishermen and some hunters hang out. I’d heard some stories about fishermen fishing along the Withlacoochee River seeing what looked like a very large man walking around around sundown who looked to be covered in moss. Some locals even claimed it was like a Bigfoot-type creature because they swore they heard monkey-type howls at night.

I just laughed because that sounds ridiculous to me. But anyways, I heard there were some pretty large catfish in that river, and I love a good fish fry, so I listened to one of the locals and went to his "spot."

My buddy and I planned to go, but he backed out last second because his girl was trippin'. I got to the water and rode my kayak down to the area where he said to go. When I got there, I grounded my kayak and dragged my cooler out to the spot so I could sit and cast out.

I was catching one catfish after another. I had landed about five big ones, and my cooler was almost full. I’d been throwing back the smaller ones.

I decided I wanted to catch one more and then I’d leave. I cast out and almost immediately snagged one. But it was BIG, and I wasn’t reeling him in quickly. As I’m walking the shoreline and fighting this fish, I hear what sounds like a howl, but almost like a monkey. I mean, that’s the best way I can describe it at least. I’m freaked out, but I’m also just trying to get this fish in so I can leave.

So then I start hearing branches cracking behind me, and it’s like a person is stomping them… it’s so loud. At this point, I’m looking into the woods trying to make something out, and I'm holding my rod in one hand. I manage to get my flashlight with one hand and shine it into the woods. I don’t know if my eyes were playing tricks on me, but I felt like I was looking at a giant shadow of a person in the distance behind a tree, and the second I turned on my light, the branches stopped. I say "person," but it had to be 400 pounds and 6 feet 6 inches. Idk… But I do know that I got my pocket knife, cut my line, and got my cooler and everything I had to my kayak as quick as possible.

I started hearing the cracking again as I pushed back off into the water, and I shined my flashlight back into the woods as I felt safe enough to turn around once I was like 15 feet offshore. I saw the reflection of what looked like eyes about 6 to 7 feet off the ground, but it could have been a raccoon for all I know because it was so dark at this point.

Anyways, whether the creature exists or something is out there and your mind is playing tricks on you… I’m not sure.
