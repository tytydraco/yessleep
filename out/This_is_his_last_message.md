“Almost five months ago, one week after my birthday, I told you I was going to the cinema with some friends, I lied, I went alone since I had no one to follow me. “I’ve seen some old movies before, black and white movies, and the images seemed to be older than any of them, this was obvious since the very first frame, seconds of a written letter in an unrecognizable language and the names of the people involved, couldn’t be more than five, but I don’t remember any name. “This went on for the first part of the movie, people talking, people walking, people fixing fences. “You never hear the music again, but you hear the humming sound of engines coming, and they do too, some people look to the sky, some point skyward to others. “I was curled on my seat, just seeing the planes was horrifying, and I was drowned by their whistle, that sound they made when they dive.
