Hi everyone!

This happened about 10 years ago, but I felt encouraged to create a Reddit account to share this story after months of listening to allegedly real horror stories online, and hearing a couple of stories eerily similar to mine.

I’m no stranger to the paranormal and I’ve had my fair share of encounters since I was a kid— from your average shadow sighting or hearing voices, to doors opening on their own, to things full-on being thrown across a room. Yet, somehow, this was the only time I ever felt in such massive danger.

My sister and I (16 and 19 at the time) had just returned from visiting my mom.

Her house was terrifying, and my sister and I would always feel like something was watching or following us— walking around the house, peeking over the shower wall when we sat on the toilet, standing on the other side of the door when we showered, peeking through doors left ajar. Day or night, there was a looming presence somewhere.

In spite of this, we still slept in separate rooms for the most part. However, that night everything felt heavier, and my sister came into my room claiming she’d heard something moving, and didn’t want to be alone. So we slept together.

We went back home the morning after, and we feel like whatever was watching her might have followed us.

We lived in a small, two bedroom apartment, where the doors to each room faced the each other directly. My sister and I shared a room and the other was my dad’s.

My bed was placed in the corner of the room, in a way that my feet faced the door and my head was directly below the window. So the moonlight always lit my bed as I slept.

When we were all getting ready for bed, my sister still felt unsettled and said she’d dreamed that something was going to try to hurt my dad, so she slept in his room.

I slept with the door closed and my back towards the wall because I always felt like something was standing behind me otherwise, and I kind of had this idea of “If I’m facing the wall, whatever is lurking sees me as easy prey even before I fall asleep, so facing the other way should make it go away”, as if ghosts behaved the same way as Boos in Mario games. 

Little did I know, that belief was about to be shattered in one of the most terrifying ways possible.

I went to bed under the perfect conditions for me to sleep comfortably: quiet moonlit night, with perfect weather. No reason to wake in the middle of the night.

I woke up at 3 A.M.

It took me a second to open my eyes, but when I did, there it was… Just a couple of inches away from my face, was a face that was clearly NOT human. Big, oval shaped, a bit elongated towards the sides. HUGE yellow eyes with pupils that resembled more those of a cat than a person, but still were not exactly like either.

A grin, almost as large as its face and so wide it occupied about a third of it. With sharp teeth that looked as if they could rip off your skin in a second.

It was hunched over, and from what I could tell, its frame didn’t look too wide, but everything, aside from the eyes and the grin, looked pitch black. I don’t remember seeing a nose, or even space for it. I don’t remember eyebrows or ears, or any other trait that could even slightly make it more human-like.

It was by far the most demonic thing I had ever seen— The type you only see in movies.

The sight of that thing reduced me to nothing more than a 6 year old in a second.

I jumped back and covered myself with the blanket, trying to process what I had just seen.

I ***knew*** it was not a person. I knew I was locked in my room, alone, and something about the look on its face made me feel like it knew I was just helpless pray.

I was trained in personal defense, and had it been a person, there’s not much to think about: A person can be fought; a person can be incapacitated. They have physical limitations like everyone else, and even if untrained you could still have a chance against them… but this? I felt so powerless— could I even punch it? If I could, would it even faze it? What could it do to me? What did it *want* to do to me

I could still *feel* it crouched next to my bed.

I reached for my phone under my pillow, still fearing it would grab me, since to me, my blanket was like my shield in that moment— as long as I remained there, it couldn’t touch me.

My plan was to turn on my flashlight, throw my blankets, jump out of bed and run to my dad’s room. What was that going to achieve should it chase me? Probably nothing, but I wouldn’t be alone anymore.

I grab my phone and lie face up, trying to control my breath, gathering courage to attempt to escape, until all of a sudden, I could *feel it* moving, and the moonlight coming through my window drew a shadow that moved from the side of my bed to above me. Like that thing had just floated to place itself on top of me.

I was terrified, but now I was also angry. Enough so, that I threw my blanket at it, I turned on my phone’s flashlight, and quickly stood on my bed and looked around hoping to catch it, but it was gone.

I could no longer feel its presence either.

I decided to reach for the light switch, but it was next to the door, meaning that I needed to either get off my bed, or jump to my sister’s bed to reach it.

I still feared that, as soon as my foot was out of my bed, that thing would grab me; I was afraid that it had gone under my bed to hide and wait for me to make the wrong move or fall back asleep.

Still feeling trapped, I gave up and yelled for my dad, and he and my sister bolted into my room.

All I could do was yell “TURN ON THE LIGHT! TURN ON THE LIGHT”, which they immediately did, finally giving me the courage to open the closet and look under my bed, still trying to find that thing. Not sure to what end.

I proceed to tell them that something was staring at me just inches away from my face, without going into any detail, and my dad looks at my sister, who looks back at him and proceeds to yell  “It’s the thing that was in the room!”.

I ask her what she meant and she tells me that she had been awake for a while, but she had pretended to be asleep after waking up and seeing a lanky, completely black figure with yellow-ish eyes and large claws standing in the corner.

She said she kept thinking “You can’t be here, you can’t touch us” over and over again until it walked out the door.

Around 5 minutes later, I screamed.

My dad then confirmed she had yelled “It went into her room!” as they jumped out of bed to come to my rescue.

I used to think that having paranormal encounters, while scary, was kind of cool? But that night I was so scared that I decided I never wanted to see anything paranormal again, and other than the occasional shadow or a random small thing moving, I haven’t since. But that much I can live with.

Honestly, I have no idea what its intentions were, but the way it was looking at me felt perverse and predatory, and that face will live in my head forever.

I know this is not that subreddit, but Grinning Man… Let’s not meet again.
