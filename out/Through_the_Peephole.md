Its been almost a week since I've moved into my new apartment. It's tiny and even though it looks modern, barely anything works.

The last time I tried using the oven it started smoking and the power went out, leaving me with the microwave as the only cooking option for now. The lights only work sometimes, so I'm forced to have emergency candles in case the lightswitch decides to not do its job.

Speaking of jobs, I'm still looking for one, so there isn't much to do in here but stay inside and browse the web. Maybe watch a few movies or play video games.

Sometimes though, while waiting for my food to cook I pace around the place.

One of my favorite things during this time was to look through my apartment door's peephole from time to time. Sometimes I can catch the stairway light being turned on and then wait for someone to pass by. Sometimes I'm late to the event and catch myself looking at the neighbour's door for a couple seconds, only for the light to then turn off, accompanied by a clicking noise.

Other times I just stare at the darkness.

The stairway has little windows on each floor that let some of the moonlight in, exposing a bit of it's contents. Mainly the neighbour's door. It's an old, elegant wooden door, painted a deep brown with the paint peeled off in some places.

Right next to it however, is a shape.

The shape blends in with the surrounding darkness perfectly, almost being a part of it.

I didn't notice it at first, but after a couple days it started standing out to me more than the vintage door, finally making me curious what the hell it actually was.

So, one time, while waiting for my nutritious meal I gave in and unlocked my door.

Upon opening it I was greeted by a familiar "click" and the stairway light in my eyes.

After readjusting my sight, it turned out that there was nothing standing beside the neighbour's door. No shape.

It was empty. Whatever.

Since then I have started opening my apartment door every time I peered through the peephole, trying to catch the shape... to no avail. I've checked the peephole itself, there was no dust on it, no dirt, no nothing. I even cleaned it to make sure, outside and inside. I wish "peephole cleaner" was a job. I feel like I'd be pretty good at it.

This became my little routine.

While waiting I'd look through the hole. If the light is on I'd wait for someone, and if it wasn't I'd stare at the shape.

It could almost come off as a humanoid shape and if I stared long enough I could find myself feeling like there's a face somewhere in there. But the thought made me feel uneasy so I'd quickly open the door just to remind myself that there is in fact no face staring back at me.

Today however, I had an idea.

While waiting for another meal, I put my phone camera through the peephole and began recording. Then, still holding the phone, I opened the door as usual, making sure nothing's there.

Empty.

When I checked the footage though, a horrible dread came over me and I could feel my heart collapse and shatter on the ground.

It moved.

I clearly saw the shape bolt back into the darkness the second I placed my hand on the handle, its sudden movement activating the stairway light. All in complete silence.

Whatever it was, it wasn't human. It looked like a black fog or… a pile of trash bags with a hint of grey somewhere in there, resembling a face.

A dreadful, dreadful face.

Its shape messed with my head. It was so unnatural and bent in all the wrong ways.

I locked my door as quickly as possible.

I stood there, replaying the footage over and over and over again, desperately wanting it to be fake.

Desperately trying to convince myself that maybe I was just seeing things.

I wanted it to be just a speck of dust or something, literally goddamn anything, but when I dared to look through the peephole again, there it was.

Standing in front of my door, so close it covered my view completely.

I stood there paralyzed, watching.

And so it stood too.

It's been hours and its still there, waiting for me to open the door.

I don't think I've got the guts to do it. I just really, really hope its gonna be gone in the morning.
