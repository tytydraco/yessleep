Journal Entry No. 1

Being blinded by the brightness of my surroundings, a searing pain in my side and a wetness running down my mouth is all I can remember. I have these nightmares pretty frequently for the past 8 months and when I do, it usually follows with me waking up somewhere else around my house with things being flung around and stuff broken, being told it's a side effect of trauma and for right now the most I can do lock my door or set alarms to try and wake me up so I don't keep destroying things. They say writing about my dreams and experiences will help with stress, which will hopefully help with sleepwalking and the nightmares so I'll be making this journal to keep records of my progress.

Journal Entry No. 2

Recently I'm starting to remember more of the nightmares. Being surrounded by snow, stomach hurting, my face covered in something thick making it hard to breath. Though I think it's making my sleepwalking worse, the alarms are no longer waking me up, belongings smashed and markings left on my door. Talking to a psychiatrist about medication, being prescribed a pill that'll help with my nightmares which theoretically will stop my nightly tantrums. 

Journal Entry No. 3

After talking with my therapist about the recent update on my dreams we came to the conclusion it's probably about something that happened to me about a year ago and they said that I should write about it and doing so might help me remember more so I can work through my nightmares and sleepwalking. A couple of friends and I were hiking in the Misquah hills, Minnesota during January to find a good snowboarding spot. It gets pretty fuzzy after that. I know there was a bad blizzard and I was found a couple weeks later in torn clothes but that was told to me after I had woken up in the hospital. But after I get my medication I won't have to worry about it anymore.

Journal Entry No. 4

The medication swapped one problem with another. I no longer seem to sleepwalk, but I have no idea whether this is a different nightmare or if it's sleep paralysis. I'll wake up and I hear noises in my room, huffing, gurgling and moaning. A tall gaunt figure with shining grey eyes that looks at me from the darkness with drool spilling from its mouth. The deeper it stares into me the more my intestines hurt like their writhing within me, I would say I'd want to vomit but it's the opposite.

And in an instance the pain goes away and the creature is gone. I think I'm going to get off the meds, the sleepwalking was more manageable.

Journal Entry No. 5

After getting off that pill my normal after hours activities started up again. But my nightmares started to switch back and forth between the snowy hellscape and that monster, but instead of just sitting in the corner, it ravaged my room, clawing at the door and panting in anger. Sometimes it climbs on top of me, I can feel the gaunt figures hot breath hitting my face while the drool piles on my cheek, all I can see is grey eyes burning into me while its long fingers press into my stomach and then I wake up kneeling on top of my bed staring down at my pillow.

Journal Entry No. 6

With the new nightmares, I'm starting to remember more of the first one I've been having. I'm in the forest during a snow storm, my friends’ leg snapped and I'm trying to get him out of the snow. It's been a week or two stuck out in the chilled wilderness and my stomach is in pain, close to starving. The rest is still blurry and hard to remember but the memories are coming back to me. 

Journal Entry No. 7

I woke up to my room door busted down and my fridge ransacked of all the raw meat, with me sitting in the middle of my living room covered in raw steak juice and ground pork. I don't feel sick but I don't know if I should mention this or not. After my last nightmare I've been getting that same pain in my day to day. Gorging myself on food and it doesn't seem to go away, I don't know what to do anymore.

Journal Entry No. 8

I'm starting to remember the rest of my experience in that forest. After some time of trenching through the snow I collapsed with my friends’ lifeless corpse on top of mine. This corruption in my stomach, screaming at me to feast on something, anything, to survive. I can't bear it so I tip his body off of mine and I look at it longingly, mouth agape like a starving animal looking at free prey. I was starving, more than starving so I had to, I had to right? But the corruption didn't stop there, my mind grew foggy and I could feel my spine crack and my teeth begin to shoot out. Then it all goes black all until I wake up at the hospital.
