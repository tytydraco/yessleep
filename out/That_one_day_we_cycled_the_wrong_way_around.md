I was only 10 at the time living in a small town back in the 90s riding my bike with my friends as we always done as usual. Often we'd cycle around the long stretch of country roads we'd done it so often we'd knew the route off by heart it was like we had a built in satnav in our minds. 

We'd go full circle very often using various routes to get there there was one path we'd never take I'd always asked my friends to go there and since I'd moved into the area years before they'd always tell me it was a dead end or told me going there was a waste of time. 

One day I decided to take the lead I told them I was going there wether they liked it or not I even dared them to go naturally they always seemed scared I never knew why but it's like a warning that's been passed down for generations in this area to never go down that route. I was stupid enough to try it. 

I cycled onwards my friend's appeared to follow me but upon looking back they weren't there. The trees we're closed in so it had a dark shade to it that got worse as I went on. There was a very rare smell coming from the stream of water that I'd cycled over that stung the inside of my nose and made my eyes water I remember being splashed on not realising the puddle beneath me which appeared to be steaming up into the air.

 After a few seconds passed I became scared and shouted on my friends to see where they went. I heard nothing not a thing from them. In fact I'd heard absolutely nothing at all no birds no sound from wind hitting the trees no insects nothing just dead radio silence. 

 I turned back and cycled on to find my friends but peddling suddenly felt difficult it was like cycling against a hard wind that wasn't there. I tried shouting my friends but felt like my voice had disappeared. I cycled on for what felt like hours even though it took mere seconds to get where I was in the first place. My wheels felt like they were sinking into the ground and my eyes became blurred I stopped for some air but felt like the air was compressing itself around me. I was constantly taking in deep breaths in a panic having no clue what was happening to me. I felt Instensly sleepy but kept fighting it off everytime my eye's were about to shut I'd purposely try to breathe harder. It'd gotten darker I decided to rest against a tree it felt like I was being pushed to one side as that side of my body felt heavy. 

Now I could finally hear some noise just the sound of the wind hissing through the tree leaves and then the whispers constant whispers I couldn't make them out but finally I shut my eye's my breathing became harder and my eye's couldn't see my hand infront of me I felt like a slow relaxed sinking feeling I was no longer forcing myself to breath. I had a dream I was sinking under water just drifting calmly staring at the sky above me looking at the moonlight shining down and then in the dream I'd realised I was drowning and swam rapidly to get back to the surface just as I saw myself rise in the dream. I woke up making a huge gasp for air. I coughed up what looked a Mismash of vomit and Mucous I could see a light shining in the darkness so I got up with my bike using it as a walking aide and started to follow it I felt better than before but very drowsy and Groggy. 

Upon following the light I could hear my friends shouting on me I absolutely cannot describe the relief just to see them again and I couldn't have ran any faster despite my legs feeling like Jelly. I was treated in hospital they didn't know exactly what was wrong with me but suspected some form of poisoning that my immune system had managed to fend off. I have never returned to that part of the woods ever again in fact all these years later the entire area has now became a housing estate with Houses replacing the entire forest.
