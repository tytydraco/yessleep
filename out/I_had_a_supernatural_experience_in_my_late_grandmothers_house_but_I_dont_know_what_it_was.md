I’m 20 years old and I live in Porto Alegre, Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil. I was born here, but I lived in another state, Santa Catarina, from the age of 8 until I was 17. My mother and stepfather are Evangelical Christians, my paternal aunts are Spiritists, my father and grandmother were Catholics, and my boyfriend is a practitioner of Umbanda. So I’ve always been surrounded by different religions, beliefs, and forms of faith.

I was never really someone who strongly believed in these things, but I’m not skeptical in an intolerant way either. I respect every religion, belief, and faith. If someone is Hindu, for example, I respect them. Even if I don’t understand their beliefs, I usually try to learn about them and ask questions while being as respectful and open-minded as possible.

I would describe myself as agnostic and Stoic, based on the way I try to live my life and see the world around me.

My grandmother's first name was Santa. Before she passed away, I would always spend my summer vacations at her house in Balneário Camboriú, where I lived in Santa Catarina before moving to Blumenau at 16. I would stay with her, help her as much as I could, talk with her, and she would take care of me during that time. She was the kind of grandmother I considered a second mother.

Earlier this year, between January and March, my grandmother became seriously ill. She went into a coma and unfortunately passed away. I still miss her. I know she was probably suffering and in a lot of pain after spending so much time in the hospital. At one point, she believed she was being tortured there and desperately wanted to leave.

Before she died, I had a dream about her.

In the dream, I was living in Porto Alegre, but the city looked different. It was nighttime, and the streets were much darker than they normally are. I arrived at an apartment that doesn't actually exist, and my grandmother was sitting on the couch waiting for me in a dimly lit room. The only light was an orange hallway light.

When I opened the living room window, it was raining extremely heavily outside, but I could still see a full moon. It looked enormous, maybe two or three times larger than the moon normally appears in the sky.

Then I woke up.

Later that night, I received the message that my grandmother had passed away. I cried a lot and had a breakdown because of it.

After the funeral, I spent about two weeks at my father's place. His house and my grandmother's house are on the same property, and since my father's house only has one bedroom while my grandmother's house had a room that was basically reserved for me, I stayed there.

During those two weeks, I didn't really have any problems in the house, except for some nightmares and strange dreams that I don't feel comfortable describing because they were absurd and didn't follow anything I consciously believe in. They involved family members, friends, the city where I currently live, death, violence, dangerous places, and many other things.

After I returned to my mother's house, where I live, those dreams completely stopped.

Now, this is where the strange experience happened.

When I went to visit my father, I originally planned to stay for three or four days, but ended up staying for about another week and a half. During that time, I started noticing that something about the house felt strange.

Outside, it was hot, but inside the house it was extremely cold. Normally, when it's hot outside, the house becomes stuffy and uncomfortable. This wasn't that kind of cold. It was cold enough that I actually needed to wear a hoodie.

At night, before going to sleep, I often felt as if someone else was inside the house. Even when my father was there, he would normally call me or make some kind of noise, so this feeling was different.

One night, I heard the bedroom window banging.

I knew it wasn't the wind because, in my mind, the sound didn't seem irregular or random. It was strong and rhythmic, almost like someone was deliberately knocking on it.

I ignored it and went to sleep.

That weekend, I went to see my boyfriend and stayed at his house. He noticed that something was wrong with me. He said I seemed weaker, as if my spirit was somehow broken.

I didn't think much of it at the time.

Then came my last night there.

I was drawing and watching a video before going to bed. Once I went to sleep, I heard the window banging again.

It stopped for a few seconds.

I turned over in bed.

And then I heard a scream from outside:

**“THEEOOO!”**

Someone was screaming my name.

It was a voice I had never heard before.

After that, I packed my things and went straight back home.

My boyfriend believes it was a bad spirit inside the house. My aunts, and I myself, think it could have been sleep paralysis.

I honestly don't know what to believe or what I can confidently consider the truth.

I've never really rejected the possibility of supernatural things, but I've also never experienced anything like this before.

What made the experience even stranger was that, looking back, I genuinely felt terrible while I was in that house. Once I returned home, I immediately started feeling better. I felt like myself again, rather than feeling like a “hostage” in that house. That's the closest word I can find to describe how I felt there.

So I wanted to ask you guys what you think this could have been.

I didn't see anything. I only heard things and felt a presence.

I'm trying to remain as open-minded as possible about what happened and accept it as something that happened to me, even if I don't have an explanation for it.

After reading everything, what do you think happened? What do you believe could explain this experience?
