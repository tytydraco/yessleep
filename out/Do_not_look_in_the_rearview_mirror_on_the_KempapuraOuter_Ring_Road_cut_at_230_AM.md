This happened last week, today only I got the courage to write about this.

That week i  took a  late night shift. On thursday night around 2:30 AM, I wrapped up and took my usual route home on my scooter. Anyone who knows the Kempapura area knows that once you turn off the main flyover grid and get into the back lanes leading toward Coffee Board Layout, the streetlights become incredibly patchy. One second it’s bright, the next you’re driving through absolute pitch black.

Last night, there was a thick, unnatural mist rolling off the nearby lake area. The air felt freezing way colder than a normal Bangalore night. As I slowed down for one of those massive, unmarked speed bumps near the dark stretch by the graveyard wall, my scooter’s headlight flickered. That’s when I felt the weight. The back suspension of my scooter suddenly compressed down hard, exactly as if someone had smoothly stepped onto the pillion seat behind me. The engine groaned under the sudden strain.

My stomach dropped. I assumed it was a local thief or a drunk guy jumping on the back at a slow point. I opened my mouth to yell, "Hey! Get off!" . The sound never came out.

But then the smell hit me.

It wasn't alcohol. It smelled like a wet lake mud, rotting leaves, and something copper like.

In my left rearview mirror, I caught a glimpse of what was standing behind my scooter.

It was wearing a faded, drenched salwar i think. But it wasn't a person. Its neck was bent at a completely impossible, sharp 90 degree angle, resting flat against its own shoulder. I couldn't see eyes just two hollow, oily black pits staring directly into the mirror reflection, right at me.

I completely froze. Every instinct told me that if I stopped the scooter, or if I turned my head around to look at it directly instead of through the mirror, I would die.

I twisted the throttle as hard as it would go. The scooter screamed, dragging the heavy, dead weight behind me. I didn't look back. I stared straight ahead, tears streaming down my face from the wind and pure terror, repeating every prayer I knew.

As soon as I tore past the gate of a well lit apartment complex with an active security guard, the weight suddenly lifted. The scooter shot forward with a jerk.

I skidded to a halt under the bright LED streetlights, gasping for air. I looked behind me. The seat was empty.

But when I got home and finally had the courage to inspect the scooter, my blood ran cold. The back grab-rail was coated in a wet, black slime. And pressed neatly into the dust on the rear mudguard were three long, drag marks from fingernails.

I am never taking that route again. If you drive through Kempapura late at night, keep your eyes on the road. And whatever you do, do not check your mirrors. Maybe it's just a sleep deprived images or it maybe real. Has anybody felt the same..
