In the early 2000s, when I was only 12 or 13 years old, my family moved into an old residential duplex on Myrtle Street in Redlands, California.  
If you aren’t familiar with what a duplex is, it is a multi story home usually split into separate units— each separate story being its own unit.  
Ours was the very bottom unit.  
Anyways— Redlands already has a reputation for having a lot of historic, haunted places, so old houses with creepy vibes aren't exactly rare there. Still, nothing prepared me for what we experienced inside that place. Looking back, that house had some of the heaviest, darkest energy I have ever felt.  
It didn’t take long after moving in for things to start feeling wrong. The atmosphere inside was oppressive, and because I was just a kid, I kept everything I was experiencing to myself. I didn't actually talk to my family about any of it until years later, when I was 16—and that’s when I realized I wasn't the only one dealing with it.  
When we finally talked about it, my aunt—who lived with us at the time—told me about the mimic. She said that while my little sister and I were away at school during the day, she would hear only our voices calling her name inside the duplex, nothing else. It wasn't muffled sounds coming through the shared wall from the neighbors; it sounded like me and my sister calling out to her inside our own unit when the house was completely empty.  
Looking back, I realized the mimic had targeted me too. I remember clearly hearing my aunt’s voice calling me by the nickname she always used for me: "Tweety," like Tweety Bird. I walked toward the sound thinking it was her, only to step into a completely empty bedroom.  
For me, the activity was also heavily visual. There were shadows that seemed to dance across the walls whenever light hit them, moving in ways that didn't match anything in the room. Worse than that was a dark shadow person that seemed tied to specific spots in the house. I saw it constantly in the living room, right near the outer window facing the street, and I also saw it in my back bedroom more than once. It wasn't a trick of the light from passing cars or trees outside—it was a distinct, pitch-black human shape.

The longer we stayed, the heavier the negative energy got. It felt like the house itself was draining everyone in it. Not long after moving into that duplex, my family completely fell apart.  
My mother, and my Aunt got into drugs and my father divorced my mother, took my sister with him and left.  
I stayed behind because I felt bad for my mom and didn’t want to leave her alone.  
Shortly after that, things got much worse…

The single most terrifying moment I experienced there happened when I saw that shadow figure sitting in a chair with the back of its head facing me. Thinking it was my mom, I walked right up to it—and as I got close, it vanished into thin air.  
I also distinctly remember another incident that happened in my back bedroom, whereas stated earlier, was a hotspot for the shadow and the mimic.  
I was sitting on my bedroom floor playing Xbox, when I felt a very irritating itch.  
Soon after trying to scratch the itch, it started to turn into a hot painful burn that felt like needles going into my skin.  
I wanted to see what was going on, so I went into my bathroom and looked at my back in the mirror, that’s when I saw three scratches that started from the middle of my shoulder blades and extended all the way down to right above my left hip.  
I was so terrified, but I did not say anything because I knew my mother would be blamed for it.  
I thought that the cops would be called and I would be taken away from my mother, and she would be accused of physical abuse.  
My mother never physically abused me, but she mentally and emotionally abused me.  
I think that is why I was attacked that night, my spirits were low, and I was in a deep depression without my sister.  
After that, I started to see shadow figures and shapes on my walls nightly.  
It was about a month later when my father came back for me after being granted full custody through the courts.

My mother eventually moved out, and I spent a few years in the high desert.  
Eventually, when I was old enough, I returned to Redlands to live with my mother again.  
What always stuck with me, though, was what happened in the years after we left. When I was older—around ages 15, 16, and 17—I used to walk down Myrtle Street regularly over those three years.  
I was attending the local high school there, and I would walk to and from school every day—Myrtle street was a great short cut to get to school faster.  
During that entire stretch, I watched the bottom portion of that duplex go through numerous tenants. Over and over again, someone would move in and then move right back out soon after. Nobody ever stayed long—it was pure revolving-door stuff.  
Eventually, the duplex was torn down altogether, and today it’s just an empty lot. I always wonder what those other tenants experienced during their brief stays in that bottom unit, or what originally happened on that property to leave behind so much dark energy.

There are a few different rumors about the city of Redlands, which is a city in Southern California, California.

One of the most popular theories is that that duplex was built on native burial ground.  
The native tribe that once owned the area, were known as the Serrano tribe.

Another theory is that Redlands was a hotspot during the “Satanic Panic” which lasted from the early 1980s all the way until the early 1990s.

Whatever happened there was dark— because I have never experienced anything like that in my life.  
Still to this day, I do experience some paranormal activity, but it is nothing compared to those years spent in that duplex.

Word of advice for everyone, if you experience, paranormal activity, even as a child, speak up.  
Because take it from me— it does get worse..
