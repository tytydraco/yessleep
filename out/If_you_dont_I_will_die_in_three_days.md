I don't remember some of the details since it caused me to want to forget it all, but the story I can piece together is that I worked at a nine to five job at night as a night guard for an abandoned building because I was out of a job and needed the money. I don't remember why I took the job, and you must be thinking why this is important, but trust me, it is. 

It didn't get me all the money I needed, but hey, it was enough for me and my girlfriend, Kat, to live together while her job paid for food and other amenities around the house. Although it wasn't the best, it was what we had and what we had is what we needed. So one day, when she asked if I could get a better job I was surprised. 

"But... I thought we were happy just the way it is." I told her with doubt and questions already forming on my face. 

"Yes, but..." She started to fiddle with her thumbs while saying this, which is never a good sign. 

*Ether this will be an argument* *or I will be felling really bad for all this. Both, I do NOT want to happen.* I thought to myself in the split second I had before she blurted, 

"If you don't I will die in three days."

I was absolutely was flabbergasted and fifty other emotions at the same time, and I defiantly had that all over my face and probably on my body too, because she stepped closer and took my hands.

"I know you're surprised and very confused, so I'm going to have you sit down for this, Jen." She pulled the chairs from the dining room table we were cleaning off because our dog somehow shitted on it.

 "Once upon a time, a little girl's ancestors," She pointed to herself. "Worshiped the devil. They would sacrifice people to try to get him on their side when they died so they would have less punishment in the after life. So when the devil told them that when only the daughters of the worshipers would satisfy him, they obeyed. They only kept the sons. And for awhile, every thing was fine. The devil got what he wanted, and so did the worshipers when they died. But when all the worshipers died off, he put a curse on the house they met at the most. Any descendent that's a girl from the worshipers, and has a connection to the house usually dies within seven to nine months." She let that sink in for me.

"Its been almost nine months." I say quietly.

"Yes." She puts on a sad smile. "Exactly."

She didn't really sound sad though, just, tired. Like she wants this all to be over and to rest after she hasn't for a while.

"Are you sure that quitting my job will stop the..." I ponder what I'm going to say for a second. "Curse?"

She gives me another small sad smile and that gives me all I need for an answer.

I quit my job, but it was to late. She died in her sleep, but the doctors don't know why. They said she was healthy and young enough were that doesn't happen often at all. I play dumb and let the doctors think what they want but I know the truth. The devil wanted her soul. so he took it.
