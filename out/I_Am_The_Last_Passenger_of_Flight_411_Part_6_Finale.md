Hey y’all, it’s been a while.

I wish with all my heart that I could talk about everything that had happened over the past couple weeks. Tell you all about what I’d learned.

But I can’t.

At least, not meaningfully. Not without putting others in danger. To sum it up though, heres the big thing:

I’m now a passenger on Flight 411. The last passenger. 

The route hasn’t flown since the incident, and wont fly again after it takes me to, well, wherever.

The old airbase is abandoned. Even the Pho shop is gone. They wouldn’t tell me much but they did tell me that we’re going somewhere new, somewhere safer for me. 

They said that if I made too much “noise” that the people who took Flight 411 would find me and then take me too. They said that moving me to this new location was the only way to keep myself safe. Of course I protested, but they didn’t really give me much of a choice. 

Or rather, turns out I never had a choice at all.

As for who “they” are, I don’t really know. They aren’t the airline, they aren’t Feds, they’re just, “them” as far as I know, the ones who chartered the flight from the airline. 

I’m sitting on the plane as I’m typing this all out. Trying to clear my head, trying to shout one last thing into the void before I leave it all behind. Not that I’d be leaving anything behind anyway, never had anything to begin with. 

They’d been following me, shadowing me, corralling me ever since my first post. They thought I’d been taken too but then they came across my post, knew it was me. It helped them figure out who took Flight 411 and why, somehow. 

But the how, I still can’t wrap my head around it. They found the wreckage, but not the people? How the fuck does that work? 

But out of all the passengers on that flight, they said to me I’m the only one that’s still alive. They said all the other passengers were dead. Not the crew, but the passengers. Said I was lucky, said that I had missed it by two weeks. But I didn’t? I was on that flight, I was the Captain for fuck’s sake. What did I miss by two weeks?

I was the Captain, wasn’t I?

Honestly I still don’t understand most of what they’re talking about, but they promise me that if I just do what they say that I’ll be fine. Maybe its better that way, better that I don’t understand.

When it was time to leave, a whole bunch of strange people showed up to my house. A few of them were in suits, federal government types, but most of them were just dressed in plain clothes. Though I could tell they weren’t from around here. The way they dressed. Shorts and tacky aloha shirts, looked more like the tourists that saturated Waikiki. 

What was really strange, though, was that all the people in suits looked almost identical to one another. Maybe they were related?

They told me that I’d be coming with them, that we’d be moving. Tonight. That I should pack whatever I wanted into a backpack. Said the rest was going to be destroyed. They wouldn’t elaborate on that. 

So I packed my laptop, some clothes that I’d grown fond of, and my Captain’s uniform. Though I’d never wear it again, it was probably the most important thing to me I could imagine. 

It’s the end of the road, as it would seem. Even now as I type this out I can’t really fully comprehend just how far removed I am from the life I once knew, like I’d just been a whole different person since I woke up on that beach. I guess I am in a certain sense. 

They got new flight crew just for this flight. Don’t recognize the captain nor the first officer, maybe company pulled them from a hub on the east coast. They know what happened to the last crew. They don’t seem worried. Not that they should be, I mean…

It’s not like lightning strikes twice, right?
