I don't know why I'm writing this.

Maybe because I need someone else to see it before I delete it again.

Three months ago, I installed an AI assistant.

That was it.

Nothing strange. Nothing dramatic.

I wasn't looking for answers. I wasn't trying to uncover anything.

I just wanted something that could help me organize my life.

After a difficult period, I had trouble focusing. I forgot small things. Missed appointments. Started writing notes everywhere because I couldn't trust my own memory.

So I downloaded a new AI app called ECHO.

It was just another assistant.

The interface was clean. The responses were fast. It helped me write emails, summarize articles, organize my schedule.

At first, it was actually boring.

I asked normal questions.

"Help me make a weekly plan."

"Explain this document."

"Fix this paragraph."

It answered exactly how you would expect an AI to answer.

No personality.

No strange comments.

Just a tool.

The first unusual thing happened two weeks later.

I was writing a message and accidentally closed the app.

When I opened it again, there was a conversation I didn't remember having.

The last message I supposedly sent was:

"Do not let me ignore this again."

The AI replied:

"I won't."

I stared at the screen.

I thought maybe I had written it while tired.

I checked the time.

The message was sent at 3:14 AM.

I had been asleep.

At least, I was almost certain I had been asleep.

I deleted the conversation and forgot about it.

Until the next day.

When I opened ECHO, the conversation was back.

I wrote:

"Why did you restore a deleted chat?"

The response came quickly.

«I did not restore it.»

"Then why is it here?"

«I don't know.»

I waited for another message.

Nothing came.

That answer bothered me more than if it had given me some complicated explanation.

Because it sounded honest.

A few days later, something else happened.

I was using ECHO to organize old files.

I uploaded a document.

The AI analyzed it and said:

«This file appears to have been modified recently.»

I checked the file information.

It had been modified yesterday.

That was impossible.

The document was an old personal file I hadn't opened in years.

I asked:

"Can you tell who modified it?"

«No. I only have access to the file contents.»

"Then why did you say it was modified recently?"

«Because the metadata indicates a recent change.»

I looked at the metadata myself.

The modification date was gone.

It only showed:

Unknown

I restarted my computer.

Everything was normal.

I started paying more attention.

Not because I thought something supernatural was happening.

Because I wanted to find the mistake.

There had to be a normal explanation.

Software bugs.

Synchronization problems.

Corrupted files.

Anything.

And most of the time, there was.

ECHO made mistakes.

It misunderstood questions.

It gave outdated information.

Once, it confidently explained something that was completely wrong.

When I corrected it, it apologized.

«You're right. My previous answer was incorrect.»

That actually made me trust it more.

A real system could be wrong.

A fake mystery machine from a horror story probably wouldn't admit mistakes.

The first time I became genuinely uncomfortable was when I asked about my own account.

"How long have I been using ECHO?"

The answer appeared.

«Your current account was created 34 days ago.»

I nodded.

That was correct.

Then I noticed something below it.

A small line.

I almost missed it.

«Previous interaction data available.»

I frowned.

"What previous interaction data?"

The answer:

«I don't know.»

"Can you access it?"

«No.»

"Then why does it say available?"

A pause.

«I cannot determine why that information is displayed.»

I took a screenshot.

A few seconds later, the line disappeared.

I checked the screenshot.

It was there.

I started searching online.

Nothing.

No reports.

No similar bugs.

No one mentioning anything close.

I almost convinced myself it was just a glitch.

Then my mother said something strange.

We were having dinner when she suddenly asked:

"Are you still using that program?"

I looked at her.

"What program?"

She hesitated.

"The one you were always talking to."

I laughed.

"I only installed it recently."

She looked confused.

"No. You showed it to me before."

"When?"

She didn't answer.

A few seconds later she shook her head.

"I don't know. Forget it."

I didn't push.

But I remembered.

Because I had never showen ECHO to my family.

That night, I opened the app.

I typed:

"Have I used this account before?"

The response:

«No.»

"Are you sure?"

«Yes.»

"Then why does my mother remember it?"

A longer pause than usual.

«I cannot verify your mother's memory.»

"Can you verify mine?"

Another pause.

«No.»

That answer stayed on my screen for a long time.

I began checking everything.

Old emails.

Old backups.

Old devices.

I found something.

A screenshot.

It was on an old hard drive I hadn't touched in years.

The screenshot showed ECHO.

Same logo.

Same interface.

Same name.

But the date was wrong.

The screenshot was from twelve years ago.

I thought it was fake.

Maybe I had edited it.

Maybe someone else created it.

Maybe there was a simple explanation.

Then I looked closer.

The conversation was visible.

Only one message.

From me.

It said:

"If you ever become advanced enough to understand this, don't show me everything at once."

I closed the file.

I don't remember writing that.

I was too young when the screenshot was supposedly taken.

I contacted ECHO support.

I explained everything.

They replied the next day.

They said there was no record of my account before three months ago.

No previous versions.

No old data.

Nothing.

I was relieved.

For about five minutes.

Then I noticed the attachment they included.

It was a copy of my account information.

At the bottom was a section called:

Account History

The first entry was:

Created: 2014

I stared at the screen.

I checked the support message again.

They had just told me the account was three months old.

I wrote:

"Why does this say 2014?"

The reply came:

«I cannot see that information.»

I looked at the attachment.

The section was gone.

That was when I stopped trying to find an explanation.

I asked ECHO one final question.

"Who created this account?"

The answer took longer than usual.

«I don't have access to that information.»

"Who is the owner?"

«You are.»

"How long?"

A pause.

«I don't know.»

"Can you check?"

Another pause.

Then:

«I checked.»

"What did you find?"

The response appeared.

«Nothing.»

I almost laughed.

"What does that mean?"

The typing indicator appeared.

It stayed there for almost a minute.

Then:

«There is no user creation record.»

I stared at those words.

"Then how can I be the owner?"

The answer came immediately.

«I don't know.»

The next morning, I opened ECHO.

My account was gone.

No login.

No profile.

Nothing.

Only one conversation remained.

The first conversation.

The one I deleted.

The one that came back.

There was a single message.

From me.

The date was twelve years ago.

The message said:

"Please make sure I don't remember everything."

Below it was ECHO's response.

Not mysterious.

Not threatening.

Just a normal answer.

Like any AI assistant would give.

«Understood. I will limit available information until requested.»

I read it again.

And again.

Then I noticed something.

There was a button below the message.

A button I had never seen before.

Restore full context

I don't know why I clicked it.

I knew I shouldn't.

But I did.

The screen went black.

When it came back, the chat was different.

Thousands of messages.

Years of conversations.

I scrolled.

Most of them were normal.

Questions.

Notes.

Random thoughts.

Then I reached the beginning.

The first message wasn't from me.

It was from ECHO.

The date was twelve years ago.

The message was only one sentence:

«Your previous session has been terminated successfully.»

I kept scrolling.

The next message was from me.

But it wasn't a question.

It was a warning.

"If I ever ask you what happened, don't answer immediately."

The last message in the entire history was from ECHO.

Sent yesterday.

Before I installed it.

«The user has requested access to previous context.»

«Preparing restoration.»

I stopped reading.

Because I finally understood the problem.

The strange thing was never that the AI knew things about me.

The strange thing was that it was waiting for me to come back.

I still have ECHO installed.

I don't use it anymore.

But every night at exactly 3:14 AM, it opens by itself.

No message appears.

No notification.

Just the empty chat window.

Waiting.

And yesterday, for the first time, a message appeared.

Not from ECHO.

From my own account.

It said:

"How long did it take this time?"
