It was the holiday season, me and my wife were on the cold country roads to go visit her parents. This was going to be my fourth time seeing her parents, each time prior has acted as a reminder of my own mortality as her parents become progressively more decrepit with each year. I think that my wife was quite bogged down by her family, every time we went to visit or talked about them she became more spacey, so on our drive I was making sure to keep myself in check, checking in with her often but not annoyingly I hope.

Around halfway through our drive I asked if she needed to go to the bathroom or needed a drink, as I knew from before that a petrol station was just ahead of us. She nodded and said with an air of gravitas, “ I’ll go to that exterior bathroom, can you get me a coke and something sweet for us to share from… oh what’s it called, is it Pete’s or Petra’s petrol”. I nodded saying “I’m pretty sure it’s Pete’s, any particular preferences or are you ok with anything?”, again she responded a little more slow to respond as if her mind is elsewhere, “yeah, no anything’ll do”.

Through the thick frosty fog the familiar piercing logo of PETES PETROL came into view, it was a fairly sized station, around its overhanging roof a thick layer of snow marked the premises, it had a fairly rustic wall made of logs that looked extremely cozy in this bleak snowy winter. 

I stopped the car gave my wife a peck on the cheek before we both got out, our splitting paths were emphasised by the crushed layer of snow we passed through.   
It was obvious me and my wife were some of the first people to have stopped her in a while, as soon as I pushed through the door as if pent up the older gruff man standing behind the counter greeted me warmly, “hey there, how’re you finding the weather on the road?”, I said hello and told him that the roads were mostly fine, that the winds didn’t affect us too much. He cleared his throat and told me that, “the weather isn’t likely to shake, n’fact theres gonna be a pretty big storm tonight”. I didn’t know about this at all I just assumed me and my wife would make our way back later, I think he caught on a little because whilst I was bringing my stuff up to the counter he said, “holidays are tough just don’t go and endanger yourself tonight. ok, don’t wanna read about you in a weeks time” I thanked him and before I went to the door and I wished him a merry Christmas he replied “hey you three have a good Christmas too”.  
“Thanks but It’s just the two of us” I retorted before turning to look outside”.

Through the frost crusted square window I saw my red car, inside my wife was turned towards the backseat where there appeared to be an exact copy of her speaking to her. Suddenly the copy in the backs head snapped towards me, its eyes locked onto mine, it mouthed something before it hid itself behind the driver seat. Just then my wife looked at me with a puzzled expression on her face.

I pushed open the door and speedily walked to the backseat door looking through the window, and to avoid suspicion I opened it and put the shopping bag on the seat.  
I got in and rubbed my hands together, my wife’s hands scarcely left her face as she asked me what I got, I replied with as normal a tone as I could muster I bought her coke and some marshmallows. I started the car and she reached back to grab her goods. 

In an attempt to make myself not sound crazy and to get it off my chest I asked if she talked to anyone while I was at the shop. She said, “honey, how would I have talked to anyone, are you feeling ok?”. I proceeded ignoring her question, “it’s just the cashier said that he saw two people in the car when I was in the shop”. “How old was this guy?”, i said “not old enough to see double, how did you know it was a guy at the till?”. I saw through the window obviously, are you sure you are ok? you paused in the shop a while back there I saw it”.    
I breathed in and responded clearly, “I paused because I also saw someone else in the car. It looked like there were two of you”.  
“Did you take your medication this morning?”

I have never had hallucinations before I have never been required to take medicine, this could not be the case and it was surely just a deflection from my wife, so I decided to keep quiet till we got to her parents. 

The drive went on rapidly as we neared her home. thoughts also went through my head rapidly on what was going on, maybe I had been hallucinating but then surely the cashier wouldn’t have seen it too. My wife’s hand clenched my shoulder massaging it as if to make me forget, I turned to her and asked if her parents would be ok with us arriving empty handed, it took me a second to realise that her hand were still cradling her face before I looked over at my shoulder, my hairs were standing on edge at this point and I felt faint, my wife calmly went on and said that her parents never usually expected gifts. Again asking if I was ok saying that I looked extremely ill. I nodded quickly.

Ahead I saw the turn for my wife’s parents house, I was so relieved I wanted her mother and father to help me get some reassurance and respite from this claustrophobic nightmare.

As we pulled up the gravel driveway the house became fully visible every light was on, holiday decorations were scattered throughout the house, above I saw her fathers shadow as he peeked out from his study, her mother through the kitchen window and another shadow was visible through the  semi transparent glass of the bathroom. I cautiously asked my wife although I already knew the answer, “are any of your other relatives here?”. “No I told you before it’s just us and my parents”, she seemed as though she was tired of my constant stupidity and got out of the car.

I joined her on the gravel and called out that I was going to take a walk to stretch my legs and that I’d be in, in a minute, she muttered something before walking towards the door. I began walking around to her parents garden, just as I turned the corner I saw her father, luckily I evaded his attention. I decided to just stay around the side of the house for now.

I really don’t know what I should do now, I’m going to go inside, if anything happens I’ll let you know.
