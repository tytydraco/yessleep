Hello all, it’s been a few weeks since my last update…in that time a lot has happened, and I’m still trying to wrap my head around it all. Guess I'll start from the beginning.

I took the plunge and decided to investigate the mysterious room in my apartment, but after pulling out my phone to record everything stopped. I couldn’t swipe up, couldn’t power it off, all I could do was stare at the frozen Home Screen. The faces of me and my girlfriend etched deeper into my brain the longer I stared.

I wondered if there was something in the walls blocking the phone from being able to function? I’m not a tech guy so I have no idea, but what I do know is that I was on my own. I traveled back up the stairs, and voile the phone worked again. I couldn’t take any chances of getting lost though, and taking an old roll of yarn from the closet, a pair of scissors, an old flashlight, and a roll of duct tape, I prepared myself for an expedition.

I figured I wouldn’t be long, an hour or two at the most and try to find anything as to how this room exists. After much time spent hesitating, I finally took the first step. The other. And another. And another.

Before long I could feel the familiar splashing encompassing my foot, the lukewarm sludge and liquid grappling to my feet like a parasite. It was disgusting, but I blocked it from my mind as I clicked the flashlight on. 

The light bounced from wall to floor, the water reflecting it all over the room. It was beautiful in a strange way, it captured my gaze like a deer in headlights, but before long I steadied myself and pulled on the yarn. Placing the edge against the wall I taped it down well, pulling on it to make sure it wouldn’t break free. With my mind at ease I unrolled the yarn slowly but surely, following along the walls.

They were dingy, old, almost like an abandoned school might look years after it’s been shut down. There wasn’t any graffiti or anything, just weird stains and cracks along it like it had been there for decades, maybe even a century. 

My feet splashed against the water, kicking droplets everywhere, even onto my pants to get them wet as well. However, after what seemed like a few minutes, I noticed that the wall had ended, and before me I was at a four-way crossroads. 

Dead ahead lay a sloped floor, ramping upward and away from the water. To my left was another flooded hallway, that seemed to lead into more blacky abyss. And finally, to my right, lay a short hallway that led to a gaping hole. Water flowed into it as if it were a waterfall, and yet the water never lowered once, it was the strangest thing I’d seen inside there yet.

I decided to go straight, I was tired of the water and I didn’t want to find out what was down that hole. As I walked along the incline my feet dripped water onto the floor, I had to make sure to watch my step as the floor was made of tile. 

Finally though I made it to the top, pointing the light straight ahead to see there was a door at the end of the walk. Excitedly I placed a piece of string down and taped it, unrolling the yarn some more and speedwalking as fast as I could over to the door. 

To my dismay, however, it was locked. I jiggled the old and rusted knob, but with no luck. As I tried to think of what I could do to get the door open, I heard a noise coming from the other side of it. Curiously I placed my ear against the door…it was groaning.

I couldn’t even tell what it was…a human? An animal? Some kind of beast? I didn’t have long to ponder though, before I knew it I could hear pounding coming from the other side. My ears began to ring, falling backward on the floor and staring straight up at the door before me.

The pounding became heavier now, as if any second the hinges on the door might rip off and unveil whatever is behind it. There wasn’t time to waste now, and I got my ass up off the floor and ran like I had never ran before. While trying to run though I slipped on some of the water, tumbling and sliding down the ramp until crashing into the water below. 

I pushed myself off of the ground, however…I hadn’t noticed it before, but underneath wasn’t tile like it was up there…it was grass. Tall grass that crept through and in between my fingers, like an unforgettable feeling I hadn’t felt in so long.

It was then I heard it again, one final pound as the sound of hinges ripping echoed through the hallway. I can’t tell what’s worse now, the stomping that followed, or the fact I heard it say something.

”HUNGER.”

It was a loud, booming voice that shredded my ears so loudly that I could only think of one thing…surviving. I ran in whatever direction I was facing, water splashing behind me as I heard the sounds of grunting and stomping following suit. I can’t tell how long I was running for now, but I sure as shit know it was longer than however long it took me to get there. 

At some point I turned a corner, even though I hadn’t turned any corners before, and my string of yarn continued. I didn’t have any time to question though, I just continued following the string until finally I was back at the stairs. My feet slipped, and I hit my chin against the wooden stairs.

Everything was blurry, my mind was disoriented, and still I could hear the grunting clear as day. It was hungry, it needed something to eat, and I was the perfect candidate. 

I used every ounce of strength I had left, gripping the stairs for my dear life and pulling myself to the top. As I heard it begin its venture upon the stairs I slammed the door shut behind me. Placing my body against it and bracing for impact.

I waited there for quite some time, my breathing even heavier than after any workout I'd ever done before. Yet nothing ever came. I waited, and waited, but it seemed I was alone now. Maybe whatever creature was chasing me decided I wasn’t worth it, or maybe I had just gotten lucky. I didn’t care though, I picked myself up off the floor and saw that I was covered in filth.

Black stained my clothes, water dripped from my hair, and I smelled of mildew. I didn’t even want to know what it was, and instead I walked out toward the living room….which is where I saw her.

Emily. Sitting on the couch. Staring straight ahead at me.

”Where were you?”

”The room, it appeared again, I took some things down there and-“

”Oh my god, Zeke…look at you!”

That’s when she noticed I was covered in stains and dripping like a wet dog. She stood up frantically, her eyes wide and her hands clearly shaking.

”Look, I know, I look like shit, but right now that’s not the point. The point is that the room appeared again, and I actually went exploring in it.”

”You-…you what?”

”That’s why I look like this, something was down there and it chased me, but thankfully I was smart enough and used some of our old yarn string to find my way back!”

I tried to go in to hug her. Gods I missed her face…she was like an angel standing there in the light. But as I approached her she pulled away, visibly concerned and in distress.

”Zeke, what the hell are you talking about? We both saw it, that room isn’t real!”

”It is, it is real Emily! I can show you right now!!”

I reached out and grabbed her hand, she was too surprised to pull away now, and I walked us both into the bedroom where we stopped just shy of the door. She stood back, not sure what to expect, and without any fear I opened the door once more. 

But there was nothing left now. Multiple pieces of cut up yarn string laid on the ground, strewn about as if they had been bitten and pulled apart.

Before I could get a word out I noticed she was gone, I poked my head into the hallway and noticed her pulling out bags from another room.

”Emily, what are you doing?”

“What does it look like I’m doing…”

“Emily, I swear, I was JUST in there!”

She didn’t respond, she just kept placing her things by the front door. As I tried to walk over to her, to try and show my clothes as proof she pushed me away.

”I can take the lying, the absences, hell I can even understand if you just want to see someone else, but this? I don’t know what’s going on, and I tried getting you help, but you won’t fucking listen. You NEVER do! I’m done, Zeke. I’m done with you, the stories, and all of the other bullshit!”

She didn’t even let me respond, she just slammed the door behind her and walked away…and that was the last time I saw her.

That was only a day after writing this, and in the past few weeks I haven’t been great. In my sadness I went searching for our pets, to maybe feel some sort of comfort, but she had taken them with her.

I was angry, bitter, and prideful, so I began tearing apart the entire apartment. I yanked the tv and cords out of the wall, throwing it down to the ground. Next came the fridge, yanking out all of the shells as glass jars and containers spilled onto the tiled floor. The pets room, the bathroom, and finally our bedroom were all torn apart in my rage.

After the rage that consumed me had worn off, I sat in the wreckage and drunk myself into a stopper. For the last few weeks I’ve been in and out of consciousness…

First it was just drinking, then I started smoking a bit of weed while drinking. Then a buddy of mine, who I’ll call Max, offered me some Molly. From there I spiraled out of control, doing anything I could to get my mind off of everything.

At one point I even started talking to this girl at the bar, we hit it off really well and it seemed as if things were looking up for me. There was one day, during this past week, where I hadn’t even thought about the room. It was peaceful.

Then one night I invited her over, obviously I made sure to clean up the apartment beforehand, but it didn’t matter much as we didn’t waste any time getting to it. It was passionate, romantic even, everything I could ever hope for. 

The apartment was slightly cold, as the months have started to drone on as winter is coming. However, our bodies were warm to the touching, holding each other so close as we danced through the night. Finally we made our way to the bedroom, and she noticed the door there.

”Hey, what’s this door lead to?”

I barely even noticed what she was saying, my face was in my phone looking at videos.

”Oh just the HVAC unit….wait-“

When I looked up, that’s when I saw her face. Her hair began to rustle slightly, the wind rustling through the air now as I could smell the moist scent of mildew. We were both naked, yet we stared ahead at the empty abyss.

The unit wasn’t even there anymore, like it had just disappeared over night. I didn’t know what was happening, but before long she grabbed on my arm lightly.

”Zeke…what’s down there?”

I didn’t know what to say, what could I say? So, without thinking, I mindlessly answered her.

”I’m not sure…I just found it one day and the last time I went exploring in it-…I don’t like thinking about it much. Here, let’s just go back to bed, okay?”

I lightly pulled on her arm, and she shut the door behind her. I could tell she was struck now though, as if she gazed into the dead lights and was no longer on the ground. I laid her down on the bed, tucking her in before deciding to get some rest myself.

That was last night however, and this morning I woke alone. A note was left half hazardly on the nightstand, and it read like this:

”Zeke,

Sorry for leaving you like this, I haven’t been able to get any sleep. You’re amazing, and one day someone will see that, but I can’t do this anymore. I have to find someone else, I’m sorry. 

Please forget about me. Please forget about our nights together. Forget.”

She tried signing her name but it was chicken scratch. I don’t even know what to think anymore, my mind is all crossed and I can’t even think straight.

We had an amazing time, but suddenly now I have to forget all about her and the time we shared? Even though it was short it was amazing, Emily isn’t coming back and I wanted something- anyone here with me.

I don’t know what to do anymore…I honestly don’t. That church never contacted me back, and when I tried calling them my number was blocklisted. 

I don’t know what’s going on, and I’m running out of solutions. I need to know what the hell is going on. What is this room? Why does everyone freak out and disappear? What the hell was the thing chasing me? And what do I do?

I hope this all makes sense, I took some pills to wind myself down before writing this, and now I can hardly even see straight. Please…

Please, help me.
