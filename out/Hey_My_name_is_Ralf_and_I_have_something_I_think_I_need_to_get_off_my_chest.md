For a good while I used to urban explore with a few friends of mine from school, although the last few times I decided to go alone. There's this myth that goes around my town every couple of years, more like a campfire story really. The myth goes that if you visit a shed hidden deep within the forest and along the river line after the sun goes down, a man in a brown suit will slaughter you. It seemed oddly specific and made up, and I never really bought into it until this incident. 

It was 2018 when this happened, but i still remember it like it was last week. It was the evening after Halloween, and I made the stupid decision of going out to that shed to see what I would find. When first trying to get inside I had to unlock this combination lock, which wasn't hard to get open at all, and it makes me think the lock was more for animals rather than people. When I went inside there were a few boxes scattered across the floor. There was also a dresser and a small box tv with a VHS player, both pushed against the wall. I decided to see what was in the dresser, so I walked over and pulled open the first drawer. There were several tapes, all of them labeled different dates from around 2013-2015. I also checked the other drawers, and there wasn't much other than a journal labled "Diary". 

I decided to flip through the journal to see the contents before watching the tapes. I had read a few passages about the writers "missions", and how they felt sick afterwards. They had described the smell of the aftermath as "metallic" and "vile". I had thought maybe they were hunting and skinning deer for the aforementioned "missions", but I was bloody wrong. I had read a passage close to the ending of the journal that revealed the writer was a male serial killer, and that he was doing it for some tall man.. or at least that's how he described it. I remember asking myself why he would write something like that, but looking back he probably didn't expect anyone to read it, let alone a stranger looking through his stuff.

I felt both uneasy and curious about what I read, so I moved on to the tapes thinking that maybe they would answer my questions. I had put the tape with the earliest date in the VHS player. It did end up playing, which was a little surprising, although it wasn't what I was expecting at all. The tape contained a regular late-night news segment with a host called "Mr. Prowler", a man who wore a brown suit. I assumed that's where the myth came from, although I still didn't know why. I decided to watch a few more of the tapes, or at least the first five minutes of them. I didn't find anything odd or out of place, leaving me with even more questions, although that was until I watched the last tape.

The tape was labeled "September 27, 2015", and it was the same as the rest of them, until around six minutes in. I was about to put the tape back and call it a night when I heard the host start choking and heaving. What was strange is he only had that reaction when the moon appeared, which was a blood moon that night. As the moon shined brighter he started clutching his stomach and gagging, holding onto the side of the chair he was sitting in. He kept repeating how he was fine and to keep rolling the cameras, although everyone around him was concerned, understandably so. He ended up vomiting before falling out of the chair onto his hands and knees. 

I started to feel really bad for him, what happened next was like body horror. He began convulsing, heaving and foaming at the mouth, and the fabric of his suit ripping at the seams. It looked like his body was growing unnaturally and rapidly, and Im genuinely convinced he was growing a tail. He was screaming as if he was in horrible pain, his screams were haunting. The people in the studio with him were also screaming, they were horrified by the sound of it. By the time I could make out what was fully happening the tape suddenly cut off, leaving me also horrified. 

I didn't bother to put the tape back, I ran out of that shed. I didn't stop running until I recognized where I was, the local gas station. I'm not sure what I was trying to achieve or run from, but I had a gut feeling about my findings that I couldn't shake off. I had made the connection In my mind that night that the man in the tapes and the author of that journal were one in the same, leaving me feeling extra uneasy. To this day I think what haunts me most is the knowledge that the man in the brown suit could still very well be out there, and I probably made my presence known that night.
