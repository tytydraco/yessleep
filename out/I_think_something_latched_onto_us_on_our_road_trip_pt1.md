My husband Jay and I were heading back from a friend's wedding in her native place (a village in a very rural part of our country) when this incident took place.

  
It was late in the evening, and every single person around us at the wedding had warned us not to go back in the dark. Some people were logical, stating that we may be too tired to stay awake on the ride back, but the rest were superstitious. 

*"The forest you'll pass through does not like visitors at night."*

*"So many strange incidents in the past few days."*

*"They say the forest spirits get offended-"*

  
So on and so forth. I rolled my eyes and assured every single one of them that no "magical" spirit would curse us for simply getting back home, wondering how these people even live with so much fear entwined in every aspect of their lives. 

One hour after we got back on the road, we rolled the windows down, breathing in the smell the Earth makes after it is rained on, I looked up at the stars and the full moon, lighting up my first cigarette of the night.   
As we went deeper into the forest, the street lamps disappeared, and the only light left was the moonlight and our car headlights. 

"Wow, we really are the only ones on the road." Jay commented, "People really take these warnings seriously, huh?"

"It's a village, these 'superstitions' have existed for eons, and people find no reason to question them." I replied back, taking a puff. 

"Yeah, I guess." he muttered.   
As i smoked the last bit of my cigarette and threw it out on the ground (After crushing the butt of course, I do not intend to start a forest fire), my husband cranked up the car's audio system as our favourite song began to play. 

As we hummed and whistled during the chorus, something changed.   
And I don't mean that it got windy, or that we saw another car. 

I mean the air stilled in a way I did not expect. 

It was suddenly humid, and then both hot and cold at the same time. Every critter, every insect we had been hearing for miles had shushed. It was only us, our song and singing echoing into the night. 

"God, what is that?" I asked as i removed my jacket. 

"What is what?" Jay asked back absentmindedly. 

I lowered the volume, "Don't you feel that? it's too quiet out of nowhere, and weirdly hot."

"The weather has been weird all weekend, and it you probably feel like it's quiet because we were singing so loudly."

"No, you don't get it."

"Aria," he chuckled, "Are you feeling spooked out?" He asked in the same way you patronise a scared child.

"Stop." I said.

"Scared the boogeyman made the weather hot? Ooooo." he said as I smacked his arm. 

He had to be right, I told myself. 

After a few minutes, when I had just about convinced myself, something happened that made my stomach churn.

I looked back into the sky, feeling hazy, smiling at the moon. And then I blinked.

The moon was missing.

This sounds stupid, inanely childish. But I swear, I swear to God. One minute the moon was there a second before I had blinked, and then it just wasn't. And I don't fucking mean a cloud covered it, I mean the stars around the moon stayed shining bright in the cloudless night but the moon had fucking disappeared.

I turned back to ask Jay what the fuck was going on, but when I did, I saw that his eyes were wide, bloodshot, and his skin pale and devoid of life.   
He brought a finger up to his lips, telling me not to make a sound, and I obliged. 

  
What the hell was happening? 

And then I saw it. 

In the darkness of our car was a hand.

A hand on my husband's shoulder. 

Dark, grimy, long, lifeless. Claws gripping his shoulder so tight I swear I saw streaks of red on his shirt. 

I did not dare look back. 

As we continued driving, I saw something that made this abysmal night worse. 

It was the same checkpost we had passed an hour ago. 

We had come full circle back to the same place we were an hour ago.

Jay stifled a sob, I know he'd come to the same realisation as well. 

It has been three hours now, we are still in the car. My messages aren't going through, neither are my calls. We haven't looked back once, and we haven't made a single sound. The moon is still missing and we are still going in a circle. 

My phone says it's six a.m., but there is no sign of dawn. I don't know what to do. I don't know if this post will go through.

It will be okay, right?
