Every night I walk the same empty park after dark, and last night something finally walked out of the bushes to meet me.

The lamps shone on the empty path. The park was empty and quiet; even the trees stood still. I loved these nights. They made me feel like I could be myself.

I got off the path and headed to the small lake. The frogs croaked in the distance. Nothing could calm me down more. I never understood why people didn't like the dark; it's the most beautiful time of day.

On my way down, I passed a tree where I first saw her. A wave of warmth passed through me. I knew I had to stop and think of her for a moment, but as I closed my eyes, a noise echoed from the lit path. 

A few men with shaved heads and bomber jackets walked on it. I tried to go into the trees, but one of them looked my way and called, "What are you doing there?"

"Just going to the lake."

"Sure, you creep."

They threw their cigarettes on the ground and walked toward me, their heavy boots echoing.

This was supposed to be a peaceful, beautiful night of remembrance. I turned and ran toward the lake.

"Hey!"

The heavy steps got louder. The air blew in my face. I was almost by the lake when a hand gripped my collar, and a boot kicked my legs out from under me. I fell hard into the grass, my body throbbing from the hit, then sharp pain shot through my ribs as the toe of one of their boots kicked me in my chest. I let out a loud grunt, and another boot hit me right in the nose.

Copper filled my mouth. Warm blood dripped down to my lip. The men above laughed as another boot kicked the back of my head. Light flashed before my eyes, and through the white I saw something rise from the bushes, the bushes I was heading toward.

The air filled with the smell of rotten garlic. A deep crack echoed through the park. A small shadow came over the group. The men turned their heads.

"What are you doing, girl? Get out."

"What's wrong with her eyes?"

She came right up to the man who knocked me down. 

"Can't you hear? Get away!"

Her right arm shot unnaturally fast from her body and grabbed his neck, lifting him. The muscles in her forearm flexed. A wet crunch sounded. Blood splashed everywhere. She now held only the neck's flapping skin in her hand. The other men turned and bolted. Under the moonlight, I could see it was her. 

"Did you come and save me?" My voice shook. "I thought you'd be angry with me."

She took a step closer. I wanted to come closer to her too, but I saw the look in her eyes. They weren't filled with love; they were filled with hate.

"My love, please, I came to see your beautiful body again."

The wound on her neck was larger than I remembered. Blood rushed to my crotch, but tonight was not the night. I turned and ran up the hill.

I got to the lamplight path, but sharp pain was shooting through my ribs. I stopped and took a few breaths, but the rotten-garlic smell tingled in my nose again. I looked over my shoulder. She was just a few feet behind me. Her pace grew faster with each step. I let out a deep grunt and ran to the edge of the park, trying to ignore the pulsing pain. 

The smell grew stronger. I heard her wet steps getting closer. The edge of the park was right by me. My car was in that parking lot. Then my toe hit something hard. I lost my balance and fell face first on the pavement. I rolled onto my back and dragged myself across the asphalt. 

She looked so pretty under the lights. The wound on her neck, the one I had left her with. Her messy hair. And those bulging eyes filled with hate. Hate for me. I knew it was over, but at least I'd die by her hand and not by some men. I took a breath, expecting the worst, but she stopped at the edge of the park, her eyes staring into mine. My love couldn't leave.

I stood up, holding my ribs.

"At least you have this beautiful park all to yourself."

She didn't answer. I didn't expect her to. I went home that night and took care of my wounds. As the sun rose, I got into my car and drove back.

"With the sun up, I'll get a good look at your beautiful body," I whispered and crossed into the park again. 

As I stepped into the park, I noticed fresh footprints in the grass beside the lake, leading toward the tree where I first met [her.](https://www.reddit.com/r/KanesHorrors/)
