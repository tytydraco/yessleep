To anyone that may be reading this, there’s likely something watching you at this very moment. 

**STOP!**

Don’t turn around, don’t try to find it, keep your eyes on these words I’m writing and it might just get bored of you. 

It wants to be seen, it always wants to be seen... eventually. 

I know because I was stupid enough to look where I shouldn’t, just the once is all it needed, now it won’t leave me alone. 

I can’t escape it; believe me I’ve tried every way possible and even my own death doesn’t seem to work. It wants me—no it *needs* me alive. 

It feeds on the terror it inflicts and it is always so hungry. 

If you try to look, it will always hide at first, just in the spaces out of the corner of your eyes. Enough at first to plant that little seed of fear.

First time I thought I was seeing things. I was quite used to sleepless nights and was prone to small insomnia-induced hallucinations. Nothing too crazy, the odd whispered voice from an empty room, a faceless shadow at the edge of my bed. 

I believed I’d seen and heard it all with nothing left to fear of the darkness,

But one night, when I was having a particularly restless episode; I saw something that didn’t go away with the simple flip of a light switch. Instead it moved to another dark place, always just out of sight the moment I tried to look at it. 

It wasn’t like the other things that’d been haunting me for all these years.

It was tangible, so close I could touch it.

Always trying so hard to be seen just enough to inspire fear. 

But in reality all it actually did is make me want to look harder, which I guess was the point.

Eventually, with the help of some lamps and torches I managed to get it cornered. Now I thought, with no dark place left to run it was finally going to truly reveal itself to me… and unfortunately, I was right.   

It started as a thin seam in a corner edge of the room, then expanded outwards; first its arms long and skeletal, then each leg, just as the arms, jutting forth pulled by its elongated clawed fingers. 

Then from the nothingness, it stepped out revealing the entirety of its being. A giant gaping maw made up its black leathery torso, like an eyeless toad with endless rows of spiralling needle-like teeth, all descending into what I can only describe as a black hole into infinity. 

Simply gazing at it had caused me to become entranced by its horrific impossibility.  

I could feel it, in my head, what it wanted me to do. I began to step towards it, closer and closer, reaching out a hand and finally finding myself climbing inside the thing’s mouth.

I grasped the first row of teeth and immediately felt them impale through my fingers. It was pure agony, I screamed, guttural and intensely, but it didn’t stop me from proceeding onwards. 

I used my other hand pulling myself up, then my legs and finally I was completely immersed within its jaw, crawling ever forward; each effort resulting in millions of the razor-sharp teeth puncturing me over and over again. 

It was if I were a lump of meat being tenderised to absolute perfection. 

Soon even my cries began to wane as my vocal chords were snagged and pulled away. Every subsequent lift of a finger or shift of weight was met with torn flesh and shredded ligaments. 

The pain was immense but I continued onwards willingly. Trapped in a cavernous iron maiden of my own curiosity. 

Deeper, deeper, never-ending. Nothing left of me but ragged muscle and flayed skin; all tethered together by the simple will to keep moving. *I had to keep going*. It wanted me to find a way out. 

My body starting to falter, bones now being ground to dust as if wood to sandpaper. I was finally about to die. The blackness about to take its last nourished gulp of my existence.

Then I was back where I started. 

Laid out on the floor like regurgitated slop. 

I was whole again. 

I looked up at the creature and could tell it was done with me, for now at least. 

Punishment served, but sentence ongoing. 

Since that night, I’ve done my best not to catch its attention again. 

But I can’t last much longer. 

Lately, it’s become more brazen and almost wilfully slow in its actions. 

Trying to get me to see it again, but I won’t let it. 

It smashes light bulbs, abruptly slams doors shut, even mimicking the voice of deceased loved ones. 

So many tricks just so it can feast once more. 

Even when I tried to take *extreme methods*, it wouldn’t let me. 

It’s lurking behind me now. I can feel the warm wetness of its breath on my neck.

That’s why I had to share this.

For if it finally has its fill of me and decides its finally satiated, I have to make sure no one else falls victim to it again.

Sometimes things are better left unknown. 

For now, I feel the pull of oblivion again. 

I’m so tired of resisting.

Good luck.
