I work from home. So I have to have a routine if I'm going to get any work done. I'm up by seven on weekdays, eating a hasty breakfast by eight, and at my desk in my home office by eight-thirty. I think I'm pretty good at my job and I maintain a good work-life balance by not doing anything work related after four o'clock. 

Anyway, I was watching basketball highlights on TV while scrolling through TikTok on my phone with my work computer open on my living room table during my lunch break two days ago. It's 2026, our brains weren't built for this, but needs must. I saw a video of a girl, she was gorgeous, but in a way that made me think she was AI. Her nose, skin, hair, and especially her eyes just looked too perfect. I usually don't have the patience for AI but I didn't scroll away. Something about what she was saying caught my attention. 

She said, “One day you will meet a girl at a cafe, she will smile at you from her table. You should walk over and join her. The two of you could build something special.”

That was it, that was her whole video. I was intrigued, this wasn't usually how this trend went. It usually went like this, the poster would say something about a chance encounter with a stranger, then the video would show how they had met the love of their lives in that exact situation, and they were still together decades later, married with children.

I checked her profile. Her account was new and this was her only video. It had one view. Me. So definitely AI. My first thought was that this was probably some dude who was great with the ladies, trying to give some motivation to all the neckbeards out there. I've had relationships before, but I've never been lucky in love. At twenty-eight I've never approached a woman I've never met and asked her out on the spot. All relationships I have had started on dating sites, sadly none of them lasted longer than six months. 

I did like the idea of having a feminine presence in my apartment, it could brighten the place up a little. So I took the bot’s advice. I went to my favorite cafe that night. It was half full with easy listening music in the background and slightly dimmed lighting to give a cozy feel. I found an empty table next to a window, off to a good start. I hadn't brought my laptop because I wanted this to evolve into a date, but in the meantime I had nothing to read or watch, so I pretended to study the menu which I knew by heart.

I'd been excited during the uber ride but now that I was there the task seemed a bit too daunting. How did this go again? Walk up to some random woman's table, if she's alone, then ask her if she wants company?

Or, be a bit more subtle and ask her if the extra seat is taken, even if she says it is, the interaction is a lot less awkward for both parties. 

I can do this, I thought, amping myself up. The worst she can say is, “Yes, the seat’s taken.” I can live with that. 

Now, how does one go about checking out women at a cafe without coming off like a creep? Like most nerds I had always aimed too high in my younger days, but as I've gotten older I've grown comfortable with aiming at eye level. 

I tried to make my roaming eyes as incidental looking as possible. Turn my head and scratch my neck, stretch my arms and sneak a glance two tables over, yawn and pretend to close my eyes, you know.

I saw a few women I really wanted to talk to. And I was going to, I really was going to. Then I saw her. The girl from the video. She was sitting alone, looking at me. And smiling. 

How had I not spotted her before? Because she hadn't been there. That's why. That table had been empty. I hadn't seen anyone come in because I'd been busy trying to look at other patrons. 

My mind went blank, it was really her. My AI spotting ability must be really shitty because there she was, even more beautiful in real life, and smiling. What had she said in the video? Right then and there I couldn't remember. 

Why was she looking at me? I had never seen her before in my life, her face isn't one anyone could forget. This was way too creepy. I slowly stood from my chair. The other patrons were also staring at her. So I wasn't the only one who thought there was a little uncanny valley in her beauty. She seemed unbothered by their attention, her eyes were focused on me. And I looked like I had seen a ghost. 

I walked out of the cafe and went home. No one followed me. The taxi ride back gave me time to calm down. Of course she was local. TikTok tests your initial videos on local audiences, I didn't think it was this local, but it made sense. I knew I could never have approached her. I would've been a stuttering fool next to her. 

When I woke up yesterday, my sense of unease had slightly dissipated. The experience hadn't been completely embarrassing, and I hadn't really been in any danger. 

That afternoon, I went for a long walk at the park. I sometimes dodge my regular late-night jogging by going for long walks. 

I was still thinking about her. A girl so beautiful I thought she couldn't be real. I knew why she smiled at me at the cafe, you can see who viewed your profile on TikTok. She must've recognized me. A more confident man might've gone to her table and asked her if we'd met before, given she was smiling at and maintaining eye contact with a stranger. I'm not that man. Sadly. 

Forty minutes into my walk, I saw her again. This time she was twenty yards away, walking in my direction. Her movement was fluid, precise, eerie in the way it looked like every step was identical to the last. She was smiling. I froze. What were the odds? Had she been looking for me? I didn't move until she was standing right next to me. I had been right the first time, she was way too perfect. I had never seen a person this flawless in real life, ever.

She said, “One day, you will meet a girl at a park. She will extend her hand to you.” She offered me her hand. “Take it, and take her home. The two of you will make beautiful memories.”

WHAT. THE. FUCK.

I ran. This was my rest day but I ran my ass off. I wasn't sure why I was scared. I knew I wasn't running from a monster. I was running from a girl who wanted to come home with me. Who was half a foot shorter. And she was very attractive. But… her lips had no visible lines or imperfections on them, her skin was flawless and toned, with no sign of wrinkles or pores, and her hair was perfectly straight, with no strand bent or out of place. She also wasn't wearing any makeup. 

When I looked back, she was standing where I'd left her, she hadn't moved. And she was still looking at me. She waved. I hightailed it home without looking back again. 

When I got home, I locked all my doors and windows. 

This had to be a prank. I didn't know why but this had to be a prank. 

I checked her profile again. She still only had the one video, with just one view. Her name was Michelle Dukersen. She had either lived a very quiet life or that wasn't her real name. I searched her on Google and found nothing. 

She said we could make memories together. What on earth had she meant by that?

Someone knocked on my front door. I wasn't alarmed at first, since it could've been a neighbor. I stood and took a few steps to the door but stopped dead when I heard, “One day, a girl will come knocking on your door. Let her in, she was made for you.”

“Go away!” I shouted. 

I retrieved a knife from the kitchen and stood five feet from the front door. Now I was genuinely terrified. I didn't question it anymore. I was reluctant to call the cops because I thought they'd laugh at me if I told them the whole story. They'd think this was a prank. 

I stood there for almost an hour before looking through the peephole. She wasn't there. She had left. 

How had she found where I lived? More importantly, what would she do if she came inside? 

I went to bed with a headache that night from over-thinking about why I was so afraid, she hadn't threatened me, and what she meant when she said she was “made for me”. 

When I woke up this morning, I opened my eyes and she was right there in bed with me, under the sheets. How had she entered my apartment? She didn't flinch as I jerked away from her. My bed is in the corner of the room, my back was to the wall and she was in front of me. She moved closer. I scooted back until I couldn't anymore. She came all the way over to me, her body pushing mine against the wall. 

She said, “One day, you will wake up next to a beautiful girl. Say good morning to her, and smile.”

She wasn't smiling as she said this, but her smile returned when I nervously complied.

Update: Six hours later. She's still here. 

She made breakfast for me this morning. Eggs, bacon, sausages, and toast, with coffee. She's a more patient and better cook than me. She didn't eat, said she wasn't hungry. 

She left me alone for hours to work in my office. I didn't call anyone. I'm more confused than afraid at this point. I did say I'd like a feminine presence in the house, she definitely is that. And she's beautiful. 

Now it's lunchtime, I have freshly baked cookies and tea in front of me. She's still not hungry. She smiles as she watches me eat. 

The door chain is still latched, just how I left it last night. Undamaged.
