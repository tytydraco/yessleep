[My world fell apart when I learned I didn't have schizophrenia.](https://www.reddit.com/r/nosleep/comments/1vw66uj/my_doctor_said_my_schizophrenia_would_make_me_see/) If my illness wasn't real, then what had I actually been experiencing? Thinking the medication might have had an adverse side effects and caused me to hallucinate, I stopped taking it. I didn't have schizophrenia anyway, so there was no need for me to take it.

A few days passed, and everything seemed normal. Until one night, just before going to bed. 

Just as I was about to turn off the television, I noticed someone calling my name. I thought I might have misheard, but I heard it again. I saw my same colleague whose head had been smashed on the screen. I couldn't believe my eyes. He told me how I had failed to save him. I tried to turn off the television, but the screen remained on. When I attempted to switch it off using the button, I realized that the same clown was reaching out from the screen and grabbing my arm. I tried to pull my arm away, but his grip was so strong I could almost hear my bones cracking. I thought I was done for, but then he let go of my arm and let out that chilling laugh again. It was as if he were playing a game with me.

My arm ached so much that I couldn't sleep all night. The first rays of sunlight were streaming through the window, yet I was still up, waiting with a knife in my hand and my eyes glued to the television. Was the same thing going to happen again? It was around nine in the morning, and my eyes were slowly starting to close; no matter how hard I tried, I couldn't fight off sleep.

When I woke up, I wasn't on my sofa. I had woken up in a terrifying forest without my knife. I had fallen asleep around nine in the morning, yet it was pitch black when I opened my eyes. That was impossible; no one could sleep that long. I was terrified, but I thought staying put might make things worse, so I started walking.

It was so dark all around that I couldn't even see what was right in front of me, so I took out my phone and turned on the light. Thank God my phone was in my pocket. After walking a bit further, I heard a voice calling for help. I was terrified to go towards it, but I thought I might find someone else like me, and we could escape from here together. 

As I started moving toward the sound, I noticed it beginning to distort, as if someone had recorded it on a tape player that was starting to malfunction. I decided to turn back, but the sound kept getting closer. It felt as though the sound was coming toward me rather than the other way around, and no matter which way I turned, it followed me. I couldn't take it anymore. "Show yourself, you coward!" I shouted. A voice rose from among the trees "Son" It was my mother's voice. I immediately started running in the direction of the sound, calling out to her as I ran. Then I saw it.

My mother was dressed exactly like that clown. "Mom, where are we?" I asked. "We're at home" she said. "I'm getting a head start on the Christmas tree, how does it look?" she asked, pointing to the tree. I froze. My colleagues, who had been hanged inside the circus at the mall, were hanging from this tree.

This woman couldn't be my mother. My mother would panic and become flustered at the sight of even the tiniest drop of blood. "Who are you?" I asked. She replied, "a friend" then reached behind her and pulled out a gun. At first, she pointed it at me and started to laugh. I recognized that laugh; it was the same chilling laugh. Then, she pointed the gun at her own forehead and pulled the trigger. A loud "BANG" rang out, and she fell to the ground.

I knew that woman wasn't my mother, yet tears still streamed down my face. She began to pick herself up off the ground, blood was pouring from a gash on her forehead. Suddenly, she started shaking her head violently, and that clown's face replaced my mother's. My heart felt like it was about to burst out of my chest. Terrified as I was, I tried not to show it and asked, "What do you want from me?" "Don't you love me? We're having such a wonderful time together" he replied. That was the first time I had ever heard him speak. He started rushing toward me at incredible speed, so fast that I couldn't even perceive his movement.

I woke up. I was at home, on my sofa, with a knife in my hand. I immediately grabbed my phone and called my mother. She answered; I was fighting back tears, and when she asked, "What happened?" I said, "I just wanted to hear your voice" It was only a dream. But what if I really am schizophrenic? That is the only logical explanation for what happened.

I decided to see a psychologist, but since it was the weekend, I had to wait until Monday. On Sunday morning, I went into the bathroom to wash up. I kept telling myself, "This isn't real" After washing my face, I looked in the mirror and saw him. He was staring back at me with a chilling smile. I turned around, and he wasn't there. I told myself, "It's all just a figment of my imagination" but when I turned back to the mirror, I saw him instead of my own reflection. He reached out from the mirror and grabbed my head. "You are my friend" he said. "You will stay that way forever" Terrified, I didn't know what to do. Then, he let go. He had such a tight grip on my neck that I couldn't move it properly all day.

I went to see a psychologist early Monday morning. I walked in and told him I had schizophrenia. He told me to calm down and take a seat. I sat down, and he asked me why I thought that. I explained that I had seen a clown. A clown that killed my friends, shoved his arm out of the television screen and grabbed me by the arm, disguised himself as my mother and shot himself in the head, and reached his arm out of my bathroom mirror to grab me by the neck.

The psychologist looked at me, there was no trace of the contempt or mockery I had expected in his gaze. He set his pen down and asked, "When did these incidents start happening?" "A few weeks ago" I replied. "And did you feel pain when he hurt you?" Pointing to the bruise on my arm, I said, "It happened when he grabbed me by the television screen"

After we had talked like this for a long time, the psychologist said to me, "Look, the human mind can sometimes be under such immense stress that a person doesn't even realize they are harming themselves. Perhaps in your sleep, or during that moment of intense panic, you squeezed your arm very hard and caused this bruise yourself" Hearing this, I felt a tightness in my chest. "I'm going to step outside and get some air" I said.

I had opened the door and was just about to leave when I heard a voice: "Jason" It wasn't the psychologist's voice. When I turned around, I saw him again. The psychologist had no lower body. The clown had thrust his hand deep inside the psychologist and was making him speak like a puppet. "You're out of your mind" "You're going to stay here with us forever" "Forever" he said, moving the psychologist's jaw.

My hands were trembling with fear. I immediately stepped outside, but the same circus I had seen at the mall was right there. With no other escape route, I went inside, and this time, the doctors from the hospital were hanged there. I didn't know what to do. Tearfully, I looked for a place to hide, and as soon as I found one, I quickly got inside.

I am still hiding in the same place, and that circus music keeps drifting in from outside. I don't know what to do. The sound is getting closer.
