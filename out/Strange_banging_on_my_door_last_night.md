Ok, I shit you not this is the most terrifying thing to happen to me

I've been a fan of someordinarygamer's deep web browsing series for a little while now so I decided to give it a go for myself. I downloaded tor for the first time and began to explore some onion sites to see what I would come across. For the most part it was the normal sort of content you'd expect to find, drugs, feds posting as hit men, the usual. That was until I found my way to this website, it initially looked like a standard porn site but redirected me to a website with a blank URL.

I look to the top right of my screen and it appears to have downloaded some sort of file, it looked harmless so I just deleted it without thinking.

The next couple days were strange, I was receiving a greater number of phone calls to my cell and when I would pick up a robotic voice said "goodbye" before hanging up. Weird shit but I thought it was the usual phone call that places send to check if someone occupied the line.

That was until I received a package at my door, I opened it and inside was a strange object that looked almost ceramic alongside a usb. On the USB were dark grainy photos that looked to be of some sort of dark room, there looked to be metal objects and some sort of dentist chair in the space but it seemed indefinite as to what was actually depicted. The further I combed through the worse they got there looked to be animal gore and blood hanging from the ceiling off a chain. I looked the object up on my AI app and it told me that it looked like half a human jaw bone. I felt sick to my stomach before resuming my look through the files of the usb and the final picture made my blood run cold.

There was a file entitled victim.jpg and it was a photo of myself sleeping taken from outside of my bedroom window.

That was all I needed to see, I immediately notified the police who agreed to monitor the house and I made arrangements with my friend to stay at theirs until I could be confident I was safe.

Two weeks went by and though I remained on edge I presumed I was safe at my friends apartment on the fifth floor. They had installed a ring doorbell at the front of their property and it was at the end of the hallway with full visibility down to the elevator. I was asleep before I heard a strange banging on the front door, not a persistent banging, something more akin to the sound of construction. We both grab weapons and wait behind the locked door in case of a break in but no such attack came. However upon opening the door in the morning the source of the banging became painfully clear. There was a human hand nailed to the door. The nail was hammered in deep enough to stick it on but not enough to penetrate through to the other side of the door. Hanging from the nail was a piece of string and at the end was an eyeball. Most likely a message that wherever I run to I cannot hide.

Unfortunately the camera on the doorbell wasn't much use. The figure was dressed in all black with a long hooded trenchcoat. Obscuring their face was a mask, it looked almost like leather and itself was smeared with blood, though there looked to be a lighter coloured lipstick around the mouth.

Due to the covering the police were unable to do much aside from offering me alternative accommodation miles out of town. 

Still even miles away from my home town I can't truly feel safe. I'll provide more updates if any new occurrences arise.
