Hey everyone, it's currently 1:43 AM and after what happened I can't really sleep. I'm mainly writing this while it's still fresh in my brain for the police, but also just so that I can actually tell someone what happened. This all only happened about thirty minutes ago but I'm still struggling to really understand what the fuck just happened. This whole situation feels like something straight out of a nightmare but I'm hoping as I write that I can properly articulate just how terrifying this whole thing has been. 

It was around midnight when I was driving home after a late shift at my job. I don’t usually work that late as I live almost an hour away from the restaurant I work at, but I'm running behind on my rent and I needed the overtime. The highway was basically empty, everyone else had clearly already been asleep, save for the occasional truck driver who would pass me as I made my way home. I was tired, so I was trying my best to be extra cautious on the road, and I started looking out for the nearest road that I could turn off of to stay off the highway. 

Eventually, I got lucky and saw Walnut Drive, a street that I knew would add five minutes to my trip but it would at least put me on a safer road given my exhaustion. I was running on fumes. I drank out of every water bottle I could find in my car in the hopes that it would keep me up for a little longer, as by this point, I was about twenty-five minutes away from the small trailer I lived in.

I was surrounded by woods and I'll admit by this point my mind started playing tricks on me as it turned the blurs of the trees passing by into almost human shapes. Of course, after what happened, who knows.

I was about fifteen minutes away from my house when I passed by Huckaberry Road, another street that I knew would be a detour. I was going at least ten under the speed limit in my attempt to just not fall asleep and possibly crash the car so any detour I could find, I decided to take. I turned onto the unpaved, gravel road and immediately noticed how dark it was compared to the regular streets. There were no streetlights nor any houses on the sides of the road. I decided at this point to turn up the radio, only to realize that all that played was static. I could hear brief sounds of a song in the background, but clearly out there the signal simply wasn't strong enough. I turned down the radio. Despite it definitely waking me up a little, the screeching sound of the static was annoying enough for me to decide against keeping it on. 

I reached a stop sign at the end of Huckaberry Road, which led onto Robertson Creek Road. I had the choice to either turn left or right. Left would lead me to my home, right would just lead me into town. As I turned left, I realized that this road was somehow even darker than the other. I didn't make it far before deciding to turn on my high beams, and that's when I saw her. 

Standing there, about one thousand feet in front of my car… was a woman. I stopped immediately. She looked incredibly old and frail. I could see tufts of white hair standing up on her head, and skin that appeared to be stretched onto her body. She was wearing what appeared to be a bright blue hospital gown, and she was slowly stumbling her way in the direction of my vehicle.

I didn't know what to do. The sight of her was apparently exactly what I needed to completely wake up. Something about her just looked… horrible, but at the same time, I thought something might've been wrong with her. I didn't want to get out of the car, but I figured I might as well step out (while obviously staying prepared) just to ask if she needed help. Don't get me wrong, the sight of her was absolutely terrifying, but what assumption could I really make? She was a frail woman who appeared to be in her mid-80s. On the off-chance she tried to attack me, what could she actually do? That was the logic I told myself as I put the car in park and opened the door, still keeping one hand on the steering wheel.

“Ma’am? Are you okay?” I hollered at the woman.

She stopped. She was no longer moving towards the car, she was just staring at me.

“Ma’am?” I hollered again.

Out of nowhere, the frail, old woman started sprinting straight towards my car. My previous logic immediately failed me as I jumped straight back into my car and slammed the door closed. I'm not sure why I didn't just drive forward, I guess some part of me didn't want to run over what I still perceived as an old woman. I put the car in reverse and immediately started backing up to prepare for a three-point turn. She was getting closer and closer. Even now, I'm not sure how she was moving that fast. Of course, I moved faster. As I put the car in drive I could see her silhouette, no longer illuminated by the headlights, getting closer. I turned sharply and heard a loud bang, as I'm sure the woman slammed the full force of her entire fucking body into the back passenger-side window of my car. I turned so hard back onto Huckaberry Road that I almost spun out. I was sure I could still see her silhouette chasing me. Of course, she disappeared quickly in the distance as I'm sure I was going at least ninety miles per hour on that gravel road. 

I didn't slow down once all the way home. I was freaking the fuck out, I knew I had lost her but I couldn't stop hyperventilating. I genuinely didn’t know, and honestly still don't know, what had just happened. I couldn't stop shaking all the way home, I knew that I had to share this with somebody. I sent each of my friends long texts telling them what the fuck just happened. Of course, there were no replies from any of them. It was late at night and they all had work in the morning. 

I cut a fifteen-minute trip down to about five just due to the pure speed I was going. I wouldn't be surprised if a few people called the cops talking about some crazy man speeding down the road. Luckily, though, I finally arrived at the trailer that I lived in. My home has a small staircase that leads up to the front door. The window of my bedroom sits directly beside the staircase so that I can actually see whoever may be outside. 

As I entered my house, the first thing I did was call the police to report the woman on Robertson Creek. If she was wearing a hospital gown, then chances are she escaped from some sort of hospital, although the nearest hospital to that gravel road is about a thirty-minute drive. I decided to go ahead and write my testimony for you guys while I waited. I’m doing this mainly to keep everything fresh in my mind, but I figured I could show it to the polic- wait. Shit. I think they’re here.

I just saw somebody walking up the stairs outside my bedroom window.

Wait.

No. No, no, no.

They’re knocking on my door.

If it is the police, they’re knocking really fucking hard.

No. Fuck. No, it can’t be.

I’m going to look through the window.

Oh god.

It’s her.
