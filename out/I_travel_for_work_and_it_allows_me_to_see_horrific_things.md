When i was freshly 23 i took a job for a company that allows me to go around the world to take photos for other smaller companies to help them advertise their own company. its amazing being able to travel all over the years, going from one place to another for free and ive been doing this for over 4 years but lately I've seen some things that have made almost quite this perfect job.

About almost 6 months ago I was on a train in Australia heading from one city to another, so you all dont go looking for it i wont be sharing any real names in this. I was on a long train one that took hours to get to its destination and whilst on long train trips I like to take photos of the scenery or even of the inside of the train if the lights hit my subjects perfectly.

For this story I was taking a photo of one of the signs on the train that tell us what stop is up next and when I took the photo it came out pitch black, now I've been doing photography for almost 14 years now ever since I was a teenager so id imagine I would be well versed in this, so I checked my ISO and Shutter speed but both were on the correct settings for what I was taking photos of, so I tried again, Still blank, I thought my camera broke and for these reasons my camera bag has 2 extra cameras, a small one for if all else fails and a slightly worse one than my main camera, so I grab out my slightly worse one, my Canon EOS 1200D and then re-took the photo, once again blank.

This got rid of the idea of camera malfunctions at least in my head it did, but either way not much i could do so I just put my cameras away and ignored it for the next few hours and by that I mean sleep, I usually sleep on long train rides and normally it all goes smoothly until then.

I remember waking up and my train had come to a complete stop in the middle of nowhere, I must of slept for hours as it was now pitch black and my phone flat so I couldn't read the time, sitting up and taking in my bizarre surroundings I realise that's its just me and this lonely train on its solitude rest in no where, I hoped it wasnt just me so getting up and looking around for someone seemed like a good plan to lift my spirits from this creepy situation but 3 laps up and down the train to no avail. 

I realise I'm alone and lost hoping it's all a dream still i returned to my seat and tried to fall asleep again hoping it'd wake me up, as my eyes closed I was hopeful to wake up and see my destination outside my window but instead was met with a voice.

"Dont fall asleep" that's when the train signals a new message on the big signs at the end of every carriage reapeting that saying 'Dont fall asleep' over and over like the train itself was telling me to stay awake, I was filled with concern and fear from this and from feeling trapped and so I paced back and forth on the train to try and just think my way out of this entrapment, I did not wish to die here on this train.

With no food and my phone being flat I couldn't do much, luckily Australian trains have emergency call buttons, the attempt of using one becomes futile the moment I press the button speaking "Please help im trapped" and nothing speaks back absolutely nothing pure silence even the buzzing of the train lights where louder that the silence coming from the emergency speaker, my breathing was louder, anything and everything possibly making sounds made more than that stupid broken machine, but i wasnt entirely out of luck.

With how in the middle of nowhere I was there would of had to be a train driver driving this thing from point A to B, so I went to the front of the train to talk to a possible crazy driver pulling a prank on me. On my arrival there I knocked, well more like pounded on the door, and in all honesty I expected it to be empty but something spoke back, something.

"I'm not alone?" This voice was so scratchy it didnt seem alive and if it was it was contagious with some vile sickness, this was enough to send me stumbling back in shock I mean I never actually expected for anything to answer me back, I keep walking back in fear, I did not nor wanted to know what said those words to me better yet I didnt want it to realise that I was here but the knocking already ruined that for me, the door swings open whilst I'm already a few metres away, I threw my hands up in the air as a defence bracing myself for whatever was to come but it never did like waiting for rain all day to come and ruin your amazing outfit like the clouds foretell but it never does rain kind of like that.

Looking around the control centre of this train all the machinery is turned off and I touch a few buttons to see if I could drive it to some town or something but nothing, pressing multiple buttons but nothing ever came of it plus I didnt even know how to drive a train so it's probably for the best it didn't work but even then anything would of been better stuck on a train in pitch black night surrounded by bushland.

I walk out of the driver's area and see those same signs that were blinking 'Dont fall asleep' over and over had changed to arrows pointing up, Up? Or foward? I so desperately wanted out once i saw the arrow I ran to the other end of the train i just wish I paid more attention.

Running the end of the train in panic I didnt realise until the second last carriage that the signs were pointing to something different a up arrow was accompanied by a stop sign right after, why not put both in the same message they could fit but also I didnt know these trains had symbols but either way I had ran too far down the train now to have heeded the warning it was telling me and instead ran towards where the train didnt want me too.

Looking down from the sign my eyes catch a humanoid being i say humanoid because it was illuminated by the signs inside the last carriage and no other lights, all other lights in the carriage were out and only the yellow arrow and stop symbols flashing on it made it appear wider than what it might of been but still I saw it, and from its pricing yellow eyes being the only thing I see apart from its silhouette piercing my eyes I feel dread, I didnt like it's pure yellow eyes reaching into me, but I knew it saw me, and it knew I saw it.

"I knew i wasnt" the same scratchy voice screeching out talking to itself helped me realise I just ran straight towards that thing behind the door, but how did it get here when I heard it behind the door, that small part of me that questions everything wanted to know but I just wanted to live more and so I ran and that thing gave chase.

Every carriage it runs into behind me turns pitch black still making this thing seem like pure shadow and not anything with a form, it seemed like pure darkness like the outside of the train leaked into the inside and was trying to consume me but I didnt want to be consumed at least not today so I ran for life trying to stay ahead of the dark.

With every passing carriage and every light that turned off only made this thing bigger I could hear its shadow like form start destroying the train like it was growing too big to even stay contained and I was scared, but also kinda of astonished by all of this mystery at least now I am, anyway the best way to take a photo in the dark is to use flash to peirce through the dark and allow your photo to develop despite the dark and given that this thing was removing the only light left on the decrepit train I had nothing else to try but my flash 

I ran to my camera bag that I left at the front not wanting to haul it all the way with me, dashing past seats and using the hand holding pole to help launch myself I start to lose hope as the darkness starts flooding into the corners of my eyes and start consuming me. ME! Who said I wanted to be engulfed into the dark like im an old film photo waiting to be developed in a red room and that last little bit of humanity and light left in this train gave me the will power to reach my bag (or at least i like to believe the train helped).

I reached for my main camera and flipped up its flash, I spun around and was met with the lights of this final carriage turning off and that thing launching at me, leaping through the dark cold air, I readied my camera and brought it to my eyes and as soon as my finger touched the Shutter button the train signs finally changed from those warning arrows to in big bright words saying *FLASH,* and so i slammed my finger on the Shutter button, it almost felt like slowmo from how long that final moment passed but once it did and the flash cleared the darkness im met with a voice.

"Hey what are you doing you idiot" I brought my camera down from my face and saw a man in a business suit on this train, he looked like he was commuting to work but his voice sounded scratchy like he was just getting over a cold. I apologised and looked at the photo just to see that man being blinded by flash and the photo itself looked overexposed from my settings but it actually was there unlike any photo I took whilst on that train.

I looked outside and was met with the starting suburbs of my city, the train was moving and the shining sun was beaming the world showing the amazing daylight that lit everything up, I sat back down in a completely different sit and didnt know what to do but just drop it into im psychotic bottle and need to go to a psychologist but I did years ago for other problems and this wasn't one of them.
