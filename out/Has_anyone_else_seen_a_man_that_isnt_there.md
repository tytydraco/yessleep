I was shoving him, and when shoving him barely did anything, that pissed me off more. At one point, I actually pushed him hard enough for him to hit the edge of the sink. It felt like time kinda paused then, like everything stopped. I couldn’t hear my music. I just wanted to leave, I was tired of the bullshit. As soon as I turned I was pushed into the counter behind me, the knife on the cutting board clattering. I grabbed it. I was terrified. He never attacked *me* before. It's like when someone you thought you knew gets violent towards you—actually, I hope you wouldn’t know. It’s foreign. His violence aimed at me was unnatural.

The moment I swung the knife towards him, he grabbed my wrist. I tried to pry it free with my remaining hand, but he caught that one too. He forced both hands to hold the knife’s handle. We were both straining for control. He was stronger. Adrenaline spiked through me, my eyes were glued to the knife. I thought he was going to make me stab myself. His grip forced it up, up, up until it was… touching the underside of his jaw. Blood was already leaking onto my hands by the time I realized the knife penetrated. Somehow, even while impaling his own head, his grasp held strong, forcing me to stay. At some point his body began to sag, weighing my arms down, still never letting go. I didn’t scream, it wouldn’t have done anything. I just watched because that was all I could do. 

It felt like forever when his legs buckled and gave out underneath him. He would’ve brought me down if his hands hadn’t let go. He fell with a heavy thud and clang of the handle, a pool already forming around his head. My hands were drenched. I stood there for a while staring at the body. My cat meowed at me for breakfast.

Eventually I ran off to clean myself. I came back a couple of hours later expecting him to have moved, but he didn’t. I went to bed and woke up later in the evening for work, and he was still there. I went to work and came home around 3am, and he was still lying there in a pool of his own blood. I tried to nudge the body but nothing. Somehow my cat has yet to try and walk where he lies. I feel a bruise forming on my lower back from the counter. Even as I write this, his body is still there in my kitchen. Days have passed. I’ve been too scared to try and clean it up, I don’t know how I would. I’m on the verge of just spilling everything out to my mom, maybe just send her the link to this. I don't know what to do. I’m *scared*—is he actually dead?

I need help. 

Has anyone else had this happen to them?
