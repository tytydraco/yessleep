Honestly, I’ll be the first to admit it, when I was growing up. Hearing stories about skinwalkers, witikew’s and all of that from older relatives or elders. I wasn’t the first to super believe it, I more or less believed these spirits disappeared after colonization. Or they ya know? Went extinct. But I’m a dumbass man or so I’ve learnt.

The other night me and my best friend, Dan, we were out for a drive. Key note. Dan is Navajo and I’m mixed but mainly Cree. We both lived away from our communities respectively. This happened in a city we both lived in at the time.

So we are out for a drive. We wanted to go find a chill spot to have a couple beers and smoke a joint. Dan, he mentions knowing a good spot to chill at on the local reservation. I shrug and agree. Less populated and the worst I think could happen is we get into a scrap with other guys. But a big Navajo and big Cree, I figure that’d give most men a pause.

About 15 mins later, he’s turning into a private road. And I’m hitting my vape watching the road ahead when I notice something flicker across the road.

I figure it’s a rabbit what’s the big deal. I glance out the corner of my eye and see Dan frowning. I look back at the road and he says we’re almost there. The road we’re driving up snakes, a few bends and I see it again. Another quick flicker to the point I gasped and asked Dan if he saw it. Dan nodded but said it looked like a rabbit.

He pulls into a parking spot a few minutes later. And I agree it’s a better view than the normal parking lot we would sit in.

I turn the radio up a bit, and Dan reaches into the back seat passing me a beer. I thank him and crack it, he grabs one and he cracks it. We cheers and take a drink. We go back and forth for a bit. Complaining about women, university, missing home and you know. Rinse n repeat. A few beers in Dan says he’s gonna pop outside to smoke a dart. He asks if I want one I say I’m good for the time. He nods and gestures with his head he’s going down by the fence.

I nod and watch him get out. Once the door slams shut I hit the lock. Cause shit, I’m feeling it and I don’t really feel like getting into it. I turn the volume down and scroll through my Spotify. Around this time I notice everything’s really quiet. I look out the window quick and Dan’s leaned up on the fence scrolling but smoking his dart. I look down at my Spotify again, clicking onto a different playlist. Then suddenly I hear foot steps outside the car. Figuring it’s Dan I ignore it. Until I realize I don’t hear anyone by the car.

I look up again and Dan is gone.

I look at the time, it’s been 20 minutes.

Immediately I whip around and I see Dan is now leaned against the trunk of the car. I furrow my brow. Dan normally isn’t the type to post up there but I figure maybe some guy pulled up on us. I lift my phone up and snap a quick pic. I save it to my Snapchat memories and then shut my phone off. Looking around out the window I notice, there’s no cars, no lights and no muffled voices. I look back out the rear view window and Dan’s still there. Rubbing my eyes I decide to click on a random song and turn the volume up. I figure if there is someone out there with Dan, might as well make it known he’s not alone. I then look out the front windshield. This odd feeling comes over me. Almost like static electricity shoots up mh arms. That buzz I was feeling from the beer? It’s gone I feel like I’m sober, like bone cold sober.

In the middle of me staring out the front windshield, I reach for my beer that’s open. Honestly at this point I’m thinking of just chugging it and stepping outside. It’s been thirty minutes-

Knock knock-

I jump and look at the window on the drivers side. It’s Dan and he’s laughing at me, I unlock the door, he gets in and he’s bugging me. Like “oh fuck bro you jumped so high.” He’s immediately reaching for a fresh can when suddenly the radio cuts out.

He’s mid reach and then pauses. He glances at me and I say it’s not me.

I then see him reach again for a fresh can and so I start trying to fix the volume. The radio then blasts on. And in the jump again, I swear out the corner of my eyes I saw someone outside my passenger window. The radio then cuts out again.

The hiss of Dan’s can opening snaps me back to reality and I blurt out.

“Why were you behind the car earlier?”

Dan looks at me weird and tells me he wasn’t. I say he was again.

Dan then pulls his phone out and goes into a random snap convo but clicks on a photo. It’s him. And it’s from tonight. Showing just under half an hour ago. Dan was there at the fence. I ask him what time it says, he says 10:32pm.

I pull out my phone, I pull up my memories, that photo of Dan I snapped earlier 10:33pm.

The distance between the fence were Dan was and the car. It couldn’t be cleared in under a minute. He was further out. Meaning that whoever that was. That was not Dan.

Dan starts awkwardly laughing, I half chuckle. As we both fade into tense silence. We both know that this is not possible. And we may be buzzed, a lil stupid and naive. But we both know too, that this is just bad.

During this silence the sounds of footsteps outside come back. We both freeze. The steps get louder and faster until it sounds like someone should be outside the car. We both whip around and see nothing. Sitting there the radio suddenly went static and cut off again. Dan tries to turn the car back on, but the radio still isn’t working. 

I’m blue toothed to the car, however nothing I’m playing or skipping too is playing audibly and then the sound.

You know on old cable tv cartoons. That tinny fake mechanical laughter?

We’re both hearing it.

Dan is browner than dirt. And I’m olive complexion.

We both look like a A4 white printer paper.

The footstep running sounds are louder.

The laughters getting louder to the point you can feel the dread. It’s that god awful your stomach fell through the ground. It’s prickling in the eyes hot burning tears. 

Before I realized it, I’m yelling at Dan to drive.

He throws it into drive. Neither of us have seatbelts on.

I have my hands over my ears, because up to this point I hadn’t realized I’m hearing shit. I’m hearing voices in indigenous languages I don’t speak. 

As Dan’s peeling out I decide with my lack of brain power to look out the window to where we were.

There’s a 7ft shadow. It’s hunched over but I know it’s looking. It’s looking at me.

Alla sudden in my mind I can see an image of an old granny. She’s got a scarf over her head. The scarfs lift and her eyes are vertical slits. Her mouth is too wide. And she’s smiling.

At this point I finally hear Dan.

He’s screaming what the fuck.

It didn’t occur to me but I’m sobbing.

It still at this point didn’t occur to me that this was not Dan. 

We get back to the apartment about 20 minutes later.

As we get back in, Dan says he’s gonna grab a pack of cigarettes I nod. I say I’m gonna get a coffee or more booze. Whichever is closer. That earned a dry laugh from Dan. Which at this point was comforting. Or so I thought.

Back in the apartment, im sitting in the chair at Dan’s desk in his room. I’m flicking though Snapchat. The normal pics from girls I was chatting too didn’t even catch my attention. 

Eventually I set my phone down and look at Dan, he’s staring blankly at a wall. I break the silence with a 

“What the fuck happened out there?”

Dan looked at me and he said “I wish I knew.”

“You got any sage or medicine bro?” I ask at this point thinking smudging could save my sanity.

Dan points to his tin and I open it grabbing the half used braid of sweetgrass. Normally, I could get any medicine to spark and smoke. Elders would say I had a good touch for medicine. However, at this current moment I’m burning my thumbs on the lighter. I couldn’t get this sweetgrass to catch. Even holding the flame in one spot wasn’t working.

I then noticed Dan was staring intently at me until we both froze.

Hiss.

What the fuck was HISSING.

I jump up, Dan does as well. We are about to start ripping the room apart. When I pointed “dude it’s the air freshener. It’s on a timer right?” The air freshener wall plug in was beside Dan’s open bedroom door. Worth noting it’s not beside either of us or near us.

“It’s a sensor.”

I blink slowly and start laughing. 

Hiss.

Hiss.

Hiss.

Hiss.

“It’s malfunctioning Dan,” I say between the laughs and hisses.

Dan looked away at it as the sensor kept going off “no. I got it yesterday.”

I bite my lip and say “something followed us,”

Dan nods as we stare at the air freshener again.

“I gotta go,” I said looking at the keys to the car, Dan nodded, he grabbed the keys beside him tossing them at me.

I nod saying a quick “bless” and “call you when I get home.”  

I essentially sprinted to the car. 

Once I got in I hit the locks. And sunk into the drivers seat.

Originally I was planning on writing this at home.

However, I’m sitting in the car, it’s like 4:27am. This happened like I said earlier around 10:30pm. I don’t know where 11-4 went. I don’t know.

I was going to call my brother back home. Because maybe he’d know what to do. 

But as I reached to start the car I glanced up. 

Dan was standing at the window from his room. He was staring at me. Which wasn’t weird cause people watch people leave. 

I waved.

He didn’t. 

I waved again.

He didn’t.

At this point I think he’s fucking with me, so I grabbed my phone, opened calls, and clicked his speed dial pop up.

His phone was ringing.

He unmoved continued to stare.

“Your call has been forwarded to voicemail box number 123-456-7890 please leave a message.”

Redial.

Ring.

Ring.

Ring.

“Your cal-“

I’m staring at Dan.

Dan is staring at me.

I wave one last time. This was truly a test.

Unmoved.

I immediately start the car. I don’t even put my seatbelt on and I’m peeling out of there. 

I don’t even notice but pretty quick I got home. Dashed inside and locked my door. 

I slid down the locked door. 

And now we are here. Me typing you reading I’m too scared to call Dan. 

When I got home I noticed I had a voicemail from Dan.

I haven’t listened to it.

So what the fuck do I do?
