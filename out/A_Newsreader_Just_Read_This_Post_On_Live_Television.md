*Anyone in the area heard anything about a murder tonight? Local news is reporting that a woman has been found dead in our sleepy little village, but I haven't heard any sirens or seen any police around. Can't find anything about it online either. Anyone know what's going on?*

That’s what I posted on my local subreddit earlier this evening. Since then I've had a handful of replies, and not one of them has any idea what I'm talking about. One person even checked the local news for me and found nothing about a murder anywhere near here. 

But that’s only the beginning of tonight’s batshit crazy events. Part of me wonders if I’m still asleep and this is all some weird dream. I say wonder. I mean hope.

I’ll start at the beginning.

Today was supposed to be one of my lazy days. You know the kind, where you make *no* plans and make absolutely sure you have precisely nothing to do? Yeah one of those. Well, it *was* one of those days, right up until about a few hours ago.

I’d fallen asleep on the sofa watching a movie, and when I woke up the local news was on. I was still drifting somewhere between asleep and awake when I heard the reporter mention my village. That snapped me awake pretty quickly. I live in a tiny, sleepy village where exactly nothing ever happens. Just the way I like it, because I'm twenty-eight going on fifty apparently. So when I heard the word ‘murder’ in the same sentence as where I live, I paid attention.

*“The remains of a woman in her late twenties were found this morning in \[REDACTED\]. Reports confirm that the remains were found in an upstairs bedroom, and appear to have been deliberately arranged, in what some are calling a ritualistic display. More on this shortly.”*

I was horrified. Something that gruesome happening so close to home was unfathomable. Like I said, my village is tiny, so for something this huge, I figured I'd be able to find something online. Plus, I was curious. Had they caught the killer? Were they still out there? Was I in danger? I needed to know! The newsreader had said it happened this morning, so surely there'd be something online about it by now.

\*\*\*

I tried all sorts of searches. The name of my village followed by ‘murder’, my village followed by ‘dismemberment’, the name of the local news station, and on and on. Every word combination I could think of to dig up anything about this shocking murder. But I got nothing. Zip, zilch, nada. Even the news station’s own website had nothing. Every single search came up empty.

That’s when I made the post to the local subreddit. *Someone* must have heard something. Surely one of the neighbours knew, or maybe it had even happened on their street. I was hopeful, because people love to talk around here. When Dorris a few doors down painted her front door pink last year, it was all anyone talked about for weeks.

While I waited for replies, I headed back to the living room. The same woman read the news, except now she was talking about roadworks or something. Not wanting to listen to that, I dug out the remote and went to put Netflix back on, when something struck me. I'd been watching a movie on Netflix before I fell asleep. It was weird that the TV had switched over to the local news once the movie finished, as that didn't usually happen. But that's about as much thought as I gave it at the time. 

I pressed the Netflix button. The TV crackled briefly, flicked off, then back on… to the local news station. I pressed it again. Same thing. I tried other buttons, but every time, the news channel came back. I turned the TV off and on again, unplugged it, plugged it back in. Same thing. Every. Single. Time.

Great, I thought, no scary movies for me tonight. But I had my computer, so I figured I’d watch something on that instead. When I got back to it, I had a couple of replies to my post, both saying they hadn’t heard anything about a murder. Seeing as I had no say in what the TV showed me anyway, I decided to wait until the woman got back to the murder, record it, and post the video.

That’s when I realised I couldn’t find my phone.

\*\*\*

I assumed it had slipped down the side of the sofa or between the cushions, but no. So I checked all my usual ‘put phone down’ places. The kitchen, the table in the hallway, the toilet, I even checked the fridge, because that happened once. But nothing. I was racking my brain trying to think where it could possibly be when I heard the newsreader talking about the murder again.

*“Police investigating the murder believe the suspect may have been inside the victim’s home for an undetermined amount of time before the attack. Investigators have also confirmed there were no signs of forced entry and are working to establish whether the victim knew their attacker. We’ll bring you further updates as they become available.”*

The thought that I might know both the victim and murderer made me feel sick. I checked both the front and back door, ensuring they were locked. They were. I’d been home all weekend too, so there was no way I wouldn’t have heard someone trying to get in. The newsreader moved on to something about church repairs, and I carried on looking for my phone.

I eventually gave up. I was getting frustrated. I had run out of places to look anyway, and I figured it would turn up at some point. I went back to my computer to see if anyone else had replied to my post, and there were a few more comments. Still, no one had seen the broadcast, and one person even told me to check my carbon monoxide detector. I got the reference, but I checked anyway. 

I was halfway through typing a reply when I heard the newsreader mention Reddit. I’m on Reddit a lot, so that piqued my interest. I went back into the living room to see what she was on about now. 

*“The victim is understood to have made a post on a local subreddit shortly before her death, asking whether anyone had heard reports of a murder in the area of \[REDACTED\]. In the post, she wrote, ‘Local news is reporting that a woman has been found dead, but I haven't heard any sirens or seen any police around.'”*

Yeah. That’s my fucking post. Word for word, that’s exactly what I wrote. My brain did this weird thing where it just sort of… didn’t work for a few seconds, like I’d paused time but only inside my own head. When it finally rebooted, it frantically searched for a reasonable explanation, because…what. The fuck. At first I thought they had probably lifted my post and used it to beef up their coverage, you know, give them something more to talk about. News outlets do that sort of thing all the time, so nothing unusual about that. But then what she said fully hit me. She said victim. She said the victim had made that post.

\*\*\*

I went back to my computer and checked the subreddit again. Maybe there would be a comment from someone at the news channel, or a DM? That would be the courteous thing to do at least. But nope. Nothing. I refreshed the page a few times, because at this point I’m thinking there *must* be some chatter about this online by now. They’ve just read my post out ON THE NEWS. But no. Crickets.

Seeing as I couldn't find my phone, I thought I’d try messaging a friend on instagram, but my account was logged out, which was weird because I never log out of that thing. I tried to log back in, but it told me my password was wrong. I went to reset it via email, but I couldn't get into that either. I checked my X account, my gmail, LinkedIn… you name it, I checked it. I was logged out of every single account that had any way of me contacting the outside world. The only account I still have access to, is Reddit. Before I started writing this post, I went back to my original post and replied to every single comment. I DM’d them and told them to call the police, or come to my home. Bang on my door, I don't care, just come! All the replies went through fine, but no one's answered, and the newest comment on that post is from over an hour ago. I've been refreshing it like a lunatic ever since.

So, I started writing everything down on the one subreddit I *know* people will take me seriously. Here. 

At first I just wanted to ask if any of you had come across anything like this before, or get some advice about what the fuck was going on, but then the newsreader said something that changed everything. 

\*\*\*

*“Police have recovered an online account written by the victim in the hours leading up to her death. The woman appears to have been documenting a series of increasingly disturbing events at her home.”*

\*\*\*

I stopped typing when I heard that. Could be a coincidence, I thought. People in this sub write accounts of weird shit all. The. Time. Probably nothing. 

Then she began reading my post. Word for word. She read *this* post, word for word, on the news. She was getting to the paragraph just above this one, when I thought of a way to really test this. 

Bobble doom bucket. 

Complete gibberish. Just the first three words that popped into my head. Then I waited, thinking there’s no way this woman is going to say bobble doom bucket on the fucking news….

*“Investigators are also examining an unusual phrase contained in the victim's final written account. The words, 'bobble doom bucket', appear towards the end of the document. Police have yet to establish whether they hold any significance.”*

I hadn’t even posted this yet, but yeah. She said it. She actually said bobble doom bucket.

\*\*\*

And, you know, that was a big fat Nope from me. It was time to visit my mum. She lives about an hour away, and that felt far enough to put this whole fever dream behind me. I was not fucking about with whatever this was. No time to even pee, I was outta there.

I shot up, beelined for the hallway, grabbed my car keys, and opened the front door. 

Well, I did not make it out. 

When I opened the door, I was looking at my own hallway, as if I were standing outside my house looking in. It was identical. I closed the door, opened it again, closed it, opened it. I did that a few times, because I honestly didn't know what the hell else to do. I stepped through the door, walked down an identical hallway, into an identical living room, and found an identical newsreader on an identical TV. The post I’d been writing, this post, was at the exact same point too. And nothing was mirrored like you may be thinking. It was an exact replica of my home. 

I went back. Front door, hallway, living room, newsreader. Then back again. I tried the back door next. My hands were shaking so badly it took multiple tries to get the key in the lock. Through the glass I could see the garden, the bins, the washing still out on the line, but when I opened it, I was looking back at my own kitchen. I ran through the kitchen, down the hallway, opened the front door to another hallway, and through another back door, into another kitchen. I lost count how many doors I ran through and how many hallways I ran down. It went on and on and on. 

I sobbed. I screamed. I put Mum’s old fruit bowl through the kitchen window. All that did was provide a permanent view into the same room. I moved to the window, stuck my head through, and screamed for help, knowing it was completely pointless. I didn’t even feel the glass slice into my hands, I only saw the blood drip onto the wooden floor. My legs struggled to hold me, my throat burned from screaming, and a wave of tiredness crept over me as my sobs turned to pathetic whimpers. I was at a complete and utter loss at what to do, and somewhere in the other room the newsreader spoke about church repairs. 

\*\*\*

I was sitting on my bottom step, my hands and arms tacky from dried blood and my eyes stinging, all my tears had fallen, and I was left with the knowing that I probably wasn’t going to make it out. I felt sick. There were so many things I hadn’t done. I didn’t have kids. Did I want kids? I didn’t know, but now i’d never even get the chance to decide. Would I see my mum again? I didn’t even have any of my favourite ice cream in the freezer. 

My thoughts were suddenly interrupted by a weird dragging sound coming from the spare bedroom. I kept as still as I could and listened as the noise continued, as it slowly dawned on me that there was someone in my house. Pure terror invaded every fibre of my being, as the newsreader’s words came back to me…

*“The suspect may have been inside the victim’s home for an undetermined amount of time before the attack.”*

I didn’t believe it. I *couldn’t* believe it. This could *not* be happening. I laughed maniacally, suddenly unbothered by whoever, or whatever, was upstairs playing musical chairs in my spare bedroom. Then the sound changed to a banging, like someone was hammering nails into the wooden floor. 

I had no way of calling the police, or *anyone*, so I figured the next best thing was to make a report online. And so that’s what I did. I went onto every police website I could find and reported that someone was in my home and I didn’t have a phone or any other way of contacting the outside world. I copy pasted the shit out of that report.

Not that it matters now though. But at least I can say I’ve tried just about everything there is, to leave. To contact someone. To tell *someone* about what’s happening to me. We’re not quite at the end yet. 

I decided to go upstairs and see what had been making those noises. It was quiet now, and I was feeling desperate and hopeless, with an unjustified level of bravery. Or maybe I just had zero fucks left. Who knows. 

Well. 

I was in no way prepared for what I saw when I opened the spare bedroom door. 

Laid out before me, and nailed to the floor, was a large blue tarpaulin. The furniture had been pushed up against the walls, and an array of tools I’d never seen before was spread in neat rows across the bed.

At the end of the last row, lined up just as neatly as everything else, was my phone. The screen had been obliterated, and the back cracked clean through, like someone had taken a hammer to it. 

Panic rose as I checked the room for…I don’t know, a person I guess. I’m glad i didn’t see anyone, I would have been utterly useless in that moment. I did go dizzy though, and had to grab the door frame to steady myself, leaving a faint, bloody half-hand print. I shut the door as quietly as I could and backed down the stairs without taking my eyes off it. 

\*\*\*

I’m back at the computer now, writing this post. I think this might actually be my last hope. And I do have hope, I really do. The lady on the news just said something that I think could be important, something that gives me the teeny tiniest bit of hope that this might not go down the way she said it would. 

*“Police have confirmed that multiple reports were submitted through the websites of police forces across the country, believed to be desperate attempts by the victim to seek help. Investigators are still examining the online post written shortly before her death, which appears to document the events leading up to the murder.*

*The final paragraph of the post describes the discovery of several unidentified tools, arranged in rows on the bed of an upstairs bedroom.”*

\*\*\*

She said the post ENDS with the description of the tools. The description of the tools!! HA! I don’t think so, I’m still going motherfuckers, this is NOT over yet.

So YOU are my last hope. I need you to call the police, call the news station, call everyone you know! Please, come help me. Get me out of this fucking nightmare before I end up on the news. 

I live at \[REDACTED\].

Please. Someone. Anyone. GET ME OUT.
