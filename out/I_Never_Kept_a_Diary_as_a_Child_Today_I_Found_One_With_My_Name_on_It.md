Hey, Chris here. Trying to muster some courage with internet strangers for a bizarre situation I have. I found this diary. A diary that’s supposed to be mine.

It says “Chris” right on the cover and I found it behind some old boxes we keep in our shed. There’s one issue, however.

I never wrote a diary when I was a kid.

Yet somehow, I’m holding it right now in my hand. I don’t understand how. But there’s something wrong with it.

It started off fairly normal. It describes events of my childhood that I recall. Things I like, things I don’t. But then it started getting strange.

Like, it’s… getting scary. I don’t have the courage to keep reading on my own. So, I wanted to post here to see if anyone had any similar experiences.

Let me show you what I mean. I’ll read the first passages.

***August 5th***

***“Woke up. Got up. Went downstairs, mom made pancakes. My favorite****!”*

Seemed pretty ordinary so far. I do love my mom’s pancakes. She used to make them every weekend.

Here’s the next one.



***August 15th***

***“Woke up. Got up. Went downstairs, mom made pancakes. My favorite!***

***I was eating them when I saw a man looking at us from outside the window.”***

Now this doesn’t seem ordinary. But it gets worse.



***August 20th***

***“Woke up. Got up. Went downstairs, mom made pancakes.***

***The man outside the window is getting closer.***

***I didn’t eat.”***

This is where I started getting concerned. A man outside the window? What the hell!?

I obviously don’t remember any of this, but I started thinking I had suppressed some part of my life. I’m not sure.

Here’s some more passages that seem normal. I’ll explain as they go.



***“October 1******^(st)***

***Went out of town with grandpa and grandma, near the woods.***

***They told me the same thing as always. Watch out for bears. Never eat near the honey trees.”***

 This I remember. Every Autumn I went to my grandparents’ house. They always said this. It basically just meant to watch out for certain animals in the woods. It was a weird thing to say, but I recall them always telling me that.



***"November 3******^(rd)***

***Used the lake behind the house.***

 ***It was freezing but water keeps them away.”***

I started getting confused with this one. I used to wash in the lake? I don’t remember that. There’s one behind my house but I don’t remember going there in the winter. And I don’t understand who is being kept away.



***January 5******^(th)***

***“Mary went to the supermarket with her mom.***

***I saw her, I wish I could go talk to her.”***

This one seems pretty simple. But there’s just one problem. I don’t know any Mary.

At least I think I don’t. I don’t recall someone named that.

But then I read the next entry.



***January 15******^(th)***

***“I saw Mary today at the park. She’s so pretty. It’s just… It scares me to go near her. She’s playing with her friends. They’re playing hopscotch. I don’t understand how they do it. I don’t understand how…***

***I stayed next to mom. We were going to the doctor’s.***

 ***I just kept looking at them.***

***How do they do it?***

***How are they not scared of that thing with no eyes behind them.”***

I remember slamming the diary shut when I read that.

That’s the last of the entries I read on my own. They’re getting more and more impossible. I remember pancakes, but I don’t recall a man watching us. I remember my grandparents Autumn visits but I don’t remember bathing in the lake near my house.

And I don’t know who this Mary person is… Or what was behind her.

I’m…I’m going to keep reading. I’ll post them as I go.



***May 5******^(th)***

***“Mom sang to me today. She said I’m her one and only. She’ll never let me go.***

***It calmed me down. It eases my nerves.***

***It makes the monsters go away.”***

I…I understand this part. Mom used to sing to me a lot when I was a kid. She did always say that…

“You’re my one and only”.

But monsters? I… I think there’s something wrong with this diary.



***June 15******^(th)***

***“Mom told me they aren’t real. But I see them.***

***Outside my room. Outside her room. Everywhere. They’re everywhere.***

***I’m hiding the key.***

***Help me. Please help me.”***

Fuck. What the hell is this? When did I write this?! Did I see something as a kid that I don’t remember?! I sound crazy.

Okay, I have to stop. This is too much. I’m going to stop reading and I’ll continue tomorrow. I’m not a kid anymore, but this is just too freaky.

I have to process and try to understand what this is about. I’ll talk to you guys tomorrow.

Sorry, I’ll be sure to update this soon.

 

 

Update:

Hey, back again. Some updates as to what happened. It’s been two days, but I have to talk about what happened.

I am now convinced that the diary is mine.

You see, the way it’s written is very unique. The way I write my vowels is sort of different to everyone else. My mom taught me to write this way. It’s an Icelandic thing, just don’t mind it.

It’s certainly my diary, and I certainly don’t remember writing it.

Speaking of my mom, I went to ask her if I wrote a diary when I was young. She just looked at me. I have never seen her face like that.

She looked lifeless. Like I said something wrong.

“No dear. You didn’t.” She replied.

There was a coldness in her voice. I’m not sure what that’s about.

She told me one more thing before I left. I… I froze.

“You weren’t in the shed, were you?”

“No.” I lied.

I had to. She must know something about this.

Something else happened. At the end of the diary is a key. I remembered there’s a locked chest in the shed where I found the diary. I think that’s what the key is for.

I’m afraid of checking. I’m afraid of what’s locked inside. I don’t know what happened to me in the past.

I’m going to keep reading. I’ll post as I go. Let me start from where I left off.

Edit:

I went to the shed. I was so scared; I grabbed the gun. I’m not sure what I was expecting. Maybe a man, maybe a monster from my past. I put the key in and it clicked. I opened the chest.

I wish I didn’t.

It’s full of photos. There are so many of them. Photos of a boy. A boy with my mother. They look so happy. He’s young, probably five years old...

I’ve never seen this boy in my life.

And then there’s a photo of a baby. I think it’s me. There’s a man and a woman holding me. I don’t know who they are.

There’s a series of papers here. I can see them now. They have my name; they have my social security number but they don’t have my face.

They have his.

His face is in every paper. From the prescription form for schizophrenia medication to his obituary. It’s just him.

I… I want to vomit. He looks just like me. I was wrong. This isn’t my diary.

It’s his.

Oh my God… Who the hell am I!?

Shit. My mom just got home. She’s inside the house now. I don’t know what to do, I don’t know what to say.

I’m scared, I don’t understand what’s going on.

I don’t think this woman is my mother. I don’t think my name is Chris.
