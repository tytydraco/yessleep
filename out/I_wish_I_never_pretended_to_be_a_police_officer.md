It was perfect; it had a big gold star with the words “Peace officer” on the top. It was also only $5 on Amazon. 

I looked like the real deal- this was it. I looked at myself in the mirror one last time before heading out, being sure not to wake my parents before I headed out to my patrol. Unfortunately, the police used different radios, and I couldn’t get onto their frequencies, so it never caught anything on their waves.

It’s funny; I had been here countless times as a kid, and I thought it would feel different as a kid, but it felt just as big. The air felt humid as I started to walk around the grounds, as I continued looking for whoever called me here.

As I went to exit the cabin  a voice cried out from my side, “help- help me officer.”
