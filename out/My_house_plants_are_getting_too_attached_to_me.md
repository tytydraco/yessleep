I never thought I’d be writing something like this online but things have gotten seriously weird lately. I’m a plant dad and proud of it. My apartment looks like a jungle. Pothos ferns monsteras succulents you name it I probably have it. I love the green life around me it makes the place feel alive you know. It started subtly maybe two months ago. A vine would reach out a little further than usual. I thought nothing of it just plants doing their plant thing. 

  
Then my prayer plant started moving when I wasn't in the room. I’d come home and find its leaves folded differently than I’d left them. I dismissed it as me being forgetful or maybe a draft. But then I put a small camera up just to check. And sure enough the prayer plant was definitely moving on its own. Not much but enough to be noticeable. It would slowly unfurl its leaves when I entered the room and fold them back up when I left. Like it was watching me. 

  
My friends joked about it called them my sentient houseplants. I laughed along but it was starting to get under my skin. One morning I woke up and my foot was tangled in a rogue pothos vine. It was wrapped pretty tight. I had to pull it off. It left a red mark. I figured I must have just kicked it in my sleep. But it had never happened before. 

  
The next week I was cooking dinner and my philodendron decided to get in on the action. A long tendril reached out and gently brushed my arm. It was almost affectionate. I jumped though. It was unsettling. I moved it away from the counter after that. But it kept finding ways to be near me. Always just a little bit closer. 

  
My biggest plant is a giant monstera. I’ve had it for years. It’s huge its leaves are like dinner plates. It sits in the corner of my living room. I’ve always felt a strange connection to it. Like it understood me. Now I’m not so sure. 

  
Last night I was watching a movie on the couch. The lights were dim. I felt something brush against my hair. I thought it was just one of the smaller plants. But when I looked up a monstera leaf was hovering directly over my head. It was tilted down almost like it was trying to see my face. I could feel its texture on my scalp. It wasn't a trick of the light. The leaf was definitely moving. Slowly deliberately. 

  
I panicked. I pushed it away. It felt wrong like I was hurting something alive. The leaf recoiled. I swear it looked sad. I ended up sleeping on the other side of the apartment. 

  
This morning I woke up to all my plants facing me. Every single one of them. Their leaves were turned towards my bedroom door. My apartment is a sea of green eyes staring at me. I feel like I'm being observed constantly. It's not just the movement anymore. It's the feeling of being watched. They’re everywhere. 

  
I tried to leave the apartment this afternoon. As I reached for the doorknob a thick vine from my money tree wrapped around my wrist. Not tightly enough to hurt but enough to stop me. It was a gentle tug. Like it didn't want me to go. I had to unwrap it. It felt like a struggle. Like I was pulling away from a child’s grip. 

  
I’m back inside now. I don’t know what to do. I’m starting to get thirsty. The plants are still looking at me. They seem to be closing in just a little. Is this what happens when you nurture something too much? Do they become too dependent? Or is something else going on? I just want to know if anyone else has experienced their houseplants developing a… possessive streak. I’m genuinely scared to water them now.
