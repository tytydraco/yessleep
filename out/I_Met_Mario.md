Hi! This is my first reddit post ever, so forgive me if this seems off, or if this just isn’t the norm. I had this dream a long time ago, I was no older than five, yet still all these years later it has stuck with me. It terrified me to my core at such a young age, yet even then I knew it was such a unique experience that I can’t put into words. The only reason I even remember it now is because my coworkers were talking about nightmares they had. This nightmare was so long ago, but it came back to me clear as day.

Something important to know about me when I was little, is that I loved video games. I wasn’t very good at them, so most of my “game playing” consisted of me watching my mom or dad play a game that looked interesting to me. Of course there were a few games I could play. I was quite fond of Epic Mickey 2. It was a game I played with my mom on occasion if I was lucky.

There was one game that I played all the time though. I loved New Super Mario Brothers, and though I wasn’t very good at it, I enjoyed it nonetheless. It wasn’t just the game though, I loved Mario. I had Mario Kart bedsheets, I had a Mario poster with my name on it, my mom even made me a cup with all of the different power-ups on it. Safe to say I was a fan.

I was an only child, and I remember one Halloween my mom let me pick what we went as. You will never guess what I picked. I was Mario, my mom was Princess Peach, and my dad was Luigi. I actually think that we shot some pictures for my birthday with me in the Mario costume shortly after (my birthday is a little bit after Halloween).

Not a lot of time passed, and my sister was born. Because I already don’t like how much information I’ve shared with strangers on the internet, we’ll call my sister Maggie. My already busy parents were busy with Maggie, but that didn’t bother me much. The next Halloween rolled around, and my mom asked me the same question she asked me last year. I picked Mario characters again, but this time there would be some changes.

This time around, I was Luigi and my dad was Mario. My sister was Toad which is actually kind of funny. I should preface that my sister was maybe one or two years old at this point. You probably want me to hurry up and get to the point already; I promise we’re almost there.

Some days went by, and my dads costume arrived. He was at work and I was with my mom. She probably thought I wanted to see the Mario costume (which I did) so she took it out of the box and showed me. The costume was cool, but it wasn’t what matters here. There was a piece of paper that displayed the entire costume.

The paper felt stiff like cardboard, something I wasn’t used to seeing as a kid. I can’t explain why, but to kid me it felt so out of place. It felt ominous for no apparent reason. Like I said, the paper displayed the costume in its entirety. It was what you’d expect, it was a Mario costume. The problem was that it was *very* different from what was displayed on the packaging.

It was Mario’s overalls and shirt. It was his gloves and shoes. It was his hat and mustache. There was one problem. It stood, yet there was no man wearing it. 

Nobody was in the suit, yet it was arranged in such a way as if a person had been there. As if a person *should* be there. There was no face, no skin, yet it stared at me. It was as if it was acknowledging my own existence, and it watched me as I questioned its existence. My mom exited the room as I examined the paper. I don’t claim to know much about evil, and labeling this thing feels silly, but I felt it. Even at such a young age I knew exactly what it was.

The night before I had a dream that I was trying to sleep, but I couldn’t. I couldn’t sleep because something was in my doorway. I didn’t know what it was, or maybe I couldn’t comprehend what it was. I just knew it wanted to hurt me.

It wanted to kill me. I wasn’t aware that I had just given it shape. Or maybe that’s what it always looked like.

I went about my day as usual, but there was this terrible sense of foreboding that followed me throughout the day. I didn’t need an adult to rationalize the situation though, I did that myself. I kept saying that it wasn’t anything to be worried about, I knew I was overreacting. It’s crazy to me that at that age I was weirdly good at rationalizing this thing as if I were an adult trying to calm down a frightened child. That night though I lost all courage.

Something else that is important to know about little me, is that I never went to sleep; ever. I would usually be up at the odd hours of the night playing with my toys, or watching Elmo when I should have been asleep. In December, since I didn’t understand how the calendar worked, I would wake up EVERY night and check the living room to see if Santa had stopped by. The point is I was very good at not sleeping. The reason I’m telling you this is because I need you to know that to me this dream felt *very* real. I still can’t really think of a time in my life where I felt more fear than in that moment.

Enough exposition, I’ll get to the dream.

It was late at night, and I was sitting on the couch. I sat next to my mom who was holding my own sister. I don’t remember if she was feeding her, or just trying to get her to sleep, but the important thing is that it was just us. The room was quiet, except for the ticking of our living room clock and the TV. My mom was watching MTV, but this was late 2000s MTV so she was probably watching Teen Mom 2 or Ridiculousness or something.

All I know is that she was in some sort of trance staring at the TV. My sister wasn’t fussing, she didn’t move. I looked around the living room. It was dark, the only illumination came from the TV. There was this mountain of dread which sat upon me, as my eyes darted around the room. We were alone, and yet–

“Mom. It’s going to kill me.” I didn’t know what I’d meant when I said it. I felt more possessed than anything. Even if the words weren’t my own, I knew them to be true. I looked to my mom for some sort of reassurance, but she remained in her trance. I spoke again, this time of my own free will, but with the same amount of conviction regardless.

“Shhhhhhh,” she told me. “I’m trying to get your sister to sleep.”

“Mom please,” I pleaded. “It’s going to kill me please!” I tried shaking her, but she shrugged her shoulder. She was trying to get my sister to sleep.

I tried this a few more times, and I soon realized she wasn’t going to help me. I often wonder if it was the TV, or the *thing* that made my mother so indifferent to my suffering at that moment. Regardless, I realized pretty quickly I was on my own. As my mother stared into the TV, I stared into the abyss of our kitchen.

The whole house was pitch black (with the exception of the living room thanks to the TV like I’d mentioned earlier). The kitchen was in complete and total darkness. It was more than the absence of light, it was something else, something that even to this day I can’t describe. In order to get to my bedroom I’d have to run through that kitchen. Despite knowing otherwise, I felt much safer here in the living room.

I sat and watched. I sat and waited. Time felt slow. Eventually I looked out into the darkness, and there was Mario.

Well, calling it Mario was wrong because it only resembled him in appearance. In actuality, it was the thing I’d seen on that paper.

It was Mario’s overalls and shirt. It was his gloves and shoes. It was his hat and mustache. There was one problem. It hovered there, not as a costume, but something else.

It was here for me, and I was sitting on the god damn couch. It stared at me, and I stared back at it. I don’t think it minded waiting. It didn’t feel like a hunter waiting to pounce on its prey. Instead it felt like an inevitability that knew its presence was known, and was merely here to confirm the worst. So I sat and I watched; it watched as well.

Finally I slid off the couch and took two steps forward. Then it happened.

The floating mass of clothes lunged at me suddenly and without warning. It wrapped itself around me, coiling like a snake. It was trying to strangle me. I cried out for help. 

“MOM PLEASE! MOM IT’S KILLING ME HELP!”

“Shhhhhhh,” she told me. “I’m trying to get your sister to sleep.”

It continued to suffocate me. I swam a lot as a kid, and was used to not being able to breath, it wasn’t a new feeling. The adrenaline that followed the instinct of survival however, was new. I kicked and I screamed (well I screamed as well as I could) but found it did nothing for me. This thought pounded against my skull. *Oh my god I’m gonna die. It’s gonna kill me, I’m gonna die.*

I persisted in my struggle. I didn’t want to go. Somehow, I can’t remember how, I managed to shimmy my way out of the things grasp. I darted to my room as fast as I could, my mother watched TV. Once I made it inside, I shut my door and climbed into bed.

I stared at my door, and something clicked in my brain.

*This is my dream from last night. This happened to me last night.*

My bedroom door opened, there was something in the doorway.

It was Mario’s overalls and shirt. It was his gloves and shoes. It was his hat and mustache. There was one problem. It stood, yet there was no man wearing it. 

We watched each other. I was paralyzed by fear. I think it knew that. We both waited, I never moved.

*Mom. It’s going to kill me.*

After what felt like several agonizing hours, I began to move slowly. After only a few steps, it stopped once more. We watched each other once more.

*Shhhhhhh. I’m trying to get your sister to sleep.*

Then it moved again.

And again.

Until eventually, it was at the foot of my bed.  My head rested against my pillow as I looked straight forward, hoping it would go away. It did not. Instead it watched. I knew it was over, and the anticipation of my own death was killing me.

I waited.

And waited.

Until eventually, it killed me. It simply wrapped itself around me, and killed me. I didn’t fight back. I let it happen. I know this was a dream because I am not dead, but that didn’t make the dream feel any less real.

I haven’t had a dream about that costume since. In fact, I acted like the dream never happened. I didn’t think it mattered, and I knew it was weirdly dark for a kid my age to come up with. Mind you, I was still watching Mickey Mouse and Spongebob. This seemingly came out of nowhere.

Ever since I remembered this dream, I’ve thought about it a lot. Truth be told, part of me wants to be a writer. If my horrible grammar is any indication, I’m not very good. Either way, I knew I had to get this story out there. Maybe my story will resonate with someone.

Believe it or don’t, that’s entirely up to you. This was a real dream I had. It was bizarre and terrifying. I haven’t felt fear like that since I was somewhere between the ages of five and six. Even now my stomach feels tense writing this.

I just wanted to get it out there.
