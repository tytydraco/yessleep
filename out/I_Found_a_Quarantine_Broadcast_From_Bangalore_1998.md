My name is Oliver Wright, and I am 27 years old. For past three years, my hobby has been hunting for lost VHS tapes and digitizing them. Many nations used different VHS systems, so I have a multi-system VHS as I am not just limited to one country only. Finding a tape in the wild is often rare and so I spend most of my time scouring flea markets, garage sale, antique dealers and bulk online lots. Finding a good VHS tape is like playing a lottery. Because, VHS tape relies on a magnetic tape, half of the time you pop in cassette and get nothing but tracking snow, warbled audio, or decades of magnetic decay. Other times, you hit jackpot and get old 1990s car commercials, local forgotten commercials, bird watching footage, public service announcements and home movies no one has seen in last thirty years. I also get to see movies from different parts of world. I am also familiar with various television logos and studios which could be found all over the world. Then I digitize the footage and upload them. I would say that unpredictability is fun part of the game. No one knows what you might get unless you watch the footage directly.

Last week was no different. I found 12 tapes on a reputable online lot. All 12 tapes were watchable and good quality. The box was covered in surprising amount of dust and a powdery substance. I wiped it all clean and paid it no attention This was surprising to me. Usually, even with reputable online lots, one or two tapes were degraded but all 12 tapes being in good condition was like hitting a lottery jackpot. I was overjoyed by the find and praised my luck too. The 10 tapes showed old commercials, a public service announcement about dangers of smoking and drugs, nothing too unusual for 1990s. Then I decided to come to eleventh tape and put it in. It started with colorful jingle and the lyrics for opening credits for an Indian live-action show called Shaktimaan was shown. It was nothing odd. Many Indian families in US had relatives and they might have sent those tapes to them. I looked at the duration. It was about an hour long. First 10 minutes went completely normally. There was a crime scene, Shaktimaan came and started looking for criminals. It was quite entertaining even though I couldn’t grasp the language completely. I noted the channel and it was DD national India.

Then the show started flickering. I didn’t think it was odd, even though tapes might be of good quality, some signs of degradation might be visible. The tape went black for twenty seconds and then it came back with a sharp hiss. I could see the Ashoka emblem of India and it displayed crisp text. There was no background music.  
The voiceover started: “This is the Government of India, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Directorate of Health Services – Karnataka. It is a special bulletin issued for the benefit of public health and welfare.”

Then it cut to a newsreader in brightly lit Doordarshan studio. He looked directly at the camera and behind him a plain blue background with Doordarshan logo could be clearly seen. His posture was rigid and straight. His eyes were unblinking and focused on something in the distance and his chest rose and fell in rapid shallow rhythms.

After few seconds he started speaking. His voice was crisp and it was underlaid with text. The tone was slightly robotic.

“Namaskar. We interrupted our scheduled telecast on DD National for an urgent health bulletin issued by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare in coordination with the government of Karnataka. “

My thumb hovered over the pause button of VCR remote. The environment in my environment changed. The ambient hum of my digitizing equipment felt suffocatingly loud. I felt a sharp pit drop in my stomach at his robotic and emotionless tone. He continued reading from papers.

“Effective 1100 today March 7 1998, a mandatory quarantine and cordon have been imposed and enforced across the Bangalore Rural and Bangalore Urban areas following the detection of highly contagious, acute and unclassified neuro-respiratory infection.

All residents in the demarcated areas are strictly advised to remain indoors until further notice. District authorities have imposed a complete prohibition of movement through National Highway 4, National Highway 7 and all other railway corridors originating from Bangalore City junction.”

I suddenly sat up straight. I didn’t know exactly about the population of Bangalore at that time, but the Bangalore region is quite big metropolitan area. It should have been in global news  for months. I hit pause and picked up my laptop. I pressed continue and the newsreader continued ahead. I decided to search for any evidence regarding this story.

“Citizens experiencing sudden onset of high fever,  sudden fatigue, audiovisual hallucinations, ocular hemorrhage must report to the authorities and be quarantined in well-ventilated room. Citizens have experienced familiar voices or figures. Citizens have also reported that these figures wore white dresses and showed signs of extreme abnormalities regarding feet. Under no circumstances must they be accepted. Please note, do not accept any gift or offering, regardless of its apparent familiarity or personal significance.

Do not attempt to leave the quarantine perimeter. Violators will be detained under the Epidemic Diseases Act of 1897.”

I paused for a moment. The description and symptoms of this disease were odd. I almost thought that I misheard something, but he continued on reading.

“Further bulletins will be telecast every half hour. Stay indoors. Remain calm. Jai Hind."

The announcement repeated one more in Hindi and Kannada  and then it faded with sharp PAL tracking snow and visible static.  I didn’t know what to think of it. My first thought was that it couldn’t be real, it might have been a prank. I decided to watch the footage again for any evidence of such altering or cuts but there were none. It was as if someone really recorded the footage on the VHS in one sitting. I had to go deeper and look for more evidence. I searched for newspaper articles, medical reports, anything on that disease in Bangalore but I found nothing. I looked in forum posts. I delved deeper and the evidence was convincing. It was a hoax and someone really played a prank on me.

I decided to move on and put the last tape in the system. It was another announcement and it was dated March 9 1998 by Doordarshan. This was different news anchor. He looked older than him and was reporting on weather in a stoic, polite and formal way. There was no background music. Someone handed him a paper. He looked surprised at this but then his expressions became stoic and formal. He decided to read it aloud in crisp voice.

“Namaskar.” His voice took on a slightly warm tone and then it became stoic and professional. “We are here to report that  an unauthorized signal intrusion interrupted regional transmissions, and a hoax was displayed on March 7 1998 about a quarantine regarding an infectious disease. The authorities have confirmed that there is no such disease and no such quarantine has been undertaken. The perpetrators are still unknown at the time, and the police is currently investigating. If you have any information regarding the perpetrators, please report them to Police authorities. Now on to next segment.” He spoke in Kannada and moved on to next segment.

I could not help but think something was wrong. I decided to watch the last tape again but there were no signs that something was wrong. If there was a hoax, then it should be in news. I searched for news reports, police reports but there was no evidence that such a hoax happened in Bangalore. I felt a faint headache and ringing in my ears. I felt my vision blurring. So, I decided to go to sleep.

I woke up feeling slightly fatigued. I realized I was bleeding slightly from eye. I smelt a flowery scent which was odd because I have never had flowers in my apartment. I saw a figure standing in the kitchen. It was my mom. “Mom.” I exclaimed with enthusiasm and then she turned back. Her gaze was gentle. I felt relieved at her sight. I looked again at her and my heart dropped into my stomach. She was wearing a white dress and while she was facing me, her feet were backwards and pointed in a opposite direction to me. She spoke in murmur. It was hard to hear. “Oliver. Here have it. I made it for you.” I saw that she was offering me a sweater. It was a sweater like she used to make in my childhood. This was a sweater that I lost. I moved to accept the sweater but suddenly the warning rang fresh in my ears. I must not accept the gifts. I ran away and locked myself in the bedroom. I could still hear her outside my bedroom.

I need to know if anyone in Bangalore remembers what happened in March 1998. Did anyone survive whatever that broadcast was warning about?

And if someone recognizes this thing, please tell me what happens if I refuse the gift.

She has stopped asking.

I can hear her crying now.
