I suppose I should be more understanding. After all, he'd know exactly the pain that my mother felt right now.

"I appreciate that, Tom. Not trying to be an a-hole, but I think you need to focus on yourself right now if you're feeling that scared. I'll be okay." I couldn't bring myself to curse in front of Thomas. It just felt wrong, like people that yell at their pets or throw cans out of the window on the freeway.

"Of course. I'll be fine; whatever comes my way."

His residence wasn't very flattering; an outdated apartment with a cracked foundation and moss-covered benches. At the very least, the inside was kept relatively clean by all the tenants. The familiar scent of old people and cheap carpet detergent could be smelled from the front door.

I opened it for him, while he gave me an exaggerated tip of an invisible hat in return. We shared a laugh.

"Well then, young man! I'll leave you to the rest of your day. Perhaps I'll see you tomorrow?"

"With any luck, it'll be without these flowers being an eyesore."

Thomas nodded strongly, giving the outside one last look before shuddering and waving me off.

I stepped from the doors and looked out to the street in front of me blankly. I wasn't sure what I was going to do with the rest of my day. I hated when plans fell apart and my schedule was suddenly empty. Empty hands were the Devil's playground, after all.

A scent wafted past my nose as I made my way from the building. Thick and nauseating, the stench was mild at first, but quickly built into a full-on brutal assault of my senses. I stifled a gag as I looked around for the source. I could hear cracking from a tree nearby.

Against my better judgment, I walked closer to inspect what was causing the foul smell. Several hand-sized growths expanded from the surface of the tree's roots. Like tumors slowly springing from skin, they expanded further and further from its bark. The pressure made its crust crack, tiny splurts of crimson liquid splattering on the ground and pooling into small puddles. The smell was like sickeningly sweet caramel mixed with week-old wet dog food.

With its appearance and foul smell, it was completely unmistakable. Torrents of blood poured from the openings, some parts runny while others were chunky and coagulated. Decorated in the viscera, and lying across the ground was another familiar sight that made my teeth clench. Just like last night, this tree was infected with them too.

The flowers. By the dozens.

My hand shot to my mouth and nose, trying to block whatever of the smell I could while I backed away in horror. Audible pops and cracks continued from the tree as I turned and ran. I wanted to excuse what I was seeing from the stress I'd been through lately, but everyone else was seeing it too. What the hell was happening?

Making it back to Dancing DJ's within minutes, I threw the door of my car open and dove inside. My chest heaved up and down as I struggled to catch my breath from burning lungs. I never slowed down, not once. I caught my pale reflection in the mirror, looking deep into my own eyes.

I caught the gaze of something else. Behind me. Wide-eyed and eager.

A yell erupted from me as I swung backwards. My eyes scanned everywhere in and around my Bronco. There was nothing. Not in the back seat, not outside of the car.

Clearing my throat, I brought the key to the ignition before hissing in pain. While panicking, I drove my funny bone directly into the metal brace of the head rest while tweaking a muscle in my back at the same time. The radio buzzed while connecting to the airwaves.

We are officially now under a travel advisory. Please do not travel unless completely necessary. If you do, avoid the interstate, along with roads of high population."

I tuned out the radio as my hands flew to my pocket for my phone. Fingers tapping sloppily on the screen as I pulled up Google, the most frequented searches immediately showed recent popular auto-fills, such as "red flowers", "gas leaks", "blood coming from ground", and "demonic nightmares". I couldn't make sense out of any of it. Luckily, the home page had multiple news headlines.

The first page I clicked on had an article that read:

"BREAKING NEWS: MYSTERIOUS FLOWERS APPEAR"

"The first reports of the strange flowers budding from the ground and other plants started in the early hours last night, between 1 AM and 2 AM. Multiple call-ins cited these growths causing feelings of dread, anxiety, paranoia, and fear. Many accounts report that the flowers are in appearance to 'Scorpion Grasses' or 'Forget-Me-Not' flowers. While not confirmed officially, social media posts from across the internet indicate that these flowers have suddenly bloomed everywhere across the globe."

"Social media is being flooded with posts across the U.S, Japan, Russia, Germany, China, India and Mexico. Afterwards, the feeling of a 'presence' wou-"

Completely enraptured in my reading, a sudden, loud siren made me jump so hard that I accidentally honked my horn. Multiple police cars and a fire truck had just pulled around the corner, skirting past our parking lot at high speeds. The screeching of their tires against the asphalt echoed against the empty street and disappeared in the direction of the city.

I was glad to be broken out of my trance. I didn't feel safe out here. I needed to go home immediately, and if these reports were true, it was gonna be hell getting there. I couldn't be completely sure, but I decided to take the street way home after seeing the emergency vehicles turn toward the direction of the highway.

I should call Mom when I get home.
