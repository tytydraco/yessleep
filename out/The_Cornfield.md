Hello, members of HQ.  
My name is Adrian Yami. I’m 24M, and I’m currently the lead researcher assigned to Anomaly Number .  
As most of you know, I received this position after the untimely passing of my father, Alexander Yami.  
I wasn’t supposed to inherit his research.  
At least, that’s what I was told.  
Three days ago, while going through his old office, I found a box hidden beneath the floorboards.  
Inside were his journals.  
Most of them were mundane. Research notes. Weather observations. Interviews with locals. Photographs of the anomaly.  
Then I found a letter.  
It was addressed to the members of HQ.  
I think you should read it.

**“Since coming to this country, I’ve always been interested in its landscape.**  
**Unfortunately, I’m stuck here, researching this 100-square-kilometer radius.**  
**I don’t understand it.**  
**The locals are calling it the Cornfield Killer, but we’ve never found a single person responsible for the murders.**  
**They all seem paranormal.**  
**There’s absolutely no way a single person has kept murdering people around here for over 180 years.**  
**I also find it interesting that the locals have only one rule around here:**  
**‘If you ever feel watched around the cornfields… you are.’**

**The rule is ridiculous, and my researchers keep using it to scare the younger colleagues.**

**Frankly, I’ve never been that brave of a man.** 

**Maybe that’s why I’m writing all of these journals.**  
**I’ll finish this one after a walk.”**

That was where the first page ended.  
The next paragraph was written in handwriting I barely recognized.

**“They were all absolutely right.”**

Then:

**“I don’t understand.**  
**I was walking past a well-lit cornfield. The same one I’ve always been passing by on my walks.**  
**But it seemed incredibly dense.**  
**I felt eyes watching me constantly.**  
**God, why must I be so perceptive?**  
**I feel like I’m dying. My nose won’t stop bleeding. My vision’s going blurry.**  
**I’ve noticed it from the moment I passed the cornfield.**  
**I’ve had three shadows. Constantly. Under any light.**  
**Gosh, is that a coincidence?**  
**No.**  
**It can’t be.”**

That was the end of the letter.

Except it wasn’t.

There was another page.  
It had been folded several times, and there was dried blood along the edge.  
The handwriting became progressively worse.  
**“They’re getting closer.”**  
**“I can hear them in the corn.”**  
**“There is no wind tonight.”**  
**“The corn is moving anyway.”**

Then a single sentence, written so hard that the pen had torn through the paper:

**“THERE ARE THREE OF THEM AND THEY ALL LOOK LIKE ME.”**

I stopped reading after that.  
I went outside.  
I needed air.  
I walked past the same cornfield my father had described.  
It was 6:17 PM.  
The sun was still up.  
The road was completely empty.  
The field was perfectly still.  
And then I noticed something.  
My shadow was on the road.  
Normal.  
One shadow.  
I laughed at myself.  
I actually laughed.  
Then I looked toward the corn.  
And my stomach dropped.  
There was a second shadow.  
It wasn’t attached to anything.  
It stretched from between the rows of corn and reached across the field.  
I stepped backward.  
The shadow moved.  
I stepped forward.  
It moved again.  
Then I realized something worse.  
There was a third one.  
Behind me.  
I didn’t turn around.  
I ran.

I’ve spent the last six hours inside the research facility.  
Every light in this building is on.  
I’ve locked the doors.  
I’ve called HQ twelve times.  
Nobody is answering.  
And now I’m sitting in my father’s old office, staring at the security monitor.  
The cameras outside are showing the cornfield.  
Nothing is moving.  
Nothing except the corn.

This place is cursed. And i understand that i am basically already dead. If my father couldn’t save himself, i most likely cannot either.  
I will keep every light on. I will also send this message to the internet… please… whoever you are reading this…  
Remember…  
If you feel watched near the cornfield…  
You are being watched.
