   
   
 Thursday 18th June  
   
This is my first time writing in a diary. I’m not really sure how to start this. I’ve always been told that doing this could be good for my mental health, so I guess we’ll see.  
   
I’m about to be a first-time mom at age 34 using a sperm donor I found on facebook. It’s pathetic and weird but I really don’t care. I’m sick of waiting for the so called “right guy” to come along and I’m perfectly happy being single anyway. My baby boy could arrive any day now. I’m excited but terrified. I’ve done my best to prepare but I still have no idea what to expect. Will I be a good mom?  
Can I do this?  
I’ve always wanted to have a baby; I just so desperately want to do it right.  
   
   
   
20th June   
   
Max is here! It happened so fast I think I’m still in shock. My water broke while I was in bed, I called maternity and they told me to go in so I got an uber (not like I have anyone to drive me)   
I got there and everything was going fine apart from this student nurse that messed up my cannula. I am not afraid of needles at all but feeling that nurse wriggling the needle around inside my hand trying to find a vein was so uncomfortable. I actually felt physically sick. Now I have a bruise that covers most of the back of my hand. But anyway, less than three hours after the cannula he was born, it was crazy it was like HE took over. I felt his tiny body wiggling its way out of me and just like that he was placed on my chest.  
   
Just like that I was a mom. He didn’t cry, he just lay there looking right at me. I can’t explain the feelings I felt in that moment. There was just so many feelings all at once, it was overwhelming. He’s mine and I love him; this is all I’ve ever wanted.  
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
21st June  
I am home with Max. It’s just me and him now, our little family. No Nurses to help when I need it and no food brought to my bed. It’s a bit daunting but I have to keep telling myself I can do this. Now that I’ve had my baby I need to bond with him, get to know him like the baby book says.  
Something I’ve noticed about Max is that he’s very alert, always staring with these big dark eyes. And he still hasn’t cried. I mentioned it to a Nurse, but she just said, “He must be content.” Which I suppose is a good thing, content is a good word.  
   
   
22nd June   
   
Breastfeeding hurts, and it’s so hard. No one mentions that or warns you while you’re pregnant. The book never mentioned it either. It's supposed to be this super natural thing between you and your baby but it’s a pain in the ass. Sometimes he latches right away but other times he just moves his head around like he’s looking for the nipple that I’m literally putting straight into his mouth. I’m not giving up though, my milk is what’s best for him, that’s what everyone says.  
   
It’s around 1am and I’m trying to breastfeed, but it hurts. The midwife said it’s normal to hurt in the beginning until you get used to it so maybe I’m overreacting. But it feels like he’s biting me, like he has teeth. This isn’t what imagined it would be.  
   
It’s about 4.30am. I thought I was prepared for everything, but I’m definitely not prepared for how tired I feel. I love Max though, my favourite thing about him is just holding him in my arms and looking at his perfect little face, smelling the top of his head and having his tiny hand grasp my finger. Hopefully I’ll get used to this tiredness.  
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
24th June   
   
Still not used to the tiredness. And still not used to the pain when breastfeeding. I swear that when he’s feeding, he’s drawing blood, I swear I’ve seen it. But no one believes me, I told the midwife, but she just told me to use nipple cream. She said some soreness is normal. This isn’t just soreness though, it feels like little pin-like teeth piercing the skin on my breast. I must just be exhausted; I’ve heard that you can hallucinate when you’re lacking sleep. That has to be it.  
   
It’s 3.35am and Max is latched on like a leech. It hurts but I think I’m getting used to the pain. I’ve never felt so tired in my entire life. I’m trying to focus on the positives, like my baby. This is what I’ve always wanted.  
   
   
25th June  
I had a visit from my midwife today. I showed her my breasts, but she didn’t seem to see anything wrong. To me they are red raw and I swear I can see these tiny bite marks like pin pricks. Something could be wrong with me. The midwife said the lack of sleep could be affecting my mood. I could have postpartum depression. I know it’s common and a lot of Women get it but I’m still disappointed and ashamed. I just wanted everything to be perfect.  
   
26th June  
I don’t think I would even know what day or time it was if I didn’t have a phone. Everything feels like a dream, nothing feels real anymore. The baby never cries but I can’t sleep. But neither does he. He just lies there staring with this blank stare. And the only reason I know to feed him is because he moves his head to one side and makes that suckling movement with his mouth. It’s like he exists to feed. And I know I sound crazy but that’s just how it seems right now. I don’t know why the midwife can’t see the bite marks, but I see them. I do love my baby, but something is wrong with him. He isn’t normal. I’m going to make a doctor's appointment in the morning.  
   
27th June  
I don’t understand what’s happening. I’m so confused. Nothing makes sense. According to everyone else Max is perfectly healthy and normal, and nothing is wrong with me. Why does no one else see what I’m seeing?  
It’s about midnight and I’ve been thinking about this all day. Max may not be a normal baby; I’ve never seen him sleep or heard him cry. It even feels insane to write this down, but he feeds off my blood. Like an actual vampire, or Leech or demon. I can’t believe I’m even writing this. Maybe I’m losing my mind, nothing feels real or normal anymore. But even so, even if Max isn’t normal, he’s still my baby. I grew him inside of me. He’s part of me, I’m his mom. And being his mom means I take care of him and protect him. When I got pregnant with him I did it on purpose, because I wanted to be a mom. I made a commitment to this and I am going to honour that. I love my baby no matter what.  
   
28th June  
I love my baby.  
I can do this.  
I can do this.  
I can do this.  
I feel so weak all the time, everything hurts. It has to be the exhaustion or maybe the blood loss. My breasts look like chewed up pieces of meat at this point. Max is hungrier now. Feeding more, I need to find a way to keep my strength up.  
I’ve been getting my groceries delivered, I just can’t face going out. And to be honest I really don’t want to. I’ve been ordering a lot of energy drinks to try and keep me going and it does help. They give me a little boost, but they do nothing for the constant buzzing in my head or all over body aches.  
I just need to dose myself up and get on with it. Start ordering pain killers with the groceries, I’ll be fine  
I can do this!  
I love my baby!  
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
I’m not sure what day it is. I’m so tired I can’t remember where I put my phone, it’s probably dead. I’ve got such a bad headache that the lights hurt my eyes. I have the curtains drawn and the main lights off. I’m only using the table lamp so that I can see what I’m doing. The buzzing in my head is louder. My breasts look awful, but I don’t care about the pain anymore.  
I’m scared. I’m feeling so weak I can barely get out of bed. I’m keeping Max in bed with me, I lay him by my side so he can feed off me whenever he needs to. There’s blood everywhere. All over me, staining the bed sheets. Even Max’s diapers are full of it. But what else would they be full of when all he consumes is my blood.  
If I can’t keep my strength up, then what will happen to Max?  
He’ll starve without me.  
I’m really scared.  
   
I think it’s Tuesday. I heard the garbage truck, they come on a Tuesday.  
When I try to stand the room spins. I’m relying on whatever I can reach from the bed. Which is basically this diary, baby wipes and diapers and the now empty water bottle.  
I think I’m going to die in this blood-soaked bed.  
And if I die no one will find me for a while, I’ll rot in this bed. I don’t have any family. I don’t have any friends. I don’t even have any more scheduled home visits from the midwife. I’m alone. I’m all alone in this blood-soaked bed with Max attached to me like a leech draining me, he never let's go anymore.  
If my baby has a soul, then I hope his and mine will be together in the end.  
I hope we can go to heaven together.  
I hope there is a heaven.  
I’m so scared that there isn’t.
