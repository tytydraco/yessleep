When I moved into apartment 407, my roommate gave me three rules.

He didn't explain them.

He just wrote them on a piece of paper and stuck it to the refrigerator.

1. Never open the front door after midnight.
2. If someone knocks three times, don't answer.
3. Never look through the peephole after 11:11 PM.

I laughed when I read them.

"Are you serious?"

My roommate, Daniel, didn't laugh.

"I'm serious. Especially the third one."

I assumed he was just one of those people who enjoyed creepy jokes.

For the first two weeks, everything was normal.

Daniel was a quiet guy. He worked nights, rarely invited friends over, and always locked his bedroom door before leaving.

Then, on Thursday night, something strange happened.

At exactly 11:11 PM, someone knocked on our front door.

Three times.

Knock.

Knock.

Knock.

I looked at the clock.

11:11.

I remembered Daniel's rules and decided to ignore it.

A few seconds later, my phone vibrated.

It was a message from Daniel.

**Daniel:** Don't open the door.

I replied.

**Me:** Someone's outside. Who is it?

He responded immediately.

**Daniel:** Just stay in your room. Don't look through the peephole.

I started getting nervous.

Then I heard a voice from the other side.

"Hey, it's Daniel. Open the door."

I froze.

Daniel was supposed to be at work.

I called him.

He answered after the first ring.

"Don't move," he whispered.

I could barely speak.

"There's someone outside saying he's you."

Daniel went silent.

Then he asked something that made my blood run cold.

"What is he wearing?"

I looked toward the door.

I couldn't see the person, but I could see a shadow underneath it.

"Black jacket. Jeans. White shoes."

Daniel started breathing heavily.

"Listen to me. Whatever happens, don't let him inside."

Then the call disconnected.

I spent the next twenty minutes sitting on my bed.

Eventually, I heard footsteps moving away from the door.

I thought it was over.

But curiosity got the better of me.

I walked toward the entrance.

The clock showed 11:34 PM.

I remembered the third rule.

Never look through the peephole after 11:11 PM.

But why?

I leaned forward.

And looked.

The hallway was empty.

Almost.

At the far end, near the elevator, stood a man.

He was wearing a black jacket, jeans, and white shoes.

He was facing my apartment.

I couldn't see his face clearly.

Then he slowly raised his hand.

And waved.

I jumped backward.

My phone vibrated.

A message from Daniel.

**Daniel:** Did you look?

I didn't reply.

Another message arrived.

**Daniel:** Please tell me you didn't.

I typed:

**Me:** I saw someone in the hallway.

Daniel called immediately.

His voice was shaking.

"Listen carefully. I'm coming home. Don't look outside again."

"Daniel, who is that person?"

He didn't answer.

Instead, he asked:

"Did he look like me?"

I stopped breathing.

I hadn't noticed it before.

The man in the hallway was wearing exactly the same clothes Daniel had described.

The same black jacket.

The same jeans.

The same white shoes.

Then Daniel said something I'll never forget.

"That's impossible. I'm standing outside your apartment right now."

I dropped my phone.

Because someone had just knocked on my bedroom door.

Three times.

And from the other side, I heard my own voice.

"Hey. It's me. Let me in."

I looked at the clock.

11:41 PM.

My phone was still on the floor.

A new message appeared.

It was from Daniel.

**Daniel:** Whatever you do, don't answer the bedroom door.

Then another message arrived.

From an unknown number.

**Unknown:** You shouldn't have looked through the peephole.

I slowly turned toward my bedroom door.

The handle started moving.

And then I heard Daniel's voice from the other side.

"Don't worry. I'm the real one."

But my phone rang again.

This time, the caller ID said:

**DANIEL — INCOMING VIDEO CALL.**

I answered.

Daniel was sitting in his car.

He looked terrified.

He whispered:

"Why are you standing outside my bedroom?"

I couldn't move.

Because I was still sitting on my bed.

And the person outside my bedroom door was now laughing.

Then Daniel said:

"There's something I forgot to tell you about apartment 407."

I whispered, "What?"

He looked directly into the camera.

**"You don't have a roommate. You've been living alone for six months."**
