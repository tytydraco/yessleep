My first time returning home after college was a complete nightmare.

I am twenty-four, two years out of college, and returning to my hometown for the first time since I left. I got lucky and caught an Engineering job out of college. I stayed there and worked until I found out about my fathers declining health.

You see, my father is a complete shut-in.

He wasn't always that way. No, he used to be my hero. I guess every boy looks up to his father and remembers the first time they witnessed them do something incredible. My father was no different and for me it was starting a chainsaw. Then when I was a boy at school my father came back to find my mother in bed with another man.

Well, my father snapped. He didn't plan on the man being 'Big Joe' from up the street. No, my father got the life beat right out of him. I remember coming home to them dragging him out on a stretcher. Big Joe leaving in handcuffs.

My mom divorced my dad soon after. Dad didn't take the whole thing very well. He became a shut-in. You know how one day as children we just didn't go to hangout and never did again? We never see it coming but people grow apart right? I think it was like that for dad and the outside. I don't think he was scared of it so much as he just stopped going out.

I had managed to relocate and find a job closer to my hometown. I had signed the lease and was on my way to surprise my father. The holiday season was coming up and I hadn't told him I was visiting.

I couldn't wait to see my old bedroom. Probably still have car posters over all the walls. Dad and I worked on this old 1966 Volkswagen Beetle when I was just a boy. I got seriously into mechanics then engineering because of him. He never drives the thing and it's probably still under a cover in the garage.

When I pulled into the driveway I could tell something was off immediately. The house looked out of repair.

See dad was never a rich man by any means. We had an older white single story house just outside of town. It had a dirt driveway and a detached garage. Dad took pride in doing most of the work himself every year.

The gutters were clogged.

The lawn wasn't mowed.

One of the shutters was hanging loose.

I got out of the car. I fished the bag out of the trunk I brought groceries for dinner in. I planned on making a couple of steaks and maybe having a beer with the old man. Beer in one hand and bag in the other, I walked up to the front door. I sat the beer down and knocked.

There was some noise inside before the door opened just a crack.

"Who's there?" A weak raspy voice said from the crack.

"It's me. Mark? Your son!" I said with a smile.

He gave me a weak smile back. "I-I didn't know you were coming."

"I figured I'd surprise you." I picked the beer back up. "Are you going to let me in dad?"

He hesitated a moment, "yeah come in." He opened the door further with some effort. Pushing a stack of newspapers to the side with it.

The horror of the house came into view as the papers moved out of the doors way.

I knew my dad was a shut-in but I was never informed he had become a hoarder.

"Dad. What's all this?" I was shocked to see the stacks of papers and bags. Amazon boxes everywhere, neat little channels down the middle of the rooms I could see.

"I-" He started then seemed to be lost for words.

I stepped inside. The smell hit me next. The smell of trash and sewage. No, not actual sewage I suppose, I didn't see any feces or anything. Just rotten food, debris, trash piled in the back room, and flies. God the flies.

"Dad, I didn't know you were living like this." I was flabbergasted.

"I didn't want you to see this. I didn't know you were coming. I would have picked it up." He said nervously as I walked into the dining room to set the bags on the table.

"Where can I?" The table was completely full.

"Oh. Uh. Over here." He walked carefully into the kitchen to a small clean spot on the counter. "Make sure you don't step there," he made an exaggerated step, "It's a little soft."

I followed him and set the bag down. "Can I put the beer in the fridge?" I asked.

"Doesn't work. Hasn't in a while." He said. "You shouldn't be here."

"For fuck sakes dad." I wasn't thinking about it. I put the beer on the counter and slid it where it wouldn't fall off. Something fell on the other side of the counter.

Something very large shifted on the other side of the house.

"CAREFUL!" My dad moved frantically to pick up what had fallen. An expired can of corn. He rushed to put it back and pushed the beer onto the floor.

The thing shifted again.

"Sorry! Sorry!" He looked around the room frantically.

"Dad, what's going on?" My blood ran cold. I had never seen my father this terrified. Of his own house at that. "What is that sound?" I moved towards where the noise came from.

"No!" He caught the back of my jacket and stopped me. "You must be careful. It doesn't like change." He looked like a frightened child when I looked back at him. Sweat beading on his forehead. He was shaking.

Literally shaking in his boots.

"Dad. What the fuck is back there?" Now, I am scared.

He searched for an answer and came up empty. "I-I don't have any idea."

"We need to call someone then." I dug my phone out.

"No, ya can't do that son. It gets territorial." He warned.

"Territorial? Dad what the fuck is going on?"

"Ya know after you left I just started buying stuff online? I guess it started with model cars, I'd put them together and it would remind me of the old days. Well eventually I was on some corner of the internet I suppose I shouldn't have been on." He backed away and leaned against the counter.

"What did you get?"

He exhaled slowly, "I just wanted a pet. Something cool. Something not everyone had."

"A pet?"

"I ordered it from this guy in Romania, it was supposed to be easy to take care of." He muttered.

"What is it?" I asked.

"I don't know. It started small and grew so quickly." He teared up. "One day when I went to take out the trash it drug me back through the house. It doesn't like change." A tear ran down his cheek. "You shouldn't have come."

"This is absolutely ridiculous. We need to leave and call someone. Lets fucking go." I took his hand and made a move for the door.

Vertical.

Horizontal.

Dragging.

I've never been pulled by my ankle through a hoarder's house but let me tell you it was not fun. Loose papers ran along every inch of bare skin. The side of my head bounced off heavy objects until eventually I came to rest in the back bedroom. My dads bedroom.

"No!" My dad ran behind me as my vision was blurred by endless refuse.

The smell made me gag immediately. My sleeve was wet with something I'd dragged through in the hallway.

Under the bed I could see two large red eyes. They glowed, reflecting the light from the hallway.

Dark slimy tentacles spread out from under the bed in every direction. One of which was wrapped around my ankle. They were a deep purple, covered in a weird slime. It looked like it was covered in water and oil. I felt something puncture my pants and rub against my skin.

"NO! Let him go!" My dad swung something at the tentacle around my ankle and the object broke immediately. "No, no, no..." He collapsed to his hands and knees and tugged at the tentacle. It tightened further. I could feel my leg struggling not to break from the force.

"Dad?" I was terrified. I couldn't speak. I couldn't look away from those piercing eyes. I could feel it. In the back of my mind. Pushing through. I could feel the hairline cracks as it tried to worm into my sanity. I was looking into the eyes of true horror. Eye's that were divided up like that of a flies.

"Anyone but him. Anyone. Please. Anyone but him." My dad was crying. The man that was once my hero and helped me pull the motor on a car in the driveway. Crying like a child at my feet.

The tentacle loosened. The thing responded. The eye's flicked over to my father before falling on me again. I saw a tentacle rest slowly on my fathers back. Almost like it was comforting him.

"You need to go and never come back." My father looked at me, shaking.

"But...dad." I started, the anger returned to it's eyes full force. It let go of me. I thought I was free. Another tentacle grabbed me from behind.

I was dragging again. Through cardboard boxes, stacks of papers, and I managed to catch the edge of a bookcase. I held of until the book case lost its balance and my fingers slipped. I heard it fall as I went airborne.

Then I landed hard, facedown on the grass of the front yard.

As I come to post this now to ask for help, my father is still in there. I need to help him. I need to save him. I know I can't just call 911 or pest control.

Something has infested my childhood home.

Who do I call?
