Hi, friendly lurker here. Trying to figure out if anyone lives under similar conditions to mine.

I live in an isolated town, we’re in the middle of nowhere.

My name is Chris, and I wanted to ask if anyone has ever lived in a place like this. A place where our version of animals is apparently different than everyone else’s. Somewhere where what you call animals doesn’t match the description of the rest of the world.

Dogs bark, birds chirp and bees sting. That’s what everyone else was taught. But that wasn’t true for me. The animals here aren’t real. They’re not proper animals.

They’re fakes.

I can’t really explain it properly.

Mimics, fakes, imitations of life. Call it whatever you want, we still call them by what they’re supposed to be. What you call a bee, we also call a bee. But ours are five inches long and they don’t make this “honey” thing, that’s apparently quite popular.

Ever since I’ve had access to the internet, I’ve realized that this town, this place, is holding this massive secret. It was only when I was over the age of fifteen that I discovered that dogs aren’t supposed to walk on two legs, that rabbits don’t jump over buildings or that birds don’t scream like people.

I was actually shocked by how different the outside world is from here.

Every bit of information I came across was different to my lived experiences. So, I researched and I learned, and I came to the conclusion that we hide these things that walk among us.

And I want to expose that secret.

I also want to know if we’re unique. If there’s other places in the world like mine. That’s why I’m writing here. What I’m doing here is... quite illegal. You’re not supposed to tell anyone about this.

I’m risking a lot by even talking about it, but I need to know if there’s anywhere out there that’s the same as here. If the town’s elders find out I’m divulging this, I’m going to get in big trouble.

But I have to know.

I guess I should start from the beginning.

Ever since I was little, I’ve had to live among these things that range from generally annoying to outright dangerous.  We call these things, animals.

They can be large or small, and I’ve discovered that either by coincidence or intent, the outside designation for these entities is the same as it is here. Like I said, we have dogs. But ours just sort of run around. Bipedal. They’re fast as hell, but largely harmless. We have squirrels, but ours are top of food chain aquatic predators.

I’ve always had an inkling that something was wrong with the fauna around here. We don’t talk about them in science class, we’re not supposed to interact with them and we’re even supposed to find them sacred.

There’s a town religion that’s inextricably tied to politics in my town. It’s called “Unification”. I tried looking it up, but it doesn’t exist anywhere else.

It’s just our religious belief that all things came from a unified being. That “being” split up, and now we people and these animals sprang forth from it. I’m not really here to give a theistic explanation, but yeah, that’s what it’s about.

It’s why people around here are very “hush-hush” about our situation. You’re not supposed to talk.

The internet here is sort of limited. But I can type my experiences here in my aunt’s house, where I kind of have free rein and she sort of looks the other way. She’s cool, she’s actually in the kitchen as I type this.

I guess I could start by giving some context on how this town works. There are basically three rules we all have to follow.

Rule nº 1: Do NOT eat the animals.

Rule nº 2: Try to not let the animals eat you.

Rule nº 3: Any and all unidentified species of animals found must be reported to the town’s library.

 

Sounds crazy, but you’ll see why we need them. I guess I should start with rule number one.

I only know two examples of someone eating animals.

One was a kid that I knew when I was in school. We were just playing around, and this extremely small fly was buzzing around us. Small animals are harmless, but not if you eat them.

My friend accidentally swallowed the fly. It just flew straight into his mouth.

I don’t like remembering what happened, but my friend just collapsed on the floor and started convulsing. It looked like some sort of seizure, and it only stopped after all the teachers and school staff came to help hold him down.

After about a minute, he was back to normal. Poor guy. He was scared shitless, and so were we. He wet his pants and got sent home for the day. So, yeah… even a small accident can have really bad outcomes.

He has a normal life nowadays. Not much changed. He just has had better eyesight since that day and has stopped wearing glasses.

The other example I know of is a bit more… ritualistic.

God, the shit they show kids around this place.

We, like many other places, have a mayor. It’s sort of a lifelong position here. And as I mentioned before, the mayor is very much linked to the church.

I don’t… I don’t understand who would willingly do this to themselves. But when our mayor was too old and was about to retire, he had to perform a sort of rite of passage. Something all retiring mayors do.

He had to eat a cooked animal.

A cooked possum, to be specific. God… I just don’t get it. Why would they agree to do this? And why did they have to do it in public?

It was… not right. After an entire life of serving his community, his “farewell party” involved eating that thing in the town square in front of everyone.

No kid should see the things that happened that day.

Shortly after he ate a bit, he collapsed on the floor. And all his limbs started to grow.

That’s the only way I can describe it. His arms, his legs. God. There were so many new elbows, so many extra knees. He squirmed around, moaning and yelling.

By the end of it all, his four limbs were about five feet long.

I never saw him again. He was taken back to his home, where he’s taken care of by his wife. I genuinely don’t know what happened to him or how he is nowadays.

A few weeks later, we got a new mayor. Most people don’t like him, they prefer the old one.

So, as you can deduce, this entire town is vegetarian. Vegan even, I guess. Technically, you could eat animal byproducts, but it’s not very advisable.

I was unfamiliar with the concept of milk, but apparently it comes from cows and other mammals. We have women in this town that obviously breastfeed, but we don’t call it milk.

And I had no idea people actually drink animal “milk”.

Our cows, as you seem to prefer to drink from, only produce a reddish, salty liquid from their udders. The only time I recall anyone drinking from them was as a dare. Stupid kid stuff, really.

I recall the person saying it tasted like the ocean mixed with dirt. Not really the greatest sounding thing. So, outside of some oddities or some dares, it’s all vegetables, all the way. We really don’t have a choice.

As for rule two, I should preface by saying that animal “attacks” are rare.

As in them trying to eat you. People around here aren’t stupid. If there’s something big walking around, something that could feasibly eat you, people try to stay away.

The animals around here are too chaotic, you’re more likely to get run over by a speeding rabbit or dog than have anything actually try to consume you.

I can only recall one event involving animal “accidents” that I personally witnessed. And I witnessed it because it happened to me.

Sigh.

Do you guys know deer? They’re fucking horrible. I read up on them, and ours are completely different. Sure, they look similar, but in our neck of the woods, deer are basically creatures that spasm around mid-air.

I can’t really explain it any other way. But they jump and sort of contort mid-air. Like they’re twitching, but it’s extremely aggressive. Their whole bodies shift and turn unnaturally.

That by itself would be dangerous. Heavy animals like those jerking around midair and hitting people or property would be pretty destructive. But the issue is that it’s not the only thing they do.

They fuse themselves with things. Like, by the skin, or even down to the bone.

It’s not uncommon to see a pack of two to three deer all fused together, convulsing midair between jumps. Glomming onto whatever unlucky thing happens to pass their path.

They’re quite dangerous. And a mandatory shoot on sight.

I’m honestly lucky I’m alive.

So, essentially, when I was younger a small deer, crossed my path. You’re supposed to avoid them, obviously. But he just came out of the woods and bumped into me.

As expected, our bodies fused by the skin, and I was dragged around with the deer as it convulsed midair, taking me with it.

My mother screamed, that’s all the noise I remembered. My ears gave out soon after. I felt myself being dragged along like I was being turned by a massive wave on a beach. I couldn’t see and I couldn’t sense things.

It was just a chaos of nerve endings screaming out of pain as this thing and I were rolling around fused.

“Luckily” enough, after a few seconds, the skin where we were combined was severed. It was probably due to the weight difference, and so, I came crashing down and was left in a ditch.

The deer continued on, and I was left behind with a broken arm, a small concussion and bruises all over my body. At least that’s what they told me when I woke up in the hospital.

My mom was crying, and with good reason. I could have died.

I’m just lucky we didn’t become fused at the bone.

So yeah… Deer, jumping pieces of shit. They’re more likely to end up fused to a car in some fender bender than actually be a risk to people.

Either way, it’s shoot on sight.

I suppose that’s technically not a story about an animal eating a person. But they do seem to try. Anything that’s bigger than a chair tries to nibble on you if it has teeth. Or consume you in some fashion.

I suspect that deer live by absorbing their “prey” with that bizarre fusion process they do.

That’s my theory at least, you’re not allowed to study these things, that’s the domain of the church.

So, I guess that leads me to the last rule, which is reporting what happens to the library. If you find any new species, you are to report to them.

I… unfortunately do have a story about that. Something that affected me personally. God… I might as well.

Some months ago, I was just minding my business when I saw a small hole near the back of my house. We live adjacent to the start of the woods, so I assumed an animal had made it.

A mole, a rabbit, maybe an elephant. It was small enough that one of those could fit.

My parents told me to check it out and clean out whatever was inside before dinner. They… didn’t know, they couldn’t have. They didn’t know about the thing inside.

When I went near the hole, it was pitch black, so I went down on the ground to peer in and see what the hell was inside.

If it was a small animal, I would just shoo it away first. If it was empty, I’d just get a shovel and destroy the hole. We’re not supposed to harm animals, as weird as that sounds, just… avoid them.

What I saw inside that hole was not an animal.

Two white, piercing eyes stared back at me, white pupils that shone bright in the darkness. When it noticed me, it slowly crept into view as the moonlight illuminated its face.

A primate. A chimpanzee, specifically. That was the face I was staring down. Its cheeks not fully covered in fur or skin. It appeared more like an ape’s skull more than a living organism.

I…I was frozen in place. I’d never seen anything like this. It wasn’t a chimp; it was attached to the body of a small reptile. Like a chameleon. It looked me dead in the eyes.

It was smiling at me. Like a person.

Broken teeth that bore ill intent. Before I had time to jolt away, it screamed, and pointy, bizarre tendrils that seem to emanate from it, shot towards my shoulders and neck.

They felt like fishhooks on nylon wire. They hurt like hell too. It… It was trying to drag me in closer inside the hole, closer to it. The small reptilian body contrasted its ape-like face. And because of that, it was too small to properly pull me in all the way. So, when I was a few inches away from being face to face with it, I managed to pull away.

It was… a mess. I told my parents. The police came, the librarians came. I even saw some clerics come. They all went to the hole, but they didn’t find anything; it was empty.

When I explained what I saw, I got this look from the church people. Like they knew something. Like they screwed up.

The animal wasn’t registered in the library. I went and checked. So, I have no idea what that thing was. The only souvenir I got from it, was two small holes I got from where it pierced me.

Apparently, according to the doctor, it was sucking out blood, since my body developed the same skin reaction one develops when bitten by mosquitoes.

I can’t even explain the events of that day, but it was the catalyst for me to ask my aunt if I could come to her farm and to write this out. I need to make contact with the outside world. I need to know, if anything similar is out there. That creature… That thing… it wasn’t an animal, it’s a fake. It’s something pretending to be one.

It was too small to eat me, but it was trying… Trying to do something. I’ve never seen anything so aggressive as that thing before. It makes me wonder.

It was two fused animals. Two different kinds together. Was it adapting to something? Evolving? And what does that make us then?

This thing can’t be an animal. It doesn’t make sense. It just tries to look like one. That’s more likely. Every living, moving thing in this space doesn’t match the outside world.

So, what are they?

It got me wondering. What if they can become like us? I understand now that these things aren’t really animals. They’re just… pretending to be animals. And I’m not sure why.

And they don’t seem to be very good at it, given what I know now about the outside world.

If it can fake an animal, can it do it to us?

God. And what about that thing? Is it still around? Wandering from place to place to find me? Did it like my taste or something?

Macabre thoughts for another time, I suppose.
