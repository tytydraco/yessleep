I've always wanted to go scuba diving. Thing is, I live in Arizona. But today I'm supposed to be going to Japan for vacation. I'm 24 and I live alone, so nobody else is going with me.

After the flight, I went to bed as I planned to go diving the next day. After a long morning of buying souvenirs, drinks, and food, I finally went diving.

As I went deeper into the water, I started to notice something above me... jellyfish? I didn't even know they could be in environments this cold, let alone so many of them. They were huge — like the ones you learn about in elementary school. I never thought I'd see any this big in my life. And in my drunken state, the only thing I could think to do was go deeper.

And I kept going deeper for a good while. I don't know how long it was until I realized how deep I'd gone, but it was significantly darker and colder than I remembered. I could barely even see the sky.

I had a very frightening realization — I'd been so distracted by the novelty of being in the ocean that I hadn't been keeping count of how long I was down there or how much oxygen I had left. Still in my drunk state, I tried distracting myself while slowly heading back up and checking out the fish. But something caught my ear — a distant but loud, high-pitched noise like I'd never heard before.

It scared the shit out of me at first, but the more I stood and listened, the more intrigued I was. Maybe a whale? Or something else? Something urged me to go toward it and check it out, and whatever it was, it was strong.

I ended up deciding to go deeper. I told myself it wasn't going to be for long or a big deal, but deep down I knew it was. I didn't really think about it too much, though — all I cared about in that moment was figuring out what the noise was.

As I got deeper, it got louder, and I felt a small rush hit my head. Maybe I'd actually see it. Maybe it was something incredible. But a huge part of my mind wanted me to get out of there as fast as possible and find oxygen. I ended up ignoring it.

As it almost became hard to see in front of me, I heard it cry out one last time. It felt like it was nearly right there, just ahead of me. But as I got closer, a crippling dread came over me, and as I scanned the area around me, I saw it. This huge, unfathomable creature that felt bigger than life. And as I looked around, slowly over time, I realized that it surrounded me.

I immediately started to panic. I started thrashing and swimming as fast as I could to go upwards, but the creature just kept calmly moving around me. As I went up, I finally saw a close-up view of its skin. This earthy, rock-hard skin made me feel like puking just looking at it. It felt like a sign that my life was ending, and as I looked around, I knew it was closing in on me.

I needed to find a way out, but its body was so monstrously huge that any direction I went would just give me the same result. It felt impossible. I didn't know what was happening. I felt disgusted in myself that this was even real. But it was, and I had no way to get out.

As I curled up in the middle of the water wondering what to do, I saw something. Something different from the earthy skin I was used to, a face. A human one. But there was one thing about it, its neck was viciously huge. The face was cold grey and had a lazy eye. It felt like I was looking at a puppet of someone, or like someone was wearing someone else's skin that was too small for them. It was hideous.

I had never been even close to how scared I was in that moment. I sat there trembling, frozen in fear, and after a couple of seconds of eye contact, I ended up vomiting. In my borderline skin-tight suit, I couldn't do anything to clean it off. I just had to let it sit there.

As we stared at each other, it slowly opened its mouth. As it did, skin started flaking everywhere, and this horrific screeching came out of its mouth. It felt like it was suffering, but it also felt fake — like someone was trying to sound like they were crying for the first time ever.

I started to cry. The screeching on top of my immense sense of doom made me feel like dying was inevitable. I was confused, scared, and the smell of my vomit made me end up vomiting a second time. I took a glimpse into its eye and saw nothing, just to close my eyes in terror. The screeching got louder and louder as I lay there completely helpless.

After a while, I decided to open my eyes, and there it was  right in front of my face. Mouth wide open, with hundreds of messed up, filthy teeth, human teeth, waiting inside its mouth. They shifted around constantly like a tumor that isn't fully attached to your body. I didn't know what to think.

My immediate reaction was to thrash away from it, but I soon realized that I had been closed in on. There was no way to get out. I was trapped. And as I started to scream in terror, but something happened, it bit me. It bit me fucking hard. From the knee down was fully inside its mouth. I couldn't move. I couldn't react. I just had to let it happen.

But it never opened its mouth again to keep eating. Instead, it let the hundreds of shifting teeth inside my leg tear it apart. Its horrible, ugly face stared into mine the whole time. Its lips were dry and thin, like an old man's. Its hair looked like something you'd see on a creepy doll your grandma owned. It looked old, frail, and vulnerable — but behind all of it was a vicious monster. And all I knew was that it needed me.

Something weird started happening. The teeth were still moving, but it was sucking my leg? It was very sudden and caught me off guard. I could feel all of my blood rushing to my leg, except I was bleeding and bleeding really badly the whole time. The creature kept sucking, but eventually the teeth stopped moving and its mouth opened.

I wanted to feel happy. I wanted to be confused. I wanted to try to find a way out. But before my lightheaded, fuzzy brain could even think, it swam away. Almost instantly, it was gone, and before I got a chance to think, I was thrashed by the water up toward where I came from. I didn't get far, I ended up hitting a rock and passing out. That's the furthest my memory of today goes.

I woke up in the hospital, confused and covered in IVs and scars. According to the doctors, I was about 200 meters under sea level with almost no oxygen in my tank and nearly fully bled out. Rescue teams found me just in time. I ended up being unconscious in the hospital for a week.

I tried telling my doctors what happened, but nobody believed me. I'm still in the hospital bed as I write this, and I'm completely dumfounded. It's been almost three weeks since that happened, and I have zero idea what could've been under the water, haunting me.
