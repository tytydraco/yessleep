As I sit to type this now, I shake in the wake of my discovery; how could I not? I’ve never been one to go far out of my bounds online, but I recognize there has been a steady slip. Barely reaching the cusp of puberty, I scoured the web for a place to download files containing my favorite bands' music and later movies recommended to me by my friends. Like most people, when COVID afflicted the world, I was shut in, and that's where I can confidently say things perhaps got a bit out of hand. My friends, which had never been many in number, were reduced to pixels on a screen, and I guess the deeper and deeper I went into my computer, that's all they would ever be. At the time, I was fine with this, as in the moment, the number of those I spoke to blossomed in chatrooms on networks below what I had once downloaded less than legal wav files through. Though these friends, older than I who had experience in these places came with reputations I would not feel comfortable repeating just anywhere. During this time, they would teach me certain trading arts; it's best I don’t get into it in detail, but it made it quite hard to back out down the line when considering financial optics. Looking back on it all now, I wish I had just given up my status in these places and focused on other aspects of my life, but I could not help myself until now.  
​  
Like any other day, I awoke late in the afternoon and found several unread messages on my dashboard. Sifting through, I found a particular message that gave me pause. A mutual member of a server where I conducted some of my practices dm’d me.  
​  
“Take a look at this, I think it’ll interest you.” For a moment, I considered the link with suspicion before letting my media addicted brain pull me in.  
​  
The site was dark and uninterestingly designed. A black color filled the entirety of the background with several grey squares to suggest where thumbnails should have been and white text which I lazily skimmed over in my morning daze. All of them contained names and dates that meant nothing to me. I laugh now about how long it took me to recognize what it was that was strange about it all, but when I finally did, a pause stopped my entire body. None of the dates that lined the titles had happened yet. 07/13/2027. 01/23/2046. 11/01/2033. 04/29/2062. Scrolling through them, I found myself at a loss and, with a thumping heart, let my cursor rest over one of the blank, grey squares. In a moment of haste, I exited the site and crashed into the messageboard to interrogate the man.  
​  
“What the hell is this?”  
​  
“It’s wonderful, isn’t it? I guarantee this will make our work a lot easier. I wouldn't search your name though, I hear it’s bad luck.”  
​  
I didn’t even bother responding. Opening up the site once more, I clicked on a video titled “Happy birthday Nina Hernandez 09/13/2029.” Inside, the tape consisted of various family members singing for a little girl around a birthday cake who smiled and clapped her hands in an exaggerated, Childlike motion. A brief smile flickered across my face before I remembered the exact situation before me. Throwing back from my computer to reach my laptop, which lay on my bed, I tore the device open and immediately began scouring the surface web for the name titled in the video. Seconds turned to minutes as the footage seen on the site could not be found anywhere else until, finally, under the name Lily Hernandez, an image was found which rocked my world. A woman who was without a doubt within the third trimester of pregnancy stood with her presumed husband in a nice scenic garden. “Baby Nina, I can’t wait to meet you,” captioned the photo, and a sickening wave cast over me. Going back to the site, I clicked on several more videos in quick succession. First dates, family trips, new pets, as well as all kinds of other mundane things were predicted within videos that had dates spanning the entire 21st century. Other things that could possibly aid in the winning of large sums of money, such as the winners of the Super Bowl in 2031 or the numbers to lottery winners decades in the future, could all be found on camera. Finally, I came across a video which caught my eye immediately.  
​  
“Mark Sade’s final day 01/01/2027.” I stared at it for a moment, unsure of anything before me. I wish I would have just turned back now. Clicking on the tab I saw it contained security footage from a factory; very little other than the figures of many working under machinery could be seen as they walked around the place like ants. This was until a ball of flame erupted from the ceiling and sent the building collapsing down. Standing in my chair and covering my agape mouth, I felt tears welling around my eyes.  
​  
Looking to the corner of my computer, I watched as the man sent me several more messages, but I did not dare look. I write this now to ask what I could possibly do? Guaranteed, if I share it with anyone in my servers, it will automatically be taken and used for little other than to serve personal gain, with no concern given. For now, I just need to know what's going on, so please, if anyone has heard of this site, let me know immediately.
