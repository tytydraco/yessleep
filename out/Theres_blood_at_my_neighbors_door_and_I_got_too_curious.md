I wanted to post this here to sort of document or journal my experiences with my neighbor. Nobody thinks anything of him, but I can see right through him. To put it simply, he’s dangerous. However, I have no idea what to do about it. The police won’t help me and the apartment management won’t either so I’m pretty stuck.  So let me start with this, two days ago, I saw him carrying up several brown grocery bags. I could tell by looking at them, they were sopping wet at the bottom. As I peered through the tiny square window of my apartment's kitchenette I saw my neighbors' long slender arms wrapping them up tightly. His black trenchcoat made a vain effort to block the frigid winter winds. His footsteps barely made a creak as he stepped on the peeling whitewashed staircase rising from the sidewalk. He turned to the left at the second floor where we both resided, and I heard his keys gently clamor inside the lock. I didn’t hear it open but I heard the door shut quietly as if the door was afraid to ever make a disturbing noise. Then I was alone again. I looked down onto the concrete pad and saw a small red drip leading to his front door. 

I know that’s not really anything to be nervous about but let me tell you all about what happened later that night. The previous info is important. 

I decided I was going to spend my night playing some video games. My studio apartment allotted ample space for a young guy like myself. No one else lives with me and I don’t have any dependents so I can pretty much do whatever I want. However, living and being alone means I face the anxieties of living in a poor neighborhood with government assisted housing all alone. Whether that be the occasional gunshots, neighbors fighting, a party that goes on way too long. Those sort of things. I was playing on this very computer I’m typing this up on. When I suddenly heard something strange outside. I normally don’t pay any mind to anything weird happening outside. Don’t start shit won’t be no shit. But this sound really got me. I got up from my desk and pressed my ear to the front door. I heard what sounded like snarling. It wasn’t like a dog snarling, it sounded like something trying to snarl to sound like something else. Something trying to be bigger and scarier than it actually was. It was close too. 

It was right outside the front door. 

Then it suddenly stopped. 

I waited with bated breath, for something else, anything else. Just to hear something that would explain what might be going on outside. And it sounded like everything was still outside. I threw on my hoodie and my slides and creaked open the door. I saw a thin trail of blood and some other kind of fluid interlaced along the blood's path that warped into some kind of gooey miasma  tracing its way back to my neighbors front door. I looked out to the parking lot past the stairwell.  The overpass behind our building was silent. No rumble of trucks and no rustling of the trees. Just an absolute silence which the universe seemed to handily accent with a blank sky. The sky seems lonely and cold without the stars to warm it. 

“Hello?” I exclaimed into the hushed night.

“May I… -”

“Holy shit!” I yelled out as a raspy voice passed through an eerie crack in my neighbors door. 

How long had that door been cracked open? I swear when I first stepped out that I saw it was shut. 

“My apologies, I didn’t mean to startle you young man.” 

“It’s okay, but what’s up with the blood? You don’t sound good…  are- are you okay?” The door silently but shakily wavered open slightly more. I think I could tell that my neighbor was using the doorknob on the inside to bear his weight. Then it hit me, a strong rotten stench that wreaked havoc on my senses. His shadowy figure towered to the top of the doorway and I could barely see his figure save for his wrinkly naked torso. The aged yellowish fluorescent light above this concrete pad didn’t quite penetrate into his shadowy abode but it did illuminate half of his pale elderly face. The other half was hidden behind the door like a child hiding behind its mothers leg. The part of his expression I could see was blank but intense. The one small blue eye stared at me sharply and pierced me until it struck my fear. The corners of his mouth turned upward. 

“May I ask you to come in and help me with something?” He said with a sandpapery voice as long spidery fingers with sharp jagged nails slithered across the door frame.

   
“Are you okay? Did you not just hear me? Wha-what’s up with the blood?” I could tell my voice was beginning to shake and I instinctively began backing into my doorway. His look intensified and the top of his upper lip began to twitch revealing his monstrous rotted teeth. I slammed my door shut. I stood breathing heavily in the middle of my living room. I could hear my heart rushing in my ears. I had no one to call. Scratch that- I *have* no one to call. 

I stayed up all night long pacing and rechecking the locks on my door and occasionally looking through the peephole. His wretched face stayed totally still in the crack of his doorway staring directly at the peephole in my door. It was like that until I checked at 3:41 AM. Then he was gone. His door was shut and the blood trail was gone. I couldn’t believe it when I saw the trail had disappeared. I mean it’s not like he could’ve made whatever was going on there disappear or cleaned it that quickly. I knew I either had to get away and stay away from this guy or figure it out. And leaving for me really isn’t an option. 

The next day I called the apartment office to put in a complaint about this guy. 

“Parkway management services, this is Mandy. How can I help you?”

“Uh, yeah. My neighbor, uh, he’s been acting really strange and he was really scaring me and some other folks last night.”

“Okay who is this? What apartment are you in?”

“This is Micah in 353.”

“Okay, and who’s the neighbor?"

“I didn’t or well, haven’t gotten his name yet but, he’s in number 354 right next to me.”

“Alright, we'll send someone from the management team to talk to him. Is there anything else I can help with?”

“So that’s it? You don’t even want to know what he was doing?”

“We’ll have a member of the management team follow up with you for more details.”

“Okay thanks, bye.” I hung up abruptly because I could tell just by talking to them that this issue was never going to get resolved through them. They barely have time to keep up with the maintenance requests I’ve put in on their shitty website. Or maybe they’re just fuckin lazy. It was time I called the police. I didn’t really have an emergency at the time so I dialed the non emergency line. 

“Hello, this is the nonemergency line. How can I help you?”

“Yes, uh can I call in like, a wellness check on someone? I don’t think they’re okay.”

After some back and forth and exchanging details she told me a police officer would be over promptly. I waited and stared out the window and peephole almost the entire time. As I waited and looked I noticed my neighbor’s window was blacked out. It looked like he had painted the inside of the windows with large brush strokes of black paint. That was really strange. I then heard the heavy stomp of the officer’s tactical boots make their way up the staircase. His partner followed him upwards shortly thereafter. One of them knocked on the door with a heavy hand and the other stood to the side of the door keeping his head on a swivel until the door opened up after an array of about 5 different locks were unlatched from inside. With muffled clarity I heard this conversation:

“Hey, good afternoon sir, I’m officer Charlie, we just wanted to check in and make sure everything was good here at your home. Would you mind if we stepped inside?” The officer spoke coolly while he craned his neck upwards at the neighbor with a bright smile. The neighbor in full daylight view was now dressed in what looks like an unassuming two piece matching World’s Best Grandpa pajama set. 

“Oh hey officers sorry I uh, you just woke me up from my nap! Haha.” The old man's voice was different today. It sounded kind and slow, how an old unassuming man should. “I’d love to have some company over if you wouldn’t mind being my guests!” He stepped out of the doorway and parted his arms open to make an entrance for the two officers. Just like he was visiting with two old friends of his. 

Time had passed for about 10 minutes or so and then the officers stepped back out; they were smiling and laughing the whole way down the steps. Then I saw him again, His eyes scorning me at a hating angle from below his furrowed grey brows. He was standing plainly in the doorway staring at me through the peephole again. His look sent chills down my spine and I immediately averted my gaze. I noticed something strange about him though. He was becoming tomato red all over. The redness was on his face and on his arms. I wasn’t sure if he was about to explode with fury or if he had a righteous allergy to something but whatever it was I think it made him go inside. After I mustered up the courage to peek again he was gone.

Then we come to yesterday.

It was late in the evening when I got off work and I had stopped by the hardware store and picked up a cheap small wireless doorway camera. The thing is, it won’t record anything unless you pay for a subscription but I can watch it for free with ads with an app on my phone. So I laid in bed, turned on the tv and watched for whatever my creepy ass neighbor might get up to. It got pretty late into the evening. I saw on the shoddy footage in a smooth twenty fps my neighbor in the same black trenchcoat from before. Creeping his way up the stairs while lugging two brown paper bags, he stopped and stared at the camera. He immediately dropped his bags to the ground and I saw that they were full of unpackaged meat as the bags whimpered into shreds under the weight of their contents when they impacted the floor. He rushed to the camera and his hand wrapped around the lens of the camera then the app said in small weak lettering "*connection lost, check settings*”. I angrily stomped my way to the door and flung it open and no one, nothing was there. I looked all around and there was no meat leaking blood to the ground, no destroyed camera nailed beside the doorway, and certainly no neighbor. 

“What the hell?”

It was silent again like it was the first night. It was the middle of the day and there was no sound. All I could hear was my own breathing. 

“Where are you?” I exclaimed with a nervous rattle.

*Wham!* A bird streaked right past my head and slammed into my kitchenette window. It lay on the concrete below twitching in its death throes. *Wham!* Another bird slammed into the window. This time a crack smeared red with blood remained on the pane. I swivelled my head in all directions out of fear. I couldn’t see any more birds. 

*Wham!*  Another struck the window enlarging the crack and leaving a splatter of bird vomit in its wake. Now after the third one I ran inside.The window, covered in this red green-yellow mix, had colored fractures splintering all across its face like some distorted gore mosaic. I finally calmed down after a pause of about fifteen minutes of no birdsmashing suicides. 

Then it came like a tornado. 

*Wham! Wham! Wham! Smash!* A flurry of birds stormed the window and broke through with no relent. The avian torrent fluttered and beat the wind violently as a whole swarm of them filled the tiny apartment like a coffee mug under a waterfall. Their wings beat past my head and their claws scratched and nipped at every uncovered part of my body. I could feel the warm ooze of blood dribble down my forehead. Anything unbolted to the walls or floors were thrashed around the apartment. I began to scream but I almost couldn’t hear myself through their deafening wings. Then his voice cut through all the noise like I was wearing headphones.

“I can make it stop if you let me iiiiiiiin!” His raspy aged tone sounded mocking and demanding.

“Fine, just make it stop! Stop it! Stop it!”

Then it all stopped. I opened my eyes and of course my home was thrashed and torn to pieces. The lights were out but I could still make out the smell and sight of dead animals strewn across the walls and floor. I turned and looked towards my doorway and there he was lumbering over me, right behind me. He grabbed me with one hand by my throat and hoisted me into the air. I could feel the pressure building in my neck and my head as he was choking me.

“We are going to come to an agreement, boy.” He spoke with a crisp authority through his ragged chords. “You will bring me five pounds of fresh meat to my doorway everyday no later than six PM. You will heed my every demand. You are now my thrall.” Light began dimming around the edges of my vision as I grasped and clawed at his loose skin. 

“Pl- plea- pl-” I could barely speak.

He let go of me and I crumpled to a ball on the floor.

“I can make your life a living hell. Know this to be the truth Micah.” 

He rolled me over onto my back and got down on one knee beside me.

“Let us practice Micah. Now open your mouth.”

“Wha-” 

As I spoke two of his fingers went into my gullet and I could feel him grab my uvula. I began to wretch violently. He began muttering something that sounded like latin but I couldn’t make it out due to my heaving. 

“Now then, you speak to anyone about me and your stomach will spite you for it.” He wiped off his hand on the carpet. “And one more thing-” Then he grabbed my arm and bit down. A lightning bolt of pain soared through  my arm and into the whole of my body. I felt something wretched and evil pierce the fiber of my soul when my blood poured into his mouth. Then I lost consciousness.
