The soulless wreck burning made me feel a new sadness, less intense, but deeper.

  


I looked at Artanis, she was like peacefully asleep. She was breathing and her heart was beating steadily. I tried to wake her up, not noticing that the vapes started to feel irritating. I was desperate to wake her and I could not calm down until she woke up. She looked at me calmly and whispered: ”Mommy, you are crying” and she touched my wet cheeks. ”I do, sweetie? It seems I do…” The tears flowed like a river on my face, mixing with dirt and makeup to make some sort of goo. 

  


”Are you ok? Are…”  A burst of tears came on and I could not speak for a few seconds. Artanis smiled and answered: ”After grandma arrived, the blonde girl came to me, held my hand and made me fall asleep.” ”What girl? Sidonia? Simona?” ”No, neither of them. No one of you.” What do you mean?” ”You know, she comes to our house sometimes, just like grandma.” ”Whom?” I was puzzled. ”She just came. She does not speak much. She goes through walls and doors.” 

  


I was completely puzzled. This seems like a spirit seen only by her. ”She is sweet and kind, mommy, you know her. Not like grandma. I told you grandma is bad.” I remembered the school girl from my vision and realized that her raven hair was just like the raven hair of Artanis. I knew whom she was talking about, it was not mom, grandma Odette she was speaking off, but my dead mother in law, who died when Artanis was less then an year old, *grandma Doina*. I looked into Artanis' eyes, who kept whispering calmly: ”She is really bad, mommy, really bad, really angry and really sad, very sad. She makes me so sad.” I hugged Artanis, even if my own soul was bruised, I was God”s love that made us live and did my best to hold on to this in order for my soul not to succumb to the wounds.

  


The vapors were stronger and the irritation I felt in my throat kind of numbed the pulsating pain in my soul, and I realized I needed to take Artanis out in my arms and move away.

  


All the Elves from the circle managed to escape, at least physically. Alice ran away and hid behind our car, as paramedics started to arrive. We managed to reunite soon with mom and Sidonia, trying to stay away from the wreck. I later found that thing had a lithium battery in it that emitted strong chemicals when burning very hot, making the air feel really chill. We all suffered intoxication from it, all the Elves. Alice did as well, not even her Vampire nature protected her completely. Mom managed to bring her inside the car before the ambulance and the police came out.

  


Some from the crowd were affected as well, not very much, those who did not realize fast enough they needed to stay away from the fumes. Florin was able to carry uncle Ștefan about 20 meters, then they collapsed. They ingested more vapor than anyone else and, even if they were moved away by others soon, it was already too late. The paramedics fought to stabilize them and a helicopter came to take them to Bucharest the same night. Florin was intubated and remains on the ICU to this day. Uncle Ștefan was intubated as well, but his old body already wrecked by the liver disease could not hold on and he passed away at sunrise. May God rest his soul and my heart longs for us to meet again, when the world would be made new after all those who are dead will be resurrected by Christ at His Second Coming!

  


We were all taken to the hospital. Mom called father George to take out our car, as Alice hid in it he took Alice to Bucharest that night. We were all put on medication for the intoxication and discharged. Alice could not get medical help, as she looked like a living porcelain doll with fangs. Me, mom and Sidonia took lower doses of medication to give her at least a bit of them, as she was clearly affected by the same thing.

  


The place was cleaned by a military squad before sunrise. Officially, it was a drone of unknown origin crashing. The testimonies of the witnesses were labeled as mass hysteria, especially since nobody was able to take any photo or film a thing since all that was electric was disabled.

  


Uncle Ștefan was buried at the Bellu Cemetery, on a plot bought by mom using funds sent by his son from Australia. 

  


Our souls are scarred. I am the most affected, more than mom or Sidonia. I think the other Elves from the circle are too. Artanis seemed the least affected, due to the protection from that spirit we could not identify. We talked to her about it, even if we tried not to push too much. She opened up mostly to Helena, who concluded that the spirit was not Andrada, as we suspected at first. In fact, our greatest comforters were Helena and Artanis, like slowing healing us and starting to warm our hearts and letting light and warmth come back, but this will take a while, probably a long time. Alice was least affected mentally, as she was not in communion with Doina and the rest of the dead Elves, even if she was clearly shaken. She laid to rest a lot in her coffin, in order to regain her strength.

  


The other Elves are undergoing psychotherapy. I feel really guilty about their state, as I feel it was my fault. Artanis could have been taken away or broken by that entity who was once her Elven grandmother Doina. I cry often. I wake up sobbing in the night and I can not stop crying for hours. Michael holds me in his arms, even if  it is not easy for him either. Since his mother died under those terrible circumstances almost years ago, he tried to man up, be there for me and Artanis and suffered in silence. All this opened the old wounds for sure, even if he refuses to talk about it. I do try to hold up a bit myself to protect him, but I can not do it too much, as all is so heavy and healing is very far.

  


Surprisingly enough, I do not feel some immense guilt for uncle Ștefan, although my rational mind states that I should. What Alice said about his last moments gave me some sort of weird and heavy peace, like he received what his heart desired. Father George said to pray for his soul and do what the Orthodox do for the departed  and hope. This death is one of those instances when, alongside pain comes a weird sense of peace and completion. Perhaps he met his cousin, my grandfather Peter, who was one of those dead Elven souls summoned three years ago by Doina and Irina, for whom I hope our Sărindars and Parastases worked out or shall work out until the end of this age.

  


I had a long discussion yesterday night with Helena and Alice, being at their place with Artanis. All of us, meaning me, Artanis and Alice are recovering well with medication, our bodies removing the poison we breathed in that night. Helena is probably right that her fledgling Irina is still out there and probably brought Doina back from death, in order to continue the evil works, themselves and the rest of those structures. They could bring her back  over and over, as Elves, not even in death, can leave Earth until the Second Coming of Christ.
