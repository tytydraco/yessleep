I live alone. 

Well, not quite alone, I suppose. My three cats keep the house full of playful chaos and unconditional companionship. Every day after returning home from work I am met with welcoming chirps and swishing tails. It's a peaceful and predictable life that I have made for myself and I couldn't ask for more. 

Tiny metal bowls clink against each other, summoning my furry familiars to the dining room, as I peel back the metal lid of a can of premium (absolutely foul-smelling) wet food. Their enthusiastic tiny licks echo off the barren walls and it makes me smile. 

*You must be hungry too.* 

A fleeting thought crosses my mind but after a quick shake of my head it disappears as quickly as it arrived. I sigh, put my hands on my hips and retire to the comfort of my unmade bed. The pillows are soft, worn and perfectly shaped with indents that instantly recognize my tired head as I collapse into them. 

*Aren't you tired?*

'I am always tired,' I say aloud. One curious cat appears in the dimly lit outline of the doorframe and cocks his head slightly. I tap the blanket beside me with two fingers and he jumps up to join me. Slowly blinking emerald green eyes stare back at me as I stroke the silky spot behind his ears. 

*Who am I?*

'It doesn't matter.' I mutter with exhausted breath. The purring pauses for just a second and then continues on like the finely tuned engine of a classic car. I feel my mind beginning to drift off into a heavy sleep. A small meow is the last sound that my tired ears perceive before..

*Footsteps. The shadow outline of a thin figure. Smoke. A burning smell.* 

*Beep. Beep.* ***Beep.*** 

The yowling of frightened animals is what shakes me into consciousness. My eyes are stinging and it's hard to breathe.  I spring into action, lunging out of my bed and sprinting to the kitchen. Flames lick around a cast iron pan on the stove while a rectangle of charred matter hisses and pops. 

*You forgot about me again.* 

It takes over an hour before the house is safe, clean and smoke-free. My eyes start to blur as I lazily whip a small towel back and forth in front of an open window. My feet feel impossibly heavy as I trudge back to the bedroom. Instinctively I reach to fluff one of the flat pillows but my hand finds none. 

*It is very late.* 

'Technically, it's very early.' I let out a small chuckle and my mouth curls into a snide smirk. 'So, where should we sleep tonight?' I ask the darkness as my fingers discover the sheets and blankets missing too. 

There is no answer. 

This time I laugh loud enough that the cat behind me arches its back in response. 

'Alright. I guess I'll figure it out.' I stumble without any semblance of balance or grace up the short flight of stairs to the spare bedroom. The lock clicks open easily under my fumbling fingers and the door opens with a quiet creak. 

*There you are.* 

I grin and with outstretched arms do a quick spin feeling my oversized t-shirt swirl around my ribcage. There is a familiar chorus of concern, meows and mews, too far away now to decipher. I fall into the stiff mattress and bury my nose into a thin pillowcase. 

*I can go away if you don't want me, you know.*

'No, no..' I whisper, 'we can wait a little more.' Again the weight of exhaustion presses down on me. As I feel my muscles and tendons relax I notice that my eyes are already closed. In fact, I can't remember the last time they were open. 

*Who is that?*

'Who... is.... what?'

It sounds like fireworks if your ears were submerged in a swimming pool. Silence. Then again. Sharper and more urgent. Furious rapid fists knocking on old wood.

*They will find me.* 

'Let them.' I pull another pillow over the left side of my face.
