I don’t know if any of you have heard of something called a Quaelian Locket.

They were a minor fad in occult circles in the early 2000s. This was the time when the Internet was just really kicking off in the mainstream and before everything was catalogued online. Information about them is scarce now since even if there was a post here or there about them it was likely deleted by this point or just buried so deep that Google has no way of finding it. Most of what I know came secondhand from other sources, and even that was inconsistent.

The Quaelians were supposedly an ancient Germanic tribe. At least, that was the story attached to the lockets. Some people claimed the design was based on artifacts attributed to them. Others said “Quaelian” was not the name of a people at all, but the name of the thing the locket was meant to summon.

I really don’t know which version is true.

Not many of the lockets were ever put into circulation. I never learned the name of the company that made them - if there even was a company. They may have been made by a small group of enthusiasts in their spare time, passing them around through occult forums, flea markets, and private meetups.

The locket itself looks ordinary enough.

It was made of a copper alloy, coated in something gold-colored that was almost certainly not real gold. There were runes engraved along its outer face and around the inner rim whose meaning I still don't know of. 

Inside was a small circular concave mirror.

The locket was supposed to be used in a ritual to communicate with entities in “the beyond.”

The instructions were simple.

You opened it so that the mirror faced you. Then you held it a few inches in front of your face, dangling from its chain like a pendulum. You had to perform the ritual in a room with no natural light, though not in complete darkness. Any artificial light would work, even a dim LED lamp, but candlelight was said to give the best results.

Then you stared into the mirror.

Eventually, you would see something.

Not directly though. Never directly.

It would appear at the edge of the mirror, or in the farthest corner of your vision. The most important thing to remember was that you were not supposed to turn your head. You were not supposed to ever look directly at it. You were supposed to keep your eyes fixed on your reflection.

Because if you looked directly at it, it would notice you.

And if it noticed you, it would pounce and it would be all over. 

The entity was supposed to draw closer over time the longer the ritual went on. If you wanted to end the ritual, you closed your eyes and snapped the locket shut.

Then it would be gone.

That was what I had been told when I got mine. I never tried it back then. I do not even remember the name of the friend who gave it to me as when I think about it two or three different people come to mind who might've given me it. 

I had tossed it away about a month after getting it and never saw it again. I assumed that it must've been lost by now. 

Recently, though, I found it again.

I had been cleaning out my attic, looking for things to sell. I lost my job a few months ago, and boredom has a way of making stupid ideas sound reasonable. The locket was at the bottom of an old box beneath some books and a broken desk lamp.

I remembered the story behind it.

So I decided to try it.

I waited until night. I turned off every light in the house except for a single candle in my bedroom. Then I opened the locket and held it in front of my face.

For the first few minutes, nothing happened. I felt rather stupid holding the locket out in front of me like that and was about to close it. 

Then I saw it.

A shadow lowered itself into the corner of my vision.

I could not make out a shape. It was just a darker patch of darkness beside the bedroom door, something that did not belong there. Instinctively, I nearly turned my head before I remembered the warning.

**Do not look directly at It.**

So I kept staring at the mirror.

At first, I only saw myself: pale face, tired eyes, the candle trembling behind my shoulder. The locket swayed slightly from my hand.

Now, I know that I was supposed to see something. I remember that part of the story. But it never mentioned that the other senses would be involved as well. 

I heard breathing.

It was slow and ragged, like someone struggling to pull air through a wet throat.

I held still.

The breathing came from somewhere beside me.

After a while, it began whispering.

The words were too quiet to understand. I could hear the shape of speech, the rise and fall of a voice trying to make a sentence, but nothing distinct enough to really understand.

Then the room filled with a smell like decaying leaves and wet soil.

That was when I had enough and decided that it was time to end it. 

I squeezed my eyes shut and snapped the locket close. 

I opened my eyes.

The shadow was still there.

At first, I thought I was imagining it. I checked the locket three times. It was closed. The mirror was hidden. The chain was wrapped around my fingers.

But the thing remained at the edge of my sight.

I could still smell the damp rot. I could still hear its breathing.

I have been trying to convince myself that this is some kind of panic response. 

Sleep deprivation. Stress. Grief over losing my job. Anything.

But it is getting closer.

I know because I can feel where it is without seeing it.

At first, it was near the bedroom door.

Then it was beside my dresser.

Now it is standing just behind my right shoulder.

I have not looked directly at it. Not once.

Every instinct I have is telling me to turn my head. To see what is standing there. But I know that is what it wants. Looking at it is the only rule I remember clearly.

If I look directly at it, it will notice me.

And if it notices me, it will pounce.

I tried leaving the room. When I went downstairs, it disappeared for a few minutes. Then it returned in the exact same place relative to me: just outside my central vision, still approaching.

I tried closing my eyes.

When I opened them, it was closer.

I tried turning on every light in the house.

It did not help.

I tried locking myself in the bathroom, but I could still smell the soil beneath the door.

I do not know anything else about the Quaelian Lockets. I cannot find any information online. There are no archived forum posts, no photographs, no ritual instructions beyond what I was told years ago.

I figure I must've done something wrong; that there's something else when it comes to ending the ritual. A step that I've forgotten. 

I don't know what that step is. 

I can hear it breathing now.

Louder than my own.

And I think it has moved from behind my shoulder to nearly beside me. I can hear it speaking clearly now. It is no language that I'm familiar with, but every word sends a shiver of terror down my spine. 

If anyone knows anything about these lockets, please answer quickly.

I do not think I can keep myself from looking at it much longer.

And once it is fully in the center of my vision, it will be all over regardless.
