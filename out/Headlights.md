Something about it, though, made me shiver and break out in a cold sweat. It just didnt feel right to me. I asked about the diffrent colored building and was told that that was  not where I would be living and I shouldnt worry about things. 



The second week living on my own I woke with a start. I couldnt remember my dream but I could remember feeling scared. So I took a walk to clear my head and smoke a J.  I headed out of my building and headed towards a little wooded area that I had spoted next to the retention pond. It was a cool night and the hour was late or early, depending on what side of sleep you are on. For me it was way too late and I wanted to calm down a get back to sleep. 



I found my way to the little wooded patch and made myself comfey on a bench someone had put there. Maybe a fellow ninja smoker. The closest building to me was Unit 14 and 13 was directly across the pond, on the backside of the apartment complex. Building 13 was of course that building that unnerved me that first day. I heard a sound from across the pond and gazed out inthat direction. 



I saw some dark form of a person emerge from the deeper shadows of the building. The other buildings all had exterior lights but Unit 13 did not. The only light came from the windows and the streetlights in the parking lot. The shadowey form moved from the lightless building and into the lighted lot. Though from that distance I couldnt make out much, exept that the figure stayed shadowey. Even when they passed under the lights. I puffed on my J and wondered if I was watching a ghost or if I was just stoned. That was when the figure stopped and locked eyes with me. 



Freaky right? No it was terrifying. Because what looked back at me had glowing violet eyes. I dropped my joint and decided at that moment to go back to my apartment and go to bed. Real life had started to get quite a bit scarier than that cloudy nightmare. 



As I moved, the figure moved also. I kept glancing over to it and nearly shit myself when I watched it open the door of a big white truck and get inside. The truck's headlights came on and that is when I ran. I dont know why, but my gut was telling me that if this thing catches me I am done for.  I made it to the main road for the apartment complex and managed to bolt across before the big white truck had even driven out of the parking lot. I looked back before I entered a building that wasn't mine but was connected to mine. The moment I looked back the truck sounded it's horn and I was not prepared for the sheer sound of it. It sounded like a train horn and I admit I screamed.

I looked up from my book only to lock eyes with that same pair of glowing pair from the previous week. Then it sounded it's horn and I screamed once again. Not expecting such a large noise from such a vehicle. Then I got worried because now it new where I lived.
