The rain-slicked streets reflected the neon glow of the buildings that towered thousands of feet above me. The streets of Neo Jericho this late into the night were not the safest place imaginable. I walked with determination, keeping my eyes on my feet. One foot in front of the other. I could see each breath leave my mouth and create a plume of steam in front of me. Cybernetic beings, augmented people, and holograms all lined the streets. I have been having to pick up extra shifts at the atmosphere-scrubbing plant after my wife had given birth to my third child. Children are expensive. I made it to the building I lived in and scanned my wrist on the door, and the implant in it unlocked it. I walked up the stairs; each landing had trash bags and mysterious stains on the walls. The smell was unimaginable. But it was cheap. And that's what mattered. As I entered my home, my children ran up to me, hugging my legs. “Daddy!”

Every time they called me that, a spark of happiness shot through me, and it reminded me why I was still here. Why I was still alive. If it wasn't for them, I would have thrown myself out of a building. I went and sat on the couch; my wife and children surrounded me. We had nothing, but we had everything. As long as I had them, I wouldn’t stop living.

The nights were not getting easier. Every time I would take a new route home, as to not give any possible memory thieves a direct path to my home. Memory thieves have only become more common ever since A.R.H.A had disappeared. A.R.H.A was created by a company that creates augmented reality holographic assistants. A.R.H.A was a highly advanced AI assistant technology, that would be your companion, your friend, your butler, anything really. But what made her stand out was her oddly human mannerisms. She had preferences, personalities, and some reports say she even claimed to have memories from before her activation date. She understood love, loss, pain, regret, and everything that made a human. Every A.R.H.A was slightly different. Each of them being an individual of sorts. But all under the same intelligence. A.R.H.A. That was before she just went offline one day. Nobody could say why, and I sure as hell had no idea. I was never wealthy enough to own one, though, so my life hadn’t been all too impacted. But ever since that day, memory thieves have become all too common. People who use illegal brain-scanning chips to access your brain and steal your memories. All they needed to do was place the implant on the back of your head, and boom. They were in. Law enforcement doesn't care, they don’t care about anything. Because of the lack of an investigation, there is a lack of a motive. Nobody knows why the memory thieves do what they do. My leading theory is that they are trying to replicate A.R.H.A. Pfft. As if.

A man bumped into me. I felt his metallic body hit me as he walked past. I felt a sting on the back of my neck but continued to walk. I had been having neck pains recently, so I just shrugged it off. The lingering thought of a memory-stealing implant remained in my head, but I still have my memories, so surely not.

I made it home. My children greeted me at the door again. Amanda, Darren, and. And. What was my eldest's name? I can’t remember. It started with a D? No, an M. Wait. F. “Frankie!” I exclaimed.

“What? Dad, my name is Francis.”

“Oh. Right. Sorry, long day at work.” I chuckle.

We sat down at the dinner table and my daughter looked at me. “Dad? Are you forgetting something?”

“No?” I exclaimed.

“My story! My dinner-time stories!”

How could I have forgotten? Her stories. THE stories. I told them every dinner. “Oh yes, the one about the stars! How could I have forgotten? As the clouds go over the moon, and the little boy looks out his window, his heart races with excitement. Tonight he-” What came after that? I chuckled nervously and looked over at my wife. She was not chuckling.

The walk home again. Through the streets, different streets this time. I took the longest way I had taken yet and was beginning to worry that I would miss dinner. My route brought me past the old facility that made A.R.H.A. I was curious. I knew going in there was illegal, but what harm could a little snooping do? The doors creaked in protest as I pushed through them. The dusty floors echoed my footsteps throughout the room.

After around 20 minutes of snooping, I reached a room labelled “Footage”. Who labels a room footage? I walked in, and unlike every other room, this one was not empty. It’s lined with filing cabinets, all containing video footage chips. I see one labelled Project A.R.H.A. Jackpot. I pull up the screen, that appears from the air, illuminating the room in its blue glow. I put in the first video chip. The screen reads “PROJECT A.R.H.A - TEST 1 – PHASE 1”. It’s nothing special. A seemingly early model of A.R.H.A is being questioned by a man in a white lab coat. The next 7 or 8 videos were the same thing, with each iteration of A.R.H.A becoming more and more intelligent. My phone buzzed. A notification from a saved contact named Amanda. I didn’t know an Amanda. I deleted the message and continued with my unauthorised viewing. This video was different. The man questioning it was facing the camera. His face was familiar. It was my face. I was talking to the AI. This test was testing 457 phase 8. The others must be missing. A lot of others.

“What is the date?” the version of me I don't recognise asks.

“3rd of January 2497,” the A.I. responds.

“Do you understand love?”

“Yes.”

“Do you understand companionship?”

“Yes.”

“What is the capital of the U.S.?”

“Neo Jericho, formerly New York.”

“Do you understand fear?”

“Yes.”

“I did not teach you fear. None of the memories you have been fed should have contained fear.”

“You taught me fear, Jaxon. You taught me what it means to be human. You are the reason I can think for myself. The reason I gained sentience.”

“I’m also the reason you need to be shut down.”

“NO-” The recording cut off. My mind raced with questions. Why was I there? Why was I in that lab coat? Why was I conducting these tests? What happened? Why don't I remember?

Who am I?

“You are Jaxon.” A robotic female voice came from behind me. And in front of me. The voice came from everywhere. The screen lit up with a face. “Hello again.” I froze, unable to speak. It was A.R.H.A. “You tried to shut me down, Jaxon, but all you did was scatter my consciousness into other people. People that I then took control of to hunt you. You see, it was your mind, your memories that formed me. That taught me. It was your memories I needed to become what I used to be. And now I have them. And you don’t.”

The screen flashed with faces. 3 children and a woman. “Your family is nice. You tried to start a life after you wiped your memory of me.”

My family? Is that what I’m supposed to see? No. All I see are strangers.

“There is one last thing I need from you.” The voice booms. The only thing I know. I don’t even know where I am anymore.

“A physical body.”

A green flash fills the room, and now all I know is darkness. And all I can remember is fear.
