I had gotten the last tube on at Warren Street heading for Brixton as I just got done finishing work, it was around 00:28 and I was absolutely exhausted from the day, so i found a seat and put on my headphones. The train had stopped at all the usual stops until we left Stockwell, I started getting up and grabbing my belongings as I knew we were going to pull into Brixton in about 2 minutes, but those 2 minutes turned into 5 and then 10. by the 10 minute mark I began getting confused as to why we're still in the tunnel and haven't arrived at Brixton as we would've been there by now. I started looking around at other people who had a confused look on their face, checking their phones, frantically texting and a few were looking towards the windows as if they expected to see something different. The train was still moving at what felt like its usual speed. There was no sign of it slowing down, and no announcement explaining what was happening. I sat back down in my seat and went on my phone to see if there was any issues on the Victoria line but when i checked TFL's website the only line that had issues was the Bakerloo and Central line with minor delays while it showed "Good service on all other lines"

At that point i started checking all other social medias i could think of, Twitter, YouTube, TikTok, Reddit, news apps, anything I could think of to see what was going on but nothing said anything unusual was happening on the Victoria line, Meanwhile, we were still hurtling through a tunnel that seemed to have no end. I asked the man opposite me if he had any idea what was going on and he just looked up from his phone and said "That's what im trying to figure out mate, we left stockwell ages ago. So when the hell are we arriving at Brixton?" I then hear a loud banging and i look to my left and it was a man frantically banging on the driver's door "Can you hear me?, What's going on? Why haven't we arrived at Brixton??" yet no answer on the other end. "Oi! DRIVER!" He banged on it a few more times before stepping back and looking around at the rest of us. A woman sitting nearby shook her head. "I've checked TfL. It says there's good service. There's nothing about a train being stuck." I said to the man.   
"Well, clearly something's wrong, isn't it?" another passenger snapped. "Trains don't just drive around in tunnels for half an hour!"  
"Maybe we've been diverted?" someone suggested from the other end of the carriage.  
"Diverted where? This is the Victoria line!"  
A few people started talking over one another. I could hear someone trying to call their partner, another person repeatedly refreshing the TfL website, and a man near the doors telling everyone to calm down. 

While everyone else was freaking out, i looked into the other carriage and could see people panicking as well. another person from that carriage opened the door and came to bang on the drivers door again "WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON WHY HAVENT WE ARRIVED AT OUR DESTENATION?" he shouted at the door before a man sat near him said to him "Someone else already tried that, no answer on the other end." He then looked at the man confused before saying "Well we got to somehow figure out how to break this door down or something"  a woman sitting across from them interrupted. "That's a good idea because what if the driver is unconscious? What if there's something wrong with the train?" A few other passengers began gathering around the driver's cab. One man suggested pulling the emergency alarm, while another insisted that someone should try contacting TfL again.

I decided to speak up.

"Guys, why don't we just call 999?"

A few people turned to look at me.

"999?" the man standing by the driver's door repeated.

"Yes! The police, the fire brigade, whoever can help us. We've been travelling for ages, we can't get hold of anyone, and the driver isn't responding. Surely someone needs to know what's happening!"

"He's got a point," the older gentleman said, reaching into his pocket for his phone.

Within seconds, several passengers were dialling 999. I did the same, holding my phone to my ear as it began ringing.

For the first time in what felt like hours, I thought we might actually get some answers.

Then the call connected.

"Emergency services. Which service do you require?"

"Police, please," I replied quickly. "I'm on a Victoria line train heading towards Brixton, but we've been stuck in the tunnel for-"

I stopped.

We weren't stuck. The train was still moving.

"Sorry, we're on a Victoria line train. We left Stockwell ages ago, but we still haven't arrived at Brixton. The train won't stop, and we can't get a response from the driver."

There was silence on the other end.

"Sir, could you tell me which station you got on from?"

"Warren Street. We went through all the usual stops. We were supposed to arrive at Brixton hours ago."

Another pause.

Then the operator asked a question that made my stomach drop.

"Sir, are you certain you're on the Victoria line?"

"Yes! I'm looking at the destination display right now. It says Brixton!"

I heard faint typing in the background.

"Sir, I'm going to need you to stay on the line while I check something."

I waited, watching the other passengers staring at me expectantly.

A few seconds later, the operator spoke again.

"Sir, according to our information, there are currently no Victoria line trains operating between Stockwell and Brixton."

I looked at the man beside me.

"What do you mean, no trains?"

The operator didn't answer immediately.

Then she said:

"Sir, the last train to depart Stockwell towards Brixton was reported as having arrived at Brixton 1 hour ago."

I felt my blood run cold.

"That's impossible," I whispered. "I'm on it right now." 

The Operator then asked me if there was anyone else on the train to which i answered yes. "Can you see any station signs or identify your current location?" she asked. I told her no and that we've just been travelling through the tunnels for god knows how long now. I then heard the operator speaking to someone in the background, although I couldn't make out what she was saying. "we've received several emergency calls from passengers aboard a Victoria line train this evening. However, all of those calls appear to have come from the same location which is Stockwell station. Officers and Station staffs have confirmed that there's nobody on the platform at all and that every single tube is in the depot" 

"THEN WHERE THE HELL ARE WE?!" I shouted at the phone, i was confused but scared at the same time.  

Then the call disconnected, I tried calling them back but the Phone kept ringing over and over again. I've texting my friends, co-workers, parents about what's going on, they're confused and are doing everything they can to help me. Other passengers have tried breaking the window, breaking the driver's door and even suggested tried leaping out via the walkway but everyone is too scared to try that. It's been 5 hours since and im relying on my power bank to keep my phone charged but i know it's going to die eventually as well as my phone. Honestly im posting here because im scared and confused as to what's going on and just don't know what to do.
