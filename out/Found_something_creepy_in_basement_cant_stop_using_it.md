My name is James and I have a confession to make. Two months ago, I noticed a strange sweet smell drifting out of my crawlspace underneath my floor, Sort of like burning plastic. I am quite the cautious person usually, so I decided to investigate to make sure there was no melting wire insulation in my house. Blame me all you want for peeking where I shouldn't, but I don't want my home ablaze.

After stepping down into the crawlspace, I had to duck underneath the ducts and boards because the ceiling was quite low, only about 4 feet high. I'm around 5'11" so it was a bit of a crunch. With great effort I searched under everything, and up into every crevice. I looked and looked for the source of the odor but I could not find it. Eventually, I reconciled that it was probably not burning insulation because if there were an electrical short, it should have already lit my house up or tripped a breaker. Neither happened, so I was able to relax before heading back up.

The next day, The smell was much more intense and woke me up before my alarm at the crack of dawn. I neared my crawlspace entrance and almost passed out. The smell had evolved from a slight sweet chemical smell to a sickly sweet and rotting smell, Like a rat had started decaying in my basement overnight. Holding back the urge to dry heave, I stepped cautiously down the stairs into the dark. When I turned the light on, I had to do a double take.

There was a cube on my floor. It was pitch black and glossy. As I neared it I realized the sheen was from a thick slime covering it. The object was about 5 inches wide and almost a perfect cube. I raced back up the stairs and closed the floor hatch to try and catch my breath.

As soon as I escaped from the smell, I fell to all fours and my vision went dark and fuzzy. Then, an intense fit of coughing shook my entire body. When my eyes refocused, I saw a pool of blood between my hands, and I realized with horror that a tiny black cube lay in the mess i had made. I can't even imagine the look on my face. I slowly got off my knees and stood up, eyes still fixed on the black, centimeter wide cube in the puddle of red. I stared at the small cube even while moving to the closet to get cleaning supplies, I didn't want to look away, in case something changed.

That night, I could barely get to sleep. The terrible smell from the slimy cube in the basement had all but faded away. I ended up putting the tiny black cube onto my countertop before going to my bedroom. It was stony and dense, as if it were made of basalt. I stared up at my ceiling from my bed while remembering curling my fingers around it's smooth, cold form. It was beautiful. So beautiful. I cleaned my blood off of it with utmost care. I then wiped the debris off of it's dry surface with a microfiber cloth, so that i could preserve it's beauty. As i drifted off to sleep finally, I could only think of my sweet little child, my black cube.

The following day, I woke up later than i usually do. I made my way to the kitchen, my house smelling completely normal again. On the countertop, just where I left it, the cube lay. Immediately though, I noticed something was off. After comparing it to other things in my house to make sure I wasn't losing my mind, I found that it had grown. It was ever so slightly larger than the last time I had seen it. I made myself some waffles, and after making myself two, I cooked one extra and put it on a second plate. At my table, I set up the two plates across from one another. My plate on one side, and my cube's plate on the other. I picked up the cube and set it down next to the waffle, so it could eat. I could tell it was starving.

I woke up on my living room floor. Dazed, I shuffled myself onto my feet and then ran to my kitchen to see what had happened. The cube had grown once more, and the three waffles were completely gone. It was much larger than it had been mere moments ago, now totaling to two inches after careful measurement. As i took my fingers off it, I saw that i had left prints in a thin clear film. Strange. I prepared more food for myself because I had not finished my waffles before it had taken them. As my stomach settled on a bowl of cereal, I suddenly had the urge to feed it again.

A week passed by with this pattern of feeding happening. Every time I would make a meal, the cube would take some or all of it, knock me out, move me to the living room, and grow an inch. By the time it had reached about a foot in width, I was thin as a skeleton, I could barely manage to feed myself. After this threshold, it seemed it wanted no more from me. What a foolish assumption that was. I left it on my countertop and started towards my bedroom as usual. A sudden new feeling overtook me. I turned back to the cube, Took it under my arm, It now being quite heavy, perhaps as much as an adult human, and lugged it to my bed.

At my bed with the cube, I started using it for my own personal pleasure. I can't explain how it happened. I truly could not make sense of it. I eventually came on it and it's thin sticky film absorbed my seed and grew slightly thicker than it had been before. I fell into my bed next to it, exhausted from what I had just done. This has continued every night for the last two months. I've moved the cube into the dark damp crawlspace, and every night I go down there and add to the slime. It smells just as bad as it did at the beginning. Soon, I'll melt into it, and it'll suck up every bit of juice I have. The next person to own this house will feed us too.
