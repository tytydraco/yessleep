Every year in my family, when Thanksgiving comes around, like many others, I am ecstatic to spend time with my family, eat delicious food, and make memories to cherish. However, this year, an uneasy feeling befell me that I could not shake.

This Thanksgiving was meant to be a special one, as it was my first time seeing my family since I moved to college last September, so you wouldn't be surprised to find out that I was extremely disappointed when I heard the news that my grandfather would not be able to join us. Off on some work trip in another country, my grandfather awaited his retirement, tying up every responsibility to his business before handing it over for good, and apparently could not miss whatever excursion awaited him away from my family this year. Though the rest of my family would be present, I could not help but feel a pit of sadness in my stomach as my grandfather had meant the most to me growing up, and as a kid I never wanted to be away from him.

Sitting at the function, I enjoyed good conversation and laughing with my family, with a game of football on the TV until the time struck. Finally it was time to eat. Standing and observing the several dishes on display, I wondered at the expanse of it all until I noticed something strange. The turkey, which typically appeared in its normal full appearance, was instead already completely shredded, visibly unrecognizable.

“Different turkey this year?” I whispered to my aunt, who sat next to me.

“Mawmaw said it's sent in especially from your grandfather this year, something fancy as his way of saying sorry.”

Nodding my head, I carried on with filling my plate and sat with my cousins before digging in. Immediately, I was taken in by the vast array of flavors that floored me from the very beginning. More and more I ate; I could not keep myself from scarfing up all of the turkey my grandfather had sent in from wherever he was until the last bite, when I felt something hard and gummy crunch between my teeth. Spitting into my napkin, I froze the moment I looked down at what had been in my mouth.

Promptly standing and dashing to the bathroom, I fell over the toilet and began vomiting my guts out. Shoving my finger deep in my throat to continue when I could not naturally anymore. Finally, when I felt I had no more to give, I sat staring down at the milky grey swirling clumps and liquid in the toilet, remembering the napkin. *A finger. There was a long, bony finger in that napkin.*

At once words from my family spilled into my mind. *Pawpaw is so sad he can’t make it this year. This turkey’s sent in especially from your grandfather this year.* I could not wrap my brain around what conclusion my mind was coming to. *What the hell was I eating?* I had to push my mind from going to the worst places.

Pushing the toilet to try and steady myself, something beneath me slipped, and my mouth gaped as the porcelain bowl moved to reveal a vast tunnel beneath. Peering my head in, I stared into a black abyss that seemed to go on and on forever. 

Climbing down, I wondered how deep tunnels could fill a house I had been around my entire life. Finally reaching a room, I glared at a beaming light that shone flatly on an empty table that was stained with maroon. Inching closer, a disgusting tearing entered my ears that made me shudder with disgust and terror.

Thinking of turning back, I made it not a single step before I heard an unearthly scream that warbled like no animal or man I had ever heard. Peeking my head in again, I looked to a separate corner, seeing a figure in a bright yellow shirt and blue jeans hunched over some squirming thing beneath him. Then, he raised a butcher knife into the air and brought it down to another one of those outrageous screams.

Allowing a noise to escape me, the man darted around, and I came face to face with my grandfather. Opening my mouth to speak, I couldn't get a word out before the thing beneath him gripped its long fingers around his ankle. Peering down, I got a glimpse for just a moment of a grey man with black eyes and no teeth screaming that strange sound before my grandfather brought his boot down upon his skull. Crushing it and spilling yellow brains on the concrete. Eyes darting around, I could see the remains of many of these things who had been sliced into bins of the grey meat I once believed was turkey.

Now there was no stopping me running up the stairs. Faster and faster I ran through the tunnel till I reached the bathroom and slammed the toilet back into place. Sitting and catching my breath, I thought of what to do but found no answers. I don't know if I can face my family after what I saw, but most of all I feel I need to get away from this house. I am at a loss as to what to do and would appreciate any advice on what I could possibly do.
