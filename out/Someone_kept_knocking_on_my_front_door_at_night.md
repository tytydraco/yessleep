I moved into my apartment at the beginning of the year. It was an older building with six apartments on each floor and pretty thin walls. Hearing footsteps, conversations, or doors closing in the hallway was normal. None of it bothered me until someone started knocking on my front door in the middle of the night.

The first time happened around 2 AM. I was watching something on my laptop when I heard three quiet knocks. I checked the peephole, but nobody was there. I assumed someone had knocked on the wrong door and left, although I hadn't heard any footsteps. A few nights later, it happened again. Three identical knocks. I reached the door almost immediately, but the hallway was empty. I even opened it and checked the stairs and elevator. Nothing.

Over the following weeks, the knocking continued, always between 2 and 3 AM. My neighbor across the hall hadn't heard anything and suggested teenagers might be playing tricks. It sounded reasonable, except I couldn't understand how they disappeared without making a sound. Eventually, I started wondering whether the noise was actually coming from another apartment.

I decided to record it using an old phone and a voice recording app. For two nights, nothing happened. On the third, I woke around 2:30 AM to the familiar three knocks. I stayed in bed and checked the recording the following morning. The knocks were clearly audible, but there was something else. About ten seconds before them, I heard a faint scraping noise, like something being dragged across the floor. Strangely, it sounded much closer to the phone than the knocking did. I listened repeatedly, but couldn't identify it.

A few days later, I was brushing my teeth when the knocking started again. I reached the front door within seconds and looked through the peephole. Nobody was there, and the hallway light was off. That seemed strange because the motion sensor usually activated whenever someone walked past my apartment. Then I heard another knock, just one, much quieter than the others. I was standing right against the door, but the sound seemed to come from somewhere near my knees. I looked beneath the door, expecting to see someone's shoes or a shadow. There was nothing. Then someone whispered my name. It was barely audible, and I couldn't tell whether the voice belonged to a man or a woman. I stood there for several seconds before backing away and locking myself in my bedroom.

The following morning, I spoke to the building manager about security cameras. Unfortunately, there weren't any on my floor. He suggested contacting the police, but I had no idea how to explain what was happening without sounding ridiculous. That evening, I pushed a small chair against my front door. I knew it wouldn't stop anyone from entering, but I figured I'd hear it move if someone tried opening the door.

I went to bed around midnight. At 2:46 AM, three knocks woke me up. I sat upright, staring toward my bedroom door. A few seconds later, I heard that same scraping sound from the recording. This time, it sounded exactly like the chair being dragged across the floor. I waited, barely breathing. Then came three more knocks. But they weren't coming from the front door anymore. They came from my bedroom door, only a few feet from my bed. I knew I was alone in the apartment. I hadn't heard the front door open, and there was no reason for anyone to be standing outside my bedroom.

I immediately switched on my bedside lamp and stared at the closed door. I couldn't bring myself to get up, so I grabbed my phone and messaged a few friends. Two of them stayed awake talking to me while I sat there, watching the door. Nothing else happened that night. When morning came, I finally gathered enough courage to check the apartment. Every window was closed. The front door was locked. Nothing appeared to have been touched. And the chair I'd pushed against the entrance was still standing exactly where I'd left it.
