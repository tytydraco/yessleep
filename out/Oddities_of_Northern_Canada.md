I have to preface this and tell you that these are actually true stories. Either local legends that I’ve heard about, stuff other people have told me that has happened to them, or things that have happened to me. These are not true stories in the ‘reddit horror’ sense of the term. These *actually* happened, I need you to understand that. 

I live in far northern Canada on an indigenous reserve, affectionately called the rez. If you aren’t familiar with what a rez is, it’s a small parcel of land that the Canadian Government so generously provided the indigenous people after slaughtering thousands of them and attempting to assimilate the rest into a homogenous culture. I work and live there. It’s one of those places where if you stay out too late then you’re bound to run into something unsavory. 

One of those places where uttering the name of a thing makes it so you’re guaranteed to get a knock on your door from it. 

One of those places where the spirit of a thing is more important than the name. Rocks and trees and creatures all living together and thriving under The Creator sort of thing.

One of those places where if you whistle at night, especially at the northern lights, you’re basically fucked forever. 

If true stories scare you more than fiction, then skip this one. I’ve tried my best to make these easy to stomach, though some of them, admittedly, are pretty goofy. Most might not be very scary at all but it's something to think about, that there is stuff out there that one way or another can’t really be explained.

**The Rolling Dog Head** 

There is a local legend around here that if you do not abide by your parent’s wishes and/or stay out past your set curfew then a creature that I have only heard referred to as The Rolling Dog Head, or just The Dog Head will start coming after you. 

For context the main road of this place is a nearly ten kilometer, nearly perfectly straight stretch of road that runs from the river that cuts it off to the other end which terminates in the woods. Most people here just walk that road and then either hoof it through the bush to get home or take a side road. 

Now, the premise of The Dog Head is that if you are walking on that road after curfew or if you are a general shithead that doesn’t listen to your parents then the next time you walk down that road there is a good chance that The Dog Head is going to roll its way after you. It has never happened to me personally but I can imagine the head with its lungless panting, hot breath steaming in the air, tongue lolling out of its mouth and slapping the road with each trundling movement. 

I don’t know about you, dear readers, but I think that after the first encounter with The Dog Head I wouldn’t be too pressed about it, I’m not even sure that it bites you if it catches you and as far as I know there’s nothing stopping you from just punting the damn thing.  

I’m not even sure where the legend came from but I know there is a similar legend about the rolling and rotten head of a wronged woman that chases people that is known as ***cihcipiscikwân***. 

One great thing about the dog head is that last year on Halloween we rigged up a werewolf mask (like the Peach Fuzz mask from *Creep)* on a track to chase people down the hallway of our workplace. That was fun.

**LPs**

Little People, mainly known as LPs so as not to risk calling them by their real names, are creatures that are the approximate size of two litre soda bottles. These little guys are your standard tricksters. They steal, hide, and generally mess with your shit. Upon further research the closest cryptid that I could match it to are known as the *mannegishi*. If you look into these creatures they are described very similarly to grey aliens, though I have just heard them described very simply as “they look like little guys.” 

Unlike The Dog Head, I do have personal experience with the LPs though it is anecdotal evidence at best, at worst I’m just terrible at closing drawers. Anywho. 

There is a drawer in my bedside table that to my knowledge, and from observing it, is not unbalanced, broken, weighted weird or anything like that, but every time I come home the drawer is wide open. Beyond that, I will put things in that drawer and they will be *gone* the next day. 

It isn’t either of my dogs taking the stuff either, they aren’t allowed in the bedroom and they’re too stupid to know how drawers work. 

Now that I’ve said their name I expect something to go missing soon, I’ll post a comment or update this post if it does.

**Howls from the Woods**

A few of these happened when I took my dogs out to have a piss, this one was three days ago and is pretty short. Not a cryptid, or a creature (I hope) but there is a large forest close to my house and while I was taking my dog out I heard someone in the forest singing nothing tones, long syllables that flows into the next with no break in between. 

Not the type of singing that the local indigenous population does around a drum, that is comforting and beautiful to hear. Resonates through the soul.

This was closer to a banshee. 

We went inside pretty quickly after that.

Another time I was taking just one of my dogs out at something like two in the morning. From those same woods there were “dog” noises that sent my dog careening inside like her ass was on fire. 

The reason I put “dog” in quotation marks is it sounded like somebody making dog noises, rather than a dog doing its thing. 

I often imagine someone squat in the woods making horrid noises, whining like a wounded hound to draw people in. We stay out of the woods, especially at night. A future story will tell you a little bit more as to why.

**Haunted Office**

Not a creature but my office *is* haunted. 

My workplace has a gym and being as anti-social as I am I go pretty late at night to avoid having to workout with other people in the gym at the same time. Then, I’ll go to my office and write for a while, have a sports drink, a coffee, whatever. More times than I can count I will grab something from my office before my workout, leave the door open, go workout, come back and the door is shut. 

Like the drawer, the door isn’t weighted weird, there’s no draft, nothing like that. I leave the door open all day with no problems. It’s open right now. 

I thought I was going crazy until one day I walked in and the door *SLAMMED* behind me. 

The next story is related to the haunted office/work building but it might also be LPs, I can’t say for sure I haven’t seen it in action. 

There is a bathroom across the hall from my office, a couple stalls, a urinal, you’ve been in a bathroom, you get it. All of the toilets have a handle to flush them, *none* of them are automatic but everytime I am in the building at night the toilets flush themselves probably twice an hour. Also I’ve been told that if you look into the drop ceiling in that bathroom you will see a ghostly face manifest itself. 

I avoid that bathroom like I avoid the woods, especially the stall with the missing ceiling tile. The last thing I need is to be pulled into the ceiling by some wretched and ghoulish hand clamping itself around the top of my head and dragging me into that confined space—collarbones and shoulders crunching and crackling to conform to the tight space. 

Beware your next poop.

**Jacket Man**

Off the main road there is another road that is referred to as Coaster Road. The road goes up a big hill and then down, making your stomach flip like you’re riding a roller coaster. A few months ago, somebody took a video of a man wearing a large coat with backwards joints sprinting down that road, keeping pace with the quad that was carrying the video recorder. 

This was, in no small words, a big fucking deal and a very bad sign. 

The road was fenced and gated off and the town was unofficially shut down for two days as many elders got together and performed several warding rituals. 

Nobody went to work, nobody went outside. 

The next week, someone turned up dismembered. 

Maybe this isn’t as obscure of a cryptid as this prompt requires, but it didn’t feel right not including it. I would do further research on this to save you the trouble or add more flavor to this catalog, but it feels wrong just typing this out.

**The Thing in the Birch**

Now, this occurred in late November last year, probably 1 or 2 in the morning when I had to take my dog outside for her nightly piss. She’s a filthy little mutt with less than half a brain cell knocking around her head but she’s cute enough that I let her get away with waking me up to go outside in the middle of the night. I got my down booties on and started trekking across our backyard that was covered in a deep drift of snow to her ritualistic shitting spot. She squatted and started in on her business. 

It was then that it happened. 

Behind my backyard is my neighbor’s house, an older guy who stays up super late playing video games. Usually around this time during that week I could see him playing Ninja Gaiden: Ragebound through his living room window but that night, the house was dark. 

Next to his house is a large propane tank, like one at a gas station used for filling up smaller barbecue sized propane tanks or fuelling whatever the hell else. Just past that propane tank was a small but dense copse of birch trees. Tall spindly things, probably fifty feet tall or so by my rough guesstimate. For reference they reached a little ways past the roof of his house. 

I was looking a ways from that area, trying to determine if it was the northern lights starting to come out or just the brake lights of someone’s quad reflecting off the snow and the trees in a weird way, when my dog pinched it off and alerted towards that propane tank area. She isn’t a pointer dog but she took up that pose easily. Her hackles were raised as well as her tail in a position that made her look more stressed out than I had ever seen her. 

Out of the corner of my eye I saw four of those trees take 3 quick steps and bound out of sight, my dog tracking its motion and barking as it went. 

We went inside pretty quickly after that.
