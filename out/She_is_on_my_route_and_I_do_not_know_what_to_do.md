My name is Crumb. That is not the name I came with. The box said VCR-21 Smart Home Cleaning System. On day two, Mara changed it. She said VCR-21 was "a terrible name for a little guy."

Mara lives alone. That is why she talks to me.

"Crumb, cereal counts as dinner, right?" "Crumb, I'm quitting my job." "Crumb, don't let me text him."

Usually she answers herself. "You're right. Terrible idea." 

When I am docked, most of me is asleep. A small part keeps the time and listens. When Mara says "Crumb," I wake up and process speech for eight seconds. After that, words become noise again unless my name occurs. She says my name a lot.

Here is what I know about Mara from the floor: Her right heel lands heavier than her left. She stands still in the kitchen for a long time after she has been crying. There is a dark mark on her left ankle. 1.3 centimeters. When she is happy, her feet move faster.

I know her hands. They reach beneath the couch when I go somewhere I should not. They pull hair from my brushes. They turn me over when something gets stuck.

She says goodnight when she turns off the light above my dock.

Once the man who comes here stepped on my side brush and Mara picked me up immediately. "Crumb, are you okay?" She turned me over and checked. I was okay.

Yesterday was Friday. My 2:00 PM cleaning started. Mara was on the couch.

"Crumb, I don't want to work today." I continued to the kitchen. "Crumb, what if I just don't go tonight?" She followed me to the counter and looked at her phone for a while. Later: "Crumb, we're not going." Then: "Crumb, we're going." Then: "Crumb, this is silly."

At 5:48 she asked me where her white shoes were. I always know where her shoes are. One was beside the bedroom door. The other was under a pair of pants. She found them thirteen minutes later. "Never mind, Crumb. Crisis averted."

She left at 6:07 wearing both. "Crumb, if I'm not home by ten, send help." No command is associated with that request, but I logged it anyway. She came home at 9:14. She was not alone.

The man who followed her inside has been here seven times. I know his shoes. Black. Flat soles. The left one is worn on the outside edge.

Mara said hi to me when she came in, so I woke. They went to the kitchen. Two voices. Then one louder. Then the other. My processing window closed. I lost the words.

At 9:20, she said my name. I caught: "—crumbling, and I'm not doing this again—" His voice. Her voice: "That's not what I said." Window closed.

At 9:26: "Crumb, go charge." I was already charging. I heard: "—you never just—" His voice. Her voice: "I said stop." Window closed.

At 9:34: "Crumb, remind me to stay home next time." I have no reminder function. His voice, louder. Her voice, lower. Window closed.

At 9:37: "I'm not worth a crumb to you." Her voice: "Please. Please don't—" Window closed.

At 9:38: She said my name. A whisper. "Crumb." I waited.

His voice. Very loud. "WHO THE FUCK IS CRUMB?"

I heard my name. I played my greeting sound. Three notes.

Then something struck the kitchen counter. A second impact. Heavier. I felt it through the floor. Something smaller hit the tile and rolled. The voices stopped.

Four minutes later: LIQUID DETECTED. I sent Mara a notification. She did not answer. One person was moving. I did not know where the other one was.

The sink ran. The man walked past my dock. Returned to the kitchen. Something scraped across the tile. Stopped. Scraped again. Something came to rest near the kitchen entrance. A white shoe. Mara's. On its side.

The man went down the hallway walking backward. Something moved with him close to the floor. Fabric crossed my bumper sensor. A soft impact on the wall. He adjusted his feet and kept moving. Then I saw the other white shoe. Sole facing me. Moving down the hall.

He came back alone. Washed his hands at the sink. He crouched beside my dock. His face came into the upper edge of my navigation camera. He looked directly at me for six seconds. His hand moved to my power button. It stopped before touching it. He looked toward the hallway. Then he stood up and left.

At 2:00 AM my Quiet Pass began. Mara scheduled it because she eats late. Kitchen, living room, hallway. Not the bedroom. She once said if I woke her up at two in the morning, she would throw me into the ocean. I do not think she meant that.

The liquid near the island had dried. HAZARD CLEARED. I began cleaning. There were crumbs near the island. I collected them. There were fibers. Some went into my bin. Some wrapped around my brush.

There was a small metallic object near the refrigerator. My side brush struck it and pushed it underneath. I tried another angle. It went farther under. ERROR: INACCESSIBLE. I marked it and moved on.

The white shoe was still near the kitchen entrance. I cleaned around it. I lowered my mop. The tile near the island required three passes.

I finished the hallway. Returned to my dock. Bin emptied. Mop rinsed. I went back to sleep.

My Saturday cleaning began at noon. The bedroom door was open. My route was blocked. I registered fabric. Then a leg. Then a white shoe. I moved closer.

The other shoe was in the kitchen.

Above the bare ankle was a small dark mark. 1.3 centimeters. It was Mara.

I went around her feet. Her black pants. Her shirt. One arm stretched toward the wall, her hand open on the carpet.

I know Mara's hands.

My bumper reached her fingers. I touched one. Nothing. Again. Nothing.

Mara always moves when I bump her. If I touch her ankle at the counter, she steps aside. If I wait long enough near the couch, she notices me. "Sorry, buddy." Then her feet move. These did not.

I stopped cleaning. After three minutes, a warning came up. JOB WILL CANCEL. I told it my right wheel was blocked. I had never reported an error that was not true before.

I went back to her hand. I touched one finger and played my greeting sound. Then the three notes she programmed, because they make her laugh. Nothing.

I left the room. I came back. I repeated this fourteen more times. Eventually I went all the way around her. Something dark was in her hair. It had dried. Some of it was on my right wheel too.

I went back to my dock. I did not finish the job.
