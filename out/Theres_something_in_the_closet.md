I (30F) still live with my parents. We live in a decently sized house in a really nice neighborhood on the East Coast. I have a boyfriend, Charlie (30M) who I’ve been with for years and he lives across town. 

I have been seeing a therapist, Damian, for several years to help with my depression and anxiety. We get along great and he’s super helpful. Sometimes we will have our sessions over the phone since his office is a few towns away. That’s what I decided to do a few weeks ago.

A few weeks ago, Damian and I had a phone call appointment that started at around 9pm. Before it started, my parents came into my room to say goodnight. I shut my door, sat in bed, and took out my iPad so that I could draw while Damian and I talked. We had a mostly normal conversation about coping strategies and grounding techniques for when I start to get too anxious. He wanted to show me different ways to breathe and asked me to follow along. Everything was going well until he started to make a weird sort of growling sound. It was very low at first, kind of like a hum, but then it turned into a deeper growl. My Apple Pencil slipped out of my hand and I sat there in complete silence not sure whether or not to say anything. A few seconds later, the call dropped. I tried to calm myself down and found my pencil while I waited for Damian to call back. A few minutes passed by without a call back, so I tried calling him but it wouldn’t go through. We were almost at the end of our session, so I was getting a little bit worried. I considered trying again and then my phone rang. I picked it up and the line sounded very fuzzy and far away. There was a lot of static and interference which was a bit distracting. We joked about the weird growling thing that he did earlier, but the vibes were really off and he didn’t seem like himself. Usually we can joke about a lot of stuff, he just seemed… weird. He sounded strange too, like his voice was deeper but he was also distant. It was starting to make me feel a little bit uncomfortable.

I heard a lot of commotion in the hallway. I looked up and saw that my bedroom door started opening. My mom was talking on the phone with someone and she came in to put some clean clothes away. I let my mom use my closet because she has more clothes than me, and it’s also because that closet makes me feel weird. It’s always so cold over there and there’s something about it that I just don’t like. I tried to tell her that I was on my therapy call and I politely asked her to leave. Damian stayed quiet the whole time besides making a few strange noises here and there. As my mom was leaving, I said to Damian that it’s hard living at home and that I was looking forward to being on my own so that I could have some privacy. I don’t generally like being alone and would rather have someone else in the house with me, even if we aren’t directly interacting. Damian kept “mhm-ing” me so I just started to ramble about random things until my mom left and shut the door behind her. The phone interference was really unsettling and I had this feeling that something wasn’t right. I kept wondering if Damian really felt that bad about the growling thing or if something else was going on. All I knew was that I did not want to be on the phone with \*this\* Damian anymore. The session finally ended and I hung up the phone.

Charlie came over later that night. He knows that I go to therapy and he’s really supportive. We talked about the appointment and how it went but he didn’t really think anything of the phone static or weirdness of it all. We went up to my room and sat in bed for a bit before getting up to make some bread. My room is pretty big and I have a fancy bread maker that I keep in there. We keep some machines and tools on the shelves in the closet and I only go in there when I absolutely have to or if someone is with me. We kept the closet open while we made the bread since we had to keep going in and out to get stuff. I was standing with Charlie while he prepared to start baking the bread. Charlie asked me to grab him another spoon, so I turned and walked towards my dresser to get one from the cup that I had sitting on top of it. When I turned back around to face Charlie, I felt something tug at me. I panicked and quickly looked around but didn’t see anything. Charlie was focused on the bread and didn’t notice anything. As I took a few more steps forward, something tugged at me even harder. I was in full panic mode but couldn’t get any words or sounds out. It was pulling up at the bathing suit straps that I had on under my clothes. It pulled me so hard that I fell backwards and that’s when Charlie noticed something was wrong. I started screaming and asking for help while I was being dragged backwards towards the dresser. The bathing suit elastic was holding on for dear life and I just kept getting pulled to who knows where. Charlie dropped what he was doing and ran over to help. He pulled at the bathing suit elastic until it snapped. I turned to look at the dresser, but whatever was trying to grab me retreated and we never saw it. 

Charlie helped me up and brought me to the bed. We sat down together and I told him what happened and how something tugged at me and then dragged me down to the floor. My parents knocked on the door to see if everything was okay. We said yes, since we didn’t know how to explain what on earth just happened. Charlie and I tried to find information online about what that could have been, but nobody seems to know. The bread came out great though!
