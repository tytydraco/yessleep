The dark, dingy motel where I had been staying for the past few nights reeked of a putrid smell I could not place my finger on. In the last few days since the incident with my wife, I had been hiding away in the least populated places I could find, watching the news of my escape grow and grow.

Faltering under the pressure, I slept most of the day and spent the rest looking out of the windows with a growing paranoia that made me question my own sanity until, on a cool night, I stepped down into the parking lot of the motel to examine the night sky from the shadows.

“You need a friend?” Startled, I bolted around and found myself just feet away from a disgruntled-looking woman. “Didn’t mean to sneak up on you. Want a cigarette?” Trying to ease my nerves, I accepted.

“Yeah, sure.” Pulling from her pockets she expelled the tiny white stick and lit it upon putting it in my mouth.

“You sure are jumpy.”

“Sorry for coming off that way; I’ve just been on the road for a while. Haven’t talked much in the meantime.” The woman whom I began to think must have been homeless looked at me searchingly.

“Well, if you want some company in your room, I'd be happy to oblige you.” I stared at her, stunned.

“No, no, I’m sorry. I can’t do anything like that. I'm-” *married.* She looked at me unchanged a moment longer.

“Suit yourself, more fun for me,” she said before shuffling off deeper into the dark.

Taking in my cigarette, I listened to the silent, cool night and thought of the terrible things I witnessed nights ago until a strange tearing began slowly, growing louder and louder.

Turning towards the darkness, the woman was completely invisible. Stepping forward with an uncertainty all too familiar, I continued until a dark, putrid-smelling liquid sprayed out, drenching me.

“Thought you were married.” The woman's voice sputtered with an unnatural tremor. Scraping forward into the dim light. “Thought you didn’t want any of this.” The woman's shirt had been torn wide open, exposing her chest, which was covered with awfully black, scabby skin. Beneath that, a gaping wound starting just above the waistline that split apart all the way to beneath the ribs. The woman's hands were positioned just below them.

“Stop, stop,” I rattled weakly before she used all of her force to tear up and out, separating the sets of ribs from one another, exposing her heart and lungs. Lolling her head back and screaming a horrifically pleased laugh, the woman shook as more of the liquid sprayed from her body. Falling back to the ground, I attempted to shield myself, but the substance only spread farther, covering me entirely. When the fountain of sickness finally ceased, the woman lay down with a terrible, shaking satisfaction.

Unable to take my eyes off the horrific sight, I examined the gore that lay out in front of me until I noticed something that made my blood run cold. Within the snaking trails of the woman's intestines, two pale white orbs shone before independently rolling around until they faced right at me. *Eyes.*

Taking a step forward, I attempted to take a closer look until a striking pain ripped through my left shoulder. Clutching it instinctively, I felt as blood poured from the wound. Turning my head instinctively, I watched as the old man I checked in with at the front of the motel struggled to adjust his jammed shotgun. Too stunned to speak, I did everything in my power to call out but failed as the man raised his gun up once again.

“You get away from that woman!” He screamed horrified before firing an ill-aimed shot which pellets scattered above my head, prompting me to dash to my car, hopping in and peeling out. The old man ran and screamed after me, but soon he was just a speck far behind.

Continuing on with a flat, blank expression, I could not help but focus on the blood upon my hands clutching the wheel. *Why is this happening?* Adrenaline fading, the wound on my shoulder turned from a dull ache to a horrifically searing pain. Heat from the wound spread from my shoulder to the rest of my body until a strange sensation came with it. *Pleasure.*

Disassociating into the road, I lost thought within myself until I hit a large bump that jolted me back into focus. Feeling a terrible squelching in my hand, I looked to my side and realized that without even perceiving it, I had dug my fingers into the holes caused by the shotgun blast.

Tearing them out instantly, I continued on, wide-eyed, questioning my own reality. I have no idea what any of this means, but I don’t know if I will be able to take it if something like this happens again. I don’t understand what my role is in these atrocities or the origin of the eyes that peered at me from the gore, but I am just ready for all of this to be over. I am debating turning myself in for medical attention and protection, but I know if I do, I will likely be facing death for the murders of my wife and now this woman. I am at a loss for what to do and would appreciate any advice going forward.
