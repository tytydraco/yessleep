Hello guys, some days have passed since my first post \[[Part1](https://www.reddit.com/r/nosleep/s/L1Oi8IZOmR)\] and unfortunately things have not gotten better.  
I have been in a frenzy all day, every day. Can't get any sleep.

Me and Thomas got to a conclusion.

“Dude we need to get to the bottom of this.” I told him.

“Steve, what the fuck are we supposed to do? Go to old man Briggs and arrest him because the fucking radio told us to?”

“Bro we should at least talk to him, like, the radio wasn't fucking lying, it matches up.”

“Alright, let's just, think this through for a second okay...? Just because some old radio somehow said Briggs killed someone doesn't make it true, who do you trust more Steve?”

“No Thomas, YOU think about it. What are the chances? This is not a coincidence, something is going on here.”

Thomas sat in silence for a second.

“I trust my gut, Steve, and it's never wrong.”

“And what's it saying?”

There was another moment of silence.

“That you're right, we should at least talk to him.”

And so we did, later that day, in a coffee fueled frenzy we drove to Briggs' house, with my Toyota mind you, the 54 hasn't left the garage since that last time.

“Hey! Steve, Thomas! To what do I owe the pleasure fellas?” Answered Briggs with his usual optimistic and happy-go-lucky personality.

Briggs was the sweetest old man, this big bald gentle giant that always knew the right thing to say to make someone at ease. If you saw him, you'd think he couldn't even hurt a fly...let alone kill a girl.

He let us in and we sat in the living room.

“Coffee? Tea? A diet Coke?”

“No thank you sir, we had plenty of that already.” Replied Thomas.

“Well then, what's on your mind fellas?”

“Uhm we were wondering, sir...if you could tell us something...anything, about this cold case from the late 80s...this girl was fou--”

“You're looking into the Gabrielle murder?” The old man bluntly interrupted me.

“Uhm...Y-yes, sir, we were jus--”

“And why is that?” His tone shifted dramatically, he now took a more serious stance and expression, his eyebrows dropping down low, transforming his eyes from that of an innocent deer to those of an hungry wolf.

“School project!” Said Thomas, saving me.

“I see.”

The room got cold, very cold out of nowhere. It felt like we were back in the old 54 on the night everything went tits up.

“Well, fire away.” Continued Briggs, his voice reaching a unheard low tone, it felt like we were talking to a completely different person.

“T-thank you, sir...we-we were really just wondering if anyone was ever caught by the police?...we-we couldn't find anything about that.” Thomas' voice was shaky, he could feel the shift in the air as well.

“No.”

“Oh.”

At this point, you could touch the tension in the air. Briggs was staring us down like we just asked about his dead wife or some shit. It was creepy af.

“The FBI took over and we never heard anything since.” He then concluded.

“T-that's interesting...sir. T-thank you very much, we...we will definitely add this detail to our little project!” I awkwardly replied, a shy grin on my face. Trying to not seem guilty as charged.

“Y-yes, thank you...we...we won't disturb you any further sir. T-thank you ag--”

“You know, boys. You should really be careful about what you look for. Some things are better left unchecked.”

And with those final words of wisdom, he showed us the door and we left.

“Dude, what the fuck was that.”

“I don't know Steve, but at this point I don't know if I can trust him bro.”

“I fucking told you man, this shit is not a coincidence, there's something up with this.”

The drive back was a quiet one.

“I think we should leave this alone man, Briggs was right, we'll be in deep shit if we keep going.” Thomas finally broke the silence.

“Are you kidding? We were chosen bro, you said this a million times, what are the chances of all of this happening?”

“Are you fucking serious? We're way past chances and science for that matter, all of this is fucking insane dude, we need to get rid of it.”

“I tried, the fucking dealership is gone, Thomas. Like it never happened, you understand?”

“Then burn it, Jesus. This is beyond ourselves, the fucking CIA or FBI or Area51 should be on this, not two fucking idiots from a random city in Montana.”

“But she chose US, Thomas. SHE. CHOSE. US.”

“Do you hear yourself? You sound like fucking Smigol. Tell me this, why would “she” choose us? Why not someone more important?”

“She's connected to the car man, that must be her only channel of communication, maybe it wa—”

“Really? Why not the guy who sold it to you?” Chuckled Thomas.

“I fucking told you dumbass, he's gone, as if he never existed.”

“Oh so now he's a ghost too? Some angel sent from heaven to sell you that piece of shit car?”

“Whatever man, Charlotte chose me, not you. You don't have to be in this.”

“Who the fuck is Charlotte?”

“I-I meant, Gabrielle.” The lack of sleep was getting to me.

“You're fucking losing it, man.”

He was right, he was so right.

“Anyway, I am in this, I was there, I know about this shit and I don't like it one bit. You want my advice? Forget it. Sell it. Burn it. Scrap it. I don't care, but if our 15 years of friendship means anything to you, you should think about what I'm saying. Trust me.”

We laid low for a while after that, I think we were both deeply thinking about what was the right thing to do but fuck man, I needed some sleep.

I just couldn't get any. No sleep, at all.

So, in the middle of the night...

I decided to go for a drive.

I took the 54 out of the garage and started cruising, the radio turned off.

I just needed to let loose, got a nice synthwave playlist going off on Spotify and I rolled down the window to feel the night breeze.

All went well for about 45 minutes, then a familiar sound filled the cockpit.

“**Bzz**...HELL-O...**bzz**...STE-VEN...”

“Fuck, here we go again.”

Truth is, I was hoping that would happen. I believe I have been chosen for a reason and I needed to know why.

I knew that would finally put me to sleep.

“Alright, hit me, Gabrielle, I'm listening.” I pulled over and took out my notepad.

The laughing track filling my ears.

“**Bzz**...BRI-GGS IS...**bzz**...GUILTY...”

“Why me, what do I do? Can you hear me?”

“**Bzzz**...**Bzzz**...**Bzzz**”

“Hello? Charlo—I mean Gabrielle?”

“**Bzzz**...THOMAS...**Bzzz**”

“What? No it's-it's me, Steven.”

“**Bzzz**...IS NOT...**Bzzz**...WHO HE SEEMS...”

“...what?” I let out, shocked.

The laughing track once more enveloped the cockpit.

“W-what are you telling me?”

The laughs grew more and more.

Distorted and uncanny laughs of people who are and have been gone for a long time.

Then a flash blinded me, as if someone had just turned on the lights in a dark room.

Immediately after, I...came to my senses? I don't know, it was as if I blinked and found myself back in my garage, sitting in the 54.

One moment I'm there, and the next one I'm here.

As I slowly realized where I was, stinging hot pain enveloped my body all at once.

My hands and knees scorched, dirty and bloody.

If you saw me you would think I fell off a skateboard and scraped my body all over the concrete.

I felt like I was dying from a thousand cuts.

A slime like substance filling the gaps in my flesh. It looked and smelled like motor oil.

“What the fuck happened?”

I looked in the rear view mirror.

My eyes bloodshot and my face black as coal.

It was 3 am.

I cleaned up and went to bed, exhaustion washing over me and the sweet release of sleep finally enveloping me.

I woke up the next day quite late but incredibly rested.

I felt my mind clear as a sunny winter day.

I knew why Gabrielle chose me, I understood.

She chose me because I believe her, because I listen to her, because I trust her.

I was gonna call Thomas and tell him everything that happened: the radio, the light, my injuries...but he had already sent me a message.

“Steve, I found something that I must show you, DO NOT take out the 54. Call me.”

I glanced over at my notepad, reading yesterday's transcription.

Whatever Thomas had to tell me, I needed to be careful.

“Steven?”

“Hey dude, what's up?” I casually answered Thomas' call.

“Bro took you fucking long enough, we gotta see eachother, I have something to sho--”

“Slow down, what is it?”

“I can't tell you over the phone, you just gotta trust me.”

“Thomas you better tell me what this is about.”

There was an hesitation in his voice.

“...Alright fuck, look you were right okay? Briggs has something to hide, something fucking huge but I just can't tell you man, I'm getting more and more paranoid, you just need to see it.”

“Wait, what happened to “just let it go bro”?”

“Don't rub it in asshole, it's not what you think...can we just fucking meet already?”

“Where?”

“Somewhere public, see you at the park in 15, I'm getting in the car right now.”

What was I thinking? Thomas, my bestfriend of 15 years is not who he seems because some fucking ghost told me?

I know him better than anyone on earth, Gabrielle might have her reasons but there's no way she's right about him.

Right guys? I should trust my friend right?

I don't know what to think anymore or who to trust.

All I know is that I got to the park to meet with Thomas but...nobody showed.

“I'M HERE”  
“HELLO?”  
“THOMAS HOW FAR ARE YOU?”  
“DUDE WTF”  
“CM ON BRO”

These are the messages I sent him while waiting.

On my way home I turned on the radio...on the Toyota that is.

“Bzz...The devastating crash today, the driver 24 year old Thomas...bzz...was rushed to the hospital with life-threatening injuries, doctors say they will do “everything they can” to save his life...”

My heart skipped a beat but my brain didn't.

I immediately turned the car around and headed for the city hospital.

“Is he gonna make it?” I asked the doctor.

“We don't know sir, the injuries are very critical and there's no way of knowing whether he will react to the...”

My ears went deaf, the doctor kept on babbling on but I just wasn't there.

My mind was buzzing, looking for answers, was it just an accident?

“Sir?...Sir.”

The voice snapped me back to reality.

“Sir we need to ask you some questions, you were the last person to talk to the victim.”

A couple of cops were now standing in front of me, looking at me with suspicion and doubt.

“The victim?”

“Thomas.”

“Y-yeah, sure. Do you know what happened?”

“Sir, it's an ongoing investigation, we can't divulge information to the public...”

The interview didn't last long. I told them the truth...well, not the whole truth but enough for them to leave me alone.

Look, I'm not the brightest tool in the shed, I know that. But I had a feeling. What happened to Thomas was no accident.

If there's one thing I learned from this whole ordeal is that coincidences don't exist.

Everything happens for a reason.

I tried getting some sleep that night but I was back to square one.

This time not because of the anxiety from Gabrielle, but because of the uncertainty in my friend's fate.

He's my bestfriend in the entire world and he is now fighting for his life, all because of me.

As I laid in my bed, eyes wide open, staring at the ceiling.

I heard a noise coming from downstairs.

If someone got to Thomas because, perhaps, he knew too much, I'd be the next in line.

I got my father's .45 from the drawer.

Barefoot, in the dark, weapon drawn, I slowly made my way downstairs.

Checking every corner, convinced that some hitman was waiting for me.

But there was nobody.

The noise was coming from the garage.

From the 54.

The cockpit was lit by the golden light of the radio.

A muffled voice mixed with white noise was drowned by the windows.

I opened it.

“**BZZ**...ITWASBRIGGS...**BZZ**...ITWASBRIGSS...**BZZ**...ITWASBRIGGS...”

I don't know what do anymore.

I don't know who to trust.

I don't even know if I can trust myself.

What do I do?
