Wow. I’m both shocked and glad so many of you had ideas on what I should do about this whole possession thing. I guess it should have been pretty obvious to go to like a priest or something, and I promise I was gonna go out today and do all that but-

It was hot as FUCK outside today. Like a freak heatwave or something the sun just really started beating down. Also doesn’t help that I think I might be sick. My body’s been really warm, almost hot. Thank God I’ve got an AC or I’d be in agony. Then again maybe Ruile would be so kind as to cure me of my sickness haha.

Speaking of, someone pointed out that I accidentally typed “Lei” instead of “Ruile” a few times. Honestly can’t believe I missed a typo like that. Though I guess it’s because “Ruile” also has the same letters as “Lei” but like, less like some sorta half-assed anagram? I dunno. But maybe I was just whacked.

Funny that, where I’m from, a “Lei” is something you’d give to like a visitor or an important person. Maybe I was secretly really smart for half a second eh? Usually made of flowers, nuts, or tea leaf though, and it’s worn around the neck, don’t think people put them inside their bodies.

I sent a message off to a friend of mine, now he’s not a priest but I’m guessing he knows one. Well, friend is a strong word. We had some classes together in Highschool. Point is, he actually goes to church so he’s already ahead of me in the knowledge department. He hasn’t replied yet but maybe he can introduce me to a priest?

So while I was trapped here in my house all day, and yes I let my professors know I was out sick, though with Ruile’s influence I could probably just coast the whole semester at this point. Right, rambling, on a fucking text document, anyway so I’m stuck here all day right? Figure, might as well chat up this Angel living inside me.

“This heat,” I spoke aloud to an empty room, “that you, Ruile?”

A flash of fire coalesced before me, the form of Ruile emerged from the split second inferno, covered in what appeared to be new, darkly colored robes.

“In a way,” they stated, “but not exactly something I did intentionally.”

“So why me?” I flatly asked.

“Hmm?” Ruile’s expression was one of feigned ignorance, or perhaps genuine inability to comprehend my asking of such a question.

“Why did you choose me and not someone else?”

“Because you were the first one to stop and help.”

“How did you get there?”

“I fell.”

“From what, heaven?” I joked.

Ruile’s expression hardened.

“Yes.”

I froze. I may not be religious but most everyone knows that Lucifer, the devil, was a Fallen Angel. Just who the hell is this?

“Do not compare me to Lucifer.”

The room, despite what I thought earlier, grew hotter.

“Ruile, why did you fall from heaven?”

“Because God is dead, and I am all that’s left.”

I didn’t know whether or not to laugh or vomit. This has to be a demon, or something, what a lie, right? God can’t be dead. The sun still rises, the world still turns, the world isn’t in disarray, so how can God be dead?

“Please, do not think so highly of yourself that you could comprehend the totality of Death.”

“Excuse me?” I stood up.

Ruile simply stared at me with Judgmental eyes.

“Just who the fuck do you think you are? I don’t understand death? Id argue the average person knows a hell of a lot more about death than all you ‘holier than thou’ fucks who hide away in some hidden paradise where death is nothing more than the fucking bus driver to and fro.”

The room fell silent, the gentle crackling of flames unseen punctuating the stillness.

Then, Ruile let out a soft, stifled chuckle.

“What?” I asked, mildly infuriated, but stifling a laugh myself.

“It’s been a while since I’ve conversed with humanity, I may have gotten a bit rusty with my word choice explaining such sophisticated concepts.”

“Are you mocking me?”

“No, my child, there would be no point.”

“So what then? Got nothing to say?”

“God’s death is not so simple as the death of a human. Heaven and God are intertwined, the same breath cycling through one another for all eternity and time immortal. When one is lost so too is the other. Without Heaven, we Angels will die off, unless we find a host to sustain ourselves.”

Ah, so thats it.

“So I’m just a lifeline for you?”

“Yes, I can no longer persist outside of you, for my home, my seat, is gone.”

“What the fuck does that mean for me? Do I need to eat for two people? Am I gonna die earlier? What-“

Ruile shushed me with a firm, quick hand.

“Ironically, I don’t know. I’ve never done this before.”

“Then whats the endgame for this?”

“Hmm?”

“Your goal, ultimate reason for being?”

Ruile smiled gently.

“Judgement.”

“…and you really are an Angel?”

“Yes.”

“Then tell me your real name.”

“You already have it, my child.”

Ruile isn’t a very angelic name, hmm?

“Then figure out why I chose to share it with you.”

Following this exchange I was overcome with a wave of exhaustion, likely feeling Ruile’s exhaustion.

While I appreciated the more direct method of conversation than the repeated and complex flashing of images and concepts, I believe it took a toll on Ruile.

I don’t know if I should believe Ruile. They have every reason for lying to me and nothing to gain from the truth. Though honestly I am happy to ride out the next few days and avoid classes. Maybe this doesn’t have to be doom and gloom. Maybe I should be abusing this favor a bit more.

But I’m really wondering what the endgame is here, will I be used up and discarded like a tube of toothpaste as Ruile emerges like a phoenix reborn? Or is this a more permanent arrangement. Oh god will I be immortal now? Fuck dude I’m kinda hyped for this now.

Though something is bothering me. The sun has been getting a LOT hotter over the past few days. Like abnormally so. I don’t know if this is a global warming thing but it’s getting a little concerning. Why would Ruile be doing that?
