I observe as I go down the woobby and squeaky wooden staircase leading to the door of the inn where I stay for a while this small municipality lost on this ignored corner of the east of the province. A few small crossed streets forming only a forgotten point on the maps...

Nothing exceptional to do with it...

A small market providing some basic necessities.. like a little alcohol. There was also a small cafe attached to the slightly smaller market... A small school that served as a library and post office since the last families had deserted this dying commune to which there were practically only a handful of aging people still attached to it to this day. The absence of a church in this village caused a certain discomfort... as if something was wrong in the painting... This fact was much more confusing than the absence of an internet area in the region, certainly unusual... but given the average age of the population, it could seem coherent...

Some young people had thought they could make a good deal by settling in this municipality because they could acquire some real estate at low prices, but before long, their idea no longer seemed so wise to them.

A river snaked to the north. Only place in the area truly appreciable. Watercourse of modest width. Shallow water and fast flow. On the edge of the forest. On the shore, some diverse and divergent shoots.

Everyone remained cloistered at nightfall. No soul to travel the streets and even less to venture to the side of the river. If it weren't for my presence at the starless night hostel, no one would live there and I had the impression that the place had not been occupied by anyone for a long time. The room where I slept, minimally furnished and devoid of decoration, was hardly inviting with the appearance as well as the aromas of a closed place where life had not deigned to manifest itself for many years. It was to be wondered why the establishment persisted in continuing to operate. An old family affair possibly fueled by the memory of a time where the days had been better on this hole. In fact, no one was willing to talk about the origins and existence of this place.

Generally, we would tend to believe that old people cut off from social relationships with individuals foreign to those who make up their usual circle of relationships who have followed their entire existence would see their tongues easily untied in the face of the approach of the first newcomer and however, here habit seemed to have led them to retreat deeply to the point of simply exchanging the most basic formulas of a banal courtesy. Their children were all gone... often decades ago. At this rate, in about ten years there would probably be no more inhabitants in the area. The two or three recently landed young couples were probably already studying ways to desert the village quickly by minimizing their losses... I met a few of them while walking, but had not been able to seize any favorable moment to start the conversation. And they seemed as evasive as the ancients...

This patelin did not tolerate at any time that the slightest word was not uttered in the place of his past. The northern river became the only one to accommodate the presence of a traveler from outside.

So, it was on this evening in November that on the edges of this river I managed to spring some flames from a pile of wood with the help of my meager skills and thus manage to warm myself and illuminate this dark and isolated corner where I liked to gather myself every evening since my arrival... Not a living soul. The branches of the trees around me danced softly to the rhythm of a light breeze. I felt the water following the course of the river imperturbably by my side.

I drank a little wine and then fell asleep. The memories of those few hours of sleepiness are very vague in my mind.

At one point it seemed to me to hear something beyond the crackle of the embers.

A throat clearing.

A breath.

A presence.

Was I dreaming? A voice that rose in the night. The village was inhabited... (possessed?) Of this spirit who came to address me that night...

The forces of the old world were hidden when human civilizations extended their bundles deeper and deeper into the once little desecrated lands of human presence... dotted with vast virgin areas of which there are many mysteries.

This new world was very different from what European immigrants knew of their passage on this land from the perspectives that were naturally offered to them on the other side of the Atlantic. If Europe had also known its demons, they were familiar to it, domesticated, framed now within its folklore.

However, things were very different on this new continent discovered late and whose proportions remained unknown and underestimated. The indigenous peoples lived there in a strange harmony. Their heterogeneous lifestyles in accordance with the vast expanses of this territory. Having seen days of millennia before, they had learned to live together and to establish a certain form of communication with the divine Entities that were the ones ruling on this unknown soil. The regions to avoid were well established.. the sacred borders scrupulously respected...

The order thus established had the principle of continuing quietly until the end of Existence...

Then the Europeans arrived.

At first, the changes were insignificant to say the least. Some of the colonies at one point regretted this path chosen in unconsciousness... but their disappearance was not too noticeable...

And there came a time where newcomers were more numerous.

These people were different from those who had been there for a long time. Their nature as explorers pushed them to set foot on all floors, to monopolize the slightest corner that was unknown to them. They erected dwellings in places where only venturing was absolutely inconceivable for individuals belonging to indigenous populations.

Towards another horizon, the big cities would announce themselves.

Then wars.

Then the taking of the land and the annihilation of this ancestral kingdom.

And the new world.

The old world of Europe that sought to spread.

Organized groups.

Industrialization.

Science and Electricity.

The abundance of light.

The old Entities could not manifest themselves under the fire that became more and more present. Now cornered in dark holes, sordid sewers, gloomy cellars and various terrifying places...

The world was stabilizing within rational thought.

Exchanges with the spirits of the forest would not be maintained.

The powers, without disappearing, were relegated to vulgar moving shadows in the hollow of the darkness of the trees away from human agglomerations. And if there was no lack of space in the north, the evil had been committed...

The world was spinning empty.

The only way to recover their ancient power would be to erase the civilizations that followed the colonization of those from the ancient world.

One day they possessed some men and were thus able to appropriate the management of the companies of which they methodically elaborated the fall. A path to the fall and the end of the era of prosperity of the new masters of the earth. We will therefore have been able to see the reason that gave way to the dementia that the human was suffering in the grip of submission caused by the will of the Entities determined to recover the kingdom that had been taken away from them.
