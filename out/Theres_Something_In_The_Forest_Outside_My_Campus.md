It's been a few days since “the transfer”, as my classmates have been calling it. The current theory is that the government is running a teleportation experiment. Some Philadelphia experiment stuff. Of course, in order for that to make sense, you have to ignore the obvious flaws.

If the government really is testing some fucked up experiment, then why us? Why choose a random highschool to run your experiment?

Anyway, some stuff has happened since my last post. I'll start where I left off.

Most of my classmates calmed down. The teachers were keeping their composure, but I could tell they were scared. I can't blame them. Just imagine being an educator one second, and finding yourself in a survival situation the next. We have procedures for lockdowns, but this? We have no procedures for this.

After about an hour of just talking, the teachers decided they were the most qualified to go out and scout the area. They gathered what they could to defend themselves, and told the hall monitors to keep an eye on us *kids*.

I tried to come with them, as I had tons of survivalist experience. But I was just a highschool kid. They said I'd be safer inside the campus.

I had this feeling in my gut. Something was about to go wrong. I started imagining that we'd never see them again. That the forest would consume them.

And I was right...

After 6 hours of waiting, one of the seven teachers that left came running back to the campus. Mrs. Sanders; the highschool's gym teacher. She had a face of terror. She was horrified by something. By whatever she saw.

Now, Mrs. Sanders is a tough woman. You'd be lucky if she didn't go off on you for having the wrong form in a sports game. She is a military veteran and her husband was divorcing her because she was "too aggressive". So it was shocking to see such a hardened woman looking as terrified as she was. Whatever she witnessed out there must've been the most horrific thing...

One of the other teachers, one of the four who stayed behind, decided to speak up in the long silence. "Sanders. Where are the other teachers? What happened?"

She took a moment to calm herself down. But even then, she still looked scared. "There's... There's something out there..."

After a few minutes of calming down and barricading the front doors via her request, she finally told us what happened.

**"I was the one leading the group. I was the most fit and fearless, so it made sense. I didn't expect much. I mean, what exactly would somebody expect in this situation? Our campus is now in the middle of a god damn forest.**

**We made it about 5 miles away when Mr. Adams suggested a break. We sat down, and I figured I should try and find some food. The cafeteria food won't last long, so it's really our only option. Even with the frozen food in storage, we'd only last maybe a year at most. Assuming we're stuck here forever, of course.**

**So I told Mr. Adams to keep the others in check while I climbed one of the nearby trees. I found some... Some strange looking... Well, I don't really know what they are. They could be fruit, but they don't look like any kind of fruit I've seen. I figured I'd test it in Miss Cherry's science classroom when we got back. I decided to get some more just in case, so I climbed higher.**

**..... When I got to the top... I was in awe... Trees and forestry for *miles*. No buildings, no roads. I saw a river nearby and figured we should also grab some water before we headed back.**

**After taking in the sight, I climbed back down. ... The silence was the first thing I noticed. Before I climbed the tree, I could hear strange bird sounds and bugs all around us. But when I reached the ground, it was silent. The only thing I could hear was my own labored breath. It is hard to breathe. At that moment, I realized how thick the air is. It took effort to breathe. And even then, it felt like I was breathing in *too much*.**

**The last thing I noticed was that nobody was asking me what I found. I turned to tell the others to start heading back. It was creepy. Even for me. I just wanted to be in the safety of the campus walls. But there was no one there.**

**It was like they vanished. As if they were never there to begin with. But they didn't leave without a trace. I saw blood. From the looks of it, one of them started bleeding, while the others ran away in a hurry in separate directions, judging by the pattern of the flattened grass around me.**

**I just wanted to go back to the campus, but I knew someone would shame me if they believed I just left them behind without a care in the world. So I followed the blood trail.**

**It didn't lead me very far. But I stopped when I heard it. This sickening squelching noise. I saw something moving. An animal of some sort. After moving my head a bit, I saw it fully. It was some kind of bird. But big and bulky. Feathers all over, especially on the tail. It wasn't long before I saw the source of that sound from earlier.**

**It was eating Mr. Adams. My god... I can still hear him in my head... He could barely speak. He was damn near dead already, but he was still trying to scream for help. But all that came out was gurgled pleas muffled by his own blood.**

**And then it looked at me.**

**Those eyes... This monster had eyes like the devil himself... It looked at me with a sick sense of curiosity. Like it was still trying to figure out what I am. It tilted its head like a curious bird, a bit of Adams' intestines still hanging from its closed mouth.**

**I think the only reason I'm still alive is because it was already eating. I guess it just didn't want to hunt when it already had a meal in front of it.**

**I ran! I just bolted toward the campus as fast as I could! I feel terrible for leaving the others out there, wherever they are. For not helping Adams. ... But what was I supposed to do...?"**

Mrs. Sanders started to freak out again after she told us what happened. I was in shock. Hell, I'm still in shock. A monster? Mr. Adams' death? I think I'm still trying to register it all.

Stacey is terrified. Derek is trying to stay calm, but I can tell he's scared shitless. And I don't blame them. Mrs. Sanders, the tough and strict woman that bent us into shape, the teacher that basically made us afraid of gym class... And she is terrified of that *thing* out there.

We're still trying to figure out what to do. Some of us want to send a search party to help find the other teachers, while the teachers who chose to stay want to set up a survival plan.

I think most of us still have hope that this nightmare will be over. ... But a few of us, I can see their eyes. A few have already given up hope...

If this really is the government's doing- If there's any possibility that they are reading this post... Please. Why us? We are law abiding citizens. Innocent people.

Whoever is responsible, I beg of you... Please let us free... Please let this nightmare end... Before anybody else dies like Adams...
