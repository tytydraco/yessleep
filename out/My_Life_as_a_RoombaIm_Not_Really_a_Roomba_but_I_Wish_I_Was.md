Now I know the title sounds crazy, but just hear me out. A lot of events have made me come to this conclusion.

In a world of giants, my job as a measly floor bug is to clean up the filth of my forefathers. If that filth came in the form of bright wrappers representative of a capitalist world fighting for the longest lasting currency. Attention. Or a dull gray can of diet coke representative of wanting to feel the cheap pleasures of life without the consequences. Until those consequences hit you like a freight train in the form of thin hair and an even thinner bank account. (cancer btw)

Nevermind my ramblings, unlike a suckerfish in a fishtank I’m not solely used for my cleaning abilities. No no no. I can do many more things, like stare at you from your floorboards as you check if you creased your shoes for the 47th time. Or make quiet skittling noise while I avoid the crushing of a steel toed boot.

As I continue my monotonous work of eating dust, dirt, and other filth my mind begins to wander. I think about time passing while other machines do work for me. I think about the world as it was before, clean, simple but at the same time so much more complex.

But I can’t really imagine a world before, as it feels like a lost dream, floating between the lines of potential reality and potential fiction. And to be honest, I’m not entirely sure how I ended up in this situation. All I remember is waking up one day with feces dripping from my mouth (just my luck lmao) as my…masters? Creators? Parents? Walked passed me with their gigantic strides and even more gigantic tentacles. Erm yeah…I’m not entirely sure what they are.

I’m not the only one however…thousands of creatures that live their lives exactly like me. Spending their days eating filth and their nights looking forward to doing the same thing tomorrow. Along with their mating of one another that I honestly don’t think is consensual. (rape is bad) But that’s what makes me different. Special. Unlike them I have dreams. Long, sensual moments of astral projection where I go back to a world that feels both familiar and forgotten at the same time.

In these dreams my name is Rom Baroney, I live at 88 Rombauer Drive. Sometimes the dreams continue throughout different nights and sometimes I feel like I’m in a different timeline. One of my favorite timelines in particular is when I’m on my couch watching my favorite youtuber, [KingTurtle1010](https://www.youtube.com/@KingTurtle-1010), make yet another Creepcast ranking video (they’re so funny). And in these dreams I like to make the most of my time. I don’t like to conform to the rules of this false world. They despise murder and molestation of what they call “minors.” So during my nightly excursions I do whatever I want, it seems to have no consequences each night. But it was one house that I raided one night that caused me to write this story. It contained a horror so horrorifying that I had to type out this post immediately. 

It was the night that I met the Roomba Machine…

Shattering the glass of another home was nothing new to me at this point, it was just the daily routine of a creature too bored to stay at home but not excited enough to go all out. It was a standard suburban home, 2 story, garage, shed in the back. The spitting image of middle America…on the outside at least.

I entered the home and explored, nothing special. A kitchen with colorful cereal, and not a lot of greens. *Probably a fat family,* *a bunch of crackers too.* I thought as I opened a box of their mini club crackers and feasted. Saying their fat isn’t the nicest thing but I can’t control myself sometimes. Not that it matters, what’s the point of being nice to people if everything resets each night?

I walked into the living room, a 75 in. flat screen Samsung TV that created a glare of my face. Rom Baroney was a good looking man, a person who you would say hi to on the street and think about for the next 5 years wondering if he thought you were cute. Heh maybe good looking was a bit of an under statement.

Anyways, I turned on their TV and saw “My 600 pound life” was playing which really confused me on the fat assumption earlier. I then went into the basement, a place that could either contain valuables or nothing at all. (I honestly don’t care about the valuables so I probably shouldn’t have written that) So I descended the stairs, one creaky step at a time. And as I walked down an old box tv suddenly came into view. One that your parents might have played NES on, and beside it was a box of old tapes.

As I looked at the tapes, most of their labels were either ripped off or worn down from time. But one was in readable condition. It read.

“The A-maz–g W—ld of -umball: Lost Episode”

My mouth was wide open. I LOVE THE AMAZING WORLD OF GUMBALL! It was always my favorite cartoon growing up and getting the opportunity to watch a potentially lost episode was thrilling to me.

I immediately put the VHS into the VHS reader and booted up the episode. The iconic intro started and I couldn’t help but bop my head to the beat. That was until we got to the title card. \\

It said…”Cumball Wetterson”

*Oh my, what a vulgar title for a kids show!* I thought, I can see why it’s a lost episode. There’s no way Cartoon Network would allow this.

Well the episode started and Gumball was in the bathroom washing his hands, and you know when you accidentally splash your pants when washing your hands and it looks like you totally pissed yourself? Well that happened and Gumball didn’t seem to notice, so he proceeded to walk into the school's hallway like usual. Everything was normal until Darwin saw Gumball and started yelling, “LOOK AT GUMBALL’S PANTS! I SURE WONDER WHAT HE WAS DOING IN THE BATHROOM” and the whole school laughed at him.

Feeling betrayed by his own brother Gumball jumped on top of Darwin and started to claw his eyes out. But his eyes were realistic. And blood coming out of him was too. The scene then cuts to the two boys in Principle Brown’s office while he was giving them a lecture on how they need to be nice to each other because brothers look out for each other.

“You boys need to be nice to each other because brothers look out for each other.” he said, “and to help encourage you I have employed help from a good friend of mine to give you two a lesson.”

Principal Brown then pointed at the wall and fifteen massive tentacles shot out of it and ripped him to pieces.

“Cuthulu?” the two boys asked as their beloved principle breathed his last breaths. Clearly this didn’t go to plan. 

Gumball and Darwin then ran out into the hallway as the tentacles chased them, killing every student that it could find in their path.

Once the boys got outside of the school they could see the true scope of horror,  Cthulhu was at least a hundred feet tall and was killing everyone in the entire town. Larry, Tina, even Banana Joe were turned into piles of their own flesh.

“What do we do?” Darwin asked frantically as Gumball was clearly thinking of a plan. 

“I know! We just need to call the guy!” Gumball proclaimed.

“NO, we can’t trust him after what happened.”

“He’s the only one that could save us now.” Gumball said as he pulled out the phone from Season 2 Episode 7 “the phone” and dialed a number. (Which I thought was strange because the phone was destroyed at the end of that episode by Ocho.

“Hey it’s me I need you here now…it’s urgent.” I was extremely curious who he was going to call.  
  
Immediately a man in a suit came running down the road at a fantastic speed and stopped in front of the boys.

“HP LOVECRAFT” I yelled at the TV when the revolutionary cosmic horror writer came on the screen.

“Heard someone needed some help” Lovecraft said while raising an eyebrow.

“Yeah…that.” Gumball then pointed at Cthulhu who was still devouring the town one by one.

“Oh..I see. Let me Lovecraft up an idea.”

He then proceeded to pull out a typewriter and type vigorously.

“What are you doing?” Darwin incurred.

“Just fixing up a few things. Should fix this problem super quickly.”

“...and there!” Lovecraft exclaimed as he slammed the final key of his typewriter and suddenly Cthulhu disappeared. Like it literally just disappeared into thin air.

“WHOA!” The two boys yelled, “How did you do that?”

“The pen is mightier than the sword.”

“Can I see what you wrote?” Gumball asked and Lovecraft obliged. “Why did you write all of these racist and xenophobic slurs in this? They don’t even apply to this story.”

“Well you know me, ol’ Lovecraft.” He said as a laugh track that I’ve never heard in the show before played. “Well I’ll see you boys later.” Lovecraft then ran back down the  street and out of sight.

The boys then returned to their home and reminisced about the day's adventure. But while this was happening I could hear a faint wiring noise in the background. I wondered what it was until a small, circular machine careened across the carpet and continued to make its noise.

*What is that?* I wondered as the machine went back and forth across the living room carpet. *Is it cleaning?* My mind then suddenly snapped back to reality and I read what word was on the machine. 

“Roomba” I read out loud as I continued to gaze upon this amazing piece of equipment. It was cleaning the floors so fast. Much faster than I ever could have done back in my other body. This machine was quick, efficient, and wasn’t subject to the toils of life. It was…free. More free than I have ever felt in my strange life.

Then the episode ended. Leaving me once again staring at myself in the glare of the TV. This episode has taught me so much about the way I should start living my life. As a roomba. But how would I accomplish this? My mind immediately went back to the lost episode. “The pen is mightier than the sword.” I said out loud as my mind suddenly shifted back to reality. 

I was waking up.

When I arose from my slumber I immediately pulled out my samsung galaxy s9 and started posting this story to r/nosleep. The very story that you are reading now.

Now it is time to manifest into the being that I always wanted and needed to be.

I am a roomba.

I have always been a roomba.

And I will clean every floor in my roomba body.
