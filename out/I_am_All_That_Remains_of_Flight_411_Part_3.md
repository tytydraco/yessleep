I am being followed.

I wasn’t sure at first. Figured maybe I was just a bit on edge given the whole “plane crash” thing you know? Started out with this guy at the grocery store a couple of days ago. Tucked in blue polo. Not very common out here, especially out where I live. Sure wearing shades in a grocery store is, odd, but hell stranger things have happened. We kept bumping into each-other as I combed the aisles. Not actually coming into contact but like, metaphorically you know? After the first few times I figured we just happened to be going the same route through the store. Then it stopped feeling a coincidence. While it was unnerving constantly seeing the man’s face in every single fucking aisle as I would turn the corner, I had quite a few other more important things on my mind at the time so I didn’t really take it to mind. 

Then it happened the next day at the DMV. Maybe I should have bought a lottery ticket at some point because not only have I survived a plane crash basically unscathed, but the man who had been following me around the grocery store the day before just so happened to be my clerk at the DMV. While I can’t say for sure if he had already been working there prior, or wether or not he was the clerk who had helped me on my last visit, but seeing him sitting there before me has to be far beyond the realm of pure chance. He never said anything off, or out of the ordinary, but honestly that made it just that much weirder. 

Well, that and the result of today’s visit.

Apparently, the whole “false death” thing isn’t that uncommon. At least from what the DMV clerk said. It happens often enough that it only took a brief scan of my fingerprints to confirm my identity and that I was, in fact, not dead. I’m not exactly sure how to feel about this. I’m glad to be on track to getting my life back, new documents and everything, but there’s no way it could have just been that easy. 

It’s also really bugging me that the one clerk who actually was able to tell me this good news, that none of the other clerks had been privy to, seemed to be following me. Sure it’s still potentially a massive coincidence, and at this point far more plausible than my prior circumstances, but you have to admit it’s really fucking odd.  

As for how easy it was to get my death reversed, I’m beginning to think the myriad of clearances and documentation I’ve acquired to be a commercial airline pilot likely helped. 

Speaking of, I fully plan on going back to flying as soon as I can. I plan on calling the company tomorrow morning to… Well really I haven’t the faintest clue of what to say. Maybe get them up to speed, try offer to help with the investigation. Not sure what I could tell them that would be of any real use in locating the aircraft.

Though, thats the other thing. Almost three weeks now and in spite of all of the massive news coverage, there’s been fuck all in terms of information. The FAA has been, almost aggressively, tight lipped. Cynical as it sounds I feel like it’s an optics thing. They haven’t learned anything in all this time and can’t tell an eager and potentially volatile public that billions of their tax dollars have effectively bought them nothing. 

Maybe I could be the one piece that could crack this case wide open. Maybe I’m the only hope anyone has of finding Flight 411. 

Possibly unrelated but better to take note of it anyway; neighbor stopped by yesterday. She’s a sweet old lady whose name I could never remember. She knew I was a pilot and got worried when she stopped seeing me at least once a week. She was worried I was on Flight 411, I didn’t have the heart to tell her I was. Off handedly she mentioned I looked a little off. I just shrugged it off and mentioned that I recently got a bit too much sun at the beach. That wasn’t really a lie. 

God, here I am rambling again. You’d think I’d be happy, no, elated that I’ve essentially gotten my life back. This massive, seemingly unscalable wall, just gone the next day as if it were a construction barrier. Yet I just grow more and more confused, more overwhelmed, more uncertain. How could I have survived that crash? Why can’t I remember the two whole weeks between the crash and waking up in the hospital? Who the hell is that man in the blue polo and why do I keep bumping into him? 

Why don’t I have any photos of myself anywhere?
