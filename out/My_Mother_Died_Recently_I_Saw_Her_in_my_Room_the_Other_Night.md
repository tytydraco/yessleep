*The Gambler's Fallacy is a logical error where someone assumes a particular outcome must happen based on metaphysical beliefs; Although no mathematical or physical laws dictate this.* 

It’s been about a week since my mom passed. The other night I saw her standing in my room.

None of this feels real. The other night, I saw her, standing right in front of me. Peaking around the corner of my room, near the door, my mother's face. She didn’t speak. I didn’t speak. I couldn’t even mutter words, the fear that gripped my body was so immense my chest froze trapping the air inside. I wasn’t sure why she was there. Or how. But she was. And seconds later she was gone. 

“Mom?” I asked, shy and confused.  
Her face was hard to make out in the dark, but she looked void of any emotion. She was breathing, slowly. Heavily. She sat there for a second watching me, as I lay paralyzed. Her head retracted around the corner, and there I was again, alone. A mountain of confusion towered in front of me; I immediately began questioning my own sanity. Did I really just see that? Was that even my mom? What was that *thing?* It couldn’t possibly be her; she was dead and had been for some time now. Her body stayed at the mortuary awaiting the embalming process. It was SUDEP. Sudden Unexpected Death in Epilepsy. She experienced a fatal seizure. An unfortunate happenstance that the doctors said was unlikely but can happen in rare cases. So how on earth was she here?

There was no response, but I could have sworn I heard her footsteps. They were delicate footsteps. They quietly left. I shot up right and scanned the darkness surrounding me. My room was rather small, but it felt like a safe space; a place of grounding familiarity. In this moment, however, the familiarity was ironic, uncanny, and impossible. It felt cold like death, and I had the sensation I was still being watched. I knew I needed to make my way to the door. I needed to know the truth. I hurriedly crawled off my bed, shot with adrenaline. 

As I approached, I was afraid to turn the corner, afraid of what I would find on the other side, but I needed to know if she was real or if I was seeing things. Perhaps hallucinating in an episode of sleep paralysis. There were a couple explanations I could muster in the short timeframe I had, explanations that immediately exploded into my mind, justifying the impossible situation so I didn’t lose my shit. Unfortunately, the answer was far from a simple explanation. I wasn’t hallucinating, but I had wished that I was. What I found sent a horrific wave of goosebumps down my spine. The door had been opened by someone. I lived alone.

I spent the rest of the night searching the house, looking for any other clue of a visitor. Something to explain what had occurred. My mind reeling with possibilities as I searched for anything; anything to justify how or why that had happened. However, amid the chaos of my search and the bewilderment that filled my mind, I realized that I could find nothing of the sort. There. Was. Nothing. - That could explain my dead mother returning. 

I didn’t sleep the rest of the night. I lay there awake. I was afraid of what she could be. Who she was. I began to wonder if she was still alive? It just wasn’t plausible. She had been ruled dead for several days, and if she was alive, I would have heard long before she snuck into my room… So, if she was dead, what was she doing back, and why did she disappear? I don’t believe in ghosts, although this particular situation definitely had me second guessing. I ruled this out though immediately as I believed there was a better explanation. Why would a ghost need to open a door anyhow? I saw her and I heard her. She was breathing. She wasn’t an apparition. While the supernatural isn’t in my realm of beliefs, I do come from a religious background. I do believe in heaven and hell. 

So, what are the chances she is possessed by a demon? She was religious, an altruistic woman. A regular church goer. She had made her repentance, for any small sin she had committed, and I do mean small. Why would *she* be possessed by some demonic entity from hell? She never acted sacrilegiously. Surely God wouldn’t let a demon take someone who followed in his lead and practiced his word. Surely, she had his blessing and acceptance, as she accepted him into her heart, so he would accept her into his. And in that moment as I pondered the morality of my religion, I measured the odds of which she was chosen, the outcome of her judgement. Why was she chosen, and to carry out what purpose? Why would she of all people have to endure this curse? Why would I? And at that moment I began to wonder… Perhaps it wasn’t a curse. Maybe she was sent back to me as a blessing.

Maybe my nightmare wasn’t a nightmare.  
Maybe it was a miracle.  
Maybe she was an angel.
