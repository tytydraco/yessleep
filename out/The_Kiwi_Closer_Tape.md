As his smooth, playful motions switched to jerky, gasping coughs he just lowered his head into the coughing fit and motioned me to come in as he walked back towards the tv room/kitchenette.

I caught up with him at the bar window cutout where he was standing in the kitchenette and I was still in the living room. In one hand he held a drink from which he was trying to time sips off to soothe his throat between coughs. His other hand slid towards me a cassette tape in a pretty standard looking tape case.The white cover featured some kind of ancient death sculptures or something. “I…” may have escaped my lips tho otherwise pursed from my squint of indignance.

“What? You don’t like Goth?”

“Well I… oh hey i liked the “Crow Soundtrack””

He made a big show of doing a bow and some kind of, I don’t know, curtsy?

“Ooooh, I'm sorry to have offended thee my dark lord.”

A tipsy wobble led him to steady himself and drop the mocking tone a little. “Yeah, that is a pretty sick soundtrack tho” he started to fade off on another train of thought before steadying himself once more then pointing at the tape and huffing “Joy Division, man!”

Having turned the thing around in my hands I could plainly see that it was, in fact, a Joy Division album. “Okay, well obviously” I raised up horns just above my heart in acknowledgement “Fuckin’ Joy Division!” I nodded “yeah? But like, ‘you got a Joy Division tape’ is what you’re telling me?”

“Okay, so it’s an import-” I was saying a bit flatly as he swiped the tape back from my hands and cut me off.

You better gimme a damn drink before whatever this nonsense is" I said laughing and gesturing to him and the tape and the whole situation. We continued to bullshit and catch up as he made me a drink and we made our way over to the couches to chill.

He explained that the most reliable provenance for the thing was that it had been bought originally in New Zealand where it had been made and sold a couple months before the official album release date. At some later point it had migrated to the second-hand shop on Cape Cod where Crater had come upon it while vacationing with his parents through New England for the last six weeks. That’s the kind of shit he trusted me not to bring up around most of the folks with which we’re acquainted. Of course I had to give him shit but for real it was a pretty dope find.

Now I was a decent ways into my cups by the time Crate put the tape in. It mostly seemed like a normal tape. It was groovier than I expected it to be but that was just my unfamiliarity with the material. I’m not sure how to explain the other part that didn’t seem like a normal tape. Okay let me try this: something about this tape freaked me the fuck out. Yeah that’s not untrue but for real, what I experienced still flashes thru my brain bin from time to time to this day. At first it kinda felt like when you’re just starting to settle into a nice, heavy shroom trip. It didn’t seem like things were re-organizing but that I was just noticing all of the beautiful geometry that interlaced to weave our wondrous world.

And then it wasn’t like that at all and also i wasn’t on shrooms and i wasn’t even smoking then cos i had a piss test comin’ up to keep my shit job. I didn’t feel drunk or high or anything anymore. It was like that was still happening where there was time but I was somewhere else. Not even close to any kind of hallucination or visuals I've experienced on psychedelics ever, before or to this day. I felt information. It felt like I was frozen or maybe sandboxed while data I could in no way comprehend was being transferred into me. Little glimpses and sensations I would later remember. Fucking Egypt? Like schematics and blueprints for tombs, pyramids, and machines I'd never seen. But at the time I was in stasis and the only thing I could *feel* was my “status.” *Offline.*

Pt.3 - Atrocity Exhibition

The tub was in a slight pit. Rune-laden molten metal troughs run up five points from the tub to the top of the pit aligned with the pentagram configuration of the bungalows. An impalement rod topping each with a bloodied broken corpse on. Some kind of control dongle duct-taped to their hand with a cable leading to the cab of a piece of excavation machinery. The men bled out down the feeder troughs as they had been impaled in place and smashed by their machines against cauldrons of molten metal.

Each of the five points had a cluster of speakers directed down into the bath in the pit. Taller stacks of 4x15 cabs in front of each bungalow pushing out a low-end so dense I felt it sway me back and forth, with each passing wave my body fell into immediate mourning and slumping into the space of its absence. My body resonated with this without my consent and it wasn’t just a cognitive distortion, I looked down and saw sidelong ruts about the size of the sides of my feet in the dirt.

This oppressive cacophony was intermittently pierced by guitar riffs that shone like lightning activating an eerie glow from the runes carved into the troughs leading down to the antique clawfoot tub containing my best friend. And there he was! In the still mostly empty tub.

Naked.

Howling the agony of being deep fried alive.

The unending ecstasy of whooping madness.

He warbled from state-to-state, back and forth. In between, his mouth open wide, popping his lips like a caught fish, gasping, and the sound of the “Closer” album being played through a DeWalt work-site jambox I guess in his fuckin’ head.

As I moved further towards the spectacle, in sheer terror and in awe of the complexity of the unimaginably brutal ritual to which I'd become witness; somehow, it seemed the heavy equipment operators were still alive? As they were rammed repeatedly by their machines against their draining poles, with a scream their arms would fly up and they would activate something. It would tell the machine to back up and when it got far enough it would just ram again. And again.

The sound around that pit just by the machines and their rhythmic squelching and juicing of these men were sounds I'd imagine you’d hear in a high volume seafood processing plant that makes fish sticks.

As the entirety of the nightmare-machine-made-real became more clear I could also see there was a boundary and beyond it things did seem quite different, so I began to pass thru.

For a moment I could see both worlds. Within the world that just an hour ago I would've told you I fairly understood the men were being pummeled and juiced by their machines.

But inside? Within the boundary and apparently in a completely different world the men and machines were engaged in a preternatural mating dance of…

Penetration.

Retraction.

Oiled rods.

Penetrating.

Orifices.

Spurting oil.

The men growing multiple phalluses all over their bodies trying to keep up with fucking all of their mechanical partners’ oily holes. But you’ve seen an udder on a factory farm, right?

It was at once the most horrific and the most beautiful thing I'd ever seen. There was no restraint. They were relentlessly attacking each other and somehow working in tandem to produce an ichor of blood and oil and metal and cum. That ichor was running down the troughs now glowing in the light from the runes and steadily dripping into the tub with Crater in.

Molten hot.

METALCUMBLOOD.

From inside Crater looked different as well. No fish out of water at all. He was simply sustained in position as his transformation commenced. When certain guitar chords would fly past me I would see them, strike him. An X-ray vision of my best friend whose bones were at once breaking and reforming with new mechanical systems, formations all laser imprinted with these runes. This madness started making sense to even me and I couldn't take it. It was too much.

The sound.

The world.

Divided.

The worlds merged.

I could feel my bones compressing under the weight of a realm for which I was not made. I was not supposed to be there. I wanted to help my friend but what could I do?

As I backed out from the brutiful realm I clocked a new sound in the din.

“Yeah, those are police sirens” I managed to mumble to myself. “Right‽” I responded. “Whatever the fuck this is i don’t really need to be here… I really don’t need to be here when other people find it.”

I finally ripped my eyes away from the intoxicating, sickening scene. I ran back behind Bungalow One and down to the lower landing where I had parked the Tempo. The property was set up so I could kinda see what was happening at the trailer but anyone there couldn’t really see what was happening over by the bungalows. Tho that night I don’t know how much you’d really need to see. It probably sounded like a high school football game on a small town Friday night.

Have you ever heard it from across a town otherwise silent? Homes and shops all abandoned for the tribal rush of adolescents crashing themselves together like excited spam molecules in a microwave? Well, there’s a horn section too.

Anyway, piggies get free coffee and to impress themselves upon the sophomore girls working the hot beverage stand. Of course the bastards were coming. I could see the lights starting to pierce the brush.

Pt.4 - Slip It To The Android

He relaxed back out of character while pulling off his gloves and setting them atop a heavily stacked pallet of eighty pound bags of some kind of material.

For the last several hours I had been helping him unload and stack these bags in four layers of four bags each onto pallets. We coulda stacked them twice as high if we were in a warehouse or something but we had to keep the weight and center of gravity low because these pallets were to be picked up one by one and taken the rest of the way up the somewhat steep and rugged hill to the bungalows. Leaning slightly on the skid of material in front of him, he now gazed with some intensity towards the gorgeously illuminated trees to the west. The trees almost looked proud to be accepting the day’s tired sun safely into themselves for a good rest. “Ceramic fiber."

“No shit? Y’know now I think I remember reading that somewhere, like on the last hundred fuckin’ bags of this shit I’ve pulled out of this storage container.” Yeah I was maybe prodding a little more than I usually would but this was all so strange. Except he wasn’t watching the sunset, not really. I could see him scanning the horizon, focusing, checking, and re-checking. He was looking for something specific.

A smirk started tugging at the edges of his lips as I saw the light in his still-focusing eyes soften so. “You never got to meet Grandpa did you?” he asked tho I knew he knew I never had. “Nah, missed him by a few years if I remember correctly” I answered a bit unsure of the situation but still infected by the purity of the look of joy and admiration and pride and love on Crater’s face at the remembrance of his grandfather. Still searching the treeline but now multi-tasking I guess, Crate took a deep breath in and out before continuing into his story.

“Grandpa was a, well he was a stubborn son of a bitch is what he was” he said, releasing some of the major emotional swell I could still see within him. “But, y’know only about twice as much when he was right and well maybe double that if anybody else agreed.” His face settled back into a more typical, semi-serious look as he continued. “Did you know he had this land before they run that State Road through here? But maybe. Maybe something did look different over there.

Still pointing he finally turned to me, catching my eyes and lowering his gaze with intent “that State Road right there” he said as I followed his finger to where the very last remnants of sunlight did shine through, outlining what could be a path through the dense brush on that side of the property tho it was impossible to tell from the distance.

A small internal chuckle brought back that look of pride to his face as he explained “Ever get a J.F.C. lunch in school?” Of course I had. But I'll be damned if that family didn’t basically have a goddamn bat-cave exit-on-ramp, Northbound on that State Road.

So my best friend got a weird tape and tried to turn himself into an android, now what do I do? I don’t know if he succeeded or died and I have no Idea if he is even in this dimension anymore. I guess Crate, old friend, wherever you are, may she smile always upon your path.
