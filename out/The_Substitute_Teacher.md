Class that morning started like any other day. Melissa and Andrew were sitting up front and behind them was Jessica and Sandra. I was behind Jessica.

It’s a special class for gifted kids on weekends. We usually discuss theoretical physics.

We were sitting and chatting to each other, spreading rumors and gossiping, when a teacher we’ve never seen before walked in without bending her knees. She marched to the front of the class and stood with her back to us, facing the blackboard, literally, like an inch from her face.

“Good morning, class,” she said, talking to the blackboard. 

I had tremors in my legs and arms watching her. She didn’t move a single limb. Just her head from side to side like she had music playing in her mind.

“Miss?” Sandra said, immediately regretting it afterwards.

The teacher swiveled on her heels and faced us. She had a crooked grin and didn’t say any thing. She kept her head tilted, staring over our heads at a map on the wall behind us. “Good morning, class.” Her voice raised an octave.

“Good morning, Miss.” our voices cracked as we spoke like a broken speaker. Jessica hugged her desk next to Sandra’s.

“Something’s not right,” she whispered.

The teacher walked between Melissa and Andrew and stood in front of Jessica blocking the path. I couldn’t take my eyes off of her. I was shaking and gripping the sides of my desk.

“I’m Miss Genet,” she announced, staring at the map on the wall as we stared at her boxed between the five of us.

She had a vanilla fragrance radiating off her clothing but under the scent lingered burnt polymer. It was the exact same smell as my 3D printer.

“Good morning, class. I’m Miss Genet.” Her voice distorted and slowed like a melting record still playing.

Sandra kicked Andrew’s chair. He turned around. She whispered, “she hasn’t blinked.”

Andrew faced Melissa and they stared at each other, her eyes were wide-open and she was frozen to her desk. Andrew spoke with his thumb, signaling if we should all just take off.

Melissa shook her head. She couldn’t move.

The teacher pressed forward and walked into Jessica’s desk. She backed up and tried again. Jessica slid it behind Melissa’s and the teacher passed through, kicking her legs in the air as if they were on stilts, all the way to the back of the classroom, where she stopped dead at the map.

Neither of us knew what to do. We stayed silent the whole time staring at the back of the teacher, where she remained until the bell rang.

We stood up and she turned around in one motion and headed back to the front of the class, strutting past me and Jessica as if we weren’t even there and stopped at Andrew and said, “good morning.” 

Her eyelids started blinking incredibly fast and her head began twitching.

We ran out at the same time to get Mr. Polanski, but when we returned, she was gone. 

He said, “class had been cancelled because Mrs. Farady called in sick.”

Each one of us told him there was a teacher here and she was acting super weird.

He said it was impossible. No one could have gotten into the building without him buzzing them in.
