I promise I’m not crazy, or hallucinating, and I don’t do drugs anymore. Havent for like a month so trust me I’m good.

This all kinda kicked off about a week ago. The route I take to college is a short 10-15 minute walk along a road. This road sucks under a freeway overpass about half way there. Usually the place is filled with trash, the occasional person too, poor bastards, but as long as you keep to yourself and move quick no one will bother ya, and even if they do they’ll rarely touch ya.

So I was a little more than scared when a bloodied hand shot out from beneath a dirty pile of cardboard and grabbed onto my leg.

I shouted and jumped away from the pile, nearly falling over the railing into the road. 

“Please…” a voice pleaded weakly, “don’t leave me here to die like the rest…” 

I’m not a particularly charitable person, rarely even kind, but something about the way this person spoke hurt my heart to ignore. Normally Id’ve just passed them by and let the next person or good samaritan handle it. But before I knew it I was watching myself reach for the person’s outstretched hand.

As we made contact I felt a shock course through my body, a singing stinging sensation lingered in my arm and hand as I pulled the person from beneath the cardboard.

They were in a bad way. Blood dripped from open gashes along their forehead, they wore a tattered robe covered in bloodstained gashes and slashes. Their breathing was labored and uneven. Their hair, a medley of all the colors of the rainbow, like light through a prism. 

“Holy shit,” I reflexively shouted, “you need a hospital.”

I reached into my pocket for my phone before the figure stopped me.

“No, please, I only need sustenance.” 

Despite its appearance, it’s voice was calm and gentle, a wavering lullaby carried on a gentle breeze. 

“Susitinence? What like food? Dude you need a fucking hospital! Were you hit by a car?”

The figure smiled. 

“I fell.”

“Even more of a reason to call an ambulance!” 

The figure stepped closer to me. As it did so I began to notice strange things about it.

The figure appeared to be androgynous, though I fear the word itself fails to accurately describe the utter sense of distilled humanity, as if all mankind had been melted down and reforged into a single individual. Their figure was slender, lighter than air, fragile, yet sturdy. Dark caked crimson matted down their otherwise heavenly radiance. 

“Please, my child, I need only sustenance and rest to restore myself.”

What was I doing here? I’m gonna be late for Econ, then professor’s gonna tear me a new asshole, and I’ll have to make up the quiz-

“Fuck it, want McDonalds?” I asked bluntly.

The figure smiled pleasantly in response, I took that as a “yes” and walked over. 

Much to my surprise, and the surprise of the other patrons, the figure which had introduced themself as “Ruile”, had followed me to the store.

After a tense moment with the cashier we sat down with our meals. In a moment Ruile inhaled their Double Quarter Pounder. Truthfully I have never seen anyone nor anything devour a burger so fast in my life. If it weren’t for all the blood Id’ve assumed the patrons were all just as shocked at that display too. 

“Well, good luck with your, ya know, all of this,” I said as I got up, vaguely gesturing to Ruile, “but I’m gonna go-“

Lei grabbed my arm firmly as I started to leave.

“Please, I appreciate your generosity, but I must find a place to rest.” 

I recoiled once more. 

I already fed this person and now they think they can just crash at my place?

“No, not your apartment,” Ruile replied.

“What the fuck?!” I exclaimed in shock, “how did you- my… How did you hear that?”

Lei looked nervous as they slumped down in their chair. The commotion drew even more attention than before. Not wanting to suffer another moment of this I made my way to the door. 

Having left the McDonalds and rounding the corner to my Apartment I practically screamed when Lei stood right before me.

“Please Damien, my child,” Ruile begged, “I must rest!”

I shoved my way past them, “then go back to the cardboard pile I-“ 

How did they know my name? When did I-

“Damien,” Ruile started again, their words struck my soul as they called my name, “do not be afraid.”

For a moment, a brief, infinitesimal moment, my vision went white. White save for the shadow of something before me. Four pairs of massive wings that emerged from a series of concentric rings. My eyes, my head, my ears burned as I felt a warm liquid trickle down my nose and my cheeks. My ears rung with a righteous and ferocious tone of bells that threatened to shake me apart. The air was thick with the smell of fire. 

Then it all slipped away, the world returned to how it was, Ruile still stood before me, but the air around them felt like it was burning.

“Do you understand now, Damien,” Ruile asked, hesitantly taking a step towards me.

I stumbled backwards and fell onto the pavement, nearly cracking my tail bone.

“W-what the hell? Are you an Angel?” 

“Yes, my child,” Ruile said as they lowered to me, “please allow me this.”

I reached out my hand to Ruile, but as they grabbed my hand, another slid past and into my chest. Their slender, frail, broken hand pierced my flesh. A searing pain erupted and radiated out from my heart as their fingers dug deeper into the bone and muscle and sinew. My breath was taken from me as I greedily and desperately gulped down air that I felt escaping and searing my exposed flesh. My voice had been robbed from me as my throat became choked by a righteous heat that began to envelop my body. With each second more and more of Ruile entered my body, until all that remained was a small tuft of bloodied and broken feathers on the ground between my legs. 

My lungs finally took in air as I hyper ventilated. My hands scanned my body for damage but found only scars where Lei had dug into me. 

“W-where-“ I started.

In my mind I heard a distant echo that grew and grew into a powerful chorus chanting fury and temperance. 

“I am here, my child. I am inside you.” 

Ruile’s words came from all directions, past, present, and future. 

“Get the fuck out! I didn’t give you permission!” 

A bubble of joy rose up from within the depths of my soul and burst into a mix of radiant laughter and sour spite. 

“I promise to repay the charity you have shown me, Damien, for you shall have my favor.” 

I wasn’t much of a religious type, went to Sunday school maybe once, but when the fuck did Angels posses people?

“It is done only in times of necessity,” Ruile answered. 

Right, you can read my mind.

“It is but one of many things we see.” 

Ruile’s voice grew less intense as we spoke. Apparently there is an adjustment period to this sort of thing. The first few days were strange. Not that having an Angel forcibly posses you isn’t strange, but other things that happened to coincide with this were. 

Firstly, my econ professor not only gave me a pass on the exam, which was extremely uncharacteristic, he gave me a C for a test I never even took. Which I’m pretty sure is like, illegal or something, not that I’m complaining. 

Then it was this one loud asshole in my history class. He’d always interrupt class with the dumbest shit you’d ever hear come from someone’s mouth. Like “didn’t they fake the moon landing?” Or “I heard JFK was killed by the Lizard Cult” among other intellectual topics. Most of the class just soldiered through it, but it just so happened that the individual in question, let’s call him Ray, had a serious incident while biking out on someone’s property. Apparently people string clothes lines or wires up to deter (or decapitate) bikers and such who illegally access their property. I won’t judge any of that because I frankly don’t give a shit, but I will say I feel a bit bad about how relieved I was to hear that Ray had ran into one. He survived but the damage to his throat was pretty bad, they say he lost all ability to speak. 

Again, I’m no saint, but I did feel a bit guilty at how happy I was to never hear his dumb ass speak again. 

And the guilt only got worse after yesterday. The whole reason I’m even writing this in the first place.

My walk to school is fairly short as you already know, what I neglected to mention was that I do have to cross a signal-less crosswalk across a freeway onramp on the way. It’s usually tame at the times I go, but rarely I’ll run into some self important asshole who would rather almost run someone over instead of waiting the roughly five seconds it took to cross the intersection. 

And every time it happened I’d sarcastically, and sometimes seriously, think to myself ‘God I hope this guy’s car explodes or something.’ And behold, a guy in some BMW comes flying down the road and nearly flattens me as I’m trying to cross. By reflex at this point I mumble my usual ‘fuck you’ and ‘hope you die’ spiel. 

Moments later an explosion rocks the area. Fragments of car and god-knows-what fly past me. My eyes are drawn to the heat, and the cloud of smoke rising from the car that had nearly hit me not even five seconds prior. It hadn’t even fully made it to the top of the onramp. Right on the cusp it sat, now nothing more than a mangled, flaming wreck. 

All I could do was stand there and watch. I didn’t move a muscle. 

Not when the firefighters came. Not when EMS arrived and walked, not ran, walked to the wreck. Not when the police went around asking for witnesses. And certainly not when the paramedics wheeled away something on a gurney covered in a soot-stained white sheet. 

So now I sit here. Typing this out, not sure what it would even accomplish. Maybe this is confessional, but these aren’t my sins are they? Maybe I’m seeking some type of absolution from all of this, maybe one of you even knows what the hell is going on. I mean I don’t expect to find a support group for people possessed by angels but like what the fuck else am I supposed to do? 

I can’t keep living like this. It was fine when it was just convenient stuff like my test scores and refilling my shampoo bottle or milk. But now someone has died. Ok sure there’s nothing concrete but I mean, it’s too big of a coincidence for me to just ignore. It’s like I felt myself die a little, like I did that. I killed someone with just a thought. It hurts. It fucking hurts. 

I’m scared. Why me? Oh God, why me?
