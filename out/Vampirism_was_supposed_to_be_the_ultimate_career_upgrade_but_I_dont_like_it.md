Hi, I am writing here because I don't know where else to ask. I took the vampirism upgrade last year with a big company (I don't know if I can name it) and I don't know if I made the right choice.

I mean, I am pretty okay with the vampire part. It is cool to be able to lift so much weight, and I can now jump very high. My acne scars are gone too, and I think red eyes are badass. But the working conditions suck.

The dorms are small and smelly. I know I don't need windows or a mattress, but this feels more like a storage closet than a room. My cat disappeared too, and I am sure it was that asshole Diego that lives next door. I know we can't feed from animals and that is disgusting to even try, but dude is such a creep and I am sure it was him.

My schedule is way too heavy. Night shifts are fine, but day shifts are a nightmare. The day suits are old and full of holes, so we keep getting stung by the light. We work eighteen hours a day. I think it is a lot. They say that it is because we are immortal, so our time is worth less. We do get a full day to rest once a month, but I still don't think it's fair. How can I enjoy my wage if I am always working?

I tried to join the Union but the humans were very hostile. They are pissed because we get paid more than them, and because we crossed the picket line during the last big strike. They called us scabs and leeches, and told us we sold our souls to the capital. They don't understand. We can't risk getting fired. It is a long and ugly process, that usually ends with termination of existence.

Diego says we should make our own Union. But like, better, because vampires are better anyway.

I am not sure what to think.

It is difficult to think.

There is this voice, always in my mind. I know it is my Sire's. I only met her once, when I went through the procedure. There were so many of us, tied to the beds. They used machines to drain us, and when she walked down the line to feed us a drop of her blood she was speaking on the phone. I hear that voice in my head all the time. She is happy when I meet my target. Angry when I am don't. This does not happen often -- I am a good worker, I work hard for my Sire. I like to work hard for my Sire.

Still, the food is just so bad. We do get dental coverage, but what's the point if we don't get to use our teeth? They don't let us close to humans at all. Biting in the wild is prohibited and if there are humans working on our floor we have to wear muzzles. To eat we have to wait for our scheduled appointment and go to our assigned Feeding Facility. There they strap us to a chair and shove a hose down our throats. It hurts, it always hurts, even when the blood starts flowing.

A nurse told me that humans are force fed through the nose but for vampires they go through the mouth. She says that is okay, because we will heal. I wanted to tell her that we do, we heal, but it is slow and painful. But I had a tube down my throat and could not speak.

Last Friday they ran out of helmets and Diego had to work without one. He fell from the third floor's scaffolding and cracked his skull on the conveyor belt. His brain was splatted all over the floor. They made me clean it. He healed, of course, but it was a pain because he screamed for days and he lives next door. I did not feel sorry for him because he got the weekend off and I think he ate my cat.

Anyway, I really don't like this. Is there a way to break the contract? I know I am not human anymore and that I don't have human rights, but I was turned less than a year ago, so maybe some still apply. 

I found a number for an interspecies lawyer in the pocket of my uniform. I think it used to be someone else's uniform. I still called the number, because I was sad and angry, and really hungry too. I don't think they are feeding us enough. I will meet him next week. I hope that at least he will help me sue Diego. I think he ate my cat.
