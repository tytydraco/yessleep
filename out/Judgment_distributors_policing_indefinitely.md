My neighbour encountered them first, he went out one night and sped, he went 2 whole miles over the speed limit. I saw them peeking at him, and I did nothing for It wasn’t me.

He went about his days feeling the weight of the world upon his shoulders. Every corner he turned they would be ahead scuttling and peaking round at him, constantly undermining and whispering hushed insults.

About a month after he left his home and got in his car. He had tried and tried with the police pleading with them to help, he told me through a letter, which prior to delivery had been heavily monitored and altered, what was left in the letter read:

To Mr Prouss

I implore you to take head, I assume you know the matter on which I’m discussing. I wish to make crystal clear that the police will serve of no use against my personally assigned judgement distributors.

I have found a way to defuse their pestering -

The rest of the letter was censored and had a sticky note attached with a frown face drawn crudely atop.

I did nothing still. The news of my neighbours disappearance was expected.

I continued painting and keeping my head down, believing sincerely that there was no point attempting to make a fuss about the “judgment distributors”. I kept furthering my art painting and painting away my time, dwindling my money down on canvases.

Then one night I became affected, I had been out trying to get some food and water to last me the week, I arrived at the shop and was knocked over by a man in a scarcely similar outfit to mine, he was a thief and I was tried guilty of his crimes, a judgement camera in the area found it easier to try me instead of searching for that thief.

Then the judgement distributors set upon me, I will now describe to you my experience, to show you why I’m going to get myself imprisoned.

That morning, I woke up the chirp of birds were gone, replaced by the chuckling of middle aged men ogling me. Obviously looking down on my lie in.  
I went downstairs made my breakfast and sat at my easel. I could hear more yammering and one distinct  judge said in a monotone voice, that ai would steal my position, The others murmured in agreement.

The day went on them pressing their flabby white faces and backwards rhetoric, whilst touting that I was despicable. It was then I learned they wanted you to talk back, I had seen it happen to my neighbour but thought it was because of some grave offence. They let out a piercing sound, making breathing hard to maintain, promising to stop if I were to step outside.

You have to give in. Once you are out they group around you seeming to form together into a wall encircling you tightly. I only heard before, now I know what is inside. Everywhere I looked was propaganda, hideous travesties and massive injustices. They did it to break me down to realise the futility of resisting.

I have long lived reclusive, afeared of change. Now I will continue to live a changed man, in a cell of my own choosing.
