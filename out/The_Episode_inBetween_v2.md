The story they read was about a man whose sister calls the landline in their mother's house. She had been dead four years, and the phone was disconnected in every way, not even power to it. Then breathing, slow and close, like someone with the phone against her ear. Then a sound that wasn't quite a word. He, he... hello. Like the word had to be put together before it could be said. By the middle she had the whole word, and she said it the way the living say it, like it might be answered. Hello... hello? And sometimes, after the hellos, his name, once, and then she would wait. He would stand in his mother's kitchen holding a dead phone, listening to his dead sister learn how to reach him, and every night he had to decide whether to say something back. He never did. He was afraid that if he answered her properly, she would ask him something. He was afraid of what he'd say if she asked.

I don't remember most of what the hosts said after that. I remember them stopping. The joking went out of the episode the way it goes out of a room, and nobody announced it.
