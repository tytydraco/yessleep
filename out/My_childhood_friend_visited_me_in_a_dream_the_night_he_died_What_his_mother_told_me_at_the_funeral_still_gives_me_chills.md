This happened to me in my mid-20s. I’ve told only a few people, and every time I get to the end, I get goosebumps.

Jimmy and I grew up together from the time we were basically infants. Our mothers were best friends, so he was always at my house, or I was at his. One of my clearest memories is the two of us playing basketball in my kitchen with one of those little plastic hoops that hang over the door. He was always more like family to me than a friend.

Around 15, we started drifting apart. Jimmy got into drugs and gangs, and I wanted no part of that. I tried to stay away from trouble, but he always went straight towards it.

I never stopped thinking of him as my friend, though. I knew who he really was. He wasn’t a bad person. He just got caught up in bad situations since we lived in a pretty rough neighborhood.

Years later, when I was working as a cashier at a supermarket in our town, I helped him avoid getting arrested. The front of the store had these big windows that looked straight out onto the main street, so I always had a clear view of the entire street. I noticed police cars rolling by, then more of them, pulling over and gathering on the street. The cops got out and started looking around. 

I turned to the cashier next to me and said, “I think they’re looking for somebody.”

A few seconds later, Jimmy came running down the sidewalk right past our windows.

He looked in. We made eye contact and he came inside, walked up to my register, and said, “Yo. You gotta hide me. The cops are looking for me.”

I didn’t think twice. I hid him behind my register. I never said a word to the police and they didn’t catch him. I never even asked him what he did.

Another time, he got out of jail and asked me to drive him back there to pick up the belongings they’d held on to. So I drove him. I never asked questions and I never judged him. He never did anything wrong to me, and to me he was still the kid who I played basketball with in my kitchen.

Years went by, with random encounters like this with Jimmy.

One night, I had a crazy dream.

Jimmy walked into the room where I was sitting. I asked him, “Where have you been? It’s been a long time since I’ve seen you.” He didn’t respond to that. He only smiled and said two words: “Thank you.”

Then the dream ended.

The weird thing about him in the dream was that his eyes weren’t looking at me. They were rolling around in his head, moving in every direction, like they weren’t attached to anything to keep them in place.

When I woke up, I lay there thinking *that it was a really strange dream*, but I thought nothing else of it.

I got up and I walked to the kitchen. My mother was on the phone, crying hysterically. When she hung up, I asked her what was wrong.

She said it was Tara, Jimmy’s mom. Jimmy had shot himself the night before. He was dead.

To this day, nobody knows for sure whether it was an accident or something he meant to do. There was an investigation, but we never got a clear answer. In my heart, I believe it was an accident. Jimmy was always joking around and always doing reckless things.

At the funeral, I went up to his mom, Tara, to tell her how sorry I was and while we were talking, she told me something she’d decided to do. Jimmy had been an organ donor and she had donated his eyes.

I’ve thought about that dream a ton of times since. I believe Jimmy came to see me that night, right after he passed, to say thank you for never giving up on him.

And I believe he showed me, in the only way he could, that it was really him there in that dream.
