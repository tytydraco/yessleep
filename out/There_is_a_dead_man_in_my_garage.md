There is a dead man in my garage. He is simply lying there and nobody besides me acknowledges it. 

It all started a week ago. There is a smaller refrigerator in my garage and sometimes my parents leave groceries there, I still live with my parents, and one night I went there just to see if they bought soda or juice, anything tasty to drink, it was a hot night.

But then, I turned the lights on and there was a man lying there, chest-to-floor next to the other side of the car, I could only see him because the door is a bit higher than the rest of the garage, a few steps. I froze, honestly, like, what the hell? So I just stood there for a couple of seconds, fearful, before slowly backing down and locking the door. My father was watching TV in the living room, so I called him, trying to remain calm.

“Dad, there is someone in the garage…” He looked at me in disbelief, but seeing my face probably changed his mind since confusion quickly became concern, however, he wanted to see it with his own eyes before calling the police. We unlocked the door, the lights still on and my father holding a broomstick.

“Who's there?!” He shouted, looking over the car. 

“There.” I pointed and he went, before resting the broomstick on the floor and started to laugh.

“Oh yeah, an invader” He pointed to a ripped cardboard box with mirrors inside, pointing at the door. 

I laughed out of worriness and horror as my father stood right by the corpse. 

The box of mirrors was there because a cousin of mine is building his own house now and needed a place to store decorations he bought in a sales shopping spree. That isn't important. I, I don't know, I couldn't sleep, how could I? He was there, before and now. I went there again, later, every light from my bedroom to the garage lit and me holding the same broomstick. I poked the corpse, it touched it, I felt contact, he wasn't a ghost or apparition.

It's a brown haired man wearing a long sleeved green shirt, generic blue jeans and black sneakers. Truly a random guy, he hasn't even started decomposing yet. However, I couldn't look at his face, I just couldn't, I feel so much disgust and dread, it's so uncomfortable, I still can't look at his face, I fear that thing, it's just bizarre, BIZARRE. Fuck, I don't know what to do.

It looks like he fell, as if he was using a ladder to grab something from a higher shelf, then just fell down face first and simply remained there, between the wall and the car.

Everyday my parents go to work, go do groceries, walk in and out of the garage, this whole week and not a single word, not a glance, not even looking like they are hiding something. It's been a week and he doesn't rot, he doesn't smell, he doesn't move, he doesn't try to kill me, he is there, I moved his arms a little with a stick and he still is in the same position I left. I don't want to touch him with my bare hands, I don't want to turn him over, I WON'T look at his face, at “his” face, at whatever the hell this thing is. I'm scared, I fear he will be smiling, or crying, or not have a face at all. I fear that it will be a normal expressionless face of a dead man I never met.

I don't know what to do. One time my brother simply ran him over with his bike and didn't even react to the bump.

“Hey, I'm back”, he said, as if there was nothing there, as if he just rode over a smooth floor, as if he didn't almost fell.

I don't know what to do, it's been 7 days of me waiting for him to stand up, to hunt us down, but nothing happens. I can't sleep with my door unlocked anymore, without the lights being on, for more than a few hours…

There is a thing that looks like a dead man in my garage and I'm the only one acknowledging it.
