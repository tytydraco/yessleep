A few years ago I was strapped for cash and between jobs, looking for ways to pay the bills. Some odd jobs paid well but what I eventually settled on was doing dog walking through an app. It was one particular walk request that really made me stop and buckle down to find a more stable job.

One evening, a request came in on the app for a walk in a neighborhood a little ways away from me. It was pretty standard:

“Hi! My name is Greg. We have been moving houses currently and are going to be away for the night. Could you come over this evening around 8-8:30 and let our dog out? He’s a very friendly mutt named Banjo! He’s older and likes to sleep upstairs in the master bedroom closet, so if you could help him down the stairs and then let him in the backyard that’s all we need!”

I replied back that I’d be happy to help and they followed up with one final message.

“Great! Thank you! There is a spare key under the front door mat! Thanks again!”

8’oclock rolled around and I pulled into the driveway of the house, it looked like a normal house in a normal neighborhood, if not a little on the older side. As I started to walk up to the front door I noticed that first floor, front facing windows had their blinds and curtains open and the lights were on. I could see right into what looked like the living room of the house. I didn’t want to look suspicious to any of the neighbors so I didn’t stop and stare inside and kept moving. But from the quick glance I took, I noticed a couch with a coffee table and a television all brightly lit.

I got to the front door and sure enough, there was a welcome mat with a big paw print on it and underneath the mat was a spare key. I unlocked the door and went inside the house. As I entered, I noticed light spilling from the living room to my right and it illuminated a hallway in front of me and a stairwell upstairs to my left. I looked to my left and saw a bank of light switches on the wall. I flicked the first one, nothing happened, I figured it was a switch to an outdoor light or something so I tried the other one, still nothing. I tried all 4 switches on the wall and nothing happened, no lights turned on, the only light was coming from the living room.

I wandered over to the living room to see if I could quickly figure out anything about the lights in there and I was met with a mildly strange sight. The living room wasn’t illuminated by any overhead lighting from the house or lamps, there was one of those LED construction lights on a stand in the corner of the room shining light at the living room area. It was one of the battery powered ones so it didn’t need to be plugged into the wall.

The furniture was also a bit odd once I took a longer look at it all. The tv was one of those old tube tvs and the screen was missing entirely. The couch and coffee table both had tags on them that seemed to be from a local rental furniture store. I thought back to the owner’s message, they said they were in the process of moving. I had assumed that meant they were moving out but maybe this was the house they were moving into and they hadn’t really set anything up yet?

Leaving their dog at the new house that doesn’t even have power or furniture yet is a pretty shitty thing to do, but a job is a job so I wanted to let the dog out and get out of here as fast as possible. So I left the living room and headed towards the stairs back near the entryway of the house.

I stared up the stairs and uselessly flicked the light switches again hoping the lights would work this time. I stood at the bottom of the stairs not wanting to wander up without any lights on.

“Banjo!” I called out up the stairs, “Come here boy! I’m here to let you go potty!”

I waited for a few seconds straining my ears to try and hear the shuffling of a dog coming to the top of the stairs but there was no movement. Deep down I knew it wouldn’t be that easy, so I gritted my teeth and turned on my phone flashlight and started to go upstairs. I got to the top of the stairs and kicked myself, I should go and grab the battery operated light in living room! Like a cruel joke, the moment I thought of that, the light went out downstairs. The battery must have run out of power. Still, I started getting a bit uneasy, I decided to get Banjo outside and get out as fast as possible.

I looked around with my phone’s flashlight, trying to find the master bedroom where Banjo should be hanging out. The upstairs looked even more empty than the downstairs. It also looked a bit more dilapidated, almost like it hadn’t really been up-kept. There were a few holes in the walls and sometimes a spider web. This confirmed my suspicion that they were moving into this house. They must be flipping it.

After a few seconds of looking around, I found what I figured was the master bedroom. I stepped inside and just like everywhere else in the house, it was empty. I called out for Banjo again and there still wasn’t a response. At that point I started to feel a pit of uneasiness grow and stay in my stomach. My main worry was that Banjo was dead, the owners had mentioned that he was an older dog. That plus the fact that he didn’t seem to be responding at all made me worry he was dead or hurt. But at that time I need the money and I did care about the dogs so I at least wanted to make sure Banjo was okay.

Sweeping my light around the room, I eventually noticed the closet in the back corner of the room. The door was open but since the whole house was dark it was like a black rectangle in the wall. From the way the house was laid out, I guessed that it was a closet that went back at least 10 feet. I shone my flashlight into the closet but the light wouldn’t penetrate all the way back. I moved slowly closer trying to see if I could see Banjo in the closet. Something moved towards the back of the closet, but it was hard to see. Regardless, the movement made me jump a bit. Even though I was expecting to see something, the whole atmosphere still kept me on edge.

“Banjo…” I called out again. “Hey buddy, I’m here to let you go potty. Want to come out?”.

I was talking out loud as I inched closer. I got close enough to illuminate the whole closet and I finally saw Banjo. He was a big dog and had his head down and away from me. Something about his body looked off, he was a bit too lumpy in some places, but even though I was getting more uneasy by the second, I still tired to rationalize it and assumed it was due to him being an older dog.

“Banjo, hey buddy. Come on let’s go outside. You want to go outside?” I said in a more placating tone.

Even then, Banjo still wouldn’t come out, he just lay there. I resigned myself to going into the closet itself to check on Banjo and started slowly moving into the closet with my phone flashlight shining inside.

“Okay buddy,” I said, “I’m coming inside to check on you. It’s all okay.”

I kept going inside the closet very slowly so I didn’t startled the dog and he attacked me. As I got closer I started hearing what sounded like some low snuffling noises, like the kind a dog makes when it’s breathing in, almost like a pug. Because I was so on edge, I thought it was growling at first so I stopped for a second, thinking he may have been mad at me. Stopping short seemed to alert him to my presence and finally moved to turn his head to look at me.

Banjo looked odd, like a dog but uncanny. My brain had a hard time registering it as a dog. It was dog-like for sure but something was very off. I could hear the heavy snuffing/breathing sound again but it sounded odd, almost muted. I panned the flashlight up and got a quick closer look and I finally noticed his eyes. Banjo’s eyes looked sunken and intelligent, like he was thinking and observing me

I stood there trying to make sense of what I was seeing, did Banjo look so odd because he was so old or hurt? A few more seconds ticked by while my brain was trying to process and then I heard something. Something low and quiet. It was a whisper, it came from Banjo.

“Pet me”

My blood ran completely cold. I swear I heard that come from the mass of fur that was Banjo. I couldn’t understand what was going on, did I hallucinate the dog talking to me? A million thoughts were running through my head but then like a sledgehammer hitting my brain, everything hit me all at once in that moment. The sunken and intelligent eyes, the muffled breathing, and the messed up face. This was a man in a dog costume, this was a human wearing a realistic dog mask.

I froze, my thoughts working overtime to overcome the fear that was coursing through my body. All I could think to do in that moment was to start backing out of the closet. But as I started to do that, “Banjo” let out a “bark”. It was such an uncanny noise because it was obviously a human trying to imitate a dog’s bark. I froze again, utterly terrified. “Banjo” looked at me and in a louder and deep human voice said,

“Come pet me.”

I didn’t know what to say or do, I was trying to tell myself to move and run but the whole situation was so uncanny and terrifying that my legs wouldn’t move. It wasn’t until “Banjo” started moving and trying to stand up on two legs that my own legs finally started to respond to the terror in my brain.

I turned and started to run away. As I tore out of the bedroom and towards the stairs I heard a thumping of legs running behind me along with the awful fake barking noises of “Banjo”. I made it to the stairs and frantically made my way down them, it was a miracle I didn’t immediately trip and fall and break my neck with the speed I was running.

My phone was still in my hand and the light from its flashlight was swinging wildly around in sync with the pumping of my arms as I sprinted down the stairs. I was almost to the bottom step but then I made a horrible mistake. In my fear I looked back over my shoulder to see if “Banjo” was following me and in doing so my foot missed the final step on the staircase. I stumbled and my phone went flying forward, cracking against the front door causing the flashlight to go out.

I watched this all happen in slow motion as my stumble turned into a trip and then into a fall. I fell and rolled into the entryway of the house into complete darkness. I knew I had to get up and find the front door and I knew it should be somewhere right in front of me but before I could make that happen I heard something. Up at the top of the stairs all I could hear was a thump of something coming to a stop and then frantic sniffing, like a dog trying to catch a scent.

Terror was trying to override my thoughts but I knew I had to get out. To my left a small rectangle of light appeared. It was my phone, I must have gotten a notification. I lunged for it and the pulled myself up quickly. Using the light of my phone again I found the front door and started to open it. As I started to run outside I took one last desperate look over my shoulder. “Banjo” was rushing down the stairs on all fours coming right for me. I sprinted even faster and jumped into my car. As I turned the car on and reversed dangerously out of the driveway I caught one last glimpse of “Banjo” standing fully upright on two legs in the front door way, just staring as I got away.

I raced home as fast as possible and when I got home I locked all of my doors and closed all my curtains and sat there in shock. I don’t think I moved from my spot the entire night. It wasn’t until that the sun started to come up that next morning that I checked my phone. I had one notification from my dog walking app, it was a review.

“1/5 Stars. Didn’t stay for the whole walk and left with my house key.”  
\-Banjo
