If you lived in or vacationed at a city named Spitslick in Texas, then you'd know there is only one true place to get breakfast or get any sweet pastries. 

  
Lickin' Ladies Bakery. I loved the place. Whether it'd be donuts, croissants, cakes, even pancakes, they had it all. EVERYONE went there, it was hard to find a time where it wasn't packed. The employees were nice, even if they didn't give out their names or wear name tags, they fed the homeless, overall it was a welcoming environment for all.

  
Though, my opinion changed drastically today, as i write this from my motel room. The bakery had been closed down for two weeks at this point, so you could consider it abandoned. A little fact about me, i love urban exploring, so naturally, i had to explore a bit. 

  
At 6 o clock, when the sun was shading, i had rode my bike from my house to the closed down bakery. The sign, advertising its name, was already rusty and falling apart, really made it feel grimmer than it was. 

  
In the back was the employee's entrance/exit, as they were almost never seen leaving through the front door. Oddly enough, that door was unlocked, as if it were open just for me, so that definitely made it easier to leave no trace. 

  
I walked in and the first thing i was met with was the storage area, where they kept the supplies. It looked like they packed up in a hurry, as there was dough on the floor, trash was scattered everywhere, and it overall just looked like a fucking mess. Surprisingly, on one of the storage racks were donuts, wrapped in plastic and preserved. I just had to tear the plastic wrap open and eat some. 

  
I had bit into one of the donuts, but after a few bites i bit into a chunk of something. I'd be damned if i was gonna spit it out, it still tasted good AND police could find me off of my DNA or something like that, so i swallowed it and kept exploring. To the right and through a hallway, i found a doorway that looked like it went downwards, which was also unlocked. 

  
You should know this by now, obviously, i opened the door and went downstairs. 

  
As i walked down the steps, i heard the crackling and pops of candles somewhere in that basement. When i fully entered the basement, i smelt the vile scent of shit, piss, and rot. How they kept this from the FDA was a shock to me. 

  
In front of the stairway was a brick wall, and a door on the right, which wasn't just unlocked, but wide open. 

  
I peered my head inside, god, why did i peer my head inside? 

  
It was horrid, candles all over the floor, symbols painted with what looked like either dark red paint or shit, was art the clown down here? The worse part was the bodies. There were bodies everywhere, missing toes and fingers. I didn't have to put two and two together, my body did it for me, and i hurled right there. In the vomit, was a thumb. I left as fast as the bile left my body, gunning it outside and back to my bike. 

Someone had to have followed me home, as only a few minutes after i came home and collapsed on my bed, i heard the mail slot on my door open and shut. That's why I'm writing this from a motel room, as in front of my front door, was the same thumb that i had just threw up. 

  
If you live in a city named Spitslick in Texas, and you went to Lickin' Ladies Bakery, you need to leave, now.
