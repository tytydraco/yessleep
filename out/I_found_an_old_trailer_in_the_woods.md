I lie in bed, unable to fall asleep, the events of today replaying in my head on a loop. No matter how hard I tried, all I could see in my mind was that book. The passages, the drawings, what did they mean? Cryptic symbols flash in my head as I toss and turn, aching pains setting in across my entire body as I break out in a cold sweat. I feel panic setting in as I reach out to get out of bed, the muscles in my arms tearing as I try. 

My mouth opens to yell, but only a silent, strangled breath escapes. As the pain crescendos, I feel myself being launched out of bed and onto my feet. I fall to my knees for a moment, but once I compose myself, I realize that the pain has already disappeared, with not a single scratch or bruise on my body. I stand there in disbelief, trying to understand what just happened to me, but suddenly, I feel an urge overcome my mind, something I had never felt before. 

I feel compelled to leave my house, but as I reach the front door, I realize that I’m still naked and stop myself. I feel a strange kind of resistance as I go back to my room to put on clothes, almost like a gut feeling that I’m doing something wrong. 

I don’t understand why I need to go outside so badly, but once I do so, the bad feeling subsides. It was still nighttime, but I had no idea what time it was. I stand around for a while before I feel another draw, this time calling me down the street. I obey without question, wandering down the same street I had walked a thousand times, yet this time, everything felt unfamiliar, unsettling in some way. 

I found the eerie glow of the streetlights particularly off-putting, as if something from above was looking down on me, judging me. Soon, I found myself at the edge of town, and still I pressed on, abandoning the place I once found comfort in. After this point, all that awaited me were miles of highway, forest, and trailer homes. The farther I got from town, the stronger my urges grew, a primal feeling welling up from the depths of a soul that was no longer mine. 

Soon, what was once a vague pull on my being became a fierce drag, a magnetic attraction leading me in a very specific direction, off the main road and into the pitch darkness of the forest. I march on, and despite not being able to see anything around me, save for the vague outlines of trees and the pale shine of the moon, I do not stumble or falter in my path. 

Soon, a faint glimmer of light pierces through the trees, and as I approach it, I recognize its source- an old trailer home. The hum of its outdoor generator fills my ears, almost deafening me as I walk up to it. An old, beaten-down truck sits off to the side of the trailer, rusty and neglected, with its tires partly sunk into the earth beneath it. 

The trailer itself was in an equal state of disarray, with boards covering the windows and vegetation creeping up on all sides. I quickly come to realize that this trailer is where I’ve been drawn to, as the feelings exponentially grow when I cautiously reach out to open the front door. 

It’s locked, but I am unable to turn back, so I try again and again, firm knocking soon becoming furious pounding as I desperately try to get whoever’s inside to come to the door. In that moment, the primal instincts within me transform into a kind of unbridled rage I had never experienced in my life, and in one strike, I break the door into pieces. I only take a single step inside the trailer before all of my senses are assaulted at once.   

The entire interior was a complete sty, with wretched smells permeating the air. In that moment, I felt nothing but disgust and contempt for whatever subhuman filth lived here. I feel footsteps in the rotting floorboards approaching me, and I look up to see a door open, and from behind it appears a disheveled man. He could hardly stand up straight, nor could he walk properly. 

Once he and I locked eyes, I could see a wave of fear wash over him as he realized what was happening. Without saying anything, the man goes to reach for something in haste, and once again, I feel myself launched towards him. I find my hands clamping down on the man’s shirt before effortlessly tossing him into the opposite wall of the trailer. 

My mind is filled with a turbulent mixture of confusion, rage, and determination, and in that moment, I could not tell how I truly felt, only that I wanted this man dead. For why, I do not know, but I rushed over to him before he could get back up, and I sent a fist crashing down into his back. 

I feel bones crack from underneath my blow, and it only serves to excite me further. I beat the man savagely, the sight of his blood and the sound of his pained screams becoming my entire world as I think of nothing but inflicting pain on my victim. I take hold of his right arm and snap it backwards like a twig before picking him up and throwing him down on his back, allowing me to attack his front side. I stomp down on his left arm with enough force to break it and the floorboard beneath it, the splintered bone now one with the floor. 

With a barrage of strikes, I destroyed his pathetic, disgusting face, leaving him choking on his teeth and blood. With one hand I grab hold of his skull, plunging my fingers into his eyes and my thumb pressed against the roof of his mouth, With my other hand, I press against his chest, and with a single yank, I break his maxilla from his skull, the skin of his face tearing like paper as I finally put an end to his misery. 

I paused for a moment to stand up and tower over my defeated foe, whose movements were now reduced to feeble twitches. My arms were covered in blood up past my elbows, and I could feel it coagulate on my face. 

The feeling returns, drawing me back outside. There, next to the generator, lay a can of gasoline about a quarter full. Without a second thought, I take the can inside and begin to douse the interior and everything within. I pull a lighter out of my pocket and light it, tossing it away to set the trash alight as I walk out of the trailer. 

I feel the urge to turn back and look at what I’ve done, but as I look behind me, a massive flame blooms out of the door and engulfs me for a second, searing my entire body and catching my shirt on fire. I instinctively tear my shirt off, but after I do so, I feel something strange on my chest. I hesitantly look down, only to see that I’ve been branded. Instantly, I recognize the symbol- the same one I had seen in my dreams, the one from the book. 

For the first time in hours, my mind was completely clear. No urges, nothing calling me anywhere, just myself alone with what I’ve done. Why did I kill that man? What did he do to me? He was just a nobody. A worthless nobody, just trailer trash. Nobody will miss him. The world is better off without people like that. The world is better off without people. 

The fire raged on in front of me, its beauty and warmth sheltering me from the cold night. The sound of leaves rustling from behind startles me, and I spin around to see a person emerging from the darkness. As they approached, I soon recognized it was Richard. He too bore a symbol on his chest, though his looked like it was carved with a knife.  

“You too?” 

“It happened to all of us.” 

“Everyone who read the book?” 

“Yes, David. We’ve all been blessed.” 

Behind him, more and more people appear in the light of the flame, all marked with symbols, burned, cut, bruised into their chests. Before I knew it, our entire class was here, standing with me around the burning trailer, which had started to collapse. 

With the rest of us here, I could sense my mind changing, the way I think and feel, warping into something different, something new. Something beautiful, powerful. I could hear their thoughts and sense their feelings, and I knew they could do the same for me. 

In that moment, we felt like God.
