[Part 1](https://www.reddit.com/r/nosleep/comments/1xmtct8/ive_taken_the_same_train_for_years_yesterday/)

I want to tell you what happened today. I’ll do it, even though some of you won’t believe me.

Most of you have been very supportive so far, and for that I thank you. The others telling me I’m being weird about a friendly old man on the train…do you have any idea what it’s like having someone continually interrupt your rhythm? It throws off your entire day when you start it with things going wrong.

I couldn’t sleep last night. Not for a lack of trying, but the questions swirling in my head about the Grey Man kept the peace away.

Who was he? Why did he start talking to me? How did he know about my wife? I needed answers.

I came into the train today completely out of my element. I spent too much time last night practicing what I was going to say to the Grey Man, how I was going to confront him and get him to leave me alone.

And that’s exactly what I did.

I stomped into the train and didn’t bother counting my steps or turns as I barreled towards my spot. Sure enough, he was already there.

I slammed into my seat hard enough that it flexed a bit. The impact hurt my back, but I didn’t care. I turned to the Grey Man immediately. He was already looking at me. I made eye contact and started talking.

“Sir,” I said. It’s always good to start formally so they know you’re serious. I straightened up as I continued.

“I don’t know who the hell you are, or how you know about her, but you need to stop talking to me. I just want to get to work. If you don’t stop, I’m getting security.”

He stayed perfectly still while I talked. Though he maintained eye contact the entire time, he seemed to look deeper into my eyes after I finished. He sighed and his shoulders slumped.

“*I apologize, I didn’t mean to disrupt your trip. Do you still think about her?”*

I paused with my mouth wide open as the memories flooded my mind.

Of course I think about her. Less frequently, but often enough.

The way she waited at the door for my car to pull out of sight.

The way she always asked if I packed my lunch.

The way she reminded me to take the indoor path from the train station when it was too cold out.

That was before I started taking the 6:12 train. I know there was a reason for me doing it. I just can’t remember what it was. Only that it happened after she started treatment.

After that, she couldn’t get up early enough to see me.

I snapped back to the moment. My hand tightly gripped the keychain in my pocket.

“You didn’t answer my question,” I said. He stayed steady.

“*You should get off soon.”*

“What the hell do you mean? Answer my question. Get off where?”

“*Anywhere. Just make sure you still can.”*

In my anger, I didn’t realize my stop was coming up. I decided that I was going to report him the instant I got off the train, even if it made me late for work today.

I grabbed my things and waited by the doors.

The train slowed, creaked, and lurched to a stop.

The brakes squealed.

The doors opened.

People started exiting the train.

My hand squeezed the keychain.

I remembered the look in her eyes when I said I had to leave earlier and stay at work later. That I would be around less. They were always tired, but I could see them dim as I said it. 

*Ding*

The doors to the train closed. I missed my stop.

I decided to get off at the next stop and walk back. The time difference wasn’t huge. An inconvenience, sure, but just the latest one in a morning full of them.

I positioned myself in front of the doors to be the first one off, when I heard a voice that made my jaw clench.

*“That’s how it starts.”*

I got off the train when it stopped. I practically sprinted back to the office. My calves burned and my shins were screaming in pain by the time I got there.

My mind raced as I planned what I would report to the transit authority. Not only did he make me miss my stop, have to walk back, and be late for work, but he also keeps being weird. He never directly answers any questions, and constantly says these cryptic, vague things.

I’ll be back tomorrow, and I’m going to find a way to end this.

I’ll let you guys know how it goes.
