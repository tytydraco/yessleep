I’ve never been a believer in ghosts or supernatural things, but something happened to me recently that I still can’t explain.  
Earlier this year, I had gone to Ahmedabad to watch the final. On our way back to Delhi, we were driving from Ahmedabad toward Rajasthan. About an hour after leaving Ahmedabad, we were still in Gujarat, somewhere around the Gujarat–Rajasthan border. It was late at night, and the highway was almost completely deserted. There were areas around us that looked more like jungle/isolated land than a populated area.  
I was sitting in the car when, for a split second, I noticed **an extremely old woman—I’d estimate 90+ years old—squatting right beside/on the highway**.  
She had **completely white hair and was wearing a saree**. When our headlights hit her, her white hair and clothes looked extremely bright.  
She wasn’t walking or trying to cross the road. She was simply **squatting there, looking down**, almost as if she was doing something on the road. She was facing toward the Ahmedabad side while we were travelling toward Delhi.  
We were going pretty fast, so I only had a few seconds to process what I was seeing. My reflex was to turn my head toward her, and my friends noticed me turning and also looked in that direction—but they didn’t see her clearly.  
Then something happened that made the whole thing much creepier.  
**Our driver suddenly laughed and asked, “Did you guys see her?”**  
I hadn’t told him what I’d seen.  
I asked him, “Who?”  
He said he was talking about the old woman.  
Our driver regularly travelled that highway. He told us that accidents were quite common on that stretch, partly because people from Gujarat sometimes travel toward the Rajasthan border to drink due to Gujarat’s alcohol prohibition, and some drive back drunk.  
Then he told us something even stranger.  
He said that **he and several other drivers had seen strange/supernatural figures on that stretch multiple times**, including figures that appeared to be people on or near the road. He didn’t claim to understand what they were, though. He basically said he didn’t like talking too much about it because he didn’t know either—but according to him, they had never harmed him or anyone he knew.  
I asked him how an extremely old woman could possibly be sitting there at that hour, in such an isolated area, with no obvious village nearby.  
He just said that this was one of the things people had seen there before.  
I’m still not someone who normally believes in ghosts. Maybe there was a perfectly normal explanation. Maybe it was a real person who somehow ended up there. Maybe it was an optical illusion.  
But I **know what I saw**, and the fact that the driver independently asked us whether we’d seen her **before I described what I’d seen** is what really bothers me.  
For literally a second or two, I saw an old woman with **long white hair and a saree, squatting on an almost empty highway in the middle of nowhere at night**.  
I’ve never experienced anything like it before.
