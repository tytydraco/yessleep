I was a child when this happened to me. I grew up believing that the supernatural was either a hallucination or some kind of a coincidence that people liked to relate with some kind of an entity. I was in second grade when in a single night, I began to question reality and the truth of my own beliefs.

It was a chilly October night, I was at my cousin's place in a different city for my winter break and we went shopping with our mom's that night. It was a supermarket at the corner of the street where my cousin lived in a seven floor apartment. We had been getting bored since we were kids (My cousin is just a year older than me), shopping felt lethargic and hence my cousin, let's call him B, suggested that we go on a walk and talk about a certain tv show we loved watching. Our moms allowed us to do it as the supermarket was close by and they knew we wouldn't wander off too far.

It was around 10:30pm and as we walked, I noticed a building under construction with only a basement built along with the foundations for the first floor. The basement was like a drive in ramp for vehicle parking and it was pretty dark. When I peered into the basement, a sense of unease built in me and I felt like something was off until I saw them, three pairs of human eyes stacked on top of each other. The pupils were pitch black and the rest of the eye was blood red, I felt them staring into my soul and I was petrified. B already ran for his life but I stood there, frozen, unable to move at all. He came back running and grabbed my hand before pulling me along but as I started to move, I saw a faint grin under the eyes, where the teeth were unnaturally long and sharp.

This incident shook me to an extent where I refused to speak to anyone for a week. As an adult, this might seem trivial but to me back then it was a real experience. A week later, I was playing in the apartment's playground when I felt the same unease creeping upon me. I looked around to find the source and I felt it coming from one of the units on the ground floor. The ground floor had a unit G0 with the door facing the playground and a window beside the door, I went towards the window and peered in. There was a plastic cup which had fallen on its side and a thick maroon liquid oozing out of it. At that time, the unit was empty cause the recent tenant had moved away recently. I was watching closely and I noticed that the liquid has spread like someone has stepped in it.

Then, the footsteps started to form, one by one, moving from the cup towards the wall and then to my surprise, on the wall and finally on the ceiling. I noticed that the footsteps were backwards and I called out to all my friends, they saw it too and we got the adults to look into the matter. Turns out there was a graveyard behind the apartment and paranormal stuff wasn't unusual around there. There was a whole purification ritual and I went back to my hometown with my parents. 

Fast forward 8 years later, I visited the old place again with B and we observed two peculiar things. One was that the unit G0 was still unoccupied and we learnt that the tenants couldn't last there for long and eventually moved out. The other was that the building was still under construction meaning that the contractor had abandoned the project due to the many casualties and labour refusing to work. We learnt the story behind what happened on that land and we understood what we saw when we were kids.

The land belonged to an old lady who had twin sons. All the three family members shared a similar trait of having beautiful blue eyes and everyone around them was fascinated by the blue eyes since they are not so common in that region. One night, the mother announced that her sons will not be getting the property and assets she owned and she is donating it to charity since they were unworthy of all that due to their gambling addiction. A fight broke out and the brothers accidentally killed the mother by accidentally hitting her too hard on her head. In panic they decided to hide the body and bury it in the yard but they started arguing over who gets what part of the assets and it escalated so high that they ended up killing each other by strangulation. The three family members were found dead by the neighbours the next week and the land went up for auction.

After learning all that, I was able to connect the dots with the three pairs of eyes I had seen. That one night had changed my belief and now I'm a believer. There have been more incidents in my life which are a story for another day, which have taught me that the supernatural is not to be messed around with.
