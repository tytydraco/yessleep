This is a follow up of another experience I had camping in the same wildlife preserve as my first one but this was the last time I’ve ever camped there. 

About two years ago now my buddy was going off to army basic and wanted one last hurrah. A few friends and I went into the woods to camp and have a good time. We travelled far off the beaten path to an area of the woods we haven’t really camped in before. It was eerie to start but overall good vibes from everyone and we were having a good time. One odd thing we noticed were that all the trees to the right of our site were in a row. Now when I say in a row I mean literally rows of trees like the pillars outside an old Greek temple. There were perfectly lined up and evenly spaced. At the time we thought it was actually really cool which was one of the reasons we wanted to camp there. 

After setting up our own tents and hammocks the sun was already starting to set so we cooked some dinner over the fire, had a couple of drinks and were just hanging out. My friend alberto suggested we go for a night hike. I highly advised against it because another one of the people who was there and I have previously had odd experiences in these woods about 3 miles away from where we were. Of course I got outvoted and I’ll be damned if I was staying at the sight alone. Now I was prepared. I had 3 lanterns with brand new batteries I replaced that day and my flashlight. I said to have one lantern or flashlight going at a time so we don’t light up the whole woods and in case any die we can have backups. Maybe a mile into the hike my buddy said he felt off and wanted to go back and I agreed. However a 2-6 vote doesn’t always go in your favor. Alberto who was shipping out soon made a joke and said “what are you scared of??? The Wendigo??? Skinwalkers???” And laughed it off. 

Now I have a strong belief in the spiritual activities and honestly got upset at him mocking it. Not even 50 feet later the planter just flickered and died. I said that was odd bc I just replaced the batteries but that’s why we have backups.  The second lantern…didn’t turn on. Neither did the third. Our last hope was my flashlight and it turned on for all of 5 seconds and when it did I’ve never been so terrified in my life. Idk if you’ve ever heard 8 teenage boys scream like little girls all at once but we did. All we saw was a dark outline above perched on a branch and at least 3 sets of eyes further back in the tree. I’ve never ran so fast in life. This is fucked but I dusted my friends and ran back to sight. 

When we got back everyone was on edge now. We were far too deep to pack up and hike back and we had numbers so we justified staying but we were alert. At around 2-3am I was woken up by what sounded like branches snapping and heavy sniffing. There are hogs in these woods so I grabbed my machete and was prepared for an animal lurking around our tent. The fire was just embers barely giving a glow by this point. That’s when my friend Nico who was in a hammock maybe 5-6ft off the ground told ME to stop trying to talk to him that he was trying to sleep. Now I was in my own tent at this time so I turned over and I saw there was a shadow or silhouette of a man at least 6ft tall outside my tent in between me and Nico. It sounded like they were mumbling gibberish and we assumed it was our buddy hunter who was drinking earlier and easily 6’2”. I said Nico it’s not me it’s probably hunter and he told him to fuck off. 

The next day we woke up and nothing strange had happened but our cooler of food was tipped over and thrown about along with our drinks, and the chains looked like they were thrown to the side. We all just assumed it was drunk idiots until my buddy Robert woke up and asked who was pressing their face against the outside top of his tent last night. Everyone in the sight confirmed they never left the tents or hammock. Robert then went on to say they were speaking a strange language that he didn’t recognize and said it sounded ugly. For reference Robert is trilingual, he speaks Russian, English, and Spanish. On top of that he added it had to have been someone with a small nose because it looked like there was no nose at all. He said he could clearly see the outline for eye sockets and a mouth but there was no nose. 

That’s when Nico and I shared what happened last night and how we assumed it was hunter. The realization of everything hit us all at once and we knew it was no one we knew in our sight last night. The food, the chairs, it all made sense now. We packed up as quickly as possible and left the woods. That was the 3rd time I’ve experienced strange things there and I refuse to go back. It’s on an old native burial ground and has a lot of history of “hauntings” and death in the woods especially on the old homestead that was there. To this day I won’t go anywhere near that place if the sun is starting to set or will be in an hour or so.
