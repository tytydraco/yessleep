I’m okay for now. I’m typing on my boss's laptop from his office. My apartment got completely burned to the ground. The police, during their investigation, couldn’t find anything as it had all burned up.  There were no bodies, no gore and viscera. I  One way or another either from the apartments or some kind of insurance deal they landed me in a hotel room for the time being. Doesn’t mean I still can’t work though. I kind of have to, I need the money. 



The mosaic of bright white fluorescent lights hummed a sterile tune synonymous with brain drilling boredom.  The rows of the droning rectangles boxed me in. They outlined my memory of daily confinement to a minimum wage job. I felt helpless and I still do. I waited in a specific anxious stillness waging a war with my memory. Like a soldier waiting to charge into no man’s land. I can almost hear the whistle when I see his face. The spindly hair, the lanky wrinkled skin, that wretched smell of blood and degrading, rotted flesh. The bodies piled on top of each other like trash in a dumpster. I wish I could just fade away from it all. I couldn't think about those things. I had to check Mrs. Jo out. 



“Good evening Micah!” She said with cheeriness that made her greeting not only inviting but soothing. She wouldn’t understand what I’m going through but that’s okay. Her kindness is enough for now. 



“Hey Mrs. Jo.” I could tell my tone was slumped and defeated. 



“Well something’s happened Micah. Go ahead and tell me about it.” She spoke plainly but sincerely just like I really was her family. I almost cried. She began to stack her groceries on the conveyer belt. The old woman was short and she dressed finely wherever she went. She had the affluence of a stereotypical matriarchal widow who was left a lump sum of money and all the time in the world to grieve. Her grief for her husband of fifty three years was reflected in a pearl necklace and weary eyes that in one vein carried no guilt for anything but in another held the weight of a long lost companion. 



“Well, my apartment burned down.” I couldn’t bear to look at her. The water that was already welling in my eyes would surely burst if I did.

  


“Oh my goodness Micah! How’d that happen? Do you have somewhere to stay?” The way she spoke I knew she would honestly let me stay wherever she was. Because in the same way my eyes couldn’t bear to look at her, her heart couldn’t bear to witness suffering. It was in her best interest to bring absolute goodness to the world. I wanted to be like her at that moment. But I kept my head down and began scanning her groceries.



“I had this, well- I just had a bad experience. Yea but I’m okay though I’ve got a hotel room the insurance or someone set me up with. I don’t know, I think it’s going to be okay.” I said with a brief smile that reflected a facsimile of hope that was mustered from some desperate place in my chest. That was all I could spare and a frown came back shortly after. The milk, eggs and chicken passed over the rubbery belt and into my unfeeling hands. Disassociated and numb, my physical body blocked the memories and sensations of the past few days. Being locked away in that awful crawl space, seeing Charles, oh God poor Charles, dead and not a soul to mourn him because of me. Mrs Jo looked up at me and I met her mournful gaze with watery eyes and a single tear wetting my cheek.



“Hon, whatever groceries are here are yours if you can take them.”  Her aged hands shakily but tightly gripped my hand. 



“It’s okay the hotel is feeding me, thanks Mrs. Jo, you don’t know what it means.” I said as my words writhed in my throat like a snake ready to chew its way out through a hard cry. I looked up to let gravity be my last hope against this war with my emotions. Maybe my tears would be held back by the weight of the universe, but was it really ever out to help me?



We shared a brief pause together. It would have to suffice for a desperately needed hug. I finished bagging her groceries while dodging her empathetic glances. 



“Micah God bless you, I’ll keep you in my prayers.” The ray of sunshine that was Mrs. Jo was never leaving without a tip to her favorite grocery clerk. This time an extra tip was in store for me. Her small hands passed a hundred dollar bill to me as one clasped my palm and the other wrapped like a security blanket around the top of mine. Her eyes spoke volumes that her withdrawn voice could never communicate. I didn’t even care about the money. I wanted her hands to wrap around me and tell me it was all okay. 



“I know you’re about to close so I’ll go but really Micah if you need anything just call me.” She took her receipt and a pen from her purse. She wrote out her phone number on it and handed it back to me. I looked down at the scratchy handwriting and a tear fell onto one of the numbers. The ink muddled and washed out to become illegible.. She wheeled out her cart to the dark parking lot and began loading her things alone outside. 



My short rotund manager, Mr Ringer, came from around the corner from one of the isles. “Hey Micah would you mind hanging out for just a second, Julio is still in the back doing some prep for the deli tomorrow and there’s still a customer hanging out too.” Mr Ringer said with a tired voice. He let out a big yawn. “I’m going to go back to my office, let  me know when the guest checks out.” The grocery store owner hobbled his way through the grocery isles. He was a passionate veteran with a concealed carry license where, ironically enough, he was always proud to show off his pistol if firearms came into conversation. His white button down tucked into his bright blue slacks stressed at the seams due to his protruding belly. 



“Ya sure thing Mr Ringer.” I said downtroddenly. 



“You alright kiddo? I’ve noticed today you've uh, kind of shut down.” He said with the hesitancy of an awkward distanced father. He stopped mid pace and swiveled around on his good leg. His other was a prosthetic he earned from his time in the military. It was an IED that took his limb apparently.



“Ya I’m okay, Mr Ringer. Just tired is all.”



“Hm. Alright just text me when the customer is gone.” He turned around and continued his journey to his office.



I sat there in a stew of my own anxiety and fear. Just begging to go back to the hotel. Then I heard a racket from the back of the store. I jumped a little bit after being snapped out of my emotional swelling. It sounded like something heavy fell. I decided to mind my own business. Like I said, don’t start shit won’t be no shit. A few more minutes passed and it was simply the sound of the building itself and the occasional car horn outside. The stillness grew until there were no more car horns. The soft hum of the lights drowned away into an imperceptible nothingness. As my mind drifted away into daydreaming I caught a sound. I wasn’t sure if I heard it or not but I swear it sounded like a dog growling and snapping its jaws. Chills went up and down my body. It had the same tone as the one I heard outside my door the other night. My breathing heavied in my chest and sweat began to bead and pool all over me. It was the same sound I knew it.



And then I saw him. Distantly. Standing alone down one of the aisles tall and imposing. The light overhead flickered as he seemed to disappear into the shadows with every blink of the light. Monte was wearing his black coat with a devilish smile that stretched unnaturally from ear to ear like the cheshire cat. A small puddle of blood was growing all around him until the whole aisle was a crimson swamp. A small stream of blood formed, and as if the stream itself was a snake hunting a mouse, it slithered and glided across the floor with a great speed straight towards me. The blood tendril hurdled at me and before it could reach my ankle, I flinched and closed my eyes. As I reopened my eyes at the nervous awareness that I felt nothing, it was all gone. The light flickered and there was no Monte disappearing between the shadows. There was no blood snake coming to get me. I was okay.



“Hellloooo Micah.”



I screamed as I looked to my left towards the door and there he was. Monte with an evil smile holding a paper bag sopping and dripping with blood onto the floor. I fell onto my back as my knees gave way to fear. 



“Oh he’s already crying like a baby! Now that’s what gets me going!” He said with sardonic joy. 



Frightened tears began to roll down my face in abject horror as I could see Mrs. Jo’s face peeking from the top of his bag. 



“Oh this old wench is quite the doll. She’s not really my type but I wouldn’t be opposed to a stiff regardless. Fresh flesh is fresh flesh after all.” He began breathing in deep as his pupils began to widen to an unnatural size like a feline who spots their prey. “Listen Micah, I believe you’re quite fun. However you must die. Your impudence had nearly cost me my life.” He spat on the last words as fury forced its way through his teeth. “And it cost me my home. The cat and mouse game that we could be playing as I hunt you to your end is quite boring, so how about I throw hounds into the mix. Charles is going to need a fresh meal to fill his hungry belly after he worked so hard to transform.” 

Horrified, I saw a silhouette of a bestial form shamble into my view from around the outside of the door. What stepped into the light is something I never wish anyone to have to witness. The mangled distorted face of Charles stepped inside first on all fours. His face was bloated and distorted like someone had moved his eyes and nose around on his bones like putty or plaster on the frame. Boils dappled his face with puss leaking from each wound. From the neck down to his waist area he was human, but after that he had the hind legs of a dog or wolf with patchy areas of fur on his pale skin. Naked, shamed and tortured, tears flowed down the face of Chalres which contrasted next to his distended jaws which were snarling and foaming at the lips. Blood dribbled from his chin.



“Sick em Charles.” 



The hunt was on and I was the prey. My body felt nothing as I rushed to my feet and ran down the aisles. I could hear the slap of Charles’s palms on the polished concrete flooring as he huffed and snarled on my heels. I had no idea where I was running to. I somehow ended up near the deli looking for the emergency exit. I saw another horrific thing and I began to wonder if this nightmare would ever end with something besides my death. I saw Mr. Ringer and Julio's bodies stacked in front of the deli emergency exit. It was behind the counter next to the fridges, and I could see through the plexiglass deli counter their blood smeared and splattered all over the walls and there he was. Monte was standing there beside them wearing Mr. Ringers face. I didn’t even have time to think about that with Charles snapping at the seams of my jeans. I ran and I ran. My breath was becoming harder to control as I huffed and began wheezing. But I found my salvation. I ran into the bathroom. The supply closet there outside the men's bathroom  had a big heavy door that could be locked from the inside. So, I rushed to the knob and thank God it was unlocked. I opened it up and slammed it, catching Charles’s neck with a sickening crunch. He made a sound that was a cross between a dog's whimper and a human cry of pain and retreated his head from the doorway. I shut the door and I was left alone in a four by four room in total darkness. 



I waited for a few minutes and I could hear nothing but the sound of my pounding heartbeat and my wheezing. After I calmed down I wiped the sweat from my brow and put my ear to the door. Almost immediately when I did, Charles launched at the door banging wildly against it. His nails grated against the door and I could hear his bony jaws gnawing at the corners and every edge. The rattling and chewing was made even louder by the confined space I was in. I held my ears in my hands and screamed to drown it out.



“Heel boy!” Monte said outside the door in a demanding tone.



“Listen Micah, you’ve only delayed the inevitable. You can have your fun in there or you can have your fun out here. It’s your choice.”



I stayed silent. He deserved no entertainment from me.



“Alright then. Quite boring. I’ll leave Charles here with you. When you’re ready to have some real fun just step on out. I know you will eventually.”



I heard his footsteps tramp off. *Who could be sure that he had actually left though? How am I gonna get out of here? Should I just give myself up and let this nightmare end? No, I’m not like that. That’s not me. I’ve never dealt with anything like this but I don’t want to just give up. I mean I think I’m safe for now but who knows. I’ve got to think of a way out.* I thought long and hard with great effort and I came up with nothing. I tried to shimmy open a small vent but I couldn't even fit my arm through it. Charles all the while would sniff and huff and whenever he would hear me do something, his tirade against the door would begin again. I’m sure he’ll - it’ll- get through eventually. Poor Charles, to be a prisoner of Monte and for me to come along and get him killed and turned into this thing. The guilt weighed on me like a boulder. Maybe I should’ve just given myself up. And Mrs. Jo, Mr Ringer, Julio too, they didn’t deserve this. I should’ve known better than to come here knowing who Monte is. 



Then, albeit muffled and distant, I heard someone speak. “Hello? This is CDPD. If anyone is here, make yourself known immediately.”  Then Charles ran off and began barking in a hurried scramble. The police are here! This is my one true chance to get out of here. 



“Holy shit!” The officer exclaimed, then the sound of gunfire rang through the desolate store. I heard two screams. Then it all fell silent. I creaked open the door. There was nothing. I was terrified to say anything in case the dog thing Charles had won the bout between him and the officer. I hope this cop called for backup. We're probably going to need it. I creeped with the softest steps I possibly could out of the door and I still heard nothing. I hugged the right wall and my plan was to make it all the way to the front door or the cop, whichever came first. I ran into the cop first.



The police officer was leaned up against the wall. She had a massive puddle of blood pooling beneath her as a grievous wound made it all pour out of her neck. Her eyes were desolate and stared off into the infinitude of death's door. She wasn’t breathing. A handgun lay bare in her lap, almost calling to me for my protection. I picked it up. I swiveled my head to look around for Charles. All I saw was a trail of blackish blood weaving through the aisles. I had no idea where he was though. I rummaged through the officers equipment to find her radio. I found it in a crushed state caused by what looked like a vengeful bite. I searched for her cell phone. She didn’t have one on her. Then I heard the wet slap of Charles’s palms and bare paws on the floor behind me.



In a low growl he said, “Hel- heeelllp m- m- meeeee.” His eyes screamed terror and agony and his mouth parsed pain and fury through a twitch and drool pooling at its corners.



 I gripped the handgun tightly. The plastic-rubber grip felt slick with blood in my palm. Its weight felt like nothing. Charles slowly walked towards me as if trying to corner me. As prey with natural instincts determining a fight or flight response, I barely moved. I could hear the rattling of the gun in my shaky hand. He continued to slowly encroach. Then with a bolt of energy like lightning I shot up and dashed for my manager's office which was only a few feet away at this point. I raced and raced. Then the overwhelming pain of a bite gripped my leg. I fell right inside the manager's office door as Charles ripped and gnawed at my calf. I screeched in pain. But instinct had another plan, I raised the gun to the doorway where hungry Charles chewed on my flesh and I squeezed the trigger until he stopped. And he did, eventually. At least he stopped chewing. 



And that’s where I find myself now. I’m in my manager's office. I bandaged up my wound but I’m still bleeding a bit. It seems as though Monte cut the phone lines as well. The landline here is dead. I’m sure more officers will arrive at the notice that their fellow officer is unresponsive. So all I have to do is wait it out. I hear Charles outside breathing heavily. I fucked him up good. But it doesn't feel good. If he could ever see this or know it then he should know, I’m sorry. And I’m sorry to everyone else too, Mr Ringer, Mrs. Jo, Julio, and the officer.
