I'm sitting in the bathroom of the Starlight Motor Lodge on Route 9. My phone is at 14% and my charger is outside on the night stand. I'm not leaving this room to get it. If you see this, please don't call the cops they won't be able to do anything.

I just want someone to know what goes on here when my battery runs out or these door hinges give out.

So yes the Starlight has a 13th floor. Its one of those old concrete monstrosities from the 70s before hotels became more corporate and afraid of bad luck. When the owner first opened it he decided to number the top floor 13 as a joke for honeymoon couples and families on vacation. It's not a joke anymore. The wallpaper in the hallways is flaking off in long strips of yellow and the ice machine by the elevator makes a noise that reminds me of a man choking on rocks every 20 mins.

I arrived at room 1313 a few days ago using a fake name. I don't want to get into the details because I would look like a piece of shit which I am. An armored car company in New Hampshire is missing 2 duffle bags full of non-sequential 20 dollar bills and my old partner is at the bottom of a quarry with 2 9mm rounds through his chest. I needed to lay low until the state police finished searching the highway. The starlight takes cash the clerk at the front desk never looks up from his tv and they don't ask for id.

There is only one rule when you have 250k in cash in a duffle bag under your bed. Don't let anyone in your room. 

I put the Do Not Disturb sign on the outside handle before I even took my shoes off. I locked the deadbolt and put the chain through the door. I spent the next 48 hours eating peanut butter crackers from the vending machine and drinking warm water from the sink. Watching the local news on 1 volume and waiting for my guy in Portland to let me know the passports were ready.

At 11:15 this morning. 

I was fast asleep under the scratchy flower bed sheet when I heard a loud clank of metal against metal.

The latch turned and I heard the door hit the chain.

My heart dropped into my stomach and I rolled out of bed onto my knees. I reached under my pillow and grabbed my Glock.

"Housekeeping" the voice said through the crack in the door. It had a heavy accent I couldn't place. Probably Eastern European or maybe a vampire I couldn’t tell the difference. 

"Room is occupied!" I shouted while staying out of the line of sight of the open 4 inches of door. "I put a sign on the door! Get lost dickwad!" 

A hand reached through the crack. It was pale and boney with large bulbus knuckles. The fingers were longer than they should have been but I didn't think about that at the time. I was on edge and paranoid and was in total tunnel vision. She used her thumb to flick the chain out of the track.

You are not supposed to be able to do that with the door closed.

The door opened slowly hitting the little rubber bumper on the floor. She pushed her grey cart through the doorway and blocked my exit.

She was probably in her 60s and short. Wearing a blue polyester uniform with a name tag that was scratched out with a sharp object. Her hair was slicked back into a bun and grey as steel wool. She had cataracts in her eyes and didn't even look at me when I was on the floor. She just picked up a spray bottle of yellow cleaner and headed towards the bathroom.

"Hey what the fuck!" I stood up and tucked my gun behind my belt. "Maam can you hear me? Get out of my room! I don't need help!" 

"I must clean" she said. "Thirteen-thirteen always dirty. They always make a mess." 

She stopped at the edge of my bed. My foot had kicked the bed skirt up when I fell to the floor.

My duffle bag was sticking out from under the box spring about 6 inches. You could see the stencil on the side of the bag that read GRANITE STATE ARMORED TRANSPORT. There was also a dark stain that looked like dried blood from my old partner's jacket.

She looked down at my bag. 

She just looked at the blood on the bag and looked up at me. I could hear the radiator under the window hissing and a fly buzzing around the room running into the glass window over and over.

I panicked. I had 2 people on my conscience and I wasn't sleeping well, but if she left that room and told the front desk there was a bloody bag of money under my bed I was done for.

I didn't shoot her. Even with a suppressor on a pistol it makes a loud noise. I jumped over the bed and put my hand over her mouth. I pulled her back before she could run to the hallway.

The first thing I noticed was she was light. Like a small child. Her skin was also cold. Not cold like working in the air conditioning. Cold like a bag of chicken in the supermarket.

We fell to the floor between the beds. I expected her to fight me. She barely did. She just looked at me with her cloudy eyes while I had my forearm on her throat.

I heard a small pop under my arm. Like breaking a plastic toy. 

Her fingers twitched on the floor and she went limp.

I sat up and caught my breath. I felt for a pulse on her neck. Nothing. She was cold and had no bruises on her neck from where I broke her wind pipe.

"Stupid" I said to myself. "Why didn't you just stay out." 

I needed to get out of there. The cart was still in the doorway. I looked out and the hallway was clear. I pulled the cart into the room and locked the door.

You can't leave a body on the floor even if you plan on checking out in an hour. I gave myself some time. I dragged her by the feet into the bathroom and threw her over the tub. I pulled the shower curtain closed and went back out. I took a bleach rag from her cart and wiped down the door knob and chain.

I began stuffing my clothes and bags of money into my duffle bag. I had about 10 minutes to pack and take the fire stairs to the back of the building. Steal a car 2 blocks away and make it over the border before they noticed the maid was missing.

I was zipping my duffle bag when I heard the shower curtain slide open.

I stood up and pulled my gun out. I pointed it at the bathroom door. 

"Who's there?" I whispered. Which was stupid because there were no windows in the bathroom. Just a small vent. 

I heard the thump of a shoe hitting the floor. Then another shoe. 

I backed up until I hit the cart.

The bathroom door opened. 

She walked out. 

Her neck was broken. Her ear was laying on her collar bone. Her eyes were open and staring straight ahead. She walked past the foot of my bed ignoring my 9mm that was pointed at her. She picked up the spray bottle of cleaner that she dropped when I tackled her.

She sprayed the night stand. Took a blue rag out of her pocket and began wiping it down.

I squeezed the trigger. 

I shot her twice in the chest. She fell back into the dresser and broke the mirror. She sat down on the floor and two holes were in her uniform.

Nothing came out of the holes. No blood just a cloud of grey lint and what looked like dried dirt.

She stood back up before my shell casings hit the floor. She placed her hand on the floor and stood up. She straightened her uniform. Her neck was still broken and air was blowing in and out of her chest from the bullet holes.

"I must clean" she said. It didn't sound like it was coming from her mouth. "Thirteen-thirteen always make a mess. Bad guests." 

I forgot about my money. I forgot about my duffle bag. I turned around and shoved the cart out of the way. I grabbed the door handle. 

I unlocked the deadbolt and pulled on the handle.

The door wouldn't open. It felt like it was stuck to the wall.

I kicked it a couple times and started yelling. I looked under the door and there was no light coming in. Just a grey wall of concrete. 

I heard the carpet move behind me. 

I turned around and backed up against the door.

She was on her hands and knees picking up the shampoo bottles from the floor. Placing them back in the cart. She was moving faster now. Her head was flopping around as she moved.

"Stop!" I screamed. I shot her in the back 5 times.

She flinched each time I shot her. I could see chunks of her uniform and grey flesh fly off her back. I ran out of bullets. 

She stopped picking up the bottles. 

She turned her head around. Not her body just her head. She was looking at me over her shoulder. 

"You made a mess on my carpet" she said.

I looked down. Where the bullets went through her body and hit the floor there was a dark stain spreading across the carpet. 

I threw my gun at her and dove into the bathroom. 

I closed the bathroom door and locked it. I put my feet against the sink and braced myself.

It's been 40 minutes. 

At first I thought she was going to break the door down. These doors are thin you could break through one in 2 kicks.

She isn't trying to break the door down.

Wait. 

She is still cleaning the room. 

I can hear her moving around in there. 

I just got on my stomach and looked under the door.

There are at least 6 other people standing in the room. Facing the bathroom door. A man in a wet suit from the 80s. His neck tie is so tight its under his skin. A woman with a hole in her head wearing a slip dress. 

All the bad guests that stayed in 1313. And all the mess they made. 

She is standing in front of the door now. I can see her shoes 2 inches from my face. She knelt down and looked under the door.

"Your check out time was at noon" she said. I watched her finger slide under the door.

My phone is at 2%.
