This happened a few weeks ago. I was hanging out with my boyfriend and his brother for a while in a bar. One of their friends worked there, which was actually great cause we got some free drinks on the house. 

But I wasn't getting drunk. I made sure of that cause I had a lot of studying to do later at home. That's actually why I left a bit early. 

My boyfriend escorted me to the bus station cause I didn't know exactly where it was. 

And I'll have to be honest here - I generally suck at orientation. I'm not horrible at it - but I often have trouble figuring out where to go when I'm in a part of town I don't know very well. 

I almost always use Google maps, which really does help. 

But I've been to this station twice with my boyfriend before so as soon as he escorted me I recognized it and remembered exactly which bus takes me home. 

Bus 27, I've been on it before and I knew it would take me to a station a few minutes away from my building. 

I had to wait for about fifteen minutes before it arrived. When I got on, it was almost empty. Which wasn't weird at all cause it was very late on a workday, and I got used to getting on empty buses when it's late. 

It just meant more empty seasts I could comfortably sit on. And I did. 

There was two other people on the bus. There was this one guy, headphones on, I think around highschool age who paid no attention to me. 

And this woman who was sitting a few seats across from me. She was older. And kept eyeing me up and down. 

Again, this wasn't anything weird yet cause I got used to people giving me weird looks for my clothes or makeup. I assumed she was just another judgy older woman. 

But I kept glancing at her every now and then because she kept looking. 

I noticed right away she also had a certain style. At least for today's day and age. She had one of those 40's or 50's vintage dresses. Heels that matched. A hat that also matched. Small purse. 

She looked like she came right out of those black and white classic movies. So I really didn't get why she was judging me when she clearly didn't fit in either. 

The rest of the ride I spent scrolling on my phone. A few minutes later I looked out the window to see where I was, but I didn't recognize the place. 

Shit, I figured I missed my station while I was scrolling on the phone. So I went on Google maps right away to find out where I was. 

And just as I suspected, I went two stations too far. I sighed but what could I do? I got off on the next station and looked around to see which bus I could catch back. 

Of course the most logical thing was to catch the same bus on the opposite side. But I looked up and down the street and there didn't seem to be a station anywhere near. 

Which means I'd have to walk all the way back to the previous one. 

At this point I was getting angry. At myself mostly. Cause I knew I sucked at orientation but I hated being reminded of it and messing up like this. Even though it happened more often than I'd like to admit. 

So, I walked in the direction heading back a little faster and determined to get home as soon as possible. 

I looked at Google maps again to see where to go. And it showed me I can walk 5 more minutes and then catch a different bus. It seemed like the quickest way, so that's where I went. 

About 3 minutes in, the buildings started to look familiar and when I passed this one restaurant I'd been to before, I knew exactly where I was. 

And I knew where the station was. Truth be told - my building was only 3 stations away, so I could technically walk the distance. But I was angry, tired and really had to study that night so I decided to take the bus. 

It arrived quickly. And I was happy to know I'd be home soon. But after two stations passed I was confused. It seemed like the bus wasn't going where I thought it would. 

It's like it was going the opposite direction even tho I clearly got on the one that drives towards my home. 

I wanted to be sure of that. I really did. But knowing my orientation troubles, I wasn't. 

So I stayed on while the bus drove two more stations and when I realized I was back in the city center, I got off right there. 

I looked around. This shouldn't have been possible. 

I was confused. But I didn't look at Google maps now cause I passed through city center every day and definitely knew this was the right station. 

That's the worst part. I decided to wait for yet another bus cause now I was even further from home. So I wanted for bus 9. At this point praying it would just get me home already. 

While I waited, just out of curiosity, I looked up the busline for number 27 and to my utter surprise it showed that the route didn't go by my building anymore. 

That was weird. But bus routes change, right? I sighed once and kept waiting. 

When the bus finally arrived, I got on and sat grumpily by the window. I was looking outside as the bus moved. This time I was the only one on it. Didn't really care, I just wanted to go home. 

The stations finally started appearing in order. Each one taking me closer to my neighborhood and boy, was I relieved. 

Then, just a few stations away from mine, another woman got on the bus. I glanced at her slightly as she checked her ticket. Then did a double take. 

It was her. The woman from the first bus I'd been on. The one in the vintage dress. 

It was m weird she ended up on this exact bus where I was again. This is where I started getting a little creeped out. But nothing too alarming yet. 

Not until she got up from a few seats behind me, where she sat originally and sat right next to me instead. Now this was definitely strange. She had the whole empty bus to sit anywhere else, why choose here? 

I turned my head away, trying not to pay attention to her. 

"Cold night, isn't it?" 

I heard her words but everything in me screamed not to engage. I just nodded politely, still looking outside. 

"How long have you been here?" 

I frowned. What did she even mean by that? 

I turned to her slowly, trying to still be polite but reserved. 

"Where?" I asked. 

"On the bus, silly." 

I tilted my head. That was an extremely weird thing to ask, especially a stranger. And I'll be damned if I was going to answer. 

"A while." I said shortly, and took out my phone to appear as if I was busy doing something. 

She chuckled. Short, controlled. 

"A while you say... Well, god knows I know how that feels." 

I didn't say anything. I figured she was just complaining about the buses running late or skipping stations. 

There was silence for a while and I thought she realized I don't want to talk. But she continued after a minute or so. 

"You know, it was always a bus with an odd number. Even back in my day." 

I said nothing. She went on. 

"27, 15, 3, 9..." 

Great. Was she gonna start naming every bus that exists in the city? 

"You're on your way home, aren't you?" 

This time I answered. 

"Yes, my boyfriend is picking me up at the station Late shift, he's a cop" 

I made that part up as some sort of a warning, in case she wanted to try something weird like rob me or follow me further when I got off.

She chuckled again. 

"Waiting, is he? Well, he'll be waiting for quite some time then." 

I shook my head. 

"Actually, I get off at the next station." 

I lied again. 

She looked at me now. With this weird smile. Kinda empathetic with a hint of pity. 

"Are you so sure?" 

I wanted to just say yes and leave it at that, but instinctually I turned to look out the window. 

And before I could even answer, my heart sank. 

What the fuck. 

I looked forward at the road and then back through the window again. I was passing the exact same neighborhood where I got off bus 27 the first time. 

How was this possible? The bus I was on doesn't even go here. I knew that. That's one thing I knew with absolute certainty. 

What the fuck was happening. 

I picked up my phone immediately to check Google maps again. That's when the woman next to me laughed a little harder. 

"Oh you people with your little smart devices nowadays. I had nothing but this little bag with me when I first got on." 

"First?" 

I heard words leave my mouth before I could stop myself. Shit, why was I engaging in this conversation? 

"Ah, yeah. 1953, I remember like it was yesterday. Buses looked different of course. But the numbers never change you know? It's always 27, 15, 3, 9..." 

She already said that before. I shook my head. This was some crackhead for sure. Some homeless junkie who just rode the bus all night with no place to sleep. That's what I told myself. That's what made the most sense, if I ignore the way she was dressed. 

I kept clicking on the Google maps icon frantically at this point cause it kept notifying me that the service is down. There's no way this was happening. 

The woman mumbled something I didn't hear this time, cause I was focused on going into contacts and calling my boyfriend. 

The line went through. One ring. Then it hung up. What? I tried again. And again. 

Until finally I heard "the number you are trying to dial is currently out of service". I hung up again. 

I tried calling my best friend. Then her sister. 

Same thing. 

No, this wasn't right. Three phone numbers out of service? This only happens when your phone is actually the one with service troubles and calls can't get through to anyone else. 

But the signal was full. I didn't understand this at all. 

"Oh, dear you're fightened" 

The woman spoke again, softly now. 

No shit, I thought. But I didn't wanna show my fear at all. I had no idea who she was and what she wanted. 

When I looked at her again I spoke briefly. 

"Could I please get past? I need to ask the driver something."  

She gave me another warm smile. Then got up and let me pass through. 

I went right towards the front of the bus. 

"Excuse me?" 

No answer.

"Excuse me. Could you tell me if this bus takes me towards the city center or the main train station?" 

The train station was the universal orientation point closest to my building. 

Still no answer. I made a confused grimace. 

Then knocked softly on the glass. Even though I was damn sure he could hear me through the opening. 

"Excuse me, sir?" 

I knocked again. No reaction at all. 

It was like I didn't exist to him. He didn't turn, didn't flinch. Didn't give me any sign he was aware of me. 

"Hey!" I spoke louder at this point. Anger, fear. Call it what you want. 

He still didn't move. 

I couldn't understand what was happening. 

I looked around frantically at this point. Not caring about hiding my fear anymore. 

Okay, okay, relax. I tried to calm myself. I went and stood by the door. I figured I'd get off on the next station. And wherever it was, I'll find a way home. Call a taxi. Walk.  Anything at all. I just needed to get off. 

But the bus didn't stop. Not at the next station. Not at the one after that. 

I could fear my heart beating in my ears at this point. This was wrong. All of this. 

"Don't be afraid, my darling." 

I nearly jumped out of my skin. The woman was now standing behind me. I didn't even notice her getting up cause I was too busy planning my escape. 

"Don't touch me." 

I put my hands up and took a step back as if this very woman was about to jump me or take out a knife. 

She did none of those things. 

Instead, she smiled again. 

What the hell was so carefree about this situation? I cursed mentally. 

"My first time was just like this. I was so scared I passed out." 

She chuckled. Then shook her head. 

"You shouldn't pass out, though. Time moves faster when you do." 

"What the hell are you talking about." 

My voice was trembling now but I tried to sound as confident and brave as ever. 

"The bus route." 

She said that so matter-of-factly like it was some public knowledge. 

At this point I didn't give a fuck that the bus was moving anymore. I turned around and started pulling the door. 

It didn't open, of course, I didn't have that fucking strength. I cursed and kicked it once purely out of anger. 

I had to try windows. I never in my life though I'd have to use that metal thing that breaks the glass in an emergency. But this was definitely an emergency to me. 

So I stepped on the seat to reach for it, when the bus driver suddenly hit the breaks extra hard. 

I flew back and onto the floor. 

It took me a moment to figure out what the hell happened. Then the door finally opened. I got up, dizzy, vision blurry from the fall but I didn't give a fuck. 

I looked out. A station. My station. 

I stepped out in less than two steps. I looked around. It really was my station. My neighborhood. My building across the street. 

I could've cried. I genuinely felt tears filling up my eyes. 

The bus door closed and it drove off in it's normal direction. 

I have never ever felt so relieved in my life. 

When the adrenaline wore off I had to sit down on the station bench to catch my breath. I didn't even realize how fast I was breathing. 

That's when my phone buzzed. It was my boyfriend. That's the happiest I've ever been to receive a phone call. I joufully picked up and greeted him, already prepared to tell him about the crazy lady and my drive home. 

But I didn't even get to say hello before he cut in. 

"Jesus where have you been? Tessa, I've been calling for hours." 

"I know, I know my reception was gone too." 

"For a whole fucking day?" 

I froze. 

"What?" 

I must've heard him wrong. 

"You haven't answered me since this morning, Jesus you fucking scared me. Do you know what's been going through my head?" 

I shook my head. 

"What do you mean, I was with you a few hours ago." 

He was silent for a second. Then...

"What?" 

"Babe, I was with you and your brother, what are you talking about?" 

"No. No what are *you* talking about? Are you kidding me right now? That was yesterday." 

I opened my mouth but no sound came out. 

Yesterday? 

I looked at the date on my phone. 17th of November. 

I went out with him and his brother on the 15th. 

Got off the bus way after midnight, that should be the 16th. I know because my exam is supposed to be... today? 

It didn't add up. 

None of it. 

"Tessa? Are you there? Are you even listening?" 

I blinked a few times frantically, trying to piece it all together. 

No this couldn't be. 

I hung up. Put my phone back in my pocket and walked to my apartment. I felt like a fucking ghost. A shell of a person. 

Like something's been taken away from me and I couldn't understand it. 

He asked me about it many times after. Wanted explanations. I had none. 

I told him once the whole story. 

He was convinced I made it up. He didn't call me crazy or anything but he was sure it was a bad dream or something. 

But bad dreams don't do that. They don't sneak up on you while you're awake and conscious. They don't take an entire day of your life away. 

To this day I don't know what happened. And I will never tell anyone about this again. But just know that if I ever get on the bus again and I'm the only passanger there, I will not he sticking around to find out more.
