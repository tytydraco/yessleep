I am disgusted, watching Namisake do the same unnatural act every day. I know we’re sisters but to live in the same house with her makes it even worse. 

My name is Yamamoto Kuzukiri and I live with my family, my mother Yagasuke, my father Namomoto and my weird sister, Namisake. Lately my sister’s behavior’s been odd, I’ve been passing it off as her form of a practical joke, but now I realize it’s a lot deeper than that. No matter what day it is or where in the house we are, she insists on doing it no matter what me or my parents say. Namisake just keeps licking the house, I know it sounds crazy but let me explain. 

Tonight, is our family night, Wednesday to be exact. We’re having one of our favorite dinners and from a bird’s eye view, our family seems like the perfect one, that is, until the dinner takes a nauseating turn.  
Pork chashu ramen, our family’s favorite is what we decided to have, this was my father’s favorite meal to make, and he was his happiest whenever we enjoyed his cooking. Everything seems fine as we share a laugh that takes over the room, when suddenly it starts, what me and my parents have referred to as, “The Licking.” Now normally, someone licking the inside of a bowl of ramen may not seem out of the ordinary, but you don’t understand, it isn’t just an innocent lick, it’s much more than that. 

Sometimes, if we don’t intervene, she’ll keep it up for hours on end. The drippings of the ramen broth inside the bowl are one thing, but when it drips onto a wooden table it’s another, she’ll still proceed with, “The Licking”, as she always does with no sign of stopping anytime soon.

“Namisake cut it out already, there’s nothing left,” my mother says as she watches Namisake lick the table clean.

The licking goes from soft to hard, the sound gets rougher by the minute as the small pieces of wood begin to stick to the roof of her mouth, forming an unusual pattern of splinters on her tongue.

“Your mother said that’s enough,”  
our father yells at Namisake, slamming both his fists onto the table, infuriating her, forcing her to stop mid-lick.

“What did you just do, this table did nothing to you,” Namisake says. 

“Why would you hurt such an innocent soul?”

“The table isn’t alive dear, what are you saying,” our mother says.

“Your obsession with this house has gone on long enough, maybe it’s time we went on a vacation of some sort and left this house for a while.”

“AGGGGHHHHHHH!!!!!”

A loud screeching voice resonates throughout the house, shaking the foundation to its core. I jump out of my chair and hide under the table, trying my hardest to remain calm. Suddenly, I notice both of my parents join me as we tremble in fear. In front of me, I see the legs of my sister remaining perfectly still as the house continues to shake. Throughout the loud screeching I can still hear my sisters’ incessant licking of the table, the screeching continues to grow louder and louder, without warning, my mother screams at the top of her lungs.

“AGGGGHHHH!!! WHAT THE HELL IS THAT? YAMOMOTO, LOOK ABOVE YOU!”

I slowly lean my head backwards and look above me to see a deformed face on the bottom of the table, staring at me with a crazed look in its eyes. Its fat tongue starts to lower from its mouth, lickingmy face violently in a rapid motion. I shriek, feeling overcome with fear, I kick the table with as much force as I can muster. I feel a large spurt of fluid hitting my body from all directions, I wipe my face and notice what looks like blood spouting from the four spots where the tables’ legs once were. 

When the table lands on the ground, I can see the deformed face again, this time two bloody hands and feet start to flail rapidly from the ends of each leg, almost like the table was morphing into some humanlike creature. 

The legs of the table suddenly start taking more of a human form, as the tables’ body begins flailing around in all directions, it feels as if I’m watching a cockroach struggle to flip over after landing on its back.

“LET’S GET OUT OF HERE!”

My father screams at all of us to follow him, we make it to the front door where he attempts to swing it open, he screams in agony, trying to pull his hand away from the door.

“AGGGHHHHH,” my father screams.

“DAD OPEN THE DOOR! WHAT ARE YOU DOING?!” 

Having no idea what happened to my poor father’s hand, I try pulling my father away from the door hoping that I may be able to open it instead. Suddenly, I notice my dad’s hand halfway inside of a large jaw with sharp teeth, the back of the door handle engulfing my father’s hand inch by inch.  
   
“NAMISAKE WHAT’S GOING ON HERE? LOOK AT DAD, HE’S…...”

I look over to my sister in shock as I watch the decrepit face of the dining room table’s underside lick her repeatedly. A tear of blood slowly creeps down through a crevice from an opening in her skin, only to be licked away by the massive tongue of the creature.

“WH- WHAT THE HELL?”

My words were starting to grow quiet, my mother still shrieking in terror, watching my father being devoured by the doorhandle. I could no longer see my father’s hand anymore, only his elbow as it made its way into the creature’s mouth. 

One by one, the walls in the house start forming decrepit faces, each one screaming so loudly that I can barely hear myself think.

“DON’T LEAVE, STAY WITH US!”

“MOM HURRY!” 

I grab my mother’s hand and run towards the back door, as we get closer to it I can see the middle of the door start to form a large, terrifying mouth, lined with two rows of sharp teeth, opening wide enough to encompass the whole door. Arms start to reach out as we get closer, me and my mother take an immediate turn towards the basement door as we force our way through it. 

We both fall down the stairs into total darkness, I stand up reaching my hands towards the ceiling trying to feel for a small pull chain. That being the only source of light in the basement, I quickly began to search for it as if my life depended on it. I finally find it, pulling on the chain as hard as I can, but to my surprise, nothing happens.

“COME ON YOU STUPID THING, WORK, WORK, WORK!!!”

The light flashes on, a red ember shines throughout the basement and surrounding the lightbulb is a growth. It looks like human flesh but to the touch it felt warm and slimy like something I had never felt before. 

I take a look around and realize that it’s not just surrounding the lightbulb, it’s covering all of the walls, the furniture, and the wooden beams in the basement. 

“AGGHHH! WHAT THE HELL IS THAT?!”

My mother points to a large heart beating in the middle of the floor, on the worktable across from the stairs we see my father’s tools, one of which being a Phillips Head Screwdriver. My mother rushes towards it and grabs it off the table, she then runs towards the heart and lifts the screwdriver towards the ceiling.

“MOM WHAT ARE YOU DOING? YOU DON’T KNOW WHAT THAT’LL DO!”

“WE HAVE TO KILL IT, OR ELSE IT’LL KILL US BOTH! I CAN’T LOSE YOU TOO!”

My mother forcefully brings the screwdriver crashing down through the vulnerable heart, the house screeches out one last cry of agony.

“AGGGHHGGHHHHHH!!!!!”

When my mother takes the screwdriver out, I can feel a sharp pain inside my chest, I press my hand to where my heart is and fall to the ground. As I scramble trying to get up, I can see my mother doing the same as I pull myself towards her and tug on her shirt. I pull my mother’s body towards me, and I see a puncture wound on her chest, her eyes start to roll to the back of her head with blood beginning to fill her eye sockets. I pull my hand from my chest and see blood leaking from my shirt and a red crimson color fills my view as my breathing halts to an end. 

I awake to see my mother, my father, and my little sister, sitting at the dinner table, laughing.

If you find this, I’ve either broken the cycle, or died trying. 

You decide.
