"What's the deal with airline food?"



Hey so, I'm kind of new to this whole Reddit thing, and I don't really know if this is the right place to put this, but this has really been getting on my nerves, so here goes.  


So I just graduated college and started this new job, it's some mediation company thing, I dunno doesn't matter. Anyway, what it means is I have to travel a ton to visit all these companies all over the world and offer advice to help "Boost their productivity through he roof!". Sounds much more exciting than it is, it's actually just endless nights in crappy hotel rooms and the same slide deck with the name of the company changed on the title page.  


What's really been bothering me has been the airline we use. My company has this exclusive deal where they book all of our air travel through this super sketchy airline I have literally never heard of. It's called "Zumloft", and I think it's Swedish?? I tried looking it up but they don't even have a website, I have to print out a physical ticket before going to the airport.



So my first few flights were spent white-knuckling the armrests, confident that the plane was going to fall right out of the sky like one of those Loony-Toons cartoons, but at then end of the day it was just a normal plane, for the first few at least. All of my first flights were domestic, but airlines don't normally offer you meals unless it's an international flight. After about three months of work, my job sends me on a trip to Korea, even gave me an extra day to enjoy the country after I got done working. So I get to the airport like normal, and I already had my ticket printed from home, so I have to get through security.



You know how airports group the same airlines together? Like, all the United flights are out of the same concourse, and all the Southwest are together, well Zumloft is grouped with all the international flights, even if it's domestic. So every time I go to the airport, I get to go through the long-ass international flight security, and have to declare that "No I don't have any items I want to declare" It's a whole thing. And then I get the privilege, the privilege, of walking to the end of the concourse, around a corner, and through an unmarked back door, to get to the Zumloft gates.  


And another thing! I have never heard of Zumloft, all of my friends have never heard of Zumloft, the freaking internet hasn't heard of Zumloft, so tell me how exactly this airline is still in business? Not even that, every single flight I have taken with them is PACKED! This flight was no different, and every person getting onto that plane looked greasy and grey, like someone put a filter over them to suck all the color and life from their skin. People shouldn't have a skin color that looks like pulpy paper, they just shouldn't.  


If nothing else, when my company books my flights, they get me a business class seat, so at the very least I have the extra inch or two of leg-room. I ended up at a window seat, there was a middle-aged man in the center seat wearing a grubby wrinkled suit, and a woman with crow's feet that would give an actual crow an inferiority complex. So I did what any self-respecting young business man would do, I put in my earbuds, turned on the noise cancelling, and promptly ignored everything around me.  


Some time between watching my third movie and debating at what point do I want to bother the people next to me to get up and use the bathroom, I felt a tap on my shoulder. I fumbled to pull out my earbuds, and look first at the man tapping me, then to the stewardess standing in the aisle. She had one of the plastered on smiles with teeth that were almost blindingly bright.



"Would you care for a meal this evening?"



"Y-yes please." I fumbled out, still half out of it.  


The lady then passes me one of those flimsy paper trays with the plastic lids, and moves her cart down the aisle. I haven't gone on a lot of international flights, but in the movies they always give you options on what food you want, so I was a little bummed I didn't get the choice of chicken or beef, but food is food, and I was starving.  


I popped open the plastic lid, and beheld my tray of vague brown sludge. I got out the utensils, and begin to stir the sludge up in an attempt to make it look even slightly more appetizing when I saw it, something curved and white.  


A fingernail.  


I almost thought it was an onion for a second until I touched it with my fork, and I felt how hard it was. I gagged, dropping my fork and putting the lid the food again. After I had time to collect myself, I looked over at my seat mates. They were eagerly digging into their meals, it wouldn't be cool to ask them to get up and hold their food in their hands while I scoot past them, so I resigned myself to wait until they were done, and then go to the stewardess and ask for another meal. Maybe this time I'll get the chicken.  


As I was sitting there though, with that gross brown slop in front of me, I kept side-eyeing the passengers next to me. They were scarfing down their meals, practically shoveling it into their mouths as fast as they could. I always assumed airplane food was supposed to be bad, but these two made it look like a gourmet steak. Honestly it was almost hard to ignore, they kept making these grunting noises as they ate, like they couldn't get enough air in between bites, like pigs during feeding. That's when I saw it. In the guy's sludge, right on top, was a hair. The only reason I noticed it was because it wasn't just one little thin hair. It was a massive clump of curly black hair, just sitting right in the pool of the gravy. I was horrified, and was about to say something, when the guy just. Scooped it up with his fork, and just. Slurped it down. Like a cannibal's spaghetti.



At this point I was done, I wanted out of there, fuck their dinners, fuck this plane, fuck off. I stood up, mumbled something about the bathroom, and scooted past them while they shoveled the slop into their gobs while holding their trays. I grabbed my dinner tray as well, figuring this has all just been a bad dream, maybe I fell asleep when watching my movies, and I'm about to wake up at any moment. I looked across the aisle of the plane, where more passengers were opening up their meals. One of the meals was a white bed of rice, with a human finger laid right on top of it. A mother was coaxing an eyeball stuck onto a fork into a whining child's mouth. A business woman was using a fork and knife to dig into what looks like a flap of human skin.  


My tray fell from hands as I felt bile begin to bubble up in my throat. I gagged, and sprinted to the back of the plane to the bathroom. It was locked, of course, so I sat aside and did my best to not look at anything going on around me. That was almost worse, because instead of seeing the horrors, I heard them instead.  


All that crunching and cracking and the *slurping*, my god the slurping. I pounded on that bathroom door like a man possessed, until mercifully it opened. The man stepping out had a look of contempt as the door opened, but I didn't care. I surged inside the bathroom, vomiting into the toilet as what I ate of my dinner came up again. After what seemed like ages of kneeling there catching my breath, I stood up, washed my face, and stared long and hard into the mirror.



I was losing my mind, I had to be. This was some kind of psychotic break, maybe the non-stop travel for work was getting to me. I took a breath, collected myself, and stepped out of the bathroom.



A man was eagerly cutting through a severed hand plopped onto his plate. He sliced through flesh and sinew, cutting the bones out as he forked mouthfuls of the meat into his drooling mouth.



Fuck it.  


I bolted. I sprinted straight down the aisle of the plane, thumping down the way to the front of the cabin, not even caring where I was going. I almost slammed into the stewardess, who was bringing the food cart back for storage. She gave me a shining smile while addressing me.



"Is everything alright sir?"



If looks could kill, I'm sure whatever expression I had on my face would make a sickly orphan drop dead.



"No." Was all I could manage "T-the food..."  


"Was something not to your liking?" Still that megawatt bright smile, rows and rows of gleaming white teeth. "If you like I can substitute your meal with something else"



"No!" I interjected way too loud, startling her. "No, that-that won't be necessary. I'm just..." I look at a young woman, bobbing her head along to music from her head phones. She was rummaging a hand into a plastic bag, pulling out handfuls of something crunchy sounding and popping it into her mouth. One slipped loose of her grasp, clattering onto the tray table. It was a tooth, off-white in color. She stopped for a second, grabbing the stray "snack" and eating it, the sickening crunch making me shudder. "I'm just not feeling too well. How much longer?"  


"Hmm, well-" The stewardess pondered, bringing a hand up to her face in concentration. "...We just finished serving dinner, so we should have another 8 hours before landing."



8 hours. 8 more hours to go in this hellish nightmare, until I landed in fucking Korea, a place where I don't speak the language, and then need to get myself to a hospital and somehow explain that I've lost my fucking mind. 8 hours.



I practically lunged back into my seat, the two passengers still holding their trays, though it looks like at some point they were given at least two more tray, stacking them one upon the other as they kept shoveling it into their mouths. The man's plate was nothing but oily, curly black hair at this point, with him using his fork to twirl it up and shove it into his gob. Strands kept poking out of his mouth as he ate more and more, so it looked like a million spiders legs were crawling out of his mouth. I turned away, blasting my music as loud as it would go to drown out the noises.  


I must've fallen asleep eventually, because the next thing I heard was the loud chime of the intercom. Opening my eyes blearily, I looked up as the crackling voice started up.



"Good evening passengers, this is your captain speaking." The voice chimed. "We first want to thank you for choosing Zumloft airlines, the only real way to fly!"  


I purposely kept my body angled towards the window, not wanting to look at my fellow passengers.  


"Just a quick update about our flight here, we hit a little snag on our route, and will be a little late to our destination. Thank you for your understanding." The intercom chimed again, signaling the end of the message.  


A little late? A little? I felt like I've been trapped on that plane for fucking days, and we're going to be "a little late?". I hazarded a look next to me. The woman sitting in the aisle seat was eagerly cutting into a row of human toes, each with little tufts of hair poking out of them. There was no way I was settling for "a little late".  


I got up again, at this point I didn't even bother with the niceties, just barreled straight through my seatmates as they continued to chow down. I kept my head down, music blasting as I walked to the front of the plane. A little late wasn't going to cut it, I wanted some answers damn it. Not seeing or hearing anything going on around me was almost a blessing, at the very least I could pretend like everything was fine, it was just a normal plane.



I finally made it to the door of the pilots cabin, which was of course, closed. I started knocking, but pretty quickly devolved into pounding, who knew a such a fragile looking door was so sturdy?



I felt a tap on my shoulder.



"Something I can help you with sir?" a voice intoned sweetly, her saccharine voice dripping with joy.



"Gah!" I spun around only to find myself staring inches away from the stewardess again. I banged my head against the door trying to get away from her and that grin as wide as her face. "Sorry, what? Fuck, can you open this door? I need to talk to the pilot"  


"Oh. The pilot is busy." I swear it's like her mouth doesn't move, just that eternal fucking smile. "But if you would like, you can take your seat, and someone will be by shortly."



"S-shortly? But I-"



"If you will take your seat sir."



I felt like I had Alzheimer's. It's like everything was moving in slow motion. I don't even remember sitting down but before I knew it I was back in my seat, the stewardess placing new trays down in front of my seat mates.  


So that's where I am now. According to my phone, it's been... 14 hours, 37 minutes since I sat down and started keeping track. Somehow I don't think anyone's going to come to speak with me. I paid 8 fucking dollars for WIFI on this plane in order to get this story out. The man next to me in the center seat is eating his way through an arm ending right at the bicep. Everyone's meals keep growing bigger and bigger, I'm afraid of what happens when it stops.  


If anyone can read this, please do something. Anything. Crash the plane, call the army, just make it stop.  


It's been 15 hours. I'm starting to get hungry.
