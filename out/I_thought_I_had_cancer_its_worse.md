The doctors keep telling me it's cancer. It’s not cancer. It’s the Devil. The small lump on my body is him growing inside me. Every week I check, the dark lord is getting bigger. Stronger. His voice louder.

When I sleep, I can hear him. The devil speaks to me. Telling me of the horrors he is going to do to the world once he is born. He is going to make me watch. Watch what he is going to do to the world. It will burn. But not by his hands. By the hand of us humans, all he has to do is whisper louder into our ears, and we shall do our darkest desires.

I keep telling the doctors they have to do something. They have to kill me. But they won’t. He stops me from taking my own life. Every time I try, he pulls my hand away at the last second.

The Doctors tried to operate on me, but they can’t get rid of it.  
Of course they can’t get rid of it. Since when has man had the power to destroy a god? Even with the power of the sun, we are just ants to him.

I told them to let me die on the operating table, but they kept me alive. I wonder some mornings. Are they working to keep me alive so he can be born? The pills I take without them would the devil be no more?

Why don’t they just let me die? If I were a dog, I would have been killed months ago.

I am spending more of my time sleeping, trying to talk to him. Trying to convince it to leave us humans, alone. But he won’t listen. It keeps telling me we are a sickness to this world and each other. I say time and time again, we bring beauty to the world. Art, Flowers, Fields, they are all grown by our hands. But his response?  
“At what cost? Pestided, Artists can’t pay their rent and take their own lives. The more good we try to do, the more harm we do instead. We are the cancer of this world.”

With each medical bill I receive, I find it harder to argue with him. Even if I can find a way to stop him, I will be homeless after all this. 40 years of work destroyed in 9 months.  

The doctor tells me I only have 6 months to live. I keep telling them that’s not the day I die. That’s when it will be born. Mark your calendar. It's coming. Every day I grow weaker, stop him.

Soon it will leave my body and come for the rest of humanity.

As I type, I can feel him growing inside me, moving, planning, waiting.

As the doctors won’t listen to me. This is my message to the world. He is coming. The dark lord is gestating inside me. Make right with your god and prepare for him. I can only fight him for so long.
