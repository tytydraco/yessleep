My Brother Left His PC Open With a Shock Video on It, and Here's What I Saw

There was a time when my brother (I'm going to call him R) was really into all that dark web stuff, you know, obscure websites that contained a lot of illegal content. He watched a lot of videos and read about hacking, hackers, crackers, and all that kind of stuff. This was when he was 19. As for me, I'd always been a curious kid. I was 14 when this happened, and my brother always made sure I didn't see the things he was looking at on his computer.

Whenever he was reading something on a website and I walked into the room, he'd immediately close the page and step away from the computer.

One day, he was invited to a party at our cousin's house. He wouldn't let me go because, as a kid, I'd probably have been a pain in the ass at the party. Besides, I was supposed to stay home with my dad. My mom was working at the hospital (she's a nurse), and my dad was still recovering from a car accident he'd been in two weeks earlier. He'd broken his leg and was already back home recovering.

The party was supposed to start around 10 PM, and it was already 9:45 PM. My brother was always obsessed with being on time. He was never late for school. He was still on his computer when my cousin started honking outside, waiting for him to come out. My brother quickly grabbed some bottles from the fridge, probably beers, and rushed to the door.

My dad was asleep, and I was playing Minecraft, but I was getting a little hungry. I wanted some junk food, and I knew my brother kept some snacks in his drawer. So I went into his room and noticed that his PC was still on. There was a forum open on the screen.

I couldn't believe what I was seeing. I went back to my room, turned off the TV and Minecraft, and then returned to his room. I sat down in his chair and started looking through the forum.

It was called YUA, and the entire website had a black background. There was a description underneath the title:

"YUA stands for 'You're Under Arrest!' This is a forum for former cops, rookie cops, veteran cops, and cop stories of all kinds. If you're not a cop, fuck off!"

The first post on the forum contained a video with a police department logo and a title beside it: "Interrogation." There was nothing else.

I clicked on it, and the footage began. It showed an interrogation room at a police department. There was a camera on the table, and the room was almost completely dark, with only a little light coming from the camera. On the screen was a man with a black eye, blood around his mouth, a cut on his cheek, and tears running down his face. His hands were cuffed to the table.

"I'm only gonna ask you once. Relax, alright? You're crying like a baby."

The voice was deep. Then the camera moved slightly, revealing a man in a police uniform. One of his hands was covered in blood, and he was holding a knife in the other.

"I can't believe this! I WAS NOT INVOLVED!"

The handcuffed man shouted.

"YOU WERE INVOLVED! YOU AND YOUR THREE FRIENDS RAPED AND KILLED #### #######, AND WE ALREADY GOT YOU, KID. SO YOU'D BETTER START TALKING, AND MAYBE YOU'LL WALK AWAY FROM THIS!"

The other man shouted while grabbing him by the neck. The hashtags are there because the names were censored with two beeps in the video.

"Alright, please let me go! It was me, ####, #######, and ###. They're hiding at the construction site, you know? You made me snitch on them, so LET GO OF ME!"

The handcuffed man shouted.

"Tell us what you did. How did you four kidnap ####?"

This was another voice, noticeably closer to the microphone and higher-pitched. I figured it was the cameraman speaking, and I assumed he was another cop.

The screen went black.

By that point, I was already crying like a little baby. My hands were shaking, and I couldn't even move. All I could do was keep watching.

Then the video came back.

"Now that I've got the information I need, I have to put you down quietly."

The cameraman said.

"WHAT DO YOU MEAN? NO, NO, WHAT THE FUCK DO YOU MEAN? I HELPED YOU!"

The man screamed.

Then the cameraman moved the camera away, and I could hear a lot of screaming, crying, and the sounds of something being cut. Then there was a final cutting sound, followed by something dripping onto the floor.

The cameraman picked up the camera again, and I saw the handcuffed man sitting in the chair. His throat had been cut, and blood was pouring out of it like water from a burst pipe.

The other cop kicked the chair, making it fall over. Then they both walked out of the room and closed the door.

The video ended.

I was so traumatized that I had to sleep in my parents' bedroom with my dad. I cried like crazy, and I kept having nightmares about what I'd seen for the next two weeks. For months afterward, I started avoiding my brother. Whenever we had dinner, I'd rather eat in my own room. Whenever he invited me to play a game with him, I'd refuse because I couldn't understand why the hell he'd been visiting that forum and watching that kind of shit.

Two months ago, he passed some tests and started working for the city's police department.

To this day, I've never gone into my brother's room again.
