I never thought I'd be spending my last minutes staring at a clock. And I don't even know how the fuck is it possible that a damn microwave oven can predict death better than a God, or demon, or whatever shit humans believe in.

So it all started a couple months ago, when I woke up and went to put the milk into the microwave oven to heat it up. Then, the microwave started acting a little bit funny. At first I thought it was breaking down. As soon as I put the milk into the microwave, the kitchen's light began to flicker and the microwave screen showed for a couple of seconds this strange time. It read 111:14:23:08. It lasted just the time of reading it, before the milk started normally heating and the lights went back to normal. So I thought to myself - was it a glitch? I wish it was.

When I got the milk out of the microwave oven when it was ready, perhaps it happened again: the lights flickered, but this time, the screen was displaying "111:14:13:53". So I understood: this thing was counting down to something. But, *what,* exactly?

Later on, I forgot about the incident and kept living my life. Then, suddenly, I think it was late afternoon, lights in my bedroom began to flicker. So I thought to myself: was it the microwave, again?  
I went to see what was wrong, and I saw my mother in front of the microwave. This time, the screen was displaying: "002:08:45:36".

I searched on the internet information about microwaves showing random timers, but found nothing. What I had to discover, was, *what was this thing counting down to?*

The answer... well, I wish it had been all a bad dream.

A couple days later, I got a call in the middle of the night, I think it was 2 in the morning or something like that.

It was the police. They said they had found my mother dead in her office during the nightshift. I went to the place and found some paramedics who explained to me that my mother had a deadly stroke caused from work stress. I mean, it makes sense, since my mother used to always come home tired after the nightshift and never slept because she keeps thinking of all the chores she has to do when she gets home.

So I immediately remembered about the microwave. She had roughly two days left. I checked the date.

The first incident had happened October 29, and since a couple of days had passed and it was 2 in the morning, the date was November 1st.

I came home, absolutely devastated by what had happened. But left with doubts: *how could a piece of hardware know this was going to happen, in the first place?*

I looked at the microwave. The lights flickered again. This time it read "109:00:17:32."

Roughly three and a half months and I would be done.

I started thinking. A lot of ideas came to my mind. *Was I able to snooze the deaths? Or were they hard-coded?*

You see, I've always had the bad habit of smoking, and thought to myself: *what if I stop smoking?*

So, the next day, I took the 4 packs of cigarettes I had bought, and threw them in the trash.

The next day, with horror, I saw that, after I had stopped smoking, the timer read "044:11:37:22". *What?* Not only it didn't increase. I lost two months.

It had to be a punishment from whatever demon is managing this. This thing *knew that I tried to test it, and shortened my life expectancy.* I had to be as clever as possible to save as much time as possible.

I started doing little things. Waking up at 6:00 and going jogging, like I always promised but never actually did, paying attention to how objects were disposed in my house so that I wouldn't get hurt. Every day I would check and see I would gain... *just a bunch of minutes. This entity was way more clever than I thought.*

Then, my 4-year old niece came to visit. She passed by the microwave, and then I heard her crying because she was scared of the flickering lights.

I looked at the microwave, and it displayed 999:23:59:59. *That couldn't be. Exactly 1,000 days was too perfect. Was it a system limit?*

Anyways, I was happy to know that she was safe at least. Whether that was a system limit or she really had 1,000 days left, I couldn't know for sure.

Surfing the internet, I had finally found a result that pointed to a forum free discussion from 2004 which described exactly what I was experiencing right now. Random deaths had been happening, and the microwave that had *my exact model* would glitch in the same exact way.

Specifically,  there was this woman, who had pretty much the same experience. Her dog passed in front of the microwave. The microwave said it had roughly 3 hours left. Then she went to walk her dog to the park... while she was distracted, the dog found a piece of meat with pills inside, and straight ate it... and died a couple of hours later.

Then I got an e-mail from an unknown address.

***"Hey. I know about the random deaths that have been happening lately. It's not you and you are not imagining it: your microwave can predict deaths. Last year, I had the same exact model and somehow encountered the same exact bug and people who saw their death date on the microwave would die on the same exact date and time the microwave had predicted. Trying to cheat it by changing your habits accordingly, though, didn't work: whatever thing was behind this know I knew my death date, and so it sensed I was trying to cheat death by doing everything "healthy" I had been ignoring until now. Please, remember me. I only have 2 days left. Also, apparently, it can only predict deaths up to 1,000 days before they happen.***

***- Unknown"***

There was only one entity who could be interested in shortening people's lives. *Death itself.*

For a second I thought: "Am I dumb or what? Demons are not real. There is no such entity. Death is a biological process, not an entity."

Well, I was wrong. Very wrong.

I got a text message from a phone number I didn't recognize, that said:

*"It's about time that you join us. I know you've been cheating it. I know you never actually wanted to go jogging, never wanted to get fit and never wanted to stop smoking. You are like everyone else. You only get better when you have a deadline on your head, huh?"*

Summing up all the information I got, I came to the conclusion that the entity was the one who texted me.

And now I had the answer to everything.

Death wasn't here to specifically make me die.

Death wanted to give a lesson to the people who have bad habits or promise they'll start good habits and then say "I'll do it tomorrow", pushing the line forward each day. But, you know, the more you pull the rope, the more it starts cracking.

I looked at the microwave.

A message to everyone: if you say "i'll go jogging tomorrow", do it. If you say "I'll stop smoking", do it. Every bad habit you have is tracked and determines how much time you have left on this planet.

I see gas leaking from the kitchen. Carbon monoxide is choking me right now. I think my time has come.

I take a last glance at the microwave.

It reads "000:00:00:12".
