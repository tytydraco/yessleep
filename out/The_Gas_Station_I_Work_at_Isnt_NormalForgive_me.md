I’ve gathered what I could over the last couple of weeks. Before you say it no, it’s not schizophrenia. Although I can’t prove otherwise. Just listen to me for a little while before the sorrow overtakes my rational thinking. 



Now, It seems like I’ve given out too much descriptive information on the whereabouts of the Gas station, to the point where someone who claimed to have read my “story” has now shown up here. So let me make this crystal clear, do NOT come here. His whole point was that he was going to prove me a “fraud” by finding that building and showing how there was nothing there. I decided to hold my tongue on how that incident happened a year ago, because if he had really “read” my writings he would already have known that information. A part of me regrets withholding that piece of data, I haven’t seen him since. Maybe he realized how stupid he was and went home, or maybe….you know?

Alright, with that weight lifted off of me, I feel like I can finally fill you all in on what’s been going on here that last couple weeks. 

For one Sarah seemed ecstatic when I made my “glorious” return on Monday night, that makes one of us at least. I don’t think I got to mention much of my personal condition last time, the best way I could describe how I feel is strange. My body all in all has felt sore which makes sense, I mean I was out for months. Yet, no one mentioned the brain fog I’ve had to deal with. I don’t know, ever since I woke up my memory has felt like a magic eight ball whenever I wanna remember something. My notes have helped with that and I will get into more of those a little later, I promise. Anyway, work hasn’t been as smooth since my return as I’d like, but when you’re dealing with monsters and entities who have the taste for human blood on a daily occurrence your definition of “good” is a little skewed. What was I talking about? Oh right. 

Sarah was extremely happy when I came back to work, like running up and hugging me when she came in. Were we really that good of friends? I get we’re coworkers and talk most days out of the week, but I didn’t realize we had gotten this close. These were all my thoughts as I could feel my bones start to crack from her tight embrace, eventually she released me from her prison with a slight bounce in her step.

“Finally! Seriously Landon, what took you so long to wake up man. For a second it was looking like you weren’t ever going to wake up.” Sliding her backpack under the counter before taking her seat next to me.

“That’s a scary thought I’d rather not think about, what kind of answer were you expecting? What, like “My glucose levels were below the threshold” I couldn’t pass as a doctor on my best days. Also, who told you I was in a coma?” 

“Hmm? What do you mean? Don’t you remember what happened back in August?” That one struck a nerve with me, “do I remember what happened?” Remember…Ugh, this is useless.

“No, my brain is completely useless when it comes to remembering anything right now. So could you just tell me?” Her face shifted into a grave expression, one that I could swear that I saw as a kid. Then again, the last thing I’d trust right now is my memory.

“Well, you and I had gone out to-”

“What’s upppp! Oh shit Landon? Decided to finally come back to work huh?!” At that moment Jacob had decided to make his grand appearance, startling both of us. Jacob proceeded to reach over the counter to dap me up, reluctantly I caved in to his nonsense. 

“Ya, I’m back. I’d really like to not have to repeat it again.” As I went to pull my arm back I felt his fingers grip into mine, keeping our fingers locked together. Looking up at him, his expression changed to a deadly serious one. 

“Stop fighting it, you can’t do anything to change the future.” His voice was not his own, it sounded like a deep voice talking through a walky talky. 

“What the hell, dude let me go.” No matter how much I struggled, his grip stayed iron tight. 

“YOU KNOW I’M RIGHT! JUST EXCEPT IT!” His screaming voice blared like a set of speakers surrounding me, the volume threatened to blow out my eardrums.

“NO, get away from me!” Violently I was finally able to pull my hand away from him, I looked back at him to continue yelling but all he did was look back in quizzical expression.

“Wooo, you good man? I get I’m not as pretty as Sarah, but I can’t be that bad. Heh, you good bro?” Both him and Sarah were now looking at me with concern, was any of that even real? I don’t know anymore.

“Uh ya, sorry. My mind’s gotta be playing tricks on me.” Trying to laugh it off myself, I pulled out my journal to jot down what had just happened. Real or not, information is king. Plus, if I’m really losing it at least people would know how right? 

“Oh ya, Landon. Why didn’t you tell us you were writing a story? It’s not half bad so far.” Confused, I looked back up to Jacob pulling out his phone.

“Wait, you write stories?! That’s awesome, show me!” Seems like that had peaked Sarah’s interest. Hold on, what are we talking about?

“What are you talking about Jacob? I don’t write stories…Wait, are you talking about-”

“Ya bro, your "journal” online. I really like the whole “mortal danger” aspect you lean into, I’d almost believe they were all true.”

“They are all-.” Wait a minute, I thought. Does he not go through the same things I do? Or maybe he’s just being an idiot, oh well. I don’t care enough to break his immersion. “Ya heh, it’s just something I do in my free time.” 

“Oh ya, could you make my character sound cooler? He kinda sounds like an idiot.” I wonder why. The rest of the night passed uneventfully, aside from Jacob and I bickering, Sarah seemed to spend most of the night deep in thought on her phone.

The following days were spent with me being dragged to parties and bars by my friends, was this their way of looking after me? I don’t know, what I do know is that a couple of days later, it was around 2 am when the night became eventful. That evening had been particularly busy because of the heatwave, it seemed to have sent people into a panic to stock up on anything they could. Why you would go to my shitty little gas station, I have no idea. All in all, the night had finally come around and with that the quiet. To celebrate, I decided to crack into the latest book in my collection. Of course, after a few minutes someone entered the store. Let me ask you a question, have you ever gotten the feeling that you were about to be robbed? If you have….sorry, either way when I looked up to see a man wearing a ski mask and brandishing a pistol. Well, he couldn’t have been cold. The man charged right up to the counter and shoved the gun in my face, I froze. For one, this was my first time ever being robbed and two, it was kinda funny. This guy was around 5’9” and I’d guess sat at about 350lbs, so he had to point the gun up to aim at my face, I’m 6’2”. For some reason I thought about a goomba holding a gun and that caused a small smirk to creep across my face. The man was not amused. “I’m not playing around, give me all the money in the register! Hurry up!” He pressed the barrel closer to my forehead to make his point known. “Alright, alright, I’m not gonna fight you on it.” Now you may think I was being too calm, and to your point I probably was. See, Jacob had given me a brownie the night before with the caption “This shit is the bomb man.” Why did I not think there was weed in there? I couldn't tell you, what I can tell you, is that it did taste good. So now I had to deal with this high pressure situation with my head drifting in the clouds. Lucky me!

I opened the register, a sad assortment of bills sat before me, what do you expect? Everyone uses cards. “You want the change too?”I said dryly. “I don’t care, all of it!” The man seemed annoyed. “Alright”. Grabbing a plastic bag I started to fill it up with the money while periodically glancing at the man every couple of moments. He seemed antsy, after a minute or so I had finished filling the bag with maybe $300, a big score for him I’m sure. But as I looked back up to hand the man his spoils I suddenly dropped it, spilling dollars and dimes across the floor. “Hey what the hell-” His voice drowned out, my eyes had locked onto another person directly behind him and her sleek jet black hair had caused my brain to flatline. She however, only gave a sweet little smile before turning her hand into jagged black fingers with knives as nails and piercing the late robber.   

The scene that followed was brutal. Fortunately for me, the counter mostly spared me of the vicious sight. The sounds however…ugh, not pretty. His screams of anguish faded fast, although the sounds that remained could only best be described as a wolf feasting on its prey. Every gnaw of flesh and scrape of bone consumed the store as the feast continued. Throughout what was probably only a couple of minutes I stood frozen, dumbfounded. My thoughts were a combination of fear for what was to come and the curiosity of what was really going on over that counter. Slowly, I inched forward without my permission getting closer and closer to seeing the horror of what was going on just feet from me. All of a sudden, the door to the back room swung open. 

“Yooo Landon” Jacob had miraculously appeared. Wait, he wasn’t supposed to work tonight, what the hell is he doing here? Is he high?!

“Dudeee, the new buds I bought are crazy good, I passed out in the back. You gotta try…..” He looked at me and then looked at the situation going on over there before grabbing his forehead. “Damn, I’m still dreaming…Better go wake up for real this time, good luck with that dream Landon.” With a wave of his hand he disappeared back the way he came. Come to think of it, I never saw him again that night…Did I imagine that? 

Turning back to the matter at hand, I couldn’t help but notice that the noises had ceased. Whipping my head back towards the scene, the only thing that met my gaze were 2 piercing crimson eyes peaking over the counter. “Fuck! No wait, don't!” Jumping back, I caught myself on the cigarette case behind me and put my hand out in front of me as some stupid line of defense. However, instead of attacking, Scarlet responded by jumping back into my frame of view covering her face while laughing at me? Her hand looked to be back to normal at least. 

“Hehehe, relax heh, relax. Is that how you show gratitude to someone who saves your life? How cold\~” she cooed

Or are you actually going to buy something this time?

“Oh no, thank you, but I think I’ve had my fill for this evening.” Licking her lips with a slick black tongue, is everything inside her the same color? “On the contrary, I’m here to offer a hand to our lost little lamb.”

“Oh\~ curious are we? That’s understandable, being the heir to the 7th and all.” Flashing her razor sharp teeth to me, I refused to back down.

“Listen, I have no damn idea what that even means, so are we gonna keep playing games or what.” Slamming my hand on the counter to try and assume control of this discussion to middling success.

“Ok ok, Mr. Collin’s already informed me of your naivety. However, I can’t tell you directly, we have rules for a reason after all. But, I can tell you one thing. Everything will become apparent to you in the near future.” Great, already getting non answers.
