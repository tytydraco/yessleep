September 15, 12:24 AM



Back again, my dreams haven’t gotten any better. I’ve felt burned out for days now even though I haven’t done anything. I keep telling myself that I’ll write down my dreams, go outside, talk to my friends, literally anything, but I’ve barely left my room in almost 2 weeks. I don’t know what's wrong with me.



I had another nightmare, I guess I finally was able to rip myself from my bed in order to type this up.



My nightmare started with me in a cave with torches on the walls. It sorta reminded me of some of the dungeons in some of the games I’ve been playing recently. Anyways, I was in some armor and had a quest; I had to find the treasure at the end after beating the boss. I saw a familiar face next to me, my best friend, Samuel. He was my best friend since we were in diapers, even if we’d grown apart a little bit, we always made time for each other at some point.



He was next to me, in huge tanky armor that reminded me of a crusader, meanwhile I was in some crappy leather armor. We cleared several rooms, him doing most of the work, I mostly just stood there being useless, every now and then tossing a rock at an enemy.



As we were getting near the end of the dungeon, I started to hear the clicking from my past dream journal entry. This time, the noise sounded more like several small hums, pitched up to sound like clicking, either it was way closer or I just was paying more attention this time.



“Samuel… do you hear that noise?” I was frantically looking around, my footsteps growing louder as I jogged to catch up with him.



He waited a few seconds before responding, “It won’t be much longer now, we’re almost at the final room.” He doesn’t even look at me when he says this, he acts like he never even heard my question. Why was he acting like this? I tried several more times to ask him about the noise, and then I tried asking him about anything else, life, school, games, but he never answered.



I ran in front of him and tried to block his path, but with his massive form, he easily pushed me by without even stopping. This was weird, he was always super good at listening and paying attention, it’s why we had such a strong bond. I always felt heard around him and he never ignored me.



When we finally made it to the final room, I noticed that Samuel’s helmet grew to fully cover his face, not even his long hair was sticking out of the sides. Even his movements had changed, he didn’t walk with broad steps anymore, but marched like someone in the army.



“S-so, you ready to face this boss?” I asked, my face a little sweaty, nervous about everything happening. Samuel wasn’t himself, the hum/clicking was getting louder, and I knew I couldn’t beat the boss by myself. Samuel didn’t even make a noise, much less acknowledge my existence. He kicked down the door to the final room and we both stepped inside.



The boss room was bare, it had a few barrels off to the sides of the walls, a large wooden chair on the other side, and some broken swords on the ground. Samuel walked into the room and sat in the wooden chair, just glaring at me and leaning forward. I explored the room, looking for traps or maybe some hidden room where the boss would be, never finding one.



“Hey man, doesn’t look like there’s a boss aftera-” I cut myself off as I turned around and saw Samuel glowing white. There were beams of light coming from the barrels and being absorbed into him. The barrels spilled over and several bodies that looked like me spilled out, all of various ages. A beam of light wrapped me up like a rope and yanked me toward what I used to think was my friend. I struggled as I tried to escape ropes made of pure light, but to no avail.



Samuel turned my head and lifted his helmet just enough to talk clearly, he whispered everything I had told him in my moments of weakness throughout the years. Every secret, every dark thought, every nightmare, every embarrassing moment. I trusted him to keep these secrets of mine, I had to get them off my chest and talk to someone about them.



He threw me on the ground as I was still bound by the ropes of light, he laughed in an awful high pitched laughter that reminded me of the hum/clicking noise. My other friends stepped out from behind him and started whispering to each other, laughing at me between their conversations.



Now this doesn’t happen often, but after I woke up from this nightmare I had sleep paralysis. This had only happened a handful of times to me before so I knew that I would be fine in just a minute or two, and then I could type all of this up. But during my previous episodes of sleep paralysis, I had never seen anything until tonight.



I saw barrels around my bed with dim white light coming out of them.



It is now currently 1:33 AM as of writing this, and I’ve had time to think about the dream. Dreams are supposed to show things about you, and what you really think. So why did this one show my best friends betraying me? He would never do that… right?
