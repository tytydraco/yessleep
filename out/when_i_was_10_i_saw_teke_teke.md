so one night, my younger sister (who was 8) was sleeping in my bed with me bc she was scared. and the setup was where i had my feet by her head and she had hers by mine. it was only a twin bed, though that’s kind of irrelevant. at the time, i had a loft bed on the left wall of my bedroom. the side of my bed was directly in line with the bathroom door, which i could perfectly see when lying on my side. the bathroom was connected to both mine and my sister’s bedrooms bc it was a jack and jill set up.

usually, i slept with all my doors closed besides my actual door to my bedroom. however, that particular night, my bathroom door was open as well. so during some random time in the middle of the night, i woke up for absolutely no reason. like eyes flicked open really quickly. and then automatically, they snapped to the left side of the room towards the bathroom door. and in the bathroom door frame and under my sink area, there was a girl with no legs, long black hair and a really eery smile on her face. i was terrified, naturally, but i also couldn’t move.

she seemed to have been mouthing words, or saying something but because i had my ceiling fan near the bed (since it was a loft) and i had ANOTHER floor fan right next to the bathroom door, i couldn’t hear her. eventually when i was able to move again, i whispered my sister’s name and said “wake up”. i looked over at my sister and nudged her legs while doing this but jst as the words had come out, there was a thump at the bottom of the bed near the latter, which made a loud banging noise. my sister woke up to it and asked me what it was. i, of course deciding i wanted to sleep SOME that night, told her that it was jst my water bottle falling into the crack between the bed and the wall.

luckily, she believed me and went back to sleep. i can’t remember whether or not i lifted my head up to look under the bed but i think i did. and nobody was there. i didn’t see her near the door either. and by some miracle, i fell back asleep. then, the next day, i was super confused as to what had even happened and if there were any spirits or beings that matched the description of what i saw. i ended up coming across teke teke and then was absolutely sure that it was her. apparently, the words she had said were “where are my legs” or smth along those lines and because i had “answered wrong”, she was going to take mine. (the right answer was the train station where she got ran over and split in half like gojo) but bc i was in the loft, she couldn’t get to me. that was my reasoning and it still is now. there are some issues with the idea that it might’ve been her though bc she died in japan and mostly haunts places in asia yet i live in the US in the midwest?? idk.

people sometimes believe me when i tell them this but other times they don’t. but i swear that this ACTUALLY happened and it was scary af 😭 i don’t sleep with my bathroom door open EVER (especially bc of this) and i no longer even sleep with my main door open since a few years ago.
