Before a week ago today, I never had any reason to suspect she wasn't human.

A little weird, sure. A bit on the spooky side. But so am I, that's why we love each other. Or at least, why I love her. I don't know if she's capable of love. Not anymore.

We live towards the top of North America, if "live" is the right word, but I'm not allowed to be specific. Just know that we're in the woods, and if you drove north you wouldn't be in the woods for very much longer. It is so cold in the winters here, that maybe that's why we all play so many board games. They weren't nearly so popular back home.

In addition to Sarah and me, our brutally deceptive game nights are completed by Jason, Gary, and Sam. Jason and Gary are another couple. Sam is a crazy cat lady who's a bit older but has an incredible sense of humor.

Last Friday night, it was after the games were over and we were all settling down to watch a movie, that I heard Sarah say to Jason, like I wasn't supposed to hear, "This body hurts."

Not, my body. Not, I'm hurting. *This* body hurts.

I didn't hear what Jason said in response, but Sarah held her finger to her lips to shush him.

It stayed with me though. I kept wondering, that night. We were watching The Thing, of all movies.

I couldn't take it anymore and alone in bed together, hours later, I asked her, "What was that you were saying to Jason? About your body?"

I thought maybe I misheard. Maybe this would clear some misunderstanding.

She looked at me, yawning.

She smiled. "This body hurts. All these random aches and cramps and things. That one does too, doesn't it?"

"Well, I did slam a hammer on my thumb the other day."

"*Your* thumb? Funny."

"Huh?"

For days I was so sure I dreamed this next part. She said, "Your memories aren't coming in yet. That's okay," and she rolled over.

Saturday nothing happened. Unless selling my Malazan special editions counts. Hard times.

Sunday Sam and I went fishing. I don't know how she always catches so many fish, but she's damn good at it. For my part I was getting frustrated, and wasn't even getting a bite. I wasn't even having good enough luck for my insane lack of skill to manifest itself.

Finally, after she caught a massive thing I don't even know the name of (I go because she's funny, not because I like fishing), I asked what I always ask. "How do you do it?"

She looked at me, dead serious. "When you saw the hook, why did you bite it? You have to put the fish in that same position."

I managed a fake laugh. It had to be a strange joke, right? Her humour did veer toward the surreal, sometimes.

Monday Sarah and I had a double date with Jason and Gary. At a restaurant serving absurdly over-priced fish n chips, which I guess makes sense, out here.

Gary was telling a story. He was saying, "Yeah, yeah, down at the Fair, I was so nauseous, so nauseous, from that nasty spinning ride, and the swirling flashing lights, and all the meals everywhere, I almost started taking my skin off. Almost, before I remembered."

"Okay this is ridiculous," I said. "You have to all be playing a joke on me. Took your skin off? Really? Some creature pretending to be my friend would just out and say it like that?"

Gary looked at me. "Pretending?"

Jason laughed.

Sarah fucking winked at him.

Gary continued, "You never just want to take your skin off?"

Tuesday nothing happened. Unless you count waking up late, after all the naughty business last night, and seeing the other two had both already left. I guess Sarah had to get to the shop. But Gary? He's an author. Wouldn't he have a flexible schedule? I woke up at 3 ish. AM. Basically the middle of the night, and they were both already gone?

Wednesday Jason showed up to my work. I sell books and the guy's not exactly a reader. Is guy too human of a word? Anyway he said, "Aspen?"

"Yeah?"

"I need to tell you something about Sarah."

"Yeah?"

"Her memories are coming in so quickly. I don't know if that body, that mammal brain, can take it."

"The memories?"

Jason laughed. "Just testing you, dude. Just testing."

He bought a book about anthropology, of all things.

I used my down time later that shift to schedule a doctor appointment. Appointment's tomorrow now, if I'm able to make it.

Thursday, yesterday, I snapped. "Sarah what is this? Why are you all saying this weird stuff? This all a game? Some prank?"

She was playing with her hair. "How could we get the whole town in on a prank?"

"The whole town?"

"Look out the window."

I hesitated. I opened the blinds.

Dozens of people in the driveway. Families, couples, seasonal workers, hermits. The lot. Every one of them mechanically reached up an arm, and waved.

I closed the blinds.

"Sarah," I said. "Something is taking over us." I felt insane saying it.

Sarah giggled whimsically. "No, dear. We're remembering what we really are."

"Love, we're humans. Always have been. Listen to yourself."

She was suddenly right up against me. I felt her breath on my cheek. "Yes, dear. This is humanity remembering itself. Remembering what it really is."

I titled this, I'm Surrounded by Pretenders. But don't take that to mean I'm surrounded by things pretending to be humans. This isn't Hollywood. I'm beginning to understand, though. Humans are the pretenders. This whole time we've been pretending to be these animals. These earthly, fleshy... things. But the memories are coming in. Oh, the memories are coming in.

Game night again tonight.
