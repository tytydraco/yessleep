Ive lived in the same town almost my entire life, its a fairly big place, 20 thousand people, but living there your whole life, you get to know every knook and cranny, or at least i thought so.

I heading home, work wasnt far, about a 20 minute walk, i took the same route every day, and hadnt seen any construction recently, so it was beyond me how a new street had been built since yesterday, it was a perfect street, not a pothole or crack, with sidewalks all along both sides, no buildings, no remnants of construction, anything. Although i thought it was weird, i just assumed it was an extremely quick construction job, or i just never noticed it, so i went home, and clocked out for the day.

The next day, after id gotten myself up and ready, i left home to head to work, same route as yesterday. I reached the new street, unable to say a word, as i see perfect rows of beautiful white suburban houses, i have no clue how they got there, no construction, nothing, at this point i was freaked out, but decided to ignore it, it wasnt my problem anyway. 

Work went by fine, still thinking about the street, i decided to ask my coworker about it, they said it had always been there, i knew this wasnt true. 

Nothing had changed to the street on my way home that day, i was very worried about it, i didnt know what was going on, i had a few drinks to take my mind off it. I collapsed onto my bed, 1 in the morning, Half awake, half asleep, and i hear something, it was a chunky wet cracking sound, and behind it was what i think was voices, extremely crackly voices, a a a... a a a... i i i... i i i...,. 

I wake up, major hangover, but still remembering what happened last night, although horrified, i get ready and go to work. I got to the street, and i couldnt breathe, there were people living in the houses, normal looking people, and at the beginning of the street laid a sign, sunset street.

Im horrified, how did they get there, who are they? Where did they come from?

I spent a week watching this street

The people living there seem normal, they had families, and the longer i watched i saw they had routines, individualities, and personalities, but they never left the street, never went to work, and never went shopping. After some thinking, i decided to knock on one of the doors, a woman openned it, dressed very properly, we chatted a small bit, but i couldnt keep my eyes off the interior of the house, completely bare, not even flooring, just concrete, 2 kids simply stood in the bare house, doing nothing but look.

I left the street, i didnt want to think about it anymore, but it kept haunting my mind, after i couldnt sleep, i stayed up watching raunchy comedies. 1 in the morning came quick, and right when the clock struck, i heard screams, not of one person, but what seemed like a hundred different people, screams of agony, the screams were so loud i almost couldnt hear the crunching and wet gushing.

I had to plug my ears just to sleep.

The next day the street was gone, no buildings, not road, no sign, the only thing i could see, was splatters of blood where the street wouldve connected.

No one else acknowledged when the street appeared, no one acklowledged when it disappeared.

I try to forget about sunset street

But those people felt real
