it was 5pm on a cool autumn evening, I was siting on a couch enjoying a cup of black coffee when I suddenly heard tapping on the window. I put my coffee aside and got up, there was a little boy tapping, I waved and pointed towards to the door to ask him what was wrong why he'd called to me.

I opened my front door, where I was greeted by a boy wearing an oversized wool sweater with white and red stripes. His name was Mike and he wanted a glass of water. I went back inside to get him one but apparently I took too long because when I returned, he was gone. I looked around but there was no sign of him. I closed to door and went back to my comfy couch but now my coffee was cold and stale. I got up to brew a fresh batch. By the time I was done, it was 7pm. I was supposed to have a couple of friends over, to play board games and have some fun. Jakey was in charge of dinner which he conveniently decided on pizza, Steve was our game master for the night and Alice, my girlfriend, brought some wine. 

I waited around a bit and when no one showed up I started calling them one by one. Steve and Jakey didn't pick up, Alice went straight to voicemail, my text all undelivered. I saw my front door neighbor, colonel, in his nighttime robes holding his phone up so I went out and it turned out there is a service disruption in the area. I was bummed, so I invited him for some black coffee or something, my favorite drink in the world. It turns out he has work in the morning so we ditched coffee for whiskey like the real men we are. 

As we were talking about life and work, I heard gentle taps coming from upstairs. Wood panels creaking, maybe someone walking. I asked if he heard anything and he said yes he did. So I went upstairs to check. I walked up slowing not to alert whomever was there and as my head peeked over before my body I saw two glowing eyes in the dark. like cat eyes but bigger. I noped the f out and went down right away. I went back acting as if I wasn't horrified. Colonel with his usual calm and cold tone asked me what it was. I said it's my girlfriend's cat, a really really cute Persian. He said he had never seen one before and he was curious and I said well by all means, he should go upstairs just don't wake him up.

  
15 minutes passed in complete silence and there was no sign of the colonel. Now I knew for sure that it wasn't an animal or my mind acting up. I got up, grabbed my keys and decided to spend the night at Jakey's or Steve or whoever would have me. Id check everything out in the morning. Just before reaching the door, I heard someone call my name, I turned around.

  
It was the colonel. I sighed and acted like I was just about to grab a beer from the fridge so I grabbed two and he was furious because there was no cat upstairs. My mind raced to make something up, and I just offhandedly said that maybe the damn cat got out the window and my girlfriend's gonna kill me but it's okay because I hate cats anyway. Then we talked about my job and hard it was and then he told me some of his thrilling war stories, like how he was stranded in east Asia roaming the jungles and surviving off stolen chicken from enemy villages before hiding in cargo or how he once killed a bison with his bare hands in the west of the Rocky Mountains.



When it was around 11pm we were both pretty tired and decided to call it a night, he got up and we shook hands, I walked him to my front door, and then watched him walk to his. As he was opening his door, he turned around and waved at me. I started to wave back and as I was waving I saw something that shook me to my core. His eyes were glinting in the dark.



Cold sweat started to run down my face. I grabbed my keys as fast as I could, to just get away but my front door wouldn't budge. Nor the windows or the backdoor. I was locked in. Then I saw the whole neighborhood lose power. Nothing but darkness. Darkness and my phone's flashlight, still out of service. Did my friends somehow know about this? is this why they didn't show up? I grabbed a knife and sat in the corner of my living room, two walls covering me and my eyes forward, constantly scanning. I turned the flashlight off but it was probably no use since if the eyes are truly like an animal it probably can see in the dark. I somehow knew that I need to finish this before the morning. In daylight there wont be glistening eyes to guide me, there would only be people. The colonel. I called him the colonel but I didn't really knew what he did nowadays. What if he has access to nuclear codes? no. That's impossible. But there has to be more to it. As I was thinking I saw a pair of eyes moving down the stairs.

  
I couldn't make it out in the dark. If I could see it, it could probably see me. I held the knife closer. And it got closer. I started to see shapes on its body. lines. stripes? Mike...? 

"That's not gonna help you." it said in a deep and mature voice.

  
"Mike? is that you?"

  
I saw a smile under the white reflective eyes, made out a bit of teeth in the dark. Two white dots and a smile.

  
"Have a seat"

  
I was too horrified to disobey. Now that I was closer and my eyes had fully adjusted to the dark I saw that it indeed was the same kid, Mike, in his oversized striped sweater. The only thing different about him was his eyes, which reflected light like a cat, and his voice, which was now really mature and masculine.

"What do you make of me?" He asked.



"Skinwalker? Secret invasion? Alien? An angel of the lord?"

  
he started to laugh really hard "An angel of the lord?????" and some.

  
"I don't know, you tell me."

  
"Look at my eyes. You know how much my eyes alone can tell you? Im a creature of the night. That's what I'm evolved for. evolved. that's a funny word."

  
"Funny how?"

  
"Why are you so calm about this? Aren't you frightened to death? come on, drop the act, I can smell the fear in your swear. Your pheromones, that's another fun evolutionary fact for you." he smiled.

  
He was right, I was horrified, but one thing I had learned in life, was that in some situations you really need to hide how you feel. It's like a breakup, even if it's killing you, you can't let the other person know. So I didn't fold. I kept myself together. 

  
"I learned that in school. Im a child after all. just a kid."

  
"..."

  
"A kid who wants to live. Just like you."

  
"..."

  
"do you want to live? do you want to live, as yourself?"

  
"Yes"

  
"Then you'll understand what I'm about to do."

  
As soon as I heard this, I didn't not plead, I did not beg, I did not run, I raised my knife and plunged it as hard as I could but as my knife started to land suddenly everything started to shake and then, it was morning and I was all alone, my knife not in my hand but in the kitchen. I checked my phone and I saw a couple of texts from Jakey and Steve apologizing for last night and a missed call from my girlfriend. I brewed some coffee, enjoyed the sweet taste of it and dressed up, ready to leave for work. As I was locking the door I saw the colonel doing the same. He waved good morning and I waved back. I sat in my car, looked at Mike through my rearview mirror and told him he wont be late for school this time.
