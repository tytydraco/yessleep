I am alive- obviously- but the lake in town, maybe less than a mile (?) away from my house, had a body get pulled up on some fisherman’s hook, and the police identified it as me.

You can see the problem here.

Yes, I have a sibling, but we’re four years apart and no way for us to have matching fingerprints (plus they live on the other side of the country.) No, there’s no possibility of some weird hospital mix-up because we have the video of me being born in my mom’s attic.

Like- I’m alive.

So what the fuck?

It had clothes that I own (but are still in my closet) it has my birthmark on my lower back, and even has the miscellaneous scars I’ve collected through the years (touching the corner of a hot pan on my left pointer, on my right forearm from a rusty nail on the back of my uncle’s couch while moving it, scraped left knee from a scooter incident.) Later, a pair of glasses just like mine washed up. The cause of death hasn’t been identified, but it is confirmed to have been a live body at some point. Water’s in the lungs, it’s bloated like it’s been in the lake for days, but there’s no telling the specific time frame, let alone if that’s what killed “me.”

This happened like a week and a half ago, and I think I’m finally done with the initial shock of it. My friends can’t believe I’ve been this calm the whole time (when it happened at least,) and I think my mom has been freaking out about this way more than I had. She’s been visiting every other day, and I swear she’s about to tell me to move back in with her.

I get it.

It’s fucking freaky, but what exactly can I do about it? I’m not actually dead, and while I keep getting questioned by police, there’s no way to actually link me to what happened beyond “my” DNA matching. Honestly, it’s exhausting to be saying “I don’t know” to every other person I meet. I still have my life, why can’t I keep living it?

What’s getting under my skin NOW is the neighbors getting all up in my business. There’s the half that show sympathy, acting like I lost a family member or a dog or something, condescending to me like I’m a child. Then, there’s the other half.

The other half thinks I’m behind it.

They think it’s all some elaborate prank and that I’m wasting resources just to make a fuss.

Here’s why that’s a load of bullshit: my routine is sleep, work, sleep. I don’t do anything. I don’t socialize *on purpose*. At most, I text my friends or play games on Steam with them, maybe visit mom up the road. I don’t want people looking at me, let alone focusing so much of their energy on me.

But these people just won’t listen to reason.

At odd hours of the night, whether or not I have my lights turned off, I’ll have someone knocking on my door, trying to get me to open up for whatever reason. Usually, it’s just one person, but sometimes it’s two or three. I’ve answered the first couple of times thinking there was some kind of emergency (because normally, we never actually talk to each other,) but once the door cracks, they’ll wedge a shoe in the door and start yelling at me.

I’ve been called all kinds of things. Psycho, sick bastard, attention whore, whatever kind of nickname they feel like it that night. They’ll throw accusations at me, that the cops are in on it to fearmonger the town, that I made the body to hide evidence for or distract from something else. Your guess is as good as mine for whatever they think I’m hiding.

Oddly, though, the worst nights are the ones where they don’t say anything.

I have a history of night terrors. There have been plenty of times where I wake up screaming because of some perceived threat, whether it be some shadow figure or just… something. I once made it all the way to the bathroom across the hall before I woke up and forgot what I was running from.

So when I wake up thinking someone’s outside my window, I’ve gotten used to being proven wrong by turning on a light.

And I was wrong.

There were *many people outside.*

None of the faces flinched when I screamed the first or second time, and their eyes followed me while I tried to back away with my phone in hand. I didn’t want to look away in case any of them tried anything, but I also needed to call the cops.

I stared at them holding a bat while I waited, all the while trying to suppress a panic attack.

The 30 or so minutes it took for the police to show up felt like hours. I could have sworn none of them blinked.

What I hated the most was that I recognized them. They were my neighbors. Even the ones that sympathized with me and tried to be there for me. I wasn’t going to open the window to ask what’s happening, but none of them tried to communicate with me either. They probably heard my call, but none of them panicked or had any response at all. And right as I was beginning to think these are cutouts to prank me, some of them shifted weight or face angles.

I hated it. I hated the adrenaline wearing off already and my body aching from how tense I was the whole time. My fingers had indents from the bat handle.

None of them moved when the cop car pulled up; I even heard the harsh knock of the officer once he got out. And I can’t get over what he said to me.

“What seems to be the problem, son?”

I just kind of gawked at him, then gestured to my bedroom window. “Didn’t you see any of them outside?? They were staring in at me! There was, like, at least ten!”

“Son, there’s a difference between a neighbor seeing through your window and gang stalking. You sure you’re not just super stressed? Because of- you know...”

Swear to god, I was willing to go to jail for assaulting an officer.

When I asked him how he didn’t see them out front, he just said something like “Nothing weird about some friends taking a late night walk.”

I was going to start yelling, but then, while I was rubbing my eyes, I caught sight of his gun.

It was late.

I’m beyond tired.

I ask him to step back so I can take a look at my window from the front porch, and a brief flashlight check later, all of them were gone. My blood had been as cold as the night air. I asked him to at least maybe do a brief check around my house, inside and out, just for my own peace of mind. He agreed, took a look around, and gave me the thumbs up before waiting for my light to be out before he pulled away.

I’ve been taking roads away from the lake.

In order to get to work, the road at its perimeter is the fastest way, but anytime I get a glimpse of that thing, I can’t find the beauty in it anymore. The boats have slowly started coming back since the investigation started, and the officers have allowed the shoreline where the body was pulled up to be fished at again under the supervision of at least one cop car.

The stalking incident means I can’t sleep at night, instead taking power naps before and after work in my car and drinking caffeine like a motherfucker.

The times that I do sleep, I feel like I’m underwater. They say that you can’t feel pain in your dreams, but that’s a bald faced lie. There’s a burning in my lungs and a sharp pulling in my arm just below my wrist. I can see my bloated cheeks in my periphery, and bubbles don’t come out of my mouth anymore. I never make it to the surface.

Despite watching my windows every night now, my neighbors have stopped showing up at night. I can’t look them in the eye anymore, and I can’t so much as wave back when they see me outside.

I’m close to asking mom for an overnight visit at her place, but the thought that I might end up dragging her into whatever the hell is happening has made me a bigger shut-in than ever before. I’ve been lying every time she asks me if I’m doing alright. It feels so ugly.

I think the thing that’s getting to me the most is my neighbor, Lilian. She’s an older woman who’s grandkids are at that age where they don’t think to talk to relatives that much, so she likes to stop by people in the neighborhood for any reason she can find. I keep turning down baked goods or other presents she says were originally for her “ungrateful grandchildren,” and ever since the body was found, she messages me saying she’s been worried sick.

I can’t stand it. Not that I’m not touched by someone who’s trying so hard for her community, but that she’s started following me every time I leave the house.

Not exactly in the way of literally getting in my car or driving directly behind me, but anywhere I go, she’s somehow there. Sometimes she’ll come up to me and chitchat, but most of the time, we make eye contact and she retreats somewhere.

Worst of all, by the time I come home, it looks like her car hadn’t moved an inch.

Before you ask, no, she wasn’t one of the faces that was watching me that night. I think that’s what’s bothering me at the core of it because I thought she was the one person I could kind of trust, but I don’t know anymore.

I’ve been here for years now. Like, a long fucking time. I know these people, I’ve shoveled their driveways, I catsat for Lilian for fuck’s sake.

…

When I was in high school, I broke my arm during PE. My whole class signed my cast, my sister fucking flew from her college to see me, I’ve had neighbors drop by when I came back from the hospital- Is it just the road I live on now? Because mom is just up the street. We’re literally in the same neighborhood.

It’s just….

…. closer to the lake.

I’m stuck at this point.

Would it be worth it to just bail? Like, would it be a bad idea to ask my sister if I can move in just so I can get as far away from this stupid place as I can?

What exactly are the chances that Lilian or anyone (or anything) else could follow me from Minnesota to Washington?

And is there a chance that this could happen to my family, too?
