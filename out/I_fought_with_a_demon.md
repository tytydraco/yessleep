When I was 19 years old, studying in college staying in hostel, something happened which shook my life and changed me forever.

  
Me and my roommate usually fall asleep in time. One night, I woke up shouting "Aaaaaaahhhh," with pain. My roommate was terrified and switched on the light. Then I got confused. I didn't know what was that pain and why I woke up like that. My friend suggested a nightmare must have caused this. Then I fell asleep.

The same happened again and again. As days went on, I tremble before going to sleep. My friend said it must be because of the relationship issues I had with my girlfriend. He said not to worry much and try to sleep well.

I tried working on my psyche to be more positive and slept and then the next few nights it went well.

Then one night, I woke up, but I couldn't move. I couldn't speak. Someone was sitting on me. I couldn't bear the weight it has on my chest. I tried to cry for help, but nothing. I could open my eyes, something dark was sitting on me. It was looking like a black smoke. I was terrified, couldn't close my eyes either. I was sweating, my body suffering and felt like my soul was being sucked, but I had assurance that it failed to do so. After few minutes, it went away. Then I breathed well and sat up. Drank some water and thought it must be sleep paralysis along with some hallucination.

Then this started happening every night. I shared it with my friend and he was supportive and motivated not to be afraid. He believed me and suggested the only way is to fight back. When I asked how, he said he didn't know, but I have to figure a way out. He said one night he wouldn't sleep and watch over me.

Next day I asked him about what happened last night since I didn't get any disturbance that night. He said, "You are already terrified but I have to tell you the truth. I was acting like sleeping but actually I was watching you as long as I could. I saw a girl like figure stand beside you. But it didn't do anything and I had a strong feeling that it knew that I was watching. I was terrified and prayed God and closed my eyes. I don't know how long I stayed like that before I slept."

The next night, I was ready. I was acting like sleeping, waiting for it to come. I didn't know what to do when it comes but I was determined that I would fight back this time. Then it happened.

It sat on me. Since I hadn't slept, I tried to push it. The smoke held my hands. I couldn't move. Then I felt something, which brings chills down my spine even when I remember now. I felt its breathing hair fall on my hands. It had a body, it was breathing, it had mass, so even though I was terrified of its breathing air falling on my arms, at least this time I knew something about it. I pushed and said, "Let's see this time who wins." It pressed its face on my hands, like it wanted to bite me, but it actually couldn't, and felt heavy smoke covering my hands. We stayed like that for a while, nobody winning. Then I felt the smoke trying to get inside my body. 

"So this is what you have been trying daily. You couldn't get in even when I was sleeping. You can't now." I pushed with one hand and I placed my other hand on my stomach where the smoke slightly got in and pulled the smoke out. "Show me what you've got," I said. Then the smoke turned into a shape. A woman like face with fangs, big eyes and hair from head to covering entire body. I angered it, it seemed and it was manifesting physically slowly. Then it was easy. I was able to push it and then, I punched it again and again. I don't know whether it really hit or it just went through the smoke like figure. But it was really satisfying punching back.

Its face was getting clearer was it went on. I could see its face with surprise and didn't want to do this anymore. I tried to hold its hands as I was not done with it. But it vanished.

My friend had woken up just during the last part and he said, "I saw it. You fought back. That thing looked terrifying."

"I think I shouldn't be an atheist anymore," I said.
