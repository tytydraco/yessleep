It could fall at any moment. But I know it will be soon. If you're not familiar with the sword of Damocles, this is something very similar: there is a sword hanging over my head by a thread that seems to come from nowhere, and the sword follows me around, always at the same distance from my head, always ready to fall.

It's the result of a deal made almost a decade ago at this point. I had a certainty that I wouldn't make it past twenty five. I won't go into detail on why, that's not the point of this post. But the thing is I knew, or I thought I knew, so a deal was made and I sort of extended that lifetime.

At the time I didn't know for how long it would be. Frankly, I was just baffled to make it past my "due date". I began trudging through the rest of my life like an expired yoghurt and, eventually, I started acting like a not expired yoghurt. I made vows, I met people, I built things, I worked on myself. Things done with a naïve hope that I could live a life.

I would like to say the sword suddenly appeared out of nowhere then and took me by surprise. But that would be lying, I saw the fucking sword. I caught glimpses of it ever since my twenty fifth birthday. First I told myself that I was just tired. Everyone sees things when they're tired right? At least in stories they do. I mean it's not usually a sword hanging above their head but whatever, I always had a vivid imagination.

Anyway, after the first few times I couldn't really ignore it anymore. It wasn't there always but it reared its blade multiple times a month. It happened often enough to know it was there but with enough time between sightings that I kept just... convincing myself it didn't matter?  
I know it's stupid okay? I know that the logical thing when you see a sword above your head is to seek help. Some would get it from a priest, or a healer of some kind. Maybe you could even ask a friend to get it for you, if you wouldn't mind sounding insane.

I did try all of that later, but not nearly as soon as I should have.

I don't remember exactly when it became a permanent fixture above me. I'd love to say it was after I married my wife, or after we bought this expensive house, or after our kid was here. I'd love to say I didn't do all of these things knowing full well that I wouldn't be here for that long. But I'm selfish, and I wanted to be happy. I even convinced myself that maybe doing things that I wanted for myself would get rid of the sword. Like, there was something missing from my life that was causing it to be there and I just had to find out what. But those were excuses, I don't think I ever truly believed that.

Back on topic, I have tried to remove the sword. I've spoken with different people I thought could help me with it but it's always the same story. I get there and I mention the sword but then I also get into other things that haunt me. There are other things, of course. Everyone has things that haunt them, tiny things, big things, smaller than the sword, less lethal. I naturally avoid the topic, or clamp down when it's brought up back to me. No one has been able to see it, so I think no one has understood the gravity (pun very much not intended) of the situation.

So I let them exorcise my other demons, or heal my other maladies, and I thank them and tell myself that I'll be fine. I'm getting better, after all. And maybe it's all connected, maybe it was some lesser demon that brought the sword to me. I think that's what happened at least. Maybe they all bunched together to make it, to hang it over my head. But the deed is done. Even though they're gone now, there's still a sword over my head.

It's been there for so long that it has began to feel like a part of me, natural, integral, inseparable (save for the half a meter or so of air between us, I guess). It started to show up in memories in a hazy manner, always unsure if it was really there or not. After a while I couldn't tell when it had shown up anymore. Then I started seeing it in old pictures. I mean I couldn't really see it see it, but I felt like it was there. I could tell, somehow. I thought. I said before that it appeared when I was twenty five, I think that's the correct time after sorting my thoughts on things, everything before is just... part of what the sword does, I suppose, or just my broken brain trying to make sense of it. Malfunctioning. Shitty thing.

Of course I've tried to take it down, physically get a hold of it. It's not ethereal for me, actually. It' not like my hands phase right through. Not so lucky. No, I can feel every part. I can't move it, take the handle and push it to the side or lower it down. It moves relative to me so I can barely reach the handle anyway. Long sword. I tried to take it by the blade after some frustration, once. It cut me, of course. Shit's sharp as hell. The blood from my hand flowed far, far faster than it had any right to. It felt like I was being emptied, like I'd opened a dam and it just, river, you know? A waterfall of blood pouring out of the cut in my hand, which wasn't even that big mind you.

It hurt somewhere in my chest. I started shaking, tearing up. And while my chest hurt, my hand felt just fine. It freaked me out. I rushed to the bathroom, opened the cabinet and knocked everything out while grabbing the roll of fabric to make a bandage. My wife called out from the living room and I was actually about to tell her that I needed help with my hand before I noticed that my hand wasn't hurt anymore. I thought of the river of blood I must have left on my way to the bathroom and I looked at the floor, left into the hallway, stared through the open door to our bedroom... nothing. I chuckled, realizing the danger was over.

I never thought that there had been no danger in the first place, though. That's the one thing that experience left with me. Certainty. That the sword could kill me. That it would. I can't explain it, it's like fate. I never believed in the thing but I guess I do since then. Some things are inevitable. And maybe it's not fate, maybe it's just physics. I don't think anyone could fully explain how the sword works but I don't need that to know that it does work.

I'm not sure I even touched it, to be honest. I think it knew I was going to and instead of letting me do that, it told me, in no uncertain terms, what would happen. It let me experience a sliver of what it was going to do to me. I think this because I've thought of trying again since then, and every time I do, and I know I'm just thinking I'm not even reaching for it, right? Every time I do think about it, it happens again. My hand on the blade and the dam bursting open. And every time it feels more vivid.

I'm writing this from the office in my house. My wife and kid are outside, arguing, nothing serious just trying to get some food in her mouth. Kid's got a royal tongue, she knows what she likes and she'll fight you for it until she literally can't shout anymore. She's awesome. They both are. God, I am sorry for what I'm doing to them and I'm sorrier that I'm not sorry enough. I'm such a selfish piece of shit. I still wouldn't do it any other way. She'll grow up without her father from now on and I did that, I brought her here just to leave her.

I just hope they know I love them. Maybe that's why I'm writing this? I want to be understood and more than anything I want them to understand that I loved them dearly and that I'm sorry. That it's not their fault I was this pathetic thing. And that they've made me so happy even when all I can do is stare at the blade above my head there is still a part of me that is filled with gratitude, and love, and happy to have lived.

I can't tell them that, though. Them or any of my friends. They'd get worried. They're not stupid, you know? They've known for some time that there was this offness to me I guess. I wouldn't want them jumping to conclusions if I started spouting what are obviously parting words.

But that would be better, wouldn't it? If they knew what was coming. Then they could prepare. It's not any less cruel to simply appear split in half some day with no warning. God, no. But I'm embarrassed.

I think that's the darndest thing about this whole ordeal. The shame. I don't even know why. It's a fucking sword inexplicably destroying my life, I don't even know what I'm ashamed of. I just am. Because it's just mine, it's only on me, only I can see it, I know it's real because I can feel it but I have no proof nothing to show for it. The worst part is I think they would believe me.

My wife certainly would. She gets this serious expression when I do something unexpected or even outright wrong, like she's analyzing. Then, after a couple seconds, she's okay with it, or she knows exactly how to tell me she isn't with the utmost respect and understanding. It's like she removes herself from the equation and is suddenly rummaging through my mind trying to see my point of view. She's like a fucking wizard. Or a diplomat. I don't know, but it's amazing. Our relationship wouldn't be nearly as strong if it wasn't for her.

So I have no idea why I can't tell her about the sword. I never have. It's the one thing I can't tell her. I think it hurts more knowing that she'd try to be there for me through it all. She'd go on a warpath to try and fix it. But she wouldn't be able to. I never want her to feel like it's her fault, you know? That she didn't do enough or she failed me. Maybe if she never knows until the end she won't have to. There's nothing she could have done because I never told her, end of story.

I don't actually have much time left. Today's my due date. The thread holding the sword began to visibly deteriorate over time. Sometimes I know it'll be worse before I even look. And now it's just the one thread, every other one having snapped. I can feel the sword falling even though it hasn't moved. The recurring waking nightmare of the gash in my hand is happening more and more often. Like contractions.

I think I'll end the post here, I really don't know if I'm gonna make it past the next five minutes. If anyone I know is reading this, know two things: I love you, and I'm sorry.
