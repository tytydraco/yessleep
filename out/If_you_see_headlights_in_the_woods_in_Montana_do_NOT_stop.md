My wife and I just moved into a new house in Montana. It's beautiful and quiet. Very remote. The house was foreclosed on because the owners vanished and stopped paying the mortgage last summer, so we got it for a good price. Unfortunately, that means there's the added stress of having to clear out their things as well. When we started moving stuff out of the basement, we came across a desk belonging to the previous owner. A stack of handwritten papers that seem to be either some kind of fiction, hopefully, or someone's final account. Should we turn around and put this back on the market? This really freaked us out. I'll share it with you all so you can tell me what you think:

"I'm alone and ive been in my basement for 2 days. I know it's outside. I'm too scared to leave. 3 days ago, I was returning from a trip to my friends family lake house. It was sometime after 1am, somewhere along highway 2, for the first time in hours, I saw headlights. As I got closer, I noticed they were  beaming from in the woods, not the side of the road. The forest is entirely too thick here for someone to have driven into there. I pulled over and reached into the backseat to grab my flashlight. I walked up to the edge of the treeline and clicked the light on. I beamed it out towards them and yelled out to ask if anyone was hurt or in need of assistance. All I could hear was giggling. Maybe 3 people? No actual response even as I asked again. It freaked me right the hell out and I ran back to the car. It made no sense. I called the police to let them know what happened, and they assured me someone would be out there as soon as possible to check it out. 

There was a freak winter storm about to sweep through in the middle of August so I worried about those folks in the woods. It's why my trip got cut short, and I'm here now at home. Trapped. Trapped under snow and with that fucking thing in my house. Whatever it is. 

About 3am I pulled into the long lane leading up to house. When I finally got to the house, there it was. Headlights. Beaming at me from inside the woods behind the house. My stomach felt like a pit of sand. I sat in my car with my headlights pointed at theirs for a few minutes. I was scared to get out of the car. Then they were gone. I ran into the house and turned on the back porch lights, which reached the treeline. I watched through the blinds for a bit. It started to snow. 

I must have dozed off watching the woods. It was daylight now. The snow had stopped for now. There were tracks leading up to the house from the woods. I could feel panic rising. I ran to each door, checking windows as I passed them. All still locked. The tracks came right up to the window I was watching from that night. As I checked the other windows and doors I noticed the tracks encircled the house. These were bizarre tracks. It looked like several different animal prints, hundreds of tiny indentions, and a wide singular path like something large was being dragged. 

I went upstairs to my room at this point. Maybe the elevation would give me an advantage in spotting whatever it was. I grabbed the phone, intending to call the police, wildlife rangers, whoever I could get. The storm had knocked out the phones. Of course. At least I still had power. As night fell, I double checked the locks again on all the doors and windows. Once it had gotten dark, I went to go turn the back light on again. There was already light shining out towards the woods. It was coming from above the house. Those lights turned off suddenly and I could hear what sounded like several people tumbling simultaneously down the roof. Then silence. Dead absolute nothing silence. I flicked the back light on and ran back up stairs. I closed my bedroom door and locked it behind me. I could hear giggling outside, periodically overwhelmed by deep groaning and wailing. Like a room full of people in extreme pain. I backed myself into the corner of the room across from the door with the old baseball bat I had from when I played in school. Probably useless but it made me feel better. 

Someone knocked on the front door. Loudly. Repeatedly. In steady repetition, about half a second apart. Bang bang bang bang bang bang, it kept going for like 30 seconds and then stopped. There's not another house for miles. Who the fuck is this?! I wanted to get a look. I slowly approached corners, looking before turning them. Down the stairs, at the front door, bright lights beaming into the house. The knocking started again, the screaming and laughter, then the lights vanished, and the knocking stopped. I could hear skittering and thumping quickly across the snow out front, and around the side of the house. Right in front of the door, was a pile of dead insects. There was a trail of them in the same direction as the tracks around the side of the house. 

I have a stocked gun closet I inherited from my father after his passing down here in the basement. At the time, I thought that was the right call. A bat wasn't going to cut it. I decided I was gonna make a run for the cellar doors outside. Once I got down here I could lock those and wait out the storm, armed, til the phone lines came back up and I could call for help. 

I needed to get to the back door. The snow started again. Heavy snow. The cellar is right outside, and the awning had prevented the snow from covering it so far but it wouldn't stay that way long. I rounded the corner to the kitchen, only the game room on the other side between me and the back door. The knocking started again, this time on the door to the game room right in front of me. It stopped me dead in my tracks. My heart pounding, I ran behind me to lock the door to the kitchen. It looked like the hinges were going to give as the steady pounding rattled the game room door. It stopped. I waited, frozen. Unsure what to do. Scared shitless. BANG BANG BANG BANG BANG on the door I had just locked behind me. I ran for the game room.

Exactly as I opened the door the knocking stopped. The back door was right there. I sprinted toward it and pulled it open. As I turned around to pull it closed, I could see in the furthest dark corner, a writhing mass of shadowy shapes. Indescribable. Howling, screaming, and pleading. To the left, a face peaked out from the kitchen at me, only the top half of the face visible. Searing bright lights where the eyes should be. Insects flittering in the beams. It giggled and disappeared behind the wall in a sudden jerky movement. 

I flung the cellar doors open, ran in, and slid the giant bar in place to lock them behind me.  I loaded the 12 gauge with buck shot and pulled a chair up to face the stairs. If that fucking thing gets in I'm going to at least go down fighting. After about an hour undisturbed, I turned and headed for the couch to try to get some sleep. Right as I was about to drift off, banging. Light coming through the crack between the door. That horrid laughter.

Every few hours it happens. I can't sleep. The fridge down here has a decent amount of food, water, and beer. This storm can't be over soon enough. If something happens to me before the lines are back up, I'm leaving this for someone to find. Please contact my mom at 406-###-#### and tell her I love her and I'm sorry. My name is Sean."
