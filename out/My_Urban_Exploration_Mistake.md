Three years ago on Christmas morning, my parents got me a GoPro. For the first couple weeks of having it, I mounted it to my bike helmet and recorded some stunts at the park. I’d post it to YouTube, where I might get ten views each video, knowing it was mainly my cousins, brother, and a couple friends seeing them. One day my best friend, Jack, came up to me and showed me some videos that some channels would post where they’d use their GoPro or other types of cameras to walk around in abandoned buildings. Urban Exploring channels. This piqued my interest immediately. 

Three years later, my friends and I had over 100 thousand subscribers on our shared channel, and we had explored so many abandoned properties across our state. The farthest one we went to was seven hours across the state: an abandoned house that looked kinda like a castle. Things had been going well till we decided to explore a fucking church. 

I woke up late this morning, my head still hurting from the party last night. My mother texted me that her and my dad would be going out to see my aunt. My brother went to work. Walking into the kitchen, I liked to my left and snatched a box of cereal, eating it dry from the box. My phone buzzed, and I saw the text from Jack, saying that we should explore the abandoned church in downtown. 

“No. I was raised Catholic” I texted. 

“So was I. Can be a first for the channel” replied Jack. 

“No!” I quickly texted back. 

Jack’s speech bubble briefly popped up, then went away. I put my phone down, only to pick it up and read this text from Jack: “Pussy”. I tossed my phone aside, put the cereal down, and took a swig from the milk gallon in the fridge. I washed up, changed, then sat down and turned on the television. My phone buzzed again, and it was Jack: 

“You’re not a pussy, but come on man.” 

“That church closed cuz of some weird shit!” I texted, getting annoyed. 

“Rumors bro. Let’s go to the mayor and ask him himself.” 

I stared at my phone. All of this for a fucking YouTube video. “Jack, fuck off.” 

“Dude he isn’t doing anything, anyway. Let’s go and ask!” 

I had a whole, annoyed speech I was typing out. But to put this shit to rest, I typed: “Fine, be here by 11. I’ll drive us.” 

Jack came promptly at 11, probably the first instance that he was ever on time for something, and we drove to city hall and asked to speak with the mayor. The woman told us to wait, and that he’d be free at 12. 

We sat down in the waiting room, and Jack looked at me and said, “Small town perks, I guess. What mayor in those big ol’ cities would have time for us, huh?” 

I looked at Jack, angry, and said “All of this for a fucking video.” 

Forty minutes passed, and we got called into the office. We greeted the mayor and thanked him for taking the time to talk to us.

“Oh, of course I can make time for two local internet celebrities. Now, what can I help ya with?” 

Jack looked at me, as if he was unsure what to say. This was all his fuckin’ idea. I rolled my eyes and started: “Well, sir, we wanted to know about the church downtown. Why’d it close?” 

The mayor laughed and twiddled his thumbs. “Well, I’m sure you guys heard tons of rumors… but it’s a boring reason. Just some permits expiring!”

Jack said “C’mon, we’re not dumb little kids! Permits, really?” 

The mayor, with the smile fading from his face, said: “Yeah, really. Is this all you wanted?” 

Jack looked at me, then back at the mayor, and blurted out: “Yeah, kinda…” 

We were sent on our way after a stern lecture about wasting professional people’s time and trespassing. Whole mood shift after calling us local celebrities. Anyway, none of this deterred Jack, and it also started to amp me up. The mayor was rude, this was our YouTube channel, and the fucking place was abandoned. We wore the right gear, didn’t vandalize anything, and thought it’d be interesting. Who the fuck were we really hurting?

The next weekend, Sunday to be exact, Jack wanted to go to the church. Our other two friends backed out, saying they had things to do, and didn’t wanna touch the creepy place. I called bullshit, but still this didn’t deter me. Jack and I met in front of the Church at 12 am Sunday morning. The doors were chained shut, but one of the windows were broken. Not wanting to be cut by broken glass and God knows what else, we broke a little more of it and snuck through the bottom frame. As we landed a few feet down on the ground, we looked around and saw busted up pews, broken statues of the saints, and ripped up Bibles. 

As we walked in a little further, a faint, red light was emanating from the altar. Ten robed figures walked to the altar. One of them stood at the front of the group and began talking, his voice booming and echoing throughout the dusty church: 

“INTRUDERS!” 

I tapped my friend Jack and tried pulling him away, but he wouldn’t budge, like a fucking deer in headlights. Two of the robed figures came running at us and pinned us down. Their brown robes scratched against my skin as I tried struggling out, but the men began punching down on us, making me weak. My vision got blurry for a bit, and I stopped. The man who pointed us out came into the light, his robe was bright red. I guess this was the leader of whatever this shit was. He reached down to my chest and ripped the GoPro off of the attachment I had around my chest, then slammed it on the floor and crushed it with his foot. 

As I lay down on the floor feeling lightheaded, I looked over at Jack. His eyes were closed and his nose was bleeding. The two robed men picked us up and carried us down some stairs into a dimly lit basement, then they threw us on the floor. The man in the red robe, followed by the rest of them, beckoned one of the men to come forward. The red robed man pulled the man’s brown robe off, exposing not only his face but his upper body. His right arm had a tattoo of an eye within a pentagram with a sword going through it.

“M-mayor?” I slurred, still feeling lightheaded from the punches. 

The man in the red robe stepped forward and smacked me, leaving a stinging, burning feeling on my cheek. He then stepped back and said “Your indiscretion will be costly. You told us these two visited your office inquiring about this church, but you scared them off. I assume your sternness and the efficacy of your tirade were overstated, yes?”

The mayor, with tears streaming down his face nodded. The man in the red robe snapped his fingers, and one of the other men ran to a cabinet and pulled out a handle with short, sharp, metal chains attached to it. The man with the red robe grabbed it and turned to the mayor, who was on his knees. 

“The members of The Group of the Holy Sword dedicated their lives to fight against the debauchery this country is facing. Free will has allowed Satan to perverse our people, poison our children, and allow un-holiness to become prevalent. Awful, third-world garbage pushed as normalcy, as virtue!”

What the fuck was this man talking about? We stumbled upon a fucking cult, with the mayor being a part of it. I don’t know how if I’ll survive this. 

“Self-flagellation isn’t enough for your misdeed, ‘Mister Mayor’,” continued the red robed man. He lifted up the whip in his hand and struck the mayor, the steel spikes and chains cutting the mayor’s skin, causing blood and bits of skin to splatter on the floor. The mayor screamed in agony and arched his back. The red robed man lifted his arm again and struck the mayor multiple times, with the fourth strike getting caught in the mayor’s back. As the red robe man pulled the spikes out of the mayor’s back, more blood splattered along the floor. The mayor’s scream went silent as he passed out. The red robed man dropped the chains, and two of the robed men picked up the mayor and carried him off. 

As the red robed man had his back to us, Jack looked at me and winked. He stood up and punched one of the robed men, knocking him back. I pushed the other one off of me and we made a run for the stairs. I made the mistake of looking back, and saw the red robed man pull a gun out and begin chasing us. 

“Run all you can, but we know you!” shouted the man. “We know you and your families. You’ve made a mistake coming here!” The man screamed over the shot of his gun, with the bullet whizzing over our heads, causing a ringing in our ears. “We’ve grown to infect your local politics and will continue to grow!” Another shot rang out, and Jack fell forward, a hole leaked blood from the back of his chest. 

The exit came into view, and I ran into the night. My new life began, all over a fucking video.
