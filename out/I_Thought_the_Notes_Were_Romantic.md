I met Chris a few weeks ago. Sometimes we spent time at his place, sometimes at mine, and sometimes we stayed at our own homes. Every time he left, he would leave me a note somewhere. I remember the first one. It said:

“ I LIKE WATCHING YOU WHEN YOU SLEEP.”

I thought it was incredibly romantic. I don't remember most of the other notes. I only started remembering them when things began to get worse. At first, I didn't see any connection at all.

Just like the first note, I remember the one that made everything start to feel strange.

“DANCE IN THE MIDDLE OF THE LIVING ROOM.”

I laughed and threw the piece of paper away. The next morning, I woke up to the strong smell of my broken perfume. I had no idea how it could have happened, but I soon forgot about it.

The next note, when Chris left, said:

“PLAY YOUR FAVORITE SONG.”

I didn't have time to think about it at all. I was in a hurry to get to work, so I threw the note away and left the house. When I woke up the next day, I had bruises on my arm. I had no idea where I could have hit myself, but even stranger was the fact that the bruises looked as if someone had made them with their fingers.

Chris was away on a business trip, so he didn't come to see me until several days later. We made up for the time apart that evening.

When he left the next morning, I found another note on my bed:

“PUT ON THE WHITE BLOUSE.”

I laughed, put it on, and sent him a photo. He immediately replied with several heart emojis.

Chris came over again the next evening and, as always, got up and left in the morning. When I closed the door behind him, I noticed another note:

“LIGHT CANDLES AROUND THE BED.”

I picked up my phone and texted him:

“Do you like candles?”

His reply came immediately.

“Yeah, kind of.”

“Then maybe we can light some together sometime, okay?”

“Sure,” Chris replied with a heart. “Tomorrow evening?”

“Okay.”

The next morning, my face hurt. When I looked in the mirror, it looked as if parts of my skin had been burned. I searched the internet for different kinds of rashes, but nothing looked like what I had. I made an appointment with a dermatologist, but unfortunately, the first available appointment was two weeks away.

When Chris looked at my face, he said that it really looked like a burn, but that it was probably nothing and that he was glad I had made an appointment with a doctor. He left in the morning, and once again I found another note. This time, I didn't look at it with a smile, but with anger.

“CHANGE THE BEDDING TO THE SATIN ONE.”

I picked up my phone and texted Chris:

“Why do you keep leaving me these notes??? Can't you just tell me in person?!”

A moment later, the doorbell rang. I opened the door and Chris walked in.

“Hey, Emily, I'm not leaving you any notes.”

“No? Then what's this?”

When I showed him the piece of paper, he just stared at it in confusion.

“Em, this really isn't from me.”

I could feel a cold sweat starting to cover me. Then we heard something moving around upstairs in the attic. There were footsteps, as if someone was walking back and forth.

We immediately went outside and called the police. They searched the entire house, but found nothing.

As we were leaving the attic, I noticed a note lying on the floor.

“I CAN SEE YOU.”

That evening, I packed my things and Chris took me to his place. The police put a patrol outside our homes for a few days.

The next day, after waking up at Chris's place, I went to the fridge and found a note stuck to it:

“I LIKE IT MUCH BETTER HERE.”

My heart was pounding like crazy. When Chris came into the kitchen and saw the look on my face, he asked me what was wrong. I lied and told him that nothing was wrong, that I just wasn't feeling well, and left the crumpled piece of paper in my hand.

I'm afraid that if Chris knew it hadn't ended, our relationship wouldn't survive it. Sometimes he does wonder about things I do that he doesn't understand, like why I put those dark blue wallpapers in the bedroom or why I cut my hair overnight, but I can always come up with an excuse.

Today should be fine.

Today, I'll only be wearing more noticeable lipstick.
