I went blind at a young age, some rare condition, doctor gave me medication, said if i keep taking it, there was a chance, although small, to regain my vision. 

About 20 years went by, i still take the medication, but have become accustomed to being blind, i got married to the woman i love, and we moved to a small isolated town advertised for blind people that was easier and more accessible for me, it was a nice town.

We lived there for 5 years, and over time i noticed small changes, to the people around me, and my wife, nothing huge, small changes in their voices, higher or deeper, and their personalities changing slightly, but people change, people get sick, i didnt think of it too much.

One day, when i woke up, after 20 years of taking the medication, it had started taking effect, small pinholes barely visible were in my vision, not being able to contain my excitement, i ran to the kitchen, where my wife was cooking breakfast. But whatever it was, was not my wife.

Im thankful that i froze in fear, it gave me a moment to think of what to do.

Through my pinhole eyes, i could see a thing that was not human, standing in front of the stove, was what seemed to be my wife, but standing behind her was a black thin almost mechanical thing, with two arms that were inserted into my wifes elbows, seemingly controlling her arms, it had a neck that went straight to her head, and its legs had appendages digging into hers.

My mind was racing, 5 seconds felt like 5 hours, not knowing what to do, i decided to pretend as if i was still blind.

I walk into the kitchen, my "wife" turns to me, seeing her face i could see, pale skin and her eyes never blinked, stuff i never noticed having never seen her. It talks to me, using her voice, its almost perfectly like hers.

I act as best as i can, and 2 whole days go by, my vision slowly gets better, what used to be small pinholes are now big blotches, covering almost half of my vision, i can see it almost perfectly now, its a spindly black tall figure, with tendril like things that are dug into my wife, or what was my wife, her face is almost completely white, eyes are glazed, and her skin is cold.

I couldnt stay there, i basically never leave the house, but i told it that i wanted to feel the air on my skin, but i made a mistake. 

I had remembered my cane, but while leaving, i did something i never should have, while saying goodbye i had turned my head to look at it. Something a blind person would never do.

It realised immediately, and started moving towards me, terrified i exited the house, and started to run.

But what i saw out there was much worse than what was in my house.

All the people walking the streets were the exact same, with tall spindly monsters using them as puppets, except for a few people, the only blind people in the town were going their merry way, the monsters were talking and interecting with them. And the blind people had no idea.

All i could do was run, run until i was no longer in that wretched town, the things noticed me, walked towards me, but i just kept running. They didnt seem to want to leave the borders of the town, but still, i kept running, until i reached the nearest town.

I tried to tell the police what i saw, but no one believed me, but i know what i saw. Doctors put me in a mental asylum, but at least its somewhat safe there, i cant sleep with the lights off anymore, and im scared to blink.

I only trust my eyes, but the more time i spend in this asylum, the more i think, i cant trust anything.
