This is all insane. I would never have believed it if I hadn't finally seen why they were leaving those bags in the park, night after night. I don’t know what I can do. Worse, I don’t know what will happen to those children now. They knew a terrible secret about what was there at the park, and the truth was more horrible than I expected.

I will tell you what I know about these disturbing events, and maybe you can tell me what I should do, now that I know what has been happening.

For the last two months, I have been working as a groundskeeper for the Lake County Public Parks. It’s not a bad gig, just some basic maintenance work and lawn care for the two larger parks in my city. I had been laid off at my last job, and I considered myself lucky to find something with the job market as it is.

The last person in my position left without notice, so they were looking for a replacement as soon as possible. I jumped at the opportunity, and honestly, the job had been pretty good. The pay was decent, and the work, though monotonous, was not too hard. The worst I had to deal with was the occasional vandalism of the park bathrooms, mild graffiti, and people toppling garbage cans. At least I thought that would be the worst, until I saw something strange last week that started this madness.

Previously, I had been working at the park on the East side of town, but I was reassigned to the municipal park closer to downtown. It was a nice place; the city was fairly small, so it never got too much foot traffic besides on weekends or holidays. It had a large field leading to a small patch of trees, and the main attraction was a decent-sized playground for kids.

It was a bit nicer than the small park I had been working at previously. A bit more work too, since it was larger and I was assigned the task as a crew of one. At the very least, I figured it would keep me busy.

My first day has gone well, and it was starting to get dark. The park closed at dusk, so my day was almost over. I was nearly done with everything, but I still had to clear the trash from the bins in the small restroom building near the playground. When I was walking over there, I saw something odd. Two kids were still out near the playground. They didn’t seem to be playing, just sitting on a bench as if waiting for something or someone.

I considered that they might be waiting for a parent or something. The park was technically closed at that point, but I decided to let them wait until I finished with the trash, and if they stuck around, I would check up on them and see if they needed help getting home.

I set down the bag I was holding and locked the door to the restrooms. I didn’t like the policy, but we had them shut down at night, likely to prevent anyone from squatting in there or using it for whatever other things people might use a public restroom for in the middle of the night.

Just then, I heard a strange shuffling sound from behind the restroom and the shifting noise of heavy metal sliding. I moved to investigate the weird noise, and when I looked behind the restroom, I saw something strange. There was a small sewer access point behind the bathroom, and the manhole cover that normally sealed it was slightly ajar.

I thought that was odd and didn't know who could have moved it. No crews were in the area that I knew of, and no one would be working down there at that hour anyway. As I was inspecting it, I heard footsteps nearby; they were running. I raced back to the front of the restroom to see what was happening.

The park was empty now, and the two kids that had been sitting on the bench were gone. I wondered if they had been playing some kind of prank and shrugged it off as kids being kids.

Then I noticed something near the bench where they had been sitting. It was a paper bag, about the size of a grocery bag.

I sighed as I considered what it might be. Those kids had either forgotten something or had left trash or something worse there intentionally. I walked over to the bench and bent down to inspect the bag. My original instinct was just to throw it away, but I hesitated and decided to look inside to see if it was something important they had left by mistake. I regretted my decision almost immediately when I unfurled the top of the bag and was assailed by the pungent odor of raw meat.

Inside was what looked like a pound or more of raw hamburger or something else. It was hard to tell, but the meat was not in a smaller sealed package; it was just sitting in the bag directly. It had a sickly sweet smell, like it was spoiled. I groaned when I realized this was likely some type of prank after all. I considered myself lucky it wasn't a flaming bag of dog crap, at least.

I closed the bag back up and grabbed it. Then I walked back to the bathroom and tossed the foul contents in with the rest of the trash to consolidate it.

Suddenly I heard a loud banging sound. This time it sounded like someone had dropped the manhole cover. I raced around and saw the metal cover lying on the ground and the small ladder leading to the sewer fully exposed.

I was getting irritated at that point. Someone was definitely there, and they were messing with me. I pulled out my flashlight and stepped over to the sewer entrance and shone my light down. I called out,

“Hello? Is anyone there? If you kids are playing some kind of prank, it's not funny. You could get hurt down there. Come on back up.” I waited in silence; no response came. I felt an icy chill blow past me, and I heard branches rustling in the distance.

After waiting a minute and seeing nothing, I shrugged and decided I would not take the bait. I resolved to just finish up and go home. I walked back to the garbage cans and was shocked when I saw the large bag I had placed everything in was torn open. There was trash everywhere, and the small paper bag I had just put in there was torn open. The rotten meat was completely gone, and all that was left was a small patch of strange coloured liquid where it had been and a rotten stench that seemed worse than the spoiled meat that had been in the bag.

I figured that some scavengers, like a raccoon or coyote, had gotten at it while I was inspecting the sewer behind the building. I pulled my shirt up over my nose and continued with my tasks. I got a new bag, scraped up the rest of the mess, and took it all to the dumpster near the maintenance building. Then I went home for the night. By itself, I could have ignored the strange event as a one-off, but it happened again the next day.

My second day at the park was busier than the first. The larger field needed mowing, and most of my morning and afternoon were spent tending to it. There were also two messy incidents in the restrooms, which were rather unpleasant to deal with.

I was tired, but it felt normal at least.

Just as twilight descended, I noticed a small gathering of kids waiting near the street. They were not technically in the park, so I didn’t think much of it at first. But as it got darker, I noticed they stayed together and seemed to be waiting just on the outskirts.

I tried to ignore it until I saw one of them holding a large bag with both hands.

They were further away than last time, so I couldn't get a good look, but after last night it seemed too much of a coincidence that more kids would be there with a bag like that.

As it got darker outside, the kids still lingered on the edge of the park. They were talking to each other, and they would occasionally steal a glance over at me and the park. I was feeling very suspicious. I had to get some bag liners from the maintenance building, but I was reluctant to leave them out of my sight.

I hurried over to the building and grabbed what I had come for quickly. When I returned, two of the kids were still waiting on the street, but the others had vanished.

Before I could consider what happened, I heard a loud metallic sound and knew the manhole cover had been moved again. I ran towards it, intent on catching them in the act. When I got there, I found a bag sitting near the opening.

I heard footsteps racing away and saw one of the kids sprinting.

I called out,

“Hey! Stop!”

But it was in vain; they were too fast and had fled out of reach. I shrugged and gave up the chase, content that they had left. I started running back, wondering what I would find, hoping it was not another trick.

When I returned, I saw the bag was gone, and the cover was closed. The same strange smear of foul-smelling liquid remained. I had no idea what these kids were doing, but I didn't like it.

The next morning I called the Park manager, Dale. I explained the situation to him, and he was sympathetic but admitted there was not a lot we could do, other than put some signs up and issue a verbal warning about trespassing. He went on a small tirade about how parents need to be paying attention to what their kids are doing at such hours of the night. But he was otherwise unhelpful.

Without any direct assistance besides an extra sign to put up, I realized I would have to wait and see if it happened again. I resolved to try and speak with one of the kids if I could catch them and see just what was going on with this continuous prank they seemed to be a part of.

I went about my daily duties as usual. As it got closer to nighttime, I started looking for any signs of kids lingering near the park or holding any strange bags. To my surprise, no one showed up. I wondered if they might have gotten bored by the gag and decided to leave it alone for now. I breathed a sigh of relief and resumed my normal routine.

I had to run to the maintenance shed to grab some things I had forgotten before I finished my work. While I was over there, I heard a loud metallic clanging sound and realized I was not as lucky as I thought I had been.

I rushed back over to the bathroom expecting to see kids dispersing and a bag. To my surprise, there was nothing there. I looked around and found no trace, no footprints. But I did smell that same rancid odor I had before. I moved to close the manhole cover again when I heard a scratching sound that made me pause. It was terrible, like nails on a chalkboard, and after the initial shock at the screeching sound, I realized something was happening in the bathroom. I grabbed my flashlight and rushed inside. I tried to hit the light switch, but the overhead light would not turn on.

I switched on my flashlight and looked inside. I stepped further inside and called out,

“If anyone is in here, you have to go; we are closing up.”

There was no response. I looked at the ground visible beneath each stall and did not see anyone's feet. I considered that they might have fled, but I felt a disturbing chill run down my spine. It felt like I was not alone in there.

If one of them was hiding, I had to find out, so I went from stall to stall, pressing the door open to check if anyone was hiding. Each one was vacant, and when I checked the last stall, I saw no one was there. I relaxed a bit, satisfied that I was alone.

Just as I was breathing a sigh of relief, I heard the scratching sound again. I jumped back out of the stall and looked at the exit, but no one was there. I heard movement, but I had no idea where they could have been or how they could have escaped without me noticing.

I was ready to rush out and try to give chase to the elusive intruder, but something on the ground made me pause. It was a small trail of putrid-smelling liquid that had not been there when I had entered earlier. I bent down to inspect it when I felt something drop down on the back of my neck. I wiped it off with my hand, and I recoiled when I realized it was the same foul liquid that had been on the ground.

I instinctively looked up to check, and I saw on the ceiling a large, bubbling mass of the foul substance, slowly dripping down onto the ground. I was shocked and disgusted. It looked like some type of leak, but I had no idea what could be leaking from the ceiling that was so disgusting. Then something else caught my eye. I trained my flashlight on the area near the damp ceiling, and saw two large slashes that seemed to be nearly three feet across. I had no idea how someone could have cut the ceiling so badly, or why. I also had no idea if the terrible liquid was a potential biohazard, for that matter.

Since it was late and I was ill-equipped to deal with it, I locked down the bathroom and put up the out-of-order sign. I decided to call Dale tomorrow and explain what happened.

I finished closing things up and started for the parking lot. I was troubled by the strange vandalism, but as I walked, I began to get that same creeping feeling again. I still had that sensation like I was being watched. I picked up the pace and rushed to my car. I was satisfied once I was inside and the doors were locked. I started the engine and began driving away. In my rearview mirror, I saw the trees shaking near the field, and at first I didn’t think anything of it and chalked it up to the wind. Then, later, when I rolled down my window while stopped at a red light, I had second thoughts. The night was deathly still outside. Whatever was out there in the field couldn't have been the wind…

The next day I woke up early and called Dale. I explained the situation, and he agreed to contact the police and see about getting some hazmat disposal for whatever that caustic substance was. I mentioned the fact that the nearby manhole cover had been tampered with and someone had been coming or going from the sewer entrance. They inspected it, and as an extra precaution, they fixed the cover with a special lock so that it could not be moved by anyone other than regulated personnel. I was still surprised that kids had managed the heavy cover by themselves at all, if it had been them. Either way, I was glad that the lock would prevent whoever was sneaking around from using the sewer again.

As the team worked, I was attending to my own tasks. I overheard one of them mentioning that a person had been killed near where they lived last night. Someone had been found, seemingly torn apart. The scene was terrible, and they mentioned that a lot of the local police were investigating, which was why the small vandalism response to the park was so minimal.

The description of the killing was awful, but soon the cleaners resumed their duties, and I couldn’t hear anything else about what had happened just a few blocks away last night. I tried to ignore it, but the story and its details troubled me.

The team had cleared things up and finished around late afternoon. They found no actual hazardous material, just that foul-smelling liquid that had mostly dripped onto the bathroom floor. They could not identify it without a lab test, but it didn't seem to exhibit any signs of being harmful. The cuts in the ceiling were still inexplicable, but they shrugged and admitted they were not sent to investigate ceiling damage. Their only job was to clean any hazardous materials. I thanked them, and they left without another word.

As the day dragged on, I felt more anxious about whatever might happen later that evening. Part of me wanted to just call out and tell Dale I was sick, but another part needed to see what was going to happen.

It was getting darker, and the park had cleared out. There were no stragglers and no kids waiting nearby. I wondered if it was going to be another repeat of the day before. I decided that I would stake out the area by the bathroom. I took some yard tools into the field and pretended to busy myself with work out there, but I slowly slipped into the tree line and watched the area behind the bathroom. If anyone tried to leave something there, I would know.

I was waiting for over half an hour, and I started to feel silly. They had not come last night; I should have figured they were done with whatever game they were playing. But just as I was about to give up and start heading back, I saw them. Two kids, one holding a large bag, the other nervously looking around, probably checking to make sure I was not there.

I waited for them to get closer, and they crept toward the bathroom building.

I moved as quietly as I could to intercept, but once I was within a few yards, the lookout noticed me and pulled on their friend's arm as they were bending down to set the large bag next to the manhole cover.

I called out to them,

“Stop! Whatever that is, you can’t leave it here!”

They started to run, and I gave chase. I got closer and managed to catch one of them. I had no authority to hold them, and I didn’t want to hurt them, so once they stopped running, I let go.

The kid turned around, and I saw he was a twelve- or thirteen-year-old boy. He had dirty blonde hair and looked up at me with a mixture of fear and guilt.

I took a moment to catch my breath and asked him,

“What are you doing out here?” He looked nervously at me, then back over his shoulder at the figure standing in the distance, which must have been his friend.

He swallowed hard and then replied,

“Trying to help you.”

I was confused by the response, but before I could say anything, I heard a terrible banging sound coming from back near the bathroom. It felt like the ground itself was shaking, and to my horror, I realized the sound was coming from the locked manhole cover. Something was bashing, scratching, and smashing against the locked cover from underneath it!

I looked back at the kid, and his eyes were wide with fear. He started to run, carrying the large bag with him. He stumbled briefly and dropped the bag, and it toppled over. I looked down at the spilled contents and felt sick. Blood leaked from the crumpled edges of the bag and from inside, where various animal carcasses lay. It looked like they had collected and assembled a small horde of roadkill. I even saw an opossum that had tire tracks on its flattened back.

I was so confused about why these kids had been carrying around a bag of animal carcasses.

Then the crashing sound reminded me of the more imminent threat of whoever, or whatever, was trying to breach the manhole cover. I let the kids go and fumbled for my phone to call the police, but to my horror I realized my phone was still charging in the maintenance shed.

I had no idea what was going on, but I resolved to get the hell out of there as fast as I could. I started to run for the parking lot. I thought if I moved fast I would be safe. I was wrong. As I made it about halfway there, the thundering, bashing sound finally gave way to the heavy crunch. Then, after a slight delay, the flying manhole cover finally smashed back on the ground.

I shuddered when I considered just what could have thrown the heavy cover with such force. I kept running until I heard a thundering tread behind me, followed by a loud rumbling growl and heavy breathing. It was getting closer. With every step, I heard the splashing sound of hideous ichor trailing onto the ground.

Then I tripped and nearly fell down. I caught myself and froze for just a moment, almost considering looking back at my pursuer. I started running again in mad desperation. I was about fifty feet from my car when I was struck from behind and fell on my face. An impossibly heavy pressure was pinning me to the ground. I felt hot breath on my neck, and more of the foul liquid splashed down around my prone form and onto the back of my head.

I thought I was dead, but I heard muffled speech just then; someone was…talking to it.

Then I heard small footsteps running and the rustle of a bag. The pressure on my back was lifted; I refused to let go of the breath I had been holding until I was sure I was safe.

I heard the dim sound of a bag being torn open and the slavering, chewing, tearing sound of something feasting on the contents. Then I heard the rumbling footsteps trail off into the distance, and the heavy sound of a broken iron cover being loosely replaced. I knew that whatever that thing was, it had retreated back into the sewer.

When I finally summoned the courage to stand back up, I saw the two kids from earlier standing by me. They were pale with fear, and one of their hands was bloody. They looked at me as if checking to see I was alright. I could barely think of what I could say; all I was able to get out was,

“What was that thing? What just happened?”

The young boy who had been holding the bag spoke,

“It was the boogeyman; we saved you from him.”

He spoke in a hushed whisper; the other kid, a similarly aged girl with dark hair and barely suppressed fear in her eyes, spoke next,

“It comes out at night. It said it would eat our parents if we didn't feed it. It has...gotten people before. It ate the previous groundkeeper here at the park. If we don’t feed it, it…hunts.”

I could barely process what I was hearing; the kids had not been pulling some prank; they had been bringing raw meat here and leaving it to satiate the hunger of some monster who lived in the sewers.

I considered my predecessor, who had stopped showing up for work. They claimed he had been eaten. Then I considered the man who had been torn apart last night. There had been no bag last night...

I realized whatever it was might have been hunting me too, until it found different prey. I shuddered when I considered just how close I had come to being a meal to that nightmare.

The two kids continued staring at me, and I finally responded,

“Thank you both. I have no idea how this is happening, but you two were very brave.” They seemed to appreciate the regard, and I started walking toward the maintenance shed to get my phone. The young boy called after me,

“What are you going to do now? You have seen it, you know it's real.”

I turned back to regard them,

“I’m sorry. I wish I could tell you what we should do, but whatever that thing is has to be stopped. We can’t just keep...appeasing it. You two should go home. I don’t know what I’m going to do exactly, but I promise I will take it from here. Stay away and stay safe until I do.”

They both nodded and ran back home. I hoped that I would be able to keep my word.

That was last night. I need to do something about this thing now. Whatever it is, it’s intelligent, brutal, and terribly strong. I need to find some way to stop it before it kills again. Those kids bought me time; they stalled it, but it's still out there, still hungering, and if we don't stop it, who knows who will become its next meal…
