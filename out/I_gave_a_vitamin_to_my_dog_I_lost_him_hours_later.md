Dogs Are Human is a company that sells vitamins for dogs. Founded in 2022, it sells its product to people around the country. The product I received three months ago was nothing like advertised.

It all started when I saw those ads on TikTok. It would show old slow dogs, some unable to walk and others just moping around. But then they gave the dogs a little vitamin treat, suddenly the dogs look young and energetic. Then, I would look over to my dog, my eighteen year old basset hound Bobby Jones. He was definitely past his prime. Small things like not being able to jump up onto the bed, or the fact he no longer chased after those squirrels bothered me. The thought of losing my best friend devastated me, and I was determined to try anything, including those vitamins.

I perused the website, simple with only two options available, beef or chicken. Bobby preferred beef so that's what I got him. When I went to check out, a little pop-up caught my eye.

"Don't want to let them go? Add our new extra strength specialty vitamins for $10.99!" I shrugged and clicked 'add to cart.'

I sat back and looked over at Bobby lying on his dog bed. He looked concerned or maybe sad? I shook it off thinking I just saw emotion in my dog that wasn't there and went back to my computer.

Two weeks later the packages came. Two separate packages, one obviously from Dogs Are Human, in their normal white packaging with their company name on it. The other was different, a large brown box sat on my porch, the cardboard was slightly damp on the bottom and the thing looked old and weathered. I brought them both inside. Excitedly I went about opening them, again one was perfectly normal but the other...

It sat on my counter, large letters spelled "YeS HuMan" and "SPeCiaL ReCiPe" in weird blocky text. When I opened it, it made a loud suction noise like opening something vacuum sealed. And then the smell, oh god it smelled terrible, like petrified rotted pork. Before I sealed it I caught a glimpse of the horrible black sludge and I almost thought I saw it squirm. I tossed it aside, obviously it had gone bad at some point. After wiping my watering eyes I saw Bobby, wagging his tail and salivating.

"Hey Bob you want a treat?"

Bobby made a tiny bark in approval and walked over. I opened up the normal vitamins and gave him one, he seemed pleased enough and trudged back to his bed. After a moment I noticed a slimy texture on my foot. Looking down I saw the black fleshy sludge on my leg and foot, even though it was only a few specks the small amount permeated a putrid stench. In a panic I ran to the bathroom and jumped in the shower. I scrubbed and scrubbed, so hard my skin turned red.

Even after half an hour in the steamy shower I still couldn't get the foul scent off. That's when I realized the smell wasn't coming from me, it was wafting in from the kitchen. Questioning whether I had sealed the cursed substance well enough, I grabbed a towel and ran towards the kitchen. What hit me first was the smell. Even stronger now it was like running into a brick wall made of feces. I pushed through with watery eyes and came upon a horrific scene.

The floors and walls were smothered in the black sludge, bits of it dripped from the ceiling. A dark trail of particularly thick ooze led over to Bobby. He lay there on his side, whimpering in pain, black ooze leaked from his mouth. Snapping out of the utter shock I went to grab Bobby. In my haste I slipped, landing on my back with a sickening splat.

I awoke with a gasp, blood coating my mouth. Getting my bearings I realized hours must've passed, the sun nearly fully set outside. Inside was a grim scene, the black ooze had congealed into a hard plaster. I tried getting up only to find I was stuck to the ground, the ooze acting like a giant fly trap keeping me glued to the floor. My eyes darted in their sockets, panic rising, blood trickling out of one of my ears.

"Oh shit, oh fuck, I'm going to die here, naked, stuck to my god damn kitchen floor."

That's when I felt a wet tongue lick my leg.

"Bobby is that you? Thank goodness you're alright, come up here so I can see you."

"BrrrMffph." A wet gurgly huff came from Bobby as he slowly trudged up to me. He looked... different. Hair was falling off him in clumps, bare pink skin shining through the gaps. His nose seemed smaller too.

"Bobby?" I felt light headed, like I was about to pass out again. Bobby's eyes bore into me with concern. The last thing I saw before drifting off was Bobby's paws. They looked distended and awkward and they made a horrific crunching sound as he dragged himself towards me.

I jolted awake, cold darkness surrounded me. "Owrooogph." Bobby bellowed, followed by the sound of bones snapping. The sound was so awful and loud that I nearly choked on some vomit. More groans and barks pierced my soul, tears filled my eyes hearing my dearest friend be... attacked? Shit, maybe a bear wandered in.

"HEY! LEAVE HIM ALONE! GET!" I screamed through a shaky voice. "LEAVE HIM BE!...please..." I coughed up blood and bile.

A heavy silence filled the room. I sobbed for my friend. Then *SPLAT* a wet noise *SPLAT* it sounded like steps approaching. *SPLAT* I prayed for a quick death *SPLAT* it was right next to my head.

"f..FaathhEER." The voice was deep and full of phlegm. "I..I..It is Okkkay now."

"What!? Who's there?! Please help me I'm stuck!"

"Y..Y..Yes." The voice sounded pained.

Two clawed hands dug under the black ooze, leaving chunks of skin still stuck to the floor. The person lifted me from my dark prison and began carrying me out of the house. Blood wept from the new wounds on my back and a now familiar feeling of unconsciousness began flooding over me. The last thing I saw was a tall dark silhouette of a man, and I could've sworn that in the dim moonlight I saw two floppy ears on the man's head.

I now write this from my hospital bed. They say someone left me at the front door. God, what just transpired? All I know is I lay here crying over the loss of my best friend, Bobby Jones.
