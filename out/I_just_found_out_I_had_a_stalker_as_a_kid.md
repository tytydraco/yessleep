Hi Reddit.

I am the eldest daughter in a family of seven, 4 siblings and 2 parents. When this first started, however, I was fresh out of the womb.

I was a baby, only a few months old, and my parents had left me with my paternal grandmother for the day as both of them had to work full time jobs, my mom on 12 hour shifts at the hospital and my dad in whatever odd job he had at the time. Two young parents trying to make ends meet.

Anyway, they come back and my grandparents aren't there and neither am I. My parents don't panic at first, they didn't really have much reason to at this point, so they just give my grandmother a call. Well, the call went batshit crazy really fast. My grandmother refuses to give me back to them, and the scariest game of phone tag they have ever played ensues. The police are never involved, they get me back, the details I was given were murky at best. Regardless, I am back with my parents within a few hours and they decide at that point to cut my grandmother off (as far as I know my grandfather didn't have much to do with it).

My parents, too poor to pay for someone to watch me while they work, end up leaving me with my aunt (mom's sister), who just had my cousin and was on maternity leave. I am watched by her for a while until she has to go back to work too and we are then left in the care of my maternal grandmother. This becomes the normal for my life, we spend every day at grandma's house even when we started school. However, something lurked in the shadows that I was never aware of, and maybe that was a blessing. My paternal grandmother began stalking me once she found out that I was being taken care of by my maternal grandmother everyday, from what I understanding it was jealousy fueled, she wanted a daughter so bad. My dad had been her second child, but her first child, her daughter, was reckless and didn't stick around for long before leaving everyone in the dust. My dad didn't even have much of a relationship with her at all.

At this point, my adult brain was making connections I never made before because I had *no idea* that any of this was happening. I was just a little kid. I went to a little private school until about 4th grade, and almost every day at recess, I would see my paternal grandmother walk past, she wouldn't wait for me to see her, but she stared at me the whole time and just keep walking, sometimes passing by more than once. At the time I didn't see anything strange about this, its a public sidewalk, people walked by all the time. However, my child self also didn't understand the fact that my grandmother lived on the *other side of town* and taking a little stroll past my school everyday exactly when I would be outside was either a drive or a multiple hour trip on foot. She also lived in a really nice part of town that had ample sidewalks that were much better for walking on. The stalking had been happening for many years at this point, my maternal grandmother would run into her when she had me and my cousins in her care a lot more than would be normal, so much so that she developed an intense fear of her and she was afraid she would try and take me.

On top of all of this, my parents would go to monthly counseling meetings with my paternal grandparents to try and work out whatever it was that was going on. They had an intense hatred for my mother that was spread out to the rest of my dad's side of the family, and I had no clue until I was in high school and we started seeing them more.

I suppose it may not be overly creepy because nothing super intense happened, but it creeps me out to know that that was happening and much worse things could have been done. It is difficult to think about how many times I may have not even been aware she was there because my grandma was really good at getting us out of places without tipping us off apparently.

I see her all the time, and its hard to believe that the frail and anxiety ridden woman I go visit with my dad could ever have done something like this in her lifetime.
