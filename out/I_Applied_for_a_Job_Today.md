As per the title, I applied for a job today. Well, truthfully, I applied to so many jobs they all blended into one. I’ve been looking for jobs all day, every day, ever since I lost my previous job to AI. I remember the day that it happened.

I finished my never-ending paperwork for the day. This office I was at was small, but it was growing. Because of confidentiality documents that’s still active, even though I don’t work there anymore, I’m not at real liberty to say so all I can offer on my previous job is that it’s in administrative healthcare. That’s all you need to know.

I don’t know if it’s a good thing, or more detrimental, but my resume is decent. I’ve worked odd jobs since my 20s but I’ve at least some admin work under my belt. Most people would go to college, then university and *usually* settle for a job in their degree, or even adjacent. With AI being as rampant as it is, though, you don’t need me to tell you just how awful it is. Just look on any social media site, or even some of the subs here on Reddit, and you can see plainly. My struggle comes from the fact I don’t have a fancy degree. It’s not that I’m ashamed that I only have Associate’s but if I mention it’s in English instead of some STEM, then I’ll get judgmental remarks. Still, I usually adapt well-enough. This economy had been extra tough, though.

I don’t know how you could replace someone in health care administration with AI, but my ex-employers found a way. I’ll fully admit to feeling betrayed though I know employers don’t owe me anything. Three years of service, only to be easily replaced in a blink of an eye. What a fucking break. I’ve been out of a job for about eight months now and I’m not any closer to getting a job. My savings are gone and I have about a little more than $100 left. Applying to retail was an option but with the amount of people vying for a job, I’ve either been rejected if I’m not outright ghosted from that as well. I did have a few interviews, though, that I haven’t been completely rejected yet. There’s hope, yet!

But I found an interesting job posting today. I’ve been feeling disheartened more and more as summer’s coming to a close. I thought I could get work at a college or even a local school, but nothing. I did *find* something though. Here’s the text of it since I wasn’t able to take a screenshot of it:

I’ve resorted to Craig’s List, after Indeed kept giving me AI “training” jobs instead of office admin, to look for jobs and there’s a reason why I’m posting here, and not another famous subreddit. It’s innocuous, right? Why would they post that? Sexism included, it’s laughable and clearly a toxic environment. From what it sounds like, though, it sounds like any other overworked and underpaid office job that I’ve been applying to, and have been ghosted by. However, my situation is something I can’t ignore. I’m at a terrible place where I’m blessed that my roommate has a decent enough job to carry our bills and food but it’s not fair to her, not by any means. I clean but I can’t cook, but at least if I could get a job – I can pay my share. I can ease the burden from her shoulders. I could even have a little spending money if I divvy up the funds just right. Even now, I still feel the same way and I’ve only *applied* to this ridiculous ad.

Against my better judgment, I applied to it.

This is such a nothingburger ad that I shouldn’t think anything of it other than another number on my job application spreadsheet. Another no for this to get closer to a yes for something else. But after I applied and emailed both this MJ and Big Al my resume, respectively, using this email that just has the first initial of my first name, and my last name. This is important to note that my name is uniquely rare. I’ve been chronically online since I was a teenager so I’m no stranger to hiding it for protection's sake, but there’s another reason that I’ll get into just a bit. However, obviously it’s unprofessional to use a silly e-mail for a job, so I used that for these types of job postings. It’s not anything new. I may only have an Associate’s in English, but I truly adore reading, writing, editing, and all those things. It’s just… well, I won’t get into it now.

I didn’t think anything else of it after I sent my resume. At this point in my job searching journey, I expect companies to put my resume into an AI whatever and spit out a social score and whether or not if that number is applicable for a glance before an interview. This is an employer’s market, after all.

Anyway, after I sent that email, I went ahead and took a break. Looking at jobs is depressing day in and day out, and I needed a reprieve. It was also lunchtime so I decided to end my search for the day and grab some grub.

My roommate and I have our own separate lives; we met through friends and she needed someone to room with her in a house that she originally bought for her and her girlfriend. She got a message from a girl she wanted to date and had to leave the house so it’s just me by myself. Even now, she’s still out and I don’t know if I want her to come home. I don’t know what I’ve done by applying to that ad.
