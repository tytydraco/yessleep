I live a relatively boring life. I wake up, I have breakfast and I go to school. I keep my head down, and when the day is through, I go home. Usually my mother welcomes me home, around four in the afternoon, and I take a nap. Every day, they'd wake me up at six for dinner, and then I'd do my homework. Everyday. That was my life.

Every single day starts the same. I wake up, and then for the next ten minutes, I wish I didn't have to. I'd then get up and have breakfast.

"How was school yesterday?" My dad asked as I sat down at the kitchen table, not even taking his eyes off the morning paper. "SATANISTS INVADING SMALL TOWNS" was the headline. I chuckled, and he looked at me, "What's so funny?" I shook my head, and he went back to reading. 

My mother sat my plate down on the table, and her long hair came loose, covering her face. "Oh, sorry," she said with a chuckle, as she fixed it. My family is, what you would call, a nuclear family. A mother, a father, a son, and a daughter. My father works at a bank. Honestly, I never really asked him what he does, since the word "bank" bores the shit out of me. My mother's just a normal housewife. Always dotting on her children. The sweet neighbour lady that takes care of everyone in the street. She always welcomes me home, and gets super pissed when I don't eat anything. So, I make sure to eat, especially when she's looking at me, like she is right now. 

My sister's...well, she's my sister. And not even at the table this morning. Pretty normal, pretty boring. The most boring family. But, we love it. We live in a nice neighbourhood, with nice neighbours, and Sunday barbeques. Once, when I was, like, ten, my father finally decided to clean out our pool, and all the neighbourhood kids came around to play.

See? It's normal.

But today, school was extra terrible. The children were loud, the teachers were angry, and the breaks were short. I did find it weird that my sister, who never skipped school, wasn't there though. Although, I forgot about it as soon as I thought about it. I just went about my day and hated every second.

When I got home around four, to my cat, with her beautiful blue eyes, pacing in a circle in the living room, it was strangely quiet. It would be a couple of hours till we had dinner, so I told my mom that I was gonna take a nap. I heard no answer, she hadn't even welcomed me home, she must've been busy. I shrugged and went upstairs. I felt a bit...off, so I looked back, and saw my cat moving towards the kitchen. I got to my room and fell asleep surprisingly easily.

I woke up after realizing that we didn't have a cat. It's dumb, I know, and that's why I tried to go back to sleep, but...it was dark in my room. And in the hallway. This isn't normal, especially this early on a summer's day. I sat up, chuckling to myself. "That fucking cat," I said to myself. Looking at the alarm clock on my nightstand, I saw that it was 8 o'clock. 8 o'clock at night. And that, for some reason, made me hungry.

Plodding down the hall in the dark, I started to wonder why all the lights were out. Not even the tv was on. That was weird. My sister was usually watching some dumb shit on tv. She's a total basic bitch. I walked downstairs, the floor creaking in the silence, and into the kitchen. The empty kitchen. I didn't even smell food. Where was everyone? I opened the refrigerator door and looked for something to eat. Nothing. Weird. I guess they maybe went out without me. But they could've at least woken me up. I closed the refrigerator door, and got startled. That fucking cat. Her blue eyes blinked as she purred. I smiled at my stupidity and walked around the kitchen. Something smelled...weird. I recognized the smell, but I couldn't put my finger on it. I flipped on the light, and felt something wet...

Blood.

Blood slowly dripping down the wall. Blood congealing on the roof. Blood covering the entirety of the kitchen floor. I looked up, and saw my mother's head, sans body, on the kitchen island, her face warped with fear. I backed away, choking down my body's attempts at vomiting, when I slipped in all the blood. I hit my head on the floor, and as I turned my head, I saw my dad's body, or whatever the fuck was left of it. He had been torn to bits. I sat there, in all the blood, frozen with fear. I tried to close my eyes, but it felt like my eyelids were glued open. All I could do was see. In that moment, I wished I was blind.

I felt something squish under my hand, and that's when I threw up. All over my hand, and whatever was under it. Tears streaming down my face and my throat hurting from vomiting, I somehow got up. My vision was blurry from the tears, as I made my way out of the kitchen, and up the stairs to my sister's room. I slipped and hit my shin on a step as I rushed. I hobbled to her door, and found it unlocked. For some reason, I sat down on her bed and looked around. I told myself to breathe, and I tried to calm down. "This is all a dream," I told myself. "If it wasn't, then I'd also be dead." I kept saying that over and over, for what felt like hours. Finally, I stood up and, forgetting that I hurt my leg, fell face down on the floor.

I came to with the taste of blood in my mouth.

Honestly, an odd thing to notice, but just the picture was gone. I then realized that whoever did this could still be in the house. The cat.

That fucking cat.

Then...

Then.
