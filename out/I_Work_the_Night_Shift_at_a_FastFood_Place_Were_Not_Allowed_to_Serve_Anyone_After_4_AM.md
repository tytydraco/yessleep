Hey all. Your friendly neighbor minimum wage fast food worker here. Wondering if anyone has the same odd problem that I have.

Does anyone work night shifts under these conditions?

I know it’s a stupid question. But this is the weirdest fucking job I’ve ever had.

Now I won’t really say where I work at. I don’t want to get sued for defamation or some shit. But let’s just say we sell burgers and fries and we’re famous. I don’t want to be more specific.

Now I don’t really know how to explain my working conditions, but if I had to, it’s just that we have a series of rules that we have to obey after midnight.

Rules that don’t make sense. Rules that have bad outcomes if you don’t follow them.

This only happens on the night shift, however. Which is fucking weird. During the day it’s pretty normal, people come in, they get burgers, they get fries. Lunch hours are a mess and so is dinner, doubly so on the weekend.

But if you ever find yourself working the night shift, especially in the drive-through, things get fucking weird.

Let me explain.

So, it’s no secret that dudebros, people who party out, or the chronically bored are usually the types of people you get at night. People who’ve worked these kinds of shifts will know what I’m talking about.

You’ll go out to the drive-through and you’ll see a car filled with teenagers or people who should know better coming in for double cheeseburgers and ice cream at this hour.

They always want fucking ice cream.

Now those people, as annoying and drunk as they might be, are not usually a problem. They get their shit and they leave. But our manager explained that after midnight, there’s a series of rules you got to follow. And he says that for a good reason.

Let me give you an example.

At exactly 00:13 A.M. on the dot. The entire line at the drive-through will be empty.

Not we have no customers so it’s empty, empty. Something makes it empty.

One time, there were five cars placing orders at that hour. I don’t think I’ve ever been so nervous working. Me and my coworker just stared at each other, sweating.

When the clock hit 00:13 A.M. was at the same time I went to the delivery window to give out bags.

It was empty.

I had just talked to a guy who paid and after he turned the corner for delivery, he was gone. Just *poof*, off the face of the Earth. I was just left standing there with two bags full of fries.

I don’t want to sound like… well… Look I’ll admit it, this creeps me the fuck out. So, please just in general, don’t go to fast-food places at this hour.

Come at 00:30 A.M. come at 11:00 P.M. or just don’t come at night. But please, I don’t want this to keep happening.

It’s now restaurant policy to not serve food five minutes before 00:13 A.M. We block the drive-through. It took some years of fine-tuning to get that, because if we did it wrong, we would anger Him.

So yeah, don’t come at this hour.

This weird disappearance act is followed up by what happens two minutes after.

At exactly 00:15 A.M. something bizarre happens. A man driving a brown Chevrolet Impala shows up.

He won’t order anything. He just drives up to the drive-through and stands there.

When he’s at the payment window you’re supposed to say “Good morning”, when he passes the pick-up window, you say “Good night”.

That’s it.

Don’t make small talk, don’t ask what his order is. Don’t do anything else. Just say good morning and good night.

He will drive off and you won’t see him again.

This happens every fucking night. Every single night, this guy wearing a fedora and a trench coat comes in and does that.

He’s harmless, I suppose. Well, almost. But at least he doesn’t creep me out like the rest of the regulars we get.

At 1:00 A.M, shit starts getting weird.

There’s this “family” (?), that comes in at exactly that hour. They’re the only people we serve after midnight that aren’t in the drive-through.

We don’t really have a choice.

It doesn’t matter that we lock the doors, close the windows, fuck, we even barricaded the entire premises once as a test. That family will always get in.

Blink and you’ll miss them. They just manifest out of nowhere. We just don’t care at this point; we leave the door open. Might as well.

The family itself isn’t very… normal. You know, besides the obvious. They don’t…talk. At least the adults don’t. It’s always the same family, but at the same time it’s not.

It’s two adults and two kids. They will always be fucking different races. Sometimes the dad’s black, sometimes it’s the kid. Sometimes they’re white or Asian. I don’t know anymore. They keep switching. And like I said the mom and dad don’t talk.

The kids always order for them.

Two large meals for the kids and two kid meals for the parents. No mayo and no pickles. That’s always the fucking order.

They get their food, look inside the bag, smile in unison and leave. Creeps me the fuck out.

I don’t like to talk about it, but I’ve worked here for about five months and there was one time that something different happened.

One time, at 1:00 A.M. in they came, but this time it wasn’t two parents and two kids. It was just one kid with a dog. Same “family”, same order, same shit.

Dogs aren’t really allowed in, but again, what the hell can we do? They came in anyways.

The kid ordered and they got their meals. They look at their food and smile. And then the mom of the group looks at me, silently.

She barked and then they all went away.

Fuck me. I try not to think about that day too much.

What else is there… Oh right. The 2:00 A.M. squad.

So yeah, one hour later, five cars will come into the drive-through. They all wear different colored shirts. Yellow, red, blue, green and the “last guy”.

Once you take their order, it doesn’t matter how you do it. They will be switched up once they get to the delivery window.

It’s extremely disorienting and not very nice for your understanding of reality.

They are mostly harmless, except for the last guy. The last guy doesn’t play nice and he’s always last.

One time, Dave, my idiot coworker, messed up his order. The “last guy” punched him in the nose. Seems a bit too much for messing up an order of fries but okay.

Don’t feel sorry for Dave. He wasn’t confused about them switching positions, he just genuinely got the last guy’s order wrong.

Like I said, he’s an idiot.

After that, things start getting… dangerous.

At exactly 2:57 A.M. All the oil in the fryers will begin sizzling extremely hot. They will gradually start increasing in temperature, regardless of whether the machine is turned off or not.

It will exponentially continue getting hotter and hotter to the point where I even see the plastic containers near the oil start melting.

At exactly 3:00 A.M. it will completely stop. All the oil will go back to normal and weirdly enough even the plastic containers that got melted will revert to being “unmelted”.

Then it starts. The hour I personally hate the most.

3:00 A.M., is the feasting hour. If you come and order after 3:00 A.M., you won’t get anything. All the food that is ordered has to be eaten. By us.

 

If we don’t eat everything, that’s when bad shit happens. So, if you come at that hour and order a cheeseburger and a shake, I have to eat that. Even if I’m not hungry.

I, like the idiot I am, ignored this completely when I started working there. Needless to say, I burned my entire arm that night.

Remember the sizzling oil? Well, Dave hit one of the fryers and sprayed hot oil all over my arm. You might think that was Dave just being an idiot, but the oil wasn’t supposed to be hot at that point.

If you ever want to experience the most painful sensation in your life, I suggest dipping your hand in boiling oil. I promise you; you won’t forget that pain.

I’m joking, by the way. DON’T DO THAT.

I was punished for not eating, I guess. It’s not so bad, I suppose. You might think I should sue or something but… Well, remember how the plastic containers go back to normal with the hot oil? Well, the next day my arm, which should have third-degree burns, was completely fine.

Don’t ask me how it works, I don’t know myself. At 3:30 A.M. the feasting hour stops. You don’t have to eat anymore.

Dave is still a fucking idiot though.

So don’t come crying to me that you didn’t get your food at that hour.

And then finally there’s the end of the day. The last hour.

From 4:00 A.M onwards, you stop.

No food, no orders. No one eats anything.

That’s it.

I’m serious this time. Don’t eat anything, not you and not the customers.

If you break this rule, the consequences stop being “innocent” or “quirky”. I’d honestly rather burn my arm again than break that rule.

We had a coworker, Steven, who worked here some months ago.

Steven was… decent at his job. He followed the rules properly, but one day, he fucked up. He was kind of a big guy, did drugs (probably) and he probably ate twice what me and Dave could eat combined. And one day he just somehow… forgot.

He ate some fries after 4:00 A.M.

Steven was fired that day because he did something insane.

He tried to eat the manager.

I don’t fucking understand it myself. But if you eat anything from this place after 4:00 A.M, you… change. You become different.

Steven is in jail right now, because he went insane and almost successfully deep-fried our manager. He got him halfway into the fryer, before we kicked him off our manager.

I’ve never seen someone look so deranged. His eyes were so open, he was drooling.

God.

My manager, he’s a hard ass, but I feel bad for the guy. The man was dunked in hot oil. The next day he was fine, of course. But yeah… there’s no scars on him, except the ones inside his head.

When the oil starts boiling at 2:57 A.M, I can see him going away. He stands near a corner, afraid to get to close.

 Poor guy.

So yeah, that’s my “normal” workday. Oh, I guess I forgot to mention. At 5:00 A.M. my work shift ends. And I guess I sort of lied. There is one more thing that happens.

The fedora Impala guy, he shows up again at 5:00 A.M. I didn’t mention this before but our manager calls him the “owner”. But we just call that guy: “Him”.

That’s a story for another time.

He shows up every night at 5:00 A.M., and if you did everything right, he just says one thing.

“Good job.”

And then he drives off.

And I fuck off home.

Now that’s my full work shift. The clusterfuck that it is. Any of this sounding familiar to anyone?

I’m writing this while I’m eating some fries from the feasting hour. Some guy ordered some a few minutes ago. I’m alone now, writing this on my phone at the restaurant.

Late-night shifts alone are so much fun.

Huh weird. The clocks on the wall are giving a different time than my phone.

At least it will be over soon.

I’ve been feeling a bit funny for some time now. I was writing this as more of a vent before I likely quit my job today. Maybe tomorrow.

This place sucks. Even if the pay is decent.

Also, yeah, I lied, I don’t make minimum wage, but this insanity is still not worth it.

The clocks… Wait was today daylight savings?

Dave is such an idiot. I think he forgot to set the clocks back an hour.

Wait, do they go backwards an hour or forward?

 

Edit:

Dave just came in; he’s taking over this shift.

I was thinking of quitting but maybe I’ll stay I don’t know. I’ll have to talk to my manager. I mean the pay is not that bad, and maybe I can cope with the insanity a bit more.

Besides, I do get pretty hungry here and the food is nice.

I’m looking at Dave right now, fumbling with his entry card. He really is an idiot.

So yeah, I’ll talk to my manager, I think I’ll work this shift too.

I’ll work a few more hours with Dave. I kinda need the money, besides…

He smells delicious.
