Hi, my name is Michael. I live alone in a small apartment and have done for close to 3 years now. Ever since I moved in I had a neighbour above me who I’ll call David, I didn’t get along with him. The reason I never got along with David was mainly because he was loud, but also because he was rude. I never minded the noise too much, it was just that when I would ask him to turn something down- he would tell me to fuck off and turn it up louder. So with the issues I’ve had with David you can imagine I was very very happy to see him moving out 3 months ago, and I was…for about a week.

Shortly after David had left I started to hear footsteps above me in his apartment, at first I just ignored it, forgetting that he had moved out. Then I thought “maybe it’s the property owners”. But I don’t think property owners come by at 3 am. I was going to sleep when I heard 3 loud bangs. Similar to bangs you’d do to a neighbours wall when they’re too loud. Naturally and unashamedly I thoroughly shat myself, not because of the bangs in general but because I knew for a fact there is no way anyone can be up there. Now I’ll admit, i did the classic childhood scared run for the light thinking it was coming from in my own apartment at first, but then it happened again (bang bang bang) this time I was positive it was coming from above me. I grabbed my phone, put on shoes and went a floor up. When I got to the upstairs apartment not only was it unlocked but there wasn’t actually a key hole- someone had taken it out. Instantly my mind jumped to squatters, so I was very hesitant on going in…but of course me being desperate to get rid of the constant noise I have to deal with- I went in. I used my phone flashlight to look around until I found a light switch, I turned it on and started to sweep the apartment, I found nothing and nobody, not even a sign someone had been there at all. I went back to my apartment, still kind of on edge. 

Fast forward a few days later, I hear 3 loud bangs again. First time I can pass it off as coincidence but second time feels like I’m being messed with. This time though I was smarter, I called the property management and asked them to check the apartment. They got back to me about an hour later and told me that nobody is there but it seems someone broke in (no shit, there’s no lock). I again, moved past it, but then I heard bangs AGAIN hours later. I started to think I was losing it, or had ptsd from Davids and was getting auditory flashbacks or something. Quite frankly, I was now scared, not just because I got jump-scared by a few bangs but because I’m definitely hearing these noises yet there is nothing that can be making the noise. After close to 2 months of dealing with the noise I really started to feel like something was wrong, I even started asking other neighbours if they were hearing things too, nobody said they were- all said I was just hearing things. 

I finally lost it and bought one of those tiny cameras (with night vision too), I placed it in the upstairs apartment and waited. Once I heard the 3 bangs again I opened up the app that I can view the footage in and when I checked it, I can assure you, it wasn’t my neighbour, it wasn’t a squatter and it wasn’t an animal. I’ve watched horror movies till 5 am and slept like a baby but what I saw on that camera scared me to the point that I didn’t sleep and I kept a light on until the sun rose. What I saw was a mix of human and dog, that’s the best way I can explain it. It was like a person who had starved themselves to the point their bones were showing, I heard what sounded like breathing and growling at the same time, then it looked at the camera. It opened its mouth and it was like a cat hissing without noise but a wider mouth. I didn’t even close the app I just dropped my phone on my bed and stood up so fast I nearly fell over. My door was unlocked at the time since I wanted to be able to run upstairs if I saw something on the footage, but you bet I ran for that door and locked the fuck out of it when I saw that. 

The next day I went to show the property manager the footage, but when I got to his office, I tried to show the footage and nothing would load. I refreshed the app about 10 times before the manager told me to go home and get some sleep. He thinks I’m fucking crazy. I had to do this on my own, so I waited for the bangs again. When they came, I grabbed a bat and booked it for the apartment, sure it was stupid but I don’t have money for a new apartment and I don’t like looking crazy to my neighbours and property manger. I got to the apartment, ran inside, turned on a light and went to the room I saw that thing in. When I got there, it was gone, I went on to the camera app again and checked the footage, it glitched for a moment before automatically going to live view, meaning I could see myself, standing there but not only could I see myself but I could see a tall, pale, skinny, demonic looking fucker behind me. I dropped my phone and swung the bat round as hard as I could, there was nothing there. I literally out loud said “fuck this” grabbed my phone and left as fast as I could. That very same night I drove to my brothers house and stayed there, he also thinks I’m crazy. I’ve now moved in with my brother until I can find somewhere else to live.

I can tell you right now, I’m never living in an apartment building ever again. And for anyone reading this, if you hear noises from an empty next door or upstairs apartment, don’t check it. Just leave. Trust me, it’s not worth it. I haven’t slept properly since all that, granted it’s only been a few days since I left but every time I close my eyes I feel like I’m being watched. It feels like I wasn’t the only one that left that apartment building that day.
