So, my (52f) dog Sam (4yo black Sheppit) went missing over a month ago. Had a new pest control guy last month instead of my usual guy, and he took my dog with him. Sam is very friendly with strangers and treat-motivated, so it probably wasn't too difficult to get him in a van. 

He was my ex's dog, and her new girlfriend didn't like dogs so she left him with me, and he didn't like me very much at first, but I felt too bad to take him to the shelter after he'd lost his person, so I kept him.

After he was taken, I scoured Facebook groups, called the local shelter once a day and after a couple weeks, I was starting to accept that he was probably living his best life with a new owner.

But then, a week ago there was a bark at the front door. No scratching, just a single loud bark. I opened the door and there he was. His tail wasn't wagging or anything. He just sat there and looked at me for a moment, completely still while I hugged him. He smelled like roadkill, like he'd been rolling on a dead rabbit or something like dogs do. 

When I brought him inside, he immediately sat in the kitchen, looking at the dream catcher hanging between the beams separating the living room from the kitchen. 

No panting. No wagging. Nothing. Just dead still, head tilted at the dreamcatcher for whatever reason.  

Immediately, I took him into the bathroom and bathed him. Typically, he's so high energy that I can never keep him in the tub for long without him trying to jump out. I'd bought one of those peanut butter suction cup thingies and stuck it to the wall to keep him busy. 

Of course, this time, he didn't care about the peanut butter. He didn't even try to jump out of the tub. He just stood in the tub and looked at the door the entire time. 

At this point, I'm wondering what the hell happened to my dog because what dog ignores peanut butter? I'd read that animals who were starved long enough might struggle to eat, but he didn't even try. 

The rest of the day, he just sat upright on the couch going from looking at the dreamcatcher to the TV, back and forth. Of course, I called the vet and set an appointment in the morning. 

That night, my concerns were somewhat alleviated when he did his old routine where he'd lick my leg while I slept and I'd have to cover my leg to get him to stop. 

The drive to the vet the next morning was the smoothest drive I ever had with him. He sat calmly in the backseat, looking at me through the rearview mirror. It was a little creepy at first, but then he started panting and looking out the window. 

The vet visit was going well, until she came back with the x-ray results. Sam was seemingly fine, not in pain or anything. Not malnourished. But in his x-ray, something was missing... 

His baculum was gone.

He had no penis bone. 

Now I'm shocked, and extremely concerned. Like, was it removed, or what? 

But nope. No incision to suggest that it was removed. So, of course they wanted to do another X-Ray to make sure. 

A second X-Ray and 275$ later, and they tell me that his penis is fine. False alarm, probably an error with the X-Ray, whatever. What mattered was that Sam was perfectly healthy. 

That night, while I was asleep, I heard a cat screaming outside. I woke up and the bedroom door was open, and not only that, but the back door was open too. I grabbed a bat and I went outside expecting to knock someone out, but the gate was shut, and there was a wet, nasty sound coming from the other side of the house. 

I went to the other side of the yard, and unfortunately, Sam was eating a cat. I won't go into any more detail than that. Sam was never violent before, but I know that prey drives are strong in Sam's breed, so that's why I bought him a flirt pole. But the week had been hectic and we hadn't gotten around to playing with it yet. 

I put Sam in his kennel and got to work giving that poor kitty a proper burial. The next day, I kept an eye out on our walk to see if I saw a missing cat sign so I could let its owners know. 

Sam was having issues, and I wanted to be what he needed. He was all I had, after all, and I lost him once already. 

I took Sam outside to try to play with the flirt pole, and I felt stupid swinging that thing around while he just sat there. 

Maybe it was trauma, or something he'd grow out of. Maybe he was upset with me for taking him to the vet and putting him the kennel, but I doubt he was as upset as I was having to bury that kitty. 

By now, I was going through the worst case scenarios in my head. What if I tell the cat's owners and they want Sam to be put down? What if he does it again, or worse, does something like that to a small child? He opened the backdoor on his own. What if he digs under the gate and runs off like a maniac. 

So, I bought a deadbolt for the front and back doors.

No deadbolt for the bedroom door, though, so he was still able to open that at night. 

I had a nightmare, but I couldn't for the life of me remember what it was about, just that I woke up feeling sweaty, and afraid, and in desperate need of a glass of water. 

I went to the kitchen, my eyes still struggling to open, and when I rounded the beam, I stopped. 

There Sam was, standing in the light of the open fridge with one paw on the closed left-side door. His back was straight and he was looking inside the fridge. He turned to me, and his eyes were glowing white. 

Next I remember waking up again, this time on the couch. I guessed it was another nightmare, but I remembered this one. I mean, what else could it have been?

Sam was laying inside his open kennel, and so I gave him his morning treat. I made myself a bowl of cereal for breakfast, and after I ate I felt sick to my stomach the rest of the day. 

Felt sick for the next couple days, and Sam laid at my feet while I rested in bed. 

Thursday morning, there was a knock at the door. The back door was open again, deadbolt hanging freely. 

Sam was nowhere in sight.

I answered the door, and much to my shock, the pest control guy was standing at the door.  He had a little girl with him, and he started apologizing. You see, his daughter was begging him for a dog, and he thought that my dog would be better suited for a family with kids, so he took her. But then his daughter found a missing poster and asked him to bring her back. 

This was strange, but even stranger was when the guy opened the sliding door to his van, and led by a leash, wagging his tail like he hadn't seen me in forever was Sam. 

The guy offered me 150$ for my trouble. 

As upset as I was for him stealing my dog, my immediate thought was the obvious one, and so I asked. 

"How long have you had him?"

"About a month, I think," He replied. 

Not three weeks. 

A MONTH. 

What dog did I have the past week? 

I refused the money, brought Sam in and bolted the door once more. I contacted the police about the deadbolt. They suggested a security system and cameras. My paranoid thinking was that Sam escaped the pest guy and came back home. The guy broke in so that he could stage giving him back and hopefully avoid the police with his methhead logic, perhaps.

I did what the police suggested and set up a home security system, and life finally started getting back to normal. No open doors. No nightmares. 

Somewhere in the midst of all the chaos, my dreamcatcher had gone missing, and I hadn't even noticed. 

Last night, I had the first nightmare in days. Another one of those nightmares I couldn't remember, but I'll never forget what happened when I woke up. 

Sam was doing his usual nightly routine, licking my leg while I slept. I kicked the cooling blanket over my legs. 

There was a smell. A rotten smell like old fish and long expired potatoes, and a low yipping sound. The sound of a dreaming dog. 

I opened my eyes, and curled up asleep at my hip was Sam. 

In my tiredness, the question of what was just licking my leg didn't cross my mind, until I heard someone speak in a hushed, deep tone. 

"Let me lick the salt from your legs."

I looked down at my feet to see what looked like half of Sam, and half of something else curled up at the edge of the bed. Something bald and pink, with white eyes and a man's torso propped up on two arms. Completely smooth down to its groin. 

I remembered the X-Ray.

The missing bone. 

As the memory came to mind, it licked its lips and smiled. 

I would've believed it was a nightmare, but I didn't wake up. I threw the blanket over whatever the hell it was, grabbed the real Sam and hurried out of the room. Set Sam on the couch, grabbed my keys and drove us straight to a hotel. 

Called the police and met them back at the house. 

No surprise. It was empty. 

I'm for sure going to move after this experience. I haven't seen the security cam footage, and I don't think I want to.  What matters is that I have Sam back, and we're safe. I'm going to learn how to use a firearm, and if it shows up again, I'm going to use it. 

If it was alien or something supernatural, I don't know. I just hope men in black don't show up my door. But most of all, I hope I never have to see or smell that thing ever again.
