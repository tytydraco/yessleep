I usually go to my grandparents house once every few weeks, they live in a small town in south Italy, the type where everyone knows each other, the thing about this town is that it’s really religious, so you can find multiple churches and tiny chapels, in fact one of those is next to their house, but it’s been closed since as far as I can remember.  
What I do in this town is hanging with friends around the neighborhood and near the park, nothing special, just a walk with people I don’t see very often. Last week though, when I was waiting for my friends to arrive, I decided to sit in the bench in front of the chapel I mentioned earlier, I waited a bit, but nobody came, so I opened up my phone to call them, FaceTime, like we usually do. They answered pretty quickly actually, I asked what’s up, they were late because one of them’s mom (I’ll call her Jane) took the wrong turn, whatever. What they said later though, made my skin crawl: “hey what’s that behind you?”  
My instincts said to just turn around, but something stopped me, I don’t know what it was, it’s weird because they always joke like this, but this time was different for some reason, maybe the chapel, which I always found terrifying. Nonetheless I ended up looking behind me, and what I saw still gives me a chill to this day: a man, in the dark, just barely visible from the faint light the street lamp made, he was staring at me, holding the bars that replace the chapel’s door. Mind you, I was still in the call, with Jane listening, she asked what was going on, interrupting the silence, the man disappeared in the shadows deep inside the chapel, he probably heard her voice.  
At that point they arrived, so we all checked the inside of the place (from outside since it was closed), it was dark so we used our phone flashlights, but somehow the figure wasn’t there, Jane said we were just imagining things, but I knew that was not a weird shadow, if it was, why would it vanish the moment it hears sounds? From that day we all just avoided that chapel but sometimes I still see that man, even outside the place, creeping over corners, I know that’s my imagination, but the first encounter wasn’t, we all saw it. How did he even get in? everyone knows that it’s been closed for years.  
I’m at my grandparents’ house the moment I’m writing this post, I don’t know what to do anymore,  I asked everyone about some homeless dude entering abandoned places, but as I said, the town’s small, so everybody knows pretty much everything about it and no one knew about this chapel squatter, I’m quite concerned, if he managed to enter there then, what’s stopping him from going in my home, or my friends’, maybe I’m just paranoid, after all, I’m not sleeping well lately, so perhaps I’m only tired and my mind is playing tricks on me, but that wouldn’t explain why my friends had seen him.  
So please explain if you had a similar experience, I’m genuinely scared to go to sleep.  Thank you
