This story happened i wanna say around 8-9 years ago, so i was in elementary school.

anyway, just for some backstory so you can really get a feel for this story, let me explain to you where i lived at the time.

I lived out in the middle of nowhere in central Indiana. The house i lived in was super old and had 2 stories and a basement. the house had a lot of history. i can't remember exactly when it was built but i know it was once a slave owners house, a brothel, and a post office. the barn beside the house was a one stop pony express. you could see evidence of the house's history everywhere.  some doors had numbers on them. we had a strange window in the kitchen (from when it was a post office). and a sealed-up hole in the basement. a hole slaves used to escape.

now let's walk you through the house. the house had 2 front doors, but we only used one. that door imminently took you to the living room. from the living room, you could go to the right and go into the kitchen and the entrance from the other front door. or, you could go straight and go into the hallway. this hallway also connected to the kitchen. from left to right standing in the hallway it was the upstairs door with my bedroom right across from it, the bathroom, my parent's room with the entrance to the kitchen directly across from it, and the basement door all the way at the end. the upstairs was much smaller and only had 2 rooms. my cousins' room and then my grandparent's room, basically connected. in my grandparents' room, there is an air vent that you could open and see right down into the kitchen. if you know what im talking about, yeah, the house is that old. im not gonna tell you what the basement looked like because its not important to this particular story.

anyway, i wanna say it was summertime. i was anywhere from 8-10 years old. and my little min thought i was basically all grown up. obviously. so, when my parents and grandparents were all gathered by the door ready to go shopping, i told them i wanted to stay home alone. it took a while for my parents to agree to it, but their logic was "it's the middle of nowhere, what could possibly happen?". i was absolutely thrilled. My first time staying home alone.

after they had left there really wasn't much to do. i didn't have a phone, so i just sat in the living room and watched tv.

about an hour into being by myself, my stomach started to growl. so, i got up and walked to the kitchen to make myself something to eat. as i was in the cabinets digging through cans of ravioli and cup mac-n-cheese, i heard the vent open. like i said, it was old, it made sound. i stopped what i was doing and stood under the vent to see if it opened. however, i couldn't tell. either it wasn't open, or the lights were shut off. i brushed it off and finished making me a snack and went right back to sitting on the couch and watching tv.

I don't remember when, but i remember drifting off and falling asleep. when i woke up the tv was off from it being inactive. i decide i would like to finish my nap on the couch so i go into my bedroom and grab a blanket. as im looking for a blanket i wanna take to the couch, i freeze. the unmistakable sound of the rattle and creek of the upstairs door filled the room. I don't know how long i stood there before turning around, but eventually, i did. i looked up the stairs and squinted my eyes to ty and see in the darkness, but i couldn't see anything. i shrug. i thought maybe it was just a loose hinge; the house is old after all. i turn around grab my blanket and head to the living room, right before i step in, i hear the door open again. but this time, the unmistakable sound of my grandfather's voice carried down the stairs.

"Madisyn. Madisyn come up here"

once again i froze. it had to have been the tv. i turned my head and saw the tv, still off. my heart begins to race as i try to come up with some sort of explanation. but nothing comes to me. while im trapped in my thoughts, i hear it again.

"Madisyn, come up here and help me"

this time i don't take any time to thing, i book it. on my way out the door i grab the wireless house phone hanging by the front door and run. i don't look back until i make it to my closest neighbor's house and start banging on her door, sobbing. she opens the door and doesn't hesitate to call the police. i told her over and over again, "that's not my grandpa, someone's in my house."

i don't remember when the cops showed up, but they searched the house and didn't find anyone. they called my parents and they rushed home. once they were there, i told them my story, and they didn't believe me. they didn't think i heard my grandpa. there was no way i could've heard him, he was out shopping.

to this day i think about this story. my grandpa's voice is truly one of a kind. Ive never met another person who sounds like him. especially the way he says my name. it was too specific. too on point for it to be anyone but my grandpa.

now, years later, the Amish own that house and refuse to go inside or sell it to anyone. they actually plan on demolishing it. from what i heard, there was something in there that they saw, and didn't like. what that was i don't know.
