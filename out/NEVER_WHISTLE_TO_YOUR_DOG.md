Back when I was 9, I had this very scary experience in my sleep. I don't even know if it was a sleep or not. I was too little to understand all that. It was from then that I started studying about things like liminal spaces and also uncanny valley. It was a very peculiar experience. Even today if I think of it, I really shudder. It was unreal. So, here is what happened basically:

It was a cold night of December, 2021. That day from the very beginning was very strange. I had a gloomy and strange feeling as if someone’s monitoring me from somewhere till morning to night. I am a book lover. Mostly during winters and winter vacations I read books late till night. That day, it was well past midnight when I went to bed. Well, around 3:17 a.m. I suddenly woke up. It was dark. Pitch dark. So dark that I wouldn’t knew if my eyes were open or not. I still possessed that same, queer feeling. I went out, and was welcomed by a grey, dark sort of view. I looked out of the window. There was nothing I could see. The whole town was covered with thick murk. My room, too, lay in vapor. The main gate of my house was open and no one was there in my house. Not my mom, not my dad, my granny or grandpa and even my dog, Max was missing. I went out with a flashlight. What I saw was obvious. I saw the whole neighborhood, empty and dark and sleeping. But in that emptiness, I found pleasure and I got much scared at the same time. I was approaching the vast fields. To tell the truth, it was surreal. It was as if I were hypnotised and I was in a simulation. I became utterly numb. And then far off I heard a woof sound. A woof very familiar. It was Max. It was his bark and, I was sure. But it came from a different direction, very different. Not the 32 named directions. Not even up or down. Mathematically, infinite directions are possible. But that too, seemed false.

The woofing continued. Then came a shadow. There was Max, wagging his tail. But he seemed quite different. He seemed like a regular dog, but seeing him made me uneasy. It was as if something, however minute, but really, it was different. Maybe something is added or subtracted from his body. I wouldn’t know, but a voice inside me told I should run away. Then I whistled him. That was the usual way I called him. But then happened the scariest part. He opened his mouth, so wide that a four-year-old could easily fit inside it. There I saw endless teeth, sharp and jagged, brimming on the wide, red, bloody gum. And the blacker core had eyes. Many, many eyes. Infinite. I ran, frightened, sweating. He followed. I ran, but the lane never seemed to end. It was endless. I ran with all my speed, but with each step my house moved a step away. I fell. I got my knees bruised and bleeding. Just then I realized I was holding the grill of my main gate. I opened the gate and went to my room. I locked the door. There were my parents in deep sleep. There was Max, in deep sleep. It all took an hour. But when I saw the clock, it struck 3:17 a.m. I went out. The main gate was closed and everything was silent. I didn’t dare to go out. I laid in my bed and don’t know how, I fell asleep. I thought it was a dream. But all of it seemed so, so real. In the morning I woke up. Everything was like before. I thought yet again it was a dream. Just then my knees hurt. I saw it bruised. Max was continuously staring at me. From that day on, I never whistled to him…
