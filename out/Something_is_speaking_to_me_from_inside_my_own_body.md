Hello all, I have a health issue that I need to report, and the people around me are attempting to silence me. My name is Benjamin Wesker, or Bennie for short, and I recently found out someone else is living in my body. I can't even recognize the voice, and I feel like I am going insane.

The first time I heard this voice was the morning after a trip to the doctor's office, my wife desperately trying to juxtapose the stress and white noise of the stale office with the warmth of our small wooded cabin, deep in the heart of the Appalachian mountainside, by baking some pancakes she knew I loved. As I was setting the table for breakfast, I heard a small murmur behind my back. Thinking my wife was trying to be playful or telling me something, I turned around and asked,

"What was that?"

She turned around as well with the most puzzled expression. "What was what?" I attempted a chuckle in response. 

"What did you say?"

"I didn't say anything."

Feeling the awkwardness of the moment like a brick wall, she followed up with a joke about "my hearing aid falling on me," and I just chuckled back and finished my respective breakfast duties.

Besides that moment, the rest of breakfast went smoothly, and we kept on with our day of tranquility and silence, but that night I heard it again on my way to bed. The voice was faint, but still at least somewhat recognizable.

"... You're moving too slow…"

At that moment I felt like I was about to fall back, but I was able to steady myself and swing around to find the source of the voice. My wife? No, she's already asleep. The radio? No, hasn't been used in years. Trespassers? Yes! Has to be it. With a burst of strength that I haven't felt in years, I made my way to the porch with a flashlight and attempted to reveal the intruder, but the forest swallowed it whole. 

"...just go back inside…"

Despite my best judgment, I obeyed the voice and slowly went back inside, resting on our dingy rocking chair that sits in the corner of the foyer. 

"W-who…no, where are you"

  
The voice seems to make an awkward squelching sound and slowly moves up, causing me to start gagging and choking, seeing the light fade from my vision as I fall forward, and I desperately grasp and claw at my throat before it moves back down, and I fall back into the chair. The next morning I wake my wife with gentle desperation:

"Honey, sweetie…I need to go to the doctor."

Because my wife and I are getting older, we are partially isolated in the mountains, and there was really no way for us to get down unless our daughter came up in her dated Jeep Wrangler to pick us up. She already has to come up to bring us groceries twice a week, so I feel terrible taking more of her time than necessary. I made the anguished call to bring her out here, but at least she seemed concerned enough to drop what she was doing and come up in 3 hours. During that dreadful time, my wife attempted to simultaneously comfort me and get our documents ready as I got dressed for the inevitable.

At about 4 PM that afternoon, a soft knock sounded at the door, and my wife hurried to answer, and there stood my daughter with loving impatience.

"Dad, you look like a mess! Hurry to the car, kay?"

She led me to the car and put me in the front passenger seat while my wife sat in the back seat. The descent was long, which only increased my nerves tenfold as I tried my hardest to take calming, deep breaths. The main road was quiet too, until we saw a sign in the distance with a hearty "Welcome to Boone" written in distinct cursive. The doctor's office was the same bleak white office from the day before, and the doctor himself was no better. He was around my age, except his age showed more, and he had a bald spot that made me glad I cared about my body. 

My wife and daughter decided to stay back in the lobby. As I walked into the examination room, the voice rang out again, but loud enough to sound like it was speaking right into my ear. 

"...Too old…"

I tried my hardest to ignore it so I wouldn't make a scene, and I sat on the examination table facing the doctor as he sat in his respective chair.

"Welcome back, Bennie."  
"Yeah, and actually it's Benj-"  
"So what brings you into my office today?  
"I- I think there's something in my body."  
"Oh? Like what? Because if it's a stomach bug, then…"  
"No- I-I mean, like a physical object."   
"I mean, digestion gets slow as we get-"  
"No!- I mean, like it made me choke, and I almost passed out from it."

  
The doctor finally stops and begrudgingly stands up, leans me back, and opens my mouth.

"Be still, please."

The doctor's expression was more hostile than before as he tested my body, sticking his fingers down my throat and using all sorts of gadgets to test me.

When the torture was finally concluded, he stepped back with a slight annoyance.

"You look as normal as when I saw you before, Bennie. Again, it might just be indigestion; get some rest and take some Tums if needed"

Our drive back to the cabin was depressing; I could feel not only my daughter looking at me with suspicion, but even my wife, which escalated when my daughter offered to stay with us tonight to check on me in case of another incident. We set up a comfortable space for her on the couch, and I did some final things while my wife went to bed at my request. When my daughter finally fell asleep, I went into the kitchen to try to clear my head. 

"...Wasting Away…" 

The voice was now ringing in my ear as if it were an explosion. I quickly clamped my hands against my ears to shut out the sound, but it only got louder this time, like it was screaming,

"...YOU FLESH IS WASTING AWAY…"

I jumped up from my seat, knocking the chair over, and sped into the bathroom and locked the door, desperately going through the drawer and attempting to find anything to rip the thing inside of my body out. 

"...YOU ARE A FOOL…"  
  
The voice now becomes disorienting as I can barely even keep my body up, along with a loud pounding on the door from my daughter and wife.

"Dad? What's that noise? Unlock the door!"

The conflicting sounds of the door, my family, and this thing practically screaming at me almost made me dizzy enough to throw up my insides, but my hands managed to touch the blade of a whittling knife. I immediately grabbed it, and closing my eyes, I attempted to thrust it deep into my throat, but then I felt the same choking sensation from before. A hand, Smooth and silky with an almost pink, mole-rat skin tone in appearance, was reaching out of my throat with the knife in hand.

When I thought it went still, it kept moving out- forearm, biceps, shoulder- it kept moving slowly as it was horrifically squelched and contorted, as I could hear the cracking and snapping of twigs and the squishing of sponges. The entire time, I felt close to death, while air couldn't even reach my lungs.

When the last foot stepped out of my body, I was lying on the floor, only managing to catch a glimpse of the figure as it pushed its fist through the window and seemed to leap out as my family broke through the door and saw my current state. 

They said I lost it and had a breakdown, but there is something out there; I could feel it- young, mangled, and in these very woods that we used to call paradise, as I now sit in my wheelchair under the constant and severe supervision of my family.
