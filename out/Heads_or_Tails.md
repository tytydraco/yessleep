I’ve never really liked autumn. Everything is so gloomy, and even the colors of the falling leaves do very little to lift my mood.

I was walking home from work, just like I did every day. The same route. Trees, dusk, an empty road. I should be home in about twenty minutes. I was looking forward to lighting a candle, pouring myself a glass of wine, and reading a book.

When I reached my house, I froze.

My house looked exactly as it always did, except the front door had been bricked up. The same was true of the windows.

What the hell?

I looked around and noticed several people. They had their hoods pulled over their heads, their heads bowed, and their eyes fixed on the ground. They looked as if they weren't aware of their surroundings at all.

"Hey!" someone called from a distance.

Three figures came rushing toward me. Two men and a woman. Each of them was holding something that looked like a weapon. One of the men, an older man with gray hair, was holding a brick. The woman had a metal rod, and the other man was carrying pepper spray.

"What's going on?" I asked them.

"Oh my God, thank goodness. You look normal," one of the men said, exhaling in relief.

"What do you mean?"

The man looked toward the people who were endlessly walking in circles.

I looked at them more closely, and I can't even begin to describe what I felt in that moment.

I started screaming.

I screamed so loudly that my throat began to hurt.

Those people had no faces.

Their faces had been completely eaten away. Only a few pieces remained, with strips of skin hanging from them. Some of the pieces were even falling off as they walked.

The three of them simply stared at me in silence, waiting for me to calm down.

As if all of this were perfectly normal.

Then they introduced themselves.

The young blonde woman was Allison. The dark-haired man, who looked about my age, was named Ethan. The older man with gray hair was Miles. He was the one holding the brick.

Apparently, they had been wandering around here for several hours.

"So we don't know where we are or how to get back. And this obviously isn't the street we know, even though it looks exactly like it."

"Exactly," Allison agreed.

The others simply nodded.

"We need to search this place and keep an eye out for anything that might help us."

"We've been fucking doing that this whole time!" Ethan shouted.

Miles calmed him down and explained that yelling at each other wasn't going to help anyone.

He suggested we head in the opposite direction from where we had come.

We walked together, passing one strange place after another. Miles led the way, with the rest of us following behind him.

The farther we went, the darker our surroundings became. Night was beginning to fall, and somewhere in the distance, we heard someone crying.

We wanted to head toward the sound, but Miles stopped us. He said it wouldn't be safe and continued walking.

The figures we passed were becoming more and more terrifying. Some of them barely had any skin or clothing left.

With every figure we passed, I could feel my heart beating faster.

Eventually, we reached a narrow alley. The figures there were no longer walking on two legs. They were on all fours, their bodies twisted into unnatural positions as they continuously turned in circles.

Miles smiled.

He raised the brick he had been holding the entire time and struck Ethan with it.

Ethan collapsed to the ground.

The figures in the circle immediately stopped moving. Their heads turned toward Ethan, and they began slowly crawling toward him.

Ethan lay on the ground, desperately trying to get up, but he couldn't. I wanted to help him, but one of the figures suddenly lunged at me and tore into my arm with its claws.

I looked away, grabbed Allison's hand, and we ran.

We didn't look back. Miles was still behind us, and neither of us knew what he intended to do next.

We ran back the way we had come, hoping to find somewhere safe.

Suddenly, we crashed into someone.

We looked up, and even though we couldn't make out their face, we knew exactly who it was.

He was wearing the same clothes he had been wearing when we had seen him lying on the ground.

"Ethan?" Allison whispered, reaching out to touch him.

He didn't react. He just kept walking, as if he couldn't see us at all.

We were desperate. We had no idea what to do. We kept walking and walking until we came across two men.

One was wearing a white suit. He was smiling.

The other was dressed in a dark robe and had an angry expression on his face.

We started shouting over each other, begging them to help us.

"ENOUGH!" the man in black roared.

He turned to the other man.

"Which one do you want?"

The man in white took a breath and pointed at me.

"Her."

The man in black frowned even more.

"I want her too."

Then he pulled out a coin.

"Heads or tails?"

The man in white smiled.

"Heads."

The man in black tossed the coin into the air. It landed on the back of his hand. When he looked at it, he announced with satisfaction:

"I win."

The man in white kept smiling and simply shrugged. Then he looked at Allison, stepped closer to her, and snapped her neck in a matter of seconds.

Then he turned to me.

"Too bad. By the way, Miles says hello."

And with that, he walked away.

The man in black grabbed me by the shoulder and started dragging me somewhere. He opened the door of a house. We walked through one doorway and then another until we found ourselves standing on a doorstep overlooking a street.

He looked at me, shoved me outside, and closed the door.

I stepped onto the street.

When I looked around, I realized I was standing just a short distance from my workplace.

A woman approached me. I was terrified at first, but when I noticed that her face was completely normal, I felt an overwhelming sense of relief.

"Oh my God, what happened to you? Your arm is bleeding terribly!"

She took me to the hospital, where they cleaned and stitched up my arm. I had several deep wounds and needed a lot of stitches.

They asked me what had happened, but all I could tell them was that I didn't know.

When I arrived at my house and reached into my pocket, I felt a coin.

I knew exactly what it was.

It was the coin that had saved my life.
