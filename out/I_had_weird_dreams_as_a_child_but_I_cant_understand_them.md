When I was a child I had some weird nightmares. In hindsight they were all always ridiculous and unreal, I wonder how I didn’t notice I was in a dream myself, though, I guess, I was a child.

For example, one night I was still sleeping in the same bed with my eldest sister in the bottom bunk. I woke up in the middle of the night and immediately noticed a hand coming out of my chest, a third limb right on top of my heart. I wasn’t horrified, oddly enough, just confused. I couldn’t control it, and it was scraping at my sister’s leg, she did not wake up though.  
  
I got off the bed and climbed up to the top hunk, where my other older sister was sleeping. I was confused and wanted to ask that sister what was happening (I was closer with her than the eldest sister). I woke her up and said: “I have a hand in my chest.” and showed her. She looked at me with an expressionless face and said: “That happens sometimes.” I woke up in the bottom bunk again, this time, free of any confusion bursting out of my chest. I tugged at my sister, waking her up, and went back to sleep at the comfort of her arms.  
  
So yeah, these kinds of weird dreams.

but this isn’t the dream that’s bugging me, but rather some that came later, a pair of dreams. I keep having the feeling that my brain was trying to scream something at me through those dreams.

I must’ve been 10 because I remember this dream happening back in the second house I lived in (third for the rest of my family), and we moved around a lot; further north, then south again, back and forth again and again. This dream started with me coming out of a dark forest, aggressively dark, like some trees were pasted on a pitch black background. It was early noon, bright and sunny outside the forest, the sun to my right. In front was a meadow. Further up the trail was a village, which now remembering the dream, I can tell had an almost fairy-tale-like whimsy to it. To the right of me there was a parallel path, it too coming out of the forest and into the village. And on my path, to my left, right by the forest’s exit, was a bench with an old lady sitting on it.

The old lady called me, and I sat down beside her. We talked for a couple of minutes, but for the life of me I cannot remember what we discussed. Something was racing out of the dashing out of the forest, a beast, maybe a ghost, maybe an animal. I got up from the bench and instinctively started fleeing toward the village.

It chased me into the village, and I found myself in the town plaza. I circled back, somehow faster than it, out the parallel trail back toward the forest. At the forest’s entrance stood a lady, a different lady, younger, seemed to be around 30-35. She grabbed both of my arms as I came sprinting from the village. I looked behind me and the beast was gone.  
  
She looked down at me. I did not find the words to speak so she warned softly and seriously: “Whatever you do, don’t listen to that old lady on that bench.” I released my arms forcefully and stepped back into the forest. Then I woke up.

Time had passed and I forgot all about that weird dream, until one night. I was 17. I fell asleep, and I found myself exiting a forest; it was the same dream. Again the forest, again the meadow, the village, the trails, the old lady. We discussed again, and again I don’t remember what about, though this time. I’m sure we were arguing and disagreeing. Again something came dashing out of the forest, but this time I remember what. Dogs, huge pit bulls, all three with a spiked black collar around their neck. I remember the old lady scoffing at me: “You better run.”

I ran as fast as I could, I felt the cold in my lungs as I ran out of breath and yet I kept running without a struggle. Somehow I was faster than the dogs, dream logic. Again we got to the plaza, a dozen men, shopkeepers and customers, looking at me as I’m actively chased and doing nothing about it, just staring with that same expressionless face my dream sister had way back when.

Out the plaza, forest’s entrance, the younger woman. She held me and I felt peculiarly safe, I didn’t look back, I knew the dogs were gone. “Don’t you remember what I told you? Don’t listen to that old lady.” I woke up then and there.



Both times I felt like I got out of an unexplainable limbo. 

Those dreams feel intentional, I feel as if my brain was trying to scream warnings at me that I didn’t pick up on. I feel crazy bloating those dreams with importance.  
  
I’m 20 now, I don’t dream anymore, not really. Not dreams, not nightmares.

My eldest sister passed away last year, we buried her in the front yard. I’m always grateful she met my boyfriend before that.

My older sister is not here to comfort me anymore, got married. Moved north but not south.

We removed the top bunk, there is no use for it anymore.

The sun is setting.

I’m alone.
