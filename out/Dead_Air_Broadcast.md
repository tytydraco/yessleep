I used to run a podcast called “The Dead Air Broadcast.” 

Some of you may have heard of it. It wasn’t wildly popular but I was getting by decently enough. A few weeks ago I had my 1 year anniversary for the podcast, then something odd happened. Across all platforms the audio on every episode became corrupted. Instead of my lovely voice, all you can hear is static. 

There is something I can hear underneath the static, I think it’s someone screaming but it’s honestly too faint to tell. It’s very weird and it has been freaking me out. I have since deleted everything and tried everything possible to reupload. Switching accounts, devices, and even my IP address. Nothing has worked so far so I'm here as my last resort. 

What’s strange about all this is that I am able to upload anything else without a problem. It’s only the podcast that won’t stay up. Every USB drive I’ve tried to save it on ends up corrupted as well. 

On the advice of one of my listeners, I have been led here. Where I will have to transcribe the episodes that the internet won’t allow. If any of you remember this please let me know.
