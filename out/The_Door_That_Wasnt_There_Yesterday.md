I moved into a small apartment three months ago.

It was cheap.

Too cheap, honestly.

But I was a student, I didn't have much money, and the place looked completely normal when I visited it.

One bedroom.

Small kitchen.

A bathroom.

A living room.

And one strange thing.

There was a narrow hallway behind the bedroom.

The landlord told me it was just an old storage area that had been sealed years ago.

I didn't think much of it.

Until the first night.

At exactly **3:17 AM**, I heard three knocks.

*Knock.*

*Knock.*

*Knock.*

They came from inside the wall beside my bed.

I ignored them.

The next night, the same thing happened.

3:17 AM.

Three knocks.

The third night, I recorded it on my phone.

I went back to sleep.

In the morning, I listened to the recording.

There were no knocks.

Instead, I heard something else.

Very faintly.

Someone whispering:

**"You're sleeping in the wrong room."**

I immediately checked the apartment.

There was only one bedroom.

So I convinced myself it was a dream.

Then I noticed something strange.

My bedroom door had a tiny scratch near the bottom.

A scratch that hadn't been there before.

It looked like someone had written a number with a fingernail.

**317**

I laughed nervously.

"Okay. Very funny."

I assumed the previous tenant had done it.

That night, I put my phone on the floor and started recording before going to sleep.

At 3:17 AM, I woke up.

Not because of the knocking.

Because someone was walking in the hallway.

Slow footsteps.

*Step.*

...

*Step.*

...

*Step.*

I couldn't move.

My bedroom door was closed.

The footsteps stopped directly outside.

Then came three knocks.

*Knock.*

*Knock.*

*Knock.*

I reached for my phone.

The screen showed **3:17 AM**.

Then I heard a voice.

My voice.

From the other side of the door.

It whispered:

**"Don't open it."**

I froze.

Because I was still lying in bed.

I hadn't spoken.

Then the voice whispered again:

**"I'm serious."**

I didn't open the door.

The footsteps eventually disappeared.

The next morning, I checked my phone.

The recording was still there.

But something was wrong.

The recording showed that I had been asleep for almost seven hours.

Except...

At **3:17 AM**, the audio suddenly became completely silent.

For exactly **17 seconds**.

Then the recording continued.

I uploaded the audio to my computer and increased the volume.

During those 17 seconds, I heard something.

Someone breathing.

Very close to the microphone.

And then...

Three words.

**"He knows you're here."**

I stopped sleeping in the bedroom.

I started sleeping on the couch.

For four nights, nothing happened.

Then, on the fifth night, I heard knocking again.

But this time...

It wasn't coming from the wall.

It was coming from underneath the couch.

Three knocks.

*Knock.*

*Knock.*

*Knock.*

I jumped up.

I checked underneath.

Nothing.

I moved the couch away from the wall.

And that's when I noticed something I'd never seen before.

Behind the couch was a small wooden panel.

It was almost invisible.

There was no handle.

No lock.

Just a tiny hole in the middle.

I took a screwdriver and opened it.

Behind it was darkness.

And a narrow passage.

I stood there for almost five minutes, trying to understand what I was looking at.

The passage shouldn't have existed.

The apartment building was too small.

There couldn't physically be another room behind my wall.

I took a photo.

The flash revealed something on the floor.

A row of objects.

Dozens of them.

Old phones.

Keys.

Wallets.

Photographs.

And hundreds of tiny pieces of paper.

I picked up one.

It said:

**"January 12 — heard the knocking."**

Another:

**"January 19 — it used my voice."**

Another:

**"February 3 — found the door."**

Another:

**"February 17 — don't trust the landlord."**

I dropped the papers.

Then I found a photograph.

It showed my apartment.

But the photograph was old.

Very old.

And standing in the window was a man.

I recognized him.

It was the landlord.

But he looked much younger.

I turned the photograph over.

There was writing on the back.

**"He rents the room every time someone disappears."**

I ran out of the apartment.

I called the landlord.

He answered immediately.

I asked him about the hidden passage.

There was silence.

Then he said:

**"You found it early."**

I asked him what that meant.

He didn't answer.

Instead, he asked:

**"What time is it?"**

I looked at my phone.

**3:17 AM.**

I hadn't realized how long I had been inside the passage.

Then I heard knocking.

Not from behind me.

From somewhere deep inside the passage.

Three knocks.

*Knock.*

*Knock.*

*Knock.*

The landlord whispered:

**"Whatever you do, don't answer."**

The call ended.

I ran.

I didn't even take my things.

I moved to a friend's house and refused to go back.

Two days later, I returned with the police.

The apartment was completely normal.

There was no hidden panel.

No passage.

No photographs.

Nothing.

The landlord looked confused.

He told the police I had been under stress.

I almost believed him.

Until one of the officers called me aside.

He showed me something on his phone.

A photograph.

It had been taken inside my bedroom.

It showed my bed.

The officer asked:

"Is this your apartment?"

I said yes.

Then he zoomed in.

Under my bed...

There was someone lying there.

I couldn't see their face.

But I could see one hand.

And on the wrist was a tattoo.

Three numbers.

**317**

The officer told me the photograph had been taken by the previous tenant.

Then he showed me the date.

**Seven years ago.**

I asked him what happened to that tenant.

He said:

"He disappeared."

I haven't slept properly since.

Yesterday, I received a package.

No return address.

Inside was my old phone.

The one I left behind in the apartment.

It shouldn't have been possible.

The phone was dead.

Completely destroyed.

But when I pressed the power button...

It turned on.

There was only one recording.

I played it.

It was the recording from my first night.

The one where I heard:

*"You're sleeping in the wrong room."*

Except this time, I noticed something I hadn't noticed before.

The whisper wasn't coming from the wall.

It was coming from **my bedroom**.

From directly beside the microphone.

And after the whisper...

There was a second voice.

A very quiet voice.

My voice.

Saying:

**"I'm still here."**

I checked the time.

It was **3:16 AM**.

I heard three knocks.

Not from the wall.

Not from the door.

From underneath my bed.

*Knock.*

*Knock.*

*Knock.*

Then my phone screen changed.

A new notification appeared.

It said:

**"17 seconds until 3:17."**

I don't know what happens at 3:17.

I'm writing this from my friend's house.

If this post suddenly gets edited after **3:17 AM**...

**I didn't edit it.**

And if you ever move into a cheap apartment and the landlord tells you there's an old sealed room...

Don't ask what's inside.

Ask instead:

**"Who was the last person who slept there?"**

Because I'm starting to think the room was never meant to keep people out.

It was meant to keep **something in.**
