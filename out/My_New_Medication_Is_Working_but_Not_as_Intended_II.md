In the weeks since my last post, things have been incredibly strange. If you did not read it, my life had taken a dark turn after becoming sick from chemical exposure over years at my job. After accepting medication from an experimental medical start-up, everything seemed to be on an upturn until, in what I once figured to be a hallucination, my hand melted off. Waking up to find my hand unscathed but a pile of flesh on the floor, I have attempted to gain contact with the start-up but have found nothing.

Going into work the next day, an incredible wave of fear and paranoia loomed over me. A terrible sweat covered my body, and I felt like a crazy person as coworkers looked upon me with weary concern. The lifting of heavy materials and the rising sun did not help a thing. Everything came to a head when, in a moment, I dropped all the material in my arms and ran to the closest portable restroom to spill my guts.

Through blurry vision, I watched as a mix of red and black liquid filled the already disgusting chamber. Standing slowly, I clutched the walls for support and, after a moment of deep breaths, took a step outside, cringing as my boss waited for me outside with a look of pitiful concern.

“You know, you should really go home if you don’t feel well. I don’t want you getting the rest of the boys sick.” I took his advice and shuttered home. Stumbling over every crack in the road, I felt myself deteriorate to a state I had not experienced since my time before the medication.  

Crashing into my apartment, I took a handful of the medication from the bottle and funneled it into my mouth. This all started from missing a day; If I could just get whatever this was in a higher dose things could go back, right?

That night, I tossed in turn as sweat covered every fiber of my sheets and at least hourly I ran to the bathroom to vomit up the same strange liquid I had at my worksite. Eventually, I did not have the strength to make it to the bathroom and dirtied my bed with the foul bile, making the only reasonable solution in my mind at the time to sleep in the tub.

When I finally awoke again, things were even stranger, though; I felt completely fine. I stretched my arms and looked upon them with curious wonder, rolling my shoulders, feeling as if a gargantuan weight had been taken off of them.

Cleaning up my bed, I wondered for any reason why such a sudden shift could have occurred. Lost in thought, I mistakenly elbowed a potted plant while changing my sheets and swore, expecting an incoming crash but heard nothing. Looking down, I watched as the vase plummeted but slowed to an easy pace right before the floor and clinked softly without a crack.

For maybe thirty minutes, I paced around the plant, which I did not dare move due to my shock, confusion, terror, and perhaps every other devastated emotion I could imagine. I crouched down, looking for signs of any kind of cushion I could have missed. When I found none, I went back to the bathroom and stared into the mirror for a long time, questioning if my mind was really all back like I had thought since waking.

My physical form looked incredibly well for the night I had just experienced. My skin was full of color, and my eyes were wide and awake, but that's when I noticed something I could believe I did not realize earlier. Around the irises of my eyes, a dark ring surrounded them that was thin but distinctly separate.

Slowly, I poked at them, at first believing them to be some kind of strange contacts, but quickly, upon a painful poke, realized they were no costume. Deeply I stared at them for a long time before realizing there could be other physical effects I had not yet noticed. Looking at my hands, I thought upon a first look that they seemed much cleaner and less worn than might be expected for a man of my work. Inspecting a bit closer, I realized how long my nails were. *I thought I had just cut them a few days ago.* Slowly, I went to get the trimmers when my phone rang. Answering it swiftly, I listened as the voice of my contact at the medical start-up spoke.

“It seems you have succeeded in the initial stage. Please come to our lab tomorrow morning and receive your next set of instructions.” Into the phone I screamed questions, but the line was already disconnected. 

Now, as I sit in my apartment, feeling more magnificent than ever before and with strange rings floating in my eyes, I feel stuck. I feel that either way whether I go or not, there is a risk of once again feeling like I did last night, and I ponder if it was even worth it to get involved in this experiment in the first place. I will make my decision soon, and when I do, I will let all of you know.
