I met this guy a couple of weeks ago.

He was an old man on the shorter side. He walked with a limp. I only met him because he came into the camera store I manage. 

It was a pretty simple interaction. He bought some cameras, and asked me if any buffalo had gotten off of the reservation. I said no, and he was on his way.

I live in a neighborhood on the outskirts of Tulsa. Is Tulsa the most interesting city ever? No. But it is pretty nice. They don’t call it the Paris of Oklahoma for no reason.

This man was pretty forgettable. I wouldn't have remembered him at all if he didn’t stand outside my house at 4:00 AM. 

I was taking the trash out. I forgot to do it last night, but I woke up early (I’m a light sleeper). I took it out, and there he stood.

He stared straight at me from the moment I opened the door to my backyard. His head pivoted unnaturally, like he was a startled cat. He was a good distance away from the streetlights, but I could tell it was the same guy. 

He followed me with his gaze. I cautiously waved, and then sprinted back behind my house. I tend to get freaked out by things like that. I just assumed he was some weirdo who just got lost, because I didn’t remember him living in my neighborhood.

When I left for work at 8:30, he wasn’t there. 

The rest of my day proceeded as normal. I got home at around 8:00 PM. I ate dinner and I went to bed. An otherwise unremarkable day. The thought of that man, though… it stayed on my mind all night. 

I heard some rustling outside the subsequent night. I checked the window. The man from last night was trying to figure out how to open the gate to my backyard! 

I ran outside and yelled at him. “HEY! What’re you doing?!” He scuttled off as quickly as possible with that gait. I thought that if he tried coming over again, I would call 911. 

I got a camera from the store I work at the next day. Business there has been slow. My shift ended early that day, so I set up the camera at my front door, and went to sleep like normal.

The next morning, I checked the camera. I saw him. He arrived at around 4:00, and tried for an hour straight to open the back gate. It was like his hand wasn’t being controlled by him, with how haphazardly he slapped it against the simple lock.

After about an hour, he got it.

All he did was take the trash out front.

The way he did it was sloppy. There wasn’t anything in there, but he banged it against seemingly every wall he could. 

I woke up when he did so. I hazily stepped out front to find the trash bin. It was all scratched up, no doubt from the banging.

I was seriously confused by this. What on earth was he doing? Was there some sort of motive that I didn’t get?

He had come over three nights in a row. My most pressing question is what he would do on the fourth.

As a kid, I read all kinds of detective books. They always did these elaborate stakeouts. I always thought they were impractical. Never once did I think I would do one of them.

I’ve never been the paranoid type. I suppose this story might say otherwise. I thought that I would rather not call 911 if this man truly had no bad intentions. So, I grabbed a lawn chair, a camera for recording, and waited for him.

The man showed up around 4:00 AM. It had been a long night (he had been very inconsistent with schedule before). He opened the gate and slowed when he saw me. 

He yelled “HEY! What’re you doing?!” in my voice, and bolted. 

I was about to give chase, but I figured it was pointless. I had evidence. I was going to show it to the police that day. I checked it.

There was no SD card.

I was bewildered. I had one in, I knew I did. Where had it gone? I wasn’t *that* absentminded… right? 

The next night, I decided to watch him. I thought he would try to break in, so I hid behind my back door with a cleaver in my hand, waiting.

I tried as hard as I could to stay awake, but I couldn’t. I fell asleep.

He didn’t come in.

All he did was take out the garbage. 

He did it around the same time, 4:00 AM. This night, he also didn’t do a good job at taking it to the front of the house. He even woke up one of my neighbors, as they yelled at me the next day. 

I found it weird that I didn't wake up.

The weirdest part though, was that he had a camera. Not a handheld one, one on a stand, like I had used the night I held a stakeout. 

That day I had work, but oddly enough, I barely thought of him. It was like I was daydreaming about nothing that day. 

That night, I felt really… lazy. I didn’t really care to see what he was doing. He was just taking out my trash, and something in my brain told me that was okay.

I fell asleep very early that night. I slept like a rock. I slept through even my alarm the next day. 

That day was the seventh since I had first seen him outside my property. It was garbage day.

I found myself struggling to stay awake at work. I guessed that all those nights of staying up had taken their toll on me. I was barely able to get the bare minimum done that day.

I fell asleep pretty much the second I got home. The same laziness as the last night overtook me, except much stronger. I didn’t even want to brush my teeth or shower. I just fell asleep in what I was wearing. 

This was the first day I didn’t even take the trash back in.

The next day was when I started writing this post. I woke up at around 2:00, and checked the camera again with the little motivation I had, and what I found was pretty interesting.



He had arrived around the same time, 4:00 AM. He was still holding the camera on the stand from a couple nights ago. He reacted strangely to the trash bin being out front. He stared it down for a couple of minutes. Then, he just… sat next to it. He rocked back and forth. 

The garbage truck came by at about 8:00. He reacted the same way. He stood up, and gazed ahead. His neck snapped to whatever direction each sound the truck came from. The man driving the truck motioned for him to move, but he didn’t. He just stood there. 

The trash man seemed pretty peeved at the old man. He stepped out of the truck and yelled at him. I didn’t hear it because the truck was being too loud. The old man simply stared ahead. He appeared to be analyzing the trash man, at least from the angle the camera was positioned at. 

The trash man pushed the old man.

The old man bent inward unnaturally, in a way that a spine should probably not do. The trash man stepped back and scrambled back into his truck. The old man yelled in my voice, “HEY! What’re you doing?!” once more. The trash man drove away quickly. The old man scrambled away. 

Anyway, I just wanted to ask all of you about this. I’m not particularly concerned, I’m just wondering if this has happened to anyone else. Like I said earlier, life in Tulsa can be boring at times, so I should be thankful for a shake-up. Sorry if this post is a little shorter than the norm on this website, it’s been hard writing it. Just haven’t really found the motivation.

If I don’t get back to any of you guys, I’m probably sleeping. 

I wonder *why* I’m so tired these days…?
