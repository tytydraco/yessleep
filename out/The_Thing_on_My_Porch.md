For a long time, I went to bed early. It wasn’t until the strange events unfolded upon 4023 Gillman Road that I began to worry for my own safety. There was no telling what exactly happened on that dreaded night of October 31st, but whatever it was, things were never the same again.

It was cold on the night of Halloween, and it had been raining a bit prior to the trick-or-treaters’ release from their parents’ houses at 7 P.M. I was just about done getting the big bowl I had together with all of the candy I could scavenge from the cabinets. Sometimes I wouldn’t have enough candy in the bowl, so I would just throw in little consolations like an air freshener or a number two pencil. The kids weren’t exactly jumping for joy, but beggars can’t be choosers.

The sound of a knock at the door came while I was evenly dispersing some caramel chews into the mix. Making sure I paused the movie playing on the TV, I made my way to the door slowly, preparing myself for the first group of the night with a straight face.

I was dressed like Dracula. These kids were in for a treat.

Opening the door, I peered down to the doorstep to see a small child looking up at me. I hadn’t seen a costume like that before. They were wearing a long, white sheet, possibly a gown, over their entire body, but it was their disguise that threw me off. A large pumpkin, gutted inside, was carved with a permanent scowl.

“Happy Halloween, little guy,” I spoke cautiously.

No response.

Picking a few candies from the bowl, I dropped them into the small bowl perched beside their feet.

Still no response.

Reaching my hand out to the pumpkin’s stem, I attempted to touch the tip to make sure the little kid was alive in there, but suddenly, the stature collapsed to the ground.

A piece of cloth and a gutted pumpkin lay limp on the front of my porch.

“What the hell?” I questioned myself.

Tapping the pumpkin with the tip of my foot, the carcass began to roll backward off the steps of my porch and smashed open against the sidewalk.  
From the shimmer of light, I noticed a small metal object that lay inside the gutted pumpkin. Kneeling down to check inside, I found a small skeleton key engraved with some type of hieroglyphics. Taking the key and walking back into my house, I made sure I locked the door behind me.

Throughout the night, there were only a few trick-or-treaters that came by my house, but I wasn’t too surprised since the neighborhood I lived in was so tiny. But it wasn’t until around 9 P.M. that things got really weird.

I had just turned on *The Breakfast Club* and was pouring myself a bowl of cereal when I heard two consecutive knocks at my front door. After taking a bite of cereal, of course, I eventually made my way to the door with the bowl of candy.

Quickly opening the door, I peered around to find no one in sight. Upon my doorstep, however, was a small black box with red metal hinges on the sides. In the middle of the box, though, was a small opening for a key.

A skeleton key.

*Oh, what the fuck.*

I grabbed the box and carried it inside the house with me. Trust me, I made sure I locked the door that time, too. This was getting way too weird.  
I placed the object on the top of my kitchen counter and examined it. The hieroglyphs lining the edges of the box seemed to match up with the ones on the skeleton key that I had found earlier. So, obviously, I placed the key inside the hole, and it unlocked with utmost ease.

Carefully pulling up the top of the box, I unveiled a dark red sheet of fabric. Placing my hand upon it, I felt something hidden underneath the fabric. Moving the fabric to the side, I grasped ahold of a thin piece of metal, and it was only when I pulled the object out as a whole that I noticed it was a knife.

The knife was engraved with actual English on the blade.

*“Trick-or-treat.”*

Good god.

Suddenly, three knocks at my door practically shook the room. It sounded like the Hulk was waiting at my door, for God’s sake. There’s no way that could be any young kid out there. I mean, no twelve-year-old that’s out trick-or-treating for Halloween is that strong, are they? I don’t know, they could be doing some serious weight lifting. Kids nowadays are built like they’ve been paying rent since birth.

Clutching the knife in my hand, I slowly made my way towards the door. I had a sixth sense telling me there would be a huge monster waiting outside my door.

“Trick-or-treat,” I heard a creepy voice bellow from beyond.

My free hand reached for the doorknob, unlocking it, and slowly pulled it open as I took cover from behind it. I heard a slight shuffle of feet, then a shadow moving closer to the doorway. The tip of a gun became visible around the edge of the door, and that’s when I choked the handle of the knife.

I wasn’t going to die here. Not tonight.

Shoving the door against the body that was making its way into my house, I lunged forward with the tip of my knife.

*Thump*.

My eyes were closed. 

Did I kill them?

Opening my eeyes, I gasped in horror as the face of a young child in a cowboy costume peered up at me. His mouth was agape with fear. I couldn’t tell if he was breathing either, but I looked down at my knife.

It was stuck in the wall.

The knife came down, missing him by inches, and he took off.
