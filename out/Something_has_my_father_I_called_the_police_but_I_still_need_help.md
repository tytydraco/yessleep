I was waiting in my car in dad's front yard. I was waiting for anyone, absolutely anyone, from Reddit to tell me who to call.

So I caved and called 9-1-1.

“9-1-1 What's the address of your emergency?” The lady asked on the other line.

“\[redacted\].” I told her.

“What's the nature of your emergency?” She asked.

“There's a dangerous animal at my fathers house.”

“What kind of animal?”

“I don't know. It has these huge tentacles and it dragged me through the house.”

“Slow down sir, you're not making any sense. Tentacles?” There was a pause.

“Look, there's a very large aggressive animal in my fathers house and he is still in there.” I pleaded.

“Alright sir, look we can send a squad car by. Animal control will also come. Do you know where the animal is right now?”

“I think it's under his bed.”

“There's a very large animal, under your father's bed?”

Well when you put it like that, “I don't know where it is to be honest. How long until someone can be here?”

“They will be there soon.” She said, I could hear a siren in the far distance getting closer. “Just stay on the line sir.”

I stayed on the line until the cop car pulled into the driveway. They shut their lights off and stepped out. A middle aged man and woman. The man is significantly larger than the woman. I step out of my car and approach them.

“Are you the one who called?” The man asked, approaching me cautiously. “We had a report of a large aggressive animal? Inside?”

“Yeah it’s in there with my father. It dragged me out of the house.” I warned them.

“Alright where did you last see it?” He asked. He was starting to walk towards the door.

“Under my fathers bed I think.” I said.

“The large animal is under your fathers bed?” The man stopped, turned around, and looked me up and down. “What did you say your name was again?”

“Mark \[redacted\].” I replied. “Aren’t you going to wait for animal control?” I honestly didn’t know how the approach to this was going to go but they seemed completely under prepared for what they were walking into.

“If he’s in danger we can’t really afford to wait. Any idea what it is?” The woman asked before following her partner.

“A….An Octopus?” I said quietly. I should have just said ‘I don’t know.’

They both turned around to look at me like I had two heads.

“What did you just say?” The woman broke the silence.

“It had these tentacles-” I started.

“Sir, have you been drinking?” The man asked, taking a step towards me.

“No! Well I had a little last night but that’s besides the point. Please check on my father!” I begged.

The man looked me up and down then motioned towards me. “Stay with him, I’ll go check it out.”

His partner moved closer to me, “got it.”

Time seemed to crawl by as the officer approached the door. When he reached the top of the stairs he gave three heavy loud knocks.

KNOCK

KNOCK

KNOCK

“\[Redacted\] Police! Here to do a wellness check! We heard there was a dangerous animal in the premises?” He spoke loudly. Something very large shifted inside.

“Did you hear that Chuck?” The woman next to me said.

“Something or someone is definitely in there,” the man said looking back.

KNOCK

KNOCK

KNOCK

“\[Redacted\] Police, your son called us! Please come to the door!” The man yelled.

This time something that sounded like breaking glass in the back of the house. The officer pulled his gun. Motioned for his partner to circle around the right side of the building. Then he took off around the left.

I stayed near the car. I could see the man circling as his partner went around the back of the house. I saw him bend down and looked into a basement window. It wasn’t even knee height. He pulled his flashlight on and shone it through the window.

“What in the fuck is that?” He asked. I could hear his partner coming around the back of the house.

One of those tentacles came out of that basement window. First time I ever saw one in the daylight. Dark purple with oillike slickness. Every foot or so there was a crease in the surface. Almost like it was plated in armor.

It quickly wrapped around the man’s waist. “What the fuck!” He yelled.

It pulled him off his feet fast. I don’t think the man had a chance in the world. When he hit the side of the building he bent.

And bent

And kept bending

Until his heels were touching his shoulder blades. He dropped the flashlight in the yard. Then he began to slip through the window. It peeled the skin off the top like a can opener. It caught on his jaw, which ripped from his skull and landed in the grass.

When I close my eyes I still hear the scream.

The moment it cut off.

The gurgling.

God the gurgling.

His partner rounded the corner in time to see the entire thing. She did what any sensible person would do. She screamed like a little fucking child.

You can’t blame her. There was blood all over the grass, blood splattering up the wall, a fine cut strip of human flesh all bunched up against the wall, and a human jaw laying on top like a king on a throne. A flashlight conveniently lighting the entire ensemble.

“401 to dispatch, officer down! Send an ambulance! I need backup here now!” She yelled into her radio.

“Copy, 401. Where do you need the ambulance?”

“Left side of the house. There’s a basement window.” She let go of the radio. She thought for a moment then hit it again. “Tell them to come to the driveway. Stay away from the house.”

She backed up until she bumped into me. I don’t think she knew I was there.

“Is there another way into that basement, Mark?”

It was the first time either one of them had used my name.

“No there isn’t. That’s the only one.” I said as the first cruise shot into the driveway.

The next ten minutes or so were a mixture of lights and sirens. They formed a formation in front of the house like it was a hostage situation. Finally, there was silence. A microphone screamed into the silence.

“\[Redacted\] if you are in there and able to escape you need to come out now. We have been authorized to use deadly force.” A man's voice sounded over the dead air.

The entire house groaned. The sounds of a house settling at night. Then to my immense surprise the front door opened and there was my father.

“Dad?! You’re okay?” I asked.

He took a few steps out onto the porch. I had already started to approach him when I saw it. The tentacle snaking up the bottom of his pant leg.

“I’m sorry my son called you. You need to leave. This is private property and I don’t want you here. I didn’t call you here.” He spoke in monotone.

“Dad what are you-?”

“Sir, that doesn’t matter now. An officer is either wounded or dead inside of that house. This is your last warning to vacate the premises.” The man spoke firmly from behind me without using the microphone.

“I am sorry about your friend. It was an unfortunate accident.” My father spoke.

“Sir, are you coming out or not?” The man demanded.

My father simply lifted off from his feet and the tentacle carried him back inside. The door slamming behind him.

“Alright ladies and gentlemen. Get ready we’re going in. There’s a man alive in there so we do our best to get him out alive!” The man spoke from behind me.

A team of men and women dressed in tactical gear approached the building quickly and quietly from both sides. They paid particular attention to avoiding any direct sight from inside.

One managed to sneak up to the basement window. He lobbed a metallic canister inside and then sprinted back.

The canister came flying back and went in under the front porch.

Gas started escaping between the floorboards, which immediately caught fire.

“We have fire!” A man yelled.

“Call fire!” Another yelled back.

The fire spread rapidly.

The fire department took twenty minutes to arrive. I don’t know if it was the newspapers or the amazon boxes or the insulation but that place went up like it was made of paper.

Then the screaming from inside began. Inhuman. Loud.

Emergency services were still setting up to tame the fire when the back wall of the house exploded.

“Dad!” I screamed, running to view the wall that had given out.

As the entire house shifted and began to cave in I saw it. It was fast, too fast for its size. A tangle of tentacles and eyes. Dark and slick it left a trail behind it that shone like oil. It bounded using its tentacles for the nearby woods. I caught just a glimpse of dad’s jacket in the mix of tentacles.

“Can you come get me, son?” I heard my dad’s voice in the back of my mind.

They are trying to track it but I am literally begging for help. Does anyone here know what to do? Who to call? This thing is on the loose and has my father.
