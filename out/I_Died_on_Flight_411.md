Hey y’all, It’s been a minute. 

I wish I could say I have good news, answers to all the mysteries, bring closure to this chapter of my life and the lives of the other souls on Flight 411. 

But I don’t.

Every time I think I’ve come close to a major break through, close to finding any sort of answers, I’m just met with another rabbit hole.

The black box was deemed unsalvageable. So the CVR, the FDR, all of it’s just scrap. The wreckage was in a pretty bad way too. Only about half of the plane had been recovered, and one of the engines. The FAA are reaching the limit of what they can learn from the crash. Hell even with a living, breathing, witness they couldn’t find anything useful in my testimony. 

Speaking of, that’s where I’d been the last little while. In and out of closed hearings with the FAA and some other politicians on some committee. We went over every detail of the flight from the pre-flight route planning and paper work, to my last moments on Flight 411.

We came to the conclusion that my survival was nothing short of a miracle, an act of god maybe. No doctor has been able to adequately explain my two weeks of memory loss. No expert had been able to explain how I ended up on a beach back home after surviving a near Mach 1 crash into the middle of the pacific. Nobody could find any fault in myself or my copilot. Nobody was able to concretely identify the cause of the crash based on the pitiful amount of wreckage recovered. 

Nobody in that room came out with answers, just more uncomfortable questions.

The FAA closed the case. Based on the totality of the damage, and the blessing that was the media’s ignorance to my survival, they ruled that the accident was, to the dismay and anger of the general public, an act of god. That is, an accident that could not have been avoided. I found that pretty strange, seeing as how the FAA tends to stray away from saying such a thing to preserve the safety of commercial aviation. I assume it’s due to the public scrutiny, that a shitty answer is better than no answer. 

Given that, while I was found innocent of any wrong doing despite having only my word to go on, the FAA had expressly prohibited me from ever flying commercial aircraft again.

I was crushed. 

Flying was the only thing I really had going for me. Without it I’m nothing. I got to keep my private license, but it’s not the same. I don’t have the money to rent a hangar or maintain my own aircraft, doubly so without a job, and no offense to the general aviation folks out there, but a Piper Cub doesn’t rile me up the way a 737 does. Even a 717, no slander, but those babies were designed for short hauls. I’d be content if that were all I was permitted to do, I just missed the whining of turbofans. 

Guess I’ll supplement it with my own whining then. 

The man in the blue polo hasn’t been around since the DMV. Maybe it really was just a coincidence. Or maybe I’m still being watched. 

I searched my emails for anything relating to this “Gemini” memory I mentioned. Nothing. Web searches were useless, Gemini is such a vague yet specific name that I’d have no way to confirm what I was even looking for. 

Truth be told, it feels like this is all getting swept under a rug. Usually I’d expect the government to be in on it, maybe we got shot down by a fighter or something like that Korean Air flight back in the 80s, but I’d argue they’re just as in the dark as I am. Cautions for sure, lots of red tape, but none of it felt like they wanted to hide what happened. More so that they wanted to keep tight lipped to stop rumors and misinformation. 

Begs the question then, if not the G-Men, then who? Or what? And why? 

But I keep asking those questions, sending it out into a universe that I pray to for answers. Oh how naive I must be to believe this universe cares enough to answer. It gave me a second chance at life, maybe I should be grateful for that.

Maybe I should leave all of this behind and just try to make some sense of whats left of my life.

Maybe I should try to just forget about all of this and move on. 

Maybe I am dead, well not ME me but you know, the Captain of Flight 411. Maybe he died on that flight and “I” survived. Maybe I’m a different person, not a pilot, maybe something else. Something different. 

Hah, I feel like an imposter wearing someone else’s skin. 

Maybe I am.
