I have been debating writing about this for a little over a year now, but I think it’s time I reveal what has been tormenting me since starting my new job.

Most people know me by Ed, a nickname i’ve used since I was little due to my name being a bit complicated for most people to know. It’s Edelberto, by the way. I’m mexican but I was raised in the U.S., so i’ve had to compromise in order to help english only speakers get comfortable with me easier and sooner. Besides my parents and my grandparents, no one else calls me by my full name. In fact, a good amount of people I know don’t even know what my full name is.

I started working as a lawn care specialist in a small company near Chicago. While it was a bit of a drive for me, about 45 minutes to an hour, the pay is excellent and the company was desperately looking for someone to fill the role as their small team had been reduced by someone abruptly quitting. Since I had recently lost my job and the economy was looking grim, I decided to accept the job. I was to work alone most days so it was a job with a lot more freedom than any other job I had before.

For anyone that is familiar with the suburbs directly north of Chicago, you’ll know that many of them are old, like still have brick roads type of old. Certain houses being historical preservations type of old. Not to mention, a good chunk of these suburbs are lake front areas, so the property value is insanely high and the people who own them are seldom home due to them working a lot. Hence, why I go to their houses to take care of their lawns.

 With that info out of the way, let me get into the story. Ever since I started working for this company, i’ve felt like things have been… off. My coworkers are great, but they seem to keep their distance from me, and from each other really. Almost like a silence pact or something from them. However, they seem to take extra measures to stay away from me. No one says hi, nor do they really help with any questions I have. I figured it was due to me being the new guy, or because I was replacing their old coworker, who from what my manager told me, was someone very well liked. The behavior never changed, which looking back should have been an indicator that something wasn’t right.

When I started working on lawns by myself, I picked up the habit of listening to either music, podcasts or videos with headphones. However, again with some of the areas I worked in being so old, the signal was not the best, often times being nonexistent on some properties. So, unless I had things downloaded, I would have breaks in what I was listening to while it loaded. It was during one of these little breaks that I first heard it. It was some slight movement to my left, like something was inching closer to me. Learning from a young age to be vigilant but discreet whenever i’m out in public, I kept my face towards the grass but used my peripheral vision to scan my surroundings. The figure I saw standing in a nearby bush made me turn my head faster than I expected, making me take a moment to register what just happened. I know I had seen something. A standing figure, full bodied and tall, standing directly in the bush as if the branches just went into it’s body. But, there was nothing there. Just a small plastic toy bird house hanging off a branch. I figured the lack of sleep and general paranoia for safety had made me imagine things. In fact, that was my explanation for 6 months, with at least 50 of these experiences.

The winter season had me at home, waiting for the spring to bring about our working time once again. I had no sightings of the kind during these months, so I had concluded that it was just my imagination running away with me. That was until I got a text from an unknown number, asking me directly: “Have you seen him yet?” Confused, I asked who they were and what they were talking about. All I got back was the same question. I didn’t answer. I knew what they meant, but I couldn’t bring myself to answer. In a panic, I used the work group chat to get one of my coworker’s number and asked if they recognized the number. She said yes, that it had been the old coworker’s number, but that he had switched his number shortly after he left the company. She asked me how I got the number, and I sent her the screenshot of the messages. All she said to me after that was: “Just make it easier on yourself and answer the question, get it over with.” Although I was confused, I didn’t pry any further and just did as she said. Something in her message seemed more of a command than a suggestion, and I felt compelled to listen. The number stopped contacting me after that. In fact, it seems to have been disabled.

What troubles me now is that the experiences have gotten more and more aggressive. What started as a vague figure in the bush about 10ft away has now become a distinct tall, burly man with a permanent scowl and fully black eyes. An untucked, black button up shirt covers it, as well as black cargo pants and some work boots. How can I know all this? Well, it’s no longer just in my peripheral vision. I see it for a second longer as I turn, and it’s staying for a bit longer each time. However, I can’t stare at it straight up from the beginning. It must start with my peripheral.

What’s worse is that it seems to be getting closer. It sometimes appears farther away, and sometimes appears closer than ever. What I guess could be called my breaking point happened late last week. I felt a weird sensation on my neck, almost as if I had a fly hovering above and slightly caressing it with it’s flight path. I move my hand back and forth as if to scare it away, but the sensation persists. Finally, I turn to see what it is and out of my peripheral, I see the entity. I freeze, not daring to fully look at it, but not being able to move out of it’s presence. I actually shut my eyes and begin take deeper breaths. As i’m doing so, my video pauses, loading up the next chunk of content and leaving me in silence. That’s when I hear this entity saying something rhythmically under it’s breath: “Edelberto, Edleberto, Edelberto.” My eyes grew wide as I realized what it was saying. When I turned to face it, there was nothing there. However, I got a text almost immediately from my coworker, with one simple sentence: “I knew he would like you.”

I’ve tried coming into work as usual for the last three days, but everything seems to be worse. I don’t see any of my coworkers at all anymore. I know they show up to work because all the trucks are gone when I go in. Well, all except one. That specific coworker has been missing since that text was sent. She seemingly vanished into thin air. Well, for everyone else at least. Not for me. Today at a lake front property, I was raking some leaves when, in my peripheral, I see two figures: A tall, burly man with an untucked button down, black cargo pants and work boots. His all black eyes staring me down, his scowl intimidating me. And by his side was a skinny, pale, blue lipped woman, with her clothes and hair appearing drenched and disheveled. Since then, i’ve seem them everywhere, including in my corner of my room.

So now I ask you: Have you seen them yet?
