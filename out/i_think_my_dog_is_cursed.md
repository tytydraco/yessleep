i've had my dog since she was 6 weeks old. she is now going on 15 years old and is a retired service animal. she's been by my side her whole life and has been the best pet anyone could ever dream of. but i've always felt like she's unnaturally intelligent. she seems to be very aware of everything going on around her. she knows whenever you are talking to her, knows her commands in 3 languages, and has found her own ways to let me know what she wants. she stands by the door when she wants to go potty and barks once to be let back inside, and she licks my water bottle if her water runs out. she's done all this since she was a puppy, and i've gotten used to her quirks over the last 15 years. however, other people meet her and say she has this look in her eyes, like she really is listening. they say that she's conscious and too humanlike. i've always thought that was funny until yesterday.

i woke up later than i'd like for work. my morning routine consists of waking up, waking my dog up and taking her outside, and getting ready for the day. since i was in a rush, i only woke her up and went straight to the bathroom. she follows me around everywhere for the most part except the bathroom, she'll typically just wander around the house. i thought nothing of it when i left the bathroom and she wasn't in the kitchen waiting to be let out. i focused on making breakfast for the two of us and eventually running to the front door. that was when it hit me, i hadn't let her out or said goodbye. i turned around expecting her right behind me, but she wasnt there. i figured i would just text my brother to let her out later. i turned to open the front door when i heard scratching coming from the other side. it didn't really shake me, as we live in a town with a stray problem. i opened the door and found her standing on the other side. i froze for a second, and then brought her inside before running around the house trying to find where she came from. all of my doors, including the garage door, were closed and locked. the windows were all closed. i was about to go talk to my brother, when i passed a window pointing to the driveway. his car was not there. he had left for a work trip yesterday. how did she get outside, more importantly, the front yard?

i didn't have time to think about it, as i was already late for work. i went back through the house, made sure every single door was locked, and drove to work. i eventually forgot about it and went through my day. but then i got home from work and things got even more weird.

as i mentioned before, my dog has been with me for 15 years. i know her in and out, her behavior doesn't just change. every time she is home alone, she lays on the bed of our spare bedroom and looks out the window until someone gets home. i didn't see her when i pulled up into the driveway, and it struck me as unusual, but not completely crazy. then i started walking toward the house. i was trying to peek through the window to see if she was just sleeping when i heard a loud bark. it was definitely her bark, but it was a little off. it was coming from inside the house, and i officially started freaking out. she rarely barks, and certainly never loud enough to be heard through the outside. i thought maybe she just got excited to see me and was looking at me through another window, or maybe barking at a stray dog, so i walked into the house expecting her to be in the living room waiting for me. instead, she runs out of the kitchen with her tail wagging. this is really weird, as there is no view of my driveway through the kitchen. it is also weird because there isnt any way for a stray dog to get into our backyard. we have 10 foot solid brick fence. why was she barking? and what was making her bark in that way?

then the night came. we also have a routine for bedtime. i give her her dinner, and i check up on all my other pets. i work with exotic animals and have a few of them in my bedroom. it is important to note a few things. first, my dog loves animals. she will try to get to know every single creature she finds, whether its a giant horse or a tiny spider. i constantly find her sitting and watching the animals in my terrariums, especially my baby frog tank, since its at her eye level. second, my baby frog tank is ridiculously hard to open. the lid fits very *very* snug, so much so that i, a recently retired gymnast, struggle opening with both hands. with that out of the way, i start examining my tanks and notice that my frog tank lid is completely off. i start panicking.  frogs, especially at 4 weeks old, are extremely sensitive to low humidity, so having their lid on tight is crucial for their survival. in addition to that, they are amazing escape artists. if they find a teeny gap in their enclosure, they will get out. i start looking around the enclosure trying to find them, and miraculously, they are all sitting on the bottom, soaking up the water still on the glass. the relief is extremely short-lived, because it moves onto wondering how on earth the enclosure was left open. i have diagnosed ocd, and i always take photos of things to ease my anxiety on whether i did them or not, like locked doors and shut-off stoves. i checked my camera roll from this morning and lo and behold, the closed frog tank was right there. i am now at a loss. what on earth could have opened that lid? it certainly is way too tight for my dog to have lifted it with her nose, and my brother is still away out of state.

i end up feeding them and the rest of the animals, making sure the lids are all shut with no gaps, and turning on a movie to try and distract myself. my dog typically lays in my bed to sleep, and she is already there by the time i'm done gettin ready for bed, so i shut the door and turn off the movie once i lay down. i already sleepy from the long day at work, and i feel my eyes shutting while the movie is playing. i close my eyes and start drifting off, when i hear a loud thud outside my window. i my quickly open my eyes and see my bedroom door is open all the way. the only light i had to see was the dim blue light coming from my fish tank, the rest of my bedroom was completely dark. i hesitated before pulling my phone out to check, and sure enough, the photo of my shut door from a few minutes ago was right there. i have no explanation as to what is happening. i get up and check my house, the doors are all locked, there is no one here. i am completely alone.

i woke up today with a migraine. she hasn't done anything strange, just keeps staring at things that aren't there. she hasn't eaten anything, not even the bit of scrambled egg i offered her when i made breakfast. and whether or not a coincidence, she has been staring at me the entire time i've been writing this. i don't know what to do.
