**The Woman We Picked Up in the Middle of Nowhere**  
This happened about five years ago when I was in college, and I still think about it because I genuinely have no idea what happened that night.  
My ex-boyfriend, two friends, and I were getting ready to do a 125-mile backpacking trip in the Uinta Mountains in Utah. If you've ever been out there, you know it's pretty much the middle of nowhere. We were planning to drive to one of the trailheads, camp for the night, and then shuttle to the other trailhead in the morning to start our trip. We were in two cars. My ex and I were in the first car, which was a little Honda Civic, and our two friends were following behind us.

It was probably around 10 p.m., and we'd already been without cell service for over an hour. We were all exhausted and just wanted to get to the trailhead and go to bed. We were driving up this winding dirt road when, out of nowhere, a truck came up behind us and passed both of our cars.  
We figured that since we were in a little Civic in the middle of the backcountry, following a truck that seemed to know where it was going might be helpful. So we sped up a little and started following it. We ended up following this truck for maybe 10 or 15 minutes, but it was going way too fast. We weren't about to go 40 mph on a dirt road in a Civic, so eventually we lost it. Somewhere along the way, we'd also lost our friends behind us, although we didn't realize that until later.

Anyways, we kept driving, and I remember going around a few corners and suddenly getting this really disgusting feeling in my stomach. I don't know how else to explain it, but I just felt like something was wrong. And then, literally out of nowhere, this woman came running out of the woods, waving her arms at us. I remember everything feeling like it was happening really fast and really slow at the same time.

My ex asked, "Should we pull over?"  
I immediately said no. He looked at me and was like, "No, I think we need to." So we pulled over, and I rolled down my window and  asked her, "What the fuck is going on? What's happening?"  
She told us that a cougar had been stalking her through the woods. My first thought was that if there was a mountain lion nearby, I should try to scare it off, so I started laying on the horn.  
And she immediately asked me to stop.  
Like, she was begging me, "Please stop. Don't honk."

My ex and I just looked at each other because what the fuck? You're telling us a mountain lion is following you, but you don't want us making noise?  
At the same time, this woman looked absolutely horrified. Her eyes were huge, and she genuinely looked terrified. So we were in this really weird position where we didn't know whether to believe her, but we also didn't want to leave someone alone in the woods if she actually needed help.  
I told her that if she wanted to get in our car, she needed to take off her jacket and show us her pockets. She did, and she didn't appear to have anything on her, so we let her into the back seat.  
I remember turning on the interior light and looking back at her while we were driving. She was probably in her 40s, smelled strongly of cigarettes, and had absolutely nothing with her. No phone, no water, no backpack, nothing. I also remember putting a pin on my map where we'd picked her up, which ended up being important later.

So we started driving, and she began telling us what had happened. She said she'd been walking downhill for about five or ten minutes because a mountain lion had been stalking her, and she'd been trying to get away from it.

She told us she was camping nearby and needed to get back to her campsite.  
Based on what she was telling us, I figured it would take maybe two or three minutes to drive her back. She couldn't have walked that far, right?  
Well, we started driving, and five minutes passed. Then ten. Then fifteen.  
And we were still driving.  
I was trying to make conversation with her because, frankly, I was uncomfortable and wanted to figure out what was going on (I’m also a psychologist and was trying to figure out if she was lying) 

I asked how long she'd been camping out there, and she said she'd been there since the night before. At some point, though, she started talking about how she'd been stalked by a moose or a deer or something, which was weird because she'd originally told us it was a mountain lion.  
I also asked if this was her first time visiting the area and where she was from. I remember her telling me somewhere in Idaho. We kept talking for another five or ten minutes, and I decided to bring it up again.  
I said something like, "Oh, you said you're from Salt Lake City, right? That's nice. You're not too far from home."And she immediately responded, "Yeah, I've lived in Salt Lake my whole life."

I remember looking over at my ex, and he looked back at me. We both picked up on it.  
She was telling us completely different things, and I couldn't figure out why.At this point, we'd been driving with this woman in our back seat for probably 20 or 25 minutes. We still had no service, our friends were nowhere in sight, and she kept insisting that her campsite was just a little farther up the road.

Every time we'd pass another road, I'd ask if that was it, and she'd look at the map and say something like, "Oh, I think we're almost there. Just keep going." I was getting increasingly nervous because none of this made sense. She'd supposedly been out on a short walk from her campsite, had been chased downhill for five or ten minutes, and somehow we'd been driving uphill for almost half an hour without finding where she was staying.  
Finally, around 27 minutes into the drive, I told her, "Hey, I'm so fucking sorry, but we really need to let you out. We're almost where we need to be, and we can't keep driving around."She kept looking at the map, trying to figure out where we were.  
And then, at around 29 minutes, she suddenly said, "Wait, this is it! Turn down this road."  
We looked over, and it was this tiny, single-lane dirt road.  
At this point, I was pretty freaked out. I remember leaning over to my ex and whispering, "Hey, if she's going to rob us, just give her whatever the fuck she wants. Like, seriously, don't fight her."  
We knew our friends were somewhere nearby, and we were getting close to our own trailhead. I just wanted this woman out of our car. So we turned down the road and slowly drove until we reached this little parking lot. There was nobody there. No other people, no tents that I could see, nothing.  
Just one van.

She immediately told us that was hers, thanked us, and said, "You saved my life." Then she got out, climbed into her van, and drove away.  
It happened so quickly that we barely had time to process anything. I remember sitting there afterward, just trying to make sense of the whole thing. I pulled up my map because I'd marked exactly where we'd picked her up, and I wanted to see if there was actually a campsite anywhere near that location.

And here's the part that still bothers me.  
The only campsite I could find near where we'd picked her up was about **two miles behind us**, in the opposite direction from where she'd had us drive.  
We'd just spent almost 30 minutes driving this woman farther up the mountain, and I still couldn't figure out where she'd supposedly been walking from or why she'd been out there with absolutely nothing on her. She'd told us different stories about where she lived, what animal had been following her, and how she'd ended up in the woods. She couldn't tell us where her campsite was until we'd been driving for almost half an hour, and then she got into her van and immediately left.

And the thing I always come back to is that when we first pulled over, she was absolutely terrified. I mean, she genuinely looked like she'd seen something horrible.But when I started honking to scare away the cougar she claimed was following her, she begged me to stop.

We never saw a cougar. We never saw any animal. We never saw anything in the woods at all.  
I have no idea if she was actually being followed by something, if she was confused or disoriented, or if there was something else going on entirely.  
I just know that we picked up a random woman in the middle of nowhere at 10 p.m., drove her around for nearly half an hour while her story kept changing, and then watched her get into a van and disappear.  
It's been five years, and I still have absolutely no fucking idea what happened that night.
