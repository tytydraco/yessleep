I never understood the appeal of going to a farmers market. Why would you want to go somewhere that costs you an arm and a leg for someone’s crappy sourdough bread, or for half a dozen of eggs? Not to mention that I live in Arizona, so it’s as hot as satan’s ass crack in a sauna. 

My girlfriend on the other hand, absolutely loves farmers markets, damn near everything she owns is from a farmers market. Soap, milk, toothpaste, cooking utensils, hell if she would buy a car if it was sold at a farmers market. 

“It’s good to support independent farmers, than the corporate giants!” She would preach as she posted TikTok’s on her brand new, up to date iPhone. She would always try to convince me into biting the bullet to go to a farmers market nearby. She finally got me after saying; “Ya’know, farmer’s markets don’t only got produce.”

“What, really?”

“Yeah! They got used clothes, old electronics, they might even have some instruments!”

After spending around $200 dollars on groceries, or if you can even call it that. $200 at a Walmart or Costco would at least last me about a week or two, meanwhile the two carton of eggs I gotten here costed me about $20. I swear the price of everything here is so high, I got a used “Death Note” shirt that cost me around $35, then I saw it on Depop for $7 a couple days later. 

Shortly after deciding that I was tired of my wallet getting molested, I started to find my way back to my car, when in my peripheral vision, I saw a glowing red orb. I blinked and turned to find multiple stacked on top of each other, I walked closer to see that they were apples. Not like any apple I’ve ever seen before, they were constantly spritzed with water, reflecting the harsh Arizonan sun. They looked absolutely delicious, they even left a sweet aroma around them.

“E-excuse me sir?” I asked around nervously the man behind the counter, “are you selling these apples?”

The chunky middle aged man gave me a stank look, before getting up from his foldable chair, grunting a bit before waddling over.

“Uhhh, the fuck do you think?” He said in a gruff voice pointing at a sign that said “Fresh, Juicy, and Sweet Apples!” In a multi colored cursive font, with flowers and hearts that danced around the words.

“O-oh! Sorry for ask-“ I was interrupted by a scruffy laugh, that sounded like someone shoved a brick down his throat. “D’ohohohoh! I’m just jerking ya chain kid. Yeah they’re for sell.”

He said leaning on the plastic foldable table; “My sister makes em herself.”

“She…makes them?”

“Yeah like bioengineering or cross-pollination, or something like that. Ya’know like how Granny Smith and Lady Hamilton make Braeburn apples.”

I remember there was a long pause after that, before I blurted out.

“…what?”

“Like how Granny fuckin’ Smith makes- ya’know what, they’re just good apples, I don’t know if it’s a type of soil, or fertilizer that she uses, but they’re pretty fuckin A.” He sounded a lot more annoyed after that. He then raised an eyebrow at me, before asking; “Ya wanna try a sample?”

Before responding; he wiped his hands on a wet towel, flipped open a pocket knife from his pocket, and firmly grasped the top apple. The knife gracefully slid into its skin, then fruit, then core. Leaving the creamy colored juices to evacuate into his palm, followed by a slight muffled sound. What remained were six even slices. “I already cut it up, you can’t say no now! It would be rude to!” He said in a smug voice.

I reluctantly accepted the wedge, and took a small bite from its corner. It was actually pretty damn good. It had a crunchy bite, but chewed up softly, like the consistency of mashed potatoes. Its initial taste was sour, but it evolved into a sweet aftertaste.

“That’s pretty good.”

“Fucking told ya! They’re about 50¢ a pop.”

“Fifty cents? Usually an apple cost about a dollar or two at Walmart.” Was my initial thought, I finally found a reasonable price for a food at this damn scalpers market. I responded, “Wow, that’s a damn good deal, how much come in a bundle?”

“Depends on the size. A small has 3, a medium has 6, and a large has 9. Anything above that is considered a basket.”

“I’ll take a medium then.” I begin to dig into my purse and pull out a five. “You can keep the change.”

He thanked me and I began to finally get to my car. I set my cold groceries into the backseat floor; milk, eggs, meat, things that could spoil easily from the heat. I then went into my passenger side, and set the nonperishables onto the seat. While driving back home, I gotten pretty hungry, all I had that day was a small bowl of cereal, and the apple slice that farmer offered me. While keeping my eyes on the road, I grabbed my wallet and looked around, I didn’t even have enough to even buy myself a Happy Meal. “Shit, why did my cheap ass have to only bring 200?” I said to myself, letting my eyes focus back on the road.

I looked to my side to see the bundle of apples, they tempted and teased me, telling me they’re all mine. Their skin was plump, they were red like an embarrassed sun, the water was still dripping off from the spritz, I ripped open the net bundle, and bobbled with my hand for the smallest one. After finding it, I examined it, before rubbing the excess water onto my shirt and taking a bite.

Maybe I was just hungry, but it tasted even better than the sample I had; it was akin to a pastry, a crunchy outside, with a soft inside. its juices were tart, then sweet, not the overbearing sweetness as store brand apple juice, but that of a natural sweet candy. Its skin was thin, the texture of wasn’t like paper, but almost that of a grape. I was lost in its flavor, until my phone rosed me to the surface.

It was my girlfriend, calling with a FaceTime request. I pressed accepted and her face met mine. She looked shocked and then started to giggle, “Woah, looks like you had a fun time!” She said through her giggles.

I gave a confused look and scoff, responding, “Can you really tell that I didn’t enjoy going there?”

“What, no? Look at your chin, it’s covered with smudged lipstick and some kind of juice.” She answered before saying in a teasing, cutesy voice, “Were you cheating on meeeeee?”

I looked at my side of the call and found exactly that. My lipstick was smudged and apple juice was dripping and sticking onto my face. I looked onto the floor and saw three out of the six apple cores lying on the floorboard. “Damn, I’ve must’ve been real hungry.” I thought, before grabbing another and saying to her, “Yeah I have, her name is Ms. Apple, and she wants you to be a cuck.”

She then yelled in a hypermasculine voice “YOU BIIIIITCHHHHHH, I’LL TURN HER INTO AN APPLE PIE!” We both chuckled, and continued our conversation.

After the course of a few weeks, I would buy, eat, occasionally cook, and try to plant these divine apples. Eventually me and the farmer would be on a first name basis, his name is Paulie. I made; apple cider, apple pies, apple fritters, caramel apples, roasted apples, etc. it’s gone to a point where I was spending forty-eight dollars a month on apples.

I was practically addicted to them, until today. I’m not even sure if I should say this, but a couple days ago Paulie got into an accident, a pretty nasty one, and he couldn’t sell apples anymore. So I stocked up and tried to preserve as many as I could and today, I grabbed the last one. It was a bit mushy, some spots almost looked purple, butI wasn’t going to let that ruin my last apple. I decided to prepare it into a childhood delicacy of mine. Whenever I was doing homework, my mom would peel an apple, cut it into 8 wedges, and give me a tiny cup of peanut butter to dunk them in.

I grabbed a potato peeler, rinsed both the apple and peeler off, and began to peel. The skin was being ripped off its juicy “meat”, my mouth salivating as they gracefully fell onto the table. I couldn’t help, but toss the excised tissue into my mouth. However, I did begin to feel a vibration in the palm of my hand, its muffled sound didn’t bother me much, as my eyes were entranced by the cream color of its flesh.

Oh it’s pulp, such a beautiful pale, light muted beige coloring, seemed to have some sort of vitiligo. Its warm ivory hue had spots of a deep red cherry color. As I removed the last piece of skin it had, I dropped the apple onto the table, it crashing into its surface, bouncing, rolling, and leaving trails of white and red juices tracking its path. My scream wasn’t the only ones that infected the air, as a saw a small face revealed on the fruit. I looked at one of the tossed aside skins, I quickly ran towards it, and picked it up. I examined the peel to find what looked like a deformed ear. I then looked at the screaming tiny face.

Its eyebrows -no, forehead muscles frowned, its eyes were sunken into its “skull” falling back into its core, they looked like seeds. Its nose was shrunken or withered, only looking like a hard to notice bump with two long nostril holes. Its small lips, its gaping mouth, its teeth and tongue turning from red to white. 

Which leads me to here, what the hell have I’ve been eating for the past two months. They can’t be people, I reject, no I REFUSE to believe that for the past two months, I’ve been scarfing down these… Ap-Peoples? Peopples? Things?!??? Whatever, I just need to know that that couldn’t be a person, there’s just no way I ate someone like some freak Dahmer. Even if it’s like just a sentient apple, like a fantasy creature, that would at least give me some form of mental ease, to know that I just didn’t commit a taboo crime against humanity.

I don’t believe it, no matter how long it screamed, as its bloody juices dripped from my lips.
