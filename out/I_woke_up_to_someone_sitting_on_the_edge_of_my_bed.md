Every now and then, I'd have the same nightmare. It wasn't particularly scary at first, just incredibly uncomfortable. In the dream, I'd be lying in my bed, exactly where I'd fallen asleep, and someone would sit down on the edge of the mattress. I could feel the bed dip under their weight, usually somewhere near my feet. I never saw who it was, but I always knew someone was there. The weirdest part was how normal it felt. You know when someone sits on your bed while you're half asleep, and you can tell they're there without even opening your eyes? It felt exactly like that.

Every time, I'd wake up a few seconds later. The room would be empty, and I'd tell myself it was just another dream. I live alone, by the way, so there was absolutely no reason for anyone to be sitting on my bed in the middle of the night. After a while, I started getting used to it. I'd wake up, check the time, turn over, and go back to sleep. I even mentioned it to a friend once, and she joked that maybe my apartment was haunted. We laughed about it, and I didn't think much of it afterward.

Then one night, something different happened. I remember waking up because I felt the mattress sinking near my legs. My first thought was that I was having that stupid dream again, but when I tried lifting my arm to reach for my phone, nothing happened. I tried turning my head, opening my mouth, even moving my fingers, but my entire body seemed completely frozen. That's when I realized I was experiencing sleep paralysis. I'd read about it before, so I knew what was probably happening. I kept telling myself to stay calm and that it would pass in a few seconds.

Then I felt the mattress move again. Whatever was sitting at the foot of my bed seemed to shift its weight, like someone adjusting their position to get comfortable. My eyes were slightly open, and I could see part of my bedroom. The curtains, the wardrobe, the little red light on my TV. Everything looked normal except for the dark shape sitting near my feet. I couldn't make out any details, but it looked like someone sitting with their back to me, shoulders slightly hunched. I remember thinking it was strange that I couldn't see their head properly, even though there was enough light coming through the curtains.

I wanted to scream, but I couldn't make a sound. Then the figure leaned forward, the mattress dipped a little more, and I felt something brush against my ankle through the blanket. I have no idea how long I stayed like that. It might have been ten seconds or several minutes. Eventually, I managed to move one finger, then my hand, and suddenly I could move my whole body again. I sat up so quickly that I almost fell off the bed, but there was nobody there.

I turned on every light in the apartment and spent the rest of the night on the couch. The next morning, I looked up sleep paralysis again and found plenty of stories about people seeing figures in their rooms or feeling someone touching them. That actually made me feel better because I finally had a reasonable explanation for everything. For the next few nights, I slept with a lamp on, and nothing happened. No nightmares, no strange sensations, nothing. I started thinking that maybe the whole thing was finally over.
