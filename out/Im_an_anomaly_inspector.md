Sort of like a health inspector but marginally more interesting. My number doesn’t exist on any keypad, and half the time I show up people assume they’re being kicked out. The last visit was average, but interesting. 

They opened the door before I knocked. 

“Whaddya want?” 

“I have reports of an infestation in your apartment, you have thirty minutes to gather any essentials. You will be fully compensated for any losses.” 

It’s so interesting to me how a facial expression can convey an entire sentence. 

“I- infestation!? I clean every–“ 

“Thirty minutes.” 

Here’s how this typically goes down. The first five minutes are spent doubting if I’m being serious or not, the next ten are spent texting friends about some *asshole* pestering them, and the next fifteen are spent being smart or stupid. Today’s gentlemen decided to be stupid, he rushed back to the door with nothing but the clothes on his back. 

*“Dude! My god–I’m so sorry I just called the cops–I- I had no idea you–“* 

“You’re fine. I doubt it’s anything serious.” 

I lied, he’ll probably be dead in a few months. Small stains like black Sea urchins rest on the tops of his eyelids. 

*“Okay look—I have a lot of-“* 

I raised my hand to stop him. 

“Don’t forget your keys. I shouldn’t be more than a few hours. My associates are in the lobby, feel free to wait there until I’m finished.” 

“Okay.” 

Past that he left with little effort, and I entered the apartment. 

Remember what I said earlier? Forget it.  

Whatever fears I had about him being dead in a few months vanish, he’ll be dead in days, hell he’s probably already gone by the time I wrote this. 

Stepping inside the apartment, everything is completely normal. That’s the issue, everything’s normal, there wasn’t so much as a speck of dust in the sunlight. 

Speaking of sunlight, this happened last Sunday. It was 94° without a single cloud. You can imagine my surprise when there’s a hail of water colliding against his window, and a nearly pitch black cloud that stretched past the horizon. 

Putting on a pair of gloves, I tap the window a few times. 

No sound, and no give, felt like tapping a brick wall. 

I tapped the wall next to it.   
There is sound, but the sound of my tapping is heard from above me, muffled in the ceiling. 

Temporal and spatial. 

Shoulda made a wish. 

Every thirty minutes I put in a few eyedrops. Turns out, eyedrops actually prevent the black stains on the eyelids, but most people don’t use eyedrops every day so… 

Not much we can do for them. 

It’s a strange regret, it’s like shooting someone in the head and somehow going to dinner with them later. 

Or maybe it isn’t I don’t know. 

In my suitcase is a flashlight, it can shine UV, IR, and visible light. I tapped the wall while walking towards the front, every tap is above me until about twelve feet from it. 

Next, I need to find out if it’s localized or not. It might seem that way, given the nature of the tapping, but sound can be especially misleading with anomalies. 

First, visible light. 

Standing at the front door, I shine from one corner of the living room to the next. In the center of the room, the rays from my flashlight warp and shine in all directions like it’s hitting a kaleidoscope.

Next, UV. 

Nothing. There was a faint whispering sound as I shined it against the window, but that was all. 

IR. 

Nothing. 

Now, this next piece of equipment is seriously hard to describe, it’s beyond what any civilian would even recognize as a “tool” and is borderline magic. 

I grabbed a small rubber ball from my suitcase and threw it in the direction of the light anomaly. It came back to me like I had originally tossed it there. 

“Hmmm…” 

I mumbled. 

I tried that in a few different directions but everything occurred as normal. Oh, also, the rest of his apartment was normal. Typically the more mundane ones manifest in a bedroom or something. 

For it to be in the living room is a big deal for the person here. I don’t know, I’ll update you guys on what happened to him if I find out, usually that info is pretty hard to get a hold of. 

The third thing to do is call somebody, preferably someone you know well. I decided to call my mother, if anything was wrong I would know almost immediately. 

*“Hello?”* 

“Hey mom, how you doing?” 

*“What do you want?”* 

“Nothing, love you, bye.” 

*“Okay.”* 

Thankfully nothing mental is happening, the headaches after those are beyond unbearable. I usually head to one of our locations and get put under until it’s over. I’ve tried sitting through one before and it’s… 

I’m not going into it, and I won’t, ever. 

The final step is the most boring. I set a timer for sixty minutes, and wait. I wait until the timer is over and repeat the same process listed earlier again to see if the zone is expanding. 

It isn’t, thank god.

After that I close the door, seal it, and head back to the lobby. 

Here’s how my notes are usually charted: 

*Resident made aware of ‘infestation’ at 1330, displayed regular signs of confusion, stars present on both outer eyelids, sclera normal, no signs of abrasion/contusion/warping on skin. Home taut,* ***storm present****, no signs of entry, no signs of break in/human alteration. One anomaly confirmed, TM-SP, no mental alterations identified. Physical anomaly present in center of living room, only when moving towards it from the door, tapping against window and walls surrounding windows for twelve feet produces tapping in the ceiling.* 

*Overall threat level: medium, bring eyedrops, no physical harm experienced while present.* 

I don’t fix the anomalies, I inspect them. Unfortunately I can’t disclose company information. I’ll try to get something though, can’t say it’ll be easy. 

Anyways that’s it, bye.
